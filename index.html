COPD GWAS Locus Prioritization Browser

This website hosts the outputs generated from the agentic COPD locus review workflow. Each locus undergoes literature review, citation verification, adversarial review, and final evidence synthesis.

Workflow Overview

Candidate genes are collected from locus definitions and prioritization methods. Literature evidence is gathered, verified, stress-tested, and combined with Open Targets evidence before creating final rankings.

Researcher

Searches PubMed, bioRxiv, medRxiv, and web resources.

Citation Verifier

Confirms publication existence and validates claims.

Red-Team Skeptic

Evaluates contradictory findings and possible biases.

Synthesis Lead

Produces final evidence tables and gene prioritization.
