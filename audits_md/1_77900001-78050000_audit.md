## Gene-level skeptic verdicts (verbatim reasoning from red-team-skeptic)

### DNAJB4 — CONTESTED
"The researcher's own cited evidence undermines this candidate more than the write-up's
framing suggests. Two independent papers (Dicanio 2022, Shi 2023), in two unrelated disease
contexts, both examine the exact lead/credible-set variant (rs34517439, PIP=0.9998) and
report it as a functional eQTL for the neighboring gene FUBP1, not DNAJB4 — Shi et al.
specifically in lung tissue. This is not a case of 'no evidence for DNAJB4'; it is
affirmative molecular evidence that the fine-mapped variant's regulatory target is a
different gene. Combined with the Mendelian phenotype mismatch (DNAJB4 loss-of-function
causes a Z-disc myofibrillar myopathy with restrictive respiratory failure via
diaphragm/axial muscle weakness — mechanistically unrelated to obstructive airway/
parenchymal COPD pathophysiology) and the complete absence of any COPD, spirometry,
airway-epithelium, or immune-pathway literature on DNAJB4, the 'nearest gene / FLAMES'
assignment looks like a proximity artifact rather than a mechanistically supported call. The
researcher does flag this appropriately (no overclaiming detected in the write-up itself —
the hedging is honest), but the strength of the contradicting eQTL evidence warrants
elevating this beyond a soft caveat.

Verdict: CONTESTED — direct, verified eQTL evidence (Dicanio 2022, PMID 36082445; Shi 2023,
PMID 37932299) at the exact credible-set variant points to FUBP1, not DNAJB4, as the
regulatory target; DNAJB4's only disease mechanism is a phenotypically discordant
(restrictive, not obstructive) Mendelian myopathy."

### ZZZ3 — CONTESTED
"Two independent problems here. First, the coordinate discrepancy is not a minor footnote —
per the researcher's own Ensembl check, ZZZ3 (chr1:77,562,416-77,683,550) does not physically
overlap the stated locus interval (1:77,900,001-78,050,000) at all; it sits >200kb away. If
confirmed (build mismatch vs. a genuinely mis-scoped PoPS window need to be distinguished),
this would mean ZZZ3 should not be a candidate gene at this locus in the first place,
regardless of its PoPS score. Second, and importantly, this also compromises the Open
Targets L2G comparison offered as 'context': ZZZ3's FEV1 L2G score (0.339, higher than
DNAJB4's 0.197) is being used implicitly to suggest ZZZ3 is competitive — but if ZZZ3's L2G
evidence derives from a credible set >200kb away, that L2G score may reflect an entirely
distinct GWAS signal, not the same rs34517439 credible set under discussion at all.
Presenting it side-by-side with DNAJB4's L2G score risks the reader assuming they're
evidence for the same locus/signal when they may not be. Beyond the coordinate issue, the
literature is uniformly non-lung, non-COPD: general ATAC-complex chromatin biology, lung
cancer xenograft tumorigenesis (not airway/COPD), a tangential single-case pancreatic cancer
TIL mutation note, and vascular smooth muscle/atherosclerosis biology. None of this is
lung-developmental or airway-immune specific.

Verdict: CONTESTED — the coordinate/locus-overlap discrepancy is a potentially disqualifying
problem that the researcher flagged but did not resolve, and should be resolved (build
check, confirm actual PoPS window definition) before ZZZ3 is used in synthesis at all. Even
setting that aside, there is no COPD- or lung-function-specific mechanistic literature; this
looks like a PoPS statistical prioritization without biological corroboration, and possibly
one anchored to the wrong locus entirely."

**Orchestrator follow-up (independent confirmation, not part of red-team-skeptic's own
search budget):** the synthesis-lead independently queried Open Targets'
`genomicLocation` field for ZZZ3 (ENSG00000036549) directly and obtained
chr1:77,562,416-77,683,422 (GRCh38, strand -1) — matching the researcher's Ensembl-derived
coordinates. Since both Ensembl and Open Targets report the same GRCh38 coordinates, a
simple genome-build mismatch (e.g. GRCh37 vs. GRCh38) is ruled out as the explanation; the
discrepancy more likely reflects the internal PoPS window for this locus being wider than
the printed credible-set/locus interval (1:77,900,001-78,050,000). This does not resolve
whether ZZZ3 belongs at this locus — it only narrows down the likely cause of the mismatch
for whoever investigates the internal pipeline further.

### FUBP1 — WEAKENED
"This is the most interesting candidate but also where the overclaiming risk is highest. The
genuinely strong point: unlike DNAJB4 and ZZZ3, the evidence for FUBP1 is anchored to the
exact fine-mapped variant (not a proxy or 'nearby gene' inference), and two independent
studies in two unrelated disease contexts (multiple myeloma GWAS, COVID-19 cross-trait
colocalization) converge on the same direction of effect (risk allele -> lower lung FUBP1
expression). That is a nontrivial form of convergent, allele-consistent evidence. However,
the researcher's framing — 'FUBP1 is the strongest mechanistic candidate at this locus' —
overreaches relative to what is actually shown:
- Neither supporting eQTL study was performed in a COPD context or used COPD GWAS data;
  both are incidental findings from unrelated disease GWAS (myeloma risk, COVID-19
  severity). No formal COPD-lung-eQTL colocalization (coloc/SMR-HEIDI) has been done for
  this locus. 'Strongest candidate' is being asserted on borrowed, not purpose-built,
  evidence.
- The proposed mechanism (epithelial-intrinsic interferon/antiviral signaling, via John et
  al. 2025 in A549 cells) is a plausible bridge to COPD given documented viral-exacerbation
  and innate-immune-dysfunction biology in COPD, but it is speculative — no study links
  FUBP1 to COPD exacerbation frequency, spirometry, or airway remodeling directly, as the
  researcher itself concedes at the end of the writeup. The topline claim and the hedge
  don't fully match; this is a mild instance of the 'strengthened-in-translation'
  overclaiming pattern the review process is meant to catch.
- FUBP1's other characterized biology (glioma tumor suppressor, neural progenitor
  differentiation) is unrelated to lung disease and adds no support either way.

Verdict: WEAKENED — the eQTL convergence at the exact credible-set variant is real and is
legitimately stronger locus-assignment evidence than nearest-gene/FLAMES heuristics for
DNAJB4, but 'strongest mechanistic candidate for COPD' overstates evidence that is (a)
borrowed from non-COPD GWAS contexts, (b) not colocalized against the COPD signal itself,
and (c) bridged to COPD via a plausible but unconfirmed antiviral-epithelial mechanism
rather than any direct COPD/spirometry association."

### NEXN and GIPC2 — HOLDS
"Both are thin (one paper each), non-pulmonary in every characterized function (cardiac
Z-disc structural protein; adrenal-specific tumor suppressor), with no COPD or lung
literature identified. The researcher's 'low priority' call matches the sparse evidence with
no apparent overreach.

Verdict: HOLDS (for both) — appropriately deprioritized, nothing to contest, though note per
the search-budget caveat above I could not independently confirm the absence of
contradicting literature."

## Process / data-quality flags

1. **ZZZ3 coordinate discrepancy (unresolved).** See above — ZZZ3 does not physically
   overlap the stated locus interval per two independent GRCh38 sources (Ensembl, Open
   Targets). Root cause not determined; flagged for the internal pipeline team to
   investigate (possible PoPS window definition wider than the printed credible-set
   interval).
2. **Red-team-skeptic search-budget exhaustion.** The red-team-skeptic subagent's web-search
   budget was exhausted before any of its planned independent contradiction-search queries
   (DNAJB4-COPD, FUBP1-COPD/spirometry, ZZZ3 coordinates, rs34517439-smoking behavior)
   could execute. Its critique is based entirely on the researcher's already-verified
   dossier, not independent confirmation. "No contradicting evidence found" statements in
   both the researcher's and skeptic's output should be read as "not found in searches
   performed," not as an exhaustively cleared literature search.
3. **Smoking-mediation prior not checked against primary GWAS summary statistics.** Neither
   the researcher nor the red-team-skeptic was able to directly query smoking-behavior GWAS
   (e.g. GSCAN) summary statistics at rs34517439; both relied on PubMed literature search,
   which returned no hits. This is flagged as the single highest-value follow-up check per
   the skeptic, given the locus's high fine-mapping PIP and its cross-trait pleiotropy with
   COVID-19/IPF/telomere length (traits with plausible smoking-related confounding).
   Treat "no smoking-GWAS overlap found" as an open item, not a cleared check.
2. **Open Targets coverage gap.** FUBP1, NEXN, and GIPC2 were not part of the original Open
   Targets structured lookup (step 1 of the pipeline), because they were only identified as
   candidates later, during the researcher's literature-driven genomic-region scan. Their
   L2G/colocalization/constraint metrics were not pulled from Open Targets in this run — a
   gap for a future iteration to fill, not a finding that these genes lack such data.
3. **Cross-trait pleiotropy interpretation.** rs34517439 is independently reported (across
   the citations above) as a shared/pleiotropic variant for multiple myeloma risk, COVID-19
   severity (two independent studies), idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis, and leukocyte telomere
   length. Per the skeptic, this pattern is at least as consistent with a broad host-defense/
   interferon-response or cellular-senescence locus as with a COPD-specific
   obstructive-airway-disease locus. This is presented as an alternative hypothesis for the
   study team's consideration, not a resolved conclusion — no cited study connects this
   locus to COPD case-control status, spirometry, or exacerbation phenotypes directly.
