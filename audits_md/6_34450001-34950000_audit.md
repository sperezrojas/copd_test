## Genes marked CONTESTED by the red-team-skeptic (excluded from the evidence table)

### PACSIN1

Skeptic verdict verbatim: "**CONTESTED** — the gene's own defining characterization
argues against lung relevance, and the sole 'immune' citation is not actually about
PACSIN1 biology. Recommend exclusion from the candidate list rather than carrying it
forward as a weak positive."

Full reasoning: "The foundational citation (Wasiak 2001) itself states PACSIN1 is
**neuron-specific**, explicitly contrasting it with the broadly-expressed paralog PACSIN2
(which is the one showing brain-vs-lung differential expression) — this is intrinsic
evidence *against* lung/COPD relevance, not merely absence of support. The second
citation (Ngo 2025) uses the Pacsin1 promoter purely as a genetic driver/marker to target
pDCs; it demonstrates nothing about PACSIN1 function and is not evidence for this gene at
all — it is citation padding that happens to contain the gene's name."

Citations discussed (both individually VERIFIED by citation-verifier, but the skeptic
judges the gene-level case CONTESTED because the citations argue against, or are
irrelevant to, PACSIN1's candidacy):
- Wasiak S et al. (2001), J Biol Chem, PMID 11352907, DOI 10.1074/jbc.M100591200 — states
  PACSIN1 is neuron-specific, unlike its paralog PACSIN2 which shows brain-vs-lung
  tissue-specific binding differences.
- Ngo C et al. (2025), Nature Immunology, PMID 41023479, DOI 10.1038/s41590-025-02288-3 —
  uses the Pacsin1 promoter only as a plasmacytoid-dendritic-cell genetic marker/driver in
  a mouse pDC-depletion study; the paper's finding concerns pDC biology, not PACSIN1
  function.

PACSIN1 has no COPD/lung-function genetic evidence (researcher found none) and no
prioritization-method support (nearest/MAGMA/PoPS/ABC all silent, no credible-set
variant).

### TAF11

Skeptic verdict verbatim: "**CONTESTED** — the one available citation, read accurately,
is evidence *against* TAF11 being the mediator gene in its own source study, and the
disease context (RA-ILD) doesn't transfer to COPD. Should not be carried forward as
supporting evidence; if kept, must be reframed as a null/negative result."

Full reasoning: "General TFIID subunit; the single citation (Liu 2024) is a
Mendelian-randomization screen in **rheumatoid arthritis → interstitial lung disease**
(not COPD) in which TAF11 was one of nine *unvalidated* candidate mediator genes, and the
study's own validated/prioritized gene was **SLAMF1**, not TAF11. Citing this as support
for TAF11 misrepresents what the source paper concluded — the paper itself deprioritized
TAF11 relative to another gene, in a different disease."

Citation discussed (VERIFIED by citation-verifier as an accurate representation of the
paper's content, but the skeptic judges the gene-level inferential use of it CONTESTED):
- Liu M et al. (2024), International Immunopharmacology, PMID 39260308, DOI
  10.1016/j.intimp.2024.113082 — identifies TAF11 as one of nine candidate blood-eQTL
  mediator genes in an RA→interstitial-lung-disease MR screen; the paper's own validated
  mediator was SLAMF1, not TAF11.

TAF11 has no COPD/lung-function genetic evidence, no prioritization-method support, and
no credible-set variant annotated to it.

reviewer applying a stricter threshold may reasonably choose to treat SPDEF's rows as
excluded entirely; the orchestrator's judgment call here is preserved for transparency.

## Process caveats affecting this locus's evidence base

1. **Red-team-skeptic's WebSearch budget was exhausted before the critique task began**
   (reported by the agent as "200 of 200" already used). It substituted two targeted
   WebFetch/PubMed queries (for ILRUN and SPDEF only, both returning zero contradicting or
   additional hits) and performed **no independent search pass at all** for SNRPC,
   BLTP3A, PACSIN1, ANKS1A, or TAF11 — its critique of those five genes rests entirely on
   re-scrutinizing the researcher's own citations and the Open Targets structured-data
   context, not on independent verification of completeness. Any literature the
   researcher might have missed for those five genes would not have been caught by this
   critique pass.
2. **PoPS-prioritized "C6orf106" is not a fourth candidate gene** — it resolves to the
   same Ensembl gene ID (ENSG00000196821) as ILRUN and is treated throughout as PoPS
   support for ILRUN, not a separate gene.
3. **Four candidate genes (SPDEF, PACSIN1, ANKS1A, TAF11) were identified by the
   researcher independently of the input pipeline's four prioritization methods**, by
   enumerating all protein-coding genes physically overlapping the 6:34,450,001-34,950,000
   region via Ensembl REST API. None of them has a credible-set variant annotated to it in
   the locus's own fine-mapping output, and none was queried against Open Targets (which
   was run only against the three pipeline-flagged genes before the researcher dispatch).
   Their absence from Open Targets querying reflects a scoping decision made before the
   researcher's region-enumeration finding was known, not a null database result.
4. **Colocalization detail** (per-QTL-study H4/PP4 values) beyond the aggregate
   Open-Targets L2G-style credible-set score was not resolvable through the GraphQL schema
   fields explored for this locus — noted as a data-completeness gap in the Open Targets
   summary, not a negative finding.
