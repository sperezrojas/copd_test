## Genes/claims marked CONTESTED or WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning)

### TNC — CONTESTED

> Invoking CONTESTED rather than WEAKENED because there is a specific, verified,
> independently-confirmed (Ensembl REST + Open Targets, per the dossier) genomic fact — TNC
> does not lie within this GRCh38-defined locus — that directly contradicts the premise that
> TNC is a candidate gene "at this locus" at all. Combined with TNC's near-zero Open Targets
> genetic_association score (0.06, literature-only), its absence from L2G at this locus, its
> absence from any locus-specific GWAS/eQTL/coloc evidence, and its own tissue-expression
> literature being internally split (Löfdahl positive vs. Muñoz-Esquerre negative), the case
> for TNC at this locus should be treated as most likely a coordinate/build artifact in the
> MAGMA/PoPS pipeline rather than genuine biological linkage. Recommend the synthesis-lead flag
> this upstream to whoever runs the statistical-prioritization step, since a systematic
> GRCh37/38 mismatch of this kind could affect other loci in the same pipeline run, not just
> this one. This should be marked for re-verification of the underlying MAGMA/PoPS input
> coordinates — that re-check is a statistical/pipeline QA issue, not a new literature
> citation, so it doesn't require citation-verifier re-routing, but it does warrant a
> pipeline-level bug report.

Underlying finding this verdict is based on: under GRCh38 (the build stated for this locus),
TNC's actual gene coordinates are chr9:115,019,575-115,118,257 (confirmed independently by both
Ensembl REST, queried by the researcher, and Open Targets `genomicLocation`, queried directly
by the orchestrator) — approximately 2.6-2.7 Mb OUTSIDE this locus's stated interval
(117,600,001-117,950,000), i.e. TNC does not physically overlap this locus at all under the
stated build. TNC's GRCh37/hg19 coordinates (chr9:117,782,806-117,880,536) DO numerically fall
inside the locus's stated GRCh38 range, which is consistent with a genome-build coordinate
mismatch having occurred somewhere in the upstream MAGMA/PoPS gene-prioritization step.

### TLR4 — WEAKENED (not CONTESTED, but flagged as overclaimed if treated as mechanistically confirmed)

> TLR4 is the nearest gene, the only protein-coding gene physically in the GRCh38 interval, and
> has a large (if literature-driven) prior COPD literature base — none of which is nothing.
> But the specific inferential chain "credible-set variant -> TLR4 expression/function -> COPD"
> has no direct support: no eQTL, no coloc with expression or lung-function traits, and a
> purpose-built systematic eQTL-integration study that looked for exactly this and did not find
> it (Lamontagne 2018). The supporting mechanistic literature is real but is about different
> variants, is directionally inconsistent across both mouse models and human populations, and
> its human-disease association score in Open Targets is explicitly noted to be
> literature-inflated rather than genetics-driven. The colocalization pattern (behavioral/
> frailty traits, not lung function, not smoking) further complicates a clean "pulmonary
> mechanism" story. I would not call this CONTESTED (there is no credible paper naming a
> different gene at this locus, and TLR4 is genuinely the only positional candidate), but the
> causal-gene claim as often stated ("TLR4 is the COPD gene at this locus") overreaches what the
> locus-specific evidence supports; it should be presented as "positional/statistical candidate
> with a large but non-locus-specific literature base and no direct functional/eQTL tie to the
> credible-set variants."

Specific internally-contradictory citation pairs flagged by the skeptic (both individually
VERIFIED, but jointly illustrating that the underlying biology/genetics is contested, not
settled):
- Zhang et al. 2006 (PMID 17053835) reports TLR4 loss-of-function causes spontaneous emphysema
  in unchallenged aging mice, while Haw et al. 2017 (PMID 29025711) reports TLR4
  loss-of-function protects against cigarette-smoke-induced emphysema — opposite direction of
  effect depending on the exposure model; Haw et al.'s own abstract states TLR2/TLR4's role in
  COPD pathogenesis is "controversial and conflicting."
- Rohde et al. 2005 (PMID 16219455) reports the TLR4 Asp299Gly minor allele is protective
  against COPD, while Gutiérrez-Romero et al. 2024 (PMID 39139567) reports the same SNP's minor
  allele (AA genotype) confers markedly increased COPD risk (OR=3.53) in a different population
  — a direct cross-population reversal of effect direction for the identical variant.
- Ito et al. 2012 (PMID 22251849) and Berenson et al. 2015 (PMID 26361369) both report null/
  negative findings for TLR4 variants once adjusted for age/smoking or compared across groups,
  further undercutting the robustness of the candidate-gene literature as a whole.

## Cross-cutting caveats carried into this audit (not verdicts on a single gene, but process/data gaps)

1. **Red-team-skeptic's WebSearch budget was exhausted before it could run any independent
   confirmation searches for this locus.** Its critique is a re-analysis of the
   researcher/citation-verifier dossier only, not a fresh independent literature check. This is
   a genuine limitation of this locus's review, not a confirmed absence of further
   contradicting evidence.
2. **The orchestrator's own WebSearch budget was also exhausted** partway through this locus's
   review, preventing independent confirmation of rs1927907's exact genomic coordinates
   relative to the credible-set cluster (see citation 6 above).
3. **Unresolved gap:** whether this exact locus (chr9:117.6-117.95Mb / rs1887781 / TLR4) is
   among the loci reported in Shrine et al. 2019 (279 lung-function signals) or
   Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019 (82 COPD loci) could not be determined from PubMed/bioRxiv
   abstract search alone; this would require direct supplementary-table lookup and was not
   completed for this review.
4. **Alternative-hypothesis flag (per pipeline prior #1/#2):** Open Targets colocalization at
   this locus is strong for subjective well-being, hip osteoarthritis, frailty (general
   factor), shortness of breath, and mood swings (5 UKB releases) — but absent for both
   spirometric lung-function traits (FEV1, FEV1/FVC) and smoking-behavior traits (initiation,
   cigarettes/day, pack-years, cessation). Neither of the pipeline's two standard alternative
   explanations (classic pulmonary biology vs. smoking-behavior mediation) is cleanly supported
   by the locus-specific colocalization data; a third possibility — a general
   frailty/subjective-symptom/behavioral-health axis — is raised by the red-team-skeptic and
   should be weighed by the study team rather than defaulted away.
