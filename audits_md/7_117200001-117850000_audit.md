# Audit trail — locus 7:117,200,001-117,850,000 (WNT2 / CTTNBP2)

This file preserves the excluded material and full skeptic reasoning that did not make it
into `evidence_tables/7_117200001-117850000.md`, for reviewer transparency. Nothing here
should be treated as verified evidence for gene prioritization on its own.

## Excluded / RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH citations

**None.** All 31 citations returned by the researcher were verified as VERIFIED by the
citation-verifier (2026-09-11). No citation was RETRACTED, NOT_FOUND, or a CLAIM_MISMATCH.
Three citations carry an explicit caveat (see below) rather than an outright exclusion.

### Citations carrying a caveat (included in the main table, flagged here for transparency)

- **Shrine et al. 2019, PMID 30804560, DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0321-7** — VERIFIED*. The
  paper exists with correct metadata and does report 279 lung-function signals (139 novel)
  predicting COPD. However, neither the researcher nor the citation-verifier could confirm,
  from abstract/metadata alone, whether WNT2 or the 7q31.3 region is actually named among
  those 279 signals — this would require checking supplementary tables directly. The paper
  also has two errata (June 2019, May 2024), which do not appear to affect the main findings
  cited here.
- **Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019, PMID 30804561, DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0342-2** — VERIFIED*.
  Same caveat: existence and general findings (82 COPD loci, 35,735 cases/222,076 controls)
  confirmed, but WNT2/7q31.3 naming among the 82 loci was not confirmed from abstract alone.
- **Wain et al. 2017, PMID 28166213, DOI 10.1038/ng.3787** — VERIFIED*. Same caveat: 97
  lung-function signals confirmed, WNT2/7q31.3 naming among them not confirmed from abstract.

Separately, **Wain et al. 2015 (PMID 26423011)** was fully VERIFIED with no naming caveat,
but its own text explicitly names a *different* set of loci (KANSL1, TSEN54, TET2,
RBM19/TBX5, NPNT, HLA-DQB1/HLA-DQA2) — WNT2 is not among the loci this particular paper
reports. It is retained in the main table only as general background on the shared genetic
architecture of lung function in smokers vs. never-smokers, not as locus-specific support.

**Orchestrator/synthesis-lead note:** taken together, this means none of the four primary
GWAS meta-analyses cited by the researcher have been confirmed — by either the researcher or
the citation-verifier — to actually name WNT2 or this 7q31.3 region as a reported signal.
The only locus-specific statistical evidence for WNT2 at this exact position is the Open
Targets L2G score (a structured database result, not a peer-reviewed literature finding).
This gap should be closed by checking the three papers' supplementary locus tables directly
before treating WNT2's GWAS attribution as resolved.

## Genes marked CONTESTED by the red-team-skeptic

### CTTNBP2 — CONTESTED (as a gene acting through direct lung/pulmonary biology)

Full skeptic reasoning (verbatim, from `evidence_tables/_status/7_117200001-117850000/critique.md`):

> **(3) Is CONTESTED fair, or is the smoking-trait profile under-weighted as evidence?** The
> researcher's own conclusion — no mechanistic rationale for a direct lung role, zero L2G
> support, zero COPD/lung disease association in Open Targets, entirely
> neurodevelopmental/ASD literature, one weak incidental asthma (not COPD) expression
> finding with no proposed mechanism — is sound and appropriately skeptical as a claim about
> CTTNBP2 acting through its own known (dendritic-spine/ASD) molecular biology. I'd affirm
> that part.
>
> However, there is a real point being under-weighted, and it cuts a specific direction:
> CTTNBP2's dominant aggregate disease associations (smoking initiation, smoking cessation,
> nicotine dependence, substance abuse, cannabis dependence, plus psychiatric traits) are
> exactly the profile the pipeline's smoking-mediation prior asks reviewers to take
> seriously. This is *not* evidence that CTTNBP2's neuronal protein function does anything
> in the lung — it doesn't, and the researcher is right to reject that. It *is*
> circumstantial evidence that **this general genomic region may harbor a regulatory
> element affecting smoking/addiction behavior**, and that the COPD GWAS signal at this
> locus (uncorrected for smoking) could be substantially a smoking-behavior proxy rather
> than a direct-pulmonary-biology signal — independent of which gene (WNT2, CTTNBP2, or
> neither) is the "true" effector. This matters for how confidently WNT2 gets prioritized
> too: if the locus's COPD association is smoking-mediated, the WNT2 developmental-biology
> case, however strong on its own terms, may be biologically true but causally irrelevant to
> why this SNP shows up in the COPD GWAS. Synthesis-lead should treat the smoking-trait
> profile as evidence *about the locus's likely mediation pathway*, not as evidence *for
> CTTNBP2 as a lung-disease gene* — those are different claims and should not be conflated
> in either direction.
>
> **CTTNBP2 verdict: CONTESTED** (as a gene acting through direct lung/pulmonary biology —
> that specific claim is well contradicted by the totality of evidence: no L2G, no
> COPD/lung disease association, no lung-relevant molecular function, purely
> neurodevelopmental biology). Flag separately for synthesis-lead: CTTNBP2's
> smoking/psychiatric association profile raises — but does not prove — an alternative
> smoking-mediation hypothesis for the *locus as a whole*, which should be weighed against
> WNT2's candidacy too, not treated as rehabilitating CTTNBP2 itself.

### WNT2 — WEAKENED (not CONTESTED, but flagged for overreach)

WNT2 was not marked CONTESTED, but the skeptic's WEAKENED verdict and full reasoning are
preserved here since they materially qualify every WNT2 row in the main evidence table:

> **WNT2 verdict: WEAKENED.** The developmental-biology case for WNT2 as essential to lung
> organogenesis and adult alveolar-niche maintenance is genuinely strong and multi-lab/
> multi-model convergent — that part holds. But the inference that *this specific COPD GWAS
> locus* acts through WNT2 overreaches what's shown: no verified primary GWAS text names
> WNT2 here, the L2G score is modest and unsupported by molecular QTL data, the lead
> variant is unassigned to any gene, the "three-way colocalization" is probably one
> non-independent signal, and the directional inconsistency between COPD ("deficient" Wnt,
> per one secondary review) and IPF ("upregulated" WNT2) undercuts the coherence of the
> proposed mechanism. Treat as a biologically plausible, unconfirmed candidate — not a
> resolved one.

Full point-by-point critique (verbatim) is preserved in
`evidence_tables/_status/7_117200001-117850000/critique.md`, covering: (1) overclaiming risk
from the "halo effect" of 14 strong developmental-biology citations obscuring the unconfirmed
GWAS-to-gene link; (2) the IPF-up vs. COPD-down directional tension; (4) the likely
non-independence of the "three-way colocalization" (same signal, overlapping cohorts) and the
unquantified LD/distance case against CFTR and ST7 as alternative genes at this locus; (5)
confirmation that no instance of unsupported causal language ("associated with" upgraded to
"causes") was found in the WNT2 material.

## Genes physically in the region but not formally scored (not prioritized candidates)

Per pipeline scope rules, only genes listed in the locus input file (nearest/FLAMES/MAGMA/
PoPS/ABC) are formal candidates. The researcher additionally enumerated all protein-coding
genes physically within 7:117,200,001-117,850,000 via Ensembl REST lookup and gave each a
one-line dismissal where no plausible COPD connection existed. These are preserved here for
transparency, not scored in the main evidence table:

- **ASZ1** — germ-cell/meiosis-restricted piRNA-pathway scaffold protein (Yan et al. 2026,
  PMID 42118831, DOI 10.1073/pnas.2533968123, VERIFIED). No documented lung, airway,
  spirometric, or immune biology. No plausible COPD connection.
- **ST7** — 7q31 tumor suppressor gene genomically clustered with WNT2 (Katoh 2002, PMID
  12012006, no DOI available, VERIFIED). The only citation found describes allelic
  loss/rearrangement of the *paralogous* 1p13 (ST7L/WNT2B) region in cancers including
  non-small-cell lung cancer — this does not describe the 7q31 locus itself. No COPD/
  spirometric/immune literature found for ST7. The red-team-skeptic noted ST7's (and
  CFTR's) physical proximity to the lead variant was not rigorously quantified against the
  actual credible-set LD span, so exclusion of these genes as candidates should be treated
  as "not ruled in, but not rigorously ruled out either" rather than definitive.
- **CFTR** (chr7:117,480,025-117,668,665) — physically within the queried region but not a
  nearest/FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS/ABC candidate per the locus input file; well-established in
  airway ion-transport biology and cystic fibrosis, with documented CFTR-modifier
  associations with lung function/obstructive disease in the broader literature. Not given
  its own literature search in this pass. Flagged by the red-team-skeptic as a plausible
  alternative gene at this locus that was not rigorously excluded (see WNT2 critique point 4
  above) — recommend a dedicated CFTR-focused search if this locus is revisited.
