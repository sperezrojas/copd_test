# Audit trail — Locus 13:109050001-109450000 (MYO16)

This file documents excluded/downgraded citations and contested gene
verdicts for this locus, preserved verbatim from the citation-verifier and
red-team-skeptic outputs, for reviewer traceability. It is separate from
`evidence_tables/13_109050001-109450000.md` so the evidence table can be
handed out without the audit trail.

## Excluded / downgraded citations

### PMID 30804560 (Shrine et al. 2019, Nat Genet) — CLAIM_MISMATCH for gene-specific attribution

Citation-verifier verdict (verbatim): "VERIFIED (for lung-function GWAS
claims) / CLAIM_MISMATCH (for MYO16 attribution). Evidence: Full-text
retrieval confirms 279 signals, 107 putative causal genes identified via
fine-mapping/eQTL-pQTL annotation, and enrichment in 'elastic fiber and
extracellular matrix organization pathways, and a number of gene ontologies
including gene sets relating to the cytoskeleton and processes involved in
ciliogenesis.' Additionally, 'enrichment of a TGF-beta superfamily signalling
pathway.' However, MYO16 is NOT explicitly named in the accessible main body
text or abstract. The researcher correctly noted it was not found in the
body text. Supplementary tables are referenced but not retrievable via
standard full-text access."

Red-team-skeptic's assessment of the downstream implication (verbatim,
point 4 of its critique): "If the verifier flagged that MYO16 is not
confirmed as named in the body text of PMID 30804560, then citing Shrine et
al. 2019 as evidence for 'a well-powered lung-function GWAS implicates
MYO16' is citation laundering: the paper's genuine authority (large N,
rigorous lung-function GWAS) is being borrowed to support a gene-specific
claim the paper may never actually make... The write-up should state plainly
that Shrine et al. establishes locus-level (not gene-level) significance for
lung function at 13q33-34, and that the gene-level assignment to MYO16 comes
from the fine-mapping/L2G tools, not from that paper's own text. This should
be corrected/caveated in the synthesis, and it modestly weakens confidence in
the 'strong lung-function evidence points specifically to MYO16' claim."

**Disposition:** Retained in the evidence table but restricted to its
VERIFIED locus-level claims only (see evidence table row); not used anywhere
as gene-specific evidence for MYO16.

### PMID 30804561 (Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019, Nat Genet) — gene-specific membership unconfirmed

Citation-verifier verdict (verbatim): "VERIFIED (for 82 COPD loci claim).
Evidence: Abstract states 'We identified 82 loci associated with P < 5 x
10-8' confirming the 82 COPD risk loci claim. Whether MYO16/13q33 is
explicitly among these 82 cannot be determined from the abstract alone."

**Disposition:** Retained in the evidence table as general background only

## Genes marked CONTESTED / WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic

### MYO16 — CONTESTED

Red-team-skeptic verdict (verbatim): "The statistical/positional evidence
(nearest gene, FLAMES, MAGMA, PoPS, L2G=0.381) is real but is (a) only
moderate by Open Targets' own metric, (b) specific to lung-function
(FEV1/FVC) rather than COPD case-control, (c) partly resting on a
mischaracterized citation (Shrine et al. 2019 CLAIM_MISMATCH) that doesn't
itself name the gene, and (d) unsupported by any lung-structural-biology
mechanism despite an active neuronal-biology literature that has had years
to make that connection and hasn't. The two COPD-specific citations are
weak, non-validated, possibly pseudo-replicated bioinformatic screen hits.
Critically, there is a credible, independently-colocalizing alternative gene
at the exact same credible set (MYO16-AS1) with its own lung-tissue
functional literature that MYO16 lacks — this is direct evidence
implicating a different gene at the same locus, which meets the bar for
contradicting evidence rather than mere caveat. Verdict: CONTESTED — not
because MYO16 is disproven, but because the balance of evidence does not
clearly distinguish MYO16 from MYO16-AS1 as the causal gene, and MYO16's own
supporting mechanistic literature is absent while a plausible
smoking-mediation alternative (via MYO16's neuronal/presynaptic biology) has
not been ruled out."

Additional supporting critique points from the same review (verbatim
excerpts):

- On the two bioinformatic screens (PMID 41137279, PMID 38933922): "They
  should be treated as close to zero evidentiary weight for a specific
  MYO16-COPD mechanism... a gene landing in such a list is only marginally
  more informative than its appearing in a random sample of expressed genes,
  absent (a) independent replication across cohorts, (b) direction-of-effect
  concordance, or (c) any individual functional follow-up... two
  independent-sounding citations both hitting 'MYO16 + COPD + immune'
  creates a false sense of convergent replication when in fact both are the
  same low-specificity study design (screen-derived gene lists), which is a
  form of pseudo-replication."
- On the FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS/L2G convergence: "convergence of 'nearest gene,
  FLAMES, MAGMA, PoPS, L2G' on MYO16 is not five independent lines of
  evidence — it is largely one shared structural prior (proximity +
  protein-coding status) expressed through five correlated tools, several of
  which were never given a fair chance to nominate MYO16-AS1 in the first
  place. That the L2G algorithm assigns '~0 weight' to MYO16-AS1 despite a
  real, colocalizing cis-eQTL for it at this same credible set is a textbook
  illustration of this bias, not independent confirmation that MYO16 is
  correct."
- On the lung-function-signal/no-mechanism disconnect: "'under-studied gene'
  is unfalsifiable and is precisely the explanation you'd also give for a
  false-positive statistical signal — it cannot distinguish the two
  hypotheses, so invoking it as if it resolves the tension is circular... A
  gene with an active, multi-paper research literature that has never once
  turned up in a lung-structural-biology, airway-epithelium, or
  alveolar-remodeling context, despite the gene having been
  genome-wide-significant in lung-function GWAS since at least 2019... is a
  gene the field has had ample opportunity to investigate for lung relevance
  and apparently hasn't found any."
- On smoking-mediation (prior #1): "This raises a plausible,
  biologically-motivated alternative hypothesis that doesn't appear to have
  been engaged: that this locus could act on COPD risk through
  smoking-related neurobehavioral pathways (via MYO16's neuronal function)
  rather than through direct pulmonary structural biology... the researcher
  should be asked to check GSCAN/smoking-initiation and nicotine-dependence
  GWAS summary statistics for rs9521268 and the credible-set region before
  the synthesis-lead finalizes this locus." (Not re-run in this pipeline
  pass; recorded here as an open follow-up item for the study team, not
  resolved.)
- On the unexplained trans-pQTL colocalization with AGER and CEACAM5: "This
  should not be silently dropped from the synthesis... Given no mechanistic
  explanation exists, this should be reported as an open, uninterpreted
  anomaly, not woven into either the MYO16 or MYO16-AS1 causal narrative."

### MYO16-AS1 — WEAKENED

Red-team-skeptic verdict (verbatim): "Weaker statistical/fine-mapping
support (L2G ~0, disfavored by the tools' structural bias against lncRNAs)
but has the only genuinely lung-tissue functional literature at this locus
(lung adenocarcinoma mechanism, IPF lncRNA co-expression), plus a real
colocalizing cis-eQTL in blood/neutrophils — the latter is at least
directionally consistent with prior #3's neutrophilic/inflammatory-program
relevance to COPD, more so than anything offered for MYO16. However, this
evidence is itself indirect (oncology and IPF, not COPD directly) and thin.
Verdict: WEAKENED — real, relevant evidence exists, but it is oncology/IPF-
context, not COPD-specific, and should not be oversold as a clean
alternative causal-gene story either; it should be presented as a genuinely
open, unresolved competing hypothesis alongside MYO16, not a subordinate
footnote to it."

## Other flagged items (not citation exclusions, but reviewer-relevant)

- **Tool-access caveat on the red-team-skeptic review itself:** the
  red-team-skeptic agent for this locus had only WebSearch/WebFetch tools
  (no file-read capability) and reported its web-search budget was
  exhausted before it could run independent confirmatory searches of its
  own; its critique was built from the detailed factual summary provided in
  its dispatch prompt (itself drawn directly from researcher.md and
  verified_citations.md by the orchestrator) rather than by directly opening
  the underlying files. The critique's reasoning and per-gene verdicts are
  nonetheless substantive and are carried forward into the evidence table
  and prioritized-gene section above; a reviewer who wants an independent
  second pass with direct file access and a fresh search budget may want to
  re-run this stage.
- **LINC00370:** not marked CONTESTED (red-team-skeptic did not flag it),
  but the researcher and citation-verifier agree it has no lung/COPD-relevant
  literature and no Open Targets genetic-association evidence at all. It is
  retained in the evidence table only for completeness/transparency, as a
  low-priority candidate not seriously considered for the "Prioritized gene"
  call.
- **Negative findings (not citations, no PMID, so not eligible for the
  evidence table):** a direct PubMed search for the lead variant rs9521268
  returned zero indexed results; no PubMed-indexed literature was found
  linking MYO16 to smoking initiation, cessation, cigarettes-per-day, or
  nicotine dependence. Per red-team-skeptic, these absence-of-evidence
  findings should not be oversold as ruling out smoking-mediation (prior #1)
  — a direct GSCAN/smoking-GWAS summary-statistics check at this locus was
  recommended but not performed in this pipeline pass.
