# Audit trail — locus 4:29,200,001-29,350,000

This appendix documents excluded/downgraded citations and contested genes for this locus,
preserved from the citation-verifier and red-team-skeptic outputs. See
`evidence_tables/4_29200001-29350000.md` for the assembled evidence table (verified
citations only) and Open Targets/prioritization synthesis.

## Excluded citations

**None.** All 6 citations returned by the researcher for this locus were verified as
VERIFIED by the citation-verifier (no RETRACTED, NOT_FOUND, or CLAIM_MISMATCH verdicts).
Two citations (Sakornsakolpat 2019 re: PCDH7/DLCO, and Al-Soufi & Costas 2023 re:
PCDH7/schizophrenia-smoking) were flagged by the researcher as tentative/unconfirmed
pending full-text check of whether PCDH7 was genuinely named — the citation-verifier
confirmed both were genuine, named associations upon full-text/results-table check (in
fact somewhat stronger than the researcher's cautious framing: Sakornsakolpat 2019
explicitly lists PCDH7/rs7664851 as a suggestive DLCO locus and reports a nominal COPD
case-control signal near PCDH7; Al-Soufi & Costas 2023 explicitly names PCDH7 as a
colocalization locus between schizophrenia and smoking-related trait genetic signals).

## Genes marked CONTESTED / WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic

### LINC02472 — verdict: HOLDS as an honest report, but WEAKENED if presented as a confident gene-level call

Verbatim skeptic reasoning:

> The researcher's own characterization — "cannot currently be prioritized on any
> independent mechanistic grounds; genuine absence-of-evidence" — is accurate and not an
> overclaim; as written it **HOLDS** as an honest report. But if synthesis-lead's final
> output assigns LINC02472 as "the prioritized gene at this locus" (rather than "no gene
> can currently be prioritized; LINC02472 is the only transcript overlapping a
> high-confidence but functionally uncharacterized signal"), that step **WEAKENED** —
> assigning a specific gene name by default, when every independent line of evidence is
> null, manufactures a false impression of resolution. I'd recommend this locus be reported
> explicitly as unresolved/uncharacterizable rather than "LINC02472."

Additional skeptic points preserved:
- "Containment in a lncRNA intron is weak causal evidence, weaker than the equivalent claim
  for a protein-coding gene... an intron_variant/non_coding_transcript_variant MODIFIER
  annotation carries essentially no functional prior beyond 'this DNA is not exonic.'"
- "The PIP=0.999 is nonetheless a real, strong statistical signal... This is a case where
  statistical resolution has outpaced biological annotation, not a case where the biology
  has been checked and found wanting."
- "The GTEx testis-only/lung-null result should be weighted as weak-to-moderate, not
  dispositive, against a lung role... A 0.0 TPM bulk-tissue null for a lncRNA is much less
  informative than the same null would be for a protein-coding gene."
- "Alternative mechanism not addressed: because this is a gene desert, the credible-set
  variant could equally be a distal regulatory element for a gene well outside the 150kb
  window (gene-desert enhancers acting over long range on distant genes are a recognized
  phenomenon, e.g., the FTO/IRX3 precedent)... 'the causal gene is LINC02472' and 'the
  causal gene is some distant unannotated target' are currently indistinguishable."

**How this is reflected in the evidence table:** the Prioritized-gene synthesis section
names LINC02472 but at confidence INSUFFICIENT, explicitly per the skeptic's
recommendation, rather than presenting it as a resolved call.

### PCDH7 (context gene, ~1.43 Mb from credible-set variant) — verdict: WEAKENED

Verbatim skeptic reasoning:

> **Verdict on PCDH7:** WEAKENED as a candidate for this specific locus — the underlying
> citations are sound and the researcher did not overclaim (explicitly called it
> "background context only," not a competing prioritized candidate), but I want to be more
> explicit than the researcher was: without an LD/coloc check, there is no basis to assume
> rs7664851 and rs76849161 represent the same causal signal, and the smoking/behavioral
> pleiotropy at PCDH7 should not be transferred to this locus's smoking-mediation
> assessment.

Additional skeptic points preserved:
- "The DLCO signal (Sakornsakolpat 2019, rs7664851, P=3.03E-06) is real but suggestive, not
  genome-wide significant, and — critically — no LD or colocalization analysis was
  performed between rs7664851 and rs76849161. At 1.43 Mb, these are very unlikely to be the
  same haplotype/signal under typical LD decay, but this pipeline did not check."
- "Using PCDH7's smoking-genetics profile to argue this broader locus is smoking-mediated
  would conflate two statistically distinct signals under one cytoband/'neighborhood'
  umbrella."

## Methodological caveat on this locus's critique stage

The red-team-skeptic's WebSearch tool budget (200/200) was already exhausted before it
could run any independent searches for this locus — it disclosed **zero independent
searches completed**. Its critique is therefore based entirely on internal-consistency
review of the researcher's and citation-verifier's reported findings, not on independent
adversarial literature search. This is a materially different (weaker) evidentiary basis
than critiques for loci where the skeptic had search budget available, and should be
weighed accordingly by the study team — this locus's critique stage should be considered
for re-running with a fresh search budget if higher confidence is needed.
