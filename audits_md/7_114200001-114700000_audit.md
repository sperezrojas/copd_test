# Audit trail: locus 7:114,200,001-114,700,000 (FOXP2 / MDFIC)

This file records everything excluded from the main evidence table
(`evidence_tables/7_114200001-114700000.md`), and why, so the exclusion is traceable
without needing to hand out this file alongside the evidence table itself.

## Excluded citations (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH)

### 1. PMID 32457453 — Sui Y, et al., "..." Scientific Reports, 2020. DOI 10.1038/s41598-020-65536-6
- **Researcher's original claim:** MDFIC regulates colorectal/lung/breast/prostate cancer
  cell growth via Wnt/β-catenin and JMJD1A.
- **Citation-verifier verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH.**
- **Reason:** Full text (via PMC) confirms the paper studies MDFIC in colorectal, breast,
  ovarian, prostate, brain, gastric, and pancreatic cancers. **Lung cancer is never
  mentioned or studied in this paper.** The researcher's inclusion of "lung" in the list
  of cancer types this paper covers is a factual overclaim. The correctly-sourced
  lung-cancer-specific claim (MDFIC and NSCLC prognosis) is separately supported by a
  different, verified citation (PMID 31911618, Chen et al., Oncogene 2020), which is
  retained in the main evidence table.

### 2. PMID 42539098 — cited generically as "FOXP2 haploinsufficiency causes speech-and-language disorder (KE family)"
- **Researcher's original claim:** FOXP2 haploinsufficiency causes speech-and-language
  disorder (developmental verbal dyspraxia), as first described via the KE family,
  supporting general background on FOXP2's dosage-sensitive biology.
- **Citation-verifier verdict: NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH.**
- **Reason:** This PMID resolves to a real but unrelated 2026 bioRxiv preprint (DOI
  10.64898/2026.07.18.739349) titled about "Molecular and cell type-specific determinants
  of inferior colliculus development and auditory function" — a paper about
  auditory-processing neurodevelopment, not FOXP2 haploinsufficiency or the KE-family
  speech-language-disorder phenotype. The citation-verifier also flagged the PMID's
  unusually high/recent numeric range as suspicious. The underlying background fact
  (FOXP2 haploinsufficiency → speech/language disorder, via the KE family) is
  well-established in the broader literature, but this specific citation does not support
  it and has been excluded. No substitute citation was independently verified for this
  claim in this review pass; it is not included in the main evidence table and should be
  treated as uncited background only if referenced elsewhere.

## Citations retained with an explicit non-replication caveat (VERIFIED, but do not corroborate FOXP2 as a COPD hit independently)

These three citations are VERIFIED as real, unretracted papers and are retained in the
main evidence table as non-replication/context evidence — but the citation-verifier
confirmed that **none of them independently name FOXP2 or the 7q31 locus as a hit** in
their own abstracts, contrary to what might be assumed from their inclusion as "COPD GWAS
literature":

- PMID 30804561 (Sakornsakolpat et al., Nat Genet 2019, 82-locus COPD GWAS meta-analysis)
- PMID 33057025 (Zhao et al., Nat Commun 2020, WGS-based COPD study)
- PMID 33106845 (Kim et al., Am J Epidemiol 2021, gene-by-smoking interaction COPD study)

The COPD association at this locus, as evaluated throughout this review, rests on this
pipeline's own input-file credible set/fine-mapping data, not on independent confirmation
from these three external landmark papers.

## Genes marked CONTESTED by the red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning)

### MDFIC — CONTESTED as the causal/prioritized gene at this locus

Verbatim from the red-team-skeptic's critique:

> **Verdict on MDFIC: CONTESTED.** I'm marking this CONTESTED rather than merely WEAKENED
> because there is a genuine, multi-source case against MDFIC as the causal gene at this
> locus: (1) it lies outside the physical credible-set interval with no regulatory link,
> (2) Open Targets' own L2G model — the same class of tool used to prioritize it —
> actually favors FOXP2 by an order of magnitude at this variant, (3) MDFIC has no
> genetic-association evidence for COPD in Open Targets' aggregate (score driven entirely
> by literature, and even that literature is not COPD-specific), and (4) every piece of
> literature evidence assembled is non-pulmonary or only cell-line/cross-context
> lung-adjacent (one A549 GR study). This isn't a single contradicting paper so much as a
> convergent absence of support plus a positional/statistical mismatch — which is exactly
> the pattern the LD/nearest-gene-bias prior warns about, except inverted (MDFIC is the
> distal, non-credible-set gene being favored by burden/PoPS-type statistical methods over
> the gene that actually contains every fine-mapped variant). Synthesis-lead should treat
> FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS prioritization of MDFIC as fragile and likely reflecting a
> false-positive gene-level statistical signal (e.g., driven by MDFIC's baseline
> expression/connectivity profile in PoPS features, or by LD/distance artifacts in MAGMA)
> rather than genuine causal biology, pending any ABC/regulatory evidence connecting the
> credible-set interval to MDFIC that has not yet been found.

### FOXP2 — direct pulmonary-biology mechanism WEAKENED (gene assignment itself HOLDS)

Verbatim from the red-team-skeptic's critique:

> **Verdict on FOXP2:** **WEAKENED.** The fine-mapping/gene-assignment inference (this
> locus = FOXP2, not a neighbor) is sound. But the case that FOXP2 drives COPD via
> *direct pulmonary biology* is weak and is actively contradicted by a well-powered
> lung-function GWAS null result (PMID 36221131) and by the overwhelmingly
> neuropsychiatric/behavioral pattern of L2G hits genome-wide. The strongest
> statistical-genetics evidence (antisocial behavior GWAS, rg with smoking) points toward
> smoking-behavior mediation (prior #1) rather than direct airway biology. The one
> lung-epithelial mechanistic finding is from an IPF model and should not be presented as
> established COPD mechanism without that caveat front and center. I would not call this
> CONTESTED at the level of "wrong gene at the locus" (fine-mapping is unambiguous), but I
> would flag the "FOXP2 → COPD via pulmonary biology" narrative specifically as
> contested/undercut by direct evidence.

## Disclosed coverage gaps (from the red-team-skeptic)

The red-team-skeptic's own independent contradiction-search calls for this locus returned
"search budget exhausted" (0 additional searches executed on its end). Its critique above
is based entirely on re-weighing the researcher's already-verified findings plus the Open
Targets structured lookup provided in its dispatch prompt — it did not independently
confirm or extend the contradiction search with new literature. This is disclosed here as
a coverage gap: neither FOXP2 nor MDFIC received an independent second-pass literature
search from the skeptic beyond critique of existing evidence.

The skeptic additionally flagged that the "no ABC link reported" statement for MDFIC
should be treated as **unresolved**, not settled-negative — it may reflect either a
genuine absence of regulatory linkage or simply that this pipeline's upstream ABC
prioritization step was not run/did not surface a hit for this locus, and a dedicated
chromatin-interaction follow-up was not performed in this review.
