## CONTESTED genes

### RXRA — CONTESTED (as the causal gene for the COPD signal at this locus)

Full red-team-skeptic reasoning, verbatim:

> **(c) Should RXRA be marked CONTESTED given the locus's overwhelming non-COPD pleiotropy?**
> Yes — **CONTESTED is the right final call for RXRA specifically as the causal gene for the COPD signal at this locus** (not for RXRA's biological role in alveolar development generally, which is well supported by independent, pre-existing literature such as McGowan et al. 2000). Reasoning, for the audit trail:
>
> - The credible set has no COPD or FEV1/FEV1-FVC entry anywhere in Open Targets, despite this being a heavily curated, cross-trait resource that does index 19 other independent GWAS signals at these exact SNPs.
> - In every one of those 19 signals where L2G made a call, RXRA (not PRRC2B, not POMT1, not VAV2) was the top gene — so if this genetic signal is doing anything, RXRA is very likely the gene being regulated. The contest is not "which gene" but "is the COPD association at this locus real/independent pulmonary biology, or is it a secondary/pleiotropic reflection of the hematologic-endocrine-anthropometric signal."
> - Two direct, targeted human studies argue against a lung/COPD-specific role for this exact genetic signal: Jolliffe et al. 2017 directly genotyped rs10881578 in COPD patients and found a **null** result for FEV1, FEV1/FVC, COPD severity, and sputum inflammatory cell counts after correction — this is a direct, targeted contradiction of a COPD-relevant phenotypic effect for a credible-set variant, in COPD patients specifically. That is about as close to a formal "argues against this gene" finding as this literature offers, and it should be weighted heavily.
> - The researcher could not find any FEV1/FEV1-FVC/COPD colocalization at this exact locus despite it being extensively tested for lung-function GWAS (Shrine 2019, 400k individuals) — and the largest COPD-specific colocalization pipelines that DO successfully find colocalized genes at other loci in the same studies (Sakornsakolpat 2018, Boueiz 2019) do not implicate RXRA.
> - Given the smoking-mediation prior: the researcher couldn't rule out smoking-behavior mediation (GSCAN not directly checked), so an alternative hypothesis — that the raw COPD case-control association here is confounded by unmeasured smoking behavior, sex-hormone status, or body composition (all of which have independent GWAS hits at this exact locus and are known confounders of COPD case-control status even after adjustment for smoking status as a covariate, since heaviness/duration are harder to fully adjust) — is at least as plausible as a direct lung mechanism.
>
> So: **RXRA — CONTESTED.** The developmental/structural mechanistic case (retinoic-acid signaling, alveolar septation, RARγ/RXRA compound-mutant mice) is genuinely strong and independent of this GWAS signal, but the inference that *this specific COPD GWAS locus* reflects that biology is undermined by (i) the total absence of COPD/FEV1 credible-set or colocalization evidence anywhere, (ii) a direct targeted null result in COPD patients (Jolliffe 2017) for the very trait class this signal is supposed to explain, and (iii) 19 non-pulmonary GWAS signals at the identical variants for which RXRA is confidently the causal gene, raising a live alternative hypothesis that the COPD signal is a pleiotropic echo of hematologic/endocrine/anthropometric biology rather than direct pulmonary biology.

Additional skeptic notes bearing on this CONTESTED verdict:

> **(b) The two RXRA-PLA2G2A COPD-mouse papers — CONTESTED/WEAKENED?**
> These should be weighted as, at best, weak/preliminary supporting mechanistic biology, not as evidence that changes the genetic-association picture... This has the hallmarks (single group, non-indexed/low-quality journals, animal-only, unreplicated mechanism, mismatched tissue in one of the two papers) of evidence that should be explicitly labeled **WEAKENED** at the gene-level narrative if it is being used to argue for a direct RXRA-lung mechanism, and treated as non-probative for the genetic-prioritization question specifically. It does not rise to "CONTESTED" in the sense of being actively rebutted by a contradicting paper, but it should not be allowed to shore up the case for RXRA at this locus.

> **1. VAV2 as an underweighted alternative gene.** VAV2 appeared as a minor secondary L2G hit in two Open Targets credible sets at this locus. The researcher's write-up doesn't mention VAV2 at all — this is a gap. VAV2 (a Rho/Rac GEF involved in immune-cell signaling, actin remodeling, and vascular/endothelial biology) is at least biologically plausible for an inflammatory or vascular COPD mechanism, and its absence from the researcher's search terms means it was never actually evaluated or ruled out — it was just not searched. This should be flagged to the synthesis lead as an unevaluated candidate, not folded into "RXRA is the answer by elimination."

> **2. eQTL/regulatory signal for a non-nearest, non-MAGMA/PoPS gene.** Because all 45 credible-set variants are intronic/upstream-gene MODIFIER-impact and annotated to RXRA by position, but the locus's actual downstream phenotypic fingerprint (hematocrit, hemoglobin, height, free androgen index, SHBG, testosterone, bilirubin, body fat%, lean mass) is a strikingly endocrine/hematologic/anthropometric signature, an alternative hypothesis is that this is a strong regulatory element (enhancer/silencer) whose target gene in blood/liver/adipose/gonadal tissue is RXRA (consistent with L2G) but whose "COPD" association arises via a **non-lung tissue mechanism entirely** — e.g., sex-hormone-axis or hematocrit/erythropoiesis effects influencing case-control COPD status through comorbidity, diagnostic misclassification (e.g., anemia/polycythemia effects on dyspnea or spirometry referral patterns), or body-composition confounding — rather than through any lung parenchymal or airway RXRA action. This is a distinct and probably more parsimonious hypothesis than "RXRA acts on alveolar septation via this exact regulatory variant," which has zero direct genetic support.

### PRRC2B — HOLDS (not contested; listed here only for completeness)

> Fairly characterized as essentially non-existent/artifactual. There is no genetic, structural, or immune evidence connecting PRRC2B to lung biology at all — the sole functional paper (Mei et al. 2013) is about rat brain development, and the two "incidental hits" (sarcoma fusion, preeclampsia proteomics) are irrelevant by the researcher's own account. The researcher's judgment that MAGMA prioritization here likely reflects positional/LD proximity to the RXRA signal rather than independent gene-level evidence is well-supported and consistent with the Open Targets background (no credible set anywhere nominates PRRC2B). This is the correct call, and there is nothing to contest — there's simply no positive claim being made that needs rebutting.
> **PRRC2B — HOLDS** (in the sense that the researcher's negative/skeptical conclusion is well-supported and not overclaimed; there is no case for PRRC2B being pursued further).

### POMT1 — HOLDS (not contested; listed here only for completeness)

> Also fairly characterized as essentially artifactual, and the researcher was appropriately careful to distinguish restrictive (muscle-weakness-mediated) respiratory insufficiency in dystroglycanopathies from COPD's obstructive/emphysematous/inflammatory pathophysiology — this is exactly the kind of specificity check the review's priors call for, and it's done correctly. One nit: the citation-verifier already caught that PMID 20301468 (GeneReviews) returned NOT_FOUND and that PMID 28815891 is actually about POMT2, not POMT1 — both appropriately disclosed by the researcher as caveats rather than silently used. The oe=0.841 (not LoF-constrained, consistent with recessive-only disease mechanism) supports the researcher's skepticism: a gene under weak constraint whose only established disease mechanism is a severe recessive dystroglycanopathy is a poor prior candidate for a common-variant, regulatory-only, non-coding COPD GWAS signal.
> **POMT1 — HOLDS** (skeptical conclusion is well-supported; no overclaiming detected).
