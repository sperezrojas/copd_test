# Audit trail — locus 6:142,250,001-142,600,000 (ADGRG6)

This file records everything EXCLUDED from the evidence table (evidence_tables/6_142250001-142600000.md),
plus the full critique record, so a reviewer can trace why. Not intended for distribution alongside

## Excluded citations (CLAIM_MISMATCH — not used in evidence table)

1. **Sakornsakolpat P et al. "Genetic landscape of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease
   identifies heterogeneous cell-type and phenotype associations." Nat Genet 2019. PMID 30804561,
   DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0342-2.**
   Researcher's original claim: companion COPD case-control GWAS (35,735 cases/222,076 controls
   plus ICGC) identifying 82 COPD risk loci; ADGRG6/GPR126 is among the well-replicated COPD loci
   carried forward from prior lung-function GWAS.
   Verifier finding (CLAIM_MISMATCH): ADGRG6 is mentioned only as a fine-mapped "deleterious
   variant" within credible sets, not as one of the 82 primary COPD loci reported by this study.
   The researcher's characterization overstated this paper's direct support for ADGRG6 as a
   primary COPD-GWAS hit.
   Disposition: excluded from evidence table. The underlying fact that ADGRG6 is a long-replicated
   lung-function locus stands on other, VERIFIED citations (Hancock 2010, Soler Artigas 2015,
   Shrine 2019, Yang 2021, Wang 2024); this specific paper's role as evidence of a "primary COPD
   locus" designation is not supported and should not be cited as such.

2. **Jackson VE et al. "Exome-wide analysis of rare coding variation identifies novel
   associations with COPD and airflow limitation in MOCS3, IFIT3 and SERPINA12." Thorax 2016.
   PMID 26917578, DOI 10.1136/thoraxjnl-2015-207876.**
   Researcher's original claim: exome-array study in COPD cases/ever-smoking controls explicitly
   "confirmed previously reported associations with COPD risk at MMP12, HHIP, GPR126 and CHRNA5"
   — independent replication of GPR126-COPD in an ever-smoker-only design.
   Verifier finding (CLAIM_MISMATCH): the abstract does state this "confirmed" language, but the
   paper's own detailed results show MMP12 (one of the four genes in that same sentence) had "no
   evidence of replication...in UK BiLEVE" (p=0.676) — i.e., the paper's own results contradict its
   abstract's blanket "confirmed" framing for at least one of the four listed genes. GPR126 itself
   was not separately checked variant-by-variant for replication failure, so this is a caution flag
   on the "confirmed" framing generally (abstract-level overclaiming), not a specific refutation of
   the GPR126 replication.
   Disposition: excluded from evidence table as an independent "confirmed" claim. Retained only as
   a documented caution about taking this paper's abstract language at face value for any of the
   four genes it lists.

### PASS 1 (initial red-team-skeptic critique, before Hall et al. re-verification)

Preliminary disclosure: search-budget constraint (from skeptic agent). Before running any
independent contradiction searches, the skeptic hit this session's web-search budget limit
(200/200 used) — all five queries submitted (ADGRG6 contradicting-evidence, NMBR-COPD, HIVEP2-COPD,
Hall et al. FASEB full-text check, GSCAN supplementary-loci check) returned zero results with a
"budget exhausted" message. The skeptic was NOT able to perform the mandated baseline
contradiction-search pass for any of the three genes in this pass. This is a genuine gap, not a
finding of "no contradicting evidence exists." A follow-up pass (Pass 2, below) was commissioned to
address this.

(a) ADGRG6 prioritization over NMBR once Sakornsakolpat, Jackson, and Hall et al. are discounted:
Discounting those three citations removes: the claim that ADGRG6 is a "well-replicated COPD locus"
in the primary 82-locus COPD GWAS (false per verifier — it's only a fine-mapped credible-set
variant, not a primary hit), the claim that ever-smoker exome data "confirmed" the GPR126-COPD
association (undermined by the same paper's own failed MMP12 replication), and — critically — the
entire immune-mechanism claim (IL33/airway-remodeling) and the only specific functional-variant
claim (Ser123Gly missense, intronic eQTL variant), since Hall et al. was at that time the sole
source for both and unconfirmed. What survives: Hancock 2010, Soler Artigas 2015, Shrine 2019, Yang
2021, Wang 2024 (statistical genetics — FEV1/FVC, not COPD case-control per se), and Werder 2023
(CRISPRi in iAT2 cells showing altered cytoskeleton/junction/proliferation/smoke-injury-response
phenotypes). This remaining evidence is genuinely strong for lung-function biology — multiple
independent, large, cross-ancestry replications of a quantitative spirometric association, plus one
solid piece of cell-autonomous functional data in a disease-relevant cell type. So the case for
ADGRG6 over NMBR on statistical-genetics-plus-function grounds still held even under the most
conservative reading. However, the researcher's framing that this is "real if incomplete
mechanistic literature connecting it to lung disease" was, under this conservative reading,
overstated in one specific respect: the immune/inflammatory dimension of that claim was entirely
unsupported once Hall et al. was discounted — zero verified citations linked ADGRG6/GPR126 to any
immune cell type or inflammatory program in lung disease under that reading. Also flagged: the Open
Targets colocalization context shows ADGRG6 eQTL coloc in adipose, skin, and BLUEPRINT T-cells — not
lung tissue. No lung-tissue eQTL colocalization is reported anywhere in the record — this gap
persists regardless of the Hall et al. re-verification outcome (see final reconciliation in
critique.md / evidence table).

(b) "Not smoking-mediated" conclusion: The researcher's own synthesis already flags this
appropriately: the conclusion "rests partly on the unverified Open Targets context... rather than
an independently-sourced never-smoker GWAS paper." This is honest hedging, not overclaiming. The
GSCAN check is correctly characterized as "weak/indirect absence-of-evidence" — an abstract not
naming a gene among 406 loci is not equivalent to checking the full supplementary table. The
strongest actual argument against smoking-mediation is not the OT data or GSCAN at all — it's
biological prior #2: direct, independently replicated FEV1/FVC associations are themselves strong
evidence of lung-intrinsic action, since spirometric traits are not simply proxies for smoking
behavior. Synthesis was directed to weight the FEV1/FVC replications more heavily than the OT
never-smoker-stratified L2G scores when making the smoking-mediation call.

(c) Skeletal/myelination vs. lung-mechanism gap: Honestly reported, not glossed over. The
researcher explicitly states "no documented mechanistic bridge... was found," explicitly labels
Bradley et al. (myelination) as "NOT lung-relevant, background only," and explicitly labels Bian et
al. (growth-plate) as "SKELETAL phenotype only, no lung phenotype examined." No critique needed.

(d) HIVEP2 Treg finding: Already appropriately caveated by the researcher: both citations
(Schumann 2020 Treg CRISPR screen; Alexander 2021 SARS-CoV-2 Calu-3) are correctly labeled as
non-COPD-specific, and the researcher's own summary judgment calls HIVEP2 "most likely
LD/regulatory bleed-through... but this can't be ruled out." Additional skeptic point: unlike
ADGRG6, no eQTL colocalization at all is reported for HIVEP2 at this locus. Combined with the
weak/distant L2G (0.06-0.11) and the gene body sitting 151kb outside the interval, the absence of
any colocalization evidence for HIVEP2 specifically (not just "weak" evidence, but none reported)
further supports treating it as bleed-through rather than an independent signal. The Treg-
immunosuppression biology should not be allowed to inflate HIVEP2's status in synthesis just
because it "sounds relevant" to COPD immunology.

(e) NMBR — ruled out or underweighted?: The researcher's "weak candidate" framing is justified and
not overclaimed: gene body 103kb outside the interval, flagged only by PoPS (not
MAGMA/FLAMES/ABC/L2G — the one L2G hit was 0.05 in an unrelated hip-index study), zero
COPD/lung-function GWAS hits, and the only mechanistic citation (Subramaniam 2003) is in
bronchopulmonary dysplasia — a neonatal disease of arrested lung development in preterm infants,
mechanistically and epidemiologically distinct from adult COPD's tissue-destruction/remodeling
pathophysiology. "No literature found" should not be read as "definitively ruled out" — NMBR's
bombesin-receptor biology is at least a coherent hypothesis that simply hasn't been tested at this
locus. Characterize NMBR as correctly deprioritized given current evidence, not falsified.

Pass 1 per-gene verdicts (conditional, later superseded for ADGRG6): ADGRG6 WEAKENED (conditional
on Hall et al. being unconfirmed); NMBR WEAKENED (final); HIVEP2 WEAKENED (final). No gene elevated
to CONTESTED in Pass 1, because the skeptic was unable to run contradiction searches (budget
exhausted).

### PASS 2 (follow-up, narrowly-scoped contradiction search)

A second skeptic dispatch ran targeted contradiction searches (WebSearch budget was again
exhausted almost immediately — inherited from prior session activity — so the agent substituted
direct WebFetch calls against PubMed search URLs and the Europe PMC REST API). Findings:

1. ADGRG6 lung-tissue eQTL / contradicting-evidence query: surfaced Hall RJ et al. FASEB J 2021
   (PMID 34165809) — the same paper already in the researcher's set, previously marked
   unconfirmable. Via Europe PMC the skeptic could retrieve abstract text (whereas the original
   verifier's paywalled-journal-site attempt failed), suggesting the claims might in fact be
   confirmable. This was flagged for re-verification rather than accepted at face value by the
   skeptic itself (correctly, since this was UNVERIFIED skeptic-sourced text, not a verifier
   verdict). No independent contradicting evidence for the ADGRG6-COPD/lung-function association
   was found. One incidental finding, NOT independently verified and NOT added to the evidence
   table: a height/birth-length GWAS (van der Valk 2014, PMID 25281659) mentions GPR126 as a
   pleiotropic height locus — consistent with, not contradicting, the already-known
   height/skeletal pleiotropy already reported by the researcher.
2. NMBR contradiction/COPD query: no new COPD-specific literature found; only the already-known
   Subramaniam 2003 BPD paper and an irrelevant oncology paper (bombesin-receptor imaging in
   carcinoid tumors). This independently confirms — rather than merely fails to overturn — the
   researcher's "no COPD-relevant literature for NMBR" finding.
3. HIVEP2 contradiction/COPD query: no COPD-specific literature found; one irrelevant
   hepatocellular-carcinoma lncRNA tissue-panel paper. Independently confirms the researcher's "no
   COPD-specific evidence for HIVEP2" finding.
4. rs4896582-specific fine-mapping query: no dedicated standalone paper found (expected null
   result — variant-level discussion is embedded in the larger GWAS meta-analyses already cited).

Bottom line from Pass 2: no genuine contradicting evidence was found for ADGRG6 despite an active
search; NMBR and HIVEP2's lack of COPD-specific literature was independently confirmed rather than
merely assumed from absence in the researcher's own search.

### Citation-verifier follow-up: Hall et al. FASEB J 2021 (PMID 34165809) re-verification

A third dispatch (citation-verifier) retried this single citation using Europe PMC/PubMed abstract
retrieval instead of the paywalled journal site. Full abstract text was retrieved. Result: paper
confirmed to exist, not retracted (CC-BY 4.0 open access, published June 2021, 11 citations per
Crossref), and ALL FIVE specific claims attributed to it by the researcher were confirmed verbatim
against the abstract text:
- "Online tools (GTEx and LDlink) identified SNPs which may have effects on GPR126
  function/expression, including missense variant Ser123Gly and an intronic variant that shows
  eQTL effects on GPR126 expression."
- "GPR126 signaling via cAMP-mediated pathways was identified in human structural airway cells when
  activated with the tethered agonist, stachel."
- "We identified ~350 differentially expressed genes at 4 and 24 hours post stimulation... genes
  regulated by GPR126 activation include IL33, CTGF, and SERPINE1, which already have known roles
  in lung biology."
- "we suggest a role for GPR126 in airway remodeling."
- "Genetic variants in this region are reproducibly associated with lung function and COPD in
  genome wide association studies (GWAS)."
This citation's final verdict is VERIFIED, not CLAIM_MISMATCH (see reconciliation below and in
verified_citations.md).

### Reconciliation and FINAL per-gene verdicts (superseding Pass 1's conditional verdicts)

**ADGRG6 (GPR126): SUPPORTED** (upgraded from Pass 1's conditional WEAKENED). With Hall et al. now
VERIFIED, ADGRG6 has: (i) multiple independent, large, cross-ancestry statistical-genetics
replications for FEV1/FVC (Hancock 2010, Soler Artigas 2015, Shrine 2019, Yang 2021, Wang 2024, all
VERIFIED); (ii) a specific, verified candidate functional variant (missense Ser123Gly) and an
independently-verified intronic eQTL variant at the locus (Hall et al.); (iii) two independent,
verified pieces of cell-type-specific functional/mechanistic evidence in disease-relevant lung
cells — iPSC-derived alveolar type II epithelial cells (Werder et al. 2023, cigarette-smoke injury
response) AND primary human airway smooth muscle/bronchial epithelial cells (Hall et al. 2021,
cAMP/IL33/CTGF/SERPINE1, explicit airway-remodeling proposal); (iv) the ONLY verified
immune-relevant mechanistic finding for this gene (IL33 induction) sits in a non-immune cell type
(airway smooth muscle) — this is real and now verified, but it is still not direct evidence of
ADGRG6 acting within an immune/inflammatory cell population, so the researcher's honest
characterization of immune evidence as "sparse" should be preserved even though it is no longer
"absent." The two CLAIM_MISMATCH citations (Sakornsakolpat, Jackson) should still be excluded from
the "well-replicated primary COPD locus" framing (see excluded-citations section above) — the
strongest, most defensible claim is that ADGRG6 is a robustly replicated LUNG-FUNCTION (FEV1/FVC)
locus with credible-set-level COPD evidence via Open Targets L2G, not that it is independently one
of the "82 primary COPD GWAS loci" in Sakornsakolpat's own gene list. No lung-tissue (whole-organ)
eQTL colocalization is reported in the Open Targets structured lookup — this remains a genuine,
disclosed gap distinct from Hall et al.'s own airway-cell-type eQTL/functional finding.

**NMBR: WEAKENED — final, unchanged from Pass 1.** Confirmed independently in Pass 2: no
COPD/lung-function literature exists for this gene beyond the neonatal-BPD mast-cell paper, which
remains a disease-context mismatch. Deprioritized, not falsified.

**HIVEP2: WEAKENED — final, unchanged from Pass 1.** Confirmed independently in Pass 2: no
COPD-specific literature exists for this gene beyond non-COPD immunology/virology papers. Most
likely LD/regulatory bleed-through from the ADGRG6 signal; no colocalization evidence supports an
independent causal role.

No gene is elevated to CONTESTED. No genuine contradicting evidence against ADGRG6 was identified
across two independent skeptic search passes.
