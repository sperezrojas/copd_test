## Genes marked CONTESTED or WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning preserved)

### NOS1 — CONTESTED

> FLAMES prioritized NOS1, but essentially every other line of evidence gathered argues
> against it being the operative gene at this locus:
>
> - No L2G signal at all at rs12424627 (vs. KSR2's ~0.69-0.70), and only two weak EuropePMC
>   text-mining co-occurrences with "pulmonary emphysema" — text-mining hits are not
>   genetic-association evidence and shouldn't be weighted as such.
> - A consistent, repeated pattern of null findings across four independent, well-powered
>   candidate-gene/exhaled-NO studies (PMID 24192154, 22590587, 22368302, 17690329), all now
>   VERIFIED by the citation-verifier. This is not "absence of evidence" in the weak sense —
>   these studies directly and repeatedly tested NOS1 polymorphisms against FeNO and
>   COPD/asthma-related phenotypes, a functional readout mechanistically downstream of NOS1
>   activity, and found nothing. That is active contradicting evidence against NOS1's
>   candidacy, and it should be weighted more heavily than a single FLAMES nomination.
> - The mechanistic nNOS/iNANC bronchodilation literature (PMID 9357865, 10330441) is real
>   and well-verified, but it is being asked to do work it can't do here. It establishes
>   that NOS1's protein product plausibly participates in non-adrenergic non-cholinergic
>   airway smooth-muscle relaxation — but this is asthma/allergic-airway and largely
>   animal-model physiology, not COPD-specific, and it predates and is orthogonal to
>   genetic-association testing. Mechanistic plausibility of a gene's biological function is
>   a different evidence type from "does the GWAS signal at this locus act through this
>   gene," and the latter is what four independent studies just tested and failed to
>   confirm. If the researcher's write-up gives the 1990s mechanistic papers comparable or
>   greater weight than the four null genetic studies, that's an imbalance — elegant
>   mechanism is compensating for absent/contradicting genetics, which inverts the correct
>   burden of proof for a GWAS-locus interpretation task.
> - Applying Prior 3 (immune/inflammatory complexity) fairly: NOS1's biology here is airway
>   smooth-muscle/neural (iNANC), not really an immune/inflammatory program at all, which is
>   itself a mismatch with the pipeline's framing of this locus as potentially
>   immune-relevant — worth noting explicitly rather than folding into a generic
>   "mechanistic plausibility" bucket.
>
> Verdict: CONTESTED. The contradicting evidence is the four VERIFIED null candidate-gene/
> FeNO studies plus OT's complete absence of L2G signal for NOS1 at this variant. These
> already-verified citations constitute the contradiction — no new unverified citation to
> send back to the citation-verifier, but the FLAMES call for NOS1 at this locus looks
> poorly corroborated and should not be presented as co-equal to KSR2 without heavy
> caveating.

### KSR2 — WEAKENED

> The strongest piece of evidence is genuinely strong on its face: a consistent ~0.69-0.70
> L2G score for KSR2 across three independent FEV1/FVC-ratio GWAS with H4=1.0
> colocalization between them, versus RFC5 at ~0.10. That's a real, reproducible
> statistical signal pointing to KSR2 as the regulatory target at rs12424627.
>
> But the inference that this makes KSR2 confidently "the" causal COPD/lung-function gene
> overreaches in several ways:
>
> - Zero dedicated PubMed literature. The researcher found no papers on KSR2 and lung
>   function or COPD at all. The entire case rests on one structured database's L2G/coloc
>   output, which was not run through the citation-verifier (it's not a citation, it's a
>   database query) and has no independent literature corroboration. Leaning this heavily
>   on an unverified structured-data signal, absent in the peer-reviewed literature, is a
>   meaningfully different evidentiary tier than the pipeline's other prioritized genes
>   typically get, and the write-up should say so explicitly rather than treating L2G=0.7 as
>   equivalent to a validated mechanism.
> - Internal Open Targets inconsistency not fully reckoned with. OT's own disease-
>   association aggregation shows zero rows for KSR2 against COPD, FEV1, lung-function-
>   measurement, or emphysema — despite the strong FEV1/FVC L2G/coloc signal. The "likely an
>   ingestion/labeling gap" explanation is speculation, not confirmed. An equally plausible
>   reading is that the disease-association layer applies a stricter/different threshold
>   that the QTL-level coloc doesn't clear. This ambiguity should be presented as
>   unresolved, not resolved in KSR2's favor.
> - Smoking-mediation gap (Prior 1) — the flagged gap is real and matters. No literature was
>   found on a smoking-initiation/nicotine-dependence GWAS signal at this locus for either
>   gene, yet OT's own broader disease-association profile shows a smoking-initiation score
>   of 0.288 for KSR2 and a response-to-bronchodilator score of 0.260 — nontrivial pleiotropy
>   that was not followed up with a targeted literature/GWAS-catalog search (e.g., GSCAN
>   smoking-initiation summary stats at this locus). Given KSR2's well-established role in
>   hypothalamic MAPK/ERK signaling, appetite, and severe obesity, there is a directly
>   plausible pathway from KSR2 -> appetite/reward circuitry -> smoking behavior and/or
>   obesity -> lung-function metrics, independent of any direct pulmonary mechanism. This gap
>   should have been actively closed before treating KSR2 as a pulmonary-biology gene; as it
>   stands, the case has not ruled out smoking- or obesity-mediation, and Prior 1 explicitly
>   says signals plausibly mediated through smoking should be distinguished from
>   lung-biology-supported ones — that distinction hasn't been made here.
> - The "obesity-mediated indirect mechanism" hedge is speculative and possibly
>   physiologically backwards. The researcher's own hedge (obesity -> mechanical
>   restriction or metabolic-inflammatory crosstalk -> lung function) is untested by any
>   cited paper. Classic obesity-related restrictive physiology tends to lower FVC
>   proportionally more than FEV1, which typically preserves or raises FEV1/FVC ratio — the
>   opposite direction from an obstructive, COPD-risk-increasing signature. If the
>   credible-set risk allele is associated with a lower FEV1/FVC ratio (obstructive
>   direction), the obesity-mechanical-restriction story as stated doesn't obviously explain
>   it, and synthesis-lead should verify the actual effect direction before accepting this
>   hedge as even a plausible placeholder mechanism, let alone present it as more than
>   speculation.
>
> Verdict: WEAKENED. The statistical-genetic case for KSR2 as the gene at this locus is real
> (L2G+coloc), but the inferential leap to "KSR2 acts on the lung via a distinct biological
> mechanism relevant to COPD" is not supported by any literature, is undermined by OT's own
> disease-term silence on COPD/FEV1/emphysema, and has not addressed a plausible
> smoking/obesity-behavioral confound that the data itself (smoking-initiation score 0.288)
> surfaced but the researcher didn't chase down.

### RFC5 — flagged (not formally CONTESTED; runner-up gene, not a pipeline-prioritized candidate)

> RFC5 sits at L2G ~0.10 (nonzero, ~7-fold below KSR2), is physically in the locus window,
> but was not one of the five prioritized-method candidate genes and was only briefly
> checked by the researcher, who found no lung/immune biology and called it "unlikely." No
> contradicting evidence to raise it to CONTESTED, but the depth of research here is clearly
> shallower than for KSR2/NOS1 — a quick negative check is not the same as an exhaustive
> literature search. Recommend treating "unlikely" for RFC5 as provisional (thin evidence
> base, not a rigorous exclusion) rather than a settled negative, and noting it explicitly
> as a runner-up in the audit trail given its nonzero L2G score, rather than silently
> dropping it.
