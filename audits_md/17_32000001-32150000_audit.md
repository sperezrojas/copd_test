# Audit trail: locus 17_32000001-32150000 (nearest gene LRRC37B)

This appendix records citations excluded from the evidence table, genes marked CONTESTED (or
flagged as incompletely evaluable) by the red-team-skeptic, and process caveats disclosed
during the pipeline, with reasoning preserved verbatim where applicable. See
`evidence_tables/17_32000001-32150000.md` for the main evidence table.

## Excluded citations

**None.** All 26 citations returned by the researcher were checked by citation-verifier and
came back VERIFIED (0 RETRACTED, 0 NOT_FOUND, 0 CLAIM_MISMATCH). See
`evidence_tables/_status/17_32000001-32150000/verified_citations.md` for the full per-citation
verdict list. A subset of 5 UTP6-related PMIDs (42150138, 40917497, 40445397, 37659689,
32524281) were verified as existing/non-retracted but were not used as evidence-table rows
because the researcher characterized them as incidental general ribosome-biogenesis findings
with no specific claim relevant to lung, COPD, or immune biology attached — they are omitted
from the evidence table for relevance, not for any verification failure.

## Genes flagged CONTESTED or otherwise disputed by red-team-skeptic

### ASIC2 — CONTESTED
Verbatim from red-team-skeptic: "it does not physically overlap the stated window at all
(~0.9-1 Mb away per independent Ensembl check) — this alone should disqualify it from 'gene
at this locus' status regardless of what MAGMA/PoPS returned, since MAGMA/PoPS gene-level
tests aggregate SNP-level signal over a window/LD-based assignment that can extend well beyond
a specific fine-mapped credible set; (2) zero colocalization support across all 31 Open
Targets credible sets at the actual lead variant; (3) zero PubMed hits combining ASIC2 with
COPD/GWAS/smoking; (4) the one relevant functional paper (PMID 31169471) shows a pulmonary
*vascular* resistance/hypertension phenotype in ASIC2 KO mice — mechanistically distinct from
the airway/parenchymal-destruction biology that defines COPD/emphysema, so citing it as
COPD-relevant would be a tissue/phenotype-context overclaim; and (5) the cough-afferent paper
(PMID 41387299) explicitly implicates ASIC3, not ASIC2, as the causally necessary channel —
the researcher was correct not to credit ASIC2 for that finding, and would have been
overclaiming had they done so... I'd go slightly further than 'weak': given the physical
non-overlap, ASIC2's presence in the candidate list should be treated by synthesis-lead as
most likely a MAGMA/PoPS window-boundary artifact rather than a genuine candidate at this
credible set."

Direct answer given: "Should ASIC2 be ruled out? Yes, effectively — it fails physical overlap
with the window, has zero colocalization support, and its supporting functional paper is in
the wrong lung compartment (vascular, not airway/parenchymal). Its status as the 'MAGMA/PoPS
winner' should be treated as a likely window/LD-based artifact rather than genuine biological
prioritization."

### COPRS — CONTESTED (flagged as incompletely searched, not as contradicted)
Verbatim from red-team-skeptic: "It has the single highest raw L2G score seen anywhere at
this locus (0.555) but zero verifiable functional literature (researcher was blocked by
access issues, not because none exists — this is a 'search incomplete' flag, not a 'gene is
null' flag). Per the thoroughness criterion, I'd flag this for synthesis-lead as a gene that
needs a repeat literature attempt (via a different access route, e.g. PMC/Europe PMC direct
rather than GeneCards/NCBI search UI) before any final call is made — the current absence of
citations should not be read as absence of biology, and it should not be dropped from
consideration on the current record. Also note it lies just outside the stated window
(telomeric), adding a boundary-definition question independent of the missing literature."

The researcher's own note on this gap (from `researcher.md`): "I was unable to independently
verify COPRS's precise molecular function via a reliable, accessible database source in this
session (GeneCards fetch was blocked by a 403 error; NCBI Gene page was blocked by a CAPTCHA
wall)... I found no citable literature for this gene beyond the Open Targets lookup provided
in the task input."

**Action taken:** COPRS is omitted from the evidence table (no citation exists to place in
a row) but is retained in the Open Targets summary and explicitly named in the Prioritized
Gene section's "what would resolve this" list as requiring a follow-up literature search
attempt before the study team treats it as ruled out.

## Other evidence-quality caveats disclosed during the pipeline (not CONTESTED genes, but material to interpretation)

- **LRRC37B, SUZ12** — WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic: strong/moderate Open Targets
  spirometry-trait colocalization (7/9 and 6/9 of 9 credible sets respectively) but no
  COPD-, spirometry-, or smoking-specific published literature; LRRC37B's only substantive
  mechanistic literature is neurodevelopmental (cortical neuron excitability, PMID 38134874),
  and SUZ12's is broad/nonspecific PRC2-chromatin essentiality (T-cell differentiation,
  hematopoiesis, tumor immunology) plus one lung-*cancer* (not COPD) carcinogenesis paper
  (PMID 40211119) where SUZ12 is a co-recruited generic PRC2 component, not a SUZ12-specific
  driver.
- **UTP6** — WEAKENED: colocalization present but weaker/less consistent (max L2G 0.327);
  literature is exclusively general ribosome-biogenesis housekeeping biochemistry with no
  lung/COPD/immune relevance.
- **RHOT1** — split verdict, SUPPORTED-for-mechanism / WEAKENED-for-genetics: "Genetics and
  mechanism disagree here and both should be visible in the final writeup," per red-team-skeptic.
  Statistical/positional support is the weakest of the group (single L2G appearance at 0.053,
  near-zero literature-only OT COPD score, gene body mostly outside the stated window), but
  functional evidence is causal, in vivo, and cell-type-specific (PMID 31593750, PMID
  35366248), explicitly framed by its own authors around COPD.
- **VILMIR** (lncRNA; not a locus-file candidate gene) — flagged as speculative: the lead
  credible-set variant physically falls within its transcript body (independently verified via
  Ensembl by the researcher), and its published biology is a lung-epithelium-specific
  interferon/antiviral mechanism (PMID 40130878, PMID 41378895) plausibly relevant to
  COPD viral-exacerbation susceptibility — but no publication ties VILMIR to this GWAS locus
  or to COPD; per red-team-skeptic, this should be read as "a hypothesis worth stating as such
  ...rather than as supporting evidence."

## Process caveats disclosed by subagents

- **Search-budget exhaustion during red-team critique:** the red-team-skeptic subagent
  reported its WebSearch budget was exhausted before it could run any independent
  contradiction searches (it attempted "RHOT1/COPD-mitochondria" and "ASIC2/COPD-GWAS"
  searches). Its critique above is therefore based on internal consistency/logic review of
  the researcher's already-verified findings, not on independently found contradicting
  literature. Per the skeptic's own disclosure: "any confidence statements below should be
  read as 'unrebutted by what the researcher found,' not 'actively checked against opposing
  literature.'" This caveat applies to every gene at this locus, not only one, and should be
  weighed by the study team accordingly.
- **Researcher database-access failures:** GeneCards (403 error) and NCBI Gene (CAPTCHA
  wall) were inaccessible during the researcher's session, blocking independent functional
  characterization of COPRS specifically (see above).
- **Open Targets is a structured database lookup, not literature:** all L2G/colocalization/
  constraint figures cited throughout this locus's evidence table, Open Targets summary, and
  this audit were pulled directly by the orchestrator via the Open Targets GraphQL API and
  were not passed through citation-verifier or red-team-skeptic (per pipeline design — see
  `evidence_tables/_status/17_32000001-32150000/opentargets.md` for the full structured
  lookup output).
- **Locus-file MAGMA/PoPS vs. physical-overlap discrepancy:** the researcher independently
  verified via Ensembl that ASIC2 (the locus file's stated MAGMA/PoPS-prioritized gene) does
  not physically fall within the stated 17:32,000,001-32,150,000 window (sitting
  chr17:33,013,087-34,174,964, ~0.9-1 Mb centromeric of the window's far edge). This is
  reported as a discrepancy for the study team's awareness of how the upstream MAGMA/PoPS
  gene-window/LD-extension methodology may differ from the strict credible-set boundary used
  to define this locus file, not as an error attributable to this literature-review pipeline.
