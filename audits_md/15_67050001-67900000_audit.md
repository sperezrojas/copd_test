## Gene-level skeptic verdicts (full reasoning, verbatim from critique.md)

### SMAD3 — WEAKENED

The mouse-knockout literature (Chen 2004, Bonniaud 2004, Farkas 2011) is genuinely strong
mechanistic evidence that loss of Smad3 produces emphysema-like alveolar pathology, and
Farkas 2011 is the most relevant single paper because it directly tests smoke exposure and
finds it aggravates the phenotype — a real point in favor of a lung-autonomous,
smoke-interacting mechanism rather than pure smoking-behavior mediation. But three things
are underweighted in the researcher's write-up:

- Two hypothesis-free, adequately powered lung-function GWAS (Hancock 2010, Soler Artigas
  2011) do not name SMAD3, and Shrine 2019's inclusion is explicitly unconfirmed. For a
  gene whose case rests partly on "concordance across COPD and spirometric traits," the
  absence of positive spirometric-GWAS evidence is a real gap, not a neutral fact.
- Open Targets shows SMAD3 with the lowest locus-specific L2G of the three genes and no
  eQTL/molecular-QTL colocalization at this exact credible set. Mouse-knockout biology
  answers "can loss of this gene cause emphysema-like pathology" — it does not answer
  "does the credible-set variant at this specific locus regulate SMAD3 in human lung."
  The researcher's original synthesis blurred these by presenting SMAD3 as the clear
  front-runner while the actual locus-specific statistical evidence points the other way.
- The two positive human COPD case-control associations (Gong 2021, Yang 2010) are
  candidate-gene studies of different variants than the credible-set lead SNP, in Chinese
  cohorts, one explicitly small/pre-GWAS-era with no correction/replication stated.
- The Tu 2026 PoPS result is a shared cross-disease ("gAirwayDisease") factor, not
  COPD-specific pulmonary structural biology, and should not be conflated with the
  emphysema-specific mouse mechanism.

Net verdict: the mouse data make SMAD3 biologically plausible as a COPD gene in general,
but the locus-specific human statistical evidence does not clearly establish that SMAD3 is
the gene at this credible set, as opposed to being a plausible neighbor picked up mainly by
proximity/nearest-gene logic.

### AAGAB — WEAKENED (correctly discounted by researcher, not held up as a genuine candidate)

AAGAB's top L2G score recurs near-identically across totally unrelated traits (adiposity,
renal, neuroticism, asthma) at this same variant, which is the classic signature of a
distance/LD-driven L2G artifact rather than trait-specific regulatory evidence. Combined
with zero PubMed literature connecting AAGAB to lung/airway biology and a
keratinocyte-specific, skin-restricted disease mechanism (palmoplantar keratoderma via
AP-1/AP-2 clathrin adaptor complex), AAGAB has no biological story at all for COPD. Flagged
WEAKENED rather than CONTESTED because there is no competing paper substantively promoting
AAGAB as causal to rebut — it is simply an artifact-prone statistical signal without a
biological anchor, already appropriately discounted by the researcher.

### IQCH — WEAKENED to the point of near-exclusion

Same L2G-artifact logic applies, and additionally IQCH is not independently prioritized by
any of FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS/ABC — it only appears via LD-tagged low-PIP variants. Its only
established biology (testis-specific spermatogenesis) has no plausible lung mechanism, and
no lung/COPD literature exists. The "recurs in credible set at low PIP" framing in the
input file likely overstates its relevance; there is no independent statistical or
biological reason to consider it above LD-tag noise.

### MAP2K5 / SKOR1 / C15orf61 — not applicable / effectively excluded

Correctly bracketed by the researcher as region-enumeration only, not overlapped by
credible-set variants, with an established alternative phenotype (restless-legs syndrome)
for MAP2K5/SKOR1 and no lung link for C15orf61. Not marked CONTESTED because they were
never seriously proposed as candidates.

### CONTESTED-adjacent finding: locus pleiotropy / non-specific-artifact alternative

No single gene was elevated to a full CONTESTED verdict (no paper was found — by the
researcher or by the skeptic, search-budget permitting — that specifically argues against
SMAD3 or specifically champions a different single gene at this exact locus with equal or
greater force). However, the totality of evidence better fits a pleiotropic/non-specific
locus story than a clean single-gene causal narrative:

1. The L2G artifact pattern spanning adiposity, renal, and neuropsychiatric traits at the
   identical variant suggests this locus/LD block has broad, possibly non-causal
   statistical pull across many GWAS, not a lung-specific regulatory element.
2. The Zhang 2024 lung-cancer/esophageal-cancer pleiotropy paper (PMID 38817868) places
   SMAD3 alongside CHRNA4 (a canonical nicotinic-receptor/smoking-behavior gene), a
   plausible (though unconfirmed by the researcher's own targeted, non-exhaustive
   GSCAN/nicotine-dependence lookup) route by which SMAD3-region signal could partly
   reflect smoking-related pleiotropy.
3. Tu 2026's shared cross-disease latent factor (COPD+asthma+bronchiectasis+CRSsNP) argues
   for a general airway-inflammatory susceptibility mechanism rather than COPD-specific
   alveolar-destruction biology.

This pattern is labeled CONTESTED-adjacent / underdetermined rather than a clean CONTESTED
verdict for SMAD3. Reviewers should read the "Prioritized gene" section of the evidence
table's LOW confidence rating in this light.

### Additional overclaiming flags (verbatim from skeptic)

(a) Calling SMAD3 "FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS-prioritized" without foregrounding in the same breath
that it has the lowest L2G and no coloc of the three genes at this locus overclaims.
(b) Guo 2024 (HHIP/4q31) and Boueiz 2019 (ACVR1B) are explicitly pathway-analogy/indirect
evidence about different genes at different loci — should not be counted as SMAD3-locus
evidence even though the researcher does label them as indirect.
(c) The candidate-gene case-control studies (Gong 2021, Yang 2010) test different variants
than the lead SNP; using them to support "SMAD3 is associated with COPD" without noting the
variant mismatch risks overclaiming specificity to this credible set.
(d) The "more consistent with a direct pulmonary-biology mechanism than a
smoking-behavior-mediated one" conclusion in the smoking-overlap section is appropriately
hedged as "provisional" by the researcher — this hedge must survive into the final
synthesis rather than being dropped.
