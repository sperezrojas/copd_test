# Audit trail — Locus 1:43,300,001-43,850,000 (nearest gene PTPRF)

This file preserves excluded citations, contested genes, and process caveats
that are deliberately left out of the main evidence table
(`evidence_tables/1_43300001-43850000.md`) so that document can be handed to
reviewers without the full audit trail, while this file remains available for
anyone who wants to check the pipeline's work.

## Excluded citations (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH)

**None.** All 23 citations produced by the researcher for this locus in the
original review were verified with a VERIFIED verdict by citation-verifier
(checked 2026-09-11). In the CDC20 re-opening addendum (see below), all 19
additional CDC20 citations were also VERIFIED. No citations were excluded from
the evidence table on verification grounds at any stage. Full per-citation
verifier reasoning is in
`evidence_tables/_status/1_43300001-43850000/verified_citations.md`.

One notable resolution during the original verification: the researcher had
flagged uncertainty about whether PMID 40964458 (Fu et al. 2025, MR+ML
hypoxia-genes/COPD study) specifically implicated SLC2A1 or a different
hypoxia-related gene in its causal estimate. The citation-verifier confirmed
from the abstract that SLC2A1 specifically is the gene with the reported causal
estimate (OR=1.32, 95% CI 1.02-1.71).

One disambiguation flagged during CDC20 verification: PMID 21088493 (Lizé et
al., Cell Cycle 2010, miR-449a/mucociliary differentiation/tobacco smoke)
concerns **CDC20B**, a distinct paralogous gene at a different genomic locus,
**not CDC20 itself** (the candidate gene at this locus). Citation-verifier
confirmed this disambiguation is correct. This citation is retained only as a
flagged disambiguation note and was deliberately excluded from the main
evidence table as CDC20-supporting evidence — it must not be read as literature
support for CDC20.

## Genes marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning)

### SLC2A1 — CONTESTED
"This gene sits ~650kb from the lead variant and outside the stated locus
boundary entirely, has zero L2G support across all 70 fine-mapped studies at
this variant, and is not on the credible set. MAGMA gene-based tests aggregate
SNP-level statistics within a window around the gene body (often extending well
beyond the gene itself); a positive MAGMA signal near SLC2A1 in a broadly-defined
~550kb locus window is fully consistent with SLC2A1 sitting near a distinct,
independent association signal unrelated to the PTPRF-annotated credible set,
rather than being a true target of rs2842171. The sole positive genetic evidence
(PMID 40964458) is a single small exploratory MR+ML study with a CI lower bound
of 1.02 — barely distinguishable from the null, and not corroborated by any
large consortium GWAS. Working against it further: the one COPD-relevant
lung-tissue expression study directly contradicts a COPD-specific role (GLUT1
differs by airway region but *not* by COPD status). The PAH/RV-hypertrophy
rodent papers and the generic-immune-cell-glycolysis papers are thematically
adjacent at best. Absent any long-range regulatory evidence (Hi-C, ATAC,
chromatin-loop data linking the credible-set region to the SLC2A1 promoter) —
none was presented — there is no legitimate basis to keep SLC2A1 'in play' for
this specific credible set. I'd treat this as effectively an artifact of the
locus-window definition rather than a genuine candidate for the rs2842171
signal."

### ERMAP — CONTESTED
"Same physical/statistical problem as SLC2A1 (~500-730kb outside the locus,
zero L2G, zero COPD/lung genetic literature at all, not implicated by any
GWAS), compounded by an added interpretive risk: the ERMAP immune mechanism
(PMID 32620788, B7-family-like T-cell/macrophage coinhibitory molecule,
EAE/T1D/tumor contexts) is genuinely the most mechanistically vivid finding in
the whole set, and that is precisely what makes it dangerous in synthesis —
it's an attractive story (COPD has T-cell/macrophage dysregulation per prior
#3; ERMAP modulates T-cells/macrophages) built entirely on non-lung, non-COPD,
non-locus-specific data. The researcher already caveated this appropriately,
but I'd underscore for synthesis-lead: narrative plausibility here is doing
work that the genetics is not. This is a case where the *interesting* biology
and the *strong* genetics point at two different genes (ERMAP has the biology;
PTPRF has the genetics), and they should not be merged into a single
prioritized story. As currently framed, ERMAP should be considered effectively
ruled out for this specific credible set absent long-range regulatory evidence,
with the immune mechanism retained only as a speculative note, clearly labeled
as such."

## Gene marked WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning)

### PTPRF — WEAKENED (as a pulmonary-biology finding; the physical/statistical
case at the locus itself was not disputed)
"The physical/statistical case (dominant credible-set gene, 32/32 variants
annotated to it, top L2G=0.331 on the COPD-labeled study) is real and shouldn't
be dismissed. But the inference that this represents *pulmonary* biology is not
supported by anything presented: zero lung-specific structural evidence
(targeted searches returned literally zero hits), zero immune literature, and —
critically — the same fine-mapped COPD credible set colocalizes at H4 0.87-0.96
with five separate smoking-behavior traits (initiation, cessation, ever-smoker,
tobacco use disorder, age of initiation). That colocalization pattern is a
textbook signature of prior #1 (smoking-mediated), not prior #2. The one piece
of independent literature that names PTPRF specifically (PMID 37173343) is for
impulsive personality/drug-experimentation traits — behavioral, not
respiratory. Additional cross-disease pleiotropy (Alzheimer's cell-adhesion
pathway, reproductive traits) is more consistent with PTPRF being a large,
regulatorily promiscuous gene that turns up in many GWAS gene-lists than with
COPD-specific causal biology. Zero FEV1/FEV1-FVC evidence for PTPRF anywhere
(confirmed by both the researcher's targeted search and the Open Targets
negative check) is a meaningful absence given prior #2's emphasis on
spirometric concordance.

This doesn't mean PTPRF is the wrong gene at the locus — it may well be the
correct target of the regulatory variant. But the inference 'PTPRF is the
prioritized COPD gene' overreaches if left unqualified; it should be reframed
as 'PTPRF is the best-supported gene *physically* at this locus, but the
mechanistic evidence currently points toward smoking-behavior mediation rather
than direct pulmonary pathology, and this should not be treated as equivalent
to a lung-function-supported hit.' A concrete follow-up that would resolve this
either way and was not reported: GTEx/lung-eQTL check of whether rs2842171 (or
credible-set variants) is a PTPRF eQTL in lung tissue specifically vs.
brain/CNS tissue — this is the kind of tissue-specificity evidence that would
let synthesis-lead actually adjudicate prior #1 vs #2 here rather than
inferring it indirectly from trait colocalization."

## Process caveats (original review)

1. **Structural finding on candidate-gene physical location:** the researcher's
   region enumeration (via Ensembl REST API) found that both SLC2A1
   (chr1:42,925,353-42,958,904) and ERMAP (chr1:42,817,078-42,845,920) lie
   ~340-500kb outside the stated locus boundary (1:43,300,001-43,850,000) and
   ~615-755kb from the lead variant rs2842171 (1:43,574,179) — i.e., neither gene
   is physically within the region despite being MAGMA/PoPS-prioritized "for
   this locus." This is a material caveat that should inform interpretation of
   any locus where MAGMA/PoPS nominate a gene physically distant from the
   credible set. **This was later confirmed to be a genome-build error in the
   MAGMA/PoPS gene-location merge file (see re-opening addendum below).**

2. **Red-team-skeptic search-budget exhaustion:** the red-team-skeptic
   explicitly disclosed that all of its WebSearch calls (covering PTPRF+COPD,
   SLC2A1+COPD, ERMAP+COPD, rs2842171 colocalization, and ST3GAL3+COPD queries)
   returned "session search budget exhausted," meaning it was unable to run any
   independent contradiction search for any of the four genes discussed. Its
   critique is based entirely on re-analysis of the researcher's own reported
   findings and the Open Targets structured data supplied in the dispatch
   prompt (which itself functions as a substantial independent check, since it
   was generated before and agnostic to the researcher's narrative), not on
   fresh independent literature search. This should be weighed when assessing
   how much additional scrutiny this locus's critique received relative to
   loci where the skeptic had full search capacity.

3. **No FEV1/FEV1-FVC/lung-function evidence found anywhere** in this locus's
   review — not in Open Targets (structured lookup), not in the researcher's
   independent PubMed search, and not contradicted by the red-team-skeptic
   (who also found none, though under the search-budget constraint above).
   This absence, combined with strong colocalization between the COPD credible
   set and multiple smoking-behavior credible sets, is the primary basis for
   this locus being characterized as more likely smoking-mediated (prior #1)
   than pulmonary-biology-mediated (prior #2), per both the researcher's and
   skeptic's independent assessments.

## RE-OPENING ADDENDUM (2026-09-11): CDC20

### Background

The genome-build error noted in caveat #1 above (SLC2A1/ERMAP both ~340-730kb
outside the stated locus window) was traced to a bug in the MAGMA/PoPS
gene-location merge file used to build `loci/copd-all-gene-prioritization.txt`.
A corrected re-merge surfaced **CDC20** as a candidate gene for this locus that
was never evaluated in the original review. This locus was re-opened
specifically to evaluate CDC20 following the pipeline's standard process
(Open Targets check → researcher → citation-verifier → red-team-skeptic →
synthesis update).

### Independent physical-overlap check (orchestrator, via Open Targets/Ensembl)

CDC20's genomic coordinates (chr1:43,358,928-43,363,207, GRCh38, via Open
Targets `target.genomicLocation`) genuinely and unambiguously fall inside this
locus's stated window (1:43,300,001-43,850,000) — unlike SLC2A1 and ERMAP,
which sit entirely outside the window. CDC20 therefore could not be ruled out
at the physical-overlap-check stage the way SLC2A1/ERMAP could, and was sent
for full researcher work-up. However, CDC20 sits ~145kb upstream of the nearest
credible-set variant and ~211-266kb upstream of the lead variant rs2842171 and
the bulk of the PTPRF-annotated credible set — i.e., it overlaps the broad
locus window but not the credible-set span itself. A direct Open Targets query
of all 70 GWAS credible sets fine-mapped at this lead variant (including the
COPD-labeled study GCST90476027) found **zero L2G support for CDC20 in any of
them** — the same absence pattern previously found for SLC2A1 and ERMAP, though
CDC20's physical placement is more defensible than theirs. Full detail in
`evidence_tables/_status/1_43300001-43850000/opentargets.md` (re-opening
addendum section).

### Gene marked WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning)

"CDC20 is genuinely inside the stated 550kb locus window... which is a real
and important distinction from the SLC2A1/ERMAP build-bug artifacts... CDC20
cannot be dismissed that way. But 'inside a 550kb analyst-defined window' is a
weak form of positional evidence on its own... CDC20 sits 145kb from the
nearest credible-set variant and 211-266kb from the lead variant/bulk of the
credible set, entirely outside the credible-set span itself... So while CDC20
escapes the 'artifact' label, it does not escape the 'distant bystander gene in
a wide window' concern — it's a plausible candidate to *keep on the list*, not
one with a physical-proximity case comparable to PTPRF's.

Zero L2G support across all 70 fine-mapped GWAS studies at this lead variant —
not just the COPD study, but every trait Open Targets has fine-mapped here — is
a strong negative signal, because Open Targets' L2G model incorporates
eQTL/pQTL colocalization and chromatin-interaction features, not just distance.
A long-range regulatory link... despite the 145-266kb gap is exactly the kind
of thing L2G is designed to catch, and it caught nothing. Combined with the
COPD target-disease association score (0.0074, ~17x below PTPRF's 0.123, below
even SLC2A1's artifactual 0.064) and zero FEV1/FEV1-FVC evidence, the
statistical-genetics case for CDC20 at this locus is essentially null, not
merely thin.

The researcher's CDC20 literature is verified and is handled with unusual care
(correctly flagging the CDC20B/CDC20 mixup..., correctly noting the
non-smoking cohort..., correctly labeling the cannabis-methylation hit as
sub-epigenome-wide-significant) — this is good-faith, non-overclaiming
synthesis, not inflation of weak evidence. But nearly all of it is in IPF
(pulmonary fibrosis) and PAH (pulmonary arterial hypertension) models, plus
lung-cancer/toxicology cell biology, not COPD/emphysema... their core
pathophysiology... is mechanistically distinct from COPD/emphysema's
protease-antiprotease-driven alveolar destruction and airway
inflammation/remodeling. This is structurally the same problem flagged for
ERMAP's immune story in the original pass: literature that is
plausible-sounding for 'lung disease' in general but not COPD-specific. The one
genuinely interesting mechanistic thread — Cdc20 downregulation triggering
p53-dependent fibroblast senescence (PMID 35988650) — is at least thematically
resonant with COPD's known senescence/SASP biology, but this is presented with
no eQTL or expression-direction link back to the actual GWAS variants; it's a
hypothesis, not a demonstrated connection...

CDC20 is better characterized as a second 'narratively appealing but
statistically unsupported' candidate, in the same family as ERMAP's immune
story — but note it is not equivalent to ERMAP, since ERMAP was outright
outside the window (artifact) while CDC20 is genuinely inside it. The right
characterization is: CDC20 is 'inside the window, but outside the signal'...

**This should not change the final call.** Reaffirm PTPRF, confidence
MODERATE, unchanged. CDC20 does not rise to co-favorite or tie status... CDC20
doesn't resolve that ambiguity in either direction, it's simply a weaker
candidate on every axis measured."

**Verdict: CDC20 — WEAKENED** (per red-team-skeptic: real, verified mechanistic
literature exists, but it is disease-adjacent [IPF/PAH/cancer] rather than
COPD-specific, and the statistical/L2G/distance evidence argues against direct
involvement at this locus; do not promote above PTPRF).

### Process caveats (re-opening)

1. **Red-team-skeptic search-budget exhaustion (again):** exactly as in the
   original review, the red-team-skeptic disclosed that its WebSearch budget
   was exhausted before its CDC20 critique turn (both of its attempted queries
   — "CDC20 COPD emphysema lung function GWAS" and a locus/lead-variant-specific
   query — failed with "session search budget exhausted," reported as 200/200
   used). The CDC20 critique above is therefore based entirely on re-analysis
   of the researcher's reported findings, the citation-verifier's verdicts, and
   the Open Targets structured data supplied in the dispatch prompt, not on
   fresh independent literature search. The skeptic explicitly recommended that
   "if search budget becomes available again, a targeted CDC20 +
   COPD/lung-function/eQTL check should be run before this gene is closed out
   either way." This is flagged for any future re-opening of this locus.

2. **Lung-tissue eQTL check not available:** both the original PTPRF critique
   and this CDC20 critique independently converge on the same missing piece of
   evidence — a lung-tissue-specific eQTL check (GTEx or equivalent) at
   rs2842171/the credible-set variants, for both PTPRF and CDC20, vs.
   brain/CNS or other tissue. Neither gene's case can be fully adjudicated
   without this, and it is recommended as the highest-value follow-up for this
   locus should the study team wish to resolve the PTPRF MODERATE-confidence
   call further.

3. **CDC20B disambiguation:** PMID 21088493 concerns CDC20B, a distinct
   paralogous gene, not CDC20. See "Excluded citations" section above.
