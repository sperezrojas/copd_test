# Audit trail — locus 19:40,700,001-41,050,000 (CYP2A6)

This file contains everything excluded from the main evidence table
(`evidence_tables/19_40700001-41050000.md`), plus supporting transparency notes, so a
reviewer can see why. Nothing here should be treated as verified/actionable evidence
without independent review.

## Citation-verifier results: no exclusions on citation grounds

All 22 citations returned by the researcher for this locus were checked by
citation-verifier. **All 22 were VERIFIED** — no RETRACTED, NOT_FOUND, or CLAIM_MISMATCH
verdicts were returned. The four citations with unusually high PMIDs / recent 2025-2026
publication dates (PMID 42219484, 40834508, 41291804, 40693506) were specifically
double-checked by the verifier and confirmed as genuine, non-retracted PubMed records —
including PMID 42219484 (Giratallah et al. 2026), the single most important paper for this
locus's smoking-mediation conclusion. Full per-citation verifier evidence is in
`evidence_tables/_status/19_40700001-41050000/verified_citations.md`.

Because no citation was excluded on verification grounds, every exclusion below is driven
entirely by red-team-skeptic's gene-level CONTESTED verdicts, not by any citation-integrity
problem.

## Genome-build mismatch flag (context for the exclusions below)

The researcher's own Ensembl GRCh38 coordinate check found: using the stated locus window
19:40,700,001-41,050,000 (GRCh38), the genes that actually fall inside it are RAB4B-EGLN2,
SNRPA, MIA, MIA-RAB4B, EGLN2, RAB4B, ITPKC, COQ8B, CYP2A7, CYP2B6, CYP2A6, and ACTMAP. The
lead variant rs7251570 (19:40,835,845, GRCh38) sits inside CYP2A6 itself. By contrast,
**AKT2** (GRCh38: chr19:40,230,317-40,285,536) and **C19orf47** (GRCh38:
chr19:40,294,427-40,348,547) — the locus file's MAGMA- and PoPS-prioritized genes,
respectively — fall approximately 350-450 kb *outside* this GRCh38 window. Checking GRCh37
coordinates for the same gene symbols shows both genes *do* fall inside a
40,700,001-41,050,000 window under a GRCh37 reading (all shifted ~505-530 kb relative to
GRCh38 in this region), suggesting the internal MAGMA/PoPS gene-overlap step for this locus
may have used GRCh37 gene coordinates against what is labeled here as a GRCh38 window (or
some other build mismatch). This is the researcher's own coordinate arithmetic, not a
literature claim, and needs no citation-verifier check — but it directly informs why
red-team-skeptic treated AKT2 and C19orf47 as CONTESTED below.

## Excluded gene: EGLN2 (red-team-skeptic verdict: CONTESTED)

EGLN2 was added as a candidate gene by the researcher beyond the three originally listed,
because it genuinely falls inside the GRCh38 locus window immediately adjacent to CYP2A6,
and because multiple independent studies nominate it (not CYP2A6) as the functionally
causal COPD gene at this exact 19q13.2 locus. All of the following citations were
individually VERIFIED by citation-verifier; they are excluded from the main table because
red-team-skeptic contested the gene-level conclusion drawn from them, not because any
individual citation failed verification.

Supporting citations (all VERIFIED):
- Cho MH et al. Hum Mol Genet 2011;21(4):947-57. PMID 22080838 / DOI 10.1093/hmg/ddr524.
  Discovery GWAS: 19q13 locus (spanning RAB4B, EGLN2, MIA, CYP2A6) genome-wide significant
  for COPD; does not distinguish EGLN2 from CYP2A6.
- Bloom AJ et al. Hum Mol Genet 2013;23(2):555-61. PMID 24045616 / DOI 10.1093/hmg/ddt432.
  Joint CYP2A6/EGLN2 analysis: the EGLN2 variant rs3733829 is independently associated with
  cigarettes-per-day and exhaled carbon monoxide even though it shows no independent
  nicotine-metabolism effect, implying at least two distinct causal mechanisms at this LD
  block.
- Lamontagne M et al. PLoS One 2013;8(7):e70220. PMID 23936167 / DOI
  10.1371/journal.pone.0070220. Lung eQTL analysis of the 19q13 locus found the strongest,
  replicated lung cis-eQTLs were with EGLN2, not CYP2A6; the authors nominate EGLN2 as "the
  most likely causal COPD gene" at 19q13 based on lung tissue expression data.
- Nedeljkovic I et al. Hum Mol Genet 2018;27(2):396-405. PMID 29092026 / DOI
  10.1093/hmg/ddx390. The 19q13.2 COPD GWAS variant rs7937 is associated with differential
  blood DNA methylation at 4 CpGs independent of smoking, with one CpG (near EGLN2) also
  associated with COPD; the variant is further associated with EGLN2 expression in blood
  (partly methylation-mediated) and with trans-effects on NUMBL, DNMT3A, and PAK2
  expression in lung tissue.
- Castaldi PJ et al. Am J Respir Crit Care Med 2014;190(4):399-409. PMID 25006744 / DOI
  10.1164/rccm.201403-0569OC. COPDGene GWAS of CT-quantified emphysema subtypes reconfirms
  the CYP2A6/EGLN2 locus among five loci previously associated with COPD susceptibility,
  with enhancer enrichment for GWAS hits predominantly in lung fibroblasts; does not
  adjudicate between CYP2A6 and EGLN2.

**Red-team-skeptic's reasoning, preserved verbatim:**

"This is the gene I'd push back on hardest. The researcher calls it 'a genuinely strong
alternative-gene candidate,' but every citation supporting it (1, 5, 6, 7, 10) is
GWAS/eQTL/methylation-association evidence from the same LD block — there is, by the
researcher's own admission, no EGLN2-specific functional/mechanistic lung study; the
HIF/hypoxia-response rationale is generic pathway biology bolted on after the fact, not
EGLN2-specific pulmonary data. That is the identical evidentiary tier as the CYP2A6 case
(statistical/eQTL, not functional), so calling EGLN2 'genuinely strong' while treating
CYP2A6's comparable-tier evidence more skeptically is an inconsistent evidentiary bar —
overclaiming by contrast.

The 'independent of smoking' claim also needs scrutiny. Citation 5 (Bloom 2013) shows the
EGLN2 variant is associated with cigarettes/day and breath CO — both are smoking-
behavior/exposure phenotypes, not lung-structural phenotypes. The paper's finding is only
that this signal is independent of the nicotine-metabolism mechanism specifically (i.e.,
it's not proxying CYP2A6's metabolic pathway) — it is not independent of smoking-behavior
mediation generally. Only citation 7 (Nedeljkovic 2018 — blood methylation at an
EGLN2-proximal CpG associated with COPD independent of smoking) offers genuine
smoking-independence evidence, and that is a single blood-methylation study, not lung
tissue, which is a meaningful tissue-specificity caveat the researcher doesn't foreground.

There's also a discrepancy with the structured context worth flagging explicitly: across
30 credible sets at this locus in Open Targets, CYP2A6 is the only gene with any non-null
L2G score in every set, and EGLN2 doesn't appear in the aggregate score summary at all. The
researcher's own note that 'no lung-tissue colocalization was tested in Open Targets'
catalog for this locus' is a fair explanation (absence of testing, not absence of effect),
but it means EGLN2's entire case currently rests on a handful of older,
non-Open-Targets-replicated academic eQTL/methylation papers rather than on modern
statistical fine-mapping/coloc evidence, and that gap should be surfaced to synthesis-lead
rather than smoothed over by 'genuinely strong.'

Verdict: CONTESTED — not because I found a paper arguing against EGLN2 (I didn't get to
search), but because the researcher's own evidence base doesn't support the confidence of
'genuinely strong alternative candidate': it is same-tier eQTL/statistical evidence as
CYP2A6, largely tangled with the same smoking-behavior phenotypes (cigarettes/day, breath
CO) the pipeline is supposed to be suspicious of, with zero EGLN2-specific functional
validation and no modern coloc/L2G corroboration in the structured data provided. This
should be reported to synthesis-lead as 'plausible, statistically visible, but not more
solidly established than CYP2A6' rather than as a clear front-runner."

## Excluded gene: AKT2 (red-team-skeptic verdict: CONTESTED)

AKT2 is the PoPS-prioritized gene in the original locus input file. No locus-specific GWAS,
TWAS, colocalization, or Mendelian-randomization study was found by the researcher. The
following VERIFIED citations found are all non-lung, non-COPD general immune-signaling
papers:
- Wang Z et al. Biochim Biophys Acta Mol Basis Dis 2024;1871(1):167554. PMID 39471914 /
  DOI 10.1016/j.bbadis.2024.167554. Mouse sepsis-induced acute lung injury: EZH2 promotes
  M1 macrophage polarization via an AKTIP-AKT2 signaling axis.
- Wang Z et al. Mol Immunol 2025;186:95-113. PMID 40834508 / DOI
  10.1016/j.molimm.2025.08.010. Sepsis-associated-encephalopathy mouse model: EZH2-AKT2
  axis in microglial polarization.
- Korner A et al. Cell Commun Signal 2025;23(1):523. PMID 41291804 / DOI
  10.1186/s12964-025-02518-z. Murine peritonitis model: AKT2 phosphorylation implicated
  (alongside mTOR) in macrophage metabolic reprogramming during inflammation resolution.

**Red-team-skeptic's reasoning, preserved verbatim:**

"Independent of the immune-biology critique (which the researcher already got right —
sepsis-ALI mouse macrophage, sepsis encephalopathy microglia, and peritonitis macrophage
papers are non-lung, non-COPD, and largely interchangeable 'AKT2 is a generic kinase in
inflammation-resolution pathways' findings that could be recruited to argue for almost any
inflammatory disease), the build-mismatch flag is the dominant issue here and the
researcher's synthesis doesn't foreground it enough. If AKT2 physically falls
~350-450kb outside the stated GRCh38 window, its PoPS prioritization for this locus may be
a coordinate-system artifact from an upstream pipeline step, not a genuine
functional-similarity signal to this locus's credible set. Combined with zero
locus-specific genetic evidence (no GWAS/TWAS/coloc/MR hit at all — Open Targets aggregate
score 0.0044, literature-only) and its LoF-constrained status carrying no locus-specific
weight without a genetic anchor, this gene's presence in the candidate list at all should be
treated as suspect pending build reconciliation.

Verdict: CONTESTED — the researcher's 'low-priority candidate' framing is too soft; given
the apparent build mismatch this gene may not belong in this locus's candidate set at all,
and synthesis-lead should treat AKT2's inclusion as provisional pending confirmation that
PoPS was run on GRCh38 coordinates for this locus."

## Excluded gene: C19orf47 (red-team-skeptic verdict: CONTESTED)

C19orf47 is the MAGMA-prioritized gene in the original locus input file. The only citation
found (VERIFIED) is unrelated to lung/COPD biology:
- Wang Z et al. Ann Clin Transl Neurol 2025;12(10):2116-2129. PMID 40693506 / DOI
  10.1002/acn3.70125. Describes a cis-splicing-derived C19orf47-AKT2 chimeric RNA highly
  expressed in glioblastoma tissue, associated with prognosis and tumor-cell proliferation
  in vitro — a glioma-specific oncology finding with no apparent connection to pulmonary or
  immune biology.

**Red-team-skeptic's reasoning, preserved verbatim:**

"Same build-mismatch problem as AKT2, and even weaker literature support: the single
citation (a glioblastoma-specific C19orf47-AKT2 chimeric-RNA paper) has no lung or immune
relevance whatsoever, and the Open Targets aggregate COPD association score is exactly 0
(no evidence in any category). The researcher's 'essentially no supporting literature in
any domain' is accurate and not an overclaim — if anything this is the one gene in the set
where the researcher's characterization is appropriately deflationary.

Verdict: CONTESTED — for the same build-coordinate reason as AKT2 (likely an artifact of
the upstream MAGMA gene-assignment step using mismatched genome build), compounded by a
complete absence of any genuine biological evidence. Synthesis-lead should treat this as
very likely a false positive from a coordinate error rather than a real candidate requiring
further weighing."

## Non-excluded caveat: CYP2A6 marked WEAKENED (not CONTESTED — kept in main table, flagged here for transparency)

Red-team-skeptic did not exclude CYP2A6, but assigned it a WEAKENED verdict rather than
accepting the researcher's synthesis at face value. Preserved verbatim:

"The researcher's synthesis ('largely, though perhaps not completely, mediated through
smoking quantity/nicotine intake') is appropriately hedged and is the one place in this
dossier where the claim-to-evidence match is tight: citation 11 (Giratallah 2026, two-step
mediated MR with a negative-control reverse-MR) and citation 12 (Khouja 2024, multivariable
MR) are exactly the right study design to test smoking-mediation, and both point the same
direction. This is not overclaimed — if anything it is conservatively stated.

Two things weaken the case further than the researcher credited, however. First, the Open
Targets structured context shows the high-confidence colocalization (H4 0.98-0.996) for
CYP2A6 is with a LIVER transcript-usage QTL for nicotine-metabolite-ratio and
COPD/airway-obstruction signals — liver is exactly where you'd expect a smoking/nicotine-
pharmacokinetic mechanism to colocalize, and no lung-tissue coloc was even tested. This is
independent structural evidence reinforcing the smoking-mediation interpretation rather
than a lung-intrinsic one; the researcher's synthesis undersells how well this fits.
Second, citation 17 (Rosser 2025) — upregulated CYP2A6 in pediatric nasal epithelium tied
to PM2.5 exposure and Th2/allergic markers, independent of active smoking — is not really
evidence FOR a COPD-relevant lung-intrinsic CYP2A6 mechanism; it demonstrates CYP2A6 is a
generic xenobiotic-response gene inducible by any airway pollutant, in a Th2/allergic-
asthma context that is mechanistically distinct from COPD's typically
neutrophilic/CD8/innate-immune profile (Prior 3). The researcher lists this finding but
doesn't flag that it argues for nonspecificity rather than for a COPD-relevant mechanism —
a mild case of the evidence being more equivocal than the one-line framing suggests.

Verdict: WEAKENED — not because the smoking-mediation conclusion is wrong (it looks
right), but because the researcher frames CYP2A6 as still having a residual 'not
completely' mediated lung-intrinsic component, when the strongest tissue-resolved evidence
available (liver coloc, MR mediation, pediatric non-smoking induction data that is itself
disease-nonspecific) points toward smoking/xenobiotic-metabolism mediation being close to
the whole story, with no clean lung-structural or COPD-specific-immune data actually
demonstrating a direct pulmonary mechanism."

## Search-coverage disclosure (from red-team-skeptic)

The skeptic disclosed that its own independent contradiction-search attempt failed
entirely because the session's web-search budget was already exhausted (200/200) before
any of its four planned queries ran. Its critique above is therefore based on internal
analysis of the researcher's own verified citations, the Open Targets structured context,
and the stated biological priors — not on new literature the skeptic found itself. This is
a genuine coverage gap, not a "search was done and found nothing" result, and is most
relevant to (a) EGLN2's causal case, and (b) confirming/refuting the AKT2/C19orf47
genome-build mismatch by an independent means.

## Cross-cutting points for the study team (from red-team-skeptic, preserved verbatim)

"1. Build-mismatch is the single highest-value flag in this whole dossier. Two of four
candidate genes (AKT2, C19orf47 — the MAGMA and PoPS top picks) apparently only fall in
this locus's window under GRCh37, not the stated GRCh38 window. This should be resolved
(confirm which build MAGMA/PoPS were actually run on) before either gene is weighted in
synthesis; if this is a genuine error it may mean this locus's originally-reported top
statistical-prioritization genes were never real candidates.
2. CYP2A6 vs. EGLN2 should not be synthesized as 'CYP2A6 confirmed, EGLN2 plausible
alternative.' Both rest on comparable-tier eQTL/statistical evidence entangled with
smoking-behavior phenotypes; CYP2A6 has the edge in structured-data corroboration (L2G
presence across all 30 credible sets, high-confidence coloc) but that coloc is
liver-based and supports smoking/pharmacokinetic mediation, not a lung-intrinsic mechanism
for either gene. The honest synthesis is: this locus is most likely
smoking/nicotine-metabolism-mediated (Prior 1 strongly applies), with the
CYP2A6-vs-EGLN2 question genuinely unresolved and probably secondary to that larger point.
3. No independent contradiction-search was actually completed for any of the four genes
due to search-budget exhaustion — this should be treated as an open task, not as 'search
was done and found nothing,' particularly for EGLN2's causal claim and for the
AKT2/C19orf47 build question, both of which are checkable facts (gene coordinates,
additional EGLN2 functional papers) rather than judgment calls."
