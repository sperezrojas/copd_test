# Audit trail (excluded / contested) — Locus 3:71,300,001-71,850,000 (FOXP1)

This file preserves what was excluded from the evidence table, and why, plus all
CONTESTED/WEAKENED reasoning from the red-team-skeptic, verbatim where possible, so a
reviewer can trace every judgment call.

## Excluded citations (verdict != VERIFIED)

**1. Sakornsakolpat P, Prokopenko D, Lamontagne M, et al. "Genetic landscape of chronic
obstructive pulmonary disease identifies heterogeneous cell-type and phenotype
associations." Nature Genetics, 2019. PMID 30804561; DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0342-2.**
- Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH
- Reason (citation-verifier, verbatim): "According to PubMed full text (PMC6546635), paper
  identifies 82 COPD loci and 156 target genes using multiple annotation approaches. FOXP1
  is NOT explicitly named in the main text as one of the 82 identified loci or 156 target
  genes. Abstract states 'We identified 82 loci...156 genes' but supplementary tables (not
  accessed) would be needed to confirm FOXP1 presence. Researcher could not confirm from
  abstract alone -- neither can external verification from main text."
- Disposition: Excluded from the evidence table. This is a real gap in the evidence base —
  the field's flagship COPD gene-prioritization GWAS paper could NOT be confirmed to name
  FOXP1 at all. The red-team-skeptic flagged this prominently: "This is a conspicuous gap
  for what should be the single most direct piece of supporting evidence, and it should be
  flagged prominently rather than absorbed silently into 'COPD-GWAS background.'" Should
  not be cited as direct support for FOXP1 unless/until supplementary tables are checked.

## Genes/claims marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning preserved)

Only one candidate gene exists at this locus (FOXP1), so nothing is CONTESTED at the
gene-identity level. The following specific CLAIMS about FOXP1 were marked CONTESTED or
required significant downgrading:

**CONTESTED: "This locus's phenotypic effect is lung/COPD-specific."**
> "This isn't a case of a new paper arguing against FOXP1 (no new external contradicting
> citation to add), but the dossier's own structured colocalization data is internally in
> tension with a lung-first narrative -- heart failure h4=0.981 is stronger than FEV1/FVC
> by a wide margin, and Liu 2022/Wang 2025 (both verified) independently establish a
> robust, mechanistically distinct cardiac role for Foxp1, while Lozano 2021 establishes an
> independent neurodevelopmental (FOXP1-syndrome) role from rare coding LoF -- a different
> variant class entirely. I mark the sub-claim 'this locus's phenotypic effect is
> lung/COPD-specific' as CONTESTED (contested by the dossier's own multi-trait
> colocalization pattern, not by an external paper), while the broader 'FOXP1 is a real
> gene with pleiotropic cardiovascular/immune/neurodevelopmental biology' is SUPPORTED and
> should be stated plainly in synthesis rather than downplayed."

**CONTESTED/unresolved: "Primary COPD-GWAS gene list (Sakornsakolpat) explicitly names
FOXP1."**
> "CLAIM_MISMATCH per verifier, should not be cited as direct support." (See excluded
> citation above.)

## WEAKENED verdicts (not excluded, but downgraded — reasoning preserved)

**"FOXP1 causal for COPD specifically via a lung-intrinsic mechanism at rs6796042" — WEAKENED**
> "At the exact lead variant, COPD-credible-set colocalization with FEV1 (h4=0.391) and FVC
> (h4=0.265) sits well below the conventional h4>0.8 'confident' bar, while colocalization
> with non-lung-function traits at the same variant is uniformly strong (asthma/COPD-overlap
> 0.916, Crohn's 0.965, heart failure 0.981, ADHD 0.741, skin cancers 0.75-0.98). The L2G
> score itself is uninformative here (0.78-0.86 for essentially every trait tested, because
> FOXP1 is simply the only gene near this variant) so it cannot be used to argue
> lung-specificity. Taken together, the case for 'FOXP1 acts on COPD through pulmonary
> biology at this variant' is WEAKENED -- the structured genetic evidence at this specific
> signal actively argues against strong lung-intrinsic mediation, or at minimum argues for
> shared/overlapping mediation with asthma rather than COPD/emphysema per se."

**"Gene-body (Andreas/Shrine) lung-function evidence as proof of mechanism at this specific
credible set" — WEAKENED**
> "Andreas et al.'s sentinel FEV1/FVC variants (rs1499894, rs35480566) are explicitly
> different from this credible set's lead (rs6796042), and FOXP1 is a large gene (~600kb)
> that plausibly harbors multiple independent, non-LD-linked regulatory signals (allelic
> heterogeneity is common at large TF genes). No LD/conditional analysis between rs6796042
> and Andreas's sentinels is presented anywhere in the dossier. Citing 'gene-level'
> lung-function association from a different signal as support for the causal mechanism at
> *this* signal -- especially when *this* signal's own direct FEV1/FVC colocalization is
> weak -- overstates what's been shown. WEAKENED: legitimate as background that
> FOXP1-the-gene is lung-function-relevant somewhere in its body, illegitimate as direct
> support for this locus's mechanism without an LD check the researcher didn't do."

**Maloy et al. 2025 bioRxiv preprint as mechanistic support — WEAKENED**
> "This should not be leaned on as confirmatory mechanism. It's non-peer-reviewed, and more
> importantly it's about FBXO24 (a different gene entirely) controlling FoxP1 protein
> stability and UPR after cigarette-smoke exposure -- it is at best indirect,
> hypothesis-generating background about FoxP1 protein turnover in smoke-exposed epithelium,
> not evidence about the GWAS variant/regulatory mechanism at this locus. Using it to
> bolster a 'lung-intrinsic, smoke-responsive FOXP1 mechanism' narrative overstates its
> evidentiary weight."

**"Not smoking-mediated" — WEAKENED**
> "The 'no smoking-mediation' conclusion rests on an absence (smoking traits didn't appear
> among 75 colocalizing traits, no PubMed hit) rather than a confirmed null; Liu et al.
> 2019's supplementary tables were never checked. Treat as WEAKENED support for 'not
> smoking-mediated,' not a confirmed answer."

**Immune mechanism specificity — WEAKENED**
> "Immune evidence overall (CD8 quiescence, B-cell GC/class-switch, Runx1-Th17 correlative
> link) is broad and multi-programmatic, exactly as the researcher concluded -- I concur
> this is WEAKENED as COPD-specific evidence, useful only as general 'FOXP1 is
> immunologically active' background."

**Other gaps flagged by red-team-skeptic (verbatim):**
> "No stated direction-of-effect: nothing in the dossier ties the COPD-risk allele at
> rs6796042 to a specific direction of FOXP1 expression change (up or down). Andreas et
> al.'s in vitro claim (lower FoxP1 -> worse smoke-injury phenotype) is directionally
> suggestive but not linked back to which allele of rs6796042 raises or lowers FOXP1
> expression. This is a load-bearing gap for any causal mechanistic story and should be
> surfaced to synthesis-lead as unresolved."
>
> "Bahr et al. 2013 (PBMC expression, COPDGene validation) is correlative disease-state
> expression data in already-diagnosed patients -- vulnerable to reverse
> causation/smoking-exposure confounding of immune cell expression, not germline-variant
> evidence. Should not be treated as strong causal support, consistent with researcher's
> own framing."
>
> "Pniewska et al. 2015 actually cuts against COPD-specificity: FOXP1 upregulation on
> allergen challenge was reported in asthmatics, not COPD (COPD PBMCs showed a different
> gene signature). This is a small but real data point *for* an asthma-skewed
> interpretation of this locus rather than classic neutrophilic-COPD biology."

## Search-coverage limitation disclosed by red-team-skeptic (verbatim)

> "Before critiquing: I attempted two targeted contradiction-searches for FOXP1
> (rs6796042/COPD colocalization; FOXP1 pleiotropy across COPD/asthma/heart-failure) and
> both failed -- the session's web-search budget (200/200) was already exhausted before I
> could run them. FOXP1 therefore received zero independent search coverage from me.
> Everything below is a critique of the dossier as given, not confirmed or refuted by new
> search. Flag this gap explicitly to the synthesis-lead: if search budget can be raised,
> FOXP1 (as the front-runner / only gene at this locus) should get the contradiction pass
> it didn't get here."

This is an explicit, acknowledged limitation of this locus's review: the red-team critique
was performed as a desk review of the researcher's dossier rather than with independent
literature search, because the skeptic's search budget was already exhausted when it was
dispatched (this was the third and last subagent dispatch in this locus's pipeline, after
the researcher and citation-verifier had already used search calls in the same session
budget). A future re-run of the red-team stage with fresh search budget could add
independent verification or contradiction that this review does not have.
