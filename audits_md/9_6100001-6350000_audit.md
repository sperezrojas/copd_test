# Audit appendix — locus 9:6,100,001-6,350,000 (nearest gene IL33)

This appendix records every citation excluded from the final evidence table
(`evidence_tables/9_6100001-6350000.md`), and every gene/claim the red-team-skeptic marked
CONTESTED, with reasoning preserved verbatim from the citation-verifier and red-team-skeptic
subagents. It exists so a reviewer can see what was found and rejected/qualified, not just
what survived.

## Excluded citations (CLAIM_MISMATCH)

### 1. Li H, Nadkarni GN, Powell CA, Kraft M, Li X. "Genome-wide Association Study Identifies IL1RL1 and IL33 Associated With Asthma-Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease Overlap in the All of Us Research Program." Allergy Asthma Immunol Res. 2026. PMID 41592539 / PMC12865170 / DOI 10.4168/aair.2026.18.1.67.

**Researcher's original claim:** dedicated ACO (asthma-COPD overlap) GWAS meta-analysis
identifies an IL33 variant rs2428305 (OR 1.16, P=4.49×10⁻⁹) associated with ACO;
distinguishes "asthma candidate genes" from "COPD candidate genes"; states ACO patients
had highest blood eosinophil counts and were predominantly smokers vs. asthma-only
patients predominantly non-smokers.

**Citation-verifier verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH.** "rs2428305 is located in KIAA1217, NOT
IL33. The paper's abstract states 'rs2428305 (OR, 1.16; P = 4.49 × 10⁻⁸) in KIAA1217
associated with ACO,' not IL33. The P-value in the researcher's claim (4.49×10⁻⁹) also
differs from the verified value (4.49×10⁻⁸). The researcher's claim that this paper
identifies 'an IL33 variant rs2428305' associated with ACO is factually incorrect as to
gene assignment — this specific variant/finding does NOT support an IL33-ACO genetic
association."

**Red-team-skeptic's assessment:** "The corrected fact is stark: the researcher's
originally-cited variant (rs2428305) sits in KIAA1217, not IL33, and even the P-value was
misstated (10⁻⁸, not 10⁻⁹). That means citation #4 provides zero direct evidence for an
IL33-ACO genetic association — it is actually evidence for a different gene (KIAA1217) at
what may be a different locus entirely. The paper's title mentioning 'IL1RL1 and IL33'
keeps the door open that IL33 is discussed elsewhere in that paper, but as verified, this
specific data point cannot be used to support IL33's ACO role. The researcher's synthesis
should not cite Li 2026 as IL33 evidence at all going forward." However, the skeptic notes
this does not collapse the broader ACO-comorbidity hypothesis for IL33, which survives on
other verified evidence (Open Targets L2G pattern, Ketelaar 2020 eosinophilic-asthma
association) — see the CONTESTED discussion under IL33 below.

**Disposition:** Fully excluded from the evidence table. Not re-cited anywhere in the
final synthesis as IL33 evidence.

### 2. Vernisse C, Tuaillon E, Suehs C, Gras D, Bedin AS, Charriot J, Knabe L, Vachier I, Chanez P, Petit A, Bourdin A. "Airway epithelial type-2 alarmin profiles: Blood eosinophil counts remain in memory." Eur J Immunol. 2023. PMID 36793156 / DOI 10.1002/eji.202250101.

**Researcher's original claim:** in air-liquid-interface cultures from COPD, asthma, and
control airway epithelium, IL-33 was only "sparsely detected" across all groups, whereas
IL-25 AND TSLP showed clearer disease-associated patterns; COPD cultures showed a "mixed"
T1/T2 signature.

**Citation-verifier verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH (partial).** "IL-33 confirmed as 'sparsely
detected' across all groups, and COPD showed mixed T1/T2 signature confirmed. However,
TSLP levels were 'similar among groups' (no disease-associated difference), contradicting
the claim that IL-25 AND TSLP show 'clearer disease-associated patterns.' Only IL-25
showed disease specificity."

**Disposition:** NOT fully excluded — this citation is retained in the evidence table
because the IL-33-sparse-detection and COPD-mixed-T1/T2-signature portions of the claim
were verified as accurate. Only the TSLP sub-claim was struck; the table row has been
corrected to state that only IL-25 (not IL-25 and TSLP) showed a disease-associated
pattern, per the red-team-skeptic's explicit instruction ("Item 20 correction... this is
a minor but real overclaim; correct before finalizing").

## Genes/claims marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic

### IL33 — CONTESTED (as a COPD-specific causal mechanism; NOT contested as the correct
gene relative to GTF3AP1/TPD52L3 at this locus)

Verbatim skeptic verdict: "The gene assignment at this locus (IL33 over GTF3AP1/TPD52L3)
is sound and effectively HOLDS on its own — PoPS, L2G dominance across 111 credible sets,
and a deep mechanistic literature all converge on IL33 as the functional gene physically/
regulatorily implicated. But the specific causal claim that this locus drives
COPD-specific pulmonary pathobiology is contested by: (a) a corrected citation that
removes an ACO-specific data point entirely (#4, now attributable to KIAA1217); (b)
uniformly negative verified-literature FEV1/FEV1-FVC associations, with the only positive
spirometric signal being an unverified database score; (c) a negative 12-gene/78-study
COPD candidate-gene meta-analysis (#9) and another negative case-control study (#11)
against one small, unreplicated positive (#10); (d) directly opposing directions of
effect in emphysema mouse models (#16 protective vs. #17 pathogenic) that remain
unreconciled; and (e) a phase 2a trial whose primary endpoint failed and whose subgroup
benefit was confined to former smokers, suggesting smoking-history-dependent modulation
rather than a clean COPD mechanism. The strongest honest characterization is: IL33 is very
likely the correct gene at this locus, and it plausibly acts through an eosinophilic/
Th2-COPD-endotype and ACO-comorbidity pathway, but the case for a distinct,
smoking-independent COPD-causal mechanism is weaker than the researcher's assembled item
list might suggest at a glance, and contains genuine unresolved contradictions rather than
merely gaps."

Specific contested/unresolved sub-points, preserved verbatim from the skeptic:

1. **Asthma/Th2 spillover vs. COPD-specific biology.** "The weight of evidence, as
   assembled, favors [asthma/Th2 spillover] over a distinct-COPD-pathobiology story... The
   Open Targets L2G pattern is about as unambiguous as this kind of database evidence
   gets: 111 indexed credible sets at this exact position, IL33 the dominant L2G gene in
   nearly all, and the overwhelming majority are asthma, eosinophil count, allergic
   rhinitis, nasal polyps, rhinosinusitis, wheeze — not COPD. Zero pure COPD case-control
   studies appear in that set. The best 'COPD' signal is a combined FinnGen 'Asthma/COPD'
   phenotype, which is exactly the kind of loosely-phenotyped, comorbidity-conflated
   endpoint prior #1/#3 warns about." Also: "the actual verified-literature spirometry
   record for IL33 is uniformly negative; the only positive spirometric signal is
   unverified [Open Targets database score]. Treating the OT lung-function L2G score as
   equivalent in weight to the three negative verified papers would be an overclaim in the
   wrong direction."

2. **Emphysema mouse-model contradiction (Morichika 2021 vs. Gimenes 2019), unresolved.**
   "IL-33's direction of effect in emphysema/COPD models is context-dependent and
   unresolved — protective at homeostatic baseline (item 16, genetic knockout, chronic
   elastase/CSE), pathogenic during viral-triggered exacerbation (item 17, ST2 blockade).
   This maps suggestively onto the itepekimab trial's former-vs-current-smoker split and
   onto a stage/context-dependent model of IL-33 biology, but that mapping is an
   inference, not something established in the cited papers — it should be labeled a
   hypothesis, not a finding."

3. **Itepekimab trial's former-vs-current-smoker split.** "Its clinical translation is
   telling: no all-comers benefit, benefit confined to former smokers only, none in
   current smokers. That is consistent with — and arguably better explained by —
   smoking-modulated IL-33 biology... than with a clean, smoking-independent COPD
   mechanism. This is an effect-modification-by-smoking-status story, which sits closer to
   prior #1's concern than to a 'distinct COPD biology' conclusion."

4. **Missing alternative explanation flagged by the skeptic (not previously surfaced by
   the researcher):** "COPD-GWAS case ascertainment (UK Biobank self-report/ICD-based,
   ICGC) is known to have substantial COPD-asthma misclassification, especially among
   older ever-smokers with partially reversible obstruction. Given that no pure COPD
   case-control study appears among the 111 top-L2G-IL33 credible sets, and the strongest
   'COPD-adjacent' phenotype is a combined Asthma/COPD FinnGen endpoint, phenotypic
   misclassification/comorbidity-driven signal is a live alternative to genuine
   independent COPD pathobiology."

### GTF3AP1 — HOLDS (correctly deprioritized), with a preserved caveat

Verbatim skeptic verdict: "HOLDS (as a 'correctly deprioritized' call), with the caveat
that this rests on an absence-of-evidence/relative-plausibility argument, not a
dispositive colocalization study distinguishing it from IL33. Synthesis should preserve
this uncertainty in wording rather than stating GTF3AP1 is definitively excluded." The
evidence is a classic absence-of-evidence argument (zero PubMed hits, zero Open Targets
disease associations, confirmed processed-pseudogene biotype) stacked against a large
convergent positive case for IL33; this is a legitimately strong relative-plausibility
argument but not a proven, dispositive result.

### TPD52L3 — HOLDS (correctly and trivially ruled out)

No prioritization-method signal, no credible-set variant assigned, no lung/COPD
literature found. Not seriously contested by the skeptic; included here only for
completeness.

## Disclosed methodological limitation (from red-team-skeptic)

The red-team-skeptic explicitly disclosed that its web-search budget was exhausted before
any of its own independent verification searches (KIAA1217/rs2428305 re-confirmation, IL33
COPD Mendelian-randomization literature, 9p24.1 colocalization/Hi-C studies, recent IL-33
monoclonal antibody COPD trial updates) could execute, returning zero results for all four
attempted queries. The skeptic performed zero independent literature searches of its own
for any of the three candidate genes (IL33, GTF3AP1, TPD52L3); its entire critique is a
critical re-analysis of the researcher's and citation-verifier's existing record, not an
independently re-searched check. This gap (no fresh search for a possible more recent
IL-33 COPD phase 3 trial readout, no independent colocalization/Hi-C study search) is
unresolved in this review and should be closed in a future pass if warranted.
