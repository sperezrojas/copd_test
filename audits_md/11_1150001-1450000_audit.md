## Excluded citations

**CLAIM_MISMATCH — Guzman-Vargas et al., "Differential Genomic Profile..." Front Med (Lausanne) 2021**

- Reported by researcher as: "Differential Genomic Profile in TOLLIP, MUC5B and DSP Between COPD
  Patients With Emphysema, IPF, and CPFE Syndrome," PMID 34490311, DOI 10.3389/fmed.2021.725144,
  claimed to show a DSP SNP (rs2609255) associated with both IPF and COPD/emphysema risk.
- Citation-verifier finding: the paper exists at this PMID/DOI and is not retracted, but its actual
  title is "Differential Genomic Profile in TERT, DSP, and FAM13A Between COPD Patients With
  Emphysema, IPF, and CPFE Syndrome" — the genes studied are TERT, DSP, and FAM13A, not TOLLIP,
  MUC5B, and DSP as the researcher reported. The rs2609255 variant discussed in the actual paper is
  a FAM13A variant, not DSP as claimed.
- Disposition: excluded from the evidence table entirely. Not usable as TOLLIP or MUC5B evidence at
  this locus. No corrected/replacement citation was independently sought in this run — if this
  locus's pleiotropy discussion is revisited, note that TERT/FAM13A (not TOLLIP/MUC5B) are the
  correct subjects of this paper's genotype-phenotype findings, and it may be worth reviewing on its
  own terms as TERT/FAM13A evidence rather than discarding outright, but that is out of scope for
  this locus's candidate-gene list (MUC5B/BRSK2/TOLLIP).

## Red-team-skeptic critique (verbatim, from evidence_tables/_status/11_1150001-1450000/critique.md)

### GENE: MUC5B — WEAKENED

The statistical case for MUC5B is genuinely strong on its own terms: PIP=0.943 for a missense
variant, highest VEP consequence score, and the top L2G score (0.778) against a lung-disease-relevant
UK Biobank trait that colocalizes with an independent lung-disease-diagnosis GWAS. This is not an
LD/nearest-gene-bias problem in the usual sense — the fine-mapping and coloc evidence do distinguish
MUC5B from its neighbors at the variant level.

The problem the researcher itself flagged, and which I confirm independently, is that essentially
none of the human disease literature marshaled for MUC5B is actually about rs2943516. Roy (mouse
knockout), Van Buren (chronic bronchitis pQTL — different variant, rs140324259), Hobbs (ILA/IPF),
Yun (clonal mutations), Baddour (air pollution modifier) — all either use different variants
(overwhelmingly the promoter variant rs35705950) or are non-human/non-genetic mechanism. My own
search for rs2943516-specific functional or association literature (dbSNP report, general searches)
turned up nothing beyond a bare dbSNP entry — confirming the researcher's finding that this exact
lead variant is functionally uncharacterized. Most importantly, Goos et al. (PMID 35651878, VERIFIED)
explicitly shows the best-characterized MUC5B variant (rs35705950) is NOT associated with COPD and is
"very specific" to pulmonary fibrosis — directly undercutting any assumption that "MUC5B = COPD-
relevant mucin biology" transfers cleanly across variants at this locus. The inference "MUC5B is the
causal gene, and MUC5B mucociliary/chronic-bronchitis biology (Roy, Van Buren) explains the COPD
signal" overreaches: it borrows mechanistic plausibility from a different variant with a demonstrated
non-COPD (IPF-specific) phenotype, and applies it to a coding variant with no direct functional data.
The gene-level statistical evidence (L2G, coloc, VEP) holds up; the mechanistic narrative built on
Roy/Van Buren/Hobbs/Yun is real MUC5B biology but not proven to be this variant's mechanism.
Additionally, the CLAIM_MISMATCH citation (Guzman-Vargas, actually about TERT/DSP/FAM13A, not
MUC5B/TOLLIP) should have been caught before inclusion — a minor thoroughness flag, correctly
excluded by the verifier.

Overclaiming check: the researcher's own synthesis language was appropriately hedged
("mechanistically distinct," "no dedicated functional characterization anywhere") — this is the
researcher correctly self-critiquing, not overclaiming. Good practice; elevating this caveat to
WEAKENED rather than letting it sit as a footnote.

Smoking-mediation: locus is absent from GSCAN smoking-behavior hits, and the biology (mucociliary
clearance, mucin structure) is a plausible direct-lung mechanism independent of smoking
initiation/heaviness — this prior is not the concern here.

### GENE: TOLLIP — WEAKENED, bordering CONTESTED

TOLLIP's supporting evidence is the weakest of the three when scrutinized for locus-specificity. The
L2G algorithm itself — the same algorithm that favors MUC5B at 0.778 — does not put TOLLIP on top
anywhere in the credible set; TOLLIP's coloc support comes from a skin-tissue eQTL, not lung. At a
gene-dense ~300kb interval containing MUC5B, TOLLIP, and others, a skin eQTL colocalizing with a
lung-disease GWAS signal is exactly the pattern expected from LD/regulatory promiscuity rather than
causal specificity — the researcher and skeptic agree this does not distinguish TOLLIP from MUC5B,
and if anything argues against TOLLIP given VEP/L2G point the other way. Independent search for a
lung-tissue TOLLIP eQTL or TOLLIP-specific COPD GWAS signal found nothing (general Lung eQTL
Consortium literature exists but no TOLLIP-specific lung eQTL or COPD case-control paper surfaced,
consistent with the researcher's finding of zero dedicated TOLLIP COPD genetic-association papers).

The gene-level mechanistic evidence for TOLLIP (Agraval — cigarette-smoke-induced epigenetic
suppression in COPD lungs; Ritter — TLR3-mediated suppression linked to viral exacerbations) is real
and COPD-specific, and is smoking-conditional in exactly the way the biological priors anticipate
(direct pulmonary/immune mechanism modulated by smoke exposure, not smoking-initiation behavior).
This is legitimate supporting biology for TOLLIP as a gene of interest in COPD generally. But it says
nothing about this locus's lead variant or credible set — none of the TOLLIP variants discussed
(rs111521887, rs5743894, rs5743890, rs3168046, rs3750920) are in the credible set, and TOLLIP's
aggregate Open Targets COPD score, while nonzero (0.142, the only one with a genetic_association
component), is still modest and mostly IPF-driven per Noth et al.

The strongest alternative-hypothesis concern, which deserves more weight than the researcher gave it:
Noth et al. establishes that MUC5B-promoter and TOLLIP variants are the two dominant, independent IPF
risk signals in this exact 11p15.5 interval. Given the extreme density of IPF risk alleles here, and
that large biobank "COPD" case definitions (hospital/ICD-based, as likely underlie this GWAS) are
known to have imperfect discrimination from ILD/IPF/CPFE, there is a real possibility that part of
this locus's "COPD" signal reflects IPF or mixed COPD-fibrosis phenotypes misclassified as COPD,
particularly given Goos et al.'s finding that the IPF-defining variant is COPD-null. This doesn't
cleanly favor MUC5B or TOLLIP over the other — both genes have their strongest human disease
genetics in IPF, not COPD — but it is a systemic caveat that applies to the whole locus.

Net: TOLLIP's case rests on (a) a skin eQTL coloc that is not lung-specific and not L2G-favored, and
(b) COPD mechanism papers that are gene-level, not locus/variant-level. This is real biology attached
to the wrong evidentiary anchor for "this locus, this credible set." WEAKENED — the inference that
TOLLIP is a serious competing causal gene at this specific locus overreaches what the coloc/L2G data
show, though TOLLIP-as-a-COPD-relevant-gene-in-general (Agraval) is credible on its own.

### GENE: BRSK2 — CONTESTED (on thoroughness grounds), not dismissible as pure noise without more work

Agree with the researcher's directional call (BRSK2 is the weakest candidate: lowest L2G, not top
gene anywhere, no smoking-behavior GWAS overlap, neurodevelopmental/neuronal-polarity literature
only), but flagging a thoroughness gap rather than accepting "bystander" as the final word. BRSK2 is
a member of the AMPK-related kinase family, and AMPK-pathway kinases have well-documented pleiotropic
roles in macrophage polarization, NF-kB-mediated inflammation, and autophagy in lung tissue (search
surfaced AMPK-mediated Ccr2 suppression in macrophages, AMPK's role in emphysema-associated cellular
senescence and inflammation, and LKB1-myeloid lung inflammation papers) — none of which were searched
for BRSK2 specifically by the researcher. The researcher's search appears to have been limited to
"BRSK2" + neurodevelopment/kinase-family papers (Barnes, Hiatt) without extending to BRSK2's specific
role (if any) in myeloid/epithelial AMPK signaling, autophagy in lung epithelium, or senescence
pathways relevant to COPD/emphysema. A newly surfaced paper (BRSK2 and autophagy via PIK3C3 in cancer
cells, Scientific Reports 2025, subsequently verified by citation-verifier as PMID 41258112, VERIFIED)
suggests BRSK2 does have non-neuronal autophagy/NF-kB/inflammatory-cytokine biology that was not
explored — this demonstrates the search space was not exhausted, though this specific paper is
breast-cancer cell-line evidence, not pulmonary.

This is not evidence FOR BRSK2 at this locus — the L2G/VEP/genetic-association data still clearly
disfavor it relative to MUC5B, and the "gene density near MUC5B/TOLLIP" bystander explanation remains
the most parsimonious read. But marking this CONTESTED on process grounds: the null result for BRSK2
was reached via an incomplete search (missed the AMPK-pathway pleiotropy angle explicitly flagged as
a prior in the task's own instructions), not via a search that affirmatively ruled out lung/
inflammatory biology. Flagged for synthesis-lead: BRSK2 should get one more targeted search pass
(AMPK-family kinase + macrophage/airway epithelium/autophagy/senescence + COPD or emphysema) before
being fully dismissed, even though that pass is still expected to leave MUC5B as the clear top
candidate.

### Summary (from red-team-skeptic)

- **MUC5B: WEAKENED** — strong locus-level statistical/coloc case, but the mechanistic narrative is
  built on a different variant (rs35705950) shown by Goos et al. to be COPD-null; this locus's actual
  lead variant has zero dedicated functional or disease literature.
- **TOLLIP: WEAKENED** (bordering CONTESTED) — real COPD-relevant, smoking-conditional gene biology
  (Agraval, Ritter) but the locus-specific evidence (skin eQTL coloc) does not favor TOLLIP over
  MUC5B and is arguably an LD/gene-density artifact; no TOLLIP COPD genetic-association paper exists
  at all, dominant human genetics is IPF.
- **BRSK2: CONTESTED** on thoroughness grounds — directionally likely a bystander call, but the
  search did not explore AMPK-pathway pleiotropy in macrophages/autophagy/senescence that the task's
  own priors flag as plausible; recommend one more targeted search before final dismissal.
- **Cross-cutting alternative hypothesis:** this locus's "COPD" association may partly reflect
  IPF/ILD phenotypic overlap in biobank COPD case definitions, given the extreme density of
  independent IPF risk alleles (MUC5B promoter + TOLLIP per Noth et al.) in this exact interval, even
  though neither of those specific IPF variants is the fine-mapped lead. This argues for caution in
  calling either MUC5B or TOLLIP a "pure COPD" gene at this locus versus a shared fibrotic/mucus-
  biology locus.

No new contradicting literature specifically refuting rs2943516's COPD relevance or naming an
alternative causal gene at this exact locus was found in the skeptic's independent search — the
absence-of-evidence for this specific variant (rather than a positive refutation) is itself the main
finding, and matches what the researcher already reported.

## Note on scope guidance test (per orchestrator's task instructions)

This locus was flagged as a test of the pipeline's variant-scope guidance: the credible set has a
missense lead variant (rs2943516) plus a second missense variant (rs2943521), a synonymous variant
(rs908229), and a splice-region variant (rs2471912). Per current researcher-agent instructions, the

individual search and was rolled into MUC5B's gene-level evidence instead, per the researcher's
explicit statement in its checkpoint file. This matches the intended scope behavior.
