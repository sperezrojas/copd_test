## Contested / weakened genes (red-team-skeptic verbatim reasoning)

### MAP4K4 — CONTESTED (candidacy itself, not merely a specific claim)

Verbatim from red-team-skeptic:

"This is the clearest case in the whole locus of a prioritization method (MAGMA/PoPS
gene-level statistical aggregation) producing a candidate with zero corroborating gene-level
GWAS evidence (zero L2G rows, no credible-set variant annotated to it) and only broad,
cross-tissue functional literature (macrophage TNF/IL-1β in sepsis, T-cell Th17/metabolic
contexts, one ozone-particulate lung-inflammation model that is explicitly not a smoke/COPD
model, an LPS acute-lung-injury model, and an off-topic cardiac paper the researcher admits
including 'for completeness' only). The researcher's own assessment already says this fairly
plainly ('lower-confidence candidate... notwithstanding its MAGMA/PoPS statistical
prioritization'), but I'd push further than the researcher did: this should be stated as a
near-textbook example of a statistical-genetics prioritization artifact rather than a gene with
any independent evidentiary support. MAGMA/PoPS methods aggregate gene-level significance from
surrounding association signal and functional-genomic similarity to other trait genes — they
are known to sometimes flag genes based on polygenic background signal or expression/pathway
similarity to true effector genes without the flagged gene itself harboring any causal variant.
With zero credible-set variant and zero L2G evidence in a locus with four other genes carrying
real fine-mapping signal, MAP4K4's presence in this candidate list looks like exactly that kind
of artifact. The functional papers cited (sepsis, adipose Th17, ozone-particulate, LPS-ALI) are
being asked to do speculative bridging work across four different disease contexts, none of
which is chronic cigarette-smoke-driven COPD, to manufacture a 'plausible story' for a gene
with no direct genetic anchor. I would flag this as CONTESTED in the specific sense that the
'candidacy' itself is contestable — not because there's a paper arguing against MAP4K4 at this
locus (none was found, by the researcher or by me — I could not independently search this
either given the budget exhaustion), but because the prioritization method itself, absent any
credible-set or colocalization evidence, does not meet this pipeline's evidentiary bar for a
serious candidate gene, and the synthesis-lead should treat it as background/low-priority
rather than a peer of IL1RL1/IL18R1. This should be stated more bluntly than the researcher's
own softened 'lower-confidence candidate' phrasing."

### IL1RL1 — WEAKENED

Verbatim from red-team-skeptic:

"The researcher's own hedged language ('leaving genuinely open... whether the specific GWAS
signal for COPD is the same variant/mechanism as the asthma signal') is honest and should be
preserved, but the gene-level narrative text around it is not equally careful. Several points
of overclaim: The 'COPD-specific mechanistic studies' list (Andersson 2026, Huang 2021, Gimenes
2019, Hu 2024) are all either observational human histology/biomarker studies or mouse models
of smoke/virus-induced ST2 signaling — none are genetic-causal evidence, and none address
whether the GWAS signal (as opposed to the ST2 protein/pathway generically) is doing anything
COPD-specific. Labeling these 'COPD-specific' conflates pathway-level biology (which nobody
disputes is active in COPD lungs) with locus-specific causal inference (which is exactly what's
in question)... Strickson 2023's RAGE/EGFR 'ST2-independent' pathway... if anything, argues the
COPD-relevant IL-33 biology may not require IL1RL1/ST2 signaling at all, undercutting rather
than supporting IL1RL1 as the causal COPD gene at this locus... The Bu & Li 2025 colocalization
finding... is the single most directly relevant statistical-genetic piece of evidence bearing
on the asthma-pleiotropy question, and it points toward shared/pleiotropic signal, not
COPD-specificity... the gene-level 'Assessment' text buries it under a longer list of
pathway-biology citations that read as more affirmative of a COPD role than the colocalization
data actually support... No FEV1/FEV1-FVC effect size specific to the IL1RL1 variant/signal was
extracted from Shrine 2019... it is asserted at the level of 'this locus family' / 'these
variants' in aggregate across 279 signals, not confirmed as applying with a specific
significant effect size to the IL1RL1 signal itself... the astegolimab Phase 2b/3 data...
significant in the ARNASA q4w arm, NOT significant in the ALIENTO q4w arm (its primary
comparison)... is a mixed/largely null efficacy readout, not confirmatory pharmacological
evidence of a COPD-specific ST2-driven mechanism... this trial data should not be cited as
validating evidence at all, only as 'an active but not yet successful therapeutic
hypothesis.'... the inference that IL1RL1 has 'a growing COPD-specific... body of literature'
overstates what is actually a body of literature about the IL-33/ST2 pathway's general
involvement in COPD lung pathology, which is not the same claim as this GWAS signal being
COPD-driving rather than asthma/Th2-pleiotropic. The asthma-pleiotropy hypothesis is, if
anything, better supported by the one piece of direct statistical evidence (colocalization)
than the COPD-specificity hypothesis is by any comparably direct genetic evidence."

### IL18R1 — WEAKENED (specifically the neutrophilic-vs-Th2 gene-splitting claim; overall gene candidacy assessed by skeptic as "reasonably supported")

Verbatim from red-team-skeptic:

"The researcher's own included citation, Sawada 2013, is an IL-18-transgenic Th2/CD4/IL-13/
OVA-asthma model — i.e., evidence for IL-18R1 pathway involvement in Th2/asthma biology, filed
under IL18R1 but directly contradicting the 'distinct from Th2 axis' framing built around this
gene. The note acknowledges this... but the Assessment text's clean neutrophilic-vs-Th2 gene
split doesn't fully absorb this contradiction — it should be stated more bluntly that the IL-18
axis itself is not Th2-exclusionary, so IL18R1 cannot be cleanly positioned as 'the non-Th2
gene at this locus' opposite IL1RL1 'the Th2 gene.' Cayrol & Girard's point that ST2 is also
expressed on neutrophils cuts the same way from the other direction — the neutrophilic/Th2
dichotomy being used to differentiate IL1RL1 from IL18R1 is more of a heuristic than something
the cited data cleanly supports. Bu & Li 2025 names IL18R1 in the same colocalized COPD-allergy
hotspot as IL1RL1 — meaning the same asthma-pleiotropy problem that afflicts IL1RL1 also
applies to IL18R1, undercutting the implicit suggestion that IL18R1 is the 'cleaner,'
asthma-independent gene at this locus."

## Methodological caveat preserved verbatim (search-budget exhaustion)

From red-team-skeptic, disclosed at the start of its critique and repeated here in full because
it materially limits how much weight this locus's critique should be given:

"My WebSearch quota was exhausted before I could run any independent contradiction-search
queries for this locus (all four queries — 2q12 fine-mapping distinctness, astegolimab trial
results, MAP4K4/COPD, IL18RAP/COPD — returned 'budget exhausted' errors), and the
ClinicalTrials.gov MCP tool was not available under the name I attempted. This means none of
the five candidate genes received an independent contradiction-search pass from me — my
critique below is based entirely on internal analysis of the researcher's own (already-
verified) findings, cross-checking their claims against their own cited abstracts and against
each other, not on new literature I found. This is a real gap relative to the instructions...
and should be flagged to the synthesis-lead: zero independent search coverage for IL1RL1,
IL18R1, IL18RAP, SLC9A4, and MAP4K4... If search budget can be raised, this locus should be
re-passed before finalizing, given how much rides on the asthma-pleiotropy question."

**Recommendation for the study team:** treat this locus's evidence base as internally
consistent and citation-verified, but not yet independently red-teamed against fresh literature
search. A follow-up pass specifically targeting (a) 2q12 COPD-vs-asthma fine-mapping/
colocalization papers published after this review, and (b) any COPD GWAS reporting this
locus's effect size after explicitly excluding ACO/self-reported-asthma cases from the primary
case definition, would be the highest-value next step before treating the IL18R1-vs-IL1RL1
call as settled.
