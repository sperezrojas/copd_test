## Genes marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic (reasoning preserved verbatim)

### TMEM232 — CONTESTED

"Open Targets shows literally no COPD association data for TMEM232, despite MAGMA/PoPS
(gene-based/polygenic statistical methods, not colocalization) prioritizing it — a real
disagreement between prioritization approaches worth flagging rather than averaging
over. The only actual literature is a cross-ancestry-replicated TMEM232-SLC25A46 locus
association with atopic dermatitis and allergic rhinitis — a Th2/allergic paradigm that
prior #3 explicitly says should be distinguished from COPD-classic biology, and
critically, none of those GWAS papers can even determine whether TMEM232 or SLC25A46 is
the causal gene at that locus — a second, independent LD-resolution ambiguity layered on
top of the COPD-locus ambiguity. Combined with gnomAD LoF-tolerance (oe=1.058, least
constrained of the four candidates), this is a case where a credible, replicated
alternative genetic association (atopic dermatitis, different gene-pair-resolution
problem, different immune paradigm) competes directly with the COPD hypothesis. This
meets the bar for CONTESTED: not a paper that argues 'TMEM232 is not involved in COPD,'
but a real, repeatedly-replicated alternative phenotype/mechanism at the same genomic
real estate that the researcher itself flagged as 'a real but distinct
alternative/competing hypothesis.' No new citation to mark UNVERIFIED here — this is a
re-weighting of already-verified citations (21666691, 23042114, 25711310, 22036096), not
new material."

Also within the FER row of the evidence table, one individual **citation** (not a whole

## Genes marked WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning, for reference — not excluded, but flagged as overreach risk)

Per pipeline instructions, only CONTESTED genes are required in this appendix; the
following WEAKENED verdicts are included for full traceability of the critique since
they materially shaped the "Prioritized gene" call in the evidence table.

### MAN2A1 — WEAKENED

"The credible-set architecture itself argues against MAN2A1: the two highest-PIP
variants (0.49 intergenic, 0.32 upstream of LINC01848 = 0.81 of the fine-mapped signal)
sit outside MAN2A1, and only the low-PIP tail (each <0.03) falls in MAN2A1 introns/
3'UTR. Calling MAN2A1 the candidate because it's the 'nearest gene' while the actual PIP
mass points elsewhere is close to a textbook nearest-gene bias — this should be stated
plainly, not softened. The researcher was appropriately honest that Shrine et al. 2023
is an *assumed*, not confirmed, source for MAN2A1's lung-function link — that assumption
should not be allowed to quietly become 'the literature supports MAN2A1' downstream. The
immune evidence, while real, is tissue- and paradigm-mismatched: gut epithelium (PMID
29343654), systemic lupus-like autoimmunity via innate lectin-ligand activation (PMID
11158608, 17681821) — none in lung, and lupus-type systemic autoimmunity is a distinct
inflammatory program from COPD's neutrophilic/innate axis (prior #3). PMID 32723834
(tumor immunology/anti-PD-L1) and the five MAN2A1-FER fusion-transcript papers are
oncology literature reflecting somatic chromosomal breakage at a physically adjacent
locus — these are not germline mechanism evidence and should never be cited as if they
inform inherited COPD risk; flagging this explicitly since a careless synthesis could
read 'MAN2A1-FER fusion in cancer' as evidence the two genes function together, which is
not what those papers show. Most importantly, MAN2A1's own Open Targets profile is
stronger for smoking initiation, alcohol drinking, schizophrenia, and COVID-19 than for
COPD (0.173, rank ~27th of 656) — this is exactly the pleiotropy pattern prior #1 warns
about, and while the researcher couldn't confirm a genome-wide-significant smoking hit at
this exact locus, the directional pattern is a real reason to suspect smoking/
neurobehavioral mediation over direct pulmonary mechanism. No FEV1/FEV1-FVC concordance
was confirmed (prior #2 gap). Net: real gene, plausible locus member, but the inference
that MAN2A1 drives COPD-relevant lung pathology overreaches what's shown."

### FER — WEAKENED

"FER has the strongest statistical genetics signal at the locus (OT L2G-proxy 0.610,
highest among candidates; FLAMES-prioritized; high evolutionary constraint, oe=0.562) —
so the prioritization case is more defensible than MAN2A1's. But the mechanistic/immune
literature has a real conflation problem that needs flagging explicitly: PMID 16959897
is about Fps/Fes, FER's paralog in the same kinase subfamily but a distinct gene — using
it to claim FER 'restrains macrophage TLR4/NF-κB/TNF-α responses' is paralog-to-target
substitution, not FER evidence. Worse, PMID 19001085 is stated by the researcher itself
to be about FES specifically, not FER — if this citation were counted toward 'FER's
mast-cell immune evidence' in any downstream synthesis, that would be a direct
overclaim, since FES and FER, despite homology, have materially different expression
patterns (FES is more myeloid/mast-cell-restricted; FER is more broadly expressed). Of
the remaining mast-cell degranulation papers (12192036, 16731527, 7559493), these are in
vitro IgE/FcεRI biology — an allergic/Th2-type paradigm, which prior #3 says should be
distinguished from COPD's classic neutrophilic/innate/CD8 programs, not folded in as
generic 'immune relevance.' The one paper that is unambiguously FER-in-vivo (PMID
16009680) is intestinal epithelium, not lung. So: real kinase-family immunobiology
exists, but once the paralog-mislabeled and off-target-paradigm citations are stripped
out, what's left specific to FER itself is thin and entirely non-pulmonary. No
spirometry concordance and no lung tissue data at all. The researcher's own line —
'strongest... literature of any gene at this locus' but 'none... in lung tissue' — is
accurate and appropriately hedged; I'd add that the strength claim should be further
discounted once the FES/paralog conflation is accounted for."

### LINC01848 — WEAKENED

"This is the one candidate where the researcher did not overclaim — they explicitly
reported zero PubMed hits and an exhausted search budget rather than papering over the
gap, which is the right call. But the underlying statistical case is not trivial to wave
off: this lncRNA carries the second- and third-highest PIP variants in the credible set
(0.32 and 0.04), meaning a third of the fine-mapped posterior probability sits here —
more than sits in MAN2A1. A locus with that much fine-mapping weight and literally zero
functional or genetic-association literature is not 'evidence that LINC01848 holds,'
it's an unresolved gap that could just as easily favor or disfavor the gene once
investigated. I'm marking this WEAKENED rather than leaving it unclassified because if
synthesis treats 'no contradicting evidence found' as tacit support, that would be an
inference error — absence of literature on an lncRNA is common and uninformative either
way. I'd explicitly recommend to the synthesis-lead that this gene needs a dedicated
GWAS Catalog/GTEx-lncRNA/PheWAS lookup pass before any confident ranking, given the
disproportionate credible-set weight it carries relative to the total absence of
characterization."

## Process caveats disclosed by subagents (preserved for transparency)

- **Researcher:** could not independently enumerate all protein-coding genes physically
  within 5:109,600,001-109,950,000 via Ensembl REST API or targeted region WebSearch
  (PubMed rate-limiting on one TMEM232 search attempt; WebSearch budget exhausted before
  GWAS Catalog/Ensembl region-listing queries could be attempted). Noted SLC25A46
  (immediately adjacent to TMEM232, co-reported in the atopic dermatitis literature) as a
  gene that was not independently searched in its own right, since no credible-set
  variant is annotated to it and no prioritization method flagged it.
- **Red-team-skeptic:** disclosed that its own WebSearch budget was exhausted before it
  could run any of its five planned independent contradiction-search queries (MAN2A1/
  Shrine locus-table check, rs9326785, FER+COPD, TMEM232/SLC25A46+COPD, LINC01848). Its
  critique above is therefore based entirely on re-reasoning over the researcher's
  already-verified findings, not on new independent literature checks. It recommended
  two highest-value follow-ups if search capacity becomes available: an rs9326785-
  specific smoking-GWAS lookup, and checking Shrine et al. 2023's (PMID 36914875)
  supplementary locus table to resolve whether MAN2A1 or FER is actually the named
  nearest/prioritized gene at this signal.
