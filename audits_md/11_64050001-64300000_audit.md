# Audit trail — locus 11_64050001-64300000 (FLRT1, MACROD1)

This file preserves the full verification and critique trail for reviewers who want to
inspect what was excluded, contested, or weakened, separate from the clean evidence table in
`evidence_tables/11_64050001-64300000.md`.

## Excluded citations (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH)

**None.** All 29 citations submitted by the researcher were returned VERIFIED by the
citation-verifier, including two unusually high PMIDs (42362029, 42574321 — both 2026
publications on FERMT3/kindlin-3) that were specifically flagged by the orchestrator for
extra scrutiny given their implausibly-high-looking PMID numbers; both were independently
confirmed to resolve to real, matching articles with matching authors, journals, and claims.
See `evidence_tables/_status/11_64050001-64300000/verified_citations.md` for the full
citation-by-citation verification record.

One genuine name-collision risk was caught and self-corrected by the researcher (not an
excluded citation, but worth recording): several initial "STIP1" search hits were actually
about the distinct gene **STUB1** ("STIP1 homology and U-box containing protein 1"),
including a paper on cigarette-smoke-induced bronchial epithelial senescence in COPD
(PMID 40895192, among others). These were excluded from STIP1's evidence base by the
researcher before submission and were never sent to citation-verifier as STIP1 evidence.
The one STIP1 citation that was submitted (PMID 33208131) was independently re-confirmed by
citation-verifier to genuinely concern STIP1 (stress-induced phosphoprotein 1), not STUB1.

## Genes flagged CONTESTED or WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (reasoning preserved verbatim)

### RPS6KA4 — CONTESTED

> This is the most overclaimed gene in the set. The "strong dual-pathway candidate" framing
> conjoins one UNVERIFIED database association (smoking initiation/cessation, not
> independently confirmed by PubMed or GWAS Catalog lookup — the researcher's own session
> says so) with one VERIFIED but generic mechanism (MSK2 suppressing macrophage IFN-β/
> cytokine output via DUSP1) that has no lung or COPD specificity at all — the other four
> MSK1/2 citations are pancreas, kidney, colon, and breast biology. Critically, RPS6KA4 has
> zero credible-set variant support — it's a MAGMA/PoPS gene-level prioritization only,
> meaning the statistical case rests on genomic proximity/expression-pattern heuristics
> rather than fine-mapped causal variants. Combining an unconfirmed smoking claim with a
> tissue-nonspecific immune mechanism and calling the composite "strong" overstates what's
> actually nailed down. The prior #1 (smoking-mediation) framing is doing rhetorical work
> here that the evidence doesn't yet support. CONTESTED: not because I found literature
> actively contradicting RPS6KA4, but because the "strong dual-pathway candidate"
> characterization is not supported by what's actually verified — one pillar (smoking) is
> explicitly unconfirmed and the other (macrophage inflammation) is non-lung-specific. This
> should be downgraded to "plausible but unconfirmed on both pillars" pending (a) an
> independent GWAS Catalog/PhenoScanner check on the smoking association, and (b) any
> lung/airway-macrophage-specific MSK2 literature. Flag for synthesis-lead: the
> RPS6KA4-smoking claim should be re-attempted via GWAS Catalog API or PhenoScanner directly
> (not PubMed, since it's a structured-database association, not a paper) before it's
> allowed to influence prioritization language.

### FLRT1 — WEAKENED

> The researcher is right that no relevant literature exists, but "no biology found" is
> being allowed to do more work than it should. This gene carries the lead variant and the
> top PIP mass, is intronic/5'UTR (i.e., a plausible regulatory-variant host), yet the
> write-up leans on FLRT1's coding biology (axon guidance, FGFR potentiation) and paralog
> biology (FLRT2/3 in cortical migration) as if absence-of-fit there closes the case. It
> doesn't: an intronic credible-set variant in FLRT1 could equally be acting as a distal
> eQTL for a neighboring gene (RPS6KA4, PLCB3, STIP1, FERMT3 are all within the stated
> 250kb window) and simply be "hosted" in FLRT1 without FLRT1 being the functional gene at
> all. No GTEx/lung-eQTL or colocalization evidence is reported for FLRT1 itself — the
> researcher's dossier is silent on whether FLRT1 expression correlates with the
> credible-set variants in any tissue, lung or otherwise. That's a real gap, not a minor
> one, given FLRT1 is nominally the top candidate by fine-mapping alone. WEAKENED: the "no
> biology" framing is being used to deprioritize the top-PIP gene without ruling out the
> LD/regulatory-target-uncertainty alternative (variant regulates a different gene) — this
> needs an eQTL/coloc check before being written off, and the case as presented is not sound
> for confidently deprioritizing FLRT1, nor for prioritizing it; it's simply unresolved.

### PLCB3 — WEAKENED

> This is the second most concerning overclaim. The researcher explicitly reports a null
> result: the tested PLCB3 missense variant (rs35169799) was NOT significantly associated
> with COPD in a 9,888/27,060 case-control meta-analysis (OR=1.05, P=0.247). The
> researcher's defense — "different variant, doesn't bear on the common-variant GWAS
> signal" — is a reasonable technical point (a null rare-variant association genuinely
> doesn't rule out a separate common regulatory variant's effect), but it is being used to
> fully neutralize a genuine negative data point rather than to merely limit its scope. The
> remaining "mechanistic" evidence is a grab-bag: CF bronchial epithelium IL-8 (a distinct
> disease context, cystic fibrosis not COPD), MUC5AC mucin regulation, and FcɛRI/mast-cell
> allergic inflammation (Th2/allergic axis, arguably in tension with prior #3's
> neutrophilic/CD8/Th1/Th17 COPD-relevant programs, and not clearly reconciled with COPD's
> non-allergic phenotype). Calling this "the strongest mechanistically supported gene at
> this locus" overstates coherence — it's a diverse set of PLCB3-airway-inflammation papers
> across different diseases (CF, allergic asthma, generic airway epithelium) that were
> assembled post hoc, alongside one directly negative COPD genetic result for a different
> variant in the same gene. WEAKENED: the null GWAS result deserves more weight than
> "doesn't bear on this" — at minimum it shows PLCB3 coding variation is not obviously
> COPD-associated at reasonable power, which is relevant context that should be surfaced in
> synthesis, not filed away. The "strongest mechanistically supported" superlative should be
> softened to something like "mechanistically plausible across multiple
> airway-inflammatory contexts, but with an existing negative COPD genetic association for a
> different variant and no COPD-specific positive evidence."

### MACROD1, FERMT3, NUDT22, PPP1R14B, FKBP2, STIP1 — HOLDS (no overreach detected)

appropriately proportionate to the (generally thin) evidence — none were contested or
weakened. Verbatim notes of interest:

> FERMT3 — ...is exactly the right hedge given kindlin-3/LAD-III causes severe monogenic

> STIP1 — I'd flag the researcher's active exclusion of STUB1 papers (including a

> exactly the kind of gene-name-collision error this locus, sitting near a lot of short
> similarly-named genes, could easily produce, and the researcher's discipline here should
> be noted positively to synthesis-lead.

## Genes/evidence flagged as scope creep (background only — no fine-mapping/MAGMA/PoPS support)

The researcher additionally enumerated all protein-coding genes physically within the
11:64,050,001-64,300,000 region via an Ensembl overlap lookup, beyond the genes flagged by
the pipeline's own prioritization methods (nearest gene, MAGMA, PoPS, credible-set
annotation). The skeptic flagged this expansion explicitly:

> This is scope creep, and should be flagged as such. None of these genes carry any
> credible-set variant, MAGMA, or PoPS support — they were pulled in purely by
> physical-overlap lookup. Including VEGFB (hypoxic vascular remodeling) and KCNK4 (airway
> mechanosensory neurons) as if they were on par with the fine-mapped/prioritized genes
> above risks diluting the evidence table and implicitly suggesting these deserve comparable
> prioritization weight... Flag for synthesis-lead: rank/label these six separately (e.g.,
> "no fine-mapping or MAGMA/PoPS support — background only") so the final table doesn't
> imply they're competitive candidates.

Per this guidance, VEGFB and KCNK4 (which had citable literature) are retained in the main
evidence table but explicitly labeled "no fine-mapping/MAGMA/PoPS support — background
only" in the Candidate gene column, and are excluded from the Prioritized-gene synthesis
comparison entirely. BAD, GPR137, DNAJC4, and TRPT1 (which had no COPD/lung/immune-relevant
literature identified at all) are omitted from the main evidence table and are recorded here
only: the researcher searched each and found no plausible connection to COPD or pulmonary/
immune biology in this session (see `evidence_tables/_status/11_64050001-64300000/researcher.md`
for the per-gene notes).

## Cross-cutting gaps flagged by red-team-skeptic (not gene-specific)

> No eQTL or colocalization evidence is reported anywhere in this dossier for any gene at
> the locus (GTEx lung, or any other tissue). For a locus where the lead variant is
> intronic and the credible set spans 8+ genes across ~250kb, distinguishing "which gene
> does this regulatory variant actually act on" is the single most important open question,
> and it appears untouched.

> Smoking-mediation (prior #1) is under-evaluated overall. Only RPS6KA4 has any
> smoking-trait association claim, and it's unverified. No gene at this locus is reported to
> have FEV1/FEV1/FVC spirometry-trait support (prior #2) either — this is worth stating
> plainly in synthesis: at present, no gene at this locus has both a smoking-independent
> lung-function signal AND a validated mechanism, which should push overall locus confidence
> down regardless of which single gene is favored.

> Overall locus verdict: given (a) no eQTL/coloc data pinning any regulatory variant to a
> specific gene, (b) the two highest-PIP genes (FLRT1, MACROD1) having literally no
> disease-relevant biology, (c) the MAGMA/PoPS gene (RPS6KA4) resting on one unverified
> claim plus one non-lung-specific mechanism, and (d) the best mechanistic story (PLCB3)
> coexisting with a negative genetic association — I'd characterize this locus overall as
> weakly resolved, and recommend synthesis-lead not present any single gene here with high
> confidence language.
