## Critique: TSHZ2 (20q13.33, locus 20:52,850,001–53,050,000)

**Search disclosure:** Web search budget for the session was exhausted before I could run a full independent contradiction-search pass on this gene. I substituted a WebFetch query directly against PubMed ("TSHZ2 AND COPD"), which returned zero results — corroborating the researcher's negative finding independently, but I was not able to run additional targeted contradiction searches (e.g., "TSHZ2 emphysema," "TSHZ2 airway epithelium," "TSHZ2 cigarette smoke") beyond this. Since TSHZ2 is the only candidate gene at this locus, this is a **single-gene shallow pass, not a full independent search** — flag this to synthesis-lead as a residual gap rather than a completed contradiction search.

**Overclaiming check:**
- Citations #4 and #5 (Saunders 2022; Karlsson Linnér 2021) were correctly downgraded by the verifier to CLAIM_MISMATCH. The researcher's attribution of these specific papers as the "likely source" of the Open Targets GCST accessions is an inferential reach beyond what's confirmed — the *Open Targets structured data* (GCST IDs, L2G scores, colocalization h4) stands on its own and doesn't need this speculative paper-matching; including it as if it were literature corroboration mildly overstates the evidentiary chain. This doesn't undermine the OT signal itself, but the citations are decorative rather than confirmatory and should be labeled as such in the final writeup.
- "TSHZ2... explicitly excluded as a cause of human PUJO" (synthesis, based on Jenkins 2010) is slightly stronger than what a 48-case mutation-negative screen supports (absence of exonic mutations in a modest cohort is not a definitive exclusion, e.g., regulatory variants untested) — minor overclaim, low stakes since PUJO is not lung-relevant anyway.
- Otherwise the researcher's synthesis is unusually well-caveated (correctly flags Udagawa as non-GWS/small-N, Zhao/Gu/Jiang as non-COPD lung diseases, Dong/Wang as tangential). No claim I found materially inflates "associated with" into "causes."

**Alternative explanations:**
1. **Smoking-behavior mediation** — well-argued by the researcher and strongly supported by the structured OT data: TSHZ2 is the consistent top L2G gene at both high-PIP variants for smoking initiation, externalizing behavior/ADHD, and risk-taking, with h4 colocalization up to 0.99, and smoking initiation ranks in TSHZ2's top-5 genome-wide trait associations. Critically, **none** of the 16 GWAS/eQTL credible sets indexed at these exact variants is COPD or lung function — this is a rare, unusually clean pattern arguing against direct pulmonary mechanism.
2. **Adiposity/metabolic mediation** — under-explored alternative. The same variants also colocalize with waist-hip ratio, waist circumference, HDL particle size, and apoA, and TSHZ2's top overall traits include T2D and "abnormality of skeletal system." Obesity/metabolic traits are known confounders of spirometric measures and COPD case ascertainment (restrictive contribution to reduced FEV1, comorbidity clustering). The researcher's synthesis mentions this only in passing ("and/or adiposity-metabolic pathways") — this deserves more explicit weight as a second non-pulmonary competing hypothesis, not folded into the smoking-mediation narrative.
3. **CD8+ T-cell eQTL** (item #17) — the researcher correctly notes no mechanistic literature exists, but it's worth stating plainly: this eQTL colocalizes with smoking-initiation/behavioral GWAS, not with COPD or FEV1. So rather than rescuing an "immune-mediated COPD mechanism" hypothesis, it actually reinforces the smoking-mediation reading (same variant, same non-pulmonary phenotype, just in a different cell type/assay).

**Contradicting/weakening evidence, explicitly:**
- Zero Open Targets COPD evidence rows for TSHZ2; FEV1 association score only 0.035 — this is a direct, database-level contradiction of any claim that TSHZ2 has documented pulmonary-function biology, and should be weighted heavily per prior #2 (absence of spirometric evidence alongside a COPD signal warrants suspicion of an alternative pathway).
- Jenkins et al. 2010 (verified) — negative screen excluding TSHZ2 coding mutations in human PUJO, undercutting extrapolation from paralog (Tshz3) developmental phenotypes to TSHZ2 itself.
- The sarcoidosis link (Zhou 2012) is the researcher's own best lung-immune evidence, but sarcoidosis is a Th1/Th17 granulomatous ILD with a distinct antigen-driven, largely non-smoking etiology — mechanistically quite far from COPD's neutrophilic/CD8+/emphysematous phenotype. Citing it as "the clearest lung-relevant immune tie" is fair as a literature summary but should not be read as evidence for COPD-relevant inflammatory biology; the researcher does not overstate this, but synthesis-lead should not upgrade it either.
- Lung adenocarcinoma tumor-suppressor role (Zhao 2021) and PAH/pulmonary-fibrosis bioinformatics correlations (Gu 2024, Jiang 2023) show TSHZ2 is transcriptionally active in lung tissue generally, but in disease contexts mechanistically unrelated to COPD (proliferation/tumor suppression, vascular remodeling) — these establish lung expression, not COPD-relevant function.

**Thoroughness:** The researcher covered all three domains reasonably given TSHZ2 is a single, sparsely-studied gene; the negative-search items are appropriately flagged as such rather than omitted. I did not find evidence of under-searching that needs redoing before synthesis, beyond the caveat that my own contradiction pass was shallow due to budget exhaustion.

**Verdict: CONTESTED** — not in the sense that another gene is a better candidate (TSHZ2 is confirmed as the sole protein-coding gene in the interval, so gene-identity is not in dispute), but in the sense that the *mechanistic inference* — that this locus reflects pulmonary biology — is directly contradicted by convergent evidence: zero COPD/FEV1 Open Targets signal, all 16 indexed colocalizing traits at the credible-set variants being behavioral or metabolic rather than pulmonary, no PubMed-indexed COPD or lung-function literature, and a developmental/paralog literature that if anything argues against a TSHZ2-specific lung phenotype (PUJO exclusion). Properly caveated, TSHZ2 should be carried forward as the locus's positional/L2G candidate by default (there is no alternative gene), but synthesis-lead should present this locus as **most parsimoniously explained by smoking-behavior mediation (with adiposity/metabolic mediation as a secondary alternative)** rather than as evidence of a novel pulmonary disease gene, pending any future direct FEV1/FEV1-FVC or smoking-adjusted COPD association for TSHZ2.
