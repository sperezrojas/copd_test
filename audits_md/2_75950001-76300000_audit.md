# Audit appendix — locus 2_75950001-76300000 (chr2:75,950,001-76,300,000, GRCh38)

This file lists every citation excluded from the main evidence table (evidence_tables/2_75950001-76300000.md)
because its citation-verifier verdict was RETRACTED, NOT_FOUND, or CLAIM_MISMATCH, and every
gene the red-team-skeptic marked CONTESTED or otherwise flagged, with reasoning preserved

evidence_tables/_status/2_75950001-76300000/verified_citations.md and .../critique.md.

## Excluded citations

### 1. Bishop, DVM. "Cerebral asymmetry and language development: cause, correlate, or
consequence?" Science. 2013;340(6138):1230531. PMID 23766329.

**Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH**

Verifier reasoning (verbatim): "The abstract does not explicitly mention MRPL19 or C2ORF3 by
name. The claim states the review 'lists MRPL19/C2ORF3 among genes studied for association
with brain structural/functional lateralization,' but these gene names do not appear in the
published abstract."

### 2. Kim S, Elkon KB, Ma X. "Transcriptional suppression of interleukin-12 gene expression
following phagocytosis of apoptotic cells." Immunity. 2004;21(5):643-53. PMID 15539151.

**Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH**

Verifier reasoning (verbatim): "The paper describes a 'novel zinc finger nuclear factor, named
GC binding protein (GC-BP)' induced by apoptotic-cell phagocytosis that 'selectively inhibits
IL-12 p35 gene transcription.' However, the abstract does not formally identify GC-BP as GCFC2
(Ensembl ENSG00000005436). PubMed gene linking for this article returns inflammatory cytokine
genes but not GCFC2 (NCBI gene ID 26998), suggesting GC-BP may be a distinct or
differently-named protein product. The researcher's claim assumes GCFC2 relevance, but this
cannot be confirmed from available abstracts and metadata. CRITICAL ISSUE: GC-BP identity
remains unconfirmed. If GC-BP is indeed GCFC2, this would require explicit confirmation in the
full text or supplementary materials not accessible via standard literature queries."

This is the pipeline's only candidate lead connecting GCFC2 to COPD-relevant macrophage/
efferocytosis immune biology (efferocytosis and IL-12/Th1-axis regulation are directly relevant
to COPD's known defective-efferocytosis and macrophage-polarization phenotypes per the
researcher's original framing), and it does NOT clear verification as GCFC2-specific evidence.
Excluded from the main table for this reason. Note: the companion commentary (Henson 2004, PMID
15539147) IS included in the main table as VERIFIED, but only as confirmation that the GC-BP/
IL-12 mechanism itself exists in the literature — not as evidence that GC-BP is GCFC2.

### 3. Kim S, Chung EY, Ma X. "Immunological consequences of macrophage-mediated clearance of
apoptotic cells." Cell Cycle. 2005;4(2):231-4. PMID 15655362.

**Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH**

Verifier reasoning (verbatim): "The abstract discusses 'macrophage efferocytosis' and
'Phagocyte-derived immunoregulatory cytokines such as Interleukin-12 and Interleukin-10' in the
autoimmune context, but does not explicitly mention GC-BP by name or the specific Kim et al.
GC-BP/IL-12 mechanism. The claim states the review discusses 'the GC-BP/IL-12 mechanism,' but
this specific molecular mechanism is not identified in the published abstract."

## Genes flagged CONTESTED, WEAKENED, or otherwise qualified by red-team-skeptic

### GCFC2 — CONTESTED (on the specific claim of a direct pulmonary/immune mechanism; NOT
contested as the best-supported positional/statistical candidate at this locus)

Red-team-skeptic reasoning (verbatim, from critique.md):

"The positive case rests on three legs: (1) physical proximity/'nearest gene' + FLAMES pick,
(2) Open Targets L2G = 0.577, (3) a large body of verified-but-off-topic neurocognitive
literature (dyslexia, IQ, hippocampal volume, white matter, schizophrenia coloc). None of these
is pulmonary or immune mechanistic evidence, and the researcher's own conclusion says so
explicitly — that hedging is appropriate and not overclaimed.

Several points push this further than 'no lung evidence found' into actual contradicting
evidence:

- The L2G = 0.577 is not even from this pipeline's own credible set — it's from a different
  lead SNP (rs17752567) ~30kb away, in what appears to be a different GWAS/fine-mapping run.
  Importing this score as support for GCFC2 at rs35328078's credible set is an unstated leap.
  0.577 is also a moderate score (not >0.7-0.8, where L2G is usually considered strong), leaving
  substantial probability mass unassigned.
- The same Open Targets data show no colocalization with FEV1/FEV1-FVC/spirometry at this
  credible set, while showing strong (h4 ~0.85-0.90) colocalization with cognitive-processing-
  speed and schizophrenia. Per prior #2, absence of spirometric concordance is exactly the kind
  of negative evidence that should weigh against a direct pulmonary mechanism — and here it's
  not merely absent, it was actively tested and came back null while adjacent traits
  colocalized strongly. That's a meaningful asymmetry, not a gap.
- The one candidate immune mechanism (GC-BP/IL-12 repression in macrophages after efferocytosis)
  failed citation verification for gene identity — the verifier could not confirm GC-BP is
  GCFC2. This isn't just 'weak evidence,' it's a claim that was proposed and then actively
  rejected on inspection.
- LOEUF ~1.618 (LoF-tolerant) argues against a haploinsufficiency-style dosage mechanism,
  undercutting one common way a 'true' GWAS gene manifests functionally.
- The near-identical ~0.21 association scores across a dozen biologically unrelated diseases...
  is itself a red flag pattern consistent with a generic/locus-level statistical signature
  rather than gene-specific causal biology for any one of them, COPD included.
- Asthma score is only 0.023 vs COPD's 0.213 — a ~10x discrepancy despite asthma and COPD
  sharing substantial airway-obstruction genetic architecture. This is more consistent with a
  signal that is COPD-cohort-specific (e.g., enriched for heavy/long-term smokers, prior #1)
  than with a shared structural-airway mechanism.

Given the failed immune lead, the null spirometry coloc set against strong cognitive/
schizophrenia coloc, and the generic pleiotropy pattern, this crosses from 'weak/absent
evidence' into actively contradicting evidence against a direct pulmonary or immune mechanism
for GCFC2, even though it doesn't refute GCFC2 as the gene at this locus statistically.

Verdict: CONTESTED — not on 'is GCFC2 the right gene at this locus' (unresolved) but on 'does
GCFC2 have a supportable direct pulmonary/immune mechanism for COPD' — the affirmative case
collapses under its own reported negative colocalization and failed immune citation."

The skeptic also explicitly disclosed it had exhausted its web-search budget before this round
and performed **zero independent contradiction searches** — this critique is reasoning over
already-assembled evidence, not independently corroborated. Treat as a rigorous internal
consistency check, not an independent literature audit.

### MRPL19 — WEAKENED

Red-team-skeptic reasoning (verbatim): "Shares nearly all the same dyslexia/cognitive
literature with GCFC2 (jointly tested in almost every paper cited), so it isn't independently
supported by anything — it's riding on the same non-lung literature. Its L2G (0.056) is ~10x
lower than GCFC2's, and mechanistically it's a mitochondrial ribosomal protein (translation
housekeeping), even less plausible a priori for a lung-structural or immune mechanism than
GCFC2's transcriptional-repressor/spliceosome functions. The Anthoni et al. 2007 finding that
the two genes are co-regulated (risk haplotype attenuates expression of both genes) cuts
against cleanly separating them... The 10x L2G gap is suggestive but not dispositive of
independence between the two genes.

Verdict: WEAKENED — essentially no independent evidentiary support (a co-regulated bystander in
someone else's dyslexia literature), and lower L2G plus implausible housekeeping function make
it a weaker candidate than GCFC2, but there's no direct contradicting paper about MRPL19
specifically — it's under-evidenced rather than refuted."

### TACR1 — originally an unevaluated coverage gap; after remediation, INSUFFICIENT-DISTANCE-EVIDENCE

First-round skeptic reasoning (verbatim, on the original omission): "Received zero literature
search — the researcher deprioritized it purely for distance (~750-900kb) without any baseline
PubMed/coloc check. TACR1 (substance-P/NK1 receptor) is arguably the most biologically
plausible of the three for airway neurogenic inflammation and COPD-relevant Th-cell/neuroimmune
crosstalk, and the entire locus is a gene desert where 'nearest gene' distance rankings between
300kb and 900kb are not obviously discriminating... Ruling out a mechanistically attractive gene
solely on relative distance, without even a shallow search, is a bias the instructions
specifically warn against."

This gap was remediated with a supplemental researcher pass (6 new citations, all VERIFIED) and
a second, TACR1-specific red-team critique. Second-round skeptic reasoning (verbatim):

"No, not as currently constituted, and the researcher's own summary judgment already concedes
the core problem: this is exactly the candidate-gene-literature pattern the task brief warns
against. TACR1/NK1R is a heavily studied, druggable GPCR with a large pre-existing pharmacology
literature spanning multiple diseases... which means it is easy to find some mechanistically
plausible paper for almost any nearby variant, independent of whether that variant actually
regulates the gene. The evidence here supplies zero genetic linkage between this credible set
and TACR1: no eQTL, no colocalization, no L2G inclusion (Open Targets' own L2G model didn't
even consider TACR1 a candidate), no Hi-C/ABC evidence, and a 750-900kb distance in a gene
desert — nearly double the distance to GCFC2/MRPL19... Absent any statistical-genetic or
chromatin bridge, 'TACR1 biology is compelling' is a hypothesis about the pathway, not evidence
about this locus. This should be labeled a nearest/plausible-gene bias in reverse.

[On the Bai et al. vs. De Swert et al. tension:] There is real tension here that the
researcher's synthesis softens rather than resolves. De Swert's causal model implies NK1R
signaling drives smoke-induced macrophage/DC recruitment and MMP-12-mediated parenchymal
destruction — i.e., more NK1R activity, more damage. If that causal direction held in humans,
one plausible prediction is that established COPD (more damage) would show higher, not lower,
NK1R expression relative to smoking controls without disease. Bai et al. instead found a
biphasic pattern — up in asymptomatic smokers, down in COPD — which is compatible with at least
two different, mutually exclusive readings... I'd treat this as unresolved tension, not
confirmatory concordance.

[On Han et al. and Bernstein et al.:] Both should carry low weight for COPD-specificity... Han
et al. is FceRI-dependent Th2/atopic biology in allergic rhinitis/asthma — a different
inflammatory program from the neutrophilic/CD8/Th1/Th17 axis more classically implicated in
COPD... Bernstein et al... is an existence proof that TACR1 is 'regulatable,' not evidence that
the credible-set variant at this locus... does the regulating. It should not be read as
transferable functional support for rs35328078 or any variant in this specific credible set.

Verdict: INSUFFICIENT-DISTANCE-EVIDENCE. The TACR1 literature is real and non-trivial (causal
mouse knockout/pharmacology data is a genuinely higher evidentiary tier than most gene-level
literature in this pipeline), but none of it is anchored to this GWAS signal... Recommend
synthesis-lead present TACR1 as a 'biologically plausible but genetically unlinked' gene
distinct in kind from GCFC2 (which has direct L2G/statistical support), not as a co-equal
alternative causal gene, unless/until eQTL or chromatin-interaction evidence is found connecting
the credible set to TACR1 specifically."

Both TACR1 critique rounds disclosed exhausted web-search budgets and performed **no
independent searches** — all reasoning is over already-assembled evidence. A dedicated search
for NK1-antagonist (aprepitant/orvepitant-class) COPD clinical-trial outcomes and TACR1 GTEx
lung eQTL/Hi-C data was recommended by the skeptic as the highest-value follow-up but was not
performed in this pipeline run.

## General caveats carried into this locus's evidence table

- Open Targets' fine-mapped lead variant for the COPD-related credible set (rs17752567) does
  not match this pipeline's stated lead variant (rs35328078); both lie within ~30kb of each
  other but likely reflect different underlying GWAS/fine-mapping runs. All GCFC2/MRPL19 L2G
  and colocalization figures cited in the main evidence table and Open Targets summary should
  be read with this caveat.
- No FEV1/FEV1-FVC or smoking-initiation evidence entries exist for GCFC2, MRPL19, or TACR1 in
  Open Targets' indexed evidence at this locus — an absence-of-data note for all three genes,
  not a confirmed negative finding.
- Both citation-verifier and red-team-skeptic dispatches for this locus disclosed exhausted (or
  near-exhausted) search budgets at various points; no independent contradiction-search beyond
  what's recorded above was completed. This locus's critique should be understood as a rigorous
  internal-consistency and overclaiming check, not an exhaustive independent literature search
  for contradicting evidence.
