# Audit trail — Locus 11:115050001-115300000 (CADM1)

This file records exclusions, contested calls, and the full reasoning trail that supports
the evidence table at `evidence_tables/11_115050001-115300000.md`. It is intended for
reviewer/methodological scrutiny and is kept separate from the evidence table itself.

## Excluded citations (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH)

**None.** All 15 citations returned by the researcher were submitted to citation-verifier
and all 15 received a verdict of **VERIFIED** — existing, non-retracted, and accurately
representing the claim attributed to them. No citation was excluded from the evidence table
on verifier grounds. Full verifier output is preserved verbatim at
`evidence_tables/_status/11_115050001-115300000/verified_citations.md`.

One correction surfaced during verification worth flagging explicitly: for citation #14 in
the researcher's list (Dragunas et al. 2024, Allergy, PMID 39484998), the researcher's
draft description was potentially ambiguous about which CADM-family gene was upregulated in
the mast-cell/sensory-neuron coculture experiment. Citation-verifier's full-text check
confirmed the upregulated gene in that specific coculture experiment was **CADM4** (a CADM1
paralog), not CADM1 itself — only the CADM1+ cell proximity-to-nerve-bundle finding (a
separate result in the same paper) is actually about CADM1. This citation is retained in
the evidence table (verdict VERIFIED — the paper exists and the claim as ultimately stated
matches it) but the paralog distinction is called out explicitly in both the evidence table
and here to prevent it being read as a CADM1-specific molecular finding.

## Genes marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic

**None.** This is a single-gene locus (CADM1 physically spans essentially the entire
~1.1 Mb locus window; no other protein-coding gene body overlaps the interval, and no
alternative candidate gene was nominated by any of nearest-gene/FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS/ABC).
Red-team-skeptic explicitly stated it found no paper arguing for a different gene at this
locus, and no paper directly refuting the CADM1 ectodomain-shedding/emphysema mechanism
papers. CADM1 itself was not marked CONTESTED as a gene — but see below for substantial
downweighting of specific claims about CADM1's relevance to COPD, which the orchestrator
has folded into the "Skeptic verdict" column of the main evidence table (WEAKENED /
OVERCLAIMED annotations) rather than as an exclusion.

## Full red-team-skeptic critique (verbatim)

Preserved in full at `evidence_tables/_status/11_115050001-115300000/critique.md`. Key
points reproduced here for the audit record:

- **Structural/developmental lung biology bucket was flagged as OVERCLAIMED if described as
  "strongest evidence."** The core emphysema-mechanism papers (Mimae 2013 PMID 24092566,
  Hagiyama 2015 PMID 26259600, Ri 2018 PMID 29892598) are a single research group's serial
  extension of one hypothesis, not independent triangulating evidence. Human tissue support
  in the key experiment is n=4 emphysema vs. n=4 controls. No independent replication by any
  other research group has appeared in the ~13 years since the original 2013 paper, despite
  availability of major COPD tissue banks (COPDGene, ECLIPSE, Lung Tissue Research
  Consortium) — flagged as a "slow-burn contestation-by-omission" that should weigh heavily
  in interpretation even though no directly contradicting paper was found.
- **Inoue 2014 (PMID 24964098, pancreatic islet/T2D paper)** was flagged OVERCLAIMED when
  used as corroborating evidence for the lung mechanism — it is the same research group
  generalizing its own shedding hypothesis to a different organ, which if anything
  undercuts lung-specificity rather than reinforcing it.
- **Kikuchi 2006 (PMID 16534787, NSCLC methylation paper)** was flagged OVERCLAIMED /
  mischaracterized when bundled into the "structural/developmental lung biology" narrative —
  it measures a different CADM1 biology entirely (epigenetic promoter silencing in cancer,
  not proteolytic ectodomain shedding in emphysema) and its inclusion risks a gene-name
  cherry-pick (any paper mentioning CADM1 + lung + smoking pulled into one bucket regardless
  of mechanistic relevance).
- **The core inferential gap:** none of the structural/developmental papers connect back to
  the GWAS credible-set variants, to germline CADM1 expression/dosage, or to any eQTL
  direction. They establish an acquired, post-translational, smoking-exposure-driven
  process in already-diseased tissue — not a mechanism for how common noncoding germline
  variants would act through CADM1 to cause or predispose to COPD. This gap was assessed as
  unestablished, not merely underexplored.
- **Immunology/inflammation bucket** was flagged as deserving a lower weight than the
  researcher's own "mixed/nonspecific" framing: general lymphocyte-adhesion biology (not
  lung-specific; Galibert 2005 PMID 15781451, Arase 2005 PMID 16091383), a skin-autoimmunity
  mouse model offered only as an analogy (Giangreco 2011 PMID 22210910), fatal-asthma
  (not COPD) tissue with a paralog-confusion risk (Dragunas 2024 PMID 39484998, Nakanishi
  2008 PMID 18761812), and a review citation contributing CADM1 only as a MeSH term with no
  specific finding (Arthur & Bradding 2016 PMID 27316557).
- **Smoking-mediation hypothesis (prior #1) was flagged WEAKENED, not merely contested.**
  Citation-verifier's direct full-text check of Xu et al. 2020 (PMID 33082346, the largest
  smoking-initiation/cessation GWAS reviewed, N=842,717, MVP+GSCAN) confirmed CADM1/rs3793957
  does NOT appear among its 99 smoking-initiation loci or 13 cessation loci. This is a
  substantive negative finding that argues against, not merely leaves open, a confirmed
  smoking-initiation-GWAS mechanism at this specific locus, despite Open Targets showing a
  smoking-initiation association score for CADM1 (likely derived from a different/broader
  analysis than the one directly checked).
- **Net conclusion (skeptic's own words):** "CADM1 is the only plausible gene in the
  interval and has an unreplicated, biologically plausible but human-genetics-disconnected
  mechanism in acquired emphysema; it is not supported by any GWAS-level lung-function,
  COPD, or (on close inspection) smoking-behavior evidence." This supports "CADM1 is the
  default/only candidate at this locus with a testable mechanistic hypothesis," not "CADM1
  is a well-prioritized causal COPD gene" — reflected in the LOW confidence assigned in the
  main evidence table's Prioritized Gene section.
