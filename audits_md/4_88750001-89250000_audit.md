# Audit trail — Locus 4:88,750,001-89,250,000 (nearest gene FAM13A)

This file records everything EXCLUDED from the evidence table
(`evidence_tables/4_88750001-89250000.md`): citations verified as RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND /
CLAIM_MISMATCH, genes marked CONTESTED by the red-team-skeptic, and process caveats
(e.g. a subagent's search budget being exhausted) that a reviewer should know about before
trusting the evidence table's completeness.

## Pipeline process caveats

- **Red-team-skeptic search budget exhausted before any independent query executed.** By
  the time red-team-skeptic was dispatched, the researcher (52 tool calls) and
  citation-verifier (38 tool calls) had already consumed the session's shared WebSearch
  quota (200/200). The skeptic's critique is therefore reasoning over the already-verified
  evidence supplied in its prompt, NOT a fresh independent literature search. It explicitly
  flagged three follow-up searches still owed if search capacity is restored: (1) whether
  rs7671167/FAM13A reaches genome-wide significance for smoking initiation in the GSCAN
  reference GWAS (Liu et al. 2019, PMID 30643251) — see below, this is also an open
  citation-verifier gap; (2) any direct genetic (not biomarker) literature testing PKD2 or
  SPP1 variants against COPD/FEV1 case-control status, to confirm the "zero independent
  literature" pattern isn't an artifact of under-searching; (3) papers arguing for a third
  causal gene at 4q22 not already surfaced by the researcher.

## Excluded citations (verifier verdict CLAIM_MISMATCH)

None of these are fabricated or retracted — the underlying papers are real and exist as
cited — but the specific claim attributed to each could not be confirmed from the source, so
none of them are used as supporting evidence in the evidence table.

1. **Siedlinski et al. 2013, Hum Genet, PMID 23299987.** Researcher's claim: mediation
   analysis established FAM13A's effect on COPD is "not smoking-mediated," in contrast to
   AGPHD1/CHRNA3. Citation-verifier finding: the paper exists and does discuss direct vs.
   smoking-mediated effects, but its abstract gives specific mediation numbers only for
   CHRNA3/IREB2, not FAM13A specifically — the FAM13A-specific "not smoking-mediated" claim
   is not confirmed at the level of detail asserted. Not used as supporting evidence; the
   smoking-mediation conclusion in the evidence table rests on Pillai et al. 2010 (PMID
   20656943), which IS verified.

2. **Kim CY et al. 2025, Sci Rep, PMID 40118907.** Researcher's claim: this Korean cohort
   study identifies DNAH11 and AADAT as gene-by-smoking-interaction loci "distinct" from
   FAM13A. Citation-verifier finding: the paper does replicate the FAM13A/FEV1-FVC-decline
   association, but its abstract only describes DNAH11/AADAT as "potentially involved in
   gene-environment interactions," not as established distinct loci — the "distinctness"
   framing is an overclaim relative to the source. The core FAM13A replication finding
   itself was not the part flagged as mismatched, but the citation as a whole is excluded
   from the evidence table out of caution since the claim as stated overreaches.

3. **Liu et al. 2019 (GSCAN), Nat Genet, PMID 30643251.** Researcher's claim/question: does
   the FAM13A locus reach genome-wide significance for smoking initiation in this standard
   smoking-behavior GWAS? Citation-verifier finding: could not confirm from the abstract
   whether FAM13A specifically reaches genome-wide significance for smoking initiation in
   this study. **This is an unresolved open question, not evidence either for or against
   smoking-mediation** — it should not be read as either confirming or refuting the
   pulmonary-biology-vs-smoking-behavior hypothesis for this locus. Flagged by red-team
   critique as the single most consequential remaining evidentiary gap for the
   smoking-mediation prior.

4. **Schneider et al. 2009, FASEB J, PMID 19720619** (partial mismatch, core finding
   retained). Researcher's claim: (a) in Ada−/− mice, osteopontin is required for
   adenosine-driven neutrophilia and alveolar airspace enlargement; (b) osteopontin/Ada
   double-knockout mice show REDUCED airspace enlargement compared with Ada−/− alone; (c)
   human COPD lungs show increased distal-airway osteopontin correlated with obstruction.
   Citation-verifier finding: (a) and (c) are confirmed; (b), the specific comparative
   double-knockout result, is not explicitly detailed in the abstract and could not be
   verified. The evidence table above retains this citation for claims (a) and (c) only
   (labeled "core finding only" in the Skeptic-verdict column), and the double-knockout
   comparative claim is not asserted anywhere in the evidence table.

## Genes marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic

### PKD2 — CONTESTED

Red-team-skeptic reasoning (preserved verbatim): "The combination of (a) exclusion from the
physical credible-set/locus window, (b) zero COPD/FEV1-FVC/smoking L2G evidence across the
entire Open Targets GWAS catalog, and (c) supporting literature describing a different gene
(PKD1) in a different disease (ADPKD) means PKD2 should be deprioritized as a candidate
causal gene at this locus. Its MAGMA nomination appears to be a statistical-window artifact,
not evidence of a real regulatory or biological link." Supporting detail: PKD2's genomic
coordinates (chr4:88,007,586-88,077,777, GRCh38) place it ~670kb outside the defined
4:88,750,001-89,250,000 locus window and away from the FAM13A-clustered credible set (all
33 credible-set variants sit at ~4:88.9-89.0 Mb, inside FAM13A). The two verified citations
supporting PKD2 (Driscoll et al. 2008, PMID 18263686; Sang et al. 2019, PMID 31411061) concern
polycystin-1 (the PKD1 gene product, a different gene) and general TRPP2/polycystin-2 channel
physiology in mouse airway smooth muscle, respectively — neither is a COPD genetic-association
study, and neither ties a PKD2 variant to COPD or lung function in humans. PKD2's own top
disease associations in Open Targets (of 1,144 total) are exclusively renal (polycystic
kidney disease 2, autosomal dominant PKD, chronic kidney disease) — COPD does not appear in
the top 25.

### SPP1 — CONTESTED

Red-team-skeptic reasoning (preserved verbatim): "Zero genetic evidence at this locus,
physical distance from the credible set, a nomination method (PoPS) that doesn't require
proximity and is prone to pathway-similarity false positives, and supporting literature that
is entirely biomarker/effector-level and non-specific to COPD — together these argue SPP1
should be deprioritized as a candidate causal gene here, while potentially remaining of
interest as a general downstream inflammatory biomarker unrelated to this GWAS signal."
Supporting detail: SPP1's genomic coordinates (chr4:87,975,667-87,983,532, GRCh38) place it
~770kb-1.3Mb outside the defined locus window. All four verified SPP1 citations
(Schneider 2009, Lee 2014, Shi 2017/2018, Mou 2022) are biomarker/functional-effector studies
(elevated osteopontin protein in COPD lung tissue/plasma/exacerbations, or a mouse
inflammation model) — none is a human genetic-association study linking an SPP1 variant to
COPD risk, and none ties SPP1 specifically to this locus. SPP1's own top disease associations
in Open Targets (of 1,890 total) are osteoporosis, bone disorder, and aortic aneurysm; COPD
does not appear in the top 25. Mou et al. 2022 additionally shows osteopontin underperforms
CRP as an exacerbation-subtype discriminator, reinforcing its lack of COPD-specificity.

### FAM13A — not contested on gene identity, but WEAKENED on mechanism/generalizability

Red-team-skeptic did not contest FAM13A's identity as the causal gene ("the gene-identity
inference itself is exceptionally well-supported and I would not contest it"), but flagged
the following as overclaiming risks for the synthesis narrative (not reasons to doubt gene
identity): (i) FAM13A's opposite risk-allele direction in COPD vs. pulmonary fibrosis/ILA
means "FAM13A drives COPD via structural tissue destruction" is not a clean, unidirectional,
therapeutically-actionable mechanism; (ii) the smoking-mediation conclusion, while
well-supported by Pillai et al. 2010 and the weak Open Targets smoking-initiation L2G signal,
is not fully closed out because of the unresolved GSCAN/FAM13A question (see CLAIM_MISMATCH
#3 above); (iii) FAM13A's immune/inflammatory evidence base is thin and largely indirect
(one whole-locus-class eQTL enhancer-enrichment finding, not gene-specific), so the dominant,
well-supported mechanism is structural/epithelial/developmental, not immune-cell-intrinsic;
(iv) two genuine non-replications (Hardin et al. 2012, PMID 22461431; Kim et al. 2014, PMID
24387323) exist and should be named rather than omitted in favor of only positive
replications. These points are reflected in the "Skeptic verdict" column of the evidence
table and in the Prioritized Gene section, which calls FAM13A confidence HIGH for gene
identity while explicitly caveating the mechanism/generalizability points above.
