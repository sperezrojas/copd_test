# Audit trail — locus 2:550,001-750,000 (TMEM18)

This file preserves everything excluded from the main evidence table
(`evidence_tables/2_550001-750000.md`), plus the full red-team-skeptic critique verbatim,
for reviewer traceability. Nothing in this file should be used as evidence on its own.

## Excluded citations

### Lamonaca P, Prinzi G, Kisialiou A, Cardaci V, Fini M, Russo P. "Metabolic Disorder in
Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD) Patients: Towards a Personalized Approach
Using Marine Drug Derivatives." Mar Drugs. 2017;15(3):81. PMID 28335527, PMC5367038, DOI
10.3390/md15030081.

**Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH.** The researcher originally submitted this citation with the
claim that it does NOT mention TMEM18 by name (i.e., that its Open Targets text-mining
co-mention with COPD was a spurious/incidental hit). The citation-verifier checked the
full text and found this claim was WRONG in direction: the paper DOES mention TMEM18 by
name, quoting: "Authors analyzed the genetic variants that cause lifelong high BMI in FTO
(fat mass and obesity-associated gene, rs9939609), MC4R (Melanocortin-4 Receptor gene,
rs17782313) and TMEM18 (transmembrane protein 18, rs6548238) genes to evaluate the
consequences of the obesity in COPD patients."

**Why excluded despite being a real, non-retracted citation that does mention TMEM18:**
on inspection, this passage is simply a restatement of the Çolak et al. 2016 (PMID
27118560) allele-score finding, which is already captured in the main evidence table
directly from its primary source. Including this citation as well would be redundant
(the same underlying finding, cited twice, once first-hand and once second-hand via a
review) without adding new evidence. It is excluded from the main table not because it is
fabricated or retracted, but because (a) the specific claim as originally framed to the
verifier was inaccurate, and (b) its actual content adds nothing beyond the already-table
Çolak et al. citation.

## Red-team-skeptic critique (verbatim)

**Disclosure on skeptic's own search pass:** The skeptic reported that its own attempted
independent contradiction-search pass (two queries: "TMEM18 lung function FEV1 COPD gene
expression" and "TMEM18 rs6548238 smoking pack-years pleiotropy neural tissue") returned
zero results because its web-search tool budget was already exhausted before its turn
began — i.e., no search was actually executed. The critique below is based on re-reasoning
over the researcher's already-verified evidence, not an independent literature check. This
is recorded transparently and should be treated as an open/undone check, not a completed
null result.

### 1. Is "BMI/smoking pleiotropy, no independent pulmonary biology" well-supported?

Largely yes — one of the better-supported pleiotropy calls in this pipeline, given: (a)
zero Open Targets L2G evidence for COPD and zero evidence of any kind for FEV1, against
127 L2G rows for BMI; (b) the single most mechanistically rigorous TMEM18 study (Larder et
al. 2017, PMID 28811369) is exhaustively CNS/appetite-focused and never examined lung
phenotype; (c) the cell-biology papers (Jurvansuu 2008, 2011) are non-lung and correctly
caveated as such. HOWEVER: "no independent pulmonary support was found" is a
literature-search statement, not equivalent to "pulmonary biology has been tested and
ruled out." Larder et al. didn't look at lungs at all — absence of inquiry, not a negative
result. The researcher does not overclaim the stronger version, which is correct
restraint, but this distinction should be preserved in the final write-up.

### 2. Alternative explanations not considered

The diffuse, 173-variant, entirely-noncoding, flat-PIP (max 0.0368) credible set spanning
~45kb is the architecture expected for a large LD block tagging a regulatory haplotype,
consistent with (strengthening, not weakening) the BMI-pleiotropy reading — this overlaps
the well-known 2p25.3 obesity LD block from the original BMI GWAS discovery papers. The
lead PIP variant (rs4423631) not itself being a known BMI hit is statistically
unremarkable in a large flat LD block (fine-mapping often surfaces a different top-PIP SNP
than the discovery tag SNP) and should not be over-weighted as evidence against the BMI
explanation. A genuine but purely speculative alternative not explored: an uncharacterized
lung-specific regulatory element/lncRNA in this interval acting independently of
TMEM18/BMI biology — zero evidence for this, but it should be named as an untested
possibility rather than treated as closed off.

### 3. Are citations #2 (Çolak 2016) and #1/Claim A+B (Mikkelsen 2025) appropriately weighted?

This is the weakest point in the researcher's synthesis. Both papers test a **composite
BMI allele score** (FTO + MC4R + TMEM18, +2 more in Mikkelsen) — not TMEM18 individually.
These studies cannot distinguish whether the observed BMI-COPD-exacerbation/pneumonia or
BMI-COPD-risk effect is driven by FTO (by far the largest-effect, most pleiotropic BMI
locus), MC4R, or TMEM18's contribution specifically. These should be understood as
evidence that *the BMI-obesity pathway* causally affects COPD outcomes/morbidity, **not**
as evidence that TMEM18's gene product itself does anything COPD-relevant. This
distinction should be made explicit in the evidence table (downgrade from "TMEM18
evidence" framing).

The ambiguity is compounded for the smoking-pleiotropy claim: the verifier could not
confirm the Mikkelsen et al. discussion sentence about "two genetic variants... pleiotropic
effects on smoking... expressed in neural tissue" refers specifically to TMEM18 (vs.
FTO+MC4R, or FTO + one of the other three BMI SNPs). The strongest actual evidence for
TMEM18-specific smoking-pleiotropy is the **Open Targets L2G background data** (7 rows,
0.42-0.44), not the ambiguous secondhand MR-paper sentence — this distinction should be
made explicit in the write-up.

### 4. Should TMEM18 be marked CONTESTED?

**No.** CONTESTED should be reserved for cases where credible literature actively disagrees
about the gene's role. Here there is no such disagreement — every piece of evidence is
compatible with one coherent story (real BMI/adiposity and smoking-behavior genetic
effects at 2p25.3, no demonstrated independent lung mechanism for TMEM18 itself). The
negative-result citations (Mishra & Mishra 2025 not implicating TMEM18; Brazel et al. 2019
not mentioning TMEM18/2p25.3) are transparently reported as documented negative searches,
not competing claims.

**Correct verdict: WEAKENED.** The evidence is real (strong BMI genetic support, moderate
independent smoking-initiation genetic support, a plausible causal MR pathway from
BMI/smoking to COPD morbidity), but the inference that TMEM18 itself (as opposed to the
BMI/smoking pathway broadly, potentially driven by FTO or other loci in composite
instruments) is a COPD-relevant gene overreaches what the gene-specific evidence supports.
Zero L2G evidence for COPD, zero FEV1 evidence, and a mouse KO study that never examined
lungs collectively mean the pulmonary-biology case for TMEM18 specifically is essentially
absent, not just weak.

### 5. Other notes

- No overclaiming detected in handling of negative/excluded citations (#7 Mishra, #8
  Brazel, and the Lamonaca exclusion) — transparent, good-faith reporting.
- The Jurvansuu papers (#4, #6) are appropriately caveated as general/non-lung molecular
  biology, not stretched into false lung relevance.
- Correction needed before synthesis: explicitly downgrade citations #2/#1 from
  "TMEM18-specific evidence" to "BMI-pathway/composite-instrument evidence in which TMEM18
  is one of several unweighted SNPs," and explicitly attribute the smoking-pleiotropy claim
  to the Open Targets L2G background stat rather than to the ambiguous Mikkelsen discussion
  sentence.
- Gene identification thoroughness is not in question (sole protein-coding gene at locus,
  Ensembl-confirmed). Main residual gap is the skeptic's own unfulfilled independent search
  pass (budget exhausted) — treat as an open, undone check.

**Overall verdict for TMEM18 at this locus: WEAKENED (not CONTESTED).**

## Citation-verifier notes on caveated (not excluded) evidence

For transparency, the following VERIFIED citation carries a verifier-noted caveat that is
also reflected in the main evidence table: **Mikkelsen et al. 2025 (PMID 40953920), Claim
B** — the paper's Discussion is confirmed to discuss pleiotropy between "two genetic
variants" in its BMI score and smoking-related traits via shared neural-tissue expression,
but full-text rendering artifacts prevented the verifier from definitively confirming that
the two variants named are FTO and TMEM18 specifically (as opposed to two of the other
three BMI SNPs in the five-SNP score). This is treated as suggestive, not gene-specific-
confirmed, in the main table, with the independent Open Targets L2G smoking-initiation
data (7 rows, 0.42-0.44) used as the primary basis for the TMEM18-smoking-pleiotropy claim.
