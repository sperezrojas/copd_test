# Audit / exclusion appendix — Locus 18:55,400,001-55,650,000 (nearest gene: TCF4)

This file preserves everything excluded from, or critically qualifying, the main evidence table
at evidence_tables/18_55400001-55650000.md — so a reviewer can see what was found and rejected
(or contested), and why, without that reasoning cluttering the primary table.

## Excluded citations

### 1. Guo L, Wang T, Wu Y, et al. "WNT/β-catenin signaling regulates cigarette smoke-induced
airway inflammation via the PPARδ/p38 pathway." Laboratory Investigation 2015/2016.
PMID: 26322419; DOI: 10.1038/labinvest.2015.101.

- **Researcher's original finding:** This paper reports that "the mRNA expression of WNT
  transcription factor TCF4 negatively correlated with the pack year" in airway epithelium of
  smokers with/without COPD, in the context of canonical WNT/beta-catenin signaling, PPARdelta,
  and p38 MAPK in cigarette-smoke-induced airway inflammation.
- **Citation-verifier verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH (wrong gene).** Justification (verbatim): "Paper
  exists and is not retracted (published 2016, not 2015 as stated). However, the paper frames
  'TCF4' as 'the WNT transcription factor,' which is canonical nomenclature for TCF7L2
  (Wnt/beta-catenin pathway), not the chr18 bHLH E2-2 gene. Researcher's gene-identity concern
  is justified — this paper appears to address TCF7L2, not the TCF4 subject of this review."
- **Why excluded:** This locus's candidate gene is TCF4/E2-2 (chr18 bHLH transcription factor,
  ENSG00000196628), the Pitt-Hopkins syndrome gene. TCF7L2 (chr10, ENSG00000148737) is the
  canonical Wnt/beta-catenin-pathway TCF/LEF-family transcription factor and was, in some older
  literature, informally also referred to as "TCF4." Because this paper explicitly frames its
  "TCF4" as the WNT transcription factor, it is very likely reporting on TCF7L2 under legacy
  nomenclature, not on the gene actually implicated at this locus. Both the researcher and the
  citation-verifier independently flagged this, and it is excluded from the evidence table
  rather than counted as smoking/airway-inflammation evidence for this locus's TCF4.
- **Note also raised by the same paper, minor:** the citation-verifier additionally noted the
  paper's actual publication year is 2016, not 2015 as originally cited by the researcher — a
  secondary reason it should not be quoted with the original year/citation details even if the
  gene-identity question were resolved differently.

## Contested / weakened gene verdicts (red-team-skeptic, verbatim reasoning preserved)

### ATP8B1 — Verdict: CONTESTED

> "The contesting evidence is already in hand (no new search needed): PMID 30636723 and PMID
> 35204783 describe a fibrotic, not obstructive, pulmonary phenotype for Atp8b1 loss-of-function
> — the opposite structural direction from COPD — and this is compounded by (a) ATP8B1 sitting
> 2Mb outside the actual credible-set window with no variant overlap, (b) the unresolved question
> of whether OT's COPD score for ATP8B1 even reflects this locus's signal, and (c) zero
> spirometric/pulmonary-function trait evidence in Open Targets despite the COPD score. I could
> not run an independent search to look for additional contradicting or supporting papers (budget
> exhausted) — flagging that gap explicitly for synthesis-lead/re-review."

Full supporting reasoning from the critique (verbatim):

> "**LD / nearest-gene bias — this is the dominant problem.** ATP8B1 sits ~2Mb outside the
> stated locus window and does not overlap any credible-set variant (lead or otherwise). PoPS is
> a similarity/network-based method precisely because it doesn't require positional proximity,
> but 2Mb is far beyond plausible LD or typical enhancer-promoter looping for a 'same regulatory
> locus' argument, and no such long-range mechanism is claimed anywhere in the evidence. This is
> a genuinely distinct genomic signal being folded into this locus's gene list on
> statistical-similarity grounds alone.
>
> The OT COPD association is not clearly the same signal. Open Targets does report a real
> gwas_credible_sets-based COPD score for ATP8B1 (0.259), but the underlying variant/study is
> unresolved (null fields), so it's unknown whether this reflects rs596668's locus at all or an
> independent, more local COPD GWAS hit near ATP8B1's own gene body 2Mb away. Treating this as
> supporting evidence for locus 18:55.4-55.65Mb specifically is not currently justified — this
> needs to be resolved (which specific GWAS/variant is behind that score) before it counts as
> evidence for this locus rather than for a neighboring, separate association.
>
> Structural/developmental biology directly contradicts, not just fails to support, the COPD
> hypothesis. Fukumoto 2019 (PMID 30636723) and Westermann-Clark 2022 (PMID 35204783) — same lab,
> same/related Atp8b1 mutant mouse — both describe a pulmonary fibrosis phenotype (alveolar
> epithelial apoptosis, aberrant club-cell proliferation, interstitial fibrosis resembling IPF;
> MMP7 upregulation, ciliated/club-cell abnormalities). This is the opposite structural direction
> from COPD/emphysema: restrictive/fibrotic tissue remodeling rather than destructive/obstructive
> airspace enlargement. Per prior #2, COPD-relevant structural evidence should show concordance
> with an obstructive/airflow-limitation phenotype; here the only mechanistic lung evidence for
> ATP8B1 points toward a different, largely opposite pulmonary disease process, and this is
> reinforced by Open Targets showing zero pulmonary-function-trait evidence for ATP8B1 (no FEV1,
> no FEV1/FVC, nothing) despite its real COPD case-control score. That combination — COPD
> case/control signal with no corroborating spirometric signal and a mechanistic literature
> pointing toward fibrosis — is close to the 'COPD association without supporting
> pulmonary-function evidence' scenario prior #2 explicitly says should raise suspicion of an
> alternative pathway (here, possibly restrictive/fibrotic lung disease misclassified or comorbid
> within a COPD case definition, or simply a different signal)."

**Disposition:** ATP8B1's two structural-biology citations (PMID 30636723, PMID 35204783) are
retained in the main evidence table because they are VERIFIED and genuinely about ATP8B1, but the
gene as a whole is treated in the Prioritized-gene synthesis as non-competitive with TCF4 at this
specific locus, per this CONTESTED verdict.

### TCF4 — Verdict: WEAKENED

> "The positional case for TCF4 is fine, but the inference that this represents COPD- or
> lung-specific biology overreaches what's shown: the pulmonary-function/FEV1 signal is real in
> Open Targets but unconfirmed in the actual literature citations, the extreme pleiotropy at this
> variant undermines any single-trait signal's specificity, the smoking-initiation alternative is
> untested and plausible, and the immune biology, while gene-specific, is not lung-specific. This
> is a locus where 'TCF4 nearest gene showed up for FEV1 and for 146 other things' is being asked
> to carry more weight than it should without the smoking-locus and lung-function-locus overlap
> questions being resolved."

This WEAKENED verdict (not a full CONTESTED — the skeptic explicitly did not find literature that
actively contradicts a TCF4-lung link, only that the case is underdetermined) is the basis for
capping the Prioritized-gene confidence at LOW rather than MODERATE/HIGH in the main evidence
table, despite TCF4 being the clear best positional/mechanistic candidate at this locus.

## Unresolved locus-overlap questions (not excluded, but explicitly unconfirmed — flagged for
## follow-up rather than asserted in the main table)

For three of TCF4's statistical-genetics citations, neither the researcher nor the
citation-verifier could confirm, from the abstract/metadata available to them, whether this exact
locus (chr18:~55.4-55.65 Mb / rs596668) is actually among each paper's reported
genome-wide-significant loci:

- Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019 (COPD case-control GWAS, PMID 30804561) — 82 loci reported; this
  locus's presence among them unconfirmed.
- Shrine et al. 2019 (lung-function GWAS, PMID 30804560) — 279 signals reported; this locus's
  presence among them unconfirmed.
- Liu et al. 2019 / GSCAN (smoking-behavior GWAS, PMID 30643251) — 406 loci reported; this
  locus's presence among them unconfirmed.

These citations are retained in the main evidence table as VERIFIED (the papers exist, are not
retracted, and are accurately described methodologically), but the claim of locus-level overlap
with each is explicitly marked UNCONFIRMED in the table rather than asserted, per the
red-team-skeptic's critique. Confirming or refuting each of these three overlaps (e.g., via each
paper's supplementary loci tables) is the single most direct way to raise or lower confidence in
the Prioritized-gene call above LOW.
