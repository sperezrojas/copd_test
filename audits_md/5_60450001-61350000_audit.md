# Audit appendix — locus 5:60,450,001-61,350,000 (nearest gene SMIM15)

This file lists everything excluded from, or flagged within, the main evidence table
(`evidence_tables/5_60450001-61350000.md`), with reasoning preserved verbatim from the
citation-verifier and red-team-skeptic subagents, so a reviewer can audit why it was
excluded/flagged without needing this file to use the evidence table itself.

## Excluded citations (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH)

### 1. Abusheikha AJ, et al. Front Nutr 2025. PMID 40709334. DOI 10.3389/fnut.2025.1564741.

**Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH.**

Researcher's original claim: "A cynomolgus monkey diet/atherosclerosis transcriptomics
study in which an SMIM15 ortholog region is mentioned incidentally."

Citation-verifier's finding (verbatim): "Paper exists (cynomolgus monkey atherosclerosis
transcriptomics study as claimed), but abstract lists transcriptome changes (ERC2, FZD9,
BAZ1B, BCL7B, TBL2, MLXIPL, etc.) with **NO mention of SMIM15 or its ortholog**. Claim of
'SMIM15 ortholog region mentioned incidentally' is unsupported by abstract."

**Disposition:** Excluded from the evidence table entirely. This citation must not be
used anywhere in this locus's evidence base — the paper is real, but does not say what
was attributed to it. Its exclusion is material: it was the only citation offering even
tangential support for SMIM15 beyond pure positional/ABC proximity, so with it removed,
SMIM15's evidentiary case rests on two non-specific bioinformatic-signature papers
(retained in the main table) plus its nearest-gene/ABC status alone.

## Genes flagged by red-team-skeptic (CONTESTED / WEAKENED)

### NDUFAF2 — CONTESTED (on logic grounds, not new contradicting literature)

Red-team-skeptic's search budget was exhausted before it could run any independent
searches for this locus (disclosed explicitly in its critique — see full critique text
below), so this CONTESTED call is a logic-based critique of how the existing verified
citation could be misread, not a newly-found contradiction.

Verbatim critique: "The Yan et al. 2025 case report is titled around neonatal persistent
pulmonary hypertension — a pulmonary *vascular* phenotype (abnormal vasoreactivity/
vascular remodeling in the neonatal pulmonary circulation), mechanistically unrelated to
COPD's airway/parenchymal obstruction and emphysema pathophysiology, and it's a single
recessive severe pediatric case, not a common-variant adult-onset association. The
researcher does correctly discount this ('mechanistically distinct pulmonary-vascular
case-report link... not common-variant airway obstruction') — that discounting is
appropriate and should be preserved, not softened, in synthesis. The bigger risk is at
the synthesis layer: because NDUFAF2 overlaps a large fraction of lower-PIP credible-set
variants, there's a temptation to let 'NDUFAF2 has *a* pulmonary case-report phenotype'
quietly upgrade its plausibility relative to genes with zero pulmonary literature at all
(e.g., LINC02057, SMIM15-AS1). That would be an error — a rare recessive vascular disease
phenotype is not evidence for a common noncoding-variant airway-obstruction mechanism,
and the researcher is right to say the Open Targets 'COPD' score is a
clinical-trial-precedence artifact, not genetic evidence. **CONTESTED, on logic
grounds** — not because I found new contradicting literature (I couldn't search), but
because the pulmonary-hypertension case report is genuinely liable to be misread
downstream as 'NDUFAF2 has a lung phenotype, therefore plausible COPD gene,' when the
correct reading is 'NDUFAF2 has a lung phenotype that argues against, not for, this
specific disease mechanism.' Flagging explicitly so synthesis-lead treats this as a
near-miss rather than support."

**Disposition:** Retained in the main evidence table (the underlying citation, PMID
40462436, is VERIFIED as a real, accurately-described paper), but explicitly marked
CONTESTED in the Skeptic verdict column, and treated in the Prioritized-gene synthesis as
a near-miss/argument-against rather than supporting evidence for NDUFAF2.

### ZSWIM6 — WEAKENED

Verbatim critique: "This is the gene I'd push back on hardest. The write-up already does
real work flagging the 'bronchial arch' mistranslation and the HNSCC IL-6/IL-8 finding as
indirect, which is good discipline. But two things are under-caveated relative to how the
computational tools (FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS) will be read by a synthesis step: (1) ZSWIM6 sits
150-500kb from the actual credible-set variant mass with *zero* eQTL/Hi-C/ABC link
offered — meaning the 'top computational pick' status is riding entirely on MAGMA/PoPS
gene-level scores, which are known to be biased toward large, well-annotated,
highly-expressed/constrained genes and can pick up signal from a neighboring gene's
LD/regulatory landscape without being the causal gene. That caveat is present in the
findings but is easy to lose under the 'top pick' framing — synthesis-lead should not let
'FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS-prioritized' read as 'best-supported.' (2) The smoking-mediation
narrative for ZSWIM6 is built on an Open Targets *aggregate* smoking-initiation score
(0.347, ranked ~10th of 743 traits) — this is a database summary statistic, not a
verified primary GWAS hit, and the researcher is explicit about this, which is correct.
But note the score is not even that dominant in ZSWIM6's own profile — it's lower than
the Mendelian disease scores and comparable to/lower than several *other* psychiatric
traits (schizophrenia 0.465, bipolar 0.341) and even lower than asthma (0.294) is close
by. A locus that associates with schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, ADHD, autism, and
educational attainment as a *bundle*, with smoking merely one trait among many, looks
more like a generic neurobehavioral/pleiotropic pathway than a smoking-specific
mechanism — the 'smoking-mediated COPD' story is one plausible thread among several, and
framing it as *the* alternative hypothesis (vs. broader neuropsychiatric pleiotropy) may
itself be an oversimplification. **WEAKENED** — evidence for ZSWIM6 real (strong
Mendelian/psychiatric gene) but the inferential chain 'computational top pick → therefore
biologically relevant to this locus → therefore probably smoking-mediated' has two
unverified links (positional/regulatory disconnect from the credible set; unverified
primary smoking-GWAS association) that should not be allowed to read as established."

**Disposition:** All ZSWIM6 citations retained in the main evidence table (all VERIFIED,
none contradicted as facts), but the gene-level interpretation is marked WEAKENED, and
the Prioritized-gene synthesis treats ZSWIM6 as runner-up rather than top pick despite its
FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS status.

## Locus-level critique (not gene-specific — applies to the whole locus)

Verbatim from red-team-skeptic: "The single most important fact in this dossier is
structural, not gene-specific: a 115-variant credible set with max PIP 0.109, zero
coding/splice variants, and — critically — **no FEV1/FEV1-FVC concordance anywhere in
Open Targets or the literature reviewed for any of the 7 genes**, despite
Sakornsakolpat/Shrine being companion COPD/spirometry GWAS papers from the same
consortium generation. Per prior #2, absence of spirometric concordance at a locus that
surfaced only in the COPD (not the lung-function) GWAS is genuinely notable and arguably
the strongest single piece of evidence in the whole dossier — more informative than any
individual gene's functional literature. The researcher/framing mentions this only in
passing... but doesn't foreground the alternative hypothesis explicitly enough: **this
locus could be a COPD-GWAS-only false-positive/statistical artifact, or a signal driven
by phenotype misclassification/comorbidity/pleiotropy (e.g., through smoking behavior,
psychiatric comorbidity via ZSWIM6's neuropsychiatric bundle, or case-ascertainment
correlated with something non-pulmonary) rather than a real pulmonary-biology locus at
all.**... the weight of evidence across this whole locus leans toward
'unresolved/possibly non-pulmonary signal' rather than toward any single gene being a
credible COPD effector. Synthesis-lead should consider stating this as the headline
conclusion for the locus rather than picking a 'best' gene among seven largely
negative-to-weak candidates."

Red-team-skeptic also disclosed: "My WebSearch budget was already exhausted (200/200)
before I could run any independent queries... None of the 7 genes received any
independent contradiction-search from me. My critique... is therefore based entirely on
scrutinizing the internal logic, consistency, and rhetorical framing of the researcher's
own findings and the Open Targets data already provided — not on new literature...
I recommend this locus get one more targeted research pass (specifically: ZSWIM6 +
smoking initiation/cigarettes-per-day GWAS primary papers; a direct check of Shrine et
al. 2019 and Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019 supplementary/gene tables for this exact locus,
which neither the researcher nor I confirmed) before synthesis finalizes a ranking."

**Disposition:** This concern is carried into the Prioritized-gene section of the main
evidence table as an explicit LOW-confidence caveat and an identified follow-up need
(direct eQTL/Hi-C evidence; independent confirmation of a locus-specific smoking-GWAS
hit; direct inspection of the Shrine/Sakornsakolpat supplementary gene tables for this
sub-locus). The orchestrator did not dispatch an additional research pass for this
because the critique did not identify a new citation requiring verification (only a
recommendation for further search), and the pipeline's per-locus loop treats this locus
as complete with the caveats above explicitly documented for the study team's review; the
study team may choose to prioritize a follow-up pass on this specific locus in light of
this recommendation.
