## Excluded citations

- **PMID 29885764** (Wachinger et al. 2018, Biol Psychiatry) — **CLAIM_MISMATCH**. The researcher cited this paper both for "ZCWPW1 is an established Alzheimer's disease GWAS locus" (VALID, verifier confirmed this specific claim) and for the mechanistic claim "ZCWPW1 is a reader of H3K4me3/H3K36me3 histone marks deposited by PRDM9 at meiotic recombination hotspots, functioning in double-strand break repair/synapsis during meiosis." The citation-verifier found this paper is a brain-imaging-genetics study (ZCWPW1 associated with brain asymmetry), and does NOT address the meiotic histone-reader mechanism at all. The AD-GWAS-locus portion of the claim (attributed instead to the properly VERIFIED PMID 38527854, Reus et al. 2024, Brain, in the main evidence table) is retained; the meiotic-mechanism claim citing PMID 29885764 is excluded from the evidence table as unsupported by this specific source. (The meiotic-histone-reader mechanism itself may be true of ZCWPW1 from other, uncited sources — that is a separate matter from whether this particular citation supports it, which it does not.)

- **Locus-by-locus smoking-GWAS overlap** — no citation excluded here (none was offered), but flagged as an unresolved open question: the researcher could not confirm or rule out, via PubMed keyword search, whether this specific 7q22.1 signal shows an independent association with smoking-initiation/cessation/heaviness GWAS traits (e.g., in the GSCAN study, PMID 30643251, cited only as general background, not as a locus-specific finding). Per prior #1, this is an important open question for future work, not resolved by the evidence at hand.

## Genes marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic

### PILRA — CONTESTED

The researcher's "strongest overall candidate" framing for PILRA is not supported once the following internal contradictions are weighted properly:

1. The single COPD-specific genetic claim for PILRA (PMID 42089751, Jiang et al. 2026, Briefings Bioinform — VERIFIED as existing and as accurately describing the paper's own claims) states PILRA "strongly colocalized at shared genetic loci" between early-onset asthma and COPD and is "significantly downregulated in macrophages from COPD patients." This directly **contradicts** the Open Targets structured colocalization pipeline (see opentargets.md checkpoint and main evidence table), which found NO colocalization support for PILRA at this locus — coloc support at this locus was found only for PVRIG. An MR/bioinformatics colocalization claim from one paper should not simply outrank a dedicated colocalization pipeline result; the discrepancy needs reconciliation, not adoption of whichever claim is more favorable to PILRA.

2. A related, independent MR paper (PMID 41286868, Jia et al. 2025, BMC Pulm Med — VERIFIED) found PILRA among 11 proteins nominally ("risk-enhancing") associated with COPD only at the discovery stage, and explicitly NOT among the most robustly replicated hits (IL27RA and TIE1 were the replicated hits). This non-replication is already present in the researcher's own writeup and undercuts the "strongest candidate" label.

3. The missense variant rs1859788 (PIP=0.147, the same or second-highest-PIP non-STAG3 variant in this credible set) is the well-known Alzheimer's disease-associated PILRA coding variant. Essentially all of PILRA's high-quality mechanistic literature located by the researcher (ligand-binding effects on ceramide/complement C4A/HSV-1 glycoprotein B, PMID 30388101, VERIFIED; AD risk-reduction association, PMID 39149451, VERIFIED but a preprint) is characterized in the Alzheimer's-disease literature context, not a lung context. Given this locus is a known, independently-replicated pleiotropic AD signal, a shared coding variant with strong AD literature but only equivocal/internally-contradicted COPD literature is a textbook LD/pleiotropy confound, not clean distinguishing evidence for a lung-specific causal mechanism.

4. The general immune mechanism literature for PILRA (neutrophil regulation, PMID 23142774, VERIFIED; monocyte/macrophage tissue infiltration, PMID 26840635, VERIFIED) is real and gene-specific, but was demonstrated in generic inflammatory/fibrotic mouse models, never in lung/airway tissue. The one immune-infiltration correlational study that comes closest to a "disease" context (PMID 37396579, VERIFIED) is in atrial fibrillation, not lung disease, and is correlational/bioinformatics-only — the researcher itself already flagged this as "mixed/nonspecific" evidence.

**Conclusion:** PILRA remains a plausible candidate gene at this locus (retained in the main evidence table, not deleted), but the "strongest candidate" framing is downgraded to CONTESTED specifically because of the internal contradiction between PMID 42089751 and the Open Targets pipeline, and the non-replication in PMID 41286868 — both already documented in the verified citation record, not new unverified claims.

## Genes marked WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic

### PVRIG — WEAKENED (genetic case strong, mechanistic case weak/borrowed from an unrelated disease context)

PVRIG is the only gene at this locus with formal Open Targets L2G and colocalization support for COPD/FEV1-FVC (h4=0.92, 0.91), and the same credible set is h3-dominant (non-colocalizing) for Alzheimer's disease — genuinely reassuring evidence that the COPD/lung-function signal and the AD signal at this locus are likely driven by distinct causal variants, not simple LD bleed-through. However: (1) this colocalization result could not be located in any peer-reviewed publication by the researcher — it exists only as an Open Targets database output, unverified by citation-verifier, and could reflect a methodological artifact of applying single-causal-variant colocalization assumptions in a dense, 17-gene-implicated LD block (per PMID 30804561); (2) all verified mechanistic literature on PVRIG/CD112R (PMID 34174928, PMID 41485718) is TIGIT-family checkpoint/NK-T-cell-exhaustion biology from tumor immunology — including one murine lung *carcinoma* model, not any COPD/airway-inflammation model — an oncology mechanism that does not map cleanly onto COPD's more neutrophilic/innate-driven or non-exhausted CD8+ cytotoxic inflammatory phenotypes. The researcher's own statement that "the statistical case is stronger than the mechanistic case" for PVRIG is judged well-calibrated, not overclaimed.

### CNPY4 — WEAKENED (fine-mapping support genuine; mechanistic bridge partly speculative)

CNPY4 carries the second-highest-PIP credible-set variant (rs60458236, PIP=0.153) and has a real, verified innate-immune mechanism (TLR1/TLR4 surface-trafficking chaperone, PMID 16338228 and PMID 22447933) — general immune-cell biology, never tested in lung tissue or a COPD model specifically. Separately, the researcher's link from a sphingolipid-homeostasis preprint (PMID 41279395, itself unreviewed) to "alveolar/surfactant biology" is the researcher's own speculative bridge — that preprint contains no lung or COPD content at all. This is flagged as a specific instance of the general "mechanism shown in unrelated context, extrapolated to lung" pattern this review is designed to catch, and is a second instance (alongside the ZCWPW1/PMID 29885764 mismatch above) of the researcher constructing a mechanistic narrative not directly stated in a cited source — treat any such uncited "bridge" sentences as speculation going forward, even when the underlying citation itself is VERIFIED to exist.

## Overall locus-level conclusion from red-team-skeptic (preserved verbatim reasoning)

"The evidence genuinely does not distinguish which single gene is causal... Given this is a 17-gene-dense pleiotropic locus previously best known for an unrelated disease (AD), the most defensible synthesis position is that no single gene is confidently prioritized, and that the locus may plausibly harbor two distinct signals — one AD/PILRA-associated (via the shared missense variant) and one potentially lung/PVRIG-associated (via the separate coloc'd signal) — rather than one gene explaining both diseases."

This orchestrator's Prioritized-gene call (PVRIG, confidence LOW; see main evidence table) is made in light of, and does not contradict, this skeptic conclusion — LOW confidence was chosen specifically because the skeptic's own analysis shows the tie between PVRIG and PILRA is not resolved by current evidence.
