## Excluded citations (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH)

is retained in the main evidence table (labeled non-substantive) rather than excluded, since it
was VERIFIED, not RETRACTED/NOT_FOUND/CLAIM_MISMATCH; it carries essentially no evidentiary
weight in the synthesis.

## Genes marked CONTESTED / WEAKENED by the red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning preserved)

Procedural caveat disclosed by the skeptic itself: its web-search budget was exhausted before
it could run any independent contradiction-search (all six search attempts failed at the tool
level, zero results retrieved). The critique below is an internal-consistency review of the
already-verified findings, NOT a fresh independent literature check — this gap is disclosed,
not resolved, and should be treated as still open.

### STAG1 — WEAKENED
"The statistical case is real and should stay prioritized, but confidence should be explicitly
tempered by the total absence of lung-relevant mechanistic or expression-QTL support; the
depressive-symptoms PRS citation should not be used as corroborating evidence and the synthesis
lead should say the STAG1 case rests on statistics alone." The Arnau-Soler et al.
depressive-symptoms PRS finding (PMID 30718454) "should carry near-zero weight: it is a
whole-genome polygenic-score cross-trait correlation... not a STAG1-specific or even
locus-specific signal — it says nothing about this variant, this gene, or lung biology, and
citing it as 'evidence' for STAG1 in COPD would be overclaiming by association-of-associations."
The cohesinopathy case-report literature is "informative for LoF intolerance/dosage sensitivity
... but report[s] no pulmonary phenotype among dozens of characterized patients, which is
itself a mild negative data point against a lung-specific loss-of-function mechanism, though
these are rare highly-penetrant developmental syndromes and may not generalize to a common
noncoding regulatory allele."

### MSL2 — HOLDS (not contested)
"This is accurately characterized by the researcher as weak with no lung/immune connection —
no overreach here because no claim was stretched." Retained in main table without downgrade.

### NCK1 — WEAKENED
"This is the case I'd push back on hardest. The oncology citations are lung-cancer cell-line
biology..., not COPD-relevant. The five T-cell-signaling papers are consistent and from what
looks like a coherent research program..., but every single one is generic T-cell immunology in
non-lung, non-COPD systems — no airway T-cell infiltration data, no COPD patient tissue, no COPD
genetic association, and NCK1 is explicitly *not* LoF-constrained (o/e ~0.49), undercutting a
dosage-sensitive common-variant mechanism... there is a real risk synthesis will read this as
meaningfully corroborating evidence when it is actually generic pathway biology that could be
recruited to support almost any T-cell-signaling gene near any inflammatory-disease locus...
it should be presented as 'a plausible but entirely non-COPD-specific pathway hypothesis,' not
as a 'genuine immune connection' to this disease, and the lack of LoF constraint is an
additional reason for caution not fully foregrounded in the researcher's framing."

### SLC35G2 — HOLDS (not contested)
"Accurately characterized as weak. The one citation... is tangential at best and not
lung-tissue-specific; the researcher does not overclaim it." Retained without downgrade.

### PPP2R3A — HOLDS (not contested)
"The researcher's 'plausible but indirect' framing is appropriate and not overclaimed. I'd flag
one alternative explanation not raised: PP2A regulatory subunit biology is very pleiotropic
(cell-cycle/phosphatase signaling across many cell types), so cross-tissue relevance could
easily be coincidental pathway overlap rather than a COPD-specific mechanism." Retained without
downgrade, but this pleiotropy caveat is preserved here for reviewers.

### PCCB — CONTESTED (as a serious candidate) — and appropriately so
"The researcher's own conclusion — that the high L2G-like score is likely a
database-aggregation artifact rather than genuine causal signal — is sound reasoning and
consistent with the near-zero PIP across all PCCB credible-set variants. A recessive
inborn-error-of-metabolism gene with no dosage-sensitivity/LoF-constraint signal is a poor fit
for a common noncoding regulatory variant mechanism, and the one lung-relevant citation
(Pcca-mutant mouse organ-panel biochemistry) is incidental systemic-metabolite biology, not
causal lung pathology. This is one of the more useful 'contradicting/alternative explanation'
findings in the whole packet: it shows that trusting L2G/aggregate score ranking alone would
have picked the wrong gene. I agree with excluding PCCB as a serious candidate." PCCB is
retained in the evidence table (its one citation was VERIFIED) but should be read as a
rejected/artifactual candidate, not a live contender, per both the researcher's own assessment
and the skeptic's independent agreement.

### IL20RB — WEAKENED (downgraded further than the researcher's own framing)
"This gene was added post hoc via region-enumeration, has no credible-set variant directly
annotated to it, and the NCK1 3'UTR-near-TSS observation is only a positional coincidence
flagged as ambiguous, not a demonstrated regulatory link (no eQTL, no Hi-C/ABC contact
reported). Its cited evidence is skin keratinocyte biology (irrelevant tissue) and lung-cancer
tumor-immune-microenvironment biology... — again non-COPD, oncology-context immunology...
tumor-microenvironment immune correlations are not evidence for a COPD airway-inflammation
mechanism... I would not carry IL20RB forward as anything more than a positional note...
treating it as a 'candidate worth downstream synthesis attention' overstates what's actually
been shown, since there is no direct genetic evidence tying any credible-set variant to
IL20RB." The skeptic recommends downgrading IL20RB from "secondary candidate" (researcher's
framing) to "positional note only, not a genetically-supported candidate," pending an actual
eQTL/regulatory link that has not been produced.

## Skeptic's overall summary judgment (verbatim)

"The most defensible overall picture, given only what's verified, is: STAG1 has the strongest
statistical/positional case but zero mechanistic lung evidence (statistics-only prioritization,
appropriately caveated); PPP2R3A has the most specific (if indirect and non-COPD)
structural-lung biology; NCK1's T-cell-signaling story is real biology being stretched to fit a
COPD inflammatory prior without a shred of COPD- or lung-specific data, and its lack of LoF
constraint further weakens a common-variant dosage argument; PCCB's high aggregate score is
correctly identified by the researcher as very likely a scoring artifact and should not be
carried forward as a serious candidate; IL20RB lacks any directly-annotated variant and should
be demoted from 'candidate' to 'positional note' pending better regulatory evidence; MSL2 and
SLC35G2 are both accurately characterized as weak with no overreach. None of the genes at this
locus currently have concordant FEV1/FEV1-FVC spirometric evidence or smoking-behavior-trait
data (both explicitly absent, per the prompt), which — per priors #1 and #2 — should keep the
whole locus's causal-gene assignment at low-to-moderate confidence regardless of which single
gene synthesis ultimately favors."

## Other notes for reviewers

- **Name-collision risk:** the symbol "STAG1" ambiguously matches two Open Targets/Ensembl
  records — the intended gene (ENSG00000118007, cohesin subunit) and PMEPA1 (ENSG00000124225,
  an unrelated TGF-beta/androgen-responsive transmembrane protein with an old alias also
  rendered "STAG1"). All literature and Open Targets data in the evidence table were confirmed
  to be about the cohesin subunit; no PMEPA1 mislabeling was found in the researcher's search
  results, but this remains a risk for any future re-search of this locus.
- **Gene-assignment ambiguity at the 3' boundary:** the two NCK1-annotated 3'UTR credible-set
  variants (rs856662, rs835647; PIP=0.0057 each) sit essentially at the transcription start
  site of the neighboring gene IL20RB, meaning these variants are plausible IL20RB
  promoter/regulatory variants as well as NCK1 3'UTR variants. This ambiguity is unresolved by
  current evidence (no eQTL or chromatin-contact data available for either gene at this
  specific position) and is preserved here rather than arbitrarily assigned.
- **Open Targets colocalization gap:** no molecular-QTL colocalization data was retrievable for
  any gene at this locus through the available Open Targets GraphQL query surface. This is
  explicitly a limitation of the API layer accessible to this pipeline, not confirmed evidence
  of a true absence of colocalization — a caveat that should not be forgotten when reading the
  "no colocalization evidence" rows in the Open Targets summary as if they were negative
  findings.
