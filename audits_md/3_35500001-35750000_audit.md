## Full red-team-skeptic critique (verbatim)

**Search limitation flagged up front:** The skeptic's own independent WebSearch budget was exhausted before a single query could be run (all four planned contradiction-searches — ARPP21xWHR, ARPP21xCOPD, rs7612999, "3p22 COPD locus" — returned "budget exhausted"). WebFetch attempts to independently check the You et al. 2025 abstract, NCBI Gene, and GeneCards were all blocked (captcha/cookie/403). **Neither the researcher nor the skeptic performed any independent contradiction search for this locus** — flagged as a genuine, unfilled gap, not a negative result. Everything below is internal-consistency critique of the evidence as given, not new literature.

### Overclaiming on the anchor citation (PMID 40155373)
The single evidence row driving the entire Open Targets COPD-association score (0.8899, aggregate 0.329) is the "COPD or GERD (MTAG)" study tied to PMID 40155373 — and the citation-verifier already flagged that this paper's main text/abstract names *neither* ARPP21 nor this locus; its three named "novel" loci are elsewhere (17q21.1, 3p26.1, 8q21.3). The researcher's framing is accurate as a description of the paper's *overall* findings, but quietly conflates "this locus appears in the underlying MTAG summary statistics" (routine — any genome-wide-significant locus would appear in an L2G/credible-set database built from those sumstats) with "this paper specifically validates ARPP21 as COPD-relevant" (unconfirmed — could be a supplementary-table entry never discussed, or could not appear at all). A reader skimming the synthesis could easily believe this locus was a highlighted finding of a peer-reviewed cross-trait paper, when its only confirmed status is "one row in a database, sourced from an MTAG whose lung/GI genetic correlation is broad, not this-locus-specific." Recommend treating the COPD-relevance of this locus as resting on database evidence, not confirmed literature discussion.

### Adiposity vs. GERD mediation — unequal evidentiary weight bundled together
The two strands of the researcher's alternative hypothesis are not equally strong and should not be presented as a single package:
- **Adiposity**: genuinely strong, locus-specific genetic evidence — 13/14 colocalizing studies at this exact credible-set lead are WHR/adiposity/renal-sodium traits with h4 = 0.92-0.99. Real, high-confidence, locus-specific signal, not generic reasoning.
- **GERD**: much thinner — rests only on the trait label of one combined MTAG study (COPD+GERD jointly; a locus can clear genome-wide significance in an MTAG driven predominantly by one component trait, and nothing here establishes which), plus four general, non-locus-specific reviews. Two of those (obesity paradox papers) describe obesity being associated with *better* COPD survival/reduced hyperinflation in already-diagnosed patients — a prognosis phenomenon — not a diagnostic-misclassification phenomenon. Using them to support "adiposity biology could inflate/confound COPD case status in GWAS" is a directional mismatch not reconciled by the researcher; the more relevant (uncited) mechanism would be obesity-driven restrictive spirometric patterns or symptom/ICD-code misclassification inflating COPD case counts, not the survival paradox. This is a soft overclaim: real papers, but not quite the right mechanism for the hypothesis being built.

### Under-explored alternative: MIR128-2, not ARPP21 protein, as the causal unit
This is the strongest additional point beyond the researcher's own writeup. Four of the verified citations (Rehfeld 2018, Donzelli 2011, Qian 2012, Tan 2021) all describe ARPP21 not as an independent lung/immune actor but specifically in its role as *host gene* of miR-128(-2), which it antagonizes post-transcriptionally. Combined with (a) an intronic-dominated ~90-variant credible set sitting inside/near ARPP21, and (b) zero eQTL/pQTL/sQTL colocalization for ARPP21 itself in Open Targets — this pattern is at least as consistent with the credible set acting on hosted-miRNA expression/processing (MIR128-2) as on ARPP21 mRNA/protein dosage. Neither the researcher nor Open Targets' structured lookup appears to have queried MIR128-2 QTL/expression data specifically. Since PoPS/FLAMES/nearest-gene assignment tools generally aren't built to separate a host protein-coding gene from an embedded miRNA, "ARPP21" being the unanimous tool output does not by itself resolve this ambiguity. Flagged as an open mechanistic question and a concrete follow-up search (MIR128-2 eQTL/GWAS at this locus) for future work.

### LD/neighbor-gene bias
Given the single-protein-coding-gene structure of the window and the intronic/intergenic-near-ARPP21 credible set, standard risk of conflating ARPP21 with a different neighboring protein-coding gene is lower than typical — but the skeptic could not independently confirm the absence of other genes in the 250kb window (all external lookups blocked), so this should be treated as asserted-but-not-independently-verified by the skeptic (it was independently confirmed by the researcher via Ensembl REST, however).

### Spirometry/smoking priors
Agrees with the researcher's own reading — spirometric concordance is genuinely contradicted (a real red flag per the pipeline's stated priors: a COPD case-control signal with zero FEV1/FEV1-FVC support, no MAGMA/ABC corroboration, and near-total colocalization with adiposity rather than lung traits is close to a textbook profile for a non-lung-mechanism confound), and smoking-mediation is honestly reported as neutral/no-data rather than stretched either way.

### Retraction handling
Appropriately conservative — the Chai et al. 2021 (PMID 34047500) item was correctly excluded with independent confirmation of the retraction notice (PMID 38767306). No concerns.

### Thoroughness flag
This locus's case for/against ARPP21 rests on a database pattern (Open Targets) that neither the researcher nor the skeptic could independently corroborate or attack via literature search this session (both hit WebSearch budget exhaustion). Given how load-bearing that single OT evidence row is, a dedicated follow-up search pass is recommended when budget resets: (1) ARPP21 + WHR/body-fat-distribution literature, (2) whether PMID 40155373's supplement actually lists this locus/ARPP21, (3) MIR128-2 QTL/GWAS data, (4) any paper proposing a different gene at 3:35.5-35.75Mb.

### Overall verdict: WEAKENED
The citations are real and accurately described in isolation, but the overall inference — that ARPP21 is a COPD-relevant lung/immune gene — overreaches what the evidence supports. The most specific, quantitatively strong genetic signal at this exact locus (colocalization) points to adiposity/body-composition biology, not lung function; ARPP21's own verified biology is neurodevelopmental/muscle-developmental via a miR-128-hosting mechanism with no lung nexus; the spirometry prior is contradicted outright; and the single "COPD" evidence row anchoring relevance is unconfirmed to even reference this locus/gene in the source paper's discussed content. This falls just short of CONTESTED only because no new citation was found (or could be searched for) naming a different causal gene or explicitly refuting ARPP21 — but the internal evidence pattern argues about as strongly against a direct COPD/lung mechanism as anything in this locus argues for one.

No new citations were identified by the skeptic requiring additional verification.
