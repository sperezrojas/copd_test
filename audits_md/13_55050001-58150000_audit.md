## CONTESTED / WEAKENED gene verdicts (red-team-skeptic, verbatim reasoning preserved)

### LOC124903233 — CONTESTED
"Zero PubMed literature, unresolved biotype conflict (lncRNA vs. 'uncharacterized protein'), and — most importantly — it sits ~63 kb *outside* the stated 3.1 Mb window despite being the pipeline's sole 'nearest gene' call. Nearest-gene heuristics are already the weakest form of locus-to-gene mapping (LD/nearest-gene bias, prior #1 in my instructions); here the nearest-gene call doesn't even fall inside the analyzed window, which suggests either a boundary-definition error upstream or that the window itself was drawn without this gene in mind. There is no biological content to evaluate — no expression, no function, no disease association of any kind. This is not 'weak evidence for a candidate,' it is 'no candidate.' I'd flag this for the synthesis lead as not a real candidate and recommend the locus-boundary/nearest-gene discrepancy be checked by whoever runs the pipeline (this may be a coordinate-system or window-padding bug worth fixing at the source, not something the researcher needs to solve alone)."

### LINC02335 — CONTESTED
"This gene has the strongest positional case (dozens of credible-set variants sit intronically within it) but zero independent literature of any kind — no expression data, no functional characterization, no disease association, nothing in PubMed. Positional overlap by low-PIP noncoding variants is necessary but nowhere near sufficient evidence for candidacy; lncRNA loci are frequently transcribed incidentally or regulate genes at a distance, and with all variant PIPs reportedly <0.0003, this credible set does not confidently implicate any single functional element, let alone this lncRNA's function. There is no biology to weigh (no smoking link, no spirometry link, no immune link) because there is no biology reported at all — the candidacy is 100% positional. Given the instruction to actively look for reasons to doubt, and given I found literally nothing to support this gene doing anything (COPD-relevant or otherwise) in the researcher's findings, this should be marked CONTESTED for lack of any functional grounding, not merely WEAKENED. This is distinct from LOC124903233 only in that it is at least genuinely inside the window and physically overlapped by the credible set — that's a positional argument, not a biological one."

### PCDH17 — WEAKENED, bordering CONTESTED
Full reasoning (numbered points from the skeptic):
1. *Distance.* ">1.3 Mb from the nearest credible-set variant, at the far edge of an arbitrarily-drawn 3.1 Mb window. Being the sole conventional protein-coding gene in a wide window is a weak, essentially default form of candidacy — it reflects an absence of alternatives more than positive evidence. This should not be treated as comparable in strength to a fine-mapped, colocalized, or even modestly LD-linked candidate."
2. *Retraction.* "One of the oncology citations (PMID 30863170, nasopharyngeal carcinoma) is retracted... Even setting that aside, none of the oncology literature (bladder, colorectal, breast, ovarian, hepatocellular, Ewing sarcoma) is pulmonary, so it has no direct bearing on COPD biology regardless of verification status."
3. *Neurodevelopmental/psychiatric literature (PMID 28070120, PMID 38747058).* "This is verified and genuinely genome-wide-significant for mood disorders via a brain-expressed, synaptic mechanism (ROCK2/actin, dendritic spines). This is real biology — but it is CNS biology with no stated or plausible mechanistic bridge to lung tissue or airflow obstruction. If this locus's COPD signal is in fact tagging PCDH17's neurobiological role, that would argue for a behavioral/smoking-mediation pathway... rather than a direct pulmonary mechanism — but the researcher found no smoking-trait or nicotine-dependence GWAS literature for PCDH17 either, so this is speculative, not evidenced. The one candidate link that could have supported smoking-mediation (the dietary-trait GWAS association for the lead SNP itself, PMID 32066663) failed verification as CLAIM_MISMATCH... That means the researcher currently has zero verified evidence connecting the lead variant to any external phenotype, smoking-related or otherwise."
4. *Immune evidence.* "The single RA peripheral-blood methylation finding (PMID 40410297) is thin: methylation-as-biomarker studies are associative, tissue-nonspecific (peripheral blood, not lung or airway), and RA is not COPD... this is exactly the kind of 'nonspecific' evidence the framework asks me to flag as such rather than let it stand in for COPD-relevant immunology. It should be read as noise/weak signal, not as support for an immune mechanism at this locus."
5. "No pulmonary or spirometric evidence whatsoever was found — this is the single most important negative result per prior #2... Its total absence, combined with the distance from the credible set, undermines any claim that PCDH17 is acting through lung biology."
6. *Constraint data* "(LoF-constrained, oe=0.295) shows PCDH17 is under strong selective pressure, consistent with an important developmental gene..., but constraint alone says nothing about tissue of action for this particular GWAS signal — it should not be used to bootstrap pulmonary relevance."

**Overclaiming check (skeptic):** "The researcher's writeup, as summarized, does not appear to overtly claim 'PCDH17 causes COPD'... But the framing of PCDH17 as 'the only one with constraint data' and the most literature-rich gene carries an implicit risk that a reader could conclude PCDH17 is the best-supported candidate at this locus, when in fact none of its literature bears on lung or COPD biology and it is the furthest gene from the credible set. I'd flag this as a moderate overclaiming risk in downstream synthesis... 'most literature found' ≠ 'most relevant literature found.'"

## Locus-level flag (red-team-skeptic, verbatim)

"I recommend this locus be flagged to the study team as **lacking a credible candidate gene**. Basis:
- All computational prioritization methods (FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS/ABC) returned NA upstream — this locus started with no gene-level support.
- Open Targets returned zero COPD evidence for all three candidates.
- No spirometric, smoking, or lung-structural evidence exists for any candidate.
- The two genes with any positional claim to the credible set (LOC124903233, LINC02335) have zero functional or disease literature of any kind, and LOC124903233's own coordinates fall outside the stated window — worth a data-QC check upstream.
- PCDH17, while richly studied in oncology and neuropsychiatry, sits >1.3 Mb outside the credible set and has no pulmonary biology; its inclusion reflects 'only protein-coding gene in a wide window' rather than genuine locus-to-gene evidence.
- The sole variant-level phenotype link for the lead SNP failed citation verification, leaving this locus with essentially no verified external phenotype association at all.

This is a case where the biological priors don't so much get contradicted as go unanswered — there simply isn't enough evidence in any domain (smoking, spirometry, immune) to adjudicate a mechanism, and the honest conclusion is that this locus needs either better fine-mapping/functional data (eQTL/colocalization in lung tissue, chromatin interaction data for LINC02335) or should be down-weighted/deprioritized in the final gene-prioritization output rather than assigned to PCDH17 by default."
