## Excluded citations (verdict: NOT_FOUND for the specific claim tested)

These three papers ARE real, peer-reviewed, non-retracted publications — citation-verifier
confirmed their existence. They are excluded from the main evidence table because the
specific claim the researcher flagged them for (whether this MAML3/4q31.1 locus appears
among their reported genome-wide-significant loci) was checked directly by citation-
verifier via full text and found NOT supported. Their absence is itself informative and is
discussed in the main table's "Prioritized gene" section as a meaningful negative result,
but the papers are not used as affirmative evidence FOR MAML3.

1. **Sakornsakolpat P et al.** "Genetic landscape of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease
   identifies heterogeneous cell-type and phenotype associations." Nat Genet. 2019. PMID
   30804561, PMCID PMC6546635, DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0342-2.
   Verdict: NOT_FOUND (locus-specific). Citation-verifier: "This paper identifies 82 COPD
   loci but MAML3 and the 4q31.1 region are not among them. The abstract and supplementary
   tables do not list this locus."

2. **Shrine N et al.** "Multi-ancestry genome-wide association analyses improve resolution
   of genes and pathways influencing lung function and chronic obstructive pulmonary
   disease risk." Nat Genet. 2023. PMID 36914875, PMCID PMC10011137, DOI
   10.1038/s41588-023-01314-0.
   Verdict: NOT_FOUND (locus-specific). Citation-verifier: "MAML3 and the 4q31.1 region do
   not appear in this multi-ancestry lung-function/COPD GWAS (1,020 signals, 559 genes),
   despite searching all available text and tables."

3. **Liu M et al.** "Association studies of up to 1.2 million individuals yield new
   insights into the genetic etiology of tobacco and alcohol use." Nat Genet. 2019. PMID
   30643251, PMCID PMC6358542, DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0307-5. (GSCAN)
   Verdict: NOT_FOUND (locus-specific). Citation-verifier: "MAML3 and 4q31.1 region do not
   appear in this GSCAN smoking/alcohol GWAS (566 variants, 406 loci) for any smoking-
   related trait."

Note (per red-team-skeptic): the absence of this locus from GSCAN 2019 should NOT be read
as clearing MAML3 of a smoking-mediation contribution — Open Targets' aggregate
association scores show a strong smoking-initiation signal for MAML3 that likely derives
from a larger/more recent smoking GWAS not checked in this pipeline. This is an open
question, not a resolved one.

## Genes/candidates marked CONTESTED or WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic

### CCRN4L / NOCT — CONTESTED
Red-team-skeptic verdict (verbatim): "The MAGMA/PoPS statistical prioritization is
directly contradicted by the researcher's own physical gene-content check, which found no
plausible mechanistic bridge (no coloc, no shared regulatory element, no intervening
evidence) between the credible-set variants and NOCT. Absent a chromatin-interaction or
long-range eQTL analysis actually connecting this locus to NOCT expression, the MAGMA/PoPS
flag should not be given independent evidentiary weight over the positional/mechanistic
case for MAML3 — it should be reported to synthesis-lead as a discordant statistical
signal whose biological basis is unverified and looks distance-implausible on current
evidence, not as a competing candidate on equal footing."

Supporting facts: NOCT (ENSG00000151014, chr4:139,015,414-139,045,939 GRCh38) sits ~800-
900 kb outside the stated locus boundary (4:139,850,001-140,150,000) and the credible-set
variant range (139,943,603-139,982,001), separated by six other protein-coding genes
(ELF2, NDUFC1, MGARP, NAA15, RAB33B, SETD7, MGST2) — none evaluated as intervening
regulatory targets. NOCT has zero recorded COPD association in Open Targets (checked
across all 86 diseases associated with the gene) and no COPD/spirometry/lung-function
literature evidence was found by the researcher.

### MAML3 — WEAKENED
Red-team-skeptic verdict (verbatim): "The physical/positional case (credible set entirely
within MAML3 introns) is solid, but the inference that this reflects 'genuine
lung-intrinsic biology' as opposed to smoking-behavior/neurobehavioral pleiotropy is not
adequately supported — it is asserted as a live possibility by the researcher (correctly)
but stated with more confidence in the synthesis than the evidence bears. The absence from
Sakornsakolpat 2019 and Shrine 2023 should be weighted as meaningful contradicting
evidence, not just a gap, and the case for 'genuinely lung-relevant rather than purely
smoking/neurobehavioral' is currently unresolved, tilting if anything toward
smoking/neurobehavioral pleiotropy given the Open Targets pattern."

## Full red-team-skeptic critique (verbatim)

### Disclosure on search coverage
Before critiquing, the skeptic attempted the mandated baseline contradiction-search pass
(one query per candidate gene: "MAML3 4q31.1 COPD GWAS lung function locus" and "CCRN4L
NOCT Nocturnin lung COPD spirometry association"). Both queries failed — the session's
web-search budget (200/200) was already exhausted before either returned results.
**Neither MAML3 nor CCRN4L/NOCT received any independent search pass from the skeptic.**
The critique was based entirely on internal-consistency analysis of the researcher's own
findings and the citation-verifier's verdicts (including the informative NOT_FOUND
pattern), not on new literature retrieved. Synthesis-lead should treat "contradicting
evidence search" as **not done** for this locus by the skeptic specifically; if a search
budget resets, this locus should be prioritized for a proper contradiction pass, especially
around (a) any smoking GWAS larger than GSCAN 2019 that might explain the Open Targets
smoking-initiation score for MAML3, and (b) NOCT/circadian-gene COPD comorbidity
literature.

### MAML3

Genomic evidence base is solid on containment, weak on disease-specificity. All 20
credible-set variants sit in MAML3 introns and no other gene physically overlaps the
interval — real, not a nearest-gene artifact. But the region isn't gene-desert-free
either: ELF2, NDUFC1, MGARP, NAA15, RAB33B, SETD7, MGST2 sit between MAML3's body and
NOCT, and none were evaluated as potential long-range regulatory targets. Unexplored
alternative-gene hypothesis worth flagging, not a fatal gap, but "only MAML3 physically
overlaps" shouldn't be read as "only MAML3 is mechanistically possible."

The negative evidence is under-weighted in the researcher's synthesis. MAML3/4q31.1 is
confirmed absent from both Sakornsakolpat 2019 (82-locus COPD GWAS) and Shrine 2023
(1,020-signal lung-function GWAS) — the two largest, most directly relevant dedicated
studies for this exact phenotype class. This is a meaningful null result from adequately
powered studies, functioning as quasi-contradicting evidence against a robust, replicable
COPD/spirometry signal at this locus. Per prior #2, the complete absence of any
FEV1/FEV1-FVC association is a real strike, not just a "gap" — it should be reported as
evidence the locus fails the spirometry-concordance test, not passed over.

The supporting phenotype associations are real but each one step removed from COPD, and
weaker than the prose suggests:
- BHR (Nieuwenhuis 2016): "nominally associated" — sub-genome-wide-significance, in an
  asthma-severity cohort, not COPD.
- CMH (Dijkstra 2014): p=7.57e-6, also below standard genome-wide significance, and
  critically, this cohort is heavy smokers without COPD — an endophenotype in smokers, not
  a COPD case-control or spirometric result. Does not establish a smoking-independent lung
  effect; compatible with a gene-by-smoking-exposure interaction that only manifests in
  already-heavy smokers, arguably still smoking-mediated biology rather than a pure
  lung-intrinsic mechanism.
- Aging (Timmers 2022), migraine (Gasperi 2025), GERD/smoking pleiotropy (Yan 2024): none
  pulmonary, and two explicitly reinforce the neurobehavioral/pleiotropy profile.

Smoking-mediation case is at least as strong as the lung-biology case, possibly stronger.
Open Targets shows MAML3's smoking-initiation, smoking-cessation, smoking-behavior,
alcohol, cannabis-dependence, and ADHD association scores are comparable to or exceed its
COPD score, all via the same gwas_credible_sets datasource — an independent statistical
genetics signal, not literature the researcher had to chase down. Yet the dedicated GSCAN
smoking GWAS (Liu 2019) does not show this locus. This is an unresolved contradiction the
researcher flagged but didn't resolve: the Open Targets smoking association almost
certainly comes from a larger/more recent smoking GWAS than GSCAN 2019 (not verified in
this pipeline), meaning the "absence from GSCAN" should not be read as clearing MAML3 of
smoking-mediation — if anything the broader Open Targets pattern argues the opposite.

Mechanistic/developmental evidence is generic Notch biology, not MAML3-lung-specific.
MamL3-single-knockout mice show no phenotype; the only phenotypes reported for maml3
specifically are cardiac (zebrafish), and the researcher's targeted search for MAML3 in
airway epithelial differentiation came up empty — an honestly flagged gap. Immune evidence
for MAML3 (as opposed to MAML1/MAML2) is entirely absent.

Overclaiming check: individual citation-level claims from the researcher are appropriately
hedged ("nominally associated," "independently replicated in aging"). The overreach is in
the synthesis sentence — "two independent, at-least-partially smoking-independent
lung-tissue eQTL/phenotype associations exist... supporting a genuine pulmonary role" —
which elevates two sub-genome-wide-significant, phenotype-adjacent, smoker-subgroup
findings to "genuine pulmonary role" while downplaying that the same locus is null in the
two best-powered, most directly relevant COPD/spirometry GWAS.

MAML3 verdict: WEAKENED (see above).

### CCRN4L / NOCT

The physical-distance finding is itself strong contradicting evidence against the
MAGMA/PoPS prioritization, and should be treated as such rather than just "context." NOCT
sits ~800-900kb from the credible set, separated by six other protein-coding genes, none
of which were shown to be in an LD block or regulatory loop with the credible-set
variants. A MAGMA gene-based test picking up a gene this far outside its typical
annotation window (unless an unusually large window/LD structure was used, which wasn't
reported) raises real doubt about whether the prioritization reflects true biology versus
gene-set/pathway signal diffusion or a MAGMA window artifact. No eQTL or colocalization
evidence was found linking the MAML3-locus credible-set variants to NOCT expression — that
absence is the crux of the problem, and it was actively checked (Ensembl gene-content
lookup), not merely unaddressed.

No COPD/spirometry signal anywhere — confirmed by the researcher's own Open Targets check
(86 diseases, none COPD-related) and no literature found. The one lung-relevant citation
(Maragozidis 2015) is a squamous-cell lung cancer prognostic/mechanistic study, a different
disease with different biology (tumor survival/cell-adhesion genes), not an
airway-obstruction or inflammatory mechanism — citing it as lung-relevant risks conflating
"lung tissue" with "COPD-relevant lung biology."

Systemic metabolic/circadian biology is well-established but off-target for this
pipeline's phenotype (liver, adipose, intestine, bone marrow — no airway, no immune cell
type implicated).

NOCT verdict: CONTESTED (see above).

### Locus-level notes for synthesis-lead
- Thoroughness: the researcher's searches were reasonably thorough for both genes
  (explicit gap-flagging for airway-epithelial Notch mechanism and MAML3 immune biology is
  good practice, not a red flag). The one real thoroughness gap is the skeptic's own, not
  the researcher's: no independent contradiction-search was completed for either gene in
  this review pass due to search-budget exhaustion.
- Alternative hypothesis meriting more explicit consideration: this locus could be a
  "false lead" in the sense of being a real, replicated genetic association with
  smoking-related neurobehavioral traits (initiation/cessation/dependence) that surfaces in
  the COPD GWAS purely because the COPD summary statistics are not adjusted for smoking
  (prior #1) — with no independent lung mechanism at all. The evidence assembled neither
  confirms nor rules this out; it is at least as well-supported by the data presented as
  the "genuine pulmonary biology" narrative, and synthesis-lead should present it as the
  leading rival explanation rather than a minor caveat.
- Neither gene has any verified spirometry (FEV1/FEV1-FVC) association — per prior #2 this
  is a significant strike against confident prioritization of either gene at this locus.

## Researcher's own flagged uncertainty (for completeness)
The researcher explicitly noted it could not confirm, from abstracts alone, whether MAML3
appears in Sakornsakolpat 2019, Shrine 2023, or the GSCAN smoking GWAS supplementary
tables, and asked citation-verifier to check directly — which citation-verifier did,
returning NOT_FOUND for all three (see above). The researcher also explicitly flagged (1)
the absence of any MAML3-specific airway-epithelial mechanistic paper (searched, 4 hits
returned, none confirmed to mention MAML3) as a genuine evidence gap rather than a
suppressed finding, and (2) the absence of any MAML3-specific (vs. MAML1/MAML2) immune-
cell-type study as a genuine absence.
