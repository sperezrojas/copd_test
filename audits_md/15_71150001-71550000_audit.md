### THSD4 — overall verdict WEAKENED (not accepted at face value as "very strongly supported")
The core statistical-genetics case (large, cross-ancestry-replicated FEV1/FVC GWAS signal;
sole gene-body overlap; COPD L2G rank 3/303 diseases) is genuinely strong, but the
researcher's overall verbal summary ("very strongly supported... highly plausible mechanism")
overreaches relative to what's actually assembled:
- (a) The mechanistic story (elastic-fiber assembly) is argued entirely by cross-tissue
  analogy (aorta -> bone/endothelium -> lung) with zero direct lung phenotype data in any
  species. Structural/developmental biology sub-score should be MODERATE, not HIGH; only the
  statistical genetics axis is HIGH. These two axes should not be blended into one verdict.
- (b) The sole eQTL/regulatory link (Kim et al. 2026) is one very recent, uncorroborated,
  multi-gene-candidate finding (THSD4 named alongside ADAM19, C4B, PSMA4) — should be treated
  as provisional/single-source, not folded into the same confidence tier as the quantitative
  spirometric replications.
- (c) COPD case-control replication (as opposed to quantitative spirometry) has a genuinely
  mixed history: Hardin 2012 (Polish severe-COPD smokers — a comparative negative result,
  since other loci in the same underpowered study DID reach significance) and Yang 2013 (Han
  Chinese null, possibly reflecting real population-specific LD/allele-frequency differences,
  not just power) are independent negative replications; Soler Artigas 2011 found only a
  "suggestive, not genome-wide significant" signal in a >31,000-person cohort — moderately
  large by 2011 standards, so "just underpowered" undersells this. The honest framing:
  THSD4's *quantitative* FEV1/FVC signal replicates robustly and repeatedly; its *COPD
  case-control* signal has a genuinely mixed replication history that should not be smoothed
  over as merely a power problem solved by later mega-GWAS.
- (d) The locus's colocalization with blood pressure, pulse pressure, and aortic-diameter/
  distensibility GWAS is at least as strong (H4>0.95) as its lung-function colocalization.
  This raises a genuine, currently unresolved alternative hypothesis: THSD4 may affect
  connective-tissue/vascular elastic properties generally, with the "lung function" GWAS
  association either genuinely reflecting shared elastic-fiber biology that also acts in lung
  parenchyma, OR partly an artifact of correlated measurement (e.g., spirometry being
  influenced by chest-wall/vascular compliance, or both traits being downstream of a shared
  aging-related connective-tissue degeneration process). The pleiotropy does not refute a lung
  mechanism, but it means colocalization with lung-function traits cannot be treated as
  lung-specific evidence when the identical signal colocalizes just as strongly with unrelated
  vascular hemodynamic traits. THSD4's most direct in vivo disease evidence (Elbitar 2020
  mouse aortic aneurysm model) is vascular, not pulmonary.
- (e) The smoking-mediation conclusion (THSD4 is lung-intrinsic, not smoking-behavior-mediated)
  partly rests on non-literature Open Targets data (never-smoker COPD colocalization,
  H4=0.9935) rather than solely on verified literature. The "zero PubMed results for
  THSD4+smoking" finding IS a verified literature-search result (an absence-of-evidence
  finding, appropriately weak on its own), but it was blended with the Open Targets
  never-smoker-colocalization point into a single directional conclusion without marking that
  the more quantitatively compelling piece is non-literature, non-citation-verified data. These
  should be tiered separately: literature = weak absence-of-disconfirming-evidence; Open
  Targets colocalization = supportive but a categorically different, lower-tier evidence type.

No paper was found (by researcher or skeptic) arguing FOR a different specific causal gene at
this locus over THSD4, other than Yao et al. 2013's own mention of neighboring UACA/TLE3 as
alternative candidates at the same FEV1/FVC signal — flagged as an unresolved loose end worth
a dedicated look in a future pass, not as a competing causal claim with its own support.

### CT62 — CONTESTED / re-labeled INSUFFICIENT (not confidently non-causal)
The researcher's dismissal of CT62 (zero PubMed literature; zero Open Targets genetic-
association score; never an L2G hit; inference that its FLAMES prioritization "likely reflects
the same underlying THSD4-region signal") rests entirely on absence of contrary data, not on
any test that directly rules CT62 in or out. Specifically, no GTEx or other lung-tissue
expression data was consulted to check whether CT62 is even transcribed in lung — a basic,
cheap check that is missing for a locus-nested lncRNA. The skeptic's verdict: CT62 should be
labeled INSUFFICIENT (deprioritized relative to THSD4, given total absence of any positive
signal) rather than confidently ruled out as non-causal, since absence of literature for an
under-studied lncRNA is expected regardless of true causal status. Recommended follow-up (out
of scope for this pipeline run): check CT62 lung-tissue expression via GTEx before treating it
as definitively subordinate to THSD4.

### LRRC49 — verdict NOT SUPPORTED (near-disqualifying, not a soft caveat)
LRRC49 does not physically overlap the locus window or THSD4's gene body per Ensembl
coordinates (15:70,853,239-71,071,702, versus the locus window 15:71,150,001-71,550,000 and
THSD4 at 15:71,096,952-71,783,383) — its MAGMA prioritization most likely reflects MAGMA's
extended gene-boundary window rather than true physical or regulatory overlap with the
credible set. Combined with LRRC49 never being an L2G hit at this locus across 88 GWAS credible
sets, a COPD genetic-association score ~13x weaker than THSD4's, and its only substantive
gene-specific literature (De Souza Santos et al. 2008) being unrelated breast-cancer estrogen-
signaling biology with zero lung/respiratory/immune relevance, the skeptic recommends this be
stated as a near-disqualifying fact, not a soft footnote, and that LRRC49 not be given equal
table-formatting weight to THSD4/CT62 in synthesis.

## Methodological limitation disclosed by red-team-skeptic

The red-team-skeptic subagent's WebSearch budget was exhausted before it could run independent

therefore based on close reading of the researcher's verified findings and the Open Targets
structured summary, not on independent literature discovery. No new citations were surfaced by
the critique step for this locus. This is a disclosed coverage gap, not a silent one, and
applies equally across all three candidate genes.
