## Excluded citations (verifier verdict != VERIFIED)

1. **Lee JJ, Wedow R, Okbay A, et al.** "Gene discovery and polygenic prediction from a
   genome-wide association study of educational attainment in 1.1 million individuals." Nature
   Genetics. 2018. PMID: 30038396. DOI: 10.1038/s41588-018-0147-3.
   - **Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH.**
   - **Claim as proposed by researcher:** this paper is the plausible source of a genome-wide
     significant educational-attainment association (P=8x10^-10) reported in GWAS Catalog for
     rs1557341, a CELF4-intronic, second-highest-PIP (0.189) variant in this COPD credible set.
   - **Why excluded:** citation-verifier found the paper's abstract identifies "1,271 independent
     genome-wide-significant SNPs" for educational attainment but does not mention rs1557341 or
     CELF4 specifically; the SNP-to-study attribution could not be confirmed. The underlying
     GWAS-Catalog phenotype association itself (rs1557341 <-> educational attainment) is a
     structured-database fact and is not disputed — only the specific attribution of that
     association to this particular paper as its literature source is unconfirmed.

2. **Howard DM, Adams MJ, Clarke TK, et al.** "Genome-wide meta-analysis of depression identifies
   102 independent variants and highlights the importance of the prefrontal brain regions." Nature
   Neuroscience. 2019. PMID: 30718901. DOI: 10.1038/s41593-018-0326-7.
   - **Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH.**
   - **Claim as proposed by researcher:** this paper is the plausible source of a major depressive
     disorder association (P=7x10^-9) reported in GWAS Catalog for the locus's own COPD lead
     variant rs7504262.
   - **Why excluded:** citation-verifier found the abstract reports "102 independent variants" for
     depression but does not mention rs7504262; the SNP-level attribution to this specific paper

## Genes marked CONTESTED, with red-team reasoning preserved verbatim

**CELF4 — CONTESTED** (the only candidate gene at this locus).

Full red-team-skeptic reasoning (Pass 1, verbatim):

> 1. CLAIM_MISMATCH verdicts vs. "behavioral/psychiatric architecture" claim. The verifier rejected
> the specific attribution of rs1557341 -> Lee 2018 and rs7504262 -> Howard 2019 (the abstracts
> don't mention the SNPs). But the underlying GWAS Catalog entries themselves (structured database
> records, not literature claims) still stand independently of which paper is cited as source — GWAS
> Catalog is a curated repository, and the phenotype-SNP associations (math ability, MDD,
> neuroticism, educational attainment) are catalog facts, not claims requiring PMID-level
> verification of that specific sentence. So the researcher's broader point — "this locus's top
> variants are catalogued for behavioral/cognitive/psychiatric traits, not lung traits" — is
> defensible on the GWAS Catalog evidence alone. What is NOT defensible is any implication that a
> specific mechanistic paper demonstrated this; the researcher appropriately avoided asserting that
> and instead flagged both attributions as failed. This is a correct, non-overclaiming use of a
> partially-rejected citation. No further downgrade needed here.
>
> 2. Smoking-mediation framing. The researcher's language — "possibly, though not confirmed,
> including smoking-related behavior" — is appropriately hedged. Notably, the researcher also ran an
> explicit, direct test of this hypothesis (Kim et al. 2021 gene-by-smoking interaction study) and
> found CELF4/18q12.2 is NOT among the genome-wide-significant smoking-interaction loci, and
> separately found zero hits searching smoking-initiation/cessation/heaviness GWAS directly for
> CELF4. This is actually evidence against a clean smoking-mediation story, not for one — the
> researcher should be given credit for not overclaiming smoking mediation despite it being a
> tempting default explanation for a neurobehavioral locus. If anything the synthesis should state
> more plainly: direct smoking-GWAS mediation is searched-for and not found; the "possible" smoking
> link is speculative and rests only on the general observation that MDD/neuroticism/educational-
> attainment loci often overlap smoking-behavior loci, not on any direct evidence at this locus.
>
> 3. Kalsotra 2008 (PMID 19075228) stretched as CELF4 evidence. Yes, this is overstretched. The
> paper is about CELF1/CUGBP1 in cardiac postnatal splicing — CELF4 is a different paralog with
> distinct tissue expression (predominantly neuronal, per Wagnon 2011 and Yang 2007) and
> non-overlapping functional data in this citation. The verifier's own verdict already flags this
> ("CELF4 itself is not the primary protein studied"), and the researcher listed it under "general
> biology" as CELF-family support rather than direct CELF4 evidence — that placement is appropriate,
> but it should not be allowed to add any weight to a pulmonary or developmental argument for CELF4
> specifically. Family-wide paralog reasoning is weak inference here: CELF1 and CELF4 are not shown
> to be functionally redundant or co-regulated in this citation, and paralogs in RNA-binding protein
> families frequently have divergent tissue-specific targets. This citation should be treated as
> background context on CELF-family biology only, contributing essentially zero weight to a lung
> mechanism.
>
> 4. Alternative explanation for the COPD/lung-eQTL signal. The structured Open Targets data is the
> most important piece of counter-evidence here and the researcher's synthesis under-weights it
> slightly: there IS a CELF4 lung eQTL, but it colocalizes with BMI and waist-hip ratio — not with
> COPD or any lung-function trait. This is a strong, specific piece of evidence that the lung
> expression signal is very likely a pleiotropic/confounded readout of an adiposity-related
> regulatory variant that happens to be measured in lung tissue in GTEx, not a lung-COPD-relevant
> eQTL. Combined with the Fjorder 2019 case report (CELF4 regulatory domain disruption causing
> obesity/hypertension) and the testis exon-usage colocalization with behavioral/psychiatric traits,
> the more parsimonious explanation is that this locus is a pleiotropic hub for
> metabolic/neurobehavioral traits (obesity, education, depression, neuroticism) that happens to
> reach nominal significance in the COPD GWAS via BMI-linked or behavior-linked
> confounding/pleiotropy — not via any independent lung mechanism, and not cleanly via smoking
> either (since direct smoking-GWAS and smoking-interaction searches were negative). The researcher
> gestures at this ("distinct metabolic/obesity-related mechanism") but the language is somewhat
> buried at the end of a sentence; this should be foregrounded as the leading alternative
> hypothesis, arguably better supported than the smoking-mediation hypothesis given the actual
> colocalization data.
>
> 5. Should CELF4 be marked CONTESTED? I'd land on CONTESTED, but for a specific, precise reason:
> not because there's a rival paper positively implicating a different causal gene at this locus
> (there's only one gene here, so classic LD/nearest-gene competition doesn't apply), but because
> there is a direct, quantitative body of contradicting evidence against the COPD-relevance of the
> mechanism being proposed — namely (a) 24 molecular QTLs across many tissues including lung with
> zero colocalization to COPD or any lung-function trait despite ample statistical power to detect
> such colocalization if it existed, (b) a dedicated gene-by-smoking-interaction COPD study that
> explicitly does not find this locus, (c) zero smoking-GWAS hits, and (d) zero
> lung-function/COPD-replication literature. This is a case where the statistical (locus-level,
> credible-set PIP-based) evidence for COPD is real but every mechanistic layer that could explain
> why — pulmonary structural/developmental, immune, smoking-mediated, or lung-eQTL/colocalization —
> has been actively searched and has come back negative or redirected toward a different trait
> domain (obesity/behavior). That pattern (real statistical signal, systematically absent or
> redirected mechanism) is precisely what CONTESTED is meant to flag for synthesis-lead, distinct
> from HOLDS (mechanism supports it) or a simple WEAKENED (mechanism is thin but not actively
> contradicted). None of the contradicting citations here are new/unverified — they were already run
> through the verifier (Kim 2021 VERIFIED as a negative data point; Open Targets colocalization data
> is structured-database, not literature, so doesn't require verifier treatment) — so no new
> UNVERIFIED flag is needed, but synthesis-lead should treat the Open Targets colocalization pattern
> and the Kim 2021 negative finding as the load-bearing contradicting evidence.
>
> Overall verdict: CONTESTED. The locus-level COPD statistical association is genuine and CELF4 is
> the only plausible gene, but the totality of eQTL colocalization (redirects to BMI/WHR, not
> COPD/lung function), the direct smoking-interaction and smoking-GWAS negative searches, and the
> complete absence of pulmonary structural/developmental/immune literature together constitute
> active, multi-layered contradicting evidence against a direct pulmonary mechanism — not just "weak
> support" but a specific, searched-for and repeatedly negative mechanistic case, with
> obesity/metabolic and neurobehavioral pleiotropy as the better-supported alternative explanations
> for the raw statistical signal.

**Process limitation flagged by red-team-skeptic (both critique passes):** the critique above is
based entirely on scrutiny of the researcher's own reported findings and internal evidence tensions
(a legitimate basis for CONTESTED), not on an independent adversarial literature search. Two
attempts were made to have red-team-skeptic run independent confirmation searches
("CELF4 COPD lung function GWAS", "CELF4 smoking initiation GWAS 18q12.2", "obesity COPD shared
genetic locus 18q12" / "pleiotropy obesity COPD GWAS"); both attempts (an initial partial attempt
within Pass 1, and a dedicated Pass 2 follow-up dispatch) failed because the session's web-search
tool budget was exhausted — 0 of the 6 total requested searches actually executed, all returning
"search budget used" errors rather than null/negative results. Per the agent's own caution: "please
do not interpret the absence of hits as evidence against or for the CELF4-COPD/smoking/obesity
hypotheses." The CONTESTED verdict is retained on the strength of the internal evidence (verified
citations + Open Targets structured lookup showing systematic absence/redirection of mechanistic
support), but this locus should be revisited with a fresh independent literature search once
web-search tool budget is available, before treating the CONTESTED call as fully adversarially
tested.
