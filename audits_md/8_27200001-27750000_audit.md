## Resolved verification discrepancy (transparency note, not an exclusion)

The first citation-verifier pass produced an internally self-contradictory result for one
citation (item 10 in the original 18-citation batch): Li Y, Yu G, Yuan S, et al., "Cigarette
Smoke-Induced Pulmonary Inflammation and Autophagy Are Attenuated in Ephx2-Deficient Mice,"
Inflammation. 2017;40(2):497-510. The pass reported "Identifier as provided: PMID 28012752
[INCORRECT]... Correct Identifier: PMID 28028752" and issued a verdict of NOT_FOUND — but PMID
28028752 (the PMID the verifier itself called "correct") was in fact exactly what had been
submitted for checking in the original dispatch prompt. This was treated as a transcription
slip inside the verifier's own search process, not a real citation problem, and was resolved via
a second, narrow, single-citation re-verification pass that independently confirmed PMID
28028752 / DOI 10.1007/s10753-016-0495-z resolves to this exact article (PMCID PMC5357505, not
retracted), with the claim fully supported by the abstract. Final verdict: VERIFIED. This
citation appears in the main evidence table with its corrected, confirmed verdict.

## CONTESTED / WEAKENED gene verdicts (red-team-skeptic reasoning, preserved verbatim)

### CCDC25 — WEAKENED, bordering on CONTESTED

> All mechanistic literature is genuine NET-receptor biology (ILK/β-parvin pathway) but
> entirely in cancer-metastasis and PAH-vascular-remodeling contexts — zero direct
> CCDC25-COPD studies exist, a fact the researcher states plainly and doesn't obscure. This is
> exactly the "real biology, wrong disease context" pattern the prompt anticipates, and it's
> being reported as such rather than overclaimed as COPD evidence — good practice. But the
> Open Targets context adds an important independent reason for skepticism beyond "just
> extrapolation": CCDC25 has zero disease-association records for COPD and never appears as
> an L2G gene in any of ~40 credible sets, despite being the MAGMA-flagged gene. That's a
> textbook signature of MAGMA (a proximity/gene-based aggregation method) picking up a
> false-positive attribution driven by regional LD around a genuine regulatory element for a
> neighboring gene (CLU/EPHX2/CHRNA2), rather than CCDC25 itself being functionally
> implicated — the LD/nearest-gene-bias concern flagged in the task instructions.
> **WEAKENED, bordering on CONTESTED** — the null L2G/disease-association result across every
> credible set functions as structural counter-evidence to the MAGMA call, even though no
> single paper explicitly argues against CCDC25.

### CLU — WEAKENED

> The two locus-relevant papers are both reactive-biomarker/correlation studies (elevated
> serum/tissue clusterin correlating with COPD severity, FEV1, emphysema on CT) plus one in
> vitro CSE-induction/partial-knockdown-rescue study. None of this distinguishes cause from
> consequence — clusterin is a stress-inducible chaperone/complement-regulatory protein, and
> elevation in more severe disease is equally consistent with reverse causation (tissue damage
> → clusterin upregulation) as with clusterin driving pathology. The in vitro rescue is
> explicitly "partial." Critically, the researcher found zero literature placing CLU as the
> fine-mapped/colocalized causal gene at this specific locus, and no eQTL/colocalization
> evidence is presented anywhere in the findings. Structurally, CLU's COPD L2G (0.137) and
> chronic-airway-obstruction L2G (0.182, essentially tied with CHRNA2) don't clearly beat
> CHRNA2, and CLU's own single highest L2G value across the entire locus is for monocyte count
> (0.69, dataset-wide max) and atherosclerosis (0.37) — i.e., the strongest statistical signal
> attached to CLU at this locus is lipid/monocyte-related..., an alternative hypothesis
> (COPD-cardiovascular comorbidity/shared inflammatory-smoking risk rather than
> airway-intrinsic mechanism) that the researcher's writeup doesn't engage with at all.
> Combined with the total absence of any spirometry-trait credible set at this locus, the case
> for CLU acting through lung-intrinsic airflow-obstruction biology specifically (rather than
> being a "nearest gene" default, or a monocyte/atherosclerosis-mediated comorbidity gene) is
> not well established by what's presented.
> **WEAKENED** — real biomarker/mechanistic association with COPD severity exists, but it is
> correlational, non-locus-specific, doesn't clearly beat CHRNA2 on L2G, and ignores a
> plausible non-airway (monocyte/atherosclerosis) alternative explanation for CLU's own
> strongest statistical signal at this locus.
>
> [Follow-up pass, after CHRNA2/eQTL searches:] Nearest-gene/PoPS status and known clusterin
> lung-injury biology are real, but this pass found no primary-literature eQTL/colocalization
> evidence tying this locus's credible-set variants to CLU expression, and
> independently-corroborated CHRNA2 smoking-gene literature plus the background's
> L2G/disease-association pattern (most credible sets are smoking-behavior traits) give a
> plausible competing explanation. The inference that CLU is "the" causal gene here overreaches
> what functional evidence currently supports — it is a nearest/statistically-prioritized
> candidate, not a functionally anchored one.

### CHRNA2 — CONTESTED as a direct-lung-mechanism gene / STRENGTHENED as the smoking-mediation explanation

> New, independently verified literature (Wang 2014; Al-Soufi 2021) confirms CHRNA2 (chr8p21)
> is a genuine, distinct-from-chr15-cluster nicotine-dependence/smoking-behavior gene with its
> own colocalization evidence (albeit colocalizing with schizophrenia/smoking in a different
> phenotype pairing, not this exact COPD credible set). Combined with the background pattern
> that ~40 shared credible sets are overwhelmingly smoking/nicotine/psychiatric traits rather
> than spirometry traits, this substantially raises confidence that this locus's COPD
> association is smoking-initiation/nicotine-dependence-mediated rather than reflecting direct
> pulmonary biology — consistent with prior #1's caution. No lung-tissue eQTL evidence was
> found either for or against a direct pulmonary role.
>
> [First pass, prior to the CHRNA2 literature search:] This is the gene most likely
> under-evaluated relative to its statistical support. Open Targets shows CHRNA2 has the
> single highest overall COPD disease-association score at the locus (0.376,
> genetic_association sub-score 0.618, both notably higher than CLU's), and ties CLU for
> chronic-airway-obstruction L2G (0.182 vs 0.182). Given that essentially all ~40 shared
> credible sets at this lead variant are smoking-behavior/nicotine-dependence/psychiatric
> traits, a nicotinic receptor subunit gene sitting under a signal dominated by
> smoking-related trait labels is mechanistically the most parsimonious explanation — yet the
> researcher found zero primary literature confirming a CHRNA2-smoking-behavior link despite
> multiple attempts, and explicitly did not search structural/immune biology for CHRNA2 at all
> (deemed out of scope for time reasons). This is a genuine thoroughness gap, not a negative
> finding... Given prior #1 (smoking-mediation must be actively weighed, not assumed away),
> this gap is exactly the wrong one to leave unresolved before a final gene call is made.

### EPHX2 — WEAKENED / UNRESOLVED

> This is the strongest mechanistic case at the locus: two independent Ephx2-knockout mouse
> cigarette-smoke studies (Li 2017, He 2026) plus a pharmacological sEH-inhibitor study
> (Podolin 2013), all concordant in direction (loss/inhibition of EPHX2 function → less
> smoke-induced inflammation/emphysema, better preserved lung function), which is
> qualitatively better causal-inference design (loss-of-function + pharmacology) than CLU's
> correlational biomarker studies. However, none of these studies test the GWAS locus, the
> lead variant, or an eQTL/colocalization link between credible-set variants and EPHX2
> expression — they establish that EPHX2 protein function matters for smoke-induced emphysema
> in mice, not that common regulatory variation at 8p21 in humans acts through EPHX2. Its
> absence from all four internal pipeline methods (nearest/PoPS/MAGMA/credible-set overlap) is
> a real gap... "chronic bronchitis" is a distinct phenotype from spirometry-confirmed airflow
> obstruction, and — as with CLU — no FEV1/FEV1-FVC credible set exists at this locus at all,
> so the single most decisive evidence type for EPHX2's specific predicted mechanism (an
> airflow-obstruction/spirometry association) is unavailable here, same gap as for CLU.
> **WEAKENED** — strong, replicated, mechanistically superior evidence that EPHX2 protein is
> causally involved in smoke-induced emphysema in mice, but no evidence in the verified
> literature ties GWAS variation at this locus to EPHX2 expression/dosage; using it as a
> synthesis "override" of the four pipeline methods requires an unstated and untested
> eQTL/colocalization assumption.
>
> [Follow-up pass:] Not flagged by any internal prioritization method, and these two targeted
> searches added no new supporting evidence... EPHX2 has plausible generic
> inflammation-resolution biology (soluble epoxide hydrolase) but nothing locus-specific
> surfaced here. Its candidacy remains speculative and should not be elevated by
> synthesis-lead absent further evidence.

## Genes appropriately deprioritized (HOLDS — no inference problem, included for completeness)

- **GULOP**: non-functional unitary pseudogene; no constraint, disease-association, or L2G
  signal; no regulatory-role literature found despite a targeted search. Credible-set variants
  overlapping its introns are best interpreted as candidate regulatory variation for flanking
  genes, not evidence of GULOP's own causal role.
- **PTK2B**: well-known Alzheimer's-disease GWAS gene physically in the region; no COPD- or
  lung-specific literature found; general macrophage/immune-kinase biology is asserted in the
  broader literature but was not confirmed via a lung-specific citation in this pipeline run —
  flagged explicitly by the researcher as "asserted but not confirmed," and red-team-skeptic
  cautioned this should not be quietly used in synthesis as if it were sourced.
- **TRIM35**: real E3-ligase biology in cardiac fibroblast remodeling (human) and antiviral
  innate immunity (avian, not human) — both off-target tissue/species for COPD; no
  lung/human-immune-specific literature found.
- **STMN4**: neuronal cytoskeletal gene; no COPD-relevant literature found despite three
  general-biology citations confirmed to exist.
- **SCARA3**: minor, never-top L2G contributor; biochemically does not bind lipoproteins like
  other SCARA family members (undercutting rather than supporting a lipid/atherosclerosis
  analogy); no COPD/lung-specific literature found.

## Thoroughness / evidence-gap flags carried forward from red-team-skeptic (not verdicts, but
## limitations to weigh)

- No gene at this locus has a published, locus-specific eQTL or GWAS-colocalization study
  (in any tissue) directly linking the credible-set variants to that gene's expression. This
  is the single biggest missing evidence category and cuts equally against CLU, CCDC25,
  CHRNA2, and EPHX2 — prioritization currently rests on statistical/database
  gene-prioritization scores (PoPS, MAGMA, Open Targets L2G) and mechanistic-but-locus-
  nonspecific functional biology, not on independently-published functional genomics tying
  variants to expression at this exact locus.
- No spirometry/FEV1/FEV1-FVC GWAS credible set was found sharing this locus's signal among
  the ~40 examined via Open Targets — a locus-wide gap in the evidence type (concordance with
  pulmonary-function traits) that the pipeline's biological priors flag as informative when
  present, and whose absence here should lower confidence in any purely lung-intrinsic story.
