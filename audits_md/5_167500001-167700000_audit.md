# Audit trail — locus 5:167,500,001-167,700,000 (TENM2)

This file lists every excluded citation (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH), every gene
the red-team-skeptic flagged as contested/weakened, and the reasoning preserved verbatim,
for reviewer traceability. It is not part of the evidence table handed to the study team.

## Excluded citations

### 1. Shrine N, Guyatt AL, Erzurumluoglu AM, et al. "New genetic signals for lung function
highlight pathways and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease associations across multiple
ancestries." Nature Genetics 2019;51(3):481-493. PMID 30804560, DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0321-7.

**Verdict: VERIFIED (paper) / CLAIM_MISMATCH (TENM2-specific link) — excluded from main
evidence table.**

Citation-verifier's verbatim finding: "VERIFIED (partial) — Shrine N et al., Nature Genetics
2019, PMID 30804560: Paper reports 279 signals for lung function (FEV1/FEV1/FVC) as claimed.
However, TENM2/5q34 region is NOT mentioned among the reported signals."

The researcher had flagged this paper as "the most likely candidate source" underlying Open
Targets' TENM2 "pulmonary function measurement" (score 0.341) and "FEV/FVC ratio" (score
0.247) association scores, but explicitly marked the linkage UNVERIFIED pending checking.
Citation-verifier's direct check confirms the linkage does NOT hold: TENM2 is not among this
paper's reported loci. Consequence: the Open Targets pulmonary-function scores for TENM2
remain of unresolved/unconfirmed origin and should not be treated as independently
corroborated pulmonary evidence in synthesis (see red-team-skeptic critique below, point (c)).

### 2. Berbeira-Santana M, et al. Nature Communications 2026. PMID 41991904, DOI
10.1038/s41467-026-71619-1.

**Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH — excluded from main evidence table.**

Citation-verifier's verbatim finding: "CLAIM_MISMATCH — Berbeira-Santana M et al., Nature
Communications 2026, PMID 41991904: Paper focuses primarily on Ten4 (teneurin-4), not Ten2.
While Ten2 structures are determined via cryo-EM, the functional study concerns Ten4's role in
cortical neuron migration through Ten4-Lphn switching, not Ten2-Lphn switching."

The researcher's original claim described this as "a structural/cryo-EM study of Ten2
(teneurin-2)-Latrophilin complex switching in cortical neuron migration" — this is not what
the paper's functional findings establish (they concern TENM4, not TENM2). Excluded from the
TENM2 evidence table entirely; not used to support any TENM2-specific structural claim.

### 3. Min Y, et al. "Single extracellular vesicle surface protein-based blood assay
identifies potential biomarkers for detection and screening of five cancers." Molecular

**Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH — excluded from main evidence table.**

## Contested / weakened gene verdicts

### TENM2 — not formally CONTESTED, but explicitly characterized as WEAKENED for a pulmonary-
disease role by red-team-skeptic. Verbatim reasoning preserved below.

> **Disclosure on my own search coverage:** I attempted two contradiction-search queries (one
> for "TENM2 COPD lung function GWAS," one to check for other genes in the 200kb window) and
> both failed — the session's WebSearch budget was already exhausted (200/200) before either
> query ran. So unlike the standard workflow, **TENM2 received zero independent verification
> searches from me**; this critique is based entirely on close reading of the researcher's
> findings and citation-verifier verdicts. Flag this explicitly to the synthesis-lead: the
> "actively search for contradicting evidence" step could not be executed for this locus,
> which is a real gap in this review's own methodology, not just an absence of contradicting
> evidence found.
>
> **(a) The Quach et al. "lung eQTL complicates the neurobehavioral confound" argument —
> overreaching.** This is the weakest link in the researcher's reasoning and should be flagged
> as overclaiming. Several problems: The eQTL is for *nicotine dependence*, not COPD or any
> spirometric trait. Even if the lung eQTL effect is real and specific, at most it supports a
> mechanism by which this variant could influence lung TENM2 expression — it does not
> establish that lung expression is what drives the nicotine-dependence phenotype, let alone
> COPD risk. No colocalization between this eQTL and a COPD/FEV1 GWAS signal exists. "Lung-
> specific" is doing a lot of work here that the underlying data may not support — GTEx-style
> eQTL detection in one tissue is heavily influenced by sample size/power in that tissue, not
> proof of true tissue-restriction of the regulatory effect. Most importantly, the
> interpretive leap — "the mechanism could act through lung TENM2 expression rather than only
> CNS expression" — appears to be the researcher's own inference layered on top of the paper,
> not a claim the cited paper itself makes. This argument is further undercut by the *other*
> statistical-genetics findings in the same section (Trofimova et al. 2026; Abdurahaman et al.
> 2025), both of which independently reinforce a CNS/synaptic mechanism, not a peripheral lung
> one. The weight of evidence, including within the researcher's own findings, points toward
> brain-mediated nicotine/substance-dependence biology, and the lung-eQTL argument reads as an
> attempt to rescue a pulmonary-mechanism story against that tide.
>
> **(b) Should TENM2 be marked CONTESTED?** Strictly, per the rubric, CONTESTED requires
> credible evidence that argues *against* the gene, reports a null finding, or implicates a
> *different* gene at the locus — I did not find (and could not search for, per above) any
> such paper. The Shrine et al. mismatch is not contradicting evidence in that sense; it's a
> citation-verifier catch that a hoped-for supporting paper doesn't actually contain TENM2,
> which removes support rather than adding an opposing claim. So I'd resist a strict CONTESTED
> label. That said, the substantive picture is close to a non-finding for a *pulmonary* role:
> zero COPD-specific literature, zero structural/developmental lung mechanism, zero
> immune/inflammatory literature, the one candidate FEV1/FEV1-FVC-supporting paper confirmed
> NOT to contain TENM2, and Open Targets pulmonary-function scores that are
> unexplained/untraceable to any verified publication. Meanwhile there are three independent,
> verified lines of evidence (human GWAS x2, rat model x1) all pointing to a synaptic/
> addiction-behavior mechanism. I'd characterize this as **WEAKENED bordering on effectively
> recharacterized**: the evidence supports TENM2 as a smoking/nicotine-dependence locus
> (consistent with prior #1's expected confound) far more strongly than as a pulmonary-biology
> locus, and the "COPD gene" framing should not stand without that caveat front and center.
>
> **(c) Other gaps/overclaiming:** The Open Targets "pulmonary function measurement" (0.341)
> and "FEV/FVC ratio" (0.247) scores are dangling and should not be used as supporting evidence
> in synthesis — they cannot be traced to any verified source (their most likely origin,
> Shrine et al., was just ruled out), and Open Targets shows zero direct COPD/FEV1 target-
> disease association evidence otherwise. These scores look like an artifact of Open Targets'
> internal genetic-association scoring pipeline (possibly picking up the same nicotine/
> smoking-adjacent signal) rather than independent pulmonary evidence — synthesis-lead should
> not double-count them alongside the smoking-initiation score as if they were separate,
> corroborating pulmonary evidence. The LoF-constraint fact (oe=0.203, most-constrained bin)
> is a generic haploinsufficiency signal; it says TENM2 is under strong purifying selection
> genome-wide, not that it's specifically disease-relevant to COPD or pulmonary biology. It
> shouldn't be presented as supporting a pulmonary mechanism. The 200kb-window gene-overlap
> gap is real, but the researcher's reasoning for treating it as low-risk is more defensible
> than most such shortcuts: all 49 credible-set variants are intronic to TENM2, and TENM2 is
> genuinely a very large gene (~700-800kb span) that plausibly encompasses the entire 200kb
> window, consistent with nearest-gene/FLAMES/PoPS convergence. Still, this was not formally
> confirmed (Ensembl query not run), and I could not independently check it either. This
> should be explicitly logged as an open item for synthesis-lead — low probability of a
> different LD-block gene being the true target, but not fully ruled out. The credible set
> being 100% intronic/MODIFIER with no coding variants is consistent with a regulatory
> (eQTL-driven) mechanism, which fits the Quach et al. eQTL finding structurally, but doesn't
> resolve *which tissue/phenotype* the regulatory effect is actually acting through.
>
> **Overall verdict for TENM2: WEAKENED.** The statistical-genetics evidence for TENM2 as a
> genuine locus (nearest gene/FLAMES/PoPS convergence, LoF constraint, consistent eQTL) is
> solid, but the inference that this represents a lung/COPD-relevant mechanism overreaches
> substantially. The verified evidence base is dominated by smoking-/nicotine-dependence and
> synaptic-neurodevelopmental biology, with a near-total absence of independent pulmonary
> structural, spirometric, or immune evidence — exactly the pattern the pipeline's smoking-
> mediation prior anticipates. The "lung eQTL complicates the confound" argument is the one
> piece of active overclaiming to correct before synthesis; the rest of the section is
> honestly reported as an absence.
