## Excluded citations (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH)

**RETRACTED**
- PMID 41133350 (retraction notice, 2025; DOI 10.1002/tox.24591) for: Lin Y, Lin P, Guo W,
  Huang J, Xu X, Zhuang X. "PLAGL2 Promotes the Stemness and is Upregulated by Transcription
  Factor E2F1 in Human Lung Cancer." Environmental Toxicology. 2023;38(4):941-949 (original
  DOI 10.1002/tox.23739). Retraction notice states the article was "accepted solely on the
  basis of a compromised peer review process." This paper would have been the single
  citation directly connecting two genes at this locus — E2F1 (PoPS-prioritized) and PLAGL2
  (proximal-cluster credible-set gene) — in a lung-cancer mechanism. It must not be used as
  supporting evidence for any E2F1-PLAGL2 relationship, and PLAGL2's Open Targets
  literature-only association score (0.014) should be treated with caution since it may
  substantially derive from this now-retracted paper.

**CLAIM_MISMATCH**
- PMID 31267263 — Li Z et al., "GNAS-AS1/miR-4319/NECAB3 axis promotes migration and
  invasion of non-small cell lung cancer cells by altering macrophage polarization,"
  Functional & Integrative Genomics, 2019. The researcher's claim asserted that NECAB3
  knockdown/overexpression experiments were performed and shown to alter macrophage
  polarization. Citation-verifier found the abstract supports a GNAS-AS1/miR-4319/NECAB3
  regulatory axis promoting NSCLC migration/invasion via altered macrophage polarization, but
  does NOT explicitly confirm that dedicated NECAB3 gain/loss-of-function experiments were
  the basis for the macrophage-polarization finding specifically. Excluded from the evidence
  table; do not cite as evidence of a NECAB3-macrophage causal mechanism without independent
  full-text confirmation.

**NOT_FOUND:** none — all 32 citations sent to citation-verifier were confirmed to exist.

## Genes marked CONTESTED, WEAKENED, or INSUFFICIENT-EVIDENCE by red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning preserved)

Full skeptic verdicts and reasoning per gene, verbatim from the critique dispatch (full
critique also preserved in `evidence_tables/_status/20_25100001-33050000/critique.md`):

**C20orf203 — CONTESTED.** "This is the nearest gene to the lead variant, but PIP=0.125 is
low and barely distinguishes it from three other proximal-cluster genes. Both verified
papers are about neurobiology/nicotine-addiction and headache-TSH pleiotropy — a specific,
actively competing explanation (smoking/nicotine-behavior mediation via brain-expressed
genes) rather than a mere absence of lung evidence. Zero Open Targets COPD score, zero
gnomAD constraint, zero lung/immune literature. The researcher's own conclusion ('consistent
with the smoking-mediation prior') already concedes the case for direct pulmonary biology is
weak; I'd go further and say the nicotine-addiction discovery history is itself evidence
against a pulmonary mechanism, not neutral."

**ASXL1 — WEAKENED.** "This is the locus's best-credentialed gene by Open Targets (only
genetic_association-datatype hit at the locus) and has real epidemiological CH-COPD
literature. But every single piece of mechanistic/epidemiological evidence concerns somatic
clonal-hematopoiesis mutations acquired with age in blood, not the germline intronic variant
(rs2424880) actually in the credible set. No paper shows the germline locus affects ASXL1
expression, dosage, or CH acquisition rate — this is a name-match, not a demonstrated
regulatory link. Compounding this: the smoking-CHIP paper (38320547) implicated the paralog
DNMT3A, not ASXL1, for smoking status, while ASXL1-CH was linked only to exacerbation
history — so even the somatic story doesn't cleanly fold into the smoking-mediation prior
either. The three Bohring-Opitz PMIDs are unverified and must not be used. Net: real gene,
wrong genetic architecture cited as support; recommend synthesis lead explicitly check
whether Open Targets' genetic_association score for ASXL1 is itself just this GWAS's own
locus-to-gene pipeline assigning the credible-set variant to ASXL1 by proximity (which would
be circular, not independent corroboration)."

**EFCAB8 — INSUFFICIENT-EVIDENCE.** "Third-highest PIP in the whole credible set (0.066) but
essentially zero biological characterization — one incidental eQTL mention in an unrelated
hip-OA paper. High statistical prior, no biology to evaluate; this is a 'needs work' gene,
not evidence against it."

**MAPRE1 — WEAKENED.** "Essential, ubiquitous, gnomAD-constrained cytoskeletal gene (EB1);
all 'lung' literature is lung-cancer cell-line mitotic-machinery studies, which would surface
for almost any core cell-division gene regardless of COPD relevance. This looks like
literature-volume bias (essential gene → heavily studied in cancer → superficially
'lung-related' hits) rather than COPD-specific signal. Same incidental hip-OA eQTL as
EFCAB8/DNMT3B (note on non-independence of that citation across three genes)."

**DNMT3B — CONTESTED.** "The one paper positioned to link a DNMT3-family gene to
smoking-CHIP in COPD patients (38320547) specifically implicates the paralog DNMT3A, not
DNMT3B — a concrete piece of evidence pointing away from DNMT3B for exactly the mechanism
being proposed. ICF-syndrome background is a severe recessive Mendelian phenotype (different
genetic architecture from a common noncoding variant) and its specific PMIDs are unverified.
The eQTL hit is the same incidental hip-OA paper shared with EFCAB8/MAPRE1 — note this single
pleiotropic eQTL is being reused as 'supporting evidence' for three different genes, which is
not independent evidence and shouldn't be triple-counted in synthesis."

**NECAB3 — INSUFFICIENT-EVIDENCE (verging on WEAKENED).** "The one paper that would have
supported a macrophage-polarization mechanism was flagged CLAIM_MISMATCH by the verifier and
must be discarded; the remaining two papers are about paralogs (not NECAB3 itself) in
neurobiology, and a negative/non-mediating finding in cord blood. MAGMA-prioritized with no
anchoring variant — weakest tier of evidence at this locus."

**E2F1 — CONTESTED.** "PoPS-prioritized with no anchoring variant; canonical pleiotropic
cell-cycle TF, heavily studied in cancer. One citation (36561957) is an outright overclaim —
E2F1 appears only in an abbreviations list, not the mechanistic text, and should not have
been cited as supporting evidence at all. The nicotine/smoke-exposure evidence is
cell-culture (mostly NSCLC lines), not COPD tissue. Most importantly, the one paper that
would have directly connected E2F1 to another gene at this same locus (PLAGL2) is retracted.
The researcher's own framing — that PoPS may be surfacing a highly pleiotropic, essential,
'statistically central' gene whose apparent relevance is an artifact of publication volume
rather than COPD-specific biology — is exactly right, and I'd elevate that from a caveat to
the operative conclusion."

**NINL — WEAKENED.** "Genuinely well-supported dual biology (antiviral IFN response +
ciliary/centrosomal trafficking, four solid verified papers) and it's the highest
cumulative-PIP gene in the distal cluster, ABC-prioritized (locus-anchored regulatory
evidence, a meaningfully stronger prioritization method than PoPS/MAGMA). But none of the
four papers touch lung, airway, or COPD; the 'antiviral immunity → COPD exacerbations' link
is a plausibility argument the researcher constructed, not a tested hypothesis. Best
-characterized real biology at the distal cluster, but currently no COPD-specific data at
all."

**PYGB — INSUFFICIENT-EVIDENCE.** "All four verified papers are glial/cardiac/muscle
metabolism, zero lung relevance. Two missense credible-set variants (rs2228976/rs2227891)
went unsearched because the search budget was exhausted mid-session — this is an explicitly
incomplete search, not a negative finding, and should be flagged to synthesis lead as a gene
that needs re-evaluation before any final call."

**NANP — WEAKENED.** "The gene's own canonical enzymatic knockout is phenotypically
dispensable/redundant — this is a specific piece of evidence arguing against NANP being an
important gene via its named pathway. The only other lead (TNFR1/NF-κB) is glioblastoma-only,
not lung/immune."

**ABHD12 — WEAKENED (borderline CONTESTED).** "This is genuinely the strongest immune/lung
mechanistic finding at the entire locus (LCMV-induced lung pathology via lyso-PS/macrophage
signaling in Abhd12-null mice). But two things cut against extrapolating to COPD: (1) it's
an acute viral-injury model, not a chronic cigarette-smoke/emphysema model — a different
disease process; (2) PHARC, the human Mendelian disease from complete ABHD12 loss-of-
function, has no reported pulmonary phenotype in humans despite the mouse lung finding. That
absence-of-phenotype in a complete human null is a legitimate piece of contradicting evidence
for the 'ABHD12 loss impairs lung host defense' story translating to human disease, and
should be weighed against the mouse data rather than treated as merely a caveat."

**GINS1 — WEAKENED.** "Ultra-rare recessive near-null phenotype (growth retardation,
neutropenia, NK-cell deficiency) with an infection-susceptibility mechanism that's
biologically plausible for COPD exacerbations, but explicitly no pulmonary phenotype
reported in any of the ~9-10 known patients. GINS1 is essential for DNA replication, making
it implausible that a common regulatory variant recapitulates anything resembling this null
phenotype. The absence of a lung phenotype in the most severe possible human genotype is a
real strike against this gene."

**ZNF337 — INSUFFICIENT-EVIDENCE.** "Generic pan-cancer (mainly renal) bioinformatics
correlation; 'immunological' in the title refers to generic tumor-immune-infiltration
correlation, not a lung- or COPD-relevant mechanism. Flag as a case where a vague
'immunological' keyword risks being overclaimed as COPD-relevant immune biology."

**ENTPD6 — INSUFFICIENT-EVIDENCE, explicit overclaiming flag.** "The cited paper states
ENTPD6's disease role 'remains unclear' — this is a citation to an absence of evidence being
used as if it were supportive purinergic-immune-signaling context. Should not be carried
forward as positive evidence in synthesis."

**KIF3B, POFUT1, PLAGL2, NOL4L, ZNF337-AS1, FAM182B, VN1R108P — INSUFFICIENT-EVIDENCE**
(literature gaps, or for VN1R108P a deliberately deprioritized pseudogene with no plausible
mechanism). "PLAGL2 additionally lost its one locus-specific connecting paper to retraction —
worth flagging to synthesis lead that PLAGL2's Open Targets literature-only score (0.014) may
now rest on nothing verifiable, since the connecting paper is retracted."

## Locus-architecture and methodology flags (verbatim from red-team-skeptic)

- **Search-budget limitation, disclosed by the skeptic itself:** "I attempted two independent
  contradiction-searches... Both failed — the session's web-search budget was already
  exhausted (200/200 used, apparently by upstream agents in this pipeline before I was
  invoked). I was unable to run any independent searches for any of the 21 genes at this
  locus. My critique below is therefore based entirely on scrutinizing the internal logic...
  of the researcher's own write-up... I could not go looking for additional contradicting
  papers myself." This should be read as a genuine limitation on this locus's red-team
  coverage: no fresh adversarial literature search was possible, only re-analysis of material
  the researcher had already surfaced.
- **Two-cluster architecture:** "An ~8 Mb window straddling the chr20 centromere, with two
  credible-set clusters ~7 Mb apart and nothing fine-mapped in the intervening gene-poor
  pericentromeric gap, is not one LD block — recombination across centromeric heterochromatin
  is essentially suppressed, so the proximal and distal clusters are statistically and
  physically independent signals that have been artificially merged into 'one locus'...
  Practically, this halves the effective 'credible-set size' argument for any single winner
  and should push the synthesis lead toward a 'no confident single gene, possibly two
  independent weak signals' framing rather than picking one name for the whole 8 Mb window."
- **Prioritization-method weighting:** "Ranking of evidentiary strength, independent of
  biology: fine-mapped credible-set genes with high/missense PIP > ABC-prioritized >
  MAGMA-prioritized > PoPS-prioritized. E2F1 and NECAB3 should be weighted below
  C20orf203/ASXL1/EFCAB8/MAPRE1 in any final ranking, not treated as peers."
- **Non-independent eQTL citation reused across genes:** "The hip-OA eQTL paper (32776659)
  is being used as supporting evidence for three separate genes (EFCAB8, MAPRE1, DNMT3B)
  simultaneously — this is one non-independent data point, not three, and should be weighted
  accordingly in any cumulative scoring."
- **Locus-wide absence of spirometry evidence:** "Its total absence across all 21 genes at
  this 8 Mb locus, combined with the nicotine-addiction/neuro literature specifically at the
  lead variant's nearest gene (C20orf203) and the diffuse, near-tied PIPs in the proximal
  cluster, should meaningfully lower confidence that this locus reflects direct pulmonary
  biology at all, as opposed to smoking-behavior-mediated or genuinely indeterminate
  mechanism."

## Other process gaps disclosed by the researcher (not independently actioned in this run)

- A dedicated Ensembl/UCSC region-overlap query to enumerate all genes physically present in
  the full 7.95 Mb window (beyond those already tagged in the locus input file's credible set
  and prioritized-gene lists) was not completed as a separate step. The researcher judged
  this acceptable given the input file's credible-set annotation already appears to cover
  essentially all genes overlapping both fine-mapped clusters, and the intervening
  pericentromeric gap is stated to be gene-poor and ungenotyped — but this was not
  independently confirmed by a dedicated genomic-coordinate query in this session, and is
  flagged here as a residual limitation rather than silently treated as complete.
- rs17123518 (the lead variant) has no variant-specific literature identified.
- rs2228976 and rs2227891 (PYGB missense credible-set variants) have no variant-specific
  literature identified; the researcher explicitly attributes this to WebSearch budget
  exhaustion mid-session rather than a confirmed negative result, and recommends re-search.
