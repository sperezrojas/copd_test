## Genes flagged WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (none reached full CONTESTED)

The red-team-skeptic's critique format used the verdict "WEAKENED" (evidence real but does not
support the inferential leap being asked of it) rather than "CONTESTED" (an active literature
dispute) for all three candidate genes at this locus. Full reasoning is preserved verbatim below
per pipeline requirements.

### CHRNB3 — WEAKENED

> The nicotine-dependence/smoking-quantity evidence is real and comes from a well-powered,
> frequently-replicated body of work (Saccone 2009, Saccone 2010, Thorgeirsson 2010, Rice 2012). But
> several things weaken any leap from this to COPD-relevant pulmonary biology:
>
> - **Phenotype-dependence, not robustness.** Rice 2012 shows genome-wide significance for
>   Fagerström-defined dependence but *not* for cigarettes-per-day (P=0.0004) — the signal tracks a
>   specific reward/dependence construct, not smoking heaviness generally, which matters for how it
>   would plausibly propagate to COPD risk.
> - **A direct non-replication exists.** Keskitalo-Vuokko 2011 found the strongest CHRN-family hit
>   for CPD/cotinine in Finnish smokers was at CHRNG/CHRND, not CHRNB3/CHRNA6 — a genuine null in an
>   independent cohort that the researcher correctly flagged but that should be weighted, not just
>   noted.
> - **No spirometric support anywhere** (confirmed twice now: Open Targets shows none, GWAS Catalog
>   shows none). Per prior #2, this absence is significant given how central FEV1/FEV1-FVC evidence
>   should be for a pulmonary mechanism.
> - **COPD support is literature-only, not genetics-backed** (Open Targets: COPD score 0.026, no
>   genetic_association datatype). That means the "COPD" link for CHRNB3 in aggregated evidence is
>   text-mining co-occurrence, not an independent COPD GWAS/coloc signal — the entire case for
>   CHRNB3-as-COPD-gene here rests on this locus's presence in the COPD credible set itself plus
>   proximity, not on convergent orthogonal evidence.
> - **Shrine et al. 2019 citation is already flagged CLAIM_MISMATCH** by the verifier and should not
>   be used either way; I could not resolve it independently.
>
> Given prior #1 (unadjusted-for-smoking COPD GWAS), this is close to a textbook case of a locus
> whose COPD signal is plausibly fully explained by smoking-initiation/nicotine-dependence biology
> rather than independent lung pathology, and the evidence assembled does not rebut that
> alternative — it actively supports it (this is one of the most replicated smoking-behavior loci in
> the genome, with no countervailing spirometric signal).
>
> **WEAKENED** — the underlying smoking/nicotine-dependence genetics are solid, but the inferential
> leap to "CHRNB3 is a COPD/pulmonary-biology gene" is not supported; the more parsimonious reading
> is smoking-behavior mediation with no demonstrated independent lung mechanism, and one direct
> non-replication (Keskitalo-Vuokko) exists in the record.

### CHRNA6 — WEAKENED

> CHRNA6 shares essentially all locus-level smoking-behavior citations with CHRNB3 (same LD block,
> divergently-transcribed co-regulated pair), but has markedly weaker gene-specific genetic support
> (Open Targets nicotine-dependence score 0.017, literature-only, vs. CHRNB3's 0.371 genetics-backed)
> and, critically, its assignment to this credible set rests on rs7001753 at **PIP=0.0015** —
> roughly two orders of magnitude below the lead variant's PIP=0.197. That is at the noise floor for
> fine-mapping and is a textbook case of prior concern #1 (LD/nearest-gene bias): CHRNA6 looks like
> it's riding into "candidate" status on CHRNB3's coattails and shared LD, not on independent
> statistical or functional support.
>
> The Zhang 2011 lung-cancer-in-non-smokers finding is the one piece of evidence that is *not*
> smoking-confounded (protective effect independent of smoking exposure) and is worth noting as a
> counterpoint to pure smoking-mediation — but it's a single Chinese Han population study of lung
> cancer, not COPD, and unreplicated. The remaining CHRNA6 immune literature (Zhao 2024, Zhang F
> 2023, Li N 2021, Szymanowska-Narloch 2013) is lung-cancer tumor-microenvironment bioinformatics
> (CIBERSORT/TIDE-style), which the researcher correctly flagged as off-target for COPD's
> neutrophilic/CD8+/Th1/Th17/macrophage programs (prior #3) — this is nonspecific, contested-
> relevance evidence and should not be counted toward COPD immune mechanism.
>
> **WEAKENED** — real locus-level association shared with CHRNB3, but gene-specific evidence is very
> thin (PIP=0.0015, weak literature-only nicotine score), and the immune/cancer literature cited is
> not COPD-relevant. Verging on CONTESTED given how weak the credible-set anchor is, but I'll leave
> it WEAKENED rather than CONTESTED since I found no paper specifically arguing against CHRNA6 (only
> weak-by-default evidence).

### THAP1 — WEAKENED

> No PubMed literature connects THAP1 to COPD, lung function, or smoking at all — candidacy is 100%
> MAGMA/PoPS statistical prioritization with zero corroborating biology, zero eQTL/colocalization
> support at this locus (Open Targets shows only an incidental adipose eQTL for the unrelated
> neighboring gene SMIM19), and zero GWAS Catalog hits specific to THAP1 (confirmed independently).
> The only functional literature that exists — DYT6 dystonia haploinsufficiency (Fuchs 2009,
> Djarmati 2009, Ruiz 2015) and vascular/endothelial proliferation (Cayrol 2006) — points toward
> CNS/neuromuscular and vascular-endothelial biology, not lung. Ruiz 2015's mouse model explicitly
> examined motor/cerebellar/transcriptional phenotypes with no lung phenotype reported, which is
> weak negative evidence (the study wasn't designed to look for one) but is nonetheless the only
> in-vivo phenotypic data point available and it doesn't support a lung mechanism.
>
> The gene's strong LoF/missense constraint (oe ~0.30/0.66) is genuinely interesting as a generic
> prior — constrained genes are more plausible true regulatory-GWAS targets — but this is a property
> of the gene, not evidence specific to COPD or lung, and shouldn't be conflated with biological
> support.
>
> **WEAKENED** — this is not a case of contradicting evidence so much as a complete absence of any
> corroborating COPD/lung/smoking biology; candidacy rests entirely on one statistical method
> category (MAGMA/PoPS) with no orthogonal genetic (eQTL/coloc) or functional confirmation at this
> locus. Should be treated by synthesis-lead as a hypothesis needing orthogonal lung-tissue
> expression/eQTL evidence before being weighted alongside CHRNB3, not as an evidence-supported
> candidate on par with it.

## Locus-level synthesis note from red-team-skeptic (verbatim)

> Across all three genes, the pattern is consistent: strong, repeatedly-replicated smoking-behavior/
> nicotine-dependence genetics at 8p11, essentially zero independent spirometric (FEV1/FEV1-FVC) or
> COPD-genetics-backed evidence (confirmed via both Open Targets and an independent GWAS Catalog
> pull), and a fine-mapped credible set that is overwhelmingly intergenic/regulatory with no coding
> variants to anchor causal-gene assignment. Per prior #1, this locus should be flagged to
> synthesis-lead as a strong candidate for a **smoking-mediated (not directly pulmonary) COPD
> association** — the case for any of the three genes reflecting independent lung biology is
> currently unsupported by the assembled evidence, and this conclusion is now corroborated by an
> independent database check rather than resting solely on the researcher's own search.
