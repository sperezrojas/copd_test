# Audit appendix — locus 8:22,500,001-22,750,000 (nearest gene BIN3)

This file contains excluded citations (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH) and every
gene the red-team-skeptic marked CONTESTED/WEAKENED, with reasoning preserved verbatim
from the citation-verifier and red-team-skeptic subagent outputs. This is the audit trail;
it is intentionally kept separate from `evidence_tables/8_22500001-22750000.md` so that
the evidence table can be shared without necessarily also sharing this trail.

## Excluded citations (CLAIM_MISMATCH — not used in the evidence table)

1. **Shrine N et al., "New genetic signals for lung function highlight pathways and
   chronic obstructive pulmonary disease associations across multiple ancestries."
   Nature Genetics, 2019. PMID: 30804560, DOI: 10.1038/s41588-018-0321-7.**
   Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH. Paper exists and is not retracted, but BIN3/CCAR2 and
   rs6558173/chr8:22.6Mb are not mentioned in the abstract. The researcher used this
   citation only as general background on the scale/type of GWAS underlying this locus's
   association; citation-verifier could not confirm the paper name-checks BIN3/CCAR2
   specifically. Excluded because it cannot support a gene-specific claim, though it
   remains accurate as general background that a large SpiroMeta/UK Biobank lung-function
   GWAS exists.

2. **Sakornsakolpat P et al., "Genetic landscape of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease
   identifies heterogeneous cell-type and phenotype associations." Nature Genetics,
   2019. PMID: 30804561, DOI: 10.1038/s41588-018-0342-2.**
   Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH. Same reasoning as #1 above — paper exists, not retracted, but
   BIN3/CCAR2 and chr8:22.6Mb are not mentioned in the retrieved abstract of this 82-locus
   COPD GWAS. Excluded for the same reason.

3. **Routhier EL, Donover PS, Prendergast GC. "hob1+, the fission yeast homolog of Bin1,
   is dispensable for endocytosis or actin organization, but required for the response to
   starvation or genotoxic stress." Oncogene, 2003. PMID: 12569356, DOI:
   10.1038/sj.onc.1206162.**
   Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH — WRONG GENE. The researcher's claim characterized this as a
   paper about "the Bin3 yeast homolog (hob3+)," but citation-verifier confirmed the paper
   is actually about hob1+, the yeast homolog of BIN1 (BIN3's paralog), not BIN3 itself.
   This is a substantive error, not a minor overstatement — the citation must be excluded
   entirely from any BIN3-specific claim, not merely downweighted.

4. **Sathishkumar C et al., "Augmentation of histone deacetylase 3 (HDAC3) epigenetic
   signature at the interface of proinflammation and insulin resistance in patients with
   type 2 diabetes." Clinical Epigenetics, 2016. PMID: 27904654, DOI:
   10.1186/s13148-016-0293-3.**
   Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH. DBC1 (CCAR2) is reduced in PBMCs from type 2 diabetics, but
   the claimed "direct inverse correlation with inflammatory markers (TNF-α, IL-6, NF-κB,
   TLR2/4)" is not explicitly demonstrated in the abstract — the actual relationship shown
   is indirect, mediated via HDAC3, not a direct DBC1-inflammatory-marker correlation.
   Red-team-skeptic flagged this as "a textbook example of a claim being strengthened in
   translation from what the abstract actually supports." Excluded from the evidence
   table; the underlying CCAR2-inflammation link is instead supported (with appropriate
   tissue-mismatch caveats) by the Moreno-Navarrete 2015 adipose-tissue paper, which is
   included.

5. **Saint-Preux F et al., "Chronic co-administration of nicotine and methamphetamine
   causes differential expression of immediate early genes in the dorsal striatum and
   nucleus accumbens of rats." Neuroscience, 2013. PMID: 23562942, DOI:
   10.1016/j.neuroscience.2013.03.052.**
   Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH. The researcher's claim stated "nicotine exposure alters egr3
   expression in rat brain reward circuitry," but citation-verifier found the abstract
   shows nicotine ALONE did NOT significantly alter nucleus accumbens egr3 — egr3 only
   decreased after methamphetamine or the nicotine+methamphetamine combination. This is
   an important correction, not just a citation-accuracy issue: per the red-team-skeptic,
   "if anyone downstream tries to build a 'smoking-mediated EGR3' story...this specific
   citation actually argues against nicotine alone being sufficient, undermining that
   story rather than supporting it." Excluded from the evidence table; the smoking-
   mediation question for EGR3 at this locus remains genuinely unresolved (absence of
   evidence either way), not supported by this citation.

No citations were found to be RETRACTED or NOT_FOUND at this locus (0 of 23 checked).

## Contested / weakened genes (red-team-skeptic verdicts, reasoning preserved)

**Every gene evaluated at this locus was marked WEAKENED by the red-team-skeptic** — no
gene emerged from critique with an uncontested, strong combination of both genetic and
mechanistic support. Full critique text is in
`evidence_tables/_status/8_22500001-22750000/critique.md`; key points summarized below.

- **BIN3 — WEAKENED.** "Not because the genetic inference is wrong, but because
  'strongest candidate' must be explicitly scoped to statistical/genetic evidence only;
  there is zero mechanistic corroboration in any domain (structural, developmental,
  immune), and one of its supporting citations must be struck entirely for being about
  the wrong paralog." The Open Targets L2G case is genuinely strong and lung-specific
  (exon/transcript/splicing QTLs), but no literature corroborates a lung-relevant
  mechanism.

- **CCAR2 — WEAKENED.** "The L2G picture here is more ambiguous than the researcher's
  writeup fully conveys: CCAR2 is not just a 'consistent second place' — it is the TOP
  L2G gene for two independent FVC GWAS at this same variant, beating BIN3. This is a
  genuine LD/gene-prioritization ambiguity, not a clean BIN3 > CCAR2 hierarchy." The
  DBC1/SIRT1/NF-κB senescence mechanism is "biologically attractive...but every
  supporting paper is liver, adipose tissue, or cancer cell lines — a clear tissue
  mismatch." Strong LoF constraint (pLoF o/e=0.20) raises doubt about a common-variant
  lung-causal mechanism for an essential, pleiotropic gene.

- **PDLIM2 — WEAKENED.** "This is the locus's clearest case of a genetics/mechanism
  mismatch running in the opposite direction from BIN3": strong, COPD-specific
  mechanistic literature (myeloid PDLIM2 repression in human COPD/ILD lungs, causal in
  mouse acute lung injury) but "no locus-level genetic evidence tying the GWAS signal to
  PDLIM2 at all." The COPD-specific claim "rests on one overlapping author group
  (Xiao/Shapiro/Qu/Gao)...publishing 3+ closely related papers in under 18 months. This
  is a single research group's narrative, not yet independently replicated." Independent
  (non-COPD, cancer-context) PDLIM2-lung literature from other groups exists but does not
  corroborate the specific COPD claim.

- **EGR3 — WEAKENED.** "Genetic support is weak, the lung-relevant mouse literature is
  in a fibrosis model (not COPD/emphysema), and the one smoking-pathway citation, once
  corrected, does not support a smoking-mediated mechanism for EGR3 at this locus
  (nicotine alone was null)." The Th17/γδ-T-cell finding (Parkinson 2014) is real but
  disease-model-mismatched (bleomycin fibrosis, not COPD/emphysema).

- **PEBP4 — WEAKENED.** "The researcher's own 'weak candidate on current literature
  evidence' framing is honest and should stand; the risk is if a synthesis step treats
  'PoPS-prioritized' as if it were independent evidence rather than a hypothesis-
  generation flag with zero corroboration found in either genetics or literature." Zero
  Open Targets genetic/L2G evidence at this locus; all literature is non-lung (myoblast
  differentiation) or lung-cancer-context (not COPD).

- **C8orf58 — WEAKENED.** "Not because a specific overreach was found, but because there
  is essentially no evidence base to support treating this as a serious candidate; any
  prioritization statement above 'not currently supported by available data' would be
  unsupported." Functionally uncharacterized in PubMed.

## Red-team-skeptic coverage disclosure (important caveat on the critique itself)

The red-team-skeptic's WebSearch budget was exhausted before it began this locus's
critique (200/200 used, apparently from prior work earlier in the same session), so it
substituted WebFetch queries with uneven success:
- **BIN3**: full independent WebFetch pass completed (surfaced the new Ramalingam 2008
  citation, subsequently verified — see evidence table).
- **PDLIM2**: full independent WebFetch pass completed (identified the overlapping-
  authorship concern and additional non-COPD independent PDLIM2-lung literature).
- **CCAR2**: attempted GWAS Catalog fetch failed to render; effectively no independent
  pass — critique relies on the researcher's original findings only.
- **EGR3, PEBP4, C8orf58**: no independent search pass at all; critique of these three
  genes is a re-analysis of the researcher/verifier record only, not independently
  corroborated or contradicted by fresh search.

This means the critique's coverage was uneven across genes, and "no contradicting paper
was found" for EGR3/PEBP4/C8orf58/CCAR2 should be read as "not found under this
constrained search budget," not as a thorough negative search.
