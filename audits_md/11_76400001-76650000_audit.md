# Audit trail — locus 11:76,400,001-76,650,000 (nearest gene EMSY)

This file preserves the excluded citations, contested-gene reasoning, and process notes
that are not included in the main evidence table (`evidence_tables/11_76400001-76650000.md`),
so that table can be handed out on its own without also handing out the full critique
trail.

## Excluded citations (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH)

None. All 24 citations produced by the researcher for this locus were checked by
citation-verifier and returned VERIFIED — existing, not retracted, and matching the
claims attributed to them (see full verdicts in
`evidence_tables/_status/11_76400001-76650000/verified_citations.md`). No citation was
excluded from the evidence table on verification grounds.

One citation (PMID 42270499, Preglej et al. 2026, Ann Rheum Dis) was initially flagged by
the orchestrator as having a suspiciously high PMID number for its stated 2026
publication date, raising concern it might be fabricated or mis-transcribed. The
citation-verifier confirmed it is a genuine, legitimate PMID for a real article published
June 2026, and the claim attached to it (incidental mention of GVQW3 in a rheumatoid
arthritis PBMC transcriptomic study, not a functional characterization) was confirmed
accurate. This is recorded here as a process note, not an exclusion.

One citation (PMID 26990894, Meyer-Martin et al. 2016, Allergy) was surfaced by the
researcher's search but initially left without an attached claim because the researcher
did not review its content in detail. The citation-verifier independently reviewed it and
confirmed it shows GARP (LRRC32) inhibition reduces allergic airway inflammation in a
humanized mouse model. The red-team-skeptic flagged this as directly lung/airway-relevant
functional evidence for LRRC32 that should not be left out. It has been restored to the
main evidence table as a VERIFIED LRRC32 finding, not excluded.

## Genes marked CONTESTED by the red-team-skeptic

### EMSY — gene assignment CONTESTED (not the underlying locus-disease association)

The skeptic's verdict: "EMSY-as-the-causal-COPD-gene is CONTESTED." Reasoning, preserved
verbatim from the critique:

> The contesting evidence is already in the researcher's own material and the Open
> Targets context, not new: (1) L2G evidence across ~15 studies at this exact interval
> favors LRRC32 over EMSY; (2) essentially every discovery paper names the locus jointly,
> reflecting unresolved gene assignment; (3) no FEV1/FEV1-FVC or COPD-specific functional
> evidence for EMSY exists; (4) the EMSY-expression eQTL claim (Amaral 2014) is tied to a
> non-credible-set variant in an atopy/immune context, not lung tissue. I'm not asserting
> LRRC32 is proven instead — only that the case for EMSY specifically (as opposed to
> "this locus, gene TBD") is weaker than the writeup's structure (which spends most of
> its mechanistic firepower on EMSY) implies. This doesn't require new verification — it's
> a synthesis of already-verified material — but flag explicitly for synthesis-lead as a
> reason to present this locus as "gene ambiguous, EMSY vs. LRRC32" rather than "EMSY
> locus."

The skeptic also specifically critiqued two individual claims as overclaiming:

> **Elias 2019 skin-barrier study over-extended to airway** (c): Filaggrin/filaggrin-2/
> ceramide-based stratum corneum barrier biology is keratinocyte-specific machinery;
> airway epithelium uses a structurally and mechanistically different barrier (tight
> junctions, mucociliary clearance, different lipid biology) and is exposed to inhaled
> irritants/smoke rather than mechanical/allergen skin exposure. Calling this "directly
> analogous" to airway epithelial barrier in COPD overstates what the cited abstract
> (keratinocyte organotypic culture, atopic dermatitis skin biopsies) actually supports...
> the phrase "directly analogous" should be struck or softened to something like
> "suggestive of a barrier-regulatory role for EMSY, untested in airway epithelium."

> **Interferon-repression "through-line" is the weakest piece of reasoning in the
> writeup** (d): It stitches together an atopy paper's *proposed* (not directly tested)
> IFN-stimulated-gene repression mechanism (Amaral 2014, via a non-credible-set variant
> rs2155219) with a separate NSCLC literature on EMSY repressing type-I IFN in tumor
> cells (Marzio 2021, Sheban 2023, Baykara 2017). Different diseases, different cell
> types, no single study connecting the two, and no COPD data anywhere in the chain...
> Recommend it be explicitly downgraded to "hypothesis-generating only; no evidence in
> any lung-relevant COPD tissue or cell type."

### LRRC32 — WEAKENED (incomplete evaluation), not fully CONTESTED

> Plausible TGF-beta/Treg airway-remodeling mechanism, original discovery paper naming,
> and Open Targets' own L2G repeatedly favors it over EMSY, but the one COPD-specific L2G
> row is unresolved/unconfirmed, and a directly relevant airway-inflammation functional
> paper (Meyer-Martin 2016) was found but never integrated [prior to this synthesis step,
> now restored to the main evidence table]. Needs another research pass before it can be
> confidently ranked against EMSY.

### ACER3 — CONTESTED / effectively unsupported

> ACER3 sits ~210 kb outside the stated window and >270 kb from the credible-set
> interval, separated from it by two other genes; it has zero L2G, zero fine-mapping,
> zero COPD/FEV1 Open Targets evidence, and zero lung/COPD literature (the only
> mechanistic hit is a colitis mouse model, non-lung). Its sole support is internal
> MAGMA/PoPS statistical prioritization — gene-based/functional-similarity methods that
> are well known to pick up signal from LD/gene-density artifacts belonging to physically
> closer genes (EMSY, LRRC32) rather than ACER3 itself. This is effectively a
> positional/statistical-bias problem in the opposite direction from typical "nearest
> gene" bias — here a non-nearest gene is being kept in contention purely by a method
> susceptible to exactly the contamination this pipeline is designed to catch. I'd
> recommend ACER3 be reported as **CONTESTED/effectively refuted as a candidate at this
> locus**, not merely "low weight."

### GVQW3 — HOLDS as a non-candidate

> Correctly included only for completeness with no inference built on it; no critique
> needed beyond that.

## Other skeptic observations preserved for the record

- **Lead variant has no literature.** rs7926914 (lead, PIP=0.247) is not tied to any
  cited paper — all literature support attaches to lower-PIP credible-set members
  (rs7130588, PIP 0.141; rs7927894, PIP 0.214). The strongest statistical signal in the
  credible set is functionally silent in the literature reviewed.
- **Cross-population caveat.** Zhang 2020 (EMSY haplotype/asthma) and Amaral 2014
  (rs2155219/expression) both use SNPs distinct from the credible set, in different
  populations (Chinese Han; ECRHS European) with potentially different LD structure.
- **Smoking-mediation (Biological Prior #1) is an incomplete negative.** The researcher's
  web-search budget was exhausted before a GWAS-Catalog cross-check for
  smoking-initiation/heaviness/cessation associations at this locus could be completed.
  No positive evidence was found in the searches that were completed, but this should not
  be read as a confirmed absence. The skeptic's inference (allergic-sensitization/atopy
  genetics are a priori less likely to be smoking-behavior-mediated than loci with direct
  neuro-nicotinic biology such as CHRNA3/5) is noted as reasoning, not a completed
  finding.
- **Process caveat from the skeptic:** its own planned contradicting-evidence searches
  ("GARP LRRC32 airway lung COPD"; "EMSY FEV1 spirometry GWAS") could not be run because
  the session's web-search budget was already exhausted by the time of its review. The
  critique above is based on the researcher's and orchestrator's materials plus
  biological-prior reasoning, not independent re-verification of literature absence. A
  second-pass literature search at this locus is recommended before treating the EMSY vs.
  LRRC32 ranking as final.

## Physical-position discrepancy note

The researcher's Ensembl-based gene-in-region enumeration found that two of the
internal pipeline's own candidate genes for this locus — LRRC32 (MAGMA/PoPS did not
flag it; it was added post hoc by the orchestrator) and ACER3 (MAGMA + PoPS prioritized)
— both lie physically outside the stated 76,400,001-76,650,000 window (LRRC32 by ~7.5 kb;
ACER3 by ~210 kb), while only EMSY and the uncharacterized gene GVQW3 fall fully or
partly inside it. This suggests the internal prioritization pipeline used a wider
LD/regulatory window than the coordinates stated in the locus input file. This is
recorded here as a data-provenance note for the study team, not resolved further by this
review.
