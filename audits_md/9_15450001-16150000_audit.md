# Audit trail / excluded appendix — Locus 9:15,450,001-16,150,000 (GRCh38)

This file lists everything excluded from the main evidence table
(`evidence_tables/9_15450001-16150000.md`), plus the full reasoning behind CONTESTED /
WEAKENED gene calls, preserved verbatim from the red-team-skeptic where possible, for reviewer
traceability. This audit trail is intentionally kept separate from the evidence table so the
evidence table can be handed out on its own.

## Excluded / unresolved citations

**Shrine et al. 2019, Nature Genetics, PMID 30804560, DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0321-7 — Claim C
(gene-naming at this locus): NOT_FOUND / unresolved.**

Claim asked of citation-verifier: "Is CCDC171 (or PSIP1) explicitly named as the nearest/candidate
gene for a sentinel signal at approximately chr9:15.5-16.1 Mb (GRCh38) / chr9p22 in this paper's
list of 279 signals?"

Citation-verifier's finding: "The full text of the paper references the complete list of 279
signals in 'Supplementary Table 3,' which is not included in the accessible full text. The main
body does not explicitly name CCDC171 or PSIP1 as nearest genes for the chr9:15.5-16.1 Mb locus.
Cannot verify from available accessible content." (Note: the verifier's own summary line labeled
this same finding "CLAIM_MISMATCH" while its body text used "NOT_FOUND" — both labels describe the
same underlying finding, that this specific gene-naming claim could not be positively confirmed
from accessible content; it is not a case of a fabricated or contradicted reference, but of an
unresolved gap requiring access to a supplementary table that was not retrievable in this pipeline
run.)

**Disposition:** the paper's general claims (ciliogenesis/centriole-gene enrichment among
lung-function loci genome-wide; smoking-screening methodology) ARE verified and are retained in
the main evidence table, explicitly labeled as pathway-level/general findings. The specific claim
that CCDC171 (or PSIP1) is the named gene at this exact signal in that paper is NOT retained as a
supported claim anywhere in the evidence table or synthesis — per red-team critique, treating this
unconfirmed link as established would be overclaiming.

**Chang LH et al., Drug Alcohol Depend, PMID 31731259 and PMID 30875648 — VERIFIED but excluded
from the main evidence table.**

Both papers were verified as accurately described (GSCAN-derived polygenic-risk-score applications
in Australian cohorts), but neither makes any claim about CCDC171, PSIP1, SNAPC3, or this locus
specifically — they were retrieved by the researcher only to document that a direct primary
GSCAN smoking-initiation/cigarettes-per-day GWAS paper naming this locus could not be found via
PubMed search. Excluded from the table because they carry no gene-specific evidentiary content;
retained here for completeness/traceability of the researcher's search process.

## CONTESTED / WEAKENED gene calls (red-team-skeptic reasoning, verbatim)

### CCDC171 — WEAKENED

"The core statistical evidence (L2G dominance across 4 independent fine-mapped FEV1/FEV1-FVC
datasets, lung eQTL h4~0.945) is real and among the stronger regulatory-prioritization cases in
this kind of pipeline, and should be reported as such. But the inference is overreaching in two
specific ways: (1) the 'ciliary/pulmonary-structural mechanism' narrative is built on a
pathway-level GWAS-wide enrichment theme (Shrine 2019) whose gene-specific link to CCDC171 is
explicitly unconfirmed, layered onto a gene with zero dedicated primary functional literature; (2)
the alternative behavioral/metabolic-pleiotropy hypothesis (ADHD, risk-taking, alcohol, BMI; COPD
absent from CCDC171's own 76-disease OT profile) is not adequately weighed against the
pulmonary-structural story, and per prior #1 this should be actively considered as a
smoking-behavior-mediated alternative rather than folded in as corroborating pleiotropy. Not
CONTESTED because no credible paper argues affirmatively against CCDC171 or names a clearly
superior single alternative gene for the FEV1/FEV1-FVC signals specifically — the L2G data
actually argues strongly *for* CCDC171 over PSIP1 at the pulmonary-trait level. But the
mechanistic story attached to it overreaches what's shown."

### PSIP1 — CONTESTED

"Grounds: (1) systematically minor/secondary L2G scores (0.05–0.06) at every one of four
independent fine-mapped FEV1/FEV1-FVC credible sets overlapping this locus, consistently
dominated by CCDC171 (0.54–0.67) — this is convergent, not a one-off; (2) direct, verified
negative evidence: Sutherland et al. 2006's Psip1-null mouse underwent extensive phenotyping
(perinatal mortality, fertility, fat pads, blepharitis, motor/behavioral, craniofacial/skeletal)
and explicitly reported no pulmonary/airway phenotype; (3) PSIP1's own cis-pQTLs are trans to this
locus, and its broad 3262-disease Open Targets profile (dominated by HIV and cancer) contains no
COPD/FEV1/FEV1-FVC signal; (4) no COPD/lung-function GWAS, TWAS, MR, or colocalization study in
the literature names PSIP1 as a causal candidate anywhere, despite extensive published
characterization of the gene in other contexts (AML fusion, HIV integration, autoantigen biology).
Its candidacy rests solely on FLAMES/ABC regulatory-interaction prioritization plus the French et
al. 2016 enhancer-sharing/PSIP1-functional-validation finding — both of which are in non-lung
contexts (French et al. is ovarian cancer). This is a genuine, citable contradiction pattern
(convergent negative L2G + explicit lung-negative knockout phenotype) that should be surfaced to
synthesis-lead as grounds for demoting PSIP1 below CCDC171 for this specific
pulmonary-function/COPD locus, even though PSIP1 is clearly not to be dismissed on essentiality
grounds (LoF-constrained but that's genome-wide essentiality, not disease-specificity)."

### SNAPC3 — not contested (no claim advanced)

"No claim was ever advanced (no prioritization support, no disease-specific literature), so there
is nothing to contest. The researcher's conclusion that it is 'not a serious candidate' is
adequately supported by the absence of evidence; this holds as an appropriately deprioritized
gene, not as a confirmed non-causal gene (absence of evidence, not evidence of absence — but with
zero prioritization signal from any of five methods, deprioritization is reasonable)."

**Orchestrator's note on this disclosure:** because the red-team-skeptic's own independent search

queries above is the recommended next step before increasing confidence beyond LOW.

## Researcher's own flagged gaps (preserved for traceability)

- CCDC171 has no dedicated primary functional/mechanistic paper in PubMed; a targeted search for
  "CCDC171 centriole cilia coiled-coil protein" returned zero hits. Its putative
  cilia/centrosome domain-architecture annotation comes only from public gene-annotation
  databases (e.g., GeneCards/UniProt-style descriptions), which were explicitly not treated as
  primary literature and are not cited as evidence anywhere in the main table.
- Whether this specific 9p22/CCDC171/PSIP1 locus reaches genome-wide significance in the primary
  GSCAN smoking-initiation or cigarettes-per-day GWAS was not established; only unrelated
  downstream polygenic-risk-score application papers were found (PMID 31731259, PMID 30875648 —
  see excluded citations above).
