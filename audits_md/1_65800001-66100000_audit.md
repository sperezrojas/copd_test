## Excluded citations

### CLAIM_MISMATCH (paper is real, but the specific claim attributed to it is inaccurate)

1. **Hsu CG et al. 2021, "Phosphodiesterase 10A Is a Key Mediator of Lung
   Inflammation," Journal of Immunology, PMID 34117108, DOI 10.4049/jimmunol.2001026.**
   Researcher's claim: "PDE4B (along with PDE10A) is highly and transiently induced by
   LPS in mouse alveolar/peritoneal macrophages, with PDE4B inhibition specifically
   blocking LPS-induced TNF-alpha (but not MCP-1) production." Citation-verifier verdict:
   CLAIM_MISMATCH. Reason: "Title focuses on PDE10A, not PDE4B as co-equal focus" — the
   paper's primary subject is PDE10A; PDE4B's role is secondary/incidental to how the
   researcher framed the claim.

2. **Lombard Z et al. 2012, "Appetite regulation genes are associated with body mass
   index in black South African adolescents," BMJ Open, PMID 22614171, DOI
   10.1136/bmjopen-2012-000873.** Researcher's claim: "LEP/LEPR variants examined among
   appetite-regulation genes for BMI association in South African adolescents (primary
   significant hits were in FTO, LEP, MC4R)." Citation-verifier verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH.
   Reason: "Paper examined LEP/LEPR/FTO/MC4R/others but significant hits were only FTO
   rs17817449, LEP rs10954174, LEP rs6966536, and MC4R rs17782313; no LEPR SNPs reached
   significance. Claim overclaims LEPR association." Red-team-skeptic flagged this as a
   documented instance of the researcher overstating LEPR's genetic evidence, which
   "raises the bar for the remaining LEPR evidence."

## CONTESTED gene reasoning (verbatim from red-team-skeptic)

### PDE4B — CONTESTED

"Not because a specific paper argues against PDE4B, but because the case as constructed
substitutes (a) an unconfirmed database attribution (smoking), (b) drug-class/
target-validation biology (COPD clinical relevance of PDE4 inhibition broadly), and (c)
mostly acute-injury/IPF mouse mechanism for the one thing that's actually missing: any
COPD or FEV1 genetic association study, TWAS, coloc, or MR naming PDE4B or its variants.
Physical overlap and 100%-intronic credible-set membership are genuinely strong
locus-assignment evidence, but locus assignment ('which gene do these SNPs sit in') is a
different claim from 'this gene's expression change causes COPD,' and the latter is
currently unsupported by direct genetic data."

Supporting detail from the same critique: "the credible set is 60+ variants, all PDE4B
introns, lead PIP only 0.208 — meaning >79% of posterior probability is either diffuse
across other variants or the fine-mapping is poorly resolved. No eQTL/colocalization
evidence was found showing these introns regulate PDE4B expression in lung or immune
cells specifically." Also: "Open Targets' own data structure makes this explicit: the
elevated COPD score is 'driven almost ENTIRELY by clinical/drug-class datatype,' with
zero genetic_association signal for COPD and zero for FEV1/FEV1-FVC... the drug works on
the whole PDE4 family (nonselective for A-D), so approved-therapy precedent cannot be
used to argue PDE4B-specific genetic causality at all." And on the smoking hypothesis:
"the CNS literature actually cited... is real and verified, but none of it is
nicotine/reward/addiction biology — it's PDE4B's general neuro-inflammatory/cognitive
role. Using it to substantiate a 'smoking-mediation' story is a category error: it shows
PDE4B is pharmacologically active in brain, not that this locus drives smoking
behavior."

### LEPR — CONTESTED

"LEPR has a genuine, replicated FEV1-relevant genetic_association signal at the
gene/trait level (unlike PDE4B, which has none) and a biologically coherent CD8+ T-cell/
leptin immune story (Bruno 2005) that fits this pipeline's stated interest in T-cell
programs — these are real strengths PDE4B lacks. But the case is undermined by total
absence from the credible set/physical window, an unreplicated and internally
contradicted (van den Borst) SNP-level pulmonary finding, a caught overstatement
(Lombard), directionally incoherent biomarker data best explained by BMI/cachexia
confounding rather than LEPR causality, and a plausible BMI-mediation alternative that
parallels the smoking-mediation concern raised for PDE4B but wasn't applied with equal
force."

On physical non-overlap specifically: "This is the single largest problem, and I'd
weight it more heavily than the researcher's framing suggests. LEPR sits ~150-190kb
outside the credible-set window with zero credible-set variants. FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS are
all genome-wide/window-based or functional-similarity statistical prioritizers, not
fine-mapping or colocalization methods — PoPS in particular is known to favor
well-annotated, feature-rich genes (LEPR has a huge, mature obesity/metabolic
literature) over the true local causal gene... Three methods agreeing is notable, but
they are not statistically independent if they share underlying feature sets... This
should be flagged as a possible shared-bias artifact, not independent triangulation."

On the Hansel/van den Borst contradiction: "Hansel is a 2009 candidate-gene study in 429
smokers with no confirmed independent replication in 16+ years, which in the post-GWAS
era is a real credibility problem — an unreplicated, uncorrected, candidate-gene-era hit
should not be treated as equivalent-weight evidence to a modern GWAS signal. 21/36
nominal hits is more than chance (~1.8 expected) but multiple-SNP LD non-independence
within one gene inflates apparent hit-count; this needs a proper conditional/replication
analysis before being counted as confirmatory."

On the discordant biomarker literature: "McNeill (general population: higher leptin =
worse FEV1%/FVC%) vs. Ruvuna (COPD cohorts: higher leptin = slower FEV1 decline) vs.
Schols/Takabatake (COPD/emphysema patients have lower circulating leptin, tracking
cachexia) is a genuinely three-way directional conflict. The researcher's own
interpretation — that this reflects BMI-mediated confounding rather than a single
LEPR-lung mechanism — is correct and should be stated more forcefully as evidence
against a straightforward LEPR-COPD causal story, not just a caveat."

On LEPROT: "LEPROT is under-searched. It physically overlaps/read-throughs with LEPR at
this locus and could be an alternative candidate for the LEPR-associated PoPS/MAGMA/
FLAMES signal, but it was 'mentioned only incidentally' with no dedicated evidence
search. Flag for synthesis-lead: LEPROT needs its own pass before this locus's
LEPR-vs-PDE4B question can be considered resolved."

### Overall locus-level assessment (verbatim)

"This is a genuine two-gene conflict with no clean winner: PDE4B has the
physical/fine-mapping/credible-set support but zero COPD or FEV1 genetic-association
evidence and a mechanistic literature that is largely acute-injury/IPF/drug-class rather
than COPD-specific; LEPR has an actual FEV1 genetic signal and an immune-cell story
fitting the pipeline's T-cell priors, but sits entirely outside the locus window and has
internally contradictory support. Both should be presented to synthesis-lead as
CONTESTED, not adjudicated as if one clearly beats the other."
