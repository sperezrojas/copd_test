# Audit trail — Locus 16:68950001-70600000 (lead variant rs889398; nearest gene NFAT5)

This file documents everything excluded from the main evidence table
(`evidence_tables/16_68950001-70600000.md`) and the reasoning behind it, plus the
red-team-skeptic's full gene-level verdicts (verbatim), so a reviewer can audit the
pipeline's judgment calls without needing the evidence table itself.

## Excluded citations

### RETRACTED

- **PMID 22198263** — Potts-Kant EN, Li Z, Tighe RM, et al. "NAD(P)H:quinone
  oxidoreductase 1 protects lungs from oxidant-induced emphysema in mice." Free Radic
  Biol Med. 2011/2012;52(3):705-715. DOI: 10.1016/j.freeradbiomed.2011.11.027.
  **Excluded reason:** Both the researcher's PubMed metadata lookup and the independent
  citation-verifier confirm this article "has been retracted at the request of the
  Authors" due to discrepancies found between raw pulmonary-physiology data and
  re-exported machine data; the NQO1-deficient mouse line used is no longer available to
  permit replication. This was the only direct causal (knockout-mouse) evidence found for
  an NQO1-emphysema-protection mechanism; its retraction substantially weakens the
  overall case for NQO1 at this locus (see NQO1 CONTESTED verdict below).

### CLAIM_MISMATCH

- **PMID 30804561** — Sakornsakolpat P, Prokopenko D, Lamontagne M, et al. "Genetic
  landscape of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease identifies heterogeneous cell-type
  and phenotype associations." Nat Genet. 2019;51(3):494-505.
  **Excluded reason:** The paper is confirmed as the principal COPD GWAS meta-analysis
  (35,735 cases/222,076 controls plus ICGC, 82 loci) and its SMAD-binding/ECM DEPICT
  pathway-enrichment finding was confirmed as genuine, but citation-verifier could NOT
  confirm — from abstract/available full text, without supplementary-table access — that
  NFAT5, WWP2, or NQO1 is explicitly named as a candidate/target gene at a 16q22.1 locus
  in this paper's tables. The researcher had already flagged this as unconfirmed before
  dispatch to citation-verifier.

- **PMID 36914875** — Shrine N, Izquierdo AG, Chen J, et al. "Multi-ancestry
  genome-wide association analyses improve resolution of genes and pathways influencing
  lung function and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease risk." Nat Genet.
  2023;55(3):410-422.
  **Excluded reason:** Confirmed as the largest multi-ancestry lung-function GWAS to date
  (580,869 participants, 1,020 independent signals, 559 genes), but citation-verifier
  could NOT confirm that WWP2 is explicitly named as a prioritized/likely causal gene at
  a 16q22.1 lung-function signal in this paper, again without supplementary-table access.
  This is consequential: it was the researcher's primary candidate explanation for
  WWP2's very strong Open Targets FEV1 L2G score (0.723, n=18), and its non-confirmation
  means that score currently rests on an untraced database computation rather than a
  literature-verified finding. See the red-team-skeptic's WWP2 critique below.

## Gene-level red-team-skeptic verdicts (verbatim)

The red-team-skeptic disclosed upfront that its WebSearch budget was exhausted before any
of its three planned independent contradiction searches (for NFAT5, WWP2, NQO1) could
return results — the critique below is internal red-teaming of the assembled,
already-verified evidence (checking for overclaiming, hedging, internal consistency, and
logical gaps), not verification against freshly-surfaced literature. This limitation is
preserved here verbatim rather than papered over.

### NFAT5 — WEAKENED

> The statistical case (modest COPD L2G 0.383/n=2, moderate FEV1 0.506/n=2, strongest
> smoking-initiation L2G 0.647/n=6, zero FEV1/FVC evidence) and the immune mechanism
> (Th17/macrophage-activating TF under hyperosmotic/TLR stress) are both real, but two
> things are being asymmetrically treated relative to WWP2, and one alternative pathway is
> under-weighted:
>
> - (a) Th17/autoimmune evidence appropriately hedged, but fragile downstream. The
>   researcher's final synthesis sentence does correctly hedge that the pro-inflammatory
>   Th1/Th17/macrophage evidence is "demonstrated almost entirely in autoimmune-disease/renal
>   models, not lung." That hedge is honest and matches what the verified abstracts actually
>   say (Choi 2020 = renal/metabolic; Aramburu 2019, Lee 2019, Ye 2020 = TLR/RA/arthritis, no
>   lung tissue anywhere). The risk is that the four individual bullet-point claims, if
>   lifted out of the synthesis paragraph by a downstream reader, read as generically
>   supportive of "NFAT5 drives inflammatory pathways relevant to COPD" — the Kästle 2017
>   paper's abstract-level framing of Th17/COPD is explicitly flagged by the researcher as
>   introduction/discussion context-setting, not data, which is the correct call, but this
>   is the single citation that could be misquoted as "NFAT5 study on COPD" if the caveat is
>   dropped. Flag for synthesis-lead: preserve the hedge verbatim, don't cite Kästle without
>   the caveat.
> - Symmetry problem with the smoking-mediation claim. The 0.647 smoking-initiation L2G
>   score is doing a lot of work in the "consistent with partial smoking-behavior mediation"
>   conclusion, but exactly like WWP2's FEV1 score, this number comes from Open Targets'
>   structured DB and is explicitly not confirmed by the one literature citation that could
>   have anchored it (GSCAN/Liu 2019). If the WWP2 case is going to be criticized (correctly,
>   see below) for resting on an unconfirmed DB score, the same standard must be applied to
>   NFAT5's smoking-mediation claim — it is equally unconfirmed by primary literature.
> - (d) BMI pleiotropy is a live, underweighted alternative for NFAT5 specifically.
>   rs889398's most robust reported association is BMI (p~10⁻³⁵), and NFAT5/TonEBP's own
>   verified literature (Choi 2020) ties it to T2D, diabetic nephropathy, and osmotic/
>   metabolic stress — i.e., NFAT5's known biology plausibly is a BMI/metabolic-stress
>   pathway, arguably a better fit to the lead variant's strongest signal than either lung-
>   intrinsic disease or smoking behavior. The researcher's synthesis never considers
>   "BMI/metabolic pleiotropy" as a fourth, arguably best-fitting alternative.
> - No FEV1/FVC support at all (obstructive-specific spirometric trait) is a genuine strike
>   against a pulmonary-obstruction mechanism per prior #2.
>
> Net: real evidence, but the inference overreaches by (i) not flagging that its own
> flagship "smoking-mediation" evidence is exactly as unconfirmed as the WWP2 evidence it
> would presumably be compared against, and (ii) omitting the BMI/metabolic-pleiotropy
> alternative that fits NFAT5's actual known biology at least as well as any COPD-relevant
> story.

### WWP2 — WEAKENED (borderline CONTESTED)

> (b) This is the single biggest problem at the locus. Both citations that were supposed to
> establish "WWP2 is the lung-function gene at 16q22.1" — Shrine 2023 and Sakornsakolpat
> 2019 — came back CLAIM_MISMATCH. Neither paper can be confirmed to name WWP2 at this
> locus. That means we have zero independently-verified primary-literature confirmation
> that WWP2 is the causal gene at this locus for any trait. The entire quantitative case
> ("strongest/most-replicated FEV1 association at locus," FEV1 score 0.723/n=18) rests
> solely on an Open Targets L2G score that the citation-verifier could not trace to a
> specific paper. Worse, WWP2 was selected as a candidate because it's the FLAMES/MAGMA/
> PoPS-prioritized gene — i.e., the gene-prioritization algorithms (which likely feed into
> or correlate with the L2G score itself) agree with each other, but none of that agreement
> has been independently checked against a primary paper's stated conclusions. This is
> closer to circular computational consensus than to replicated literature, and the
> researcher's synthesis language ("strongest/most-replicated FEV1 association") overstates
> this — "most replicated" implies independent literature replication, which does not exist
> here; it should read "highest algorithmic L2G score, unreplicated in named literature."
> - COPD L2G = 0 rows. Despite being the top statistical-genetics candidate at the locus,
>   WWP2 has literally no Open Targets COPD evidence — only quantitative-trait (FEV1)
>   evidence. That's consistent with prior #2 but also means the case for WWP2 driving
>   COPD (rather than population variation in lung function generally) is unestablished.
> - Mechanism is real biology but wrong tissue. Chen 2019 (Nat Commun) is a legitimate,
>   verified finding — WWP2-N ubiquitinates and promotes SMAD2 activity driving fibrosis —
>   but it is a cardiac mouse model, with no lung tissue, airway fibroblast, or COPD model
>   anywhere in the citation trail. Presenting it as support for a "lung-structural/fibrotic-
>   remodeling mechanism" is an extrapolation across organ systems that should be labeled as
>   such, not treated as tissue-relevant mechanistic support.
> - No WWP2 immune/Th17/Treg literature at all — so WWP2 offers zero anchor for the
>   inflammatory heterogeneity emphasized in prior #3; its case must stand entirely on the
>   (currently unconfirmed) structural/fibrotic story.
> - LD/nearest-gene bias: in a ~1Mb, ~19-gene window, being "the PoPS/MAGMA/FLAMES top pick"
>   doesn't by itself distinguish WWP2 from neighboring genes with a literature-confirmed
>   identity — it is one plausible answer among several, not a demonstrated one.
>
> Net: I stop short of full CONTESTED only because I found no literature that actively
> argues against WWP2 or names a rival gene at this exact sub-locus (and I could not search
> for one given budget exhaustion — this absence should not be read as exculpatory). But
> the evidentiary chain for WWP2's headline claim is thinner than presented: it is currently
> a database score plus a cardiac mechanism paper, not a literature-anchored lung finding.

### NQO1 — CONTESTED

> This gene should be treated as close to ruled out, not merely weak:
> - (c) The one paper that would have supplied causal (not just correlative) evidence —
>   Potts-Kant 2012, the NQO1-knockout emphysema-protection mouse study — is retracted for
>   data discrepancies. Both researcher and citation-verifier correctly excluded it from the
>   synthesis, but its absence guts the causal case rather than just weakening it.
> - Everything else remaining is one of: (i) purely observational human-tissue correlation
>   (Goven 2008), (ii) NQO1 used only as a downstream oxidative-stress biomarker, not a
>   manipulated gene, in two mouse models (Chrusciel 2016; Li 2024), (iii) a lung-cancer
>   (not COPD) candidate-gene study (Walia 2022) — wrong disease entirely, and (iv) an old,
>   likely underpowered candidate-gene-era COPD case-control study (Korytina 2014) that
>   associates a different variant (rs1131341) than the actual credible-set variant
>   (rs2917677), with the reported smoking-interaction effects not clearly attributed to
>   NQO1 itself.
> - Fine-mapping: NQO1's tagged variant carries a PIP of 0.011 — negligible causal
>   probability within the credible set.
> - Structured-DB support: COPD 0 rows, FEV1 weak (0.151/n=12), FEV1/FVC none, and LOEUF
>   ≈1.23 (LoF-tolerant) — consistent with the well-known common NQO1*2 loss-of-function
>   polymorphism being tolerated in humans without a strong disease signature, which cuts
>   against a major causal role.
> - This constitutes credible contradicting evidence in aggregate (retraction + near-null
>   fine-mapping + near-null statistical genetics + wrong-variant candidate-gene data), even
>   though no single new paper is "arguing against" NQO1 by name. I'm marking this
>   CONTESTED on the strength of the internal contradiction already present in the verified
>   evidence set, not from a new external source.

### Locus-level pleiotropy concern (applies across all three genes, verbatim)

> rs889398's most robust reported association is by far BMI (~10⁻³⁵), in a dense ~1Mb
> window with ~19 genes, and no direct literature on rs889398 itself links it to COPD,
> FEV1, or smoking initiation. Three different prioritization heuristics (nearest-gene →
> NFAT5; algorithmic PoPS/MAGMA/FLAMES → WWP2; lowest-PIP credible-set annotation → NQO1)
> point to three genes with three non-overlapping, individually weak-to-moderate phenotype
> profiles. None has independent literature naming it at this specific COPD/lung-function
> locus. Given that the lead variant's strongest and most robustly replicated association is
> a metabolic trait (BMI), and that NFAT5's own known biology (osmotic/metabolic stress,
> T2D, diabetic nephropathy) plausibly connects to a BMI-mediated pathway, a credible
> alternative hypothesis is that this locus's primary driver is metabolic/adiposity biology
> that secondarily correlates with COPD case status and lung function through BMI's
> well-known effects on spirometry and systemic inflammation — rather than any of the three
> genes acting through a lung-intrinsic or smoking-behavior mechanism specifically for COPD.
> This locus currently does not support a confident single-gene call.

## Methodology gaps disclosed (not evidence exclusions, but limitations to weigh)

- **Open Targets colocalization data:** a raw colocalization posterior-probability field
  could not be resolved via the Open Targets GraphQL API within this session — repeated
  schema/type-dependency introspection calls all exceeded the tool's return-size limit,
  and the resulting dump files were single-line JSON that could not be chunked via the
  Read tool either. L2G-informed evidence scores (via `disease.evidences(datasourceIds:
  ["gwas_credible_sets"])`) were used instead as the closest available proxy. This is a
  gap in the structured-database layer, not a literature-verification issue.
- **Red-team-skeptic search budget:** the red-team-skeptic's WebSearch budget was
  exhausted before it could run any of its three planned independent contradiction
  searches (one per gene). Its critique is internal red-teaming of the already-assembled,
  verified evidence rather than a check against freshly surfaced literature. No new
  citations were surfaced by the critique, so no additional citation-verifier pass was
  triggered, but a future re-run of this locus with restored search budget could
  legitimately surface additional contradicting or supporting literature not captured
  here.
