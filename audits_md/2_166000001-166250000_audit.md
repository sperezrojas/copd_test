# Audit trail (excluded/contested appendix) — locus 2:166,000,001-166,250,000 (SCN1A)

This file preserves everything excluded from the main evidence table
(`evidence_tables/2_166000001-166250000.md`) — retracted/not-found/claim-
mismatched citations, and every gene the red-team-skeptic marked CONTESTED —
along with the reasoning, preserved as close to verbatim as possible, so a

## Excluded citations

### 1. PMID 31229899 — CLAIM_MISMATCH
Netsrithong et al., Stem Cell Res 2019 (DOI 10.1016/j.scr.2019.101487).
Researcher cited this as an example of an "incidental" NAV1.2/SCN2A mention in
an unrelated iPSC-line-generation methods paper, offered only to show the
researcher's search was not naively dismissive of an SCN2A-immune link — not
as substantive evidence.

Citation-verifier's verdict (verbatim): "Real article, not retracted. However,
this is a methods paper for iPSC line generation. The abstract text focuses
entirely on 'Generation of two induced pluripotent stem cell lines...from
peripheral blood T lymphocytes.' NAV1.2/SCN2A appears only in MeSH subject
indexing terms, not as a substantive finding discussed in the paper.
Researcher's caveat that this is 'just an incidental/methods mention' is
correct—no substantive SCN2A research is present."

**Disposition:** Excluded from the evidence table. The researcher's own
framing (that this is not substantive evidence) was independently confirmed
correct by citation-verifier; it is preserved here only as a record that the
search space was checked, not as evidence for SCN2A.

### 2. PMID 30643251 — CLAIM_MISMATCH
Liu M et al. (GSCAN consortium), Nat Genet 2019 (DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0307-5).
Researcher's claim: the paper's main text reports pathway/gene-set enrichment
of "sodium, potassium, and calcium voltage-gated channels" as an enriched
exemplar pathway class in the smoking-initiation (SmkInit) GWAS gene-set
analysis, offered as indirect support for a smoking-behavior-mediated
alternative explanation for this locus's SCN1A/SCN2A/SCN9A gene family.

Citation-verifier's verdict (verbatim): "Real GWAS consortium paper, not
retracted. However, abstract states: 'We report evidence for the involvement
of many systems in tobacco and alcohol use, including genes involved in
nicotinic, dopaminergic, and glutamatergic neurotransmission.' It does NOT
mention 'sodium, potassium, and calcium voltage-gated channels' as a
highlighted pathway class in the abstract. Researcher's claim that pathway/
gene-set enrichment found this pathway cannot be verified from the
abstract—the main findings emphasize nicotinic/dopaminergic/glutamatergic
systems instead."

Red-team-skeptic's assessment (verbatim, from cross-cutting critique): "This
is a load-bearing failure: it was the only piece of evidence connecting this
specific gene family to smoking behavior, and it didn't hold up even at the
abstract level. Until someone checks the GSCAN full text/supplement directly,
this claim should be treated as unsupported, not merely 'unresolved' — the
burden of proof was on the researcher's specific claim, and it failed
verification."

**Disposition:** Excluded from the evidence table and from the smoking-
mediation narrative in the Prioritized-gene section. Note for any future
follow-up: citation-verifier checked only the GSCAN abstract, not full text
or supplementary tables, so it remains possible (but unconfirmed) that this
detail appears in the body text — this should be treated as an open item for
a future pass with fuller document access, not as a settled negative.

## CONTESTED / WEAKENED gene verdicts (red-team-skeptic, preserved verbatim)

### SCN2A — CONTESTED
"Contested evidence: the locus's own coordinates. Per direct GRCh38 Ensembl
lookup, SCN2A (165,194,993–165,392,310) sits 600–850 kb outside the stated
2:166,000,001–166,250,000 window — i.e., it fails the pipeline's own stated
inclusion criterion that PoPS-prioritized genes overlap the locus. This isn't
a subtle LD-block ambiguity; it's a direct physical non-overlap that
undermines the premise for including SCN2A as a candidate at all. No
credible-set variant sits in or near it, no COPD/lung-function GWAS or TWAS
evidence exists, no pulmonary-structural literature was found, and the one
immune citation is CNS-microglia-specific (Que et al., PMID 39557580) — not a
peripheral/systemic inflammatory program relevant to COPD. A second candidate
immune citation was independently caught by the verifier as a MeSH-indexing
artifact with no substantive content (PMID 31229899). I recommend the
synthesis-lead treat SCN2A's inclusion as presumptively an artifact of PoPS's
gene-level (expression/pathway-similarity-based) scoring rather than a
genuine locus-level signal, pending a second independent coordinate check
(e.g., UCSC Table Browser) to rule out a build-mismatch explanation on the
pipeline's side. As things stand, the coordinate evidence directly
contradicts SCN2A's candidacy and nothing in the biology rescues it."

**Orchestrator follow-up:** independently re-checked via Open Targets
Platform GraphQL (`target.genomicLocation`, ENSG00000136531) — returned
chr2:165,194,993-165,392,310, GRCh38, strand +1 — an exact match to the
researcher's original Ensembl REST finding, from a second independent data
source. This corroborates that the coordinate discrepancy is real and not a
lookup error, and reinforces the CONTESTED verdict.

### SCN1A — WEAKENED
"The mouse brainstem-chemoreception literature is genuinely well-verified and
internally consistent (six VERIFIED citations), but the researcher's own
caveat is correct and should be sharpened, not softened, in synthesis: this
is a central respiratory drive/apnea mechanism (CO2/H+ chemoreception,
serotonergic modulation, SUDEP) tied to pediatric monogenic Dravet syndrome —
a completely different physiological axis from adult airflow obstruction.
There is no lung-structural, airway-epithelial, or spirometric evidence at
all, no GWAS/TWAS/MR/colocalization hit, and Open Targets shows zero genetics
contribution (only literature text-mining, score 0.0155) for COPD.
Critically, only 3/47 credible-set variants (each PIP≤0.0016) fall in SCN1A
itself — virtually none of the statistical signal mass actually sits in this
gene, which undercuts the 'nearest gene' label as a reason for inclusion. The
gnomAD LOF-intolerance (oe=0.056) also argues against a common noncoding GWAS
signal acting through the same haploinsufficiency mechanism that causes
Dravet syndrome — that mechanism requires rare damaging coding variants, not
common regulatory variants. Net: real biology, wrong tissue/mechanism/
variant-mass profile for this specific COPD signal."

### SCN1A-AS1 — WEAKENED
"This gene has the strongest statistical claim of the four (lead variant +
44/47 PIP mass sits in its body), but the inferential chain built on top of
that overreaches in two separate, stackable ways: (1) transcript-identity
between the Ensembl-curated gene model SCN1A-AS1 (ENSG00000236107) and the
literature-described antisense transcripts (SCN1A-NAT/dsAS/usAS) is
explicitly unconfirmed — nomenclature for antisense RNAs is inconsistent
across databases, and this was never independently resolved; (2) even
granting identity, every mechanistic citation (Hsiao 2016, Schneider 2024,
Diaz 2026, all VERIFIED) is from brain/neuronal tissue in a
pediatric-epilepsy context, with zero lung, airway, or immune-cell
expression/functional data anywhere in the literature base. So the case
reduces to 'the credible set concentrates here' plus 'an antisense transcript
that might be the same gene regulates SCN1A in neurons' — a plausible
hypothesis, not an established pulmonary mechanism. Zero Open Targets data
and zero PubMed hits under the exact symbol (verifier-confirmed) further mean
there is no independent corroboration. This is the best positional candidate
at the locus but the functional story attached to it is speculative across
two unverified links (transcript identity, tissue relevance)."

### SCN9A — WEAKENED
"Thinnest case of the four: not flagged by any prioritization method
(nearest-gene/FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS/ABC), added ad hoc by the researcher via
boundary overlap only, zero credible-set variants, zero COPD/lung/TWAS/MR
evidence, and zero SCN9A-specific immune literature — the two immune
citations offered (equine pan-Nav-blocker synovitis study; SCN7A/Nax in
psoriasis) are both explicitly conceded by the researcher to be about
different genes/mechanisms and are cited only as 'class context,' not
evidence. This is appropriately disclosed by the researcher rather than
papered over, which is good practice, but it means SCN9A currently has no
gene-specific evidentiary basis at all for inclusion beyond touching the
locus boundary. Its known biology (Nav1.7, peripheral nociception/cough-
reflex sensory afferents) is a biologically plausible alternative hypothesis
for COPD symptom biology (dyspnea/cough sensation) that was never actually
raised or tested by the researcher — worth flagging as an unexplored
hypothesis, not evidence."
