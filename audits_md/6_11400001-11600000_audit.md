# Audit / excluded-evidence appendix — Locus 6:11,400,001-11,600,000 (GRCh38, TMEM170B)

This file records everything excluded from the final evidence table
(`evidence_tables/6_11400001-11600000.md`), plus the full CONTESTED reasoning for the gene, so a
reviewer can trace why each item was left out or flagged. Reasoning is preserved verbatim from the
citation-verifier and red-team-skeptic subagent outputs where indicated.

## Excluded citations (RETRACTED / CLAIM_MISMATCH / NOT_FOUND)

All six citations below were offered by the researcher as hedged, explicitly-unconfirmed
"candidate background papers" for pleiotropic trait colocalizations flagged by the Open Targets
structured lookup (platelet traits and cognitive/behavioral traits) at this locus — none were
claimed by the researcher to specifically discuss rs693829 or the TMEM170B region; the researcher
asked citation-verifier to check whether they could be locus-confirmed. None could be.

1. **Vuckovic D, et al. "The Polygenic and Monogenic Basis of Blood Traits and Diseases." Cell.
   2020. PMID 32888494.**
   Verdict: **CLAIM_MISMATCH**. Citation-verifier: "paper exists, not retracted, published Cell
   2020. However, abstract reports 5,106 genetic variants associated with 29 blood phenotypes but
   does NOT specify chr6:11.4-11.6Mb, rs693829, or any locus-specific information. Researcher
   acknowledged locus-specific match was NOT confirmed. Cannot verify this paper reports the
   platelet-trait association at the specified TMEM170B locus from available abstract text."

2. **Astle WJ, et al. "The Allelic Landscape of Human Blood Cell Trait Variation and Links to
   Common Complex Disease." Cell. 2016. PMID 27863252.**
   Verdict: **CLAIM_MISMATCH**. Citation-verifier: "paper exists, not retracted, published Cell
   2016. Abstract describes identification of hundreds of genetic variants and 36 blood cell
   properties but does NOT name specific loci, chromosomal coordinates, or rs693829. Researcher did
   not confirm locus-specific match."

3. **Lee JJ, Wedow R, Okbay A, et al. "Gene discovery and polygenic prediction from a genome-wide
   association study of educational attainment in 1.1 million individuals." Nat Genet.
   2018;50(8):1112-1121. PMID 30038396.**
   Verdict: **RETRACTED** per citation-verifier ("PMID 30038396 was RETRACTED on 2018-08-01
   according to PubMed MEDLINE record. This paper cannot be used as a valid source regardless of
   its original findings.").
   **IMPORTANT CAVEAT FOR THE STUDY TEAM:** red-team-skeptic independently flagged this verdict as
   surprising and likely incorrect: "this is one of the largest and most widely-cited/actively-used
   social-science-genomics GWAS papers, still standardly cited and used in the field as far as I'm
   aware, with no retraction on record... This needs independent human verification of the PubMed
   retraction flag before the pipeline trusts it elsewhere." This citation was only ever a hedged,
   non-load-bearing background reference here (not claimed to be locus-specific evidence for
   TMEM170B), so the RETRACTED verdict does not change TMEM170B's status in this locus's evidence
   table either way — but the study team should independently confirm the true retraction status of
   PMID 30038396 before this verdict is reused or trusted in any other locus review in this
   pipeline.

4. **Savage JE, Jansen PR, Stringer S, et al. "Genome-wide association meta-analysis in 269,867
   individuals identifies new genetic and functional links to intelligence." Nat Genet.
   2018;50(7):912-919. PMID 29942086.**
   Verdict: **CLAIM_MISMATCH**. Citation-verifier: "paper exists, not retracted, published Nat
   Genet 2018. Abstract mentions Mendelian randomization showing 'protective effects of
   intelligence for Alzheimer's disease and ADHD' (matching the ADHD-protective claim), but does
   NOT specify chr6:11.4-11.6Mb or rs693829. Researcher did not confirm locus-specific

5. **Coleman JRI, Bryois J, Gaspar HA, et al. "Biological annotation of genetic loci associated

   Verdict: **CLAIM_MISMATCH**. Citation-verifier: "paper exists, not retracted, published Mol
   Psychiatry 2019. Abstract focuses on cell-type enrichment (pyramidal neurons, GABAergic neurons)

6. **Liu M, Jiang Y, Wedow R, et al. "Association studies of up to 1.2 million individuals yield

## Gene marked CONTESTED

### TMEM170B — CONTESTED (per red-team-skeptic)

Full verbatim critique is preserved in `evidence_tables/_status/6_11400001-11600000/critique.md`.
Key reasoning, preserved here for traceability:

- **Search-effort disclosure:** red-team-skeptic's own WebSearch budget was exhausted before any
  query returned results; it performed **zero independent searches** for this gene and explicitly
  flagged this as a gap requiring follow-up (rs693829 in GWAS Catalog; TMEM170B +
  platelet/megakaryocyte; TMEM170B + Wnt + lung epithelium).
- **"Only protein-coding gene in the region" is a weak standalone argument**: it ignores the
  lncRNA ENSG00000233656, which physically contains the entire credible set, as an alternative
  functional unit, and ignores possible long-range regulatory action on genes outside the queried
  window. ABC's silence at this locus (no enhancer-promoter call at all) is itself mildly
  discordant with a straightforward TMEM170B cis-regulatory story.
- **Colocalization pattern cuts against a TMEM170B-lung-transcription mechanism**: strong
  colocalization (H4 ~0.95-0.98) with platelet-trait and cognitive/behavioral-trait GWAS signals,
  but no eQTL colocalization for TMEM170B itself with the COPD signal in any tissue, despite eQTL
  data existing for the gene. The L2G score (0.808) is a gene-agnostic ML prediction, not evidence
  the variant regulates TMEM170B expression specifically.
- **Smoking-mediation question is open, not resolved**: the same signal colocalizes with
  educational attainment and cognitive-performance traits, which are genetically correlated with
  smoking behavior generally — "a neurobehavioral pathway masquerading as a lung-disease locus" is
  a live hypothesis per red-team-skeptic, not ruled out by the (incomplete) GSCAN cross-check.
- **Wnt/β-catenin cancer biology as a "bridge" to lung is speculative, not supportive**: none of
  the six verified cancer-biology papers make any claim about lung or airway tissue; treating this
  as biological plausibility for a COPD gene would be overclaiming.
- **Overclaiming check passed**: the researcher's own restrained conclusion ("rests essentially
  entirely on the unverified Open Targets L2G score and its status as the only protein-coding gene
  ... not on any independent published functional, expression, or disease-genetic literature") was
  endorsed by red-team-skeptic as accurate and should be preserved, not softened.
- **Final critique verdict: CONTESTED** — "the structured evidence itself is discordant with the
  'TMEM170B causal via lung transcription' hypothesis... Recommend synthesis-lead treat TMEM170B as
  low-confidence/mechanistically unresolved rather than a settled call."

This CONTESTED verdict, and the LOW confidence assigned in the Prioritized Gene section of the
main evidence table, are consistent with each other and should not be read as contradictory: LOW
confidence in TMEM170B as the causal gene does not mean a different gene is better supported — no
competing protein-coding gene exists in the region — it means the evidence for TMEM170B itself is
thin and partly discordant, and the study team should treat this locus as unresolved pending the
follow-up searches recommended above.
