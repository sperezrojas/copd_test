# Audit trail — locus 16:51,950,001-52,250,000 (nearest gene TOX3)

This file contains everything excluded from the main evidence table
(`evidence_tables/16_51950001-52250000.md`), preserved for traceability: RETRACTED /
NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH citations, and every gene the red-team-skeptic marked CONTESTED,
with reasoning preserved verbatim from the checkpoint files.

## Excluded citations

### PMID 30643258 — CLAIM_MISMATCH
- Karlsson Linnér R, Biroli P, Kong E, et al. "Genome-wide association analyses of risk
  tolerance and risky behaviors in over 1 million individuals identify hundreds of loci and
  shared genetic influences." Nat Genet. 2019. DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0309-3.
- **Claim as originally proposed by researcher:** SNP rs17201148 (this locus's lead
  credible-set variant, 16:52,095,190 GRCh38; reported under gene symbol C16orf97) reached
  genome-wide significance (P=9.0x10^-10) for "risk-taking behaviour" as part of this
  >1-million-individual GWAS, per GWAS Catalog structured-database record GCST007324. This was
  explicitly flagged by the researcher as "the single most load-bearing claim in the entire
  report" underpinning the hypothesis that this locus's COPD association reflects
  behavioral/smoking-related genetics rather than pulmonary-structural or immune mechanisms.
- **Citation-verifier verdict (first pass): CLAIM_MISMATCH.** "The article exists and is not
  retracted. However, the specific SNP rs17201148 at chr16:52,095,190 with P=9.0x10^-10 for
  risk-taking behaviour is NOT mentioned in the main text or abstract. The paper references
  supplementary tables containing 'lead SNPs' and 'genomic loci' but the specific SNP cannot be
  verified from the accessible article content. While the paper identifies 'hundreds of loci'
  for risk-taking behavior, the claimed association for rs17201148 cannot be confirmed from
  the available text."
- **Red-team-skeptic follow-up (independent structured-database re-check, not a verifier
  action):** queried the GWAS Catalog REST API directly for rs17201148 and its associations
  endpoint. GWAS Catalog's own curated record confirms SNP rs17201148-A (risk allele freq
  0.4212), trait "risk-taking behaviour" (EFO_0008579), P=9.0E-10, beta=0.012 (95% CI
  0.0082-0.0159), study accession GCST007324/PMID 30643258, mapped gene C16orf97 -- an exact
  match to the researcher's original claim on every field. A full-text Europe PMC search for
  the literal string "rs17201148" returned zero hits in any indexed narrative literature,
  confirming this SNP-level result exists only in the paper's supplementary table (unsurprising
  for a study reporting ~99 genome-wide-significant loci), not fabricated or misattributed.
- **Why this remains excluded from the evidence table despite the corroborating re-check:**
  per pipeline rules, only citations with a citation-verifier verdict of VERIFIED go in the
  main table; the verifier's CLAIM_MISMATCH verdict was not itself upgraded (the follow-up
  check was performed by red-team-skeptic/orchestrator via a structured database, not a
  re-verification of the primary-text claim by citation-verifier). Readers should weigh this as
  "very likely a real, correctly-reported statistical association, but not confirmable by
  reading the cited paper's own accessible text" -- a meaningfully different evidentiary status
  than a fully verified literature claim, and specifically NOT strong enough, on its own or
  combined with the rest of the evidence base, to support the researcher's original framing
  that this locus's COPD signal "looks more consistent with a behavioral/smoking-related
  mediation pathway... than with a documented pulmonary-structural or immune mechanism." Also
  note: "risk-taking behaviour" is a genetically-correlated but distinct trait from
  smoking-initiation/nicotine-dependence traits specifically named in this pipeline's prior #1
  -- treating it as equivalent evidence for smoking-mediated COPD risk requires an additional
  inferential step that the red-team-skeptic flagged should be stated explicitly, not elided.

## Genes marked CONTESTED

### C16orf97 (=LINC02911) — CONTESTED
Reasoning preserved verbatim from red-team-skeptic (first pass):

> The single PubMed hit under this symbol (Dong et al. 2016, olfaction in African-American
> older adults, PMID 27878761) is about a different phenotype in a different population and
> does not itself validate the C16orf97=LINC02911 identity claim used to route MAGMA/PoPS
> prioritization here. More importantly, the locus background note that LINC02911's
> coordinates (52,005,486-52,078,516) almost entirely overlap the span the pipeline separately
> assigned to "LINC00919" is a serious structural problem that the researcher's literature
> search did not surface or resolve — if these are the same regulatory region under two
> catalog names (NCBI/RefSeq vs Ensembl/GENCODE), then treating "C16orf97/LINC02911" and
> "LINC00919" as two distinct pieces of corroborating credible-set evidence double-counts a
> single signal, and the "MAGMA/PoPS-prioritized gene" claim may just be re-describing the
> LINC00919-annotated PIP mass under a different name. This needs direct coordinate/annotation
> reconciliation (not literature search) before any weight is placed on "C16orf97 is
> prioritized by MAGMA/PoPS" as if it were independent evidence layered on top of the
> LINC00919 credible-set mass. I'm marking this CONTESTED not because I found a paper arguing
> against it, but because the pipeline's own background section already surfaces a
> naming-collision problem the researcher's writeup doesn't address or caveat.

**Orchestrator's direct follow-up (Ensembl REST coordinate lookup, structured database, not
literature):** confirmed the coordinate overlap is real and essentially total. Ensembl's
"LINC00919" gene body (ENSG00000260268) spans chr16:52,005,425-52,085,177 (GRCh38); NCBI
RefSeq's LINC02911/C16orf97 spans chr16:52,005,486-52,078,516 -- the entire LINC02911/C16orf97
span (~73 kb) sits inside the Ensembl LINC00919 gene body. This upgrades the naming-collision
concern from "plausible, unresolved" to "confirmed by direct coordinate lookup": C16orf97/
LINC02911 and the credible-set gene "LINC00919" are very likely the same underlying genomic
feature described under two different gene-model catalogs, not two independently converging
lines of evidence.

**Red-team-skeptic follow-up (second pass, after independent literature re-search):**

> The naming-collision issue from the prior pass is unresolved (no literature disambiguating
> LINC02911 vs. LINC00919 vs. Ensembl coordinates was found). This pass adds a concrete
> illustration of the LD/nearest-gene-bias concern: GWAS Catalog's own automated "mapped gene"
> annotation for rs17201148 is C16orf97, even though the SNP is physically intronic to
> LINC02180 — i.e., the very structured database the researcher relied on for gene-mapping
> evidence is itself inconsistent about which gene "owns" this variant. Literature search adds
> only one tangential nicotine-dependence pleiotropy hit (PMID 33144568, not independently
> verified, off-topic keyword overlap only), no direct support.

**Status: remains CONTESTED.** Not excluded from the main table (its one literature citation,
PMID 27878761, was independently VERIFIED by citation-verifier as a real, accurately-described
finding), but flagged prominently in the evidence table's "skeptic verdict" column and in the
Prioritized Gene section given the unresolved double-counting risk this creates for the
overall MAGMA/PoPS-vs-credible-set convergence narrative at this locus.

## Additional context not fitting the RETRACTED/NOT_FOUND/CLAIM_MISMATCH/CONTESTED categories,
included for completeness of the audit trail

- **Red-team-skeptic's first pass was compromised by an exhausted session-wide WebSearch
  budget** and returned zero independent contradiction-search coverage, producing only an
  internal-consistency critique. This was disclosed explicitly by the subagent and is recorded
  in full in `evidence_tables/_status/16_51950001-52250000/critique.md`. The orchestrator
  dispatched a second red-team-skeptic pass instructed to use WebFetch against PubMed
  E-utilities / Europe PMC / GWAS Catalog REST endpoints directly (bypassing the exhausted
  WebSearch tool), which did complete a full independent contradiction search across all four
  candidate genes plus locus-level and true-nearest-gene (CASC22) checks, and additionally
  surfaced the one new citation (PMID 32099002) that was subsequently verified and added to
  the main evidence table. Both critique passes are preserved verbatim in critique.md.
- **TOX3 vs. TOX paralog distinction:** the researcher explicitly and correctly avoided
  conflating TOX3 with its paralog TOX, which has well-documented CD8+ T-cell exhaustion
  biology in the immunology literature. No evidence was found that TOX3 shares this specific
  function. Red-team-skeptic confirmed this was handled correctly and is not itself a red flag.
- **Fine-mapping-noise alternative hypothesis:** both red-team-skeptic passes explicitly
  flagged that, given the low maximum PIP (0.139) across a diffuse ~50-variant credible set,
  all-noncoding variant consequences, and the near-total absence of independent
  locus-specific/COPD-specific literature for any of the four candidate genes, the possibility
  that this signal represents fine-mapping noise or an unresolved LD signal without a single
  identifiable causal gene deserves consideration alongside any gene-specific biological
  narrative. This alternative was not conclusively ruled in or out by this review and is
  carried forward into the Prioritized Gene section of the main evidence table as an open
  consideration for the study team.
