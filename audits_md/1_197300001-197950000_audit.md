### Full red-team-skeptic critique (verbatim)

## C1orf53

The dossier's own conclusion already matches the weak evidence — there is no overclaiming to catch. Candidacy rests solely on the `upstream_gene_variant` annotation of the lead SNP, i.e., nearest-gene proximity. The one retrieved paper (Pang 2023) is irrelevant (endometrial cancer statistical panel, no mechanistic content). Open Targets reinforces the weak case: lowest respiratory-relevant L2G of any candidate (smoking-initiation 0.051), a disease profile dominated by AMD/COVID/hypothyroidism with nothing respiratory, and high LOF tolerance (oe=2.07) — the last point actively cuts against a dosage-sensitive causal role in a Mendelian-adjacent disease mechanism. No positive causal case exists to weaken or contest.

**Verdict: WEAKENED.** Not because a real claim overreaches, but because "nearest gene" proximity alone is being used as a rationale with essentially zero corroborating biology — this is the LD/nearest-gene-bias failure mode named in the task instructions in its purest form. Should not be prioritized above genes with genuine mechanistic or genetic support.

## DENND1B

This is where the most scrutiny is warranted, per the task's explicit question about overclaiming.

**Is "top pick"/plausible-causal-COPD-gene an overreach?** Yes, materially. The evidentiary chain is: (1) genome-wide-significant *asthma* GWAS hit (Sleiman 2010), (2) a real Th2-specific TCR-signaling mechanism in a knockout mouse (Yang et al. 2016 — the strongest single paper in the dossier), (3) broad immune/autoimmune pleiotropy (narcolepsy, psoriasis, IBD), and (4) MAGMA+ABC prioritization with the credible set inside the gene body. None of these four legs touches COPD, emphysema, or spirometry directly. The researcher's own assessment is honest about this gap ("NO COPD-specific or spirometric... literature for DENND1B at all"), but the framing as "the strongest genetic/mechanistic candidate" for *this COPD locus* risks laundering strong asthma/atopy evidence into COPD causality by narrative adjacency rather than data.

**The ACOS (asthma-COPD overlap syndrome) argument specifically:** this is invoked implicitly but never actually tested against data. ACOS is a real, biologically plausible phenomenon (shared Th2/eosinophilic inflammation in a subset of COPD patients), but invoking it here is a plausible-sounding hand-wave, not an evidenced claim — the dossier presents no COPD-cohort data showing DENND1B variants associate with the eosinophilic/Th2-high COPD subtype, no colocalization between the asthma signal and any COPD/FEV1 signal at this locus, and Open Targets confirms zero COPD GWAS/coloc evidence exists here at all. Without that, "DENND1B could act through ACOS biology" is an untested hypothesis dressed as inferential support.

**A point the dossier under-weights:** Open Targets' own numbers show DENND1B's *strongest* documented genome-wide genetic signal across all human traits is **smoking initiation (L2G=0.840)** — over three times its asthma L2G (0.244). The researcher's narrative foregrounds the Th2/asthma mechanism and treats the smoking-initiation link almost as an incidental caveat ("the L2G=0.840 claim comes only from Open Targets, not from having read DENND1B named in either [smoking GWAS] paper"). That caveat about *citation* provenance is fair, but it shouldn't be used to wave away the *magnitude* of the structured signal itself — by the numbers actually in hand, smoking-behavior genetics is the dominant documented trait association for this gene, arguably outcompeting the immune story as the more parsimonious mediator of any COPD signal at this locus, exactly the smoking-mediation scenario Prior #1 asks reviewers to weigh actively. The dossier doesn't seriously entertain "this is a smoking-initiation locus that shows up in COPD GWAS because COPD GWAS isn't smoking-adjusted" as a competing (and by the numbers, better-supported) hypothesis to "this is a Th2/asthma-mediated COPD gene."

**Additional softening evidence already in the dossier that argues against overweighting the human genetic case:** Ferreira 2011 failed to replicate the original Sleiman variant in an independent asthma GWAS; Duffy 2019's founder-population signal was explicitly *not* seen in large modern asthma meta-analyses; Vyhlidal 2013 found no case-control expression difference in DENND1B itself. The strongest support (Yang et al. 2016) is mouse-knockout + human TH2-cell mechanism for *asthma*, not COPD, and Kinoshita et al. 2020 attributes the RabGEF-family epithelial-polarity function specifically to paralogs DENND1A/folliculin, not DENND1B — a mild internal contradiction about how general vs. specific the DENND1/Rab35 mechanism really is.

**On Nédélec 2016:** checked specifically — it does **not** appear to be smuggled back into the assessment paragraph. It's correctly quarantined as CLAIM_MISMATCH / background-only in both its own bullet and the final assessment, which cites only the genuinely DENND1B-relevant papers. This is a pass for the researcher/verifier on that specific overclaiming check.

**Verdict: WEAKENED.** The immune/asthma biology is real and well-supported, and MAGMA+ABC prioritization plus credible-set overlap is legitimate statistical support for DENND1B as *a* gene at this locus. But calling it the leading *COPD* candidate overreaches: zero COPD/spirometric evidence exists, the ACOS bridge is asserted rather than shown, and the dossier under-addresses that DENND1B's single strongest genome-wide genetic association is smoking-initiation behavior, a live competing (and prior-consistent) explanation for why it shows up in an unadjusted COPD GWAS. Recommend synthesis-lead present DENND1B as "best-supported gene at the locus generically, but via smoking-behavior and/or asthma/Th2 pathways whose relevance to intrinsic COPD/emphysema pathophysiology is unestablished," not as a confirmed COPD mechanism gene.

## CRB1

The researcher's "likely false positive" call is broadly fair but one nuance in the Open Targets data complicates and then, on reflection, reinforces it. CRB1's genome-wide asthma L2G (0.368) is actually *higher* than DENND1B's (0.244), which superficially could be read as CRB1 having an independent pulmonary-immune signal worth taking seriously. But given that CRB1's 3' end sits inside the same credible set/LD block as DENND1B (they are adjacent, with the 172-variant credible set spanning both), the far more parsimonious explanation is that CRB1's asthma L2G score is parasitic on DENND1B's genuine, mechanistically-supported asthma signal via LD-tagging — not evidence of independent CRB1 biology. This is exactly the LD/nearest-gene-bias scenario the task asks reviewers to watch for, and here it cuts against CRB1, not for it. Combined with: (a) the only "lung" citation (Watanabe 2004) actually describing a skin/keratinocyte-differentiation role for a secreted splice isoform incidentally detected in lung tissue, not a lung-function role; (b) hundreds of well-characterized retinal Mendelian-disease papers showing zero lung/immune crossover; (c) Open Targets showing no immune/lung QTLs at all (only muscle/nerve/brain) and only moderate LOF constraint — the case against CRB1 as causal here is solid.

**Verdict: WEAKENED**, converging with the researcher's own "false positive" framing but for a sharper reason: the one piece of Open Targets data that superficially looked like independent support for CRB1 (asthma L2G) is better explained as LD contamination from DENND1B's real signal, strengthening rather than undermining the false-positive call. Not CONTESTED, since no paper actively argues CRB1 causes/protects against COPD — there's simply no case to contest, only a case to fail to build.

## LHX9

This is the gene I'd flag most strongly for a methodological/inclusion-criteria concern, directly matching the task's warning about "gene happens to be nearby AND happens to have a COPD paper" being over-weighted relative to genes with actual fine-mapping support.

LHX9 was not flagged by MAGMA, ABC, PoPS, or nearest-gene — i.e., **all four independent statistical-genetics prioritization methods applied to this locus declined to nominate it.** It entered the dossier only because the researcher manually enumerated protein-coding genes physically in the interval and then found an unrelated PubMed hit. There is no eQTL, colocalization, or fine-mapping evidence connecting any of the 172 credible-set variants (or rs10922326 specifically) to LHX9 expression or function — this is explicitly acknowledged in the dossier itself. The supporting paper (Okutomo 2021) is a genuinely interesting, COPD-specific, small (n=11 vs 10) unreplicated expression/mechanism study, and the second citation (Kusama 2025) is an explicit, self-flagged speculative cross-tissue extrapolation from endometrial stromal cells. Neither paper says anything about the genetic variants at this locus regulating LHX9.

This is a real inversion of the pipeline's evidentiary hierarchy: a gene with zero statistical-genetic linkage to the locus is being kept in play primarily because a keyword search surfaced a COPD-labeled paper, while the actual fine-mapped, multiply-prioritized gene (DENND1B) gets scrutinized for lacking COPD-specific literature. The absence of prioritization by all four methods is itself a form of (indirect) contradicting evidence for LHX9's genetic relevance to *this* signal, even though it doesn't contradict the AT2-biology finding on its own terms.

**Verdict: WEAKENED**, bordering on the inclusion itself being questionable rather than the specific claim. The Okutomo 2021 finding should be retained as an interesting hypothesis-generating aside (COPD-relevant AT2/IFN-γ biology in this genomic neighborhood), but it should not be presented to synthesis-lead as comparable-weight evidence to genes with actual credible-set/prioritization support, and should be explicitly labeled "no genetic link to this locus's variants established" whenever surfaced.

## Cross-cutting observations for synthesis-lead

- **Thoroughness flag:** because my own search budget was already exhausted, none of the four genes received even the mandated shallow contradiction-search pass from me. If this critique is being used to green-light synthesis, that gap should be closed before finalizing — particularly (1) a targeted search for "DENND1B COPD/FEV1/emphysema" and "DENND1B smoking initiation mechanism" specifically, since that trade-off (asthma/Th2 vs. smoking-behavior mediation) is the crux of the whole locus call, and (2) a search for whether any large COPD/lung-function GWAS (post-2019, e.g., more recent multi-ancestry COPD or spirometry meta-analyses) has independently hit this exact interval, since Sakornsakolpat 2019 is the only COPD GWAS the researcher checked and it's now several generations behind current COPD GWAS sample sizes.
- **Net locus-level takeaway:** none of the four candidates has both (a) COPD/spirometric genetic evidence and (b) a mechanistically specific, non-LD-confounded case. The most defensible framing is "credible set most plausibly acts through DENND1B, via either smoking-behavior neurobiology or Th2/atopic-immune biology (ACOS-adjacent, unproven), with CRB1 and C1orf53 best treated as proximity-driven false positives and LHX9 as an intriguing but genetically unlinked hypothesis" — a materially more hedged statement than "DENND1B is the top COPD candidate at this locus."
