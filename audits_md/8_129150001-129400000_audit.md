# Audit trail (excluded citations + contested findings) — Locus 8:129,150,001-129,400,000 (GSDMC)

This is the audit/excluded appendix for `evidence_tables/8_129150001-129400000.md`. It is
kept in a separate top-level directory so the evidence table can be handed to reviewers
without also handing out this trail. Contents: every citation NOT carrying a clean

plus every CONTESTED gene verdict from red-team-skeptic, with reasoning preserved verbatim.

### CLAIM_MISMATCH

1. **Feng et al. 2022, Journal of Physiology and Biochemistry. "Pyroptosis in
   inflammation-related respiratory disease." PMID 35819638, DOI
   10.1007/s13105-022-00909-1.**
   Researcher's claim: review describes a caspase-8/GSDMC pyroptosis pathway among
   GSDM-family pathways, and states pyroptosis (implicating GSDM family members including
   GSDMC) may contribute to pathogenesis of COPD, asthma, acute lung injury, silicosis, and
   pulmonary hypertension.
   Citation-verifier finding: Paper exists and is correctly cited bibliographically, but
   full-text analysis shows the review actually attributes COPD pyroptosis specifically to
   the **NLRP3 inflammasome-induced pathway** and **CS-induced ER stress via ROS** — the
   caspase-8/GSDMC pathway is described in the review in the context of *other* conditions,
   but is **not** tied to COPD pathogenesis in this paper. The claim as stated overstates
   what the paper supports for GSDMC specifically in COPD.
   Disposition: excluded from evidence table; noted in red-team critique as directly
   contesting evidence against a GSDMC-COPD pyroptosis mechanism (see CONTESTED verdict
   below).

2. **Cui et al. 2024, Heliyon. PMID 39130462, DOI 10.1016/j.heliyon.2024.e34360.**
   Researcher's claim: bioinformatic (TCGA/GEO) study of GSDM-family prognostic value in
   gastric cancer; GSDMC reported upregulated in gastric cancer, correlated with
   immune-cell infiltration and pyroptosis.
   Citation-verifier finding: PMC full-text confirms this paper's actual title is "ELANE is
   a promising prognostic biomarker that mediates pyroptosis in gastric cancer" — it is
   primarily an ELANE (neutrophil elastase) study; GSDMC appears only incidentally as an
   ELANE-interacting protein, not as the study's subject. The claim mischaracterizes the
   paper as a GSDM-family study.
   Disposition: excluded from evidence table.

## CONTESTED gene verdicts (red-team-skeptic, reasoning preserved verbatim)

### GSDMC — CONTESTED

"The positive case rests on: (1) physical proximity/FLAMES prioritization, (2) one
correlative WGCNA bioinformatic paper (Liu/Huang/Xu 2023, PMID 37194062) placing GSDMC in
an immune-correlated module in COPD airway epithelium, and (3) a cluster of
pyroptosis-mechanism citations that mostly failed verification.

That last point is the most damning pattern here. Four separate citations were marshaled
to build a "GSDMC drives pyroptosis in COPD" narrative, and three of four did not hold up:
Liu et al. 2023 Cell Biol Toxicol (NOT_FOUND — CSE/LPS upregulation claim
unconfirmable), Feng et al. 2022 (CLAIM_MISMATCH — the review actually ties COPD
pyroptosis to NLRP3/caspase-1/GSDMD, explicitly not to caspase-8/GSDMC), and the
researcher's own flagged, uncitable "recalled" GSDMC/caspase-8/PD-L1 mechanism. This is a
classic sign of a hypothesis being reverse-fitted to citations rather than derived from
them — three failed attempts to source the same mechanistic claim is not noise, it's a
pattern, and it should weigh against the gene, not just against the individual citations.

Independently, Open Targets — a structured database, not literature-mining susceptible to
the same narrative bias — shows GSDMC's actual genetic-association-backed trait
associations are osteoarthritis, back pain, vertebral disorders, and CNS/prostate cancer,
with zero genetic-association-datatype contribution to its COPD score (the 0.014 score is
100% literature-datatype, likely driven by the very speculative papers above — a
circularity risk worth naming explicitly). No lung eQTL/sQTL was found. Combined with the
350 kb physical gap from the credible set, this is credible contradicting evidence, not
merely an overreach: the actual genetic signal attached to GSDMC points to non-respiratory
biology, while the literature case for a COPD mechanism collapses on inspection.

Verdict: CONTESTED. Contesting evidence = (a) Open Targets genetic-association trait
profile (musculoskeletal/oncologic, not respiratory) — already-verified structured data, no
new UNVERIFIED flag needed; (b) the Feng et al. 2022 CLAIM_MISMATCH directly refuting the
claimed caspase-8/GSDMC-COPD mechanism; (c) 350 kb physical exclusion from the credible
set."

### CCDC26 / LINC00977 — CONTESTED

"The positive case rests almost entirely on positional containment (credible set and lead
variant sit inside CCDC26 introns) plus two independent gene-prioritization pipelines
(FLAMES-adjacent GSDMC call aside, ABC prioritizes LINC00977=CCDC26) agreeing on the same
lncRNA — but both of those pipelines are essentially re-deriving the same positional fact,
not independent biological evidence.

Every piece of actual functional/disease literature on this gene is oncological (glioma via
rs55705857, leukemia via IDH-mutant AML/CML pleiotropy) and tied to a different variant
whose LD relationship to rs10095076 was never confirmed this session. None of it touches
lung biology. Open Targets shows zero COPD genetic-association score for CCDC26/LINC00977
and no lung eQTL/sQTL.

Most importantly, the single most mechanistically informative paper in the whole dossier —
Oktay et al. 2016 (VERIFIED, PMID 27282637) — is itself a contradiction of the "CCDC26 is
the effector gene" hypothesis for this region: it shows the region's flagship functional
variant acts as a long-range enhancer on the MYC promoter ~1.4 Mb away, that CCDC26 RNA is
rarely expressed, and that expression does not vary by genotype. That is a verified,
mechanistic demonstration that in this same broader LD block, DNA elements sitting inside
"CCDC26" do not act through CCDC26 transcript function. It doesn't prove the same is true
for rs10095076 specifically, but it is a directly on-point precedent that should shift
weight away from CCDC26-as-effector toward "the credible set is sitting inside a long-range
regulatory scaffold whose real target is distal (MYC/PVT1), and CCDC26 is a hitchhiking
transcript, not the causal gene." The Sousa et al. pneumothorax paper (different variant,
different interval, underpowered, unreplicated) does not rescue this — it's correctly
flagged by the researcher as weak and off-locus.

Verdict: CONTESTED. Contesting evidence = Oktay et al. 2016 (already verified,
mechanistically demonstrates distal-MYC-enhancer, non-CCDC26-transcript mechanism in this
exact region) + Open Targets zero-COPD-association + absence of any lung eQTL/expression
evidence for CCDC26."

## Additional red-team-skeptic notes (overclaiming / missed alternatives / process gaps)

- **MYC/PVT1 as unexplored alternative candidate**: MYC/PVT1 lie ~1.0-1.5 Mb centromeric of
  this locus and the region's best-characterized mechanism (Oktay et al. 2016) is exactly a
  distal-MYC-enhancer mechanism — but the researcher never ran a dedicated MYC/PVT1
  lung-biology or COPD literature search. Red-team-skeptic recommends MYC and PVT1 receive
  their own gene-level evidence pass before final determination, since "overlap" is not the
  relevant criterion once a region is established to act via long-range enhancers.
- **Circularity risk**: GSDMC's Open Targets COPD association score (0.014) is 100%
  literature-datatype and may be partly self-referential, reflecting the same
  bioinformatic/WGCNA-type papers already under scrutiny in this review.
- **No GWAS Catalog record at all for rs10095076**: this locus has never been reported as a
  hit for any trait outside the pipeline's own COPD meta-analysis. This should temper
  confidence in any gene call here, independent of which gene is chosen.
- **Unresolved process gaps flagged by both researcher and red-team-skeptic**: (1) no
  direct supplementary-table check was performed of Shrine et al. 2019 (PMID 30804560) or
  Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019 (PMID 30804561) for this exact genomic position — only
  abstract-level review; (2) no explicit smoking-initiation/heaviness/cessation GWAS
  (e.g., GSCAN) lookup was performed at this position. Red-team-skeptic attempted both of
  these independently but could not complete them because the session's web-search budget
  was already exhausted (200/200 used) before its turn began. Both remain **open,
  unconfirmed items**, not settled negatives, and should be prioritized if this locus is
  revisited.
