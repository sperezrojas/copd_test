This file preserves everything excluded from the main evidence table

CLAIM_MISMATCH / PARTIAL_VERIFIED, plus full reasoning behind gene-level CONTESTED/WEAKENED

## Excluded citations

**Sakornsakolpat P et al., Nat Genet 2019 (82-locus COPD GWAS meta-analysis). PMID 30804561,
DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0342-2.**
Verdict: **CLAIM_MISMATCH**. The researcher claimed ADK/10q24 is not among this paper's
genome-wide-significant loci. Citation-verifier found the abstract confirms "82 loci with
P < 5×10⁻⁸" but does not enumerate them; the negative claim could not be verified from
accessible text without the full supplementary locus table. Excluded because this pipeline
has no verified positive or negative statement from this paper about ADK.

**Shrine N et al., Nat Genet 2019 (lung-function GWAS / author correction). PMID 31110354,
DOI 10.1038/s41588-019-0438-3.** Verdict: **CLAIM_MISMATCH**, same reasoning as above.

**Peter-Okaka U et al., J Inherit Metab Dis 2025. PMID 40393929, DOI 10.1002/jimd.70033.**
Verdict: **CLAIM_MISMATCH**. Title confirmed as "Adenosine Kinase: An Epigenetic Modulator and
Drug Target" — the paper covers ADK's role in epigenetics and drug discovery for epilepsy,
pain, inflammation, and cancer; it does not address respiratory/COPD phenotypes. The
researcher had flagged this citation as found only by title-match without asserting a firm
claim; excluded as not relevant/supportive of any claim in the evidence table.

**Fonseca MJ, Sant'Anna CC, et al. 2020, Genes. "Genomic Regions 10q22.2, 17q21.31, and 2p23.1
Can Contribute to a Lower Lung Function in African Descent Populations." PMID 32899814,
PMC7565985, DOI 10.3390/genes11091047.** Verdict: **PARTIAL_VERIFIED** — excluded from the main
table because the verdict is not a clean VERIFIED. This citation was surfaced by the
red-team-skeptic (who mis-attributed first authorship to "Bonfim"; the corrected first author
per PubMed is Fonseca). The core region-level claim IS verified: admixture mapping in a
pediatric Brazilian asthma cohort (SCAALA, n=958) found African local ancestry at 10q22.2
(the cytoband containing ADK) associated with lower FEV1/FVC (p<1.9×10⁻⁴), replicated with
five fine-mapped SNPs (rs10999948, rs373831475, rs8068257, rs6744555, rs1520322) — none of
which is rs7919010 or confirmed to lie in ADK. Critically, **the abstract does not mention ADK
as a candidate gene in the 10q22.2 region**, and citation-verifier could not confirm this
without full-text access. Additional caveats (from the red-team-skeptic, preserved here):
admixture-mapping regions are very low resolution (can span tens of Mb and many genes), the
cohort is pediatric/asthma, not adult COPD, and the association is ancestry-level, not a
direct variant/gene association. Net effect: this citation shows the broader 10q22.2 region
is not a complete blank for spirometric-trait evidence, but it does not rescue ADK-specific
causality and was judged too uncertain (PARTIAL_VERIFIED, not VERIFIED) to include as direct
support in the main table.

**Stewart CE et al. 2009, BMC Medical Genetics. "PLAUR polymorphisms and lung function in UK
smokers." PMID 19878584, PMC2784766, DOI 10.1186/1471-2350-10-112.** Verdict:
**CLAIM_MISMATCH**. Surfaced by the red-team-skeptic in support of reopening PLAU as a
candidate. Citation-verifier confirmed the paper supports a PLAUR-baseline-FEV1 association
("five SNPs showed a significant association (p<0.01) with baseline lung function") but
explicitly *contradicts* the COPD-risk part of the claim as originally offered: the paper
states "the case-control analyses do not support the conclusion that PLAUR is a major COPD
susceptibility gene in smokers." Additionally and importantly: **PLAUR (chromosome 19) is a
different gene from PLAU (chromosome 10)**, the actual regional neighbor at this locus (~290
kb from the credible-set interval). This citation is excluded from the main table both for the
claim mismatch and because it does not even pertain to the correct gene for this locus.

**D, Rekha et al. 2025, Cureus. "Serum Soluble Urokinase-Type Plasminogen Activator Receptor: A
Promising Biomarker for Stable Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease Patients." PMID
40151757, PMC11947502, DOI 10.7759/cureus.79594.** Verdict: **VERIFIED** as to its own claim
(serum suPAR rises with COPD severity/GOLD stage, F=428.83, p<0.001) but **excluded from the
main evidence table** because suPAR is the protein product of PLAUR (chromosome 19), not PLAU
(chromosome 10, the actual gene near this locus) — included here for completeness since the
red-team-skeptic surfaced it while investigating PLAU, but it is not evidence for the gene
actually being weighed at this locus.

## Gene-level WEAKENED / re-evaluation-flagged verdicts (full skeptic reasoning)

### ADK — WEAKENED (see main evidence table for the citation-by-citation detail; full
narrative reasoning below)

The red-team-skeptic's full critique (verbatim, also at
`evidence_tables/_status/10_74200001-74450000/critique.md`):

> **What the evidence actually shows vs. what's being inferred.** The two citations that would
> matter most for a direct-pulmonary case — Sakornsakolpat 2019 (COPD GWAS) and Shrine 2019
> (lung-function GWAS) — are both CLAIM_MISMATCH for an absence claim ("ADK not among loci")
> that was never actually confirmed against the locus tables. That means the researcher's
> statgen section currently asserts nothing verified in either direction about ADK's presence
> in the two most important COPD-adjacent GWAS. The Open Targets summary is actually the
> stronger and more informative piece of evidence here — 55 credible sets at rs7919010, zero
> COPD/lung-function, ADK top L2G only for BMI/adiposity/body-composition traits, and a COPD
> disease score of 0.0096 from text-mining alone. The best available evidence is an absence of
> genetic-association support for ADK-COPD/lung-function, not an unconfirmed absence claim from
> two abstracts.
>
> **Overclaiming risk in the adenosine-pathway narrative.** The Schneider (Ada-/-) and Collum
> (ADORA2B) papers are about different genes in the same pathway. Mechanistically the direction
> is at least plausible (ADK clears intracellular adenosine, so ADK loss-of-function would be
> expected to raise tissue adenosine similarly to ADA loss), but this is an untested hypothesis
> about direction of effect for a variant of unknown functional consequence — should not be
> weighted as gene-specific mechanistic support; flagged as borderline overreach if treated as
> more than "pathway plausibility, gene not tested."
>
> **The BMI-mediation alternative is under-weighted relative to its actual evidentiary
> strength.** Given (a) ADK's own established biology is adiposity/metabolic regulation in
> liver, adipose, and beta cells (three separate VERIFIED mechanistic papers), (b) ADK is the
> consistent top L2G gene across 55 independent OT credible sets that are overwhelmingly
> anthropometric, and (c) three independent VERIFIED MR studies establish BMI/body-fat as
> causally reducing FEV1/FVC and increasing COPD risk — this is not merely a plausible
> alternative, it is currently the better-supported explanation for any COPD signal at this
> locus. The direct-pulmonary case for ADK is close to absent; the adiposity-mediated case has
> convergent genetic and causal-inference support.
>
> **Smoking.** The researcher's smoking check is a non-finding (didn't confirm ADK/locus
> overlap with GSCAN smoking-initiation/heaviness loci one way or the other). Given ADK's
> substantial neuropsychiatric Mendelian phenotype profile, a genuine look at smoking-behavior
> GWAS loci near 10q22.2 was warranted and wasn't done thoroughly. This is a real gap, not just
> an absence-of-finding.
>
> **Verdict: WEAKENED.** The inference "ADK is the prioritized gene at this locus" overreaches:
> the two citations meant to establish (or rule out) a direct pulmonary link are unverified as
> to content, Open Targets shows zero genetic-association evidence tying ADK to COPD/lung
> function at the fine-mapped variant, the adenosine-pathway evidence is gene-nonspecific and
> directionally speculative, and a well-evidenced alternative (BMI/adiposity mediation) is
> currently better supported than direct lung biology. This locus should be presented to
> synthesis as "L2G-clear for ADK, but the trait being mediated is very plausibly BMI/adiposity
> rather than lung biology" — not as a straightforward pulmonary candidate.

### MICU1 — WEAKENED on evidentiary narrative; exclusion-from-locus conclusion HOLDS

> **Thoroughness gap identified.** The researcher's claim "no lung-specific... MICU1 literature
> identified at all" is factually incomplete. A real, on-topic paper exists: Ali M et al. 2022,
> JCI Insight, "MICU1-dependent mitochondrial calcium uptake regulates lung alveolar type 2
> cell plasticity and lung regeneration" (PMID 35050901, PMC8876408) — AT2-cell-specific MICU1
> deletion in mice impairs AT2-to-AT1 differentiation during repair after S. pneumoniae lung
> injury, via mitochondrial-Ca2+-uniporter-dependent gatekeeping. AT2-to-AT1 differentiation and
> alveolar regeneration capacity is directly mechanistically relevant to emphysema
> pathophysiology, so MICU1 does have bona fide, cell-type-specific lung biology that the
> researcher's search missed.
>
> **Why this doesn't change the bottom line for this locus.** Even with this correction, MICU1
> should remain deprioritized as the causal gene for this specific credible set: it sits ~1.5 Mb
> outside the fine-mapped interval (confirmed via Ensembl REST, not just an LD-block ambiguity),
> it was never a top L2G gene at the lead variant across any of the 55 OT credible sets, it has
> zero OT COPD disease-association evidence of any kind, and it is flagged only by MAGMA/PoPS.
> Neither method provides evidence that this specific credible-set signal acts through MICU1
> rather than through the genes actually sitting in the interval. A gene with real lung biology
> but 1.5 Mb of physical separation and no fine-mapping/eQTL/coloc support is a materially
> weaker candidate than a gene with no known lung biology but that sits under the credible set
> and dominates L2G.
>
> **Verdict: WEAKENED / split.** The exclusion-of-MICU1-from-this-locus argument on genetic-
> distance grounds HOLDS. But the supporting narrative that MICU1 has "no lung-specific
> literature at all" is WEAKENED — it's incorrect, and the corrected picture (MICU1 has genuine
> alveolar-regeneration biology) should be visible to the synthesis lead so the ~1.5 Mb distance
> argument is being weighed against real competing biology, not a biological blank.

### PLAU — flagged for re-evaluation, not independently contested or confirmed

> **Thoroughness flag on PLAU specifically.** My own search for "PLAU urokinase plasminogen
> activator emphysema COPD" returned substantial hits: PLAUR (the uPA receptor, a different gene
> on chromosome 19) SNPs are reported associated with baseline FEV1 in smokers (though the same
> paper explicitly refutes a COPD case-control association), and multiple studies report
> uPA-system components (uPA, uPAR, PAI-1) altered in COPD lung tissue/macrophages and correlated
> with emphysema/small-airway fibrosis, plus serum suPAR as a COPD severity biomarker. The
> researcher reported a "zero-hit targeted PubMed search" for PLAU, which is very likely an
> artifact of searching the bare gene symbol rather than the established synonym "urokinase
> plasminogen activator" / "uPA" — a well-known indexing pitfall for this gene. This is a
> genuine thoroughness gap, not a settled null result. I recommend the synthesis lead not treat
> PLAU as ruled out; recommend the researcher re-run a proper search (urokinase, uPA, PLAU,
> PLAUR system) and evaluate genetic distance/L2G/coloc evidence for PLAU at this locus before
> finalizing gene prioritization. Note PLAUR itself is a different gene on a different
> chromosome — the relevant chr10 gene is PLAU (urokinase itself); its precise coordinates
> relative to the 74.2-74.45 Mb credible-set interval should be confirmed, as it may sit outside
> the interval similarly to MICU1.

(Researcher's own follow-up coordinate check, from the researcher checkpoint: PLAU is at
10:73,909,177-73,917,501, i.e. ~290 kb outside/upstream of the 74,200,001-74,450,000
credible-set interval — similar distance-exclusion logic to MICU1 applies, though this was not
independently re-verified by a second researcher pass as the skeptic recommended.)
