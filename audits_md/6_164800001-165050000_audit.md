# Audit trail — locus 6:164,800,001-165,050,000 (COPD GWAS)

This appendix lists every excluded citation (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH) and every
gene marked CONTESTED by the red-team-skeptic, with reasoning preserved verbatim. This file is
intended for the study team's traceability review; it is deliberately kept separate from
`evidence_tables/6_164800001-165050000.md` so the evidence table can be shared without the audit
trail.

## Excluded citations

### Hernandez-Pacheco et al. 2021 — CLAIM_MISMATCH

- **Citation as originally proposed by researcher:** Hernandez-Pacheco N, et al. "Identification of
  PDE10A as a Potential Locus Associated with Inhaled Corticosteroid Response in Childhood Asthma."
  J Pers Med. 2021;11(8):733. PMID: 34442380; PMC8399629; DOI: 10.3390/jpm11080733.
- **Researcher's original claim:** A GWAS of change in lung function after inhaled corticosteroid
  (ICS) treatment in childhood asthma patients found a suggestive SNP (rs1166980) attributed to
  PDE10A, with some gene-level replication for asthma exacerbations despite ICS use in Europeans,
  not replicated in the CAMP validation cohort. The researcher had already flagged the gene-name
  attribution as tentative, noting the gene name was garbled/stripped from the returned PubMed
  metadata and required independent confirmation.
- **Citation-verifier finding (verbatim):** "According to PubMed and the full-text article, the
  gene associated with SNP rs1166980 is **ROBO2 (Roundabout), NOT PDE10A**. The full text's
  discussion section states the gene 'encodes one of the members of the roundabout guidance
  receptor's family' and specifically discusses ROBO2/SLIT signaling in detail. The gene name was
  stripped from the published version (title shows 'Identification of...as...'), but the full-text
  manuscript clearly identifies ROBO2. The user's attribution to PDE10A is factually incorrect —
  this is a significant gene identification error."
- **Disposition:** Excluded entirely from the PDE10A evidence base. The paper itself is a real,
  existing, non-retracted publication (PMID/DOI resolve correctly) — the issue is solely that it
  is about a different gene (ROBO2) than the one it was cited in support of (PDE10A). It is not
  relevant to any gene evaluated at this locus and is not carried into the evidence table.

No other citations returned RETRACTED or NOT_FOUND verdicts at this locus. All 7 remaining
citations (Duek 2021, Hong 2017, Odimba 2023, Melén 2011, Li 2021, Lackey 2021, Uemasu 2026) were
VERIFIED by the citation-verifier and appear in the evidence table.

## Genes marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic

### QKI — CONTESTED (on locus-specificity, not on general COPD relevance)

**Skeptic's reasoning, preserved verbatim:**

"**Verdict: CONTESTED** — specifically on the inference that QKI is the effector gene at THIS
locus. Not contesting QKI's role in COPD pathobiology generally (that evidence — Li 2021, Lackey
2021, and to a lesser extent the unreviewed Uemasu preprint — looks solid and directly mechanistic
for COPD/emphysema in general). What is contested is the leap from 'QKI is a strong COPD gene' to
'QKI is therefore a credible candidate for this specific credible set,' which is supported by zero
genetic-locus evidence and 1.2Mb of distance. This is a case where strong general disease-gene
biology is being used to backfill a statistical-genetics gap that doesn't otherwise exist — the
opposite failure mode from C6orf118 (which has locus proximity but no biology)."

Supporting detail from the skeptic's critique: "Per the structured Open Targets context, QKI has
zero gwas_credible_sets evidence rows for COPD at all (versus C6orf118 and PDE10A, which at least
have some evidence rows, albeit with unconfirmed locus linkage). QKI's presence in this write-up
is driven entirely by prior biological plausibility and physical proximity (1.2Mb — well outside
typical credible-set-to-gene assignment windows) with no colocalization, no TWAS, no eQTL,
nothing."

**Note on the Uemasu et al. 2026 citation specifically (bioRxiv preprint, PMID 42465480):**
verified by citation-verifier as a real, currently-unpublished preprint (not peer-reviewed, no
published_doi as of verification). The skeptic separately flagged: "this caveat needs to stay
attached every time this study is cited, since it currently carries a lot of the mechanistic
weight in the narrative."

**Disposition:** QKI's three citations (Li 2021, Lackey 2021, Uemasu 2026) remain in the main
evidence table (all VERIFIED as real, accurately-described papers) but are each individually
annotated CONTESTED in the "Skeptic verdict" column, and QKI is explicitly not selected as the
prioritized gene in the synthesis section, despite having the deepest mechanistic literature of
any candidate at this locus.

## Other caveats raised by the skeptic (not gene-specific CONTESTED verdicts, but material to interpretation)

- **Search-budget disclosure:** the red-team-skeptic's web search budget was exhausted (200/200)
  before it could run any independent queries for this locus; its critique is based entirely on
  re-analysis of the researcher's and verifier's existing findings, not fresh literature search.
  This is a limitation of this run, disclosed here for transparency.
- **Locus-wide absence of spirometry concordance:** none of C6orf118, PDE10A, or QKI shows any
  FEV1 or FEV1/FVC association in Open Targets — flagged as a uniform caveat across all three
  candidates.
- **Unexplained GWAS-Catalog-zero-hits for rs6902631:** the lead variant returned zero indexed
  associations of any kind (not even the presumptive COPD signal) in a GWAS Catalog web lookup,
  meaning there is no independent replication or PheWAS cross-reference available for this signal
  at all. The skeptic recommended this be run down further (e.g., checking for a proxy/tagged
  variant under a different rsID, or whether this reflects a very recent, not-yet-indexed
  meta-analysis) before treating the locus's existence as settled — this follow-up was not
  completed in this run due to search-budget exhaustion.
- **PDE10A evidence thinner than "modest" once the wrong-gene citation is removed:** with
  Hernandez-Pacheco excluded, PDE10A's remaining evidence is a single non-replicated
  sex-stratified COPD signal (Odimba 2023) plus a different-disease (asthma) developmental
  expression-timing note (Melén 2011). The skeptic noted PDE10A's only strong biological
  characterization is CNS/striatal pharmacology, which may point toward a smoking-behavior-
  mediated mechanism rather than direct lung biology — an open tension, not resolved by current
  evidence.
- **C6orf118's "primary candidate" status reflects nearest-gene convention, not evidentiary
  weight:** per the skeptic, "this is not 'weak evidence for C6orf118,' it is an absence of
  evidence plus a naming artifact."
