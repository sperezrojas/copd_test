## Genes marked CONTESTED, WEAKENED, or flagged with unresolved caveats by red-team-skeptic

Verbatim from `evidence_tables/_status/14_76900001-77200000/critique.md`:

### CIPC — WEAKENED

> The statistical-genetics case is thin and, where it exists, points away from lung: the
> only disease-specific GWAS/TWAS hit is a brain-tissue ASD association, and Open Targets
> L2G at this locus favors CIPC only for diastolic blood pressure and a smoking-behavior
> credible set with negligible PIP — never for the one directly pulmonary trait tested
> (FEV/FVC ratio), where IRF2BPL wins. The circadian-clock mechanistic literature (Mills
> bioRxiv, Sundar x2, Baniya, Panda, Hu, Chinnapaiyan, Rivas) is real and well-verified but
> is about BMAL1/CRY1/CRY2/PER/REV-ERBα, not CIPC — CIPC itself is never functionally
> tested in a lung/COPD model in any cited paper. The researcher was appropriately explicit
> about this gap rather than papering over it. WEAKENED — nearest-gene status plus
> class-level clock biology is being used to carry a candidate whose own gene-specific
> genetic evidence is null-to-negative for pulmonary traits.

Additional skeptic commentary (question 1 of the critique):

> This is a real inferential leap... the leap is larger than "circadian genes matter for
> lung," because CIPC is not one of the transcription-factor core loop components
> (CLOCK/BMAL1/PER/CRY/REV-ERB) that the cited mouse models actually manipulate — it's a
> peripheral interacting repressor of that loop. The mechanistic distance between "CRY1/2
> double-knockout causes emphysema" and "a SNP 10-12kb upstream of CIPC, with no eQTL
> evidence cited for CIPC, causes COPD via CIPC" is two unproven steps (CIPC's own
> necessity within the loop; the variant's regulatory effect on CIPC expression). This is
> overclaiming if presented as gene-level evidence; it is legitimate only as a stated
> hypothesis-generating analogy... no eQTL/TWAS link between the lead variant and CIPC
> expression was reported at all — an important omission the researcher didn't address
> (was CIPC eQTL colocalization checked and simply absent from Open Targets output, or not
> checked?).

### IRF2BPL — WEAKENED (not in original locus-file candidate list)

> Correctly flagged as a non-candidate-list addition. Its NEDAMSS neurodevelopmental
> disease literature (7 verified papers) is substantial and has zero lung/immune content.
> Its only pulmonary-relevant evidence is a single unverified Open Targets L2G score
> (0.533) with no PubMed corroboration. The researcher appropriately labeled this as an
> "unverified structured-database finding" and raised the pleiotropy/constraint-artifact
> alternative explicitly. WEAKENED — real signal (best L2G for FEV/FVC ratio in the region)
> but resting entirely on one non-literature data point, with a well-documented competing
> explanation (de novo constrained gene → generically strong L2G/PoPS across many unrelated
> traits) that the researcher did not resolve, only named.

Additional skeptic commentary (question 2 of the critique):

> Not sufficient on its own to elevate IRF2BPL as a serious COPD candidate. L2G scores are
> pleiotropy-prone by design — constrained, ubiquitously-expressed regulatory/
> transcription-associated genes (like IRF2BPL, given its LoF intolerance) routinely score
> well across many unrelated GWAS credible sets in this style of pipeline simply from
> genomic/functional annotation features correlated with constraint, not necessarily from
> tissue-specific causal biology... the more parsimonious explanation is a statistical
> artifact of constraint-driven L2G scoring rather than a true lung mechanism. I'd label
> this WEAKENED-to-CONTESTED-by-parsimony rather than a basis for elevation; synthesis-lead
> should not promote IRF2BPL above CIPC on this basis alone.

### ESRRB, LINC02289, LINC02288, ZDHHC22, TMEM63C — HOLDS

All five received a **HOLDS** verdict, meaning the skeptic found the researcher's
(uniformly low-confidence/deprioritizing) characterization of each to be accurate and not
overclaimed. Verbatim reasoning is in
`evidence_tables/_status/14_76900001-77200000/critique.md`; summarized: ESRRB rests on a
single PoPS flag with zero corroboration; LINC02289/LINC02288 host credible-set variants
but are never themselves L2G targets and have only tangential (non-pulmonary) functional
literature; ZDHHC22 and TMEM63C have well-characterized but lung-irrelevant primary biology
(CNS/cancer, and hereditary spastic paraplegia/renal, respectively), with ZDHHC22's one
airway-cell-line data point actually showing a *negative* result (no functional phenotype
from knockout).

## Unresolved questions and thoroughness gaps flagged by red-team-skeptic (not gene exclusions, but material caveats)

1. **Lead-variant gene assignment is unresolved, not just under-annotated.** Verbatim:
   > Neither is well supported. rs204983/rs204984 (PIP 0.39/0.29, combined ~0.68 of the
   > credible set) are intergenic, downstream_gene_variant annotations for CIPC and
   > roughly equidistant/plausible for the lncRNA 3' ends — Ensembl's
   > "downstream_gene_variant" annotation itself is a distance-based VEP call, not
   > evidence of regulatory function on CIPC specifically. No eQTL, chromatin-interaction
   > (Hi-C/ABC), or literature evidence was reported linking these specific variants to
   > CIPC (or to LINC02288/89) expression... This is a genuine "LD-block, multiple
   > plausible genes, can't distinguish" situation... CIPC's status as "nearest gene" is a
   > naming convention, not a demonstrated mechanism. Synthesis-lead should treat gene
   > assignment at the lead-variant level as unresolved.

2. **Smoking-mediation evidence is too thin to conclude either way.** Verbatim:
   > A single L2G=0.078... on a variant with PIP=0.0022 — three orders of magnitude below
   > the lead variants' PIP — cannot support a "this locus is smoking-mediated" claim. But
   > it likewise cannot be used to rule smoking mediation out... Correct conclusion:
   > insufficient evidence either way, not evidence of absence.

3. **Red-team-skeptic's own contradicting-evidence search is INCOMPLETE, not negative.**
   The skeptic's session web-search budget was exhausted (200/200) before any of its five
   planned queries executed. Verbatim:

   > different things, and I want to be explicit that this item is incomplete, not
   > negative.
   This should be re-run in a fresh session before treating "no contradicting evidence" as

4. **Supplementary-table-level review of major lung-function/COPD GWAS papers was not
   possible for either the researcher or the skeptic**, and is flagged as a real,
   actionable gap. Verbatim:
   > It's entirely possible one of these papers assigns a specific gene (IRF2BPL, CIPC, or
   > another) to this exact 14:76.9-77.2Mb region in its locus table or discusses it as a
   > known signal, which would materially change the confidence level of this writeup from
   > "literature-poor locus" to "known locus with an established candidate-gene
   > assignment." I flag this explicitly for synthesis-lead: before finalizing, someone
   > with full-text/supplementary-table access should check Shrine et al. 2019 Nat Genet

## LOC283575 identity resolution (structured database inference, not literature)

The ABC-prioritized candidate gene symbol "LOC283575" could not be resolved to an Ensembl
target ID via Open Targets. The researcher independently cross-referenced NCBI Entrez Gene
ID 283575 (the numeric ID underlying the inactive placeholder symbol) and found it now
resolves to the current official symbol **LINC02288**, consistent with Ensembl coordinates.
This is a **database inference, not a literature-verified claim**, and is reported in the
main evidence table's Open Targets summary accordingly — it did not go through
citation-verifier since it is not a literature citation.
