## CONTESTED / WEAKENED gene verdicts (red-team-skeptic reasoning, preserved verbatim)

### CSMD3 — CONTESTED

> Yes — CONTESTED, for the inferential claim "CSMD3 is the functional/causal gene at this
> COPD locus" (not a claim that McGeachie et al. is fabricated or wrong). The contradiction
> is internal/structural:
> - MAGMA/PoPS prioritization of CSMD3 is confounded by a documented statistical artifact:
>   CSMD3 is one of the largest genes in the genome, and gene-based tests (MAGMA) and, to a
>   lesser extent PoPS, are known to over-prioritize large genes from SNP density/gene-body
>   size alone, independent of true signal. Self-acknowledged in the researcher's own
>   record.
> - Open Targets shows ZERO genetic_association datatype score and NO gwas_credible_sets
>   evidence record at all for CSMD3-COPD — an independent large-scale aggregator sees no
>   COPD genetic signal at CSMD3 whatsoever, in direct tension with "MAGMA/PoPS-
>   prioritized."
> - No physical overlap (~1.0 Mb away), no ABC, no FLAMES.
> - The "immune infiltration" literature (CD8 T-cell/NK/Tfh infiltration correlating with
>   CSMD3 co-mutation, TMB) is textbook cancer-genomics confounding — TMB drives both
>   increased neoantigen load (→ infiltration) and passenger mutation accumulation in large
>   genes like CSMD3, independent of germline function. NOT evidence of immune mechanism;
>   should carry zero evidentiary weight on the immunology axis, not "weak evidence."
>
> Net: one real but weak/unreplicated regulatory data point, versus a documented
> statistical artifact explaining the "prioritization," a null from an independent
> aggregator, and confounded cancer-genomics citations. CONTESTED, not quietly accepted
> because "PoPS picked it."

On the McGeachie et al. finding specifically (WEAKENED, not outright rejected):

> No — suggestive but far short of load-bearing. Discount factors, stacked: n=581, single
> discovery cohort (CAMP, pediatric/adolescent asthma, not COPD). The interacting variant
> (rs4445257) sits ~113kb outside the actual 137-variant credible-set boundaries given for
> this locus — evidence about the region, not demonstrated to be the same statistical
> signal as rs290632/the credible set. No LD figure or conditional analysis reported to
> establish this is the same signal. Phenotype is a lung-function-decline trajectory in
> childhood asthma, related but distinct from adult COPD case-control status; "examined in
> a COPD meta-analysis cohort for replication" is reported without stating whether
> replication succeeded — a critical missing detail that should be resolved before leaning
> on this finding. Corrected p-value (6.7×10⁻⁹, not 6.7×10⁻⁸ as researcher stated) makes
> the original discovery MORE significant, not less — this correction cuts against
> overclaiming, but statistical significance in an underpowered, unreplicated (or
> ambiguously replicated) n=581 cohort still doesn't answer the causal-gene question. The
> chromatin interaction (3C, >1Mb) is the most valuable piece — an actual physical link
> across the desert to the CSMD3 promoter — but is a single assay in a single study, no
> orthogonal Hi-C/ABC/eQTL corroboration (ABC returned NA for this exact window), and 3C
> spanning >1Mb is technically harder to have high confidence in without validation.

### TRPS1 — WEAKENED, bordering CONTESTED

> WEAKENED to the point of effectively unsupported, bordering CONTESTED for the "TRPS1 is
> lung-relevant" implication specifically:
> - Zero PubMed hits for TRPS1+COPD, +spirometry/FEV1/GWAS, or +smoking — total absence,
>   not just weak evidence.
> - No physical overlap (~1.0-1.4 Mb away), no eQTL/chromatin literature bridging the
>   desert to TRPS1, no ABC/FLAMES support.
> - The two IHC papers closest to "lung-relevant" data (PMID 40170411, and CLAIM_MISMATCH
>   38041048) actually show LOW/absent TRPS1 in primary lung carcinoma relative to breast —
>   if this generalizes at all to non-neoplastic lung epithelium, this is mild
>   contradicting evidence, not supporting evidence (researcher's framing correctly caught
>   this).
> - Mouse knockout respiratory-failure phenotype (PMID 12446778) is a severe homozygous
>   GATA-domain-deletion model dying from a mechanical/skeletal (rib cage/thoracic spine)
>   defect, not parenchymal lung or airway biology — a poor analogy for a common noncoding
>   variant with modest effect size (MAF 0.45, PIP 0.176); should not be treated as
>   supporting a COPD-relevant lung mechanism.
> - Open Targets aggregate score (0.305) is driven mostly by animal_model (0.519, same weak
>   knockout analogy) and genetic_association (0.474) tracing to a single
>   gwas_credible_sets record whose variant/study IDs are UNRESOLVABLE — cannot confirm
>   this pertains to the same locus under review. Should be treated as non-informative
>   until resolved, not counted as corroboration.
> - oe=0.058 extreme LoF-constraint is interesting context (dosage-sensitive developmental
>   gene) but doesn't argue for or against a common regulatory variant's relevance to COPD.

### Locus-level verdict — recommend UNRESOLVED

> The case for "unresolved / causal gene not identified" is strong: true gene desert — no
> protein-coding gene overlaps either the 800kb locus or the 137-variant credible set. Lead
> variant PIP only 0.176 out of 137 variants spanning ~600kb — very high fine-mapping
> uncertainty, no missense/coding variants, no dedicated variant-level literature for
> rs290632 (zero PubMed hits, no GWAS Catalog phenotype at all, including no smoking-trait
> association reported — so smoking-mediation cannot be assessed one way or the other, only
> noted as data-absent). Neither candidate has ABC or FLAMES support. Neither candidate has
> ANY Open Targets evidence for FEV1 or FEV1/FVC — directly relevant to prior #2
> (spirometric concordance strengthens a locus); its total absence for both genes should be
> weighted as evidence against a well-characterized direct pulmonary mechanism for either
> gene, or at minimum as a sign the causal gene/mechanism hasn't been correctly identified
> yet. The two prioritization methods disagree (nearest-gene = TRPS1, statistical/size-prone
> method = CSMD3), and neither is corroborated by Open Targets' own aggregate COPD
> genetic-association evidence in an independently verifiable way.
