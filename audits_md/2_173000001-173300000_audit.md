## Genes flagged CONTESTED / WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning preserved)

Full critique verbatim is preserved at `evidence_tables/_status/2_173000001-173300000/critique.md`.
Key verdicts, reproduced here:

### MAP3K20 — WEAKENED
"Statistical genetics is real and locus-dominant (L2G 0.671), but (1) no spirometric concordance at
all, (2) Open Targets pattern actively suggests smoking-initiation mediation is at least as plausible
as pulmonary mechanism, and (3) every mechanistic biology citation is off-tissue. 'MAP3K20/ZAK is a
strong COPD candidate via ribotoxic-stress/inflammatory biology' overreaches; the defensible claim is
'MAP3K20 is the dominant statistical candidate at this locus, with unresolved smoking-mediation vs.
pulmonary-mechanism ambiguity, and no COPD- or lung-tissue-specific functional evidence.'"

Contributing detail: "Cheng 2009 (cardiac myoblast MMP-2/MMP-9/TIMP shift) points toward a net
anti-proteolytic effect of ZAK signaling — if generalized to lung (a big 'if,' wrong tissue), that
would argue AGAINST a protease/emphysema-promoting role... Feng 2017 lung-cancer SNPs are not in this
credible set — LD-region evidence at best (same gene, different variants, different phenotype), not
concordant genetic evidence for this signal."

Smoking-mediation analysis (specifically requested of the skeptic): "That combination — strong COPD
signal, absent spirometric concordance, stronger behavioral-trait signal at the same locus — is close
to the textbook profile of a smoking-behavior locus masquerading as a lung-disease locus... Absence
of literature on the smoking mechanism should not be read as absence of the mechanism — the database
pattern is the stronger prior here than the literature gap."

### ITGA6 — WEAKENED, bordering on should-be-deprioritized
"Given the researcher's own Ensembl GRCh38 coordinates... ITGA6 sits 500-750 kb *outside* the defined
locus window entirely, with zero gwas_credible_sets/L2G/coloc support of any kind... That is a
fundamentally different situation from 'PoPS prioritized a distal gene within the locus window' — it's
'PoPS prioritized a gene the pipeline's own coordinate check says isn't in the window.' ... this
pattern is consistent with either a genome-build mismatch somewhere upstream in the pipeline (GRCh37
vs. GRCh38) or a PoPS false positive driven by generic epithelial-adhesion gene features. Either way,
I would not let the PoPS flag alone sustain ITGA6 as a candidate without the build/coordinate
discrepancy being resolved first — **this should go back to the pipeline as a data-quality flag**, not
just be weighed qualitatively by synthesis-lead."

"Verdict: WEAKENED, bordering on should-be-deprioritized pending build/coordinate verification. Zero
statistical-genetics anchoring, zero spirometric or smoking data, generic (non-lung-disease-specific)
structural biology, and a serious unresolved physical-location discrepancy. I would not call this
CONTESTED in the sense of 'credible contradicting paper found' (I found none, though I also couldn't
search), but the internal Open Targets/Ensembl evidence itself is already close to disqualifying."

### RAPGEF4 — WEAKENED / partially CONTESTED
"This gene was added purely because 54 kb of its 3' end overlaps the locus start — it appears in none
of the four prioritization methods... There is literally zero statistical-genetics link (GWAS/coloc/
TWAS/MR) between this credible set and RAPGEF4 expression or function — this is the single largest gap
for this gene... RAPGEF4's inclusion rests on 54 kb of incidental 3'-end overlap plus generic
biological plausibility, not on any statistical fine-mapping signal."

Contested internal contradiction: "Oldenburger 2014 is the strongest single piece of evidence in this
entire dossier for any of the three genes... direction: RAPGEF4 *promotes* CS-induced inflammation.
Chen 2019 shows the opposite direction of effect for Epac pathway activation, but in an OVA/Th2 asthma
model using non-isoform-selective Epac activation. Per prior #3 (heterogeneous inflammatory programs),
this is plausibly reconcilable as context-dependence... but it does mean 'RAPGEF4/Epac2 signaling is
inflammation-promoting in the lung' is an overclaim; the honest claim is 'RAPGEF4/Epac2 loss is
protective specifically in a CS-neutrophilic/macrophage model, while pathway activation is protective
in a distinct Th2/allergic model' — a genuinely mixed, isoform- and context-dependent picture that the
researcher already flagged correctly and should not be resolved in synthesis by picking the more
COPD-flattering study."

"Verdict: WEAKENED / partially CONTESTED. The Oldenburger 2014 mouse-genetic CS-inflammation data is
genuinely the most mechanistically on-target finding across all three candidate genes at this locus,
but it stands alone, is not human, is not COPD tissue, is directionally complicated by Chen 2019 in a
different immune context, and — critically — has no statistical-genetics link to this specific
credible set. I would not let the strength of this one mechanistic paper outweigh the fact that
RAPGEF4 was not independently nominated by any prioritization method and its physical link to the
locus is a 54 kb 3'-UTR overlap."

## Recommended follow-up actions for the study team

1. Resolve the ITGA6 genome-build/coordinate discrepancy (verify which build PoPS was run against for
   this locus; confirm whether ITGA6 belongs to this locus at all).
2. Directly check whether rs16861404/this credible set appears among the 279 signals reported in
   Shrine et al. 2019 (PMID 30804560).
3. Re-run red-team-skeptic's contradiction-search pass for MAP3K20, ITGA6, and RAPGEF4 once web-search
   budget is available, since this pass returned zero independent search results for all three genes.
4. Consider a smoking-adjusted or multivariable MR analysis to distinguish a direct pulmonary effect
   of MAP3K20 from a smoking-behavior-mediated one, given the Open Targets smoking-initiation signal
   exceeding the COPD signal at this locus.
5. Consider a targeted search for human COPD lung tissue or single-cell expression data for RAPGEF4
   and MAP3K20, both currently absent from the evidence base.
