   respiratory disease: a Mendelian randomization study." BMC Pulm Med. 2025;25(1):71.
   PMID 39934773, DOI 10.1186/s12890-025-03527-x.
   Researcher's original claim: "genetically proxied APOB inhibition (mimicking

   here).

2. **Delporte C, Van Antwerpen P, Vanhamme L, et al.** "Low-density lipoprotein modified
   by myeloperoxidase in inflammatory pathways and clinical studies." Mediators Inflamm.
   2013;2013:971579. PMID 23983406, DOI 10.1155/2013/971579.

## Genes marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic

### APOB
Full skeptic reasoning (verbatim): "This is the section I'd push back on hardest. The
write-up devotes 7 verified citations to APOB/apoB-lipid-COPD/lung-function literature,
which risks creating a visual/rhetorical impression of substantial support — but the
researcher's own conclusion correctly notes: (1) the two studies that actually test
APOB's own causal role in COPD via MR (Chen 2026, Huang 2025) are both null, with ApoA-I
(a different gene/protein) carrying the protective signal instead; (2) none of the 7
citations reference this locus, its variants, or LINC01830; (3) APOB sits >1 Mb from
every credible-set variant with no coloc/eQTL link offered. This is a textbook
'candidate-gene magnet' effect — APOB is a famous, heavily-studied lipid gene, so a locus
search inevitably surfaces a lot of APOB-lipid-lung literature regardless of whether it's
relevant to this specific desert locus. The literature present is real (verified), but
it's generic pathway-level lipid biology, not locus-specific evidence, and the two
studies most directly on point actively contradict an APOB-COPD causal link." Verdict:
CONTESTED — "not because there's a competing gene claim, but because the direct
causal-inference literature (Chen 2026, Huang 2025) refutes the only hypothesis (APOB →
COPD) that would justify including this gene at all, and the remaining supportive-looking
citations are distance-blind and non-causal (observational/MR-on-other-lipids).
Synthesis-lead should not let citation volume substitute for locus-specific relevance
here."

## Genes/framing marked WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic

### LINC01830
Full skeptic reasoning (verbatim): "The researcher is appropriately honest that this is
an uncharacterized lncRNA with zero relevant literature. But the framing that
credible-set variants 'consist exclusively' of LINC01830 intronic variants overstates
what that containment means: with 49 variants and max PIP ~0.044, this is a
statistically unresolved signal, not a fine-mapped signal to LINC01830. The variants fall
in this transcript because it's apparently the only annotated feature spanning that
stretch of gene desert, not because fine-mapping concentrated probability there.
'Physically in the credible set' is doing more rhetorical work here than the PIP
distribution supports — it should be described as an artifact of transcript annotation
over an empty region, not as genuine (even if weak) fine-mapping support for LINC01830 as
a target." Verdict: WEAKENED — the "only gene physically present" framing implies more
localization than a flat, unresolved 49-variant credible set actually provides.
