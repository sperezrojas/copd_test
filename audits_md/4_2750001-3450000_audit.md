## Genes marked CONTESTED by the red-team-skeptic (reasoning preserved verbatim)

### HTT

> **HTT** (2nd-highest PIP variant)
> The gene with the clearest overreach risk. HTT's dominant OT L2G/structured scores are very likely an artifact of the sheer volume of Huntington's-disease literature (3145 associated diseases in OT) rather than genuine common-variant lung biology — a classic literature-volume confound in L2G-style scores. The Jones 2016 ERJ citation (HD patients show FEV1/FVC decline) and the BACHD diaphragm-NMJ paper are both monogenic CAG-expansion, large-effect, neuromuscular-control mechanisms — mechanistically a different causal pathway (effort-dependent spirometric artifact from bulbar/respiratory muscle dysfunction) than typical airway-remodeling/inflammatory COPD pathology. Applying prior #2 (FEV1/FEV1-FVC concordance strengthens pulmonary relevance): yes, there is spirometric concordance for HTT, but it comes from a completely different disease mechanism (neuromuscular weakness in monogenic HD) with no established link to how a common noncoding 3'UTR SNP of small effect would act. Spirometric concordance without mechanistic relevance to airway/immune COPD biology should not be treated as equivalent to what the concordance prior was designed to reward. Additionally, HTT was NOT top L2G in the one actual COPD-labeled FinnGen credible set (GRK4 was) — so the case for HTT specifically driving the COPD phenotype (as opposed to the FEV1/body-composition traits where it clearly does dominate) is weaker than its raw OT dominance suggests.
>
> VERDICT: **CONTESTED** — not via a new external contradicting paper (none found this session), but via the internal evidence already in hand: HTT's apparent dominance is plausibly a literature-volume/L2G artifact, its supporting spirometric mechanism (monogenic neuromuscular effort-dependent decline) is mechanistically distinct from the common-variant/airway-inflammatory pathway this GWAS is presumably capturing, and HTT is not the top L2G gene in the actual COPD credible set. Synthesis should not let HTT's "2nd-highest PIP + dominant OT scores" translate into "leading candidate" without carrying forward this mechanistic caveat explicitly.

## Genes recommended for exclusion from the evidence table entirely (no fine-mapping/prioritization signal)

The researcher additionally surveyed three protein-coding genes physically within the locus boundary (via an Ensembl REST overlap query) that were not flagged by the nearest-gene, FLAMES, MAGMA, PoPS, or ABC prioritization methods, nor assigned to any credible-set variant: **TNIP2, SH3BP2, and HGFAC**. The red-team-skeptic reviewed these and recommended they be dropped rather than carried into the evidence table:

> **Region-wide genes with no fine-mapping signal (TNIP2, SH3BP2, HGFAC):** Agree these should be EXCLUDED from the final gene-level evidence table rather than carried forward as candidates. No statistical (fine-mapping/PoPS/MAGMA/coloc) signal ties any of these three to this specific locus, and each verified citation found is in a clearly unrelated context (progesterone/endometrial-cancer NF-kB biology for TNIP2; a general immune-signaling review not COPD-specific for SH3BP2; NSCLC lung-cancer cell-line HGF-Met signaling for HGFAC, not COPD/airway-remodeling). Including them risks treating "a plausible-sounding immune gene happens to sit in the region" as evidence when the pipeline's own statistical layer did not select them. Recommendation: drop, not weigh.

Per this recommendation, these three genes and their citations are omitted from `evidence_tables/4_2750001-3450000.md`. For completeness, the citations found for them (all VERIFIED by citation-verifier, but excluded on relevance/scope grounds per the skeptic's recommendation above) were:

- **TNIP2**: Davies et al., Gynecologic Oncology, 2004. PMID 15297189, DOI 10.1016/j.ygyno.2004.05.028 — VERIFIED. Progesterone-receptor induction of ABIN-2 (TNIP2)/A20 inhibits NF-kB DNA binding in endometrial cancer cells; general NF-kB biology, not COPD/lung-specific.
- **SH3BP2**: Hatani & Sada, Current Medicinal Chemistry, 2008 (review). PMID 18336269, DOI 10.2174/092986708783769795 — VERIFIED. SH3BP2 positively regulates IgE-mediated mast-cell activation and B-/T-cell receptor (NFAT) signaling; cherubism mutations increase myeloid-cell (osteoclast precursor) responsiveness to M-CSF/RANKL. General immunoreceptor-signaling biology, not COPD/lung-specific, and no locus-level statistical signal implicates this gene.
- **HGFAC**: Shi et al., Molecular and Cellular Biochemistry, 2025. PMID 41182650, DOI 10.1007/s11010-025-05423-8 — VERIFIED. Cathepsin L transcriptionally upregulates HGFAC, driving HGF/Met signaling and chemoresistance/stemness in non-small-cell lung cancer cell lines/xenografts. Lung-cancer oncology context, not COPD/airway-remodeling biology; gene also sits at the edge of/possibly just outside the stated locus boundary.

## Other WEAKENED verdicts (for reference; full reasoning is also carried in the main evidence table's "Skeptic verdict" column)

The red-team-skeptic applied a WEAKENED verdict (not full exclusion, but explicit caution against overclaiming) to MSANTD1, NOP14, ADD1, SLC75A1/MFSD10, RGS12, and GRK4. Selected reasoning excerpts preserved verbatim:

> **MSANTD1** ... That's a red flag pattern (one hallucinated-relevance citation among four) worth noting as a data-quality signal, even though it's already excluded. Combined with OT showing MSANTD1 has the weakest structured score of all 7 candidates for both COPD and FEV1, and is never top L2G at its own lead variant (HTT dominates instead), the "nearest gene / top PIP" heuristic looks actively misleading here.

> **ADD1** ... If synthesis wrote "ADD1 links smoking to COPD via adducin pathway," that would be overclaiming — the actual evidence chain is ADD1->hypertension (smoking-interaction) and ADD1->smoking-initiation (OT-only, uncited). These are two separate unverified/weak links being chained together to manufacture a smoking-mediation story that no single paper actually makes.

> **GRK4** ... the paper's "exacerbating inflammatory responses" claim is explicitly GeneMANIA-network-derived speculation, not experimental immune-cell data — restating this as "GRK4 drives inflammatory COPD pathology" would be a real overclaim relative to what the cited abstract actually supports ... GRK4's own COPD OT score is "substantially literature-driven" rather than genetic_association-driven — meaning some of GRK4's apparent prioritization may be circularly influenced by the very same TWAS paper being counted twice ... Synthesis should NOT double-count the Chen 2024 paper as if it were two separate corroborating signals.
