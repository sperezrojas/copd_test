## Gene-level critique verdicts (verbatim from red-team-skeptic)

### SPATS2L — verdict: WEAKENED (as COPD-specific evidence)

> The genetic-association and FEV1/FVC-colocalization evidence for SPATS2L at this locus is
> real, but the inference that this reflects COPD-specific pulmonary/inflammatory biology
> overreaches: (1) the dominant, mechanistic literature is asthma-bronchodilator-
> pharmacodynamic (via a variant not confirmed to be in LD with the credible-set lead),
> raising a live and previously unflagged alternative that the signal partly reflects
> post-bronchodilator measurement/ascertainment effects on COPD case definition rather than
> disease biology; (2) the immune evidence offered is cross-disease pleiotropy (ARDS,
> psoriasis), not COPD-specific; (3) smoking-mediation is untested, not negative.

Full supporting reasoning from the critique (verbatim):

> **The asthma-vs-COPD identity problem (directly asked about):** This is the load-bearing
> issue at this locus, and I think the researcher under-weighted one specific mechanism.
> Nearly every high-confidence, replicated SPATS2L finding is not about asthma
> *susceptibility* — it's about **bronchodilator pharmacodynamics** (SPATS2L knockdown
> ↑ADRB2 protein; rs295137 associated with %FEV1 change post-SABA; ED-management-failure
> during exacerbations). That's a drug-response/measurement mechanism, not an inflammatory
> or structural-remodeling mechanism. This matters specifically for COPD case ascertainment:
> COPD is typically defined by **post-bronchodilator** FEV1/FVC<0.7 (GOLD criteria). If
> SPATS2L variants genuinely modulate the pharmacological response to bronchodilator
> challenge, a variant here could shift case/control status at the diagnostic threshold
> *independent of any underlying difference in disease biology* — i.e., a
> measurement/ascertainment artifact baked into how COPD is defined, not evidence of a
> COPD-causing pathway. This is a distinct and arguably more parsimonious alternative to the
> "asthma-COPD-overlap misclassification" explanation the researcher already flagged as
> unresolved. Neither the researcher's synthesis nor the Hayden 2018 ACO analysis addresses
> this measurement-mechanism explanation at all — it should be surfaced to synthesis-lead as
> a live alternative, not folded into the vaguer "ambiguous/mixed" framing already given.
>
> Separately, the Open Targets FEV1/FVC L2G colocalization (0.783) is real supporting
> evidence per prior #2, but two things temper it: (a) it's a database output, not an
> independently verified literature claim, so it carries different evidentiary weight than
> the PMIDs; (b) population-based FEV1/FVC GWAS cohorts (e.g., UK Biobank) contain
> substantial undiagnosed/subclinical asthma, so this colocalization does not itself
> distinguish "SPATS2L affects lung function via COPD-relevant biology" from "SPATS2L
> affects lung function via asthma/airway-hyperresponsiveness biology that also moves
> population spirometry." The researcher's own "genuinely ambiguous/mixed, not resolved"
> verdict is the right call — I'd keep it exactly that calibrated, not stronger.
>
> One more gap: the strong asthma-pharmacogenetic literature is built on **rs295137**, and
> the credible-set/GWAS lead is **rs985256** (PIP=0.47). No LD figure between these two
> variants is reported anywhere in the package. Without that, treating the rs295137
> pharmacogenetic literature as evidence about *this* signal is an assumption of shared
> mechanism by gene-proximity, not an established genetic link — a variant on the
> LD/proximity-bias axis, just within-gene rather than between-gene. This should be
> explicitly checked before the asthma literature is used to characterize the COPD
> credible-set signal.
>
> **Immune evidence:** ARDS interferon-signature (Englert 2019) and psoriasis-activated-
> T-cell (Palau 2013) papers are both from diseases mechanistically distant from COPD (acute
> lung injury; skin autoimmunity) and neither paper claims COPD relevance — the researcher
> reported them as SPATS2L pleiotropy findings, not as direct COPD-immune evidence, which is
> fair, but there's a real risk synthesis-lead reads "SPATS2L is immunologically active" and
> over-generalizes toward COPD's neutrophilic/Th17 programs (prior #3). Recommend these be
> labeled explicitly as *not COPD-specific, tangential pleiotropy* in synthesis, not as
> supporting evidence for a COPD immune mechanism. The thymulin paper is appropriately
> caveated already as speculative/non-human. Notably absent: no direct COPD-tissue (sputum,
> BAL, lung explant) expression or immune-cell-specific study of SPATS2L was found at all —
> worth flagging as a real gap in the immune section rather than assuming the search was
> exhaustive.
>
> **Smoking-mediation:** genuinely untested (tooling limitation, not a null result) — should
> not be read as "smoking-mediation ruled out."

### SATB2 — verdict: CONTESTED

> The case against prioritizing SATB2 is the strongest part of this locus package: no COPD,
> lung-function, or smoking GWAS/TWAS/MR hit anywhere; absent from L2G at all three queried
> credible-set variants (SPATS2L was the *only* gene returned); ~679kb outside the stated
> locus and farther still from the lead variant; extreme constraint (pLoF o/e=0.016)
> consistent with a haploinsufficient developmental TF where common noncoding variants are
> less plausible drivers of a population-scale quantitative trait; and every lung-related
> SATB2 paper is either a diagnostic marker for *excluding* lung origin of tumors, an NSCLC
> oncogene/circRNA mechanism, or a cadmium-carcinogenesis mechanism — none is an
> airway-remodeling or inflammatory mechanism relevant to COPD.
>
> This is not literally a "paper argues against SATB2 as the COPD gene at this locus"
> contradiction (no such paper was found or would be expected — SATB2 isn't in the COPD
> literature at all), but the L2G-negative result across all three queried variants is
> itself a systematic, structured contradiction of the MAGMA/PoPS prioritization, and it
> comes from the same database, not a different one that might disagree. MAGMA/PoPS are
> gene-set/expression-similarity-based methods known to occasionally return genes based on
> genomic windows or transcriptomic similarity rather than a direct causal-variant link;
> here that risk is realized about as clearly as it can be (distance + no
> fine-mapped-variant-level support + no disease-relevant mechanism + extreme constraint
> arguing against a common-variant regulatory effect).
>
> (using "contested" loosely — the contradiction is the L2G-negative finding + physical
> distance + absence of any COPD/lung mechanism, not a literature paper disputing SATB2;
> flag this distinction to synthesis-lead so they can decide if a database-level
> contradiction meets their bar for the label). Recommend SATB2 be deprioritized relative to
> SPATS2L; the MAGMA/PoPS signal should be treated as most likely a gene-window/expression-
> similarity artifact, as the researcher itself already suggested (correctly, in my view)
> while appropriately flagging it as interpretive judgment rather than a citable conclusion.

## Process/tooling limitations disclosed by subagents (for transparency)

- **Citation-verifier**: no limitations reported; all 31 submitted citations were checked
  against PubMed metadata/abstracts.
- **Red-team-skeptic**: reported that its WebSearch tool budget was already exhausted
  (200/200 used) before it could run any of its four planned independent contradiction
  searches (SPATS2L-COPD-specificity, SATB2-lung-function, SPATS2L-smoking-GWAS,
  ACO-misclassification). Its entire critique is therefore a re-analysis of the researcher's
  and verifier's existing findings against this pipeline's stated priors, not the product of
  new independent literature search. This should be treated as an open item: if search
  budget allows in a future pass, an independent red-team literature search for this locus
  has not yet actually been performed.
- **Researcher**: reported it could not directly query GWAS Catalog's populated association
  data or GSCAN/smoking-behavior GWAS summary statistics (JS-rendering/tooling limitations
  via WebFetch), so the "no smoking-behavior GWAS association found" conclusion for
  SPATS2L/SATB2 is an absence-of-evidence-in-PubMed-literature-search finding, not a
  confirmed tested null against primary GWAS summary statistics.

## Open Targets tooling note

`get_open_targets_graphql_schema` (tried with categories `disease-associations`,
`genetic-associations`, `target-safety`, and combined) and `get_type_dependencies` (tried
with type `Target`) all returned responses exceeding the tool's token ceiling even when
narrowed to a single category/type, and the saved overflow files were single-line JSON that
could not be paged with line-based Read offsets. The orchestrator worked around this by
constructing GraphQL queries directly from known Open Targets Platform field names and
iteratively fixing field-name errors (short error messages, not large dumps) rather than
retrieving the schema — this is disclosed for transparency, not because it changes the
validity of the query results obtained (each query returned a successful, directly
inspectable JSON payload).
