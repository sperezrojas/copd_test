## Excluded citations (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH)

### 1. Liu M, Jiang Y, Wedow R, et al. "Association studies of up to 1.2 million individuals yield new insights into the genetic etiology of tobacco and alcohol use." Nat Genet 2019;51(2):237-244. PMID 30643251, PMC PMC6358542, DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0307-5.

**Verdict: NOT_FOUND** (for the specific sub-claim tested; the paper itself exists and is not retracted).

Citation-verifier's finding, verbatim: "Paper exists and is not retracted (confirmed from PubMed/full text access). However, the specific sub-claim that KCNIP4 is listed as one of the 406 genome-wide significant loci for smoking initiation or tobacco traits cannot be verified. Full text search of main text and supplementary locus tables shows no mention of chr4p15.2, KCNIP4, or rs11733529 in the locus catalogues. While the paper is valid and reports 406 loci, KCNIP4/chr4p15.2 is not among them."

Reason for exclusion from the evidence table: the researcher had proposed this paper as the likely primary source underlying Open Targets' "smoking initiation" association score for KCNIP4, but this specific attribution could not be substantiated on full-text/supplementary-table check. The paper's mere existence does not support a KCNIP4-specific claim, so it is excluded from the main evidence table rather than presented as if it corroborated a KCNIP4-smoking-initiation genome-wide-significant signal.

### 2. Sakornsakolpat P, Prokopenko D, Lamontagne M, et al. "Genetic landscape of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease identifies heterogeneous cell-type and phenotype associations." Nat Genet 2019;51(3):494-505. PMID 30804561, PMC PMC6546635, DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0342-2.

**Verdict: NOT_FOUND** (for the specific sub-claim tested; the paper itself exists and is not retracted).

Citation-verifier's finding, verbatim: "Paper exists and is not retracted (confirmed from PubMed/full text access). However, the specific sub-claim that chr4p15.2/KCNIP4 is listed as one of the 82 genome-wide significant COPD loci cannot be verified. Full text search of main text and supplementary tables shows no mention of chr4p15.2, KCNIP4, rs11733529, or the chr4:21.5Mb region among the 82 reported COPD loci. KCNIP4 is not reported in this landmark COPD GWAS."

Reason for exclusion from the evidence table: this is the major published COPD GWAS meta-analysis and was the researcher's presumed anchor citation for "why this is a COPD locus," but the KCNIP4/chr4p15.2 locus does not appear among its 82 reported loci. Excluded from the main table to avoid implying this landmark paper corroborates the KCNIP4-COPD association — it does not. Note this does not contradict the locus's own credible set existing: Open Targets independently shows a distinct, biobank/PheWAS-derived COPD credible set at this locus (study GCST90476027, "Chronic airway obstruction," PheCode 496), which is a different underlying GWAS than Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019. The citation-verifier and red-team-skeptic both flag that the actual source GWAS for this pipeline's own credible set at this locus has not been identified/cited in the literature reviewed — this remains an open thoroughness gap for any follow-up review of this locus.

## Contested / weakened gene verdicts

### KCNIP4 — red-team-skeptic verdict: WEAKENED

Preserved verbatim from the red-team-skeptic's report:

> **Search disclosure (read this first):** My web-search budget was exhausted before I could run any queries — both of my attempted contradiction-search queries ("KCNIP4 COPD lung function FEV1 GWAS" and "KCNIP4 smoking initiation nicotine dependence GWAS chr4p15") returned budget-exhaustion errors with zero results. Since KCNIP4 is the only candidate gene at this locus, it received zero independent search coverage from me — my entire critique below is derived from re-reading and cross-checking the pasted researcher findings and Open Targets data, not from new literature. Synthesis-lead should treat the "contesting evidence" search as not done for this locus and should not assume my silence on a contradicting paper means none exists.
>
> **1. "Uncontested single gene" framing — overclaiming risk.** This phrase is true only at the level of gene identification (KCNIP4 is the sole protein-coding gene in the interval, all 14 credible-set variants are intronic within it, FLAMES/PoPS agree, no LD-sharing with a neighboring gene). It is not evidence that KCNIP4 is the causal gene for COPD via a lung mechanism — those are two different claims that risk being conflated at synthesis. "Uncontested" should be read narrowly as "no competing gene for this LD block," not as "uncontested COPD causal mechanism."
>
> **2. The two NOT_FOUND landmark claims are a bigger deal than a citation footnote — this is a thoroughness gap.** The literature review, as assembled, does not actually contain a citation for why this locus is a genome-wide-significant COPD hit at all — the researcher never identified which GWAS this pipeline's credible set/lead variant (rs11733529) actually comes from, if not Sakornsakolpat 2019. Given that Open Targets separately shows an independent PheCode-496-based credible set (GCST90476027) at this exact locus, it's plausible the pipeline's source GWAS is a newer/different (likely biobank-scale) COPD meta-analysis that was never identified or cited in the statgen section. This is a genuine thoroughness gap. Similarly, the GSCAN NOT_FOUND weakens (does not eliminate) the smoking-initiation-mediation hypothesis as a literature-supported claim — it remains supported only by the Open Targets aggregate structured score, which is explicitly not a literature citation.
>
> **3. Himes 2013 (asthma/AHR) and the cancer smoking-interaction papers — are they stretched into COPD/pulmonary evidence?** The researcher's own prose is appropriately hedged. But push back harder than the researcher did on AHR as pulmonary-biology evidence for COPD specifically: spirometric traits (FEV1, FEV1/FVC) are the strong pulmonary-biology signal for COPD; airway hyperresponsiveness is a related but mechanistically distinct trait (smooth-muscle reactivity/bronchoconstriction, classically asthma-associated) and is not interchangeable with the fixed airflow obstruction that defines COPD. Himes 2013 is nominal-significance-only, asthma/AHR-specific, with no functional follow-up ever found. If synthesis treats this as "pulmonary biology support," that would be overreach.
>
> **4. More parsimonious alternative explanations — should be stated explicitly, with two competing alternatives, not one.** (a) Smoking-behavior/neurobehavioral mediation: KCNIP4 is characterized exclusively in neuronal/CNS contexts in every piece of retrieved molecular biology; Open Targets' aggregate association profile is dominated by psychiatric/neurobehavioral/reproductive traits, and smoking initiation outranks COPD in association score — a textbook signature of a CNS-pleiotropy locus acting on smoking behavior rather than lung tissue, though currently supported only by structured-database evidence, not a verified literature citation (that specific claim was NOT_FOUND). (b) Metabolic/cardiovascular pleiotropy or comorbidity/phenotyping artifact: the Open Targets COPD-specific credible set colocalizes only with fasting glucose and systolic blood pressure, not lung function or any lung molecular QTL, raising a real possibility that the "COPD" (PheCode-defined, biobank/EHR-derived) phenotype association is driven by metabolic/cardiovascular comorbidity leakage in phenotyping rather than by smoking behavior or lung biology at all. This is a distinct alternative from (a) and should not be conflated with it. Given total absence of FEV1/FEV1-FVC/spirometric association anywhere in a comprehensive 242-trait Open Targets list, and total absence of lung-tissue colocalization in the one COPD-specific credible set that does exist for this locus, direct pulmonary/structural lung-disease mechanism is the least-supported of the three hypotheses, not the default one implied by treating "COPD locus, only gene KCNIP4" as settled.
>
> **5. Overclaiming check summary.** The researcher's own sentence-level hedging is generally faithful to the underlying abstracts. No place found where a verified citation's claim was strengthened in the write-up beyond what its quoted content supports. The overclaiming risk is at the level of section framing/headers ("uncontested single-gene prioritization") rather than individual citation sentences.
>
> **Verdict: WEAKENED.** Reasoning for the audit trail: Gene identification at this locus is genuinely uncontested (sole protein-coding gene, all credible-set variants intronic within it, FLAMES/PoPS concordant) — there is no LD/neighboring-gene ambiguity to contest. However, the inference that this represents a direct COPD/pulmonary-biology signal overreaches the evidence: (i) zero literature or Open Targets support for FEV1/FEV1-FVC/spirometric association despite a comprehensive 242-trait check; (ii) the one directly pulmonary paper found (Himes 2013) supports asthma/AHR, a related but distinct trait, at nominal significance only; (iii) the two landmark papers that would have anchored a COPD- or smoking-initiation-specific genome-wide signal (Sakornsakolpat 2019; GSCAN/Liu 2019) were both found NOT_FOUND for the specific claims made about them — indicating the actual source GWAS for this locus's COPD signal has not yet been identified/cited (flagged as a thoroughness gap requiring researcher follow-up before synthesis finalizes); (iv) the only COPD-phenotype-specific credible set with colocalization data (Open Targets GCST90476027) colocalizes with fasting glucose and systolic blood pressure, not lung function or any lung molecular QTL, actively pointing away from a lung-tissue mechanism; (v) KCNIP4's entire characterized molecular biology and dominant genetic pleiotropy profile is neuronal/CNS and neurobehavioral. This does not rise to CONTESTED because no new paper was found (or searched for, given exhausted budget) that names a different candidate gene at this locus or directly refutes KCNIP4's genetic association with the COPD phenotype itself — the contest here is over mechanism/pathway, not gene identity or the existence of an association. Synthesis should present KCNIP4 as a statistically robust but mechanistically unresolved locus, with smoking-behavior mediation and metabolic/comorbidity confounding as at least as plausible as direct pulmonary biology, and should explicitly flag the unresolved "which GWAS actually contains this locus" question before final write-up.
>
> **New citations requiring verification:** None generated — no new searches were completed due to search-budget exhaustion, so no new UNVERIFIED citations are introduced. This absence should itself be logged: KCNIP4 got a zero-search contradiction pass from the red-team stage, not a "searched and found nothing" pass.
