# Audit / excluded-evidence appendix: locus 2:103,100,001-104,150,000 (nearest gene TMEM182)

This appendix lists (1) every citation excluded from the evidence table due to a
RETRACTED/NOT_FOUND/CLAIM_MISMATCH verdict, and (2) every gene the red-team-skeptic marked
CONTESTED, with its reasoning preserved verbatim. Not intended for distribution alongside the
evidence table to end users of the prioritization result — retained here for full
traceability of the review process.

## Excluded citations (verdict other than VERIFIED)

**PMID 35095769** — Wang W et al., "LINC00467 Is Upregulated by DNA Copy Number Amplification
and Hypomethylation and Shows ceRNA Potential in Lung Adenocarcinoma," Frontiers in
Endocrinology, 2022. DOI 10.3389/fendo.2021.802463.
- **Original claim (researcher):** "TMEM182 identified as one of five mRNAs in a lncRNA
  (LINC00467)-mediated ceRNA network in lung adenocarcinoma (LUAD), with TMEM182 flagged as a
  potential prognostic biomarker for LUAD survival."
- **Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH.**
- **Verifier's reasoning (verbatim):** "PubMed abstract shows TMEM182 was included in a ceRNA
  network with four other mRNAs (BARX2, BCL9, KCNK1, KIAA1324), BUT Kaplan-Meier survival
  analysis identified only BCL9 and KIAA1324 (and KCNK1 in one database) as prognostic
  biomarkers — NOT TMEM182. Claim overstates TMEM182's role as 'a potential prognostic
  biomarker'; it was part of the network but not identified as independently prognostic."
- **Why excluded:** the paper does not support the specific claim made about TMEM182; it
  supports a claim about other genes in the same network. Excluded from the evidence table in
  full; not used to support any prioritization judgment.

No RETRACTED or NOT_FOUND verdicts were returned for any citation at this locus (16 of 17
line items / 22 of 23 individual PMIDs were VERIFIED).

## Genes marked CONTESTED by the red-team-skeptic (full reasoning preserved verbatim)

### TMEM182 — CONTESTED

> The verified evidence is exclusively about myogenesis, adipogenesis, cardiomyocyte
> differentiation, and blood pressure×BMI interaction — in mouse, chicken, iPSC, and
> human-BP-cohort contexts. Zero verified findings touch lung structure, airway epithelium, or
> pulmonary immune biology. The one lung-adjacent claim (PMID 35095769, LUAD ceRNA prognostic
> biomarker) was already caught by the citation-verifier as a CLAIM_MISMATCH — the paper does
> not actually support TMEM182 as prognostic — which is itself a warning sign about how this
> gene's story has been built (a pattern of the initial researcher pass reaching for
> adjacent-sounding but unsupported claims).
>
> On the specific questions posed: (1) the verified evidence supports unrelated tissue biology
> (muscle/fat), not a COPD mechanism; (2) the adiposity/body-composition hypothesis is far
> better supported than a direct pulmonary hypothesis — TMEM182's own functional literature is
> adipocyte/myocyte differentiation, and this dovetails with the Open Targets pleiotropy
> pattern (~100 shared credible sets dominated by body-composition/anthropometric traits) far
> more naturally than with the smaller airway-obstruction cluster; (5) the
> sarcopenia/respiratory-muscle-wasting or obesity-comorbidity connection to COPD is a
> plausible-sounding narrative but is a pure post-hoc bridge — no cited study tests TMEM182 in
> respiratory muscle, diaphragm, or COPD-associated cachexia/sarcopenia specifically. It is
> exactly the kind of "stretch" the task instructions warn against.
>
> Additionally, TMEM182 sits ~80kb outside the stated locus window per Ensembl GRCh38 — it is
> the nearest gene, not a gene inside the credible-set region, and there is no L2G/
> colocalization data anywhere for the ~100 credible sets sharing this lead variant. This is
> textbook nearest-gene bias: a body-composition/muscle-development gene assigned to a COPD
> locus by proximity alone, with a smoking-behavior SNP (rs12616219, a distinct variant, ~74kb
> from the COPD lead SNP) also nearby, further diluting any claim of a lung-specific
> mechanism.
>
> **CONTESTED** — the verified literature affirmatively supports an alternative, non-pulmonary
> biology (muscle/adipose development) as TMEM182's only established function; there is no
> lung evidence at all; the gene is physically outside the locus window; and one of its few
> lung-adjacent claims was already debunked by the verifier. Any COPD-relevance claim for
> TMEM182 rests entirely on nearest-gene proximity plus an untested comorbidity narrative, not
> on the cited biology itself.

### CAPZBP1 (probable CAPZB pseudogene) — CONTESTED

> This is the clearest case of symbol conflation in the set. The credible-set annotation
> "CAPZBP1" resolves via HGNC/Ensembl to a ~200bp processed pseudogene (CAPZB pseudogene 1,
> ENSG00000231723) physically inside the locus window, annotated only on very-low-PIP (≤0.002),
> non-coding/downstream variants. All the literature actually cited (RCSD1-ABL1 leukemia
> fusions, CapZIP immune-cell signaling, RCSD1 LUAD/LUSC prognostic association) is about
> RCSD1, a real protein-coding gene on chromosome 1 with no documented trans-regulatory link to
> this chr2 pseudogene. The only bridge offered is that RCSD1's protein product physically
> interacts with the CapZ complex that CAPZB (the pseudogene's parent gene) is part of — a
> mechanistic non-sequitur for GWAS-locus attribution, since a protein-protein interaction
> between CapZIP and CapZ says nothing about the pseudogene's regulatory function or whether
> it's even transcribed/translated.
>
> This deserves essentially zero evidentiary weight for this locus. The RCSD1 literature (Qiao
> et al. 2022 LUAD/LUSC immune-infiltration finding is genuinely interesting biology, and the
> leukemia fusion literature is well-established) is real and correctly verified, but it should
> not be cited as evidence *for this locus* — it's evidence for a different gene on a different
> chromosome. If the input file's parenthetical "(also known as RCSD1)" is driving this
> attribution, that alias should be independently checked against HGNC's official synonym table
> before propagating further; nothing in the researcher's own genomic-identity work supports
> it.
>
> **CONTESTED** — not because the RCSD1 biology is wrong, but because attributing it to this
> locus is unsupported by the genomic facts already established by the researcher. Recommend
> treating CAPZBP1/RCSD1 as effectively no-evidence for this locus, and flag the input file's
> alias annotation as a likely error worth correcting upstream.

### SLC9A2 — WEAKENED (not CONTESTED, but reasoning preserved here given it materially affects the confidence call)

> The strongest evidence (Tu et al. 2026, PMID 42653451) is real and on-point — a lung MTWAS
> naming SLC9A2 as a top hit for a shared airway-disease latent factor. But several things
> weaken its ability to anchor a MODERATE/HIGH call: (a) it is a single, very recent (2026)
> study with no independent replication cited; (b) it is a cross-disease latent-factor TWAS
> ("gAirwayDisease" spanning COPD/asthma/bronchiectasis/CRSsNP), not a COPD-specific
> fine-mapping or colocalization result, so COPD-specific causality is unestablished; (c)
> SLC9A2 is ~390kb outside the stated locus window, and there is no L2G/coloc for any of the
> ~100 signals sharing this locus's lead variant, so there is no direct statistical link tying
> rs17343925 to SLC9A2 expression — PoPS/MAGMA prioritization is a similarity/proximity-weighted
> heuristic, not a causal-gene assay; (d) the paper's own immune-cell MTWAS axis (which would
> speak to prior #3) highlighted a different gene (IL18R1), not SLC9A2, so the "immune" pillar
> is essentially empty for this gene; (e) contradicting evidence exists in the verified record
> itself — Wang et al. 1993 (PMID 7685026) found lung was among the *lowest*-expressing tissues
> for NHE2/SLC9A2 mRNA, and the Sun et al. 2018 mucociliary-clearance phenotype (PMID 30300884)
> was produced by pan-NHE pharmacological inhibition, not an SLC9A2-isoform-specific
> manipulation — so isoform specificity for a lung mechanism is not actually demonstrated.
>
> On smoking vs. pulmonary-biology: nothing in the SLC9A2 evidence base engages smoking at all,
> so this gene's case (such as it is) is not smoking-confounded — but it also isn't clearly a
> "direct lung mechanism" case either, given the caveats above, especially the low native lung
> expression and lack of COPD-specific coloc.
>
> **WEAKENED** — the evidence is real and the single TWAS finding is a legitimate, verified
> signal worth reporting, but treating it as sufficient for MODERATE/HIGH confidence
> overreaches: it's one recent multi-disease study, not COPD-specific, with no coloc/L2G tying
> it to this exact locus, a 390kb physical gap from the window, and classic expression data
> arguing lung is a low-expression tissue for this transporter. This should be reported as a
> suggestive, hypothesis-generating signal, not a confidently prioritized gene.

## Locus-wide observations from the red-team-skeptic (verbatim)

> - The Open Targets pleiotropy pattern (~100 shared credible sets, dominated by
>   body-composition/anthropometric traits, secondary smoking-behavior and
>   airway-obstruction/bronchitis clusters, zero L2G/coloc anywhere, zero spirometry/FEV1
>   overlap on the exact lead SNP) is itself the strongest piece of evidence at this locus, and
>   it argues against a clean direct-pulmonary-biology story (prior #2 is explicitly unmet —
>   "COPD association without supporting pulmonary-function evidence warrants consideration of
>   alternative pathways"). Given TMEM182's muscle/adipose functional literature, an
>   **adiposity/body-composition-mediated pathway** (distinct from both smoking-mediation and
>   direct lung mechanism) is a coherent alternative hypothesis that should be weighed as a
>   third live hypothesis rather than defaulting to smoking-vs-pulmonary framing alone.
> - Both literature-prioritized genes (TMEM182, SLC9A2) sit physically outside the stated locus
>   window (80kb and 390kb respectively), and the two genes genuinely inside the window are a
>   pseudogene with no function evidence and an unannotated lncRNA. Combined with zero L2G/
>   coloc, this locus has essentially no clean causal-gene assignment — any single-gene call
>   here should be low confidence regardless of which gene is favored.
> - No gene at this locus should be called HIGH confidence on the current evidence; SLC9A2 is
>   the least-weak of the four but still only supports a modest/suggestive call for the reasons
>   above.
