### SENP7 — WEAKENED

The statistical case (nearest gene, ABC, top 3 PIP variants, lead variant PIP=0.127) is the
strongest local fine-mapping signal at this locus and is fairly presented as such, but the
researcher's synthesis risks conflating "best-supported gene for this locus's regulatory
signal" with "COPD candidate gene with a plausible mechanism" — these are not the same
claim.
- The Open Targets L2G cross-trait pleiotropy pattern is being asked to do more work than
  it can support. It is database-derived context, appropriately hedged by the researcher,
  but the actual pattern argues against a lung mechanism as much as for one: SENP7 is top
  L2G gene for blood counts, biochemical/anthropometric traits, clonal hematopoiesis, and
  skin conditions, but NO FEV1, FEV1/FVC, or smoking-behavior GWAS credible set tags these
  variants at all in Open Targets. This absence is directly relevant to the pipeline's
  spirometry-concordance prior and cuts against SENP7, not for it. A more honest framing:
  this locus behaves like a blood-cell-count/anthropometric/dermatologic pleiotropic locus
  that also shows up in COPD GWAS, plausibly via hematologic-trait mediation (e.g.,
  eosinophil/neutrophil counts, themselves COPD-subtype-relevant) rather than via a primary
  lung-tissue mechanism in SENP7. "Most plausible bridge to COPD biology" undersells how
  speculative and directionally two-sided this bridge is.
- Established SENP7 biology is genome-maintenance/meiosis, not lung or even generically
  immune — the one immune-adjacent citation (Liu 2025, PMID 40584830) is an incidental
  WGCNA-style "hub gene" correlate in kidney-transplant rejection, not a mechanistic study,
  and a second paper (Zhang 2025, PMID 40358846) reports a NEGATIVE finding for
  SENP7-immune-infiltration correlation specifically (SENP5, not SENP7, correlates). This
  negative finding deserves more prominence than the researcher gives it — it mildly
  contradicts any generic "SENP7 is immune-relevant" gloss.

Not CONTESTED (no competing causal-gene claim published), but the inference from
statistical prominence to biological plausibility overreaches as currently phrased.
Recommendation: pair "strongest candidate by fine-mapping" with "zero lung/COPD/FEV1/
smoking evidence of any kind, and the cross-trait pleiotropy pattern is not respiratory."

### NFKBIZ — WEAKENED (sharper than "report both, resolve neither")

NFKBIZ has ZERO credible-set variant overlap — not even a low-PIP passenger like PCNP or
TRMT10C, it was pulled in purely by PoPS (a genome-wide functional-similarity method, not a
local-association method). Open Targets corroborates: no genetic-association datatype
score at all for COPD, only a weak literature co-mention score. This is categorically
weaker evidentiary status than SENP7/IMPG2/PCNP.
- The most COPD-proximate citation used to support NFKBIZ (Jamieson 2018, PMID 30504421,
  IL-17C in COPD bronchial epithelium) does NOT name NFKBIZ/IκBζ as the mediator — the
  researcher is transparent about this, but the citation is still doing rhetorical work
  ("pathway-plausibility support") for a gene it never actually studied — a soft form of
  overclaiming (a pathway-adjacent paper doing work for a specific-gene claim).
- The strongest NFKBIZ biology (Tsoi 2015, Lorscheid 2019) is in psoriasis/skin, a
  different tissue and disease; the goat-mastitis paper (Capoferri 2021) is non-human/
  non-lung. Real IL-17/NF-κB pathway biology, tempting under the Th17-adjacent-programs
  prior, but pathway-analogy is not locus-specific genetic evidence.

Recommendation: treat NFKBIZ as biologically interesting as a pathway hypothesis but
genetically unsupported at THIS specific locus (no credible-set variant, PoPS-only, no OT
genetic-association score) — state this plainly rather than implying near-equipoise with
SENP7. Not CONTESTED in the sense of a contradicting paper (nothing argues against NFKBIZ
biology generally), but the case for placing it at this locus specifically is very thin.
