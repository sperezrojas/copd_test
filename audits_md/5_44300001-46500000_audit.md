# Audit trail — Locus 5:44300001-46500000 (nearest gene: MRPS30)

This appendix lists every citation excluded from the final evidence table
(`evidence_tables/5_44300001-46500000.md`) because its citation-verifier verdict was RETRACTED /
NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH, plus every gene the red-team-skeptic marked CONTESTED, with its
reasoning preserved verbatim. Intended for internal review only — do not distribute alongside
the evidence table.

## Excluded citations (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH)

1. **Smith BM, Traboulsi H, Austin JHM, et al. "Human airway branch variation and chronic
   obstructive pulmonary disease." Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A. 2018;115(5):E974-E981.**
   PMID 29339516, DOI 10.1073/pnas.1715564115.
   Original claim: a common central-airway branch variant associated with COPD among smokers,
   with narrower airway lumens, and "genetic polymorphisms within [FGF10]" implicated.
   **Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH.** Citation-verifier: "Citation exists (verified in PubMed).
   Abstract confirms airway branch variant associated with COPD in two cohorts (n=5,054),
   narrower airway lumens among smokers, BUT the abstract states 'genetic polymorphisms within
   thegene' (truncated/missing gene name—not 'FGF10'). The paper does NOT explicitly name FGF10
   in the abstract text retrieved. The researcher correctly flagged uncertainty about explicit
   FGF10 mention; claim overclaimed gene identification." Excluded from the evidence table; not
   used as FGF10-specific human-genetics support anywhere in the synthesis.

2. **Evans MJ, Fanucchi MV, Plopper CG, Hyde DM. "Postnatal development of the lamina
   reticularis in primate airways." Anat Rec (Hoboken). 2010;293(6):947-954.** PMID 20503389,
   DOI 10.1002/ar.20824.
   Original claim: FGF-10 binds perlecan in the airway basement membrane zone and is trafficked
   between epithelial and mesenchymal cell layers, relevant to basement-membrane thickening in
   airway remodeling.
   **Verdict: NOT_FOUND** (functionally a claim/source mismatch — the article itself exists but
   does not support the claim). Citation-verifier: "According to PubMed, article exists
   (Anat Rec (Hoboken) 2010;293(6):947-954, DOI 10.1002/ar.20824), BUT title is 'Postnatal
   development of the lamina reticularis in primate airways'—abstract does NOT mention FGF-10
   binding perlecan or basement membrane trafficking. Claim about FGF-10 and perlecan not
   supported by article title/abstract." Excluded entirely.

3. **Chiles JW, Rocco A, Srinivasasainagendra V, et al. "Whole Genome Sequence Analysis of
   Weight Loss in 16,972 Participants With COPD Reveals Novel Risk Loci in DRAIC and RFX3."
   J Cachexia Sarcopenia Muscle. 2026;17(3):e70293.** PMID 42026014, DOI 10.1002/jcsm.70293.
   Original claim: an intronic HCN1 variant was associated with weight-loss/cachexia risk in
   COPD patients of Black/African-American ancestry.
   **Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH.** Citation-verifier: "the PubMed record title... shows this paper
   focuses on DRAIC and RFX3 as novel loci... Full-text retrieval shows... HCN1 variant
   (chr5:45271359) mentioned but NOT as a novel/primary finding—title explicitly states DRAIC
   and RFX3 as the novel risk loci. HCN1 is a secondary/incidental mention, not a headline
   finding as claim implies." Excluded from the evidence table as HCN1-COPD support; the paper's
   actual headline genes (DRAIC, RFX3) are not candidate genes at this locus and are out of
   scope here.

4. **Chang LH et al. Drug Alcohol Depend. 2019;205:107704.** PMID 31731259,
   DOI 10.1016/j.drugalcdep.2019.107704.
   **Chang LH et al. Drug Alcohol Depend. 2019;197:271-279.** PMID 30875648,
   DOI 10.1016/j.drugalcdep.2019.01.015.
   These were submitted by the researcher explicitly as documented "search misses" (real papers,
   confirmed by citation-verifier to exist and be non-retracted, but confirmed to NOT mention
   HCN1) — included here for transparency only, not excluded due to any integrity problem, but
   kept out of the evidence table because they provide no HCN1-supporting or HCN1-refuting
   content.

## Genes marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning)

### HCN1 — CONTESTED

> "The single citation offered as direct HCN1-COPD literature evidence (Chiles et al. 2026,
> PMID 42026014) is CLAIM_MISMATCH: the paper's actual novel loci are DRAIC and RFX3; HCN1
> appears only as a secondary intronic hit associated with a *different phenotype*
> (weight-loss/cachexia, not airflow obstruction), in a single ancestry stratum, unreplicated
> elsewhere. This citation should not appear in any final table as 'HCN1 associated with COPD'
> — that would be a claim strengthened well beyond what the source supports. There is otherwise
> zero pulmonary-structural literature and zero direct literature confirmation of the
> HCN1-smoking-GWAS link the researcher went looking for (an active, appropriate check that
> came back empty).
>
> The Open Targets structured context itself is the strongest source of
> contradicting/alternative-explanation evidence here, and it cuts against a direct-lung-
> mechanism story: at the identical variant (rs62367470), HCN1 dominates L2G for smoking
> initiation/cessation/status and educational-attainment/behavioral traits (~5x over MRPS30),
> and the COPD credible set colocalizes strongly with those same smoking/behavioral traits (h4
> 0.85-0.93) while showing near-zero coloc with height/blood pressure (arguing against generic
> pleiotropy/pseudo-signal). This is close to a textbook case of prior (a): a locus whose COPD
> association plausibly runs through smoking-initiation/nicotine-dependence neurobiology rather
> than lung biology, especially given HCN1's well-established, unrelated roles in neuronal
> excitability and cardiac pacemaking (both verified, both non-pulmonary). HCN channels are also
> mechanistically plausible players in neural reward/dependence circuits, which would make this
> a coherent (if indirect) COPD-risk pathway.
>
> Compounding this, even the COPD signal's gene assignment is internally unstable: L2G
> *disagrees* between the two source COPD GWAS (0.64 vs 0.13 for HCN1 in one; 0.46 vs 0.69 in
> the other, favoring MRPS30), and colocalization between the two COPD studies at this variant
> is only weak-moderate (h4=0.44) — the two COPD GWAS don't clearly agree with each other, let
> alone with a single causal gene.
>
> Taken together: the one dedicated COPD citation is undermined by the verifier, there is no
> lung-structural or spirometric literature at all (a real gap under prior b), and there is a
> well-supported, plausible non-lung alternative pathway (smoking-behavior mediation) actively
> competing with a direct-lung-mechanism story, on top of unstable/disagreeing statistical gene
> assignment. I judge this CONTESTED — not because a paper explicitly refutes HCN1, but because
> the combination of a mismatched primary citation, a strong competing gene (MRPS30) at the
> identical variant, and OT-native evidence for a smoking-mediated alternative pathway together
> constitute credible grounds to doubt a direct-lung-biology causal role for HCN1 at this locus.
> (This is inferred from the structured OT context and the verifier's CLAIM_MISMATCH
> determination, not a new independent literature find — I could not run the smoking-GWAS
> search myself due to budget exhaustion, so this should be treated as moderately, not maximally,
> confident.)"

### MRPS30 — CONTESTED

> "The researcher's own conclusion here is candid and I agree with it: MRPS30 has no
> lung-specific, COPD-specific, or spirometry-specific literature support of any kind. Both
> statistical-genetics citations (Guo 2018, Guan 2014) are legitimate papers but establish
> MRPS30 as an effector gene at an entirely separate, independent 5p12 breast-cancer GWAS locus
> — real biology, wrong disease and (presumably) wrong tissue context; this should not be
> cross-applied to lung as if it were transferable evidence, and the researcher did not claim it
> was, which is appropriate restraint to note.
>
> MRPS30's candidacy as the COPD gene at this locus rests entirely on L2G favoring it in one of
> two COPD GWAS studies (0.69 vs 0.13 for HCN1 in that study; the other study favors HCN1 0.64
> vs 0.13 for MRPS30) — i.e., the two primary data sources disagree on which gene is even more
> likely, and inter-study colocalization is weak (h4=0.44). There is no mitoribosomal/apoptosis
> biology (housekeeping structural protein, verified but non-lung-signaling) that offers a
> plausible mechanistic story for airflow obstruction or COPD-relevant inflammation, and no
> immune literature at all. The one thread that gestures toward lung relevance — Wang et al.
> 2022 (PMID 35191803) noting in passing, secondhand, that MRPS30-DT lncRNA 'is recently
> reported to promote lung cancer' — is a tangential citation-of-a-citation the researcher
> explicitly did not verify further upstream; it should not be treated as MRPS30 lung evidence
> and should be flagged as such rather than allowed to quietly read as 'prior lung literature
> exists.'
>
> I judge this CONTESTED: the credible contradicting evidence is the competing gene assignment
> for HCN1 at the identical variant (from Open Targets structured data, already in hand, not
> requiring new UNVERIFIED citation flagging) combined with total absence of independent
> biological corroboration and gnomAD LoF-tolerance (oe~0.96) that offers no dosage-sensitivity
> rationale either. This reads as the 'nearest-gene / LD-proximity' scenario the priors
> explicitly ask reviewers to watch for, and the researcher's own hedge language ('may instead
> be attributable to LD/regulatory proximity to HCN1') should be preserved verbatim in the audit
> trail rather than softened in synthesis."

## Other caveats raised by red-team-skeptic (not gene-specific exclusions, but relevant to interpreting the evidence table)

- The red-team-skeptic's web-search tool budget was exhausted before it could run any
  independent contradiction searches for this locus; its critique was based on close reading of
  the researcher's findings, the verifier's verdicts, and the Open Targets structured context
  already supplied, not fresh literature search. This is disclosed by the skeptic itself and
  should be treated as a residual gap — a future re-run with a fresh search budget could
  strengthen or weaken the CONTESTED calls above, particularly for HCN1's smoking-GWAS link.
- No gene at this locus (FGF10, HCN1, or MRPS30) has any direct FEV1/FEV1-FVC spirometric-trait
  literature — this gap applies uniformly across all three candidates and is not unique to the
  weaker ones.
- For FGF10, the immune evidence that does exist (asthma Th2/eosinophilic model, PMID 37956773;
  ARDS/acute-lung-injury model, PMID 41482636) should not be read as COPD-specific
  neutrophilic/CD8+ T-cell evidence — both are HOLD verdicts but for adjacent, not identical,
  disease contexts.
