# Audit trail (excluded/contested appendix): locus 6_26000001-32800000 (HLA-DQA1)

This file preserves what was EXCLUDED from the final evidence table
(`evidence_tables/6_26000001-32800000.md`) and WHY, plus every gene/citation
the red-team-skeptic flagged CONTESTED, with reasoning preserved verbatim,
so a reviewer can see the full trail without needing this file to trust the
evidence table itself.

## Scope decision (orchestrator, before research began)

Per explicit task instructions for this locus, candidate-gene research was
restricted to 5 genes — HLA-DQA1 (nearest gene/lead+missense variant),
HLA-DQB1 and HLA-DRB1 (immediately flanking classical class II genes in tight
LD with HLA-DQA1), CSNK2B (MAGMA-prioritized), and HIST1H4A/H4C3
(PoPS-prioritized) — rather than exhaustively enumerating every gene across
the full 6.8 Mb MHC window (dozens to hundreds of genes, out of reasonable
scope given only 17 credible-set variants, all clustered in a 24 kb span at
HLA-DQA1).

## Excluded citations

**Tang J et al. 2018/2019, "Genetic susceptibility to Tuberculosis:
Interaction between HLA-DQA1 and age of onset," Infection, Genetics and
Evolution, PMID 30553063, DOI 10.1016/j.meegid.2018.12.014.**
- Claimed by researcher: rs9272785 showed suggestive association with
  young-onset (20-40y) tuberculosis in a Chinese cohort.
- Citation-verifier verdict: **NOT_FOUND (claim not verifiable)**. The paper's
  existence was confirmed (correct title, authors, journal, PMID via PubMed

No other citations were rejected as NOT_FOUND, RETRACTED, or CLAIM_MISMATCH;
14 of 15 citations submitted for verification were VERIFIED (one, the

important caveat, preserved in the evidence table itself since it is a
legitimate, verified background finding rather than an exclusion).

## Genes/findings marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic (reasoning preserved verbatim)

### HLA-DRB1 — CONTESTED

> This is the one gene in the table with a direct internal contradiction.
> Díaz-Peña 2020 (Chile, N=214/193) reports HLA-DRB1*01:02 as a COPD risk
> allele (OR=2.07), unreplicated elsewhere. Karzakova 2017 (Chuvash silicon
> workers, N=50/38) reports HLA-DRB1*01 (a related but distinct allele
> designation) as *protective* against COPD in a different population and
> exposure context (occupational silica, not general smoking-related COPD).
> Both studies are small, underpowered, single-cohort, and not independently
> replicated; one reports risk, the other protection, for closely related
> alleles at the same locus. The Drosos 2025 citation is RA-specific
> (shared-epitope/citrullination pathway) and doesn't bear on general COPD at
> all.
>
> **Verdict: CONTESTED.** Flag for the evidence table: two small,
> non-replicated, non-overlapping cohort studies report opposite-direction
> effects for closely related DRB1*01-family alleles on COPD/lung function.
> This should not be treated as converging evidence — it should be treated as
> an open contradiction. (Both citations were already independently verified
> by the citation-verifier per the input; this is not a new unverified source,
> just flagging the internal conflict as CONTESTED rather than as
> corroborating evidence.)

Both underlying citations (Díaz-Peña et al. 2020, PMID 32948369; Karzakova et
al. 2017, PMID 30615373) remain VERIFIED as citations (i.e., they accurately
report what their source papers say) and are retained in the evidence table
with the CONTESTED flag attached, rather than being excluded outright — the
contradiction itself is the finding that needs to reach the study team, not a
reason to hide either study.

### HLA-DQA1 — WEAKENED (not excluded, but flagged; reasoning preserved)

> The statistical case is real...but the gene-level causal attribution is
> weaker than the evidence table implies... The strongest population-scale
> spirometry/COPD evidence cited (Hancock 2012, Wain 2015, John 2022) is for
> lead SNPs (rs7764819, rs9274600, rs9273410) that are intergenic or nearer
> HLA-DQB1/DQA2, not inside HLA-DQA1. None of these papers establish that the
> same causal signal underlies rs9272466. In a region with this much extended
> LD, citing DQB1/DQA2-proximal GWAS hits as support for DQA1 specifically is
> exactly the nearest-gene bias the reviewer brief warns about... Nearly every
> mechanistic citation across DQA1/DQB1/DRB1 is borrowed from a different
> disease (TB, celiac, T1D, RA) and then implicitly presented as illuminating
> COPD biology. None of the citations directly test HLA-DQ/DR-mediated antigen
> presentation as a mechanism in COPD lung tissue or COPD immune cells.

### HLA-DQB1 — WEAKENED (not excluded, but flagged; reasoning preserved)

> Essentially the same LD/attribution problem as DQA1, arguably worse: the
> actual lead SNPs in the cited GWAS papers sit nearer HLA-DQA2, a gene that
> isn't even in this pipeline's candidate list. The Oka 2016 finding
> (DQB1*03:01 risk / *03:02 protective for bronchiectatic emphysema) is
> entirely conditioned on a rheumatoid arthritis patient population, not
> general-population COPD, so it's evidence for an RA-lung disease pathway,
> not COPD per se.

### CSNK2B and HIST1H4A/H4C3 — effectively refuted / deprioritized (HOLDS as null findings)

> CSNK2B: No COPD-specific literature at all; Open Targets shows zero evidence
> of any kind... The researcher's own conclusion — that this is most
> parsimoniously explained as a MAGMA statistical artifact of dense regional
> LD/gene density rather than real CSNK2B biology — is appropriately cautious
> and matches the evidence.
>
> HIST1H4A/H4C3: Same pattern: zero COPD/lung-specific literature, zero Open
> Targets evidence, not constrained (oe=1.76), 6.6 Mb from the credible-set
> cluster, and confounded by multiple near-identical H4 paralogs in the HIST1
> cluster that make single-paralog attribution from PoPS/expression data alone
> inherently unreliable.

The orchestrator preserves this disclosure verbatim because it materially
qualifies the critique above: the WEAKENED/CONTESTED/HOLDS verdicts reflect a
careful re-analysis of already-assembled, already-verified evidence, not a
fresh independent literature search for contradicting findings. A future
re-run of this locus with available search budget could usefully re-attempt
an independent contradiction search before any higher-confidence claim is
made about this locus.

## Cross-cutting caveat carried into the main evidence table

MHC extended LD undermines any single-gene causal claim at this locus with
unusual force (more than the standard genome-wide caveat), and the primary
COPD GWAS this pipeline is built on (Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019, PMID
30804561) itself excludes/segregates the MHC region from its standard locus
list — see the evidence table's locus-level background row for the verified
citation supporting this.

## ADDENDUM (2026-09-11): HSPA1L checked and ruled out following corrected MAGMA/PoPS gene-location re-merge

**Trigger:** The study team discovered a genome-build error in the
MAGMA/PoPS gene-location merge file used to generate
`loci/copd-all-gene-prioritization.txt`. A corrected re-merge surfaced
HSPA1L as a candidate gene newly attributed to this locus, which was never
evaluated in the original review above. This addendum documents the check
performed on HSPA1L specifically, per explicit task instructions to verify
physical overlap with the actual credible-set span before dispatching any
further research.

**Coordinate check (Open Targets Platform GraphQL, `target(ensemblId:
"ENSG00000204390")`, GRCh38):**

- **HSPA1L:** chr6:31,809,619–31,815,788 (minus strand). This places HSPA1L
  in the MHC class III region, immediately adjacent to TNF/HSPA1A/HSPA1B —
  not in the class II region.
- **This locus's actual 17-variant credible set:** chr6:32,619,017–32,642,624
  (a tight ~24 kb span), all variants at or immediately flanking HLA-DQA1
  (MHC class II region) — see `loci/6_26000001-32800000.md` and the original
  evidence table's credible-set summary.
- **Gap between HSPA1L and the nearest edge of the credible set:**
  32,619,017 − 31,815,788 ≈ **803 kb**.

**Conclusion: HSPA1L ruled out for this locus's actual credible-set span.**
An ~800 kb gap is substantial even by the standards of MHC extended LD, and
it crosses the well-documented class III/class II boundary — a region where
recombination is known to attenuate LD relative to the extreme LD found
*within* each class (e.g., within the tight HLA-DQA1/DQB1/DRB1 class II
block, or within the TNF/HSPA1A/HSPA1B/HSPA1L class III block). HSPA1L falls
inside the pipeline's broad 6.8 Mb candidate window (26.0–32.8 Mb) only
because that window is intentionally wide and gene-dense across the whole
extended MHC; it is not physically or regulatorily implicated by this
locus's 17-variant fine-mapping signal, which clusters entirely within a 24
kb span roughly 800 kb away, on the other side of the class II/class III
recombination boundary, near HLA-DQA1. This is precisely the scope boundary
the original review's "IMPORTANT SCOPE AND CAUTION NOTE" anticipated: the
6.8 Mb window is far too gene-dense to exhaustively review, and candidate-gene
research was deliberately restricted to genes at/immediately flanking the
actual credible-set cluster.

**Action taken:** No researcher, citation-verifier, or red-team-skeptic
dispatch was made for HSPA1L — per task instructions, a locus that fails
this physical-overlap check is ruled out at this stage without further
literature review. The final prioritized-gene call in
`evidence_tables/6_26000001-32800000.md` is **unchanged**: HLA-DQA1/HLA-DQB1/
HLA-DRB1 haplotype block, confidence LOW, no single gene named. This
addendum is recorded here for traceability in case the corrected gene list
is audited again in the future.
