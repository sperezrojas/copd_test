# Audit appendix — locus 11:107,100,001-107,550,000 (GRCh38)

Excluded citations (CLAIM_MISMATCH / RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND) and CONTESTED/WEAKENED gene
verdicts, with reasoning preserved verbatim from citation-verifier and red-team-skeptic.
Kept separate from `evidence_tables/11_107100001-107550000.md` so the evidence table can be
shared without also sharing this internal audit trail.

## Excluded citations

### CWF19L2

**Chen L, Ye X, Li Y, Ran X. "Systematic identification of therapeutic targets for
coronary artery calcification: an integrated transcriptomic and proteomic Mendelian
randomization." Frontiers in Cardiovascular Medicine, 2024. PMID 39526184 / DOI
10.3389/fcvm.2024.1419440.**
- Claim as originally reported by researcher: CWF19L2 expression showed a
  colocalization-supported positive MR association with coronary artery calcification risk,
  using eQTLGen cis-eQTL instruments.
- Verdict: **CLAIM_MISMATCH**
- Reason (citation-verifier): Article exists (confirmed via PubMed), no retraction. Abstract
  confirms positive MR association: "we observed positive associations between CWF19L2,
  JARID2, and MANBA and the risk of CAC." However, the abstract does NOT explicitly mention
  "colocalization-supported" mechanism or "eQTLGen cis-eQTL instruments" as specified in the
  claim — the methodological details claimed are not supported in the abstract text
  provided.
- Note: even if verified, this concerns coronary artery calcification, a non-pulmonary
  trait, and would only be weak precedent that CWF19L2 expression is MR/coloc-amenable, not
  COPD-relevant evidence.

### ALKBH8

**Feng S, Xu Z, Peng J, Zhang M. "The AlkB Family: Potential Prognostic Biomarkers and
Therapeutic Targets in Glioblastoma." Frontiers in Oncology, 2022. PMID 35371987 / DOI
10.3389/fonc.2022.847821.**
- Claim as originally reported by researcher: ALKBH8 upregulation is associated with worse
  overall survival and correlates with tumor immune infiltration in glioblastoma
  bioinformatic analyses.
- Verdict: **CLAIM_MISMATCH**
- Reason (citation-verifier): Article exists, no retraction. Abstract confirms ALKBH8
  upregulation and worse survival: "ALKBH8 were significantly up-regulated in GBM" and
  "patients exhibiting elevated ALKBH8 levels demonstrated notably worse survival outcomes."
  However, the claim's specific assertion of "correlation with tumor immune infiltration"
  for ALKBH8 is not explicitly confirmed — the abstract mentions the AlkB family's general
  role in "immune system responses within tumors" but not an ALKBH8-specific immune
  infiltration correlation.
- Note: non-pulmonary (glioblastoma) regardless of verdict.

**Honda K, Hase H, Tanikawa S, et al. "ALKBH8 contributes to neurological function through
oxidative stress regulation." PNAS Nexus, 2024. PMID 38550277 / DOI
10.1093/pnasnexus/pgae115.**
- Claim as originally reported by researcher: Alkbh8-knockout mice show reduced brain
  weight, ischemic cortical/hippocampal pathology, behavioral deficits, and reduced
  mitochondrial membrane potential in neurons/glia via an oxidative-stress mechanism.
- Verdict: **CLAIM_MISMATCH**
- Reason (citation-verifier): Article exists, no retraction. Abstract confirms "cognitive
  and motor impairments, along with structural brain changes" and reduced mitochondrial
  membrane potential in neurons/glia via an oxidative-stress-regulatory mechanism. However,
  the specific claimed findings of "reduced brain weight" and "ischemic cortical/
  hippocampal pathology" are not explicitly confirmed in the abstract text.
- Note: non-pulmonary (neurological); the underlying oxidative-stress/selenoprotein
  mechanism is the same pathway implicated in the (verified) lung naphthalene paper
  (Leonardi et al. 2020, PMID 32303148), but this specific citation's claimed pathological
  details are not abstract-confirmed.

## Gene-level verdicts (red-team-skeptic)

### CWF19L2 — WEAKENED

"Colocalization + convergent prioritization is real and gene-discriminating (vs. ALKBH8),
but it is non-lung-tissue QTL evidence with zero direct human COPD/lung-function/smoking
literature; treat as 'statistically prioritized, mechanistically unconfirmed in lung,' not
as an established lung-disease gene."

Full reasoning (verbatim from red-team-skeptic): The strongest evidence is the Open Targets
background — two independent CWF19L2 exon-usage-QTL colocalizations (GTEx adipose-visceral
H4=0.995, GEUVADIS LCL H4=0.906) with the COPD GWAS signal (GCST90476027), plus three-way
convergence of nearest-gene, FLAMES, and MAGMA prioritization. Open Targets found zero
colocalizing QTLs for ALKBH8 anywhere in the region, a meaningful discriminating result.
Tempering factors: the colocalizing QTLs are in adipose and lymphoblastoid tissue, not
lung — tissue relevance to COPD is inferred, not direct. The credible set's high-PIP mass
sits ~50-60kb upstream, intergenic; the low-PIP tail inside CWF19L2 introns (each <0.007
PIP) is cumulatively negligible and doesn't meaningfully strengthen the case beyond the
coloc signal. Zero PubMed literature connects CWF19L2 to lung, COPD, spirometry, or
smoking (confirmed independently by the skeptic). The functional biology found
(spliceosome debranching, spermatogenesis, retinal splicing) is real but entirely
non-pulmonary; the researcher's own framing of lung relevance as "extrapolation, not
established evidence" was judged appropriately hedged, not overreach. No contradicting
paper or alternative-gene claim at this specific locus was found. Smoking-mediation:
absence of smoking/behavioral literature plus a coloc signal tied directly to a COPD/
airway-obstruction phenotype (not psychiatric/behavioral) makes smoking-behavior mediation
seem less likely than for a typical nicotinic-pathway locus, but this is absence-of-evidence
since GSCAN summary statistics were never directly queried. Flagged as a real thoroughness
gap worth closing before finalizing, though it doesn't change the skeptic's read of current
evidence.

### ALKBH8 — WEAKENED, bordering CONTESTED

"Not because a rival gene is directly implicated by name, but because the
statistical-genetics evidence (no coloc, ~10x weaker L2G, non-nearest, non-FLAMES/MAGMA)
actively argues against ALKBH8 relative to its neighbor, and the sole supporting
mechanistic paper is a single non-human, non-smoking toxicology study whose relevance to
human COPD is inferential."

Full reasoning (verbatim from red-team-skeptic): The case rests on three legs, none
locus-specific: (1) PoPS, a genome-wide functional-similarity method that by design doesn't
require a locus-specific eQTL/coloc signal; (2) zero colocalization for any ALKBH8 QTL in
this region per Open Targets, and ~10x lower L2G than CWF19L2; (3) a single mouse
toxicology paper (Leonardi 2020, PMID 32303148, VERIFIED) showing Alkbh8-deficient mouse
lungs have increased oxidative stress and heightened susceptibility to naphthalene-induced
injury — suggestive given naphthalene is a smoke/combustion-relevant PAH, but a
knockout-mouse exposure study, not human genetics, not COPD, and not smoking per se;
extrapolating it to "supports ALKBH8 as the COPD-relevant gene at this locus" is a real
inferential leap the researcher appropriately labeled as lacking human corroboration, but
which could be overclaimed downstream if not handled carefully. Two of seven ALKBH8
citations are CLAIM_MISMATCH (vs. one of six for CWF19L2) — a higher mismatch rate that,
combined with reliance on oncology/neurodevelopmental/osteoarthritis bioinformatic-
correlation papers, suggests some reach toward tangential literature to construct an
immune/lung narrative where direct evidence is absent (flagged as a locus-wide caution, not
unique to ALKBH8, since neither gene has direct COPD literature). LD/nearest-gene bias cuts
against ALKBH8: it sits 50-100kb farther from the credible set than CWF19L2, is not
nearest/FLAMES/MAGMA-prioritized, and has no colocalizing molecular QTL of its own.
Long-range enhancer action reaching past CWF19L2 to ALKBH8 is not impossible, but nothing in
the evidence supports this over the simpler proximity + coloc explanation favoring CWF19L2.
Smoking-mediation: no human smoking-behavior literature found; the naphthalene mechanism is
a gene-by-environment (toxicant exposure) hypothesis rather than a smoking-initiation/
behavioral pathway, so if this locus matters for ALKBH8 it would more plausibly be
smoking-exposure-modifying than smoking-behavior-mediated. No new contradicting citation
was surfaced requiring additional citation-verifier passes.

## Locus-wide caveats flagged by red-team-skeptic

- Neither CWF19L2 nor ALKBH8 has any published human COPD, spirometry, or
  smoking-behavior-GWAS association in the literature searched (PubMed, bioRxiv/medRxiv,
  WebSearch, GWAS Catalog). The entire case for prioritizing either gene rests on
  statistical/functional genomics (Open Targets L2G + colocalization) plus extrapolated,
  non-pulmonary mechanistic biology — this should be treated as a locus where confidence is
  inherently limited by an absence of disease-specific literature, not a gap unique to one
  candidate.
- GSCAN (smoking-initiation/cessation/cigarettes-per-day) summary statistics were never
  directly queried by either the researcher or red-team-skeptic (only GWAS Catalog web
  lookups and PubMed/WebSearch, which returned no hits) — this is flagged as an open
  thoroughness gap that a follow-up review with direct GSCAN summary-statistics access
  could close.
- The red-team-skeptic's own GWAS Catalog API lookup for rs1388168 returned a 404 error, so
  the researcher's "no trait associations found" claim for the lead variant could not be
  independently re-confirmed by the skeptic (though it was not contradicted either).
