## Locus-level data-quality note (from researcher)

The researcher flagged that SAP30L (ENSG00000164576), the MAGMA-prioritized gene for this
locus, is physically located at 5:154,445,976-154,461,053 — approximately 246kb beyond the
stated locus end (154,200,000) — and did not appear in a direct Ensembl overlap query of the
locus interval itself. MAGMA gene-based tests use a SNP-to-gene annotation window that can
extend gene assignment beyond a locus's nominal fine-mapping boundary, which is a plausible
explanation, but this is recorded as an unresolved discrepancy rather than an error, per the
researcher's own framing.

The researcher also independently identified, via direct Ensembl region query (not via any
of the internal pipeline's four prioritization methods), three additional protein-coding
genes physically in or at the edge of the stated locus: FAM114A2, MFAP3, and GALNT10. These
were researched and are included in the evidence table for completeness, but none carry
internal-pipeline prioritization support.

## Genes marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning)

### SAP30L — CONTESTED

"Two independent problems. First, tissue/phenotype relevance: the only functional citation
(Teittinen 2012, PMID 22821512) shows cardiac/hematopoietic zebrafish defects — not lung,
not immune/inflammatory in the COPD sense. The remaining six citations (PMID 16820529,
18070604, 19015240, 26609676, 29599190, 32294434) are pure Sin3A/HDAC biochemistry with no
disease phenotype at all — corepressor-complex structural biology being used to backfill a
gene with literally zero COPD genetic-association evidence in Open Targets. Second, and more
serious: SAP30L sits ~246kb outside the stated locus boundary (154,445,976-154,461,053 vs.
locus end 154,200,000) and did not even appear in the direct Ensembl overlap query for the
interval. MAGMA gene-based tests aggregate SNP signal in a window around a gene body
(typically ±10-50kb, occasionally larger), so it's possible the association is driven by
boundary-proximal SNPs, but a candidate gene whose body sits nearly a quarter-megabase away
from the credible-set/locus definition, with zero disease-relevant literature and zero Open
Targets support, is a weak nomination on physical-proximity grounds alone — genuine reason
to distrust the MAGMA prioritization here rather than treat it as equally-weighted alongside
GRIA1. Verdict: CONTESTED — both on physical-distance/LD grounds and on total absence of
tissue-relevant biology."

### LINC01470 — CONTESTED (for the "regulates GRIA1" inference specifically)

"Carries the lead variant and nearly the entire credible set, but has zero independent
literature and zero Open Targets evidence. The researcher's 'bystander/positional
annotation, most plausibly regulating GRIA1' framing is explicitly labeled inference, which
is appropriate — but this is pure speculation with no eQTL, no Hi-C/chromatin-interaction
data, and no expression data cited to support the 'regulates GRIA1' hypothesis specifically
(as opposed to regulating SAP30L, FAM114A2, MFAP3, or nothing at all). Long intronic/
non-coding credible sets like this are exactly the scenario where the nearest-protein-coding-
gene assumption is weakest. 'Most plausibly GRIA1' should not be treated as established — it
is one of several equally unsupported guesses. Verdict: CONTESTED for the specific 'regulates
GRIA1' inference (not for the gene's existence/role generally, which the researcher correctly
declines to claim)."

### MFAP3 — CONTESTED

"Clean case of paralog substitution: the only lung-relevant citation (Zhu et al. 2020, PMID
32572962) is about MFAP4, not MFAP3, showing emphysema-like pathology in Mfap4-knockout
mice. The researcher correctly flags this, and correctly notes MFAP3 itself has no
lung-specific knockout phenotype anywhere and is 'ubiquitously expressed.' The original
cloning paper (Abrams 1995, PMID 7782085) locates MFAP3 in ocular-zonule microfibrils — eye
tissue, not lung, and 30 years old with no follow-up disease association. There is no
genuine MFAP3-COPD or MFAP3-lung-function evidence at all; the entire case is 'sits near a
locus, has a paralog with an unrelated emphysema phenotype.' That is not evidence for MFAP3,
it's an absence of evidence dressed up by proximity. Verdict: CONTESTED."

### GALNT10 — CONTESTED

"Only overlaps the last ~10kb of the locus (mostly outside it), and its only citations are
three oncology papers in unrelated organs (gastric PMID 33275228, cholangiocarcinoma PMID
33301605, HCC PMID 25422324). The researcher's implicit rationale — GALNT10 is a
mucin-glycosylation enzyme and mucin biology is COPD-relevant — is a plausible mechanistic
hypothesis but is not supported by any citation actually shown; it's pattern-matching from
gene function class, not literature. No lung/airway/mucociliary citation was found despite
what should be a fairly directed search space (airway mucin biology is a well-studied COPD
theme). Reads as a gene included for completeness with essentially no supporting evidence.
Verdict: CONTESTED."

## Genes marked WEAKENED (not full CONTESTED, but flagged for overclaiming risk) — verbatim reasoning

### GRIA1 — WEAKENED, bordering on CONTESTED

"The researcher's own account undercuts the headline claim. Every statistical-genetics
citation for GRIA1 is a 'could not confirm' (Sakornsakolpat PMID 30804561, Shrine PMID
36914875, Saunders/GSCAN PMID 36477530) except one: Nievergelt et al. 2024 (PMID 38637617),
a PTSD GWAS that names GRIA1 as a prioritized gene in an entirely different phenotype. The
'smoking-mediated' framing is built on (a) a PTSD gene list, (b) a paralog (GRIA4, PMID
30287806) associated with nicotine-dependence/depression comorbidity, and (c) a CNS
mouse-genetics paper on alcohol relapse (PMID 26609150) — none of which is GRIA1-COPD,
GRIA1-lung-function, or GRIA1-smoking-GWAS evidence at *this* locus. The neuronal/
psychiatric literature is more plausible than the (nonexistent) pulmonary literature, but
'consistent with a smoking-behavior-mediated mechanism' overstates what's shown — it should
read 'no direct pulmonary evidence exists, and the only positive genetic signal for this
gene family is in neuropsychiatric/addiction traits, which is suggestive but not a confirmed
smoking-GWAS overlap at this specific locus.' The airway-smooth-muscle papers (PMID
25888577, 27371735, 22447835) are explicitly NMDA/GRIN receptors, not AMPA/GRIA1 —
correctly flagged by the researcher as off-target, but this is a receptor-subtype mismatch,
a classic overclaiming trap (glutamate receptor superfamily != GRIA1-specific mechanism).
Given (1) zero direct GRIA1-COPD or GRIA1-lung-function evidence, (2) zero confirmed overlap
with smoking-behavior GWAS loci, and (3) a credible set 100% annotated to LINC01470 rather
than GRIA1 itself, GRIA1's case rests entirely on nearest-gene + FLAMES/PoPS computational
prioritization plus indirect family/PTSD-list inference. Verdict: WEAKENED, bordering on
CONTESTED given total absence of both pulmonary and confirmed-smoking evidence for GRIA1
itself."

### FAM114A2 — WEAKENED

"Not flagged by internal prioritization, found only by manual Ensembl query — should be
weighted below the internally-prioritized genes. The De et al. 2022 citation (PMID 34380996)
is about anti-TNFalpha nonresponse in IBD via a FAM114A2 eQTL variant (rs34767465) and
macrophage autophagy/TNFalpha secretion — a plausible inflammatory mechanism (macrophage/TNF
axis is relevant to COPD's innate-immune programs per the locus prior on inflammatory/immune
complexity), but different disease, different variant, and critically no LD check was done
between rs34767465 and this locus's credible set. Without that, this is a 'same gene symbol,
different signal' risk — cannot be used as if it were locus-specific evidence. Ding et al.
2025 (PMID 41131870, HIV-latency/PKC-beta-NF-kB) is essentially irrelevant to COPD and reads
like a keyword-match citation. Verdict: WEAKENED — the TNF/autophagy mechanism is
biologically interesting and worth flagging for follow-up (specifically an LD/coloc check
against rs34767465), but as presented it is not locus-specific evidence and shouldn't be
scored as if it were."
