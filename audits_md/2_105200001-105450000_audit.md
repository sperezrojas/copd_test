# Audit trail — locus 2:105,200,001-105,450,000 (nearest gene C2orf49)

This file documents every excluded citation (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH) and
every gene the red-team-skeptic marked CONTESTED, with reasoning preserved verbatim, for
locus 2_105200001-105450000. Not intended for distribution alongside the evidence table
without this context.

## Excluded citations

### RETRACTED

**PMID 31085559** — Lee CM, He CH, Park JW, et al. "Chitinase 1 regulates pulmonary
fibrosis by modulating TGF-β/SMAD7 pathway via TGFBRAP1 and FOXO3." Life Science
Alliance, 2019.

- Original claim: CHIT1 (chitinase 1) enhances TGF-β1-driven fibrotic responses by
  suppressing the TGF-β feedback inhibitor SMAD7, acting through direct interaction with
  TGFBRAP1 and FOXO3; CHIT1 levels reported elevated and inversely correlated with SMAD7
  in IPF and scleroderma-associated interstitial lung disease tissue.
- Citation-verifier's finding: "According to PubMed, article_types field includes
  'Retracted Publication'. Publication date: 2019-05-13."
- Correction notice: PMID 37037591 (2023-04-10, "Published Erratum" article type) —
  preceded the full retraction.
- Retraction notice: PMID 39209538 (2024-08-29, "Retraction Notice" article type) —
  full retraction of the original paper.
- Why excluded: this is the foundational paper for the entire CHIT1-TGFBRAP1-FOXO3-SMAD7
  pulmonary-fibrosis mechanistic model. Its retraction means the specific claim that
  TGFBRAP1 is a functionally important mediator of CHIT1-driven pulmonary fibrosis rests
  on invalid primary data. Two further papers (PMID 35679109, PMID 35370755) that remain
  formally non-retracted nonetheless depend on the same disputed CHIT1-TGFBRAP1
  interaction and are flagged CONTESTED in the main evidence table rather than treated
  as independent support (see TGFBRAP1 CONTESTED reasoning below).

### CLAIM_MISMATCH (round 2 — GWAS Catalog citations surfaced by red-team-skeptic)

4. **PMID 39715877** (Akimova et al. 2024) — claimed: rs3087523 and occupational

transparency, but the final synthesis in the main evidence table does not treat it as

## Genes marked CONTESTED / WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning)

### TGFBRAP1 — CONTESTED

> "The researcher already identified the critical fact: the most lung-specific
> mechanistic literature (CHIT1–TGFBRAP1–SMAD7 axis in pulmonary fibrosis) is built on a
> retracted foundational paper (PMID 31085559, retracted 2024 after a 2023 correction),
> and the one non-retracted paper claiming TGFBRAP1 knockout 'phenocopies' antifibrotic
> effects (PMID 35679109) is mechanistically entangled with the same disputed
> CHIT1-TGFBRAP1 interaction — so it should not be treated as independent replication.
> This is a textbook case of the 'pattern of rejected/retracted citations for a gene'
> being informative in itself. Add to this: TGFBRAP1's other described function
> (CORVET/HOPS vesicle-tethering subunit, PMID 38698024) is a completely distinct,
> non-TGF-β mechanism, so even the surviving literature doesn't converge on one pathway.
> Open Targets shows weak respiratory L2G, strong non-respiratory top hits, and strong
> LoF constraint (suggesting a GWAS signal from subtle expression modulation on an
> essential gene, not disease-driving coding change). All credible-set variants are
> intronic/upstream — no missense/splice variant to anchor a coding mechanism.
>
> **Verdict: CONTESTED** — the central mechanistic claim linking TGFBRAP1 to pulmonary
> fibrosis has been formally retracted; the one surviving paper's TGFBRAP1-specific claim
> depends on the same disputed interaction and should be treated as unreplicated pending
> independent confirmation, not as standalone support."

### C2orf49 (TSLIG2/ASW/ashwin) — WEAKENED

> "The researcher's case rests on proximity + ABC score + the single strongest (but
> still modest, 0.702, for a broad non-COPD EFO term) L2G score among the three genes.
> There is no dedicated lung/COPD literature, and the two functional papers
> (tRNA-splicing-ligase subunit; Xenopus neural development) are genuinely non-lung,
> housekeeping/developmental biology — the researcher did not overstate this. My GWAS
> Catalog check adds a concrete alternative: the lead variant itself (rs6749911) is
> genome-wide significant for BMI, and the second credible-set variant (rs4851758) for
> substance use disorder — both squarely in the 'smoking/behavior/metabolic pleiotropy'
> bucket rather than lung biology, consistent with Open Targets' smoking-initiation L2G
> (0.21/0.20) for this gene. [NOTE: this specific GWAS Catalog claim was subsequently
> returned CLAIM_MISMATCH by citation-verifier — see exclusions above — and should be
> discounted accordingly.] No paper argues explicitly against C2orf49, so this isn't
> CONTESTED in the strict 'someone implicated a different gene' sense, but there is
> essentially zero positive lung-mechanism evidence and now positive evidence pointing
> the credible-set variants toward non-lung pleiotropy.
>
> **Verdict: WEAKENED** — nominal-strongest L2G score, but the underlying case is
> proximity/enhancer-linkage only, with no independent lung biology, and new
> variant-level evidence pointing toward BMI/behavioral pleiotropy rather than pulmonary
> mechanism."

### FHL2 — WEAKENED

> "The researcher's positive case (non-retracted bleomycin/IPF fibroblast knockdown
> study, PMID 36641135; IPF biomarker ML study, PMID 38784225) is real and appropriately
> caveated as fibrotic/IPF biology rather than COPD/emphysema biology, and appropriately
> notes the cardiac and lung-cancer literatures are off-target for COPD. What the
> researcher's write-up doesn't fully weight is that FHL2's entire credible-set support is
> one low-PIP synonymous variant (rs3087523, PIP 0.041 — lowest at the whole locus), and
> my independent GWAS Catalog check shows that exact variant is a highly pleiotropic
> SES/behavioral/metabolic hit (income, occupational score, BMI, cigarettes/day-linked CRP
> pleiotropy), not a lung-function hit. [NOTE: these specific GWAS Catalog claims were
> subsequently returned CLAIM_MISMATCH by citation-verifier — see exclusions above — and
> should be discounted accordingly; the low PIP of rs3087523 itself is independently
> confirmed from the locus input file and stands regardless.] Combined with Open Targets
> showing zero COPD/FEV1 signal for FHL2 and dominant cardiac + smoking-initiation
> associations, the inferential chain 'FHL2 drives lung fibrosis in IPF models →
> therefore FHL2 is the COPD-relevant gene at this GWAS locus' is a plausibility argument,
> not a genetically supported one... IPF and COPD have substantially different (fibrotic
> vs. destructive/protease-antiprotease) pathophysiology, so cross-disease extrapolation
> is a meaningful stretch. The lung-cancer literature (10 papers) demonstrates FHL2 is
> bioactive in lung tissue generally but is not COPD-relevant and shouldn't be counted as
> COPD-supportive evidence in a synthesis tally.
>
> **Verdict: WEAKENED** — real, non-retracted lung-fibrosis mechanistic evidence exists,
> but it is extrapolated across disease contexts (IPF→COPD) from a single low-PIP
> synonymous variant whose own genome-wide-significant associations are dominated by
> income/BMI/smoking-behavior pleiotropy, not lung function."

### GPR45 — WEAKENED (no positive case; not in original candidate list)

> "Agree with the researcher: no credible-set variant, no prioritization-method flag,
> brain/hypothalamus-restricted expression, and the one in vivo phenotype (obesity via
> hypothalamic POMC/JAK-STAT, PMID 27500489) has no lung involvement. There is no
> mechanistic thread connecting this gene to airway or lung biology at all, and its
> inclusion appears to be pure regional-overlap bookkeeping rather than an
> evidence-based candidacy. I did not run an independent search on this gene (budget
> exhausted before reaching it) so I can't rule out something I haven't seen, but nothing
> in what's presented supports carrying it forward.
>
> **Verdict: WEAKENED** (there was never a positive case to begin with; recommend
> dropping from the prioritized candidate list, note for synthesis-lead that this gene
> received no independent verification pass from me)."

## Locus-level synthesis note from red-team-skeptic (verbatim, caveated)

> "Across all three seriously-considered genes, every credible-set variant I was able to
> check in the GWAS Catalog (independent of the researcher's PubMed-based literature) has
> its strongest genome-wide-significant catalogued association with a behavioral,
> socioeconomic, or metabolic trait (BMI, substance use disorder, household income,
> occupational score, cigarettes/day) rather than any lung-function or COPD trait — and
> Open Targets independently shows non-trivial smoking-initiation L2G scores for all
> three genes but no COPD/FEV1 signal for any of them. Taken together with TGFBRAP1's
> retracted mechanistic anchor and FHL2's single low-PIP variant, this is a locus where
> [smoking/behavior mediation] looks unusually well-supported relative to a
> direct-lung-biology story, and where an SES/behavioral-pleiotropy confound
> (income/education correlating with smoking prevalence, occupational exposures, and COPD
> risk through non-genetic-lung-specific channels) is a plausible alternative explanation
> that none of the three gene write-ups considered explicitly. I'd recommend
> synthesis-lead treat this locus as low-confidence for a lung-specific mechanism
> regardless of which gene is nominally 'prioritized,' and flag that a
> smoking/behavioral-trait-adjusted or MR sensitivity analysis (if available) would be
> more informative here than further gene-level literature mining."

**Orchestrator's note on this synthesis note:** the specific SNP-level GWAS Catalog
citations underlying this note all returned CLAIM_MISMATCH on verification (see
exclusions above) and cannot be cited as established fact. However, the general,
already-independently-supported observations that (a) Open Targets shows smoking-
initiation L2G signal at all three genes and no direct COPD/FEV1 signal at any of them,
and (b) TGFBRAP1's primary mechanistic literature is retracted, and (c) FHL2's only
credible-set variant is synonymous and lowest-PIP at the locus, all stand independently
of the mismatched citations and are reflected in the main evidence table's Prioritized
gene section. The stronger claim — that the specific credible-set variants are
individually confirmed GWAS-significant hits for BMI/income/occupation/cognition — is
not supported and has been excluded.
