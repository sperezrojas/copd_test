## Excluded citations

### RETRACTED

**Lee JJ et al., "Gene discovery and polygenic prediction from a genome-wide association
study of educational attainment in 1.1 million individuals," Nat Genet, 2018.**
PMID 30038396, PMC6393768, DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0147-3.

- Researcher's claim: cited as a plausible (not gene-confirmed) source of the
  educational-attainment locus-to-gene (L2G) signal reported by Open Targets for CEP57 at
  this locus.
- **Citation-verifier verdict: RETRACTED**, with justification: "PubMed metadata confirms
  retraction on August 1, 2018. Paper exists but is withdrawn from literature."
- **Orchestrator flag (important):** This verdict is surprising and should be treated with
  suspicion pending independent human re-verification. This is a widely known, extremely
  heavily cited (>3,000 citations) social-science-genomics GWAS ("EA3") that, to the
  orchestrator's and red-team-skeptic's knowledge, remains an active, non-retracted paper
  in the literature as of each agent's respective knowledge. The verifier's own stated
  retraction date (August 1, 2018) is suspiciously identical to the paper's original
  publication date — a pattern consistent with the verifier having misread or
  misattributed a PubMed metadata field (e.g. confusing a "received"/"published" date
  field with an actual retraction notice) rather than having found a genuine retraction
  notice.
- **Red-team-skeptic's independent comment (verbatim):** "I'm confident, from general
  knowledge, that Lee JJ et al. 2018 Nat Genet ... is a landmark, extremely heavily-cited
  (>3,000 citations), **non-retracted** paper — it remains foundational in the EA/cognition
  GWAS literature as of my knowledge cutoff. The orchestrator's suspicion is well-founded:
  a 'retraction date identical to publication date' is a classic signature of a
  metadata-parsing error ... not a real retraction. I'd recommend this specific verifier
  call be sent back for manual re-check rather than trusted."
- **Disposition:** Per pipeline rules, the orchestrator does not overrule the
  citation-verifier's verdict, so this citation is excluded from the verified evidence
  table (`evidence_tables/11_95700001-96000000.md`) regardless of this suspicion. This
  exclusion is not load-bearing for any conclusion in the evidence table: the citation was
  only ever used as an unconfirmed, gene-unspecific "plausible source" of a locus-level
  educational-attainment signal already independently corroborated (for the whole
  FAM76B/MTMR2/CEP57 block, not CEP57 specifically) by the VERIFIED Sanna et al. 2020
  citation. **A human reviewer should independently check PubMed/Retraction Watch for PMID
  30038396 before treating this exclusion as settled**, and consider re-running citation
  verification on this specific PMID if a manuscript depends on it.

## CONTESTED / WEAKENED gene-level reasoning

No gene at this locus was marked outright CONTESTED (i.e., actively disputed as
non-credible) by the red-team-skeptic. Two genes were marked **WEAKENED** relative to how
the researcher initially framed them; two were marked **HOLDS**. Full reasoning preserved
verbatim from the critique:

### MTMR2 — WEAKENED
"MTMR2 is the best-supported gene call structurally for this locus, but the inference
should be explicit that its association profile is smoking-behavior-mediated, not
lung-intrinsic — there is no positive evidence of a direct pulmonary mechanism, and the
missense variant's low PIP (0.038) shouldn't be read as strong causal-variant support."

### MAML2 — WEAKENED, bordering on unsupported
"No credible-set variant of its own, no L2G support whatsoever in Open Targets — its case
rests entirely on convergence of three correlated computational prioritization methods
(FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS), which can share systematic gene-level biases (e.g., gene
length/expression features) rather than constituting independent lines of evidence. The
sole biological citation, Tsao 2011, is being asked to carry more weight than it supports:
it manipulates Pofut1 and Rbpjk (upstream Notch receptor activation / CSL transcription
factor), not MAML2 or any MAML paralog, and doesn't test the coactivator step at all.
Given MAML1/MAML2/MAML3 have partially redundant function, this citation cannot establish
MAML2-specificity even at the pathway level — 'pathway-adjacent, upstream-only, not
MAML-family-tested' is a more accurate characterization than 'pathway-level Notch
evidence.' No smoking-behavior connection either, so MAML2 isn't even rescued by the
locus's dominant behavioral-pleiotropy explanation — its case is simply thin. ... flag to
synthesis lead that this is essentially a hypothesis generated by statistical convergence
with no direct genetic or gene-specific functional grounding."

### CEP57 — HOLDS (as LD/credible-set passenger)
"No prioritization by any fine-mapping/functional method — appears only via low-PIP
intronic variants. Open Targets L2G is strong but exclusively for cognitive/
educational-attainment traits. Sieben 2020 was actively searched for and found
non-supportive (studies BubR1, not CEP57; lung neoplasms mentioned only incidentally
within a broader MVA-syndrome cancer spectrum) — the researcher's explicit inclusion of
this null result satisfies the 'actively search for contradicting evidence' requirement
well, and the fact that this was the only CEP57+lung hit found is itself meaningful
negative evidence. ... appropriately evidenced by an explicit negative search rather than
silence."

### FAM76B — HOLDS (as weakest candidate)
"Lowest L2G, no prioritization by any method except locus membership. Zhang 2019 is doubly
tangential — it concerns a lncRNA in the FAM76B locus (not the FAM76B protein-coding
gene), in pulmonary tuberculosis (an infectious disease, not COPD), from one exploratory
transcriptomic screen. This is essentially uninformative and should not be read by anyone
downstream as 'FAM76B implicated in lung disease.' ... no changes to researcher's framing."
