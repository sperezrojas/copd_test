## Items marked CONTESTED / WEAKENED (verbatim reasoning from red-team-skeptic)

**CONTESTED (framing, not the citation itself):** "Open Targets colocalization (H4=0.986) between
chronic-airway-obstruction credible set and smoking-initiation credible set at this SNP" — if used
in synthesis to support a lung-biology case for ESR1, this is a misreading; a near-1 H4 for
airway-obstruction-vs-smoking-initiation is evidence FOR smoking-mediation, not for lung-autonomous
ESR1 biology. Also flagged as unresolved: researcher could not corroborate an ESR1/6q25
smoking-initiation signal in the primary Liu et al. 2019 GSCAN paper (PMID 30643251) — source of
this OT credible set (newer GSCAN release vs. different phenotype definition vs. curation
artifact) is unverified and should be checked before being cited as evidence.

**WEAKENED:** "All fine-mapping methods agree (nearest/FLAMES/PoPS all ESR1)" — true but not
strong independent corroboration, since ESR1 is the sole gene in the window; agreement here is
largely definitional, not confirmatory of causal-gene assignment in a competitive sense.

**WEAKENED:** Immune-mechanism citations (Keselman et al. 2017 PMID 28760880, Ray et al. 2024 PMID
38071600, Smith et al. 2024 PMID 38897669, Henriquez Pilier et al. 2026 PMID 41686163 — asthma/
particulate/ozone models) — real findings in their own contexts, but should not be presented as
direct COPD mechanistic evidence without an explicit "extrapolated from asthma/particulate models,
not COPD-tested" caveat.

**WEAKENED (review citations):** Ekpruke & Silveyra 2025 (PMID 41474472), Siegfried 2022 (PMID
34927202), Reyes-García et al. 2021 (PMID 34019274) — all use hedged, hypothesis-generating
language ("proposed to explain," "may promote") to extend ERα-asthma/inflammation biology to COPD;
these are not demonstrated COPD-specific findings and should not be presented as such.

**Noted contradiction (verified citation, carried forward, not excluded):** Oh et al. 2021 (PMID
33711637) — high ESR1/PGR expression associated with DECREASED CD4+/CD8+ T-cell infiltration in
NSCLC tumors, opposite direction from a simple pro-inflammatory ERα model (different, tumor-immune
context, but undercuts a clean unidirectional mechanism claim).

**HOLDS as characterized (no further weakening needed — reproduced for completeness):**
Researcher's own hedging on Obeidat et al. 2011 (PMID 21625484) (nominal P=5.4×10⁻⁴,
ever-smoker-subgroup-only, not genome-wide significant, underpowered by modern standards) and on
the absence of this locus from Wain et al. 2017 (PMID 28166213), Shrine et al. 2019 (PMID
30804560), and Liu et al. 2019 (PMID 30643251) — these caveats are accurately stated in the
researcher's original write-up, not overclaimed, and represent the strongest and most
honestly-reported negative evidence in the packet.

**HOLDS as strong mechanistic support (not contested):** Tam et al. 2016 (PMID 26599602) and
Sathish et al. 2015 (PMID 26113109) — both genuine cigarette-smoke/COPD-relevant mouse or
cell-line models showing sex-dimorphic, ERα-dependent airway remodeling and airway-smooth-muscle
dysfunction under smoke exposure specifically. The skeptic explicitly identified these as the two
strongest, least-contestable mechanistic citations in the packet.

## Skeptic's overall verdict (verbatim)

"WEAKENED. ESR1's status as the candidate gene at this locus is solid on pure positional grounds
(only gene in the window) and the locus itself is clearly not noise at the genome-wide/biological
level (strong, consistent L2G across dozens of unrelated GWAS). But the specific inference that
this represents lung-autonomous or COPD-specific ERα biology overreaches the evidence: three major
primary GWAS find no significant signal here for lung function or smoking initiation; the one
direct gene-specific COPD/lung-function association (Obeidat 2011) is weak and smoking-subgroup-
restricted; the Open Targets coloc pattern that would most directly support a COPD link is
unconfirmed in primary literature and, if anything, points toward smoking/behavioral mediation
rather than pulmonary mechanism; and most of the mechanistic immune-cell literature is drawn from
asthma/particulate models extrapolated to COPD rather than tested in COPD models directly. A
synthesis that treats this as a confidently lung-autonomous ERα-COPD mechanism would be
overclaiming; a synthesis that flags it as 'plausible pleiotropic locus, most parsimoniously
explained by smoking-behavior mediation with secondary/uncertain lung-autonomous contribution,
weak direct COPD/spirometry support' would match the evidence."

## Alternative hypotheses raised by the skeptic (verbatim)

"1. Smoking-behavior/neuropsychiatric-pleiotropy mediation. ESR1's pleiotropic signature at this
exact SNP (smoking initiation, depression/anxiety/PTSD/mood disorders/suicidality, educational
attainment, reproductive-timing traits) is far more characteristic of a CNS/behavioral locus than a
lung-epithelial one. Under this hypothesis, any COPD/airway-obstruction association at this locus
is substantially a downstream consequence of effects on smoking initiation/persistence (and
possibly correlated psychiatric/behavioral traits affecting smoking exposure), consistent with
prior #1's 'neurobiological pathways affecting smoking' scenario — and the H4=0.986 coloc with
smoking initiation (if corroborated) would be direct evidence for exactly this.

2. Weak/noise or winner's-curse signal specifically for COPD. PIP of 0.633 (moderate, not high), 11
credible-set variants, absence from all three major primary lung-function/smoking GWAS, and
absence from Open Targets' own curated COPD/FinnGen credible sets at this locus collectively
suggest the COPD-specific signal driving this locus's inclusion in the present pipeline may be
marginal/borderline rather than robust, even though ESR1 clearly does harbor genuine, well-powered
signal for other traits (breast cancer, smoking initiation, psychiatric traits, reproductive
timing)."
