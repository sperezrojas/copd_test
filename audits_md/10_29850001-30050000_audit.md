# Audit appendix — locus 10:29,850,001-30,050,000 (GRCh38) (JCAD / SVIL)

This appendix records everything excluded from the main evidence table
(`evidence_tables/10_29850001-30050000.md`) plus the full reasoning behind any CONTESTED gene
verdict, preserved verbatim from the red-team-skeptic where applicable. Intended for reviewers who
need the trail behind exclusions, not for routine distribution alongside the evidence table.

## Excluded citations

### Edelstein LC, et al. "Human genome-wide association and mouse knockout approaches identify platelet supervillin as an inhibitor of thrombus formation under shear stress." Circulation. 2012. PMID: 22550155, DOI: 10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.112.091462, PMC: PMC3515852.

- **Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH -- excluded from evidence table.**
- Surfaced by red-team-skeptic (initially mis-cited as "Wang et al."), then checked by
  citation-verifier.
- The paper itself is real and its scientific claims are accurately reported in the abstract: a
  genome-wide-significant association (P<5x10^-8) between 2 SNPs in SVIL (chromosome 10p11.23) and
  platelet thrombus formation under shear stress in African Americans (not European Americans),
  corroborated by a murine Svil-knockout platelet phenotype.
- **Why excluded:** (1) author attribution error -- no author named "Wang" appears on this paper;
  first author is Edelstein LC. (2) More importantly, the two GWAS-significant SNPs are rs7070678
  (chr10:29,523,673, GRCh38) and rs10826650 (chr10:29,525,355, GRCh38) -- both approximately 420,000
  bp upstream of this locus's credible-set span (29,947,500-29,992,800). While both SNPs fall within
  the SVIL gene body, they represent a **distinct genomic signal**, not the locus under review here.
  Citing this paper as evidence for locus 10_29850001-30050000 would be citing a real finding about
  the wrong genomic region under the same gene name. Retained here for transparency (it is a
  legitimate SVIL-platelet association elsewhere in the gene) but not usable as evidence for this
  specific COPD credible set.

## Citations with a partial/qualified verdict (retained in main table, flagged here for visibility)

### Shrine N, et al. Nat Genet. 2019. PMID: 30804560, PMC: PMC6397078, DOI: 10.1038/s41588-018-0321-7.

- **Verdict: VERIFIED for general claims; NOT CONFIRMED for the specific per-gene supplementary
  claim.**
- Citation-verifier confirmed via full text: 400,102 individuals, 279 lung-function signals, and
  explicit smoking-driven-signal exclusion testing (naming CHRNA5-region and PDE4C-region signals as
  examples of exclusions; this locus was not named among those exclusions).
- Citation-verifier could **not** confirm from the available full text whether this paper's
  supplementary tables make a specific gene-level causal-gene call of JCAD or SVIL at 10p11.23 --
  gene-level putative-causal assignments are in supplementary tables not captured by the full-text
  retrieval tool used. This paper is retained in the main table as general supporting context for
  the locus's plausibility as a lung-function signal (and as the likely source of the FEV1/FVC ratio
  credible set GCST004185 that Open Targets reports colocalizing at this locus), but should not be
  read as a confirmed direct per-gene citation for JCAD specifically without further supplementary
  table review.

## CONTESTED gene verdict: SVIL

Red-team-skeptic verdict, preserved verbatim:

> **SVIL — CONTESTED.**
> The Ensembl-confirmed physical non-overlap with the locus window (>112-210 kb gap) combined with
> weak Open Targets L2G scores directly contradicts the FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS causal-gene call, and no
> chromatin-interaction (Hi-C/ABC-model) evidence was presented to rescue a long-range-regulation
> explanation. The SVIL-specific literature (13-19) is uniformly off-tissue (cardiac muscle,
> skeletal muscle, colorectal cancer, one LAM cell line) or underpowered/off-target for COPD
> specifically (Adami 2018: nominal, uncorrected, n=152, different SNP not in this credible set).
> Recommend flagging to synthesis-lead that SVIL's prioritization likely reflects a
> statistical/window artifact and should be deprioritized unless a chromatin-interaction check (not
> yet performed by either agent) turns up genuine evidence connecting the credible-set region to the
> SVIL promoter — this is a thoroughness gap, not a closed question.

Related skeptic reasoning on the physical-overlap discrepancy (Q3, preserved verbatim):

> The Ensembl-confirmed physical gap (>112 kb from the window, >210 kb from the credible-set span)
> combined with weak/absent Open Targets L2G scores (0.05-0.09, or missing entirely for FEV1/FVC
> ratio) is a strong reason to suspect a window-based statistical artifact (MAGMA gene-based tests
> and some PoPS window definitions can pull in genes tens to hundreds of kb away, especially in
> gene-sparse regions). No promoter-capture Hi-C, TAD-boundary, or long-range enhancer evidence was
> presented to justify treating SVIL as reachable from this credible set — so the "legitimate
> long-range regulation" explanation is currently speculative, not evidenced.

## Other caveats flagged by red-team-skeptic (not exclusions, but worth surfacing for reviewers)

- **Directionality gap (Q2):** Suraya et al. 2024 (PMID 39273437) is a knockout/silencing study
  showing JCAD loss is protective against emphysema/inflammation in mice; it does not establish
  which allele of rs4747692 (or any credible-set variant) increases vs. decreases JCAD
  expression/activity in humans. No claim that this paper "validates" or "confirms" the GWAS
  association should be read into the main evidence table -- it is mechanistically supportive only.
- **Pleiotropy/tissue-specificity caveat (Q1, Q6):** the large majority of JCAD's mechanistic
  literature (7 of 12 JCAD citations in the main table) comes from cardiovascular/coronary-artery-
  disease contexts, not pulmonary tissue. Strong locus-level colocalization with white blood cell
  count, monocyte count, and platelet count (h4 up to 0.98) suggests this locus may reflect a
  broadly pleiotropic vascular/inflammatory mechanism rather than a COPD- or lung-specific one. This
  does not contradict JCAD's candidacy but should temper any claim of lung-specific certainty.
- **Smoking-mediation nuance (Q5):** the researcher's finding of no smoking-behavior-GWAS overlap is
  an absence-of-evidence finding, not a directly tested negative for this locus. The newly-verified
  Boueiz et al. 2017 emphysema-distribution GWAS was conducted entirely in smokers, so the pulmonary
  phenotype could still be smoking-injury-conditional (JCAD/endothelial dysfunction mattering only in
  the context of smoke-induced injury) rather than acting independently of smoking exposure.
- **Alternative candidate genes:** the researcher's Ensembl REST query of the full stated locus
  window (10:29,850,001-30,050,000) found JCAD as the only protein-coding gene physically overlapping
  the window, alongside one pseudogene (SSBP1P1) and several unannotated lncRNA loci, none of which
  have documented COPD-relevant literature. The red-team-skeptic independently raised this as a
  thoroughness question before locating the researcher's existing Ensembl check; no additional
  protein-coding candidate genes were identified by either agent.
