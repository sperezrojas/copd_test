# Audit appendix — Locus 8:8,150,001-11,050,000 (GRCh38; nearest gene PRAG1)

This file documents everything excluded from the main evidence table (`evidence_tables/8_8150001-11050000.md`)
and preserves red-team-skeptic reasoning verbatim, per pipeline requirements. It is intended for the study
team's internal review, not for external distribution alongside the evidence table.

## Excluded citations (verifier verdict ≠ VERIFIED)

### SOX7 — PMID 41859020 / DOI 10.2147/COPD.S572586 — verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH

Researcher's original claim: in a cross-trait GWAS pleiotropy analysis of COPD and cardiovascular disease
(hypertension, coronary heart disease, heart failure), SMR/PLACO colocalization prioritized SOX7 (alongside
ZNF652, XRCC3, SLC22A5) as a candidate shared therapeutic target gene.

Citation-verifier finding: "Chen et al. 2026 (Int J COPD) identifies SOX7 colocalization signal at 4q22
(chromosome 4), NOT at chr8 where the researcher's locus is located (chr8:10.5-10.6 Mb). The chromosomal
location is incorrect for this chr8 locus-specific evidence; SOX7 on 4q22 is on a different chromosome and
therefore not relevant to the chr8 locus." The researcher had already flagged this exact discrepancy in the
original write-up as needing verification before use; the citation-verifier confirmed it is a genuine mismatch,
not a transcription error. This citation is excluded from the evidence table in its entirety — it does not
support SOX7 (or any gene) at this locus. With this citation removed, SOX7's evidentiary base at this locus
is effectively empty (weak Open Targets scores only; no qualifying literature).

No other citation for this locus received a RETRACTED or NOT_FOUND verdict; the remaining 25 of 26 citations
screened were VERIFIED. No rsID-level literature was found for the lead variant (rs2979192) or the two missense
credible-set variants (rs74990397, rs35602868) — the citation-verifier independently confirmed zero PubMed
results for all three, consistent with the researcher's original finding (this is an absence, not an excluded
citation).

## Gene marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic

### RP1L1 (carries the locus's only missense credible-set variants: rs74990397, rs35602868)

Red-team-skeptic verdict, preserved verbatim:

> I'd mark this **CONTESTED**, not merely "excluded for lack of evidence," because there is actual evidence
> *against* relevance beyond mere absence: (a) constraint metrics (LoF oe=1.63, missense oe=1.53) indicate
> tolerance to loss-of-function and missense variation, arguing against a dosage-sensitive role, which cuts
> against RP1L1 being the functional target of a fine-mapped regulatory or missense signal; (b) its only
> established disease biology (occult macular dystrophy) is retina-restricted with a distinct
> photoreceptor/microtubule mechanism with no known pleiotropy into airway/lung biology; (c) my independent
> search found zero lung/respiratory literature; (d) the two missense variants that justified its inclusion
> (rs74990397 PIP=0.000235, rs35602868 PIP=0.0000275) are both several orders of magnitude below the lead
> variant's PIP (0.413) and have no functional literature of their own. This combination — active evidence
> against dosage sensitivity plus a mechanistically unrelated known disease biology plus vanishing PIPs — is
> stronger than a simple "no evidence found" case and should be flagged as CONTESTED so the audit trail shows
> this isn't just an unstudied gene, it's a gene with genomic evidence weighing against relevance.
>
> **RP1L1: CONTESTED** — recommend exclusion from the final candidate table; reasoning above should be
> preserved verbatim.

Synthesis-lead note: RP1L1's two VERIFIED retina-biology citations (PMID 41555797, PMID 40507975) remain in
the main evidence table for full traceability (they are accurately characterized, VERIFIED citations, just
about a different tissue), but the prioritized-gene synthesis explicitly does not recommend RP1L1 as a
candidate, consistent with this CONTESTED verdict.

## Other red-team-skeptic verdicts preserved for transparency (WEAKENED, not full exclusions)

These genes were not marked CONTESTED, but the skeptic's per-gene "WEAKENED" verdicts materially changed how
the evidence should be read relative to the researcher's original framing. Full reasoning (verbatim) is in
`evidence_tables/_status/8_8150001-11050000/critique.md`; key points summarized here for convenience:

- **PRAG1** — WEAKENED. The researcher's write-up treated PRAG1's Open Targets COPD L2G score (0.80, highest
  at the locus) as a strong prioritization signal without flagging that its cigarettes-per-day L2G score is
  even higher (0.886), a pattern the skeptic argues is "at least as consistent with smoking-mediation as direct
  pulmonary biology" per biological prior #1. Zero lung-specific mechanistic literature exists for PRAG1.
- **XKR6** — WEAKENED. No COPD L2G evidence at all in Open Targets; dominant signal is smoking initiation; no
  lung literature of any kind.
- **TNKS** — WEAKENED. Good smoking-independent spirometry L2G (FEV1/FVC 0.78) but zero lung-tissue literature.
  The researcher's "shared telomere module with PINX1" argument (via PMID 12768206) was judged by the skeptic to
  be a "just-so story": telomere/shelterin biology is present in virtually all proliferating cell types and
  supplies no COPD-specific rationale; it should be read as background biology, not independent support for
  either TNKS or PINX1.
- **PINX1** — WEAKENED. The skeptic corrected the premise that PINX1 lacks lung-tissue data (it does have two
  genuine lung/airway-tissue citations, PMID 33819185 and PMID 29305433), but judged the researcher's framing
  ("best-supported candidate across all three domains") to overstate the case by conflating a correlational
  transcriptomic finding (PMID 39798034) with causal/experimental evidence, and by not addressing a
  smoking-status confound in the NSCLC citation (PMID 28372542).
- **MFHAS1** — WEAKENED. Mechanistically plausible (TLR2/macrophage polarization, consistent with biological
  prior #3) and statistically the strongest smoking-independent, spirometry-concordant candidate after PRAG1's
  smoking-confounded profile is discounted, but all three supporting citations are non-lung (sepsis/systemic
  monocyte-macrophage) experimental systems; the researcher's "compelling candidate" language was judged to
  oversell how directly this mechanism has been shown to apply to airway/lung tissue.
- **MSRA** — HOLDS (researcher's own cautious call affirmed). The skeptic agreed that MSRA more likely
  represents "a separate, adjacent GWAS signal... incidentally captured by this locus's wide credible set" given
  its variants sit ~1.7-2.1 Mb from the lead variant at all PIP<0.00026, despite having the single most
  on-target COPD-pathway-specific literature at the whole locus (the alpha-1-antitrypsin/protease-antiprotease
  mechanism). The skeptic escalated this into a locus-definition concern: the 2.9 Mb credible set may merge two
  distinct GWAS signals, one around PRAG1/XKR6/TNKS/CLDN23/PINX1/MFHAS1 and a separate one around MSRA.
- **CLDN23** — HOLDS. Correctly characterized by the researcher as the weakest of the four originally
  pipeline-prioritized genes; no overclaiming detected. Note: the skeptic's search budget was exhausted before
  reaching CLDN23, so this gene received no independent adversarial search beyond the citation-verifier's
  standard checks (see coverage disclosure below).
- **SOX7** — WEAKENED to near-null once PMID 41859020 is excluded as CLAIM_MISMATCH (see above); the skeptic's
  search budget was also exhausted before reaching SOX7 independently.
