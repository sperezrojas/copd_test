## Excluded citations

### CLAIM_MISMATCH

**Madera-Salcedo IK, Danelli L, Tiwari N, et al. "Tomosyn functions as a PKCδ-regulated
fusion clamp in mast cell degranulation." Sci Signal 2018. PMID 29970602, DOI
10.1126/scisignal.aan4350.**

Researcher's original claim: "tomosyn-1 dissociates from STX4 (and associates with
STX3) during IgE-mediated mast-cell activation, implicating STX4 as one of several SNARE
partners regulating mast-cell mediator release; tomosyn-1 abundance is also elevated in
allergic patients."

Citation-verifier's verdict (verbatim): "CLAIM_MISMATCH - Title is 'Tomosyn functions as
a PKCδ-regulated fusion clamp in mast cell degranulation.' The claim states 'tomosyn
dissociates from STX4 (associates with STX3)' but the title does not mention
dissociation or STX3/STX4 specifically - it focuses on tomosyn function as a 'fusion
clamp' in degranulation. Claim appears to overstate or mischaracterize the primary
finding (verifier examined title/abstract-level evidence only, could not confirm the
specific STX4-to-STX3 partner-switching detail as stated)."

Excluded from the evidence table. Not used to support or oppose any gene in this
locus's synthesis.

No RETRACTED or NOT_FOUND citations were identified for this locus (38 of 39 citations
returned VERIFIED).

## Genes marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning)

The red-team-skeptic's full critique is preserved verbatim in
`evidence_tables/_status/16_29850001-31300000/critique.md`; the CONTESTED-gene
reasoning is reproduced here for standalone audit purposes.

**Search-budget disclosure (applies to all verdicts below):** "My independent
WebSearch calls returned 'budget exhausted' before returning any results (0 of my
planned queries executed). My critique below is therefore based entirely on
internal-consistency analysis of the researcher's own findings, the citation-verifier's
verdicts, and the stated priors — not on new independent literature. This is a real
coverage gap on my end: no gene got an independent contradiction-search pass from me
this session."

**Locus-level framing:** "This is the single most important finding in the whole
dossier, and it's somewhat buried: Open Targets structured L2G shows zero genetic
evidence for COPD, FEV1, or FEV1/FVC for any of the 9 genes, and the lead variant's own
GWAS Catalog hits are triglycerides/CVD, not lung function or smoking behavior. ... The
dominant colocalization signal (lipids, anthropometrics, warfarin, obesity,
hypertension, PD) plus the fact that COPD case-control status is frequently confounded
by comorbid cardiometabolic disease in EHR/case-ascertainment-based GWAS makes a
parsimonious alternative hypothesis plausible: the COPD association at this locus is a
pleiotropic/comorbidity artifact (obesity-cardiometabolic-driven case misclassification
or shared regulatory architecture with lipid/CVD traits) rather than a lung-mechanism
signal, and the PIP concentration on PRSS8/KAT8 reflects LD and regulatory density in
this haplotype block, not lung relevance. ... Note also that prior #1
(smoking-mediation) doesn't obviously rescue the locus either — there's no
smoking-initiation/cessation/pack-years signal reported at the lead variant. So this
doesn't look like a 'smoking behavior gene' case either; it looks more like a
metabolic/pleiotropy-driven false-positive-for-lung-biology candidate."

**PRSS8 — CONTESTED.** "The mechanistic case (ENaC/mucociliary clearance) is real but
built almost entirely on cystic fibrosis and kidney knockout models, not COPD or
cigarette-smoke models — a disease-context leap the researcher discloses but that would
become overclaiming if softened in synthesis. More importantly, there is genuine
contradicting evidence already in-hand: Casselbrant et al. 2022 found PRSS8 associated
with a combined CAD+airflow-obstruction phenotype but explicitly not with airflow
obstruction alone — i.e., the one COPD-adjacent genetic finding argues against a pure
lung mechanism and toward the cardiometabolic-pleiotropy explanation above. Combined
with zero COPD/FEV1 L2G despite PRSS8 harboring the lead SNP at PIP=0.43, 'mechanistic
plausibility without genetic corroboration' understates how much active counter-evidence
exists. This is oversold if presented as the front-runner without heavy caveats."

**BCKDK — CONTESTED.** "The only genetic-adjacent evidence (rs14235 PD association)
failed replication in two independent studies. There is no COPD/lung/smoking literature
at all. The case for this gene isn't just weak, it's actively undermined by its own
best-available genetic evidence."

**STX1B — CONTESTED.** "Shares the same failed-replication PD signal with BCKDK (can't
even discriminate between the two genes at that variant), has an unrelated
neuronal-epilepsy disease biology, and the one mast-cell paper cited in its favor
actually shows other SNAREs (STX4, VAMP7/8, SNAP-23) driving degranulation
functionally, not STX1B. This is a case where the cited evidence contradicts the gene's
own candidacy."

**STX4 — CONTESTED.** "Direct head-to-head contradiction already in the dossier:
Sanchez et al. 2018 concludes STX3 (not STX4) is required for mast-cell exocytosis,
directly opposing the Lam 2020/Puri 1999-era STX4 mast-cell claims. Compounding this,
the citation-verifier already flagged Madera-Salcedo et al. 2018 as CLAIM_MISMATCH —
the specific 'tomosyn dissociates from STX4 and associates with STX3' mechanism was not
supported at the abstract level. This is a pattern (contested underlying biology + an
overclaimed citation) that should push STX4 to CONTESTED rather than merely weakened."

**KAT8 — WEAKENED (bordering CONTESTED), not formally CONTESTED but reasoning
preserved here for completeness.** "The neutrophil/M2-macrophage evidence comes
exclusively from allergic-asthma (papain, HDM) and LPS acute-lung-injury models — none
in COPD or smoke-exposure models. This is a real overclaiming risk ...: COPD's
inflammatory signature (neutrophilic, CD8+/Th1/Th17-skewed, chronic) is mechanistically
distinct from Th2-skewed allergic-asthma biology, even though LPS-ALI neutrophilia is a
closer analogy than HDM/papain. ... Also note KAT8's own top Open Targets L2G hits at
this locus are metabolic/anthropometric/neurological, directly undercutting a
lung-mechanism story."

## Answers to orchestrator's specific critique questions (verbatim from red-team-skeptic)

- **PRSS8 "mechanistic plausibility without genetic corroboration" framing:** "oversold
  as currently stated. It should read 'mechanistic plausibility in a different disease
  (CF), with a genetic association that, where tested against the actual COPD-relevant
  phenotype (airflow obstruction), came back null.' That's a materially weaker claim."
- **KAT8 generalization from allergic/ALI models to COPD:** "yes, this is a live
  overclaiming risk; the model systems used don't match COPD's more
  neutrophilic/CD8+/Th1-Th17 chronic phenotype, and this should be stated as a
  limitation, not smoothed over."
- **More parsimonious null-ish interpretation:** "yes — given zero L2G evidence for
  COPD/FEV1/FEV1FVC locus-wide, and dominant lipid/CVD/obesity pleiotropy, the most
  defensible read is that the COPD signal at this locus is unresolved and possibly a
  comorbidity/pleiotropy artifact, with PRSS8/KAT8 PIP dominance reflecting
  LD/regulatory architecture rather than demonstrated lung causality. This should be the
  headline caveat synthesis-lead carries forward for this locus."
- **Thoroughness:** "coverage looks reasonably even across all 9 genes (every gene got
  at least some literature search and an honest weak/strong characterization); I did not
  find a gene that appears unsearched or arbitrarily dismissed. No additional
  pre-synthesis gene searches need to be flagged as mandatory, beyond the disclosed fact
  that I could not independently verify any of this with new searches this session."
