**Full red-team narrative on the "smoking-mediated" framing (verbatim):**
"The researcher is right to flag smoking-trait enrichment, but the inferential chain has a circularity
problem the write-up doesn't address. Modern smoking-behavior GWAS (GSCAN, Liu et al. 2019; its
African-ancestry replication Piga et al. 2022; the externalizing-dimension GWAS; even some RLS/psychiatric
GWAS) draw on heavily overlapping biobank cohorts (UK Biobank, 23andMe-adjacent, etc.) that also feed into
COPD GWAS discovery samples, and COPD GWAS is not smoking-adjusted. So 'SEMA6D turns up in
smoking-initiation GWAS AND in COPD Open Targets score' is not two independent lines of evidence
converging — it may be the same underlying genetic-behavioral signal (nicotine/dopaminergic/neuronal
gene-set architecture) being counted twice through correlated but non-independent cohorts... Given the
note that SEMA6D's Open Targets COPD score (0.268) is *lower* than nearly every smoking/behavioral/
addiction/psychiatric trait score (0.342-0.440) and RLS (0.440) is even higher than nicotine dependence,
the pattern is far more consistent with 'this is fundamentally a neurobehavioral/addiction-pathway gene
that COPD GWAS partially picks up because it's not smoking-adjusted' than with 'this is a lung gene that
happens to also affect smoking.'"

**Alternative explanation flagged by red-team, not previously raised by the researcher (verbatim):**
"Regional/statistical pleiotropy via neuronal gene-set enrichment, not causal lung biology. SEMA6D is a
semaphorin/axon-guidance-and-neuroimmune gene with strong, repeated, cross-ancestry signal in smoking
initiation, nicotine dependence, cannabis use, ADHD, RLS, insomnia, MDD, schizophrenia, alcohol use, and
cognitive decline (APOE-e4 interaction). This is a textbook profile for a gene sitting in a broadly
pleiotropic neurobehavioral locus that MAGMA/PoPS gene-set methods will enrich for (neuronal/synaptic/
behavioral gene sets), independent of whether it does anything causal in lung tissue. Because SEMA6D is
the sole protein-coding gene in this 650kb interval, nearest-gene/FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS convergence is
somewhat less informative than usual — there's no competing gene to distinguish it from, but conversely
there's also no way to rule out that the 'COPD' signal at this locus is purely a downstream/proxy
consequence of pleiotropic behavioral effects... rather than a lung-intrinsic causal variant."
