# Audit trail — Locus 7:1700001-2250000 (MAD1L1)

This file records everything excluded from the final evidence table
(`evidence_tables/7_1700001-2250000.md`), with reasoning preserved verbatim from
citation-verifier and red-team-skeptic, plus other process notes relevant to a reviewer
checking traceability.

## Excluded citations (CLAIM_MISMATCH / RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND)

### 1. Chang S et al., PLoS One 2015, PMID 26193471, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0133404
**Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH — EXCLUDED**

Researcher's original claim: "Placed MAD1L1 within gene-network modules enriched for
'neurotrophin signaling pathway,' 'synaptosome,' and (separately) 'antigen processing and
presentation' and other immune-related GO terms" (a network-based analysis of
schizophrenia GWAS).

Citation-verifier's finding: "From PMC full text, MAD1L1 is listed as a validated disease
gene but is NOT specifically connected to the enriched pathway modules (neurotrophin
signaling, synaptosome, antigen processing). The claim implies MAD1L1 is placed within
these pathway-enriched modules, but the paper only validates it as a gene and separately
identifies pathway enrichment without linking MAD1L1 to them."

Red-team-skeptic's comment on downstream impact: "The excluded Chang et al. 2015
citation: correctly excluded, and since the researcher had already caveated the immune
claim as weak/network-level *before* exclusion, and explicitly declined to build a
broader immune case afterward, this exclusion doesn't materially change the picture —
there was never a real immune-mechanism claim here, just a thin one that's now
appropriately gone. No downstream overclaiming risk remains on the immune axis, but
synthesis should not resurrect any implied immune story for MAD1L1."

**Disposition:** Excluded from evidence table. No replacement citation was sought since
the researcher had already explicitly scoped out a full immune-domain literature search
for MAD1L1, judging there is no biological rationale connecting its mitotic-checkpoint
function to a COPD-specific immune/inflammatory program (see researcher checkpoint,
"Immune/inflammatory domain — explicit scope judgment" section).

## No genes marked CONTESTED

Red-team-skeptic's explicit verdict was **WEAKENED-BUT-SUPPORTED**, not CONTESTED, for
MAD1L1's gene-identity assignment at this locus:

> "Not contested in the sense of 'wrong gene at this locus' — the statistical-genetic
> case for MAD1L1 as the gene at this locus is genuinely strong and multiply concordant:
> nearest gene, FLAMES, MAGMA, and PoPS unanimously agree, no ABC-implicated alternative
> exists, and Open Targets L2G places MAD1L1 well ahead of NUDT1/MRM2 at this exact
> variant across 85 credible sets, including a COPD-specific one (L2G 0.627 vs.
> 0.204/0.107). NUDT1 and MRM2 have zero supporting lung/COPD literature of their own —
> there is no rival gene case to make. On 'which gene,' this holds."

However, red-team-skeptic distinguished sharply between gene identity (SUPPORTED) and
causal pathway/mechanism (WEAKENED, bordering on NOT SUPPORTED for a primary-pulmonary-
biology interpretation):

> "'The COPD association at this locus reflects primary pulmonary/structural or
> immune-inflammatory biology': WEAKENED, bordering on NOT SUPPORTED. No FEV1/FEV1-FVC
> concordance anywhere in the literature or Open Targets; the strongest lung-disease
> link (Allen 2020) is for a different disease (IPF, fibrotic) with different
> pathophysiology; the microbiome correlation (Gao 2024) is confounding-prone and
> non-mechanistic; there is no dedicated COPD functional/mouse model; and the gene is
> not LoF-constrained. Meanwhile the trait-association profile is dominated by
> neuropsychiatric disease and smoking-initiation genetics, with a literal
> shared-variant overlap with cross-psychiatric-disorder risk in this locus's own
> credible set (Levey 2020)."

This distinction (gene identity vs. mechanism) is carried through into the "Prioritized
gene" section of the main evidence table with MODERATE (not HIGH) confidence for exactly
this reason.

## Unresolved evidence gaps flagged by researcher and/or red-team-skeptic (not excluded,
but should be weighed as open questions, not settled findings)

1. **Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019 (PMID 30804561) MAD1L1-specific inclusion/replication
   status** — the primary 82-locus COPD GWAS confirms 13/35 novel loci replicated with
   FEV1/FEV1-FVC in SpiroMeta, but neither the researcher nor the citation-verifier could
   confirm from the retrieved full text whether MAD1L1/this 7:1.7-2.25Mb region is one of
   the 82 loci, nor its replication status if so (supplementary gene-prioritization
   tables were not accessible via the tools used). Red-team-skeptic calls this "the
   single most decision-relevant unresolved item in this dossier."
2. **Primary smoking-initiation GWAS paper(s) (e.g., GSCAN/Liu et al. 2019)** — Open
   Targets flags a smoking-initiation genetic_association sub-score of 0.526 for MAD1L1
   (one of its highest of any trait), but the researcher could not independently locate
   and confirm the underlying primary GWAS paper via PubMed keyword search. This
   Open-Targets-only data point is reported as structured-database context in the main
   evidence table's Open Targets summary, not as a verified literature citation.
3. **Red-team-skeptic's own search-budget limitation** — the red-team-skeptic subagent
   noted its WebSearch budget was exhausted before it could run independent queries for
   this locus (having already used 200/200 calls on prior locus reviews in the same
   session), and relied on two WebFetch calls instead. It found no contradicting paper via
   this partial check but explicitly flagged this as "a shallow independent pass, not a
   deep one" rather than a clean, fully independent negative search. Future re-review of
   this locus (or a re-run with a fresh red-team-skeptic session) could usefully repeat a
   fuller independent search.
