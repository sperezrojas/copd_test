## Coordinate discrepancy flag (STRBP, PTGS1)

Both the researcher (via direct Ensembl REST API query) and red-team-skeptic
(independently, via its own Ensembl REST API fetch) confirmed the following GRCh38
coordinates:
- **STRBP**: chr9:123,109,494-123,269,102 — approximately **1.53 Mb outside** (upstream/
  centromeric of) the stated locus start boundary (9:124,800,001).
- **PTGS1**: chr9:122,370,530-122,395,877 — approximately **2.40 Mb outside** the stated
  locus start boundary — the largest offset of any candidate gene evaluated at this locus.

Both genes were provided as candidates via internal prioritization methods (MAGMA for
STRBP, PoPS for PTGS1) that, per the pipeline's own methodology description, are expected
to nominate genes that physically overlap the locus window. Neither researcher nor
red-team-skeptic could resolve the cause of this discrepancy from outside the pipeline —
possible explanations raised include a genome-build mismatch (GRCh37 coordinates misread
as GRCh38) in the internal MAGMA/PoPS pipeline, a MAGMA gene-assignment window wider than
the locus's own stated boundaries, or a locus-boundary-definition error upstream of this
analysis. **This should be checked against the internal pipeline's own coordinate source
before either gene is weighted further at this locus.** By contrast, PBX3
(chr9:125,656,780-125,976,687), MAPKAP1, and PBX3-DT/LOC51145 were all confirmed to fall
within the stated region as expected.

## Genes marked CONTESTED or WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (full reasoning, verbatim)

### MAPKAP1 — CONTESTED

**(a) Specificity:** Entirely borrowed context — general mTORC2/SIN1 biochemistry and
cancer-cell (including a Kras-driven lung-tumorigenesis mouse model) biology. Zero COPD or
lung-function GWAS literature.

**(b) Smoking/pulmonary/immune:** None of the three priors are supported by anything
beyond proximity; no citation touches airway inflammation, spirometry, or smoking
behavior.

**(c) Internal contradiction / LD bias:** This is the textbook nearest-gene-bias case the
framework warns about. OT's own L2G-style scores at the same shared variants are
consistently lower for MAPKAP1 than for PBX3 across 7 independent GWAS studies — the
statistical evidence actively argues against MAPKAP1 relative to its neighbor, at every
comparison point.

**Verdict: CONTESTED.** Nearest-gene status is not supported by fine-mapping (41/45
variants are in PBX3 introns, not MAPKAP1), L2G ranking is systematically lower than
PBX3's at shared variants, and the literature is oncology/general-biochemistry with no
lung-disease link. This looks like a clean instance of "nearest gene by name, not by
evidence."

### STRBP — CONTESTED

**(a) Specificity:** All four citations are testis/spermatogenesis biology (fish, mouse)
or EMT/splicing regulation in pancreatic cancer and lens epithelium — none lung- or
COPD-relevant.

**(b) Smoking/pulmonary/immune:** Unsupported; no citation touches any of the three.

**(c) Coordinate discrepancy — independently confirmed:** [Skeptic] fetched Ensembl
directly (not just trusting the researcher's own lookup) and got the identical
coordinates: chr9:123,109,494-123,269,102 (GRCh38), ~1.53 Mb outside the locus window's
start boundary (124,800,001). STRBP is not plausibly at this locus as the locus is
currently bounded. This is either a genome-build mismatch in the MAGMA pipeline (e.g.,
GRCh37 coordinates misread as GRCh38), an overly generous MAGMA gene-window setting, or a
locus-boundary-definition error upstream of this analysis — any of which should be checked
before STRBP is carried forward. Combined with zero OT COPD evidence and zero
disease-relevant literature, there are now three independent reasons to doubt STRBP (wrong
location, no genetic database support, no relevant biology).

**Verdict: CONTESTED.** Recommend synthesis-lead treat this as likely a MAGMA/coordinate-
pipeline artifact rather than a genuine candidate at this locus, and flag for a
build/window sanity check.

### PTGS1 — CONTESTED

**(a) Specificity:** The OT COPD score (0.294) is driven almost entirely by
clinical_precedence (approved NSAID drug target), not genetics — zero gwas_credible_sets
evidence. This is a case where a superficially substantial-looking database score
conflates "this gene is a drug target for a respiratory-adjacent condition" with "this
gene has genetic evidence at this COPD locus." The literature itself is pharmacology of a
different disease (aspirin-exacerbated respiratory disease, a subset of asthma) via
drug-induced COX-1 inhibition — not genetic variation in PTGS1 causing COPD.

**(b) Smoking/pulmonary/immune:** Not supported; the AERD mechanism (loss of protective
PGE2 → mast-cell/leukotriene bronchoconstriction upon NSAID exposure) is a
pharmacodynamic adverse-event pathway, categorically distinct from smoking-mediated or
structural COPD biology.

**(c) Contradicting evidence:** Pidgeon et al. 2004 is a direct negative finding — COX-1
knockout had no effect on hypoxia-induced pulmonary hypertension in rats, while COX-2
knockout worsened it. This argues against a structural/vascular pulmonary role for COX-1
loss-of-function, which is the more relevant genetic model (COPD GWAS signals typically
act via expression/dosage, closer to a knockout/knockdown model than to acute
pharmacological inhibition).

**(d) Coordinate discrepancy — independently confirmed:** Ensembl REST lookup gives
chr9:122,370,530-122,395,877 (GRCh38), ~2.40 Mb outside the locus window — the largest
offset of any candidate gene at this locus. PTGS1 is not plausibly within the credible-set
LD block as currently defined.

**Verdict: CONTESTED.** No genetic (L2G/credible-set) evidence at all, a coordinate offset
larger than any other candidate, a literature base describing a different-disease
pharmacological mechanism rather than genetic etiology, and one direct negative finding in
a pulmonary vascular-remodeling model. This is close to the clearest case of overclaiming
among the five genes: a nontrivial-looking OT score that on inspection is genetics-free.

### PBX3-DT / LOC51145 — CONTESTED

**(a) Specificity:** No literature exists under either symbol — an evidence vacuum, not a
refutation.

**(b) Smoking/pulmonary/immune:** Cannot be assessed; no data.

**(c) Internal contradiction / LD bias:** PBX3-DT is divergently transcribed from PBX3,
meaning the two genes very plausibly share a bidirectional promoter/regulatory element. An
ABC (activity-by-contact) enhancer-gene link to PBX3-DT could simply be indexing the same
regulatory activity that also targets PBX3, rather than providing independent evidence
that the lncRNA itself is the functional unit. This is a case worth flagging explicitly
under the "LD/nearest-gene bias" heading, generalized to "shared-promoter bias": the ABC
signal may not distinguish PBX3-DT from PBX3 at all.

**Verdict: CONTESTED** — not because of contradicting evidence, but because of total
absence of evidence beyond a single computational prioritization method whose signal is
plausibly non-independent of the far-better-evidenced neighboring gene PBX3. Recommend
synthesis-lead treat ABC-prioritization of PBX3-DT as likely reflecting shared regulatory
architecture with PBX3, not as independent support for the lncRNA as a distinct causal
gene.

### PBX3 — WEAKENED (not CONTESTED, but flagged with substantial caveats)

**(a) Specificity:** Mixed. The strongest quantitative case (highest OT COPD score 0.431,
highest genetic_association score 0.709, L2G-style scores 0.47-0.78 consistently beating
MAPKAP1 at every shared variant across 7 studies, 41/45 credible-set variants in its
introns) is a database/statistical case, not a literature case. The literature is almost
entirely borrowed from unrelated contexts: hematopoiesis/leukemia biochemistry (Milech,
Abramovich), NSCLC oncology (Zhao et al. — cancer cell lines, not COPD), and Pbx1/2/3
combined-knockout developmental biology in spleen/general organogenesis (Zewdu, Selleri)
— not lung. A targeted search for PBX3 + FEV1/lung-function GWAS, + asthma GWAS, +
smoking-initiation GWAS, + lung branching morphogenesis, + lung immune-cell function
returned zero hits, per the researcher. That is a striking literature vacuum for the
top-ranked gene at the locus.

**(b) Smoking vs. pulmonary-function vs. immune:** The OT colocalization pattern is
informative and should be weighed carefully: strong coloc (h4>0.8) with "Smoking status –
Current," asthma, and FinnGen "chronic lower respiratory diseases," but inconsistent coloc
with FEV1 (0.36-0.81) and with COPD itself (0.02-0.95 depending on study pair). That
pattern — strong on smoking/asthma/broad-chronic-respiratory-disease, weak/inconsistent on
FEV1 and COPD-specific — is more consistent with prior #1 (smoking-mediated or broad
airway-inflammatory-disease pathway) than with prior #2 (direct pulmonary-function/
structural biology). But this is a database-coloc-only claim: the one primary-literature
citation offered to substantiate the smoking link (GSCAN, Liu et al. 2019) was flagged
CLAIM_MISMATCH — the abstract never mentions PBX3 or 9q33. So the smoking-mediation story
is asserted by OT's pipeline, not corroborated by any primary literature the researcher
could find. Treat it as a plausible but literature-unconfirmed hypothesis, not established
biology.

**(c) Internal contradictions:** Two significant ones. (1) The one piece of evidence that
most directly ties PBX3 to lung tissue — Williams et al.'s PBX3-intronic variant,
genome-wide-significant for hospitalised respiratory infection and colocalizing with a
lung eQTL (risk allele → decreased PBX3 expression) — failed to replicate in the Stage-2
meta-analysis of 11 cohorts. This was the single most mechanistically direct (tissue-
specific, directionally-resolved) result in the entire PBX3 literature base, and it didn't
hold up. (2) The COPD-itself colocalization ranges from h4=0.02 to h4=0.95 across
different GWAS Catalog COPD study pairs — internally inconsistent evidence for the trait
the pipeline actually cares about, on top of a locus that is described as a broad
pleiotropic LD block (co-localizing with HDL subfractions, height, dental/pain
phenotypes), raising real concern about whether "the PBX3 signal" is one causal mechanism
or several distinct signals bundled by LD.

**(d) TALE-homeodomain identity — smoking/neurobehavioral vs. lung-developmental vs.
ambiguous:** Genuinely ambiguous, but if forced to weight the available evidence, it leans
slightly toward smoking/broader-airway-inflammatory-disease over classic
structural/developmental COPD biology — driven by the OT coloc pattern (strong with
smoking-status and asthma/chronic-lower-resp-disease, weak/inconsistent with FEV1 and
COPD-itself) and by the complete absence of any literature on PBX3 in lung development or
airway epithelial biology specifically (its developmental role is demonstrated in
hematopoiesis/spleen/general organogenesis, not lung — extending that to "lung
development" is analogy, not evidence). This lean is weak and should be reported as a
hypothesis, not a conclusion.

**Verdict: WEAKENED.** The fine-mapping and relative L2G ranking genuinely distinguish
PBX3 from its neighbors (this is not a nearest-gene-bias problem — quite the opposite,
PBX3 correctly outranks the nearest gene MAPKAP1). But the inference that PBX3 has a
demonstrated, COPD-specific, lung-relevant mechanism overreaches what the evidence shows:
the single lung-tissue-specific finding failed replication, the smoking-mediation claim
lacks primary-literature confirmation, the COPD/FEV1 colocalization is internally
inconsistent across studies, and the locus is pleiotropic enough that mechanism (smoking
vs. structural vs. immune vs. something else entirely) remains genuinely unresolved.
Report PBX3 as the strongest gene-identity candidate at this locus with an unresolved,
contested mechanism — not as a validated COPD-lung gene.

## Disclosed search-coverage limitations (both subagents)

- **Researcher**: WebSearch budget was exhausted partway through the research task
  (session-level limit); all further research relied on PubMed and bioRxiv/medRxiv MCP
  tools (already the pipeline's stated primary source). WebFetch (used for Ensembl REST
  API lookups) remained available.
- **Red-team-skeptic**: All WebSearch calls failed on an exhausted session-wide budget
  before returning any results — no independent literature search or contradiction-hunting
  was performed for any gene in this critique. The two WebFetch coordinate checks (STRBP,
  PTGS1 via Ensembl REST) succeeded and independently corroborate the researcher's own
  coordinate flags exactly. Everything else in the skeptic's critique above is
  critique-by-reasoning over the supplied verified findings, not new evidence-gathering.
  This gap is most consequential for PBX3-DT and MAPKAP1, where an independent
  contradiction-search was never completed by anyone in this pipeline run.

evidence table, per the researcher's own judgment call, which synthesis-lead did not
