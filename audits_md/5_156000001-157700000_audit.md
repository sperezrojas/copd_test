# Audit trail — locus 5:156,000,001-157,700,000 (nearest gene ADAM19)

This file records everything EXCLUDED from the final evidence table
(`evidence_tables/5_156000001-157700000.md`), plus the full red-team critique reasoning
(including CONTESTED/WEAKENED calls), and process notes on how ambiguous verifier verdicts
were handled. This is a separate top-level directory from `evidence_tables/` so evidence

## Excluded citations (CLAIM_MISMATCH / ambiguous-verdict)

1. **Pérez-Rubio et al. 2016, COPD (journal); PMID 27078193; DOI
   10.3109/15412555.2016.1161017.**
   Claim as submitted by researcher: in smokers with/without COPD, ADAM19 rs1422794
   associated with lower risk of severe (GOLD III+IV) disease, while an ADAM19 haplotype
   was associated with higher risk (OR=2.83) of severe COPD.
   Verifier verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH. The article exists (confirmed via PMID/DOI) but full
   text is paywalled; the verifier could not confirm the specific rs1422794/OR=2.83 claims
   from any accessible source. Excluded pending independent access to full text.

2. **Kim W et al. 2026, Genome Biol; PMID 41535900; DOI 10.1186/s13059-025-03921-y.**
   Claim as submitted by researcher: large multi-ancestry TOPMed WGS study found
   colocalization with lung-tissue eQTL (LTRC) for ADAM19 expression, and lung scRNA-seq
   showed epithelial/immune-cell enrichment specifically for ADAM19.
   Verifier verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH. The eQTL-colocalization portion of the claim (ADAM19
   colocalizing with LTRC lung-tissue eQTL) was independently confirmed from the abstract.
   However, the scRNA-seq epithelial/immune-cell enrichment finding is attributed in the
   actual abstract to a DIFFERENT gene, HMCN1, not ADAM19 — the researcher's claim
   misattributed this specific finding. Per pipeline rule, a CLAIM_MISMATCH verdict
   excludes the citation wholesale from the evidence table rather than allowing a
   claim-by-claim split; the correctly-supported eQTL-colocalization fact is separately
   available from the Open Targets structured-database lookup (see main evidence table's
   Open Targets summary section) and does not depend on this citation. Note: the verifier
   also found this paper has a published erratum (PMID 42265570, 2026-06-09) — worth
   checking whether the erratum affects the eQTL-colocalization finding if this paper is
   revisited.

3. **Chesneau V et al. 2003, J Biol Chem; PMID 12682046; DOI 10.1074/jbc.M302781200.**
   Claim as submitted by researcher: recombinant ADAM19 cleaves TNF-alpha, TRANCE/RANKL,
   and KIT-ligand-1 cleavage-site peptides in vitro, and negatively regulates KIT-ligand-1
   shedding in cells.
   Verifier verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH. The in vitro cleavage of TNF-alpha and TRANCE/RANKL
   peptides was confirmed. However, the claim that ADAM19 "negatively regulates KIT-
   ligand-1 shedding" overstates what the abstract actually supports; the paper's finding
   about constitutive TNF-alpha release increase is a different (and separately, correctly
   cited) claim from Zheng et al. 2004, which IS included in the main table. Excluded
   wholesale per pipeline rule.

## Full red-team-skeptic critique (verbatim)

### 1. Is "ADAM19 is best-supported" justified, or overclaimed?

Partially overclaimed. The statistical-genetics tier is genuinely the strongest of the five
candidates: Hancock 2010 is a real genome-wide-significant FEV1/FVC hit (P=8.32e-10), which
under prior 2 (spirometry concordance) is legitimate strong evidence for pulmonary biology.
That part holds.

But the synthesis paragraph claims "convergent statistical genetics + structural/
developmental biology + eQTL colocalization," and two of those three legs don't hold up:

- Structural/developmental biology is not pulmonary. Kurohara 2004 is a cardiac phenotype
  (VSD, immature valves via endocardial-cushion EMT) — this is being used analogically to
  argue for a lung-structural mechanism, exactly the same evidentiary move the researcher
  correctly criticized for TIMD4 (non-pulmonary tissue precedent). Applying a double
  standard here is the clearest overclaim in the write-up.
- eQTL colocalization is not lung-specific. The colocalizing tissues are esophagus, skin,
  testis, stomach, LCL, fat, muscle, colon, breast — explicitly not lung in the sampled
  data. Citing "eQTL colocalization" as a pillar of a "genuine pulmonary mechanism"
  argument, without noting it doesn't include lung tissue, is an overclaim.

Additional weaknesses the synthesis underweights: COPD case-control evidence for ADAM19
itself is nominal (Wilk 2012, not genome-wide-significant; Castaldi 2011, FDR 5% only) — the
strong signal is for a spirometry trait, not disease case-control. London 2014's sequencing
found no rare-variant burden and only one additional common variant on conditioning —
arguing against a rich coding/causal-variant story despite the missense rs1422795 being in
the credible set (PIP only 0.046 — low). The TNF-alpha sheddase role (Zheng 2004) is
explicitly secondary to ADAM17. Immune evidence is muscle (Dehmel, myopathy)/kidney (Wang,
AKI)/exploratory bioinformatics (Liu 2025, rat model + herbal-formula intervention, no
functional validation of ADAM19 itself) — none is confirmed human lung tissue mechanism.

Worth noting for the record: of the four citations the verifier rejected across this whole
locus, three concern ADAM19 (Pérez-Rubio, Chesneau, and the Kim 2026 paper whose
cell-type-enrichment claim was actually about a different gene, HMCN1, entirely). ADAM19
has the most total citations so some base-rate effect is expected, but this pattern is still
worth flagging as a sign the ADAM19 case was assembled somewhat loosely and deserves the
most scrutiny, not the least.

Net: the genetic-association tier genuinely favors ADAM19 over the other four candidates.
The mechanistic "convergent evidence" framing is overclaimed — most of it is analogical/
non-pulmonary/exploratory, no better in kind than what was dismissed for TIMD4 and SGCD.

### 2. Does never-smoker stratification rule out smoking-mediation?

No — it rules out one specific hypothesis (that the association is purely an artifact of
nicotine-dependence/smoking-behavior pathways operating only in smokers) but does not
establish the alternative the researcher jumps to ("genuine pulmonary-structural biology").
Legitimate points in ADAM19's favor: the signal persists in never/non-current smokers (Kim
2020 GWAS) and in an 8-year-old pediatric cohort pre-dating meaningful smoking exposure
(Panasevich, though this is a single modest, non-replicated study, p=0.03). Together these
are reasonable evidence against the locus being a pure smoking-behavior/neurobiological
signal. But: (a) this doesn't tell us the mechanism is structural rather than immune,
developmental-non-structural, or an environmental-exposure-response pathway unrelated to
nicotine; (b) it doesn't address whether ADAM19 acts on smoke-exposure-conditional lung
damage response (a form of smoking-mediated pulmonary biology distinct from
nicotine-dependence behavior) — the Vishweswaraiah cigarette-smoke-exposed-mouse-lung
finding and the Liu 2025 COPD-rat-model finding are both explicitly smoke-exposure-
conditional, which sits awkwardly next to the "not smoking-mediated" framing. The
researcher's inferential chain (not-smoking-behavior-mediated -> therefore genuine
structural biology) has a gap.

### 3. CONTESTED calls

SGCD vs. ADAM19 — flag as the real tension at this locus, short of full CONTESTED but
close. SGCD has something ADAM19 conspicuously lacks: a lung eQTL (P=2.57e-9) and protein
localized to airway smooth muscle — an actual COPD-relevant lung tissue/cell type. ADAM19's
eQTL colocalization, by contrast, spans nine non-lung tissues and none in lung. If
tissue-specific eQTL/expression evidence is a criterion for prioritization (as priors 2/3
imply it should be), SGCD's evidence is more tissue-relevant than ADAM19's on this specific
axis, even though SGCD's actual statistical association (Hansel 2015) is weaker (suggestive
only, P 9.57e-8-4.6e-6, not genome-wide-significant, and restricted to a narrow
subphenotype — airway hyperresponsiveness — within an all-smoker cohort, so smoking-
mediation cannot be excluded for SGCD either). No paper explicitly arguing "it's SGCD, not
ADAM19" at this locus was found (searches could not be run — budget exhausted), so this
stops short of full CONTESTED, but the researcher's framing of SGCD as merely having a
"plausible mechanistic story" while ADAM19 is "clear best-supported" undersells this
asymmetry and should be revisited if a real contradiction search becomes possible.

TIMD4 — WEAKENED, not CONTESTED. Zero L2G, zero COPD/lung evidence of any kind; all six
citations are non-pulmonary tissue analogies (skin, liver, gut, bone, Listeria infection).
Worth flagging that MAGMA gene-based tests are proximity/LD-sensitive and can prioritize a
gene purely because it sits near the true signal in a gene-dense region — the disconnect
between MAGMA prioritization and zero L2G evidence is itself a red flag consistent with
LD/nearest-gene-block bias rather than a real TIMD4 mechanism.

CYFIP2 and NIPAL4 — HOLDS as bystander calls. PIP <0.001 for both, no COPD-specific
literature beyond one exploratory bioinformatic clustering paper (CYFIP2), and both genes'
well-established Mendelian disease biology (severe neurodevelopmental disease; congenital
ichthyosis) is respiratory-irrelevant. The researcher's "likely bystander" conclusion for
these two is well-supported by what's verified.

### 4. Credible-set size/LD as alternative explanation

A 163-variant credible set spanning a locus window of 1.7 Mb (156.0-157.7 Mb) is large and
diffuse — consistent with a broad, incompletely resolved LD block rather than a single
cleanly fine-mapped causal gene. Given that rs4579242 (lead variant) sits in an ADAM19
intron and ABC/L2G prioritization is heavily influenced by physical proximity, the "highest
L2G score" finding for ADAM19 is at least partly consistent with a nearest-gene/proximity
prior rather than independent causal proof. SGCD's presence as a PoPS-prioritized candidate
in the same broad interval, with its own lung-relevant expression data, is exactly the
scenario the LD caveat warns about: the fine-mapping/statistical evidence does not clearly
distinguish ADAM19 from SGCD as the operative gene. The researcher's synthesis does not
engage with this alternative explanation for the pattern of evidence.

### Per-gene verdicts (red-team-skeptic's final calls)

- ADAM19: WEAKENED. Real, comparatively strong genetic-association evidence (GWS FEV1/FVC,
  highest OT L2G/composite score) but the "convergent mechanistic" case is overstated — the
  developmental biology is cardiac not pulmonary, the eQTL colocalization is explicitly
  non-lung, immune evidence is drawn from muscle/kidney/exploratory-bioinformatic sources,
  and COPD case-control evidence itself is only nominal/FDR-level. Never-smoker
  stratification argues against nicotine-pathway mediation but does not establish a
  structural mechanism specifically.
- TIMD4: WEAKENED. Zero COPD/lung evidence; entire case is analogical (efferocytosis
  biology from skin/liver/gut/bone/infection contexts). MAGMA-only prioritization with zero
  L2G is a plausible LD/proximity artifact.
- SGCD: WEAKENED, but flagged as the strongest rival to ADAM19 on tissue-specificity
  grounds (lung eQTL, airway-smooth-muscle protein localization) even though its own
  association evidence is only suggestive and confined to a smoker-only subphenotype
  cohort, so smoking-mediation isn't excluded for SGCD either. This gene/ADAM19 pairing
  deserves a dedicated contradiction search before synthesis finalizes a single "best" gene.
- CYFIP2: HOLDS as bystander (PIP<0.001, no respiratory phenotype in its well-characterized
  disease biology, only exploratory bioinformatic COPD "hit").
- NIPAL4: HOLDS as bystander (PIP<0.001, no lung phenotype, skin-barrier/ichthyosis biology
  only).

## Other process notes

- **Smoking-behavior GWAS overlap:** the researcher explicitly flagged that no dedicated
  smoking-initiation/cigarettes-per-day/cessation GWAS hit at this 5q33 locus was found in
  PubMed searches (searches for rs4579242, rs1422795, and general terms returned zero
  hits). This is an absence-of-evidence finding, not a confirmed non-overlap — a dedicated
  GSCAN/smoking-GWAS-catalog lookup (as opposed to PubMed literature search) was not
  independently performed in this session and would be worth doing if this locus is
  revisited.
- **RNU6-390P**, hit by one low-PIP (0.0013) downstream credible-set variant (rs7724554),
  is a non-coding U6 pseudogene; per pipeline scope rules no dedicated literature search was
  performed on it, and nothing notable surfaced in passing.
- **Region-completeness check:** the researcher cross-referenced the five candidate genes
  (ADAM19, TIMD4, SGCD, CYFIP2, NIPAL4) against gene names recurring across all retrieved
  GWAS/eQTL papers for this region and found no additional protein-coding genes; however,
  this check was literature-based only (an independent Ensembl/region-lookup query was not
  run in this session) and should be treated as a secondary check, not a substitute for a
  formal region gene-list query if greater completeness assurance is needed.
