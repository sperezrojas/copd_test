## Full red-team-skeptic critique (verbatim)

### Disclosure on search coverage

I attempted four independent contradiction/verification searches before writing this critique (AGBL1 + lung function/FEV1/COPD; KLHL25 + COPD/emphysema; rs1431229 + COPD; and "what else sits in this genomic window besides AGBL1/KLHL25"). All four failed — the WebSearch tool reported the session's search budget was already exhausted (200/200 used) before I could run any of them. **I performed zero independent searches for either gene.** This critique is therefore based entirely on close, skeptical reading of the researcher's dossier and the Open Targets lookup as given, not on fresh literature. Flag this to synthesis-lead as a gap: neither AGBL1 nor KLHL25 got my independent contradiction pass, so my "CONTESTED" calls below rest on internal tensions in the provided evidence rather than new external citations I found myself.

### (a) Verdicts

**AGBL1 — WEAKENED (bordering CONTESTED).** The positional case (sole gene overlapping all 89 credible-set variants) is real but is undermined by three things the researcher's own dossier surfaces: (1) the identical fine-mapped variant/credible set is the top L2G hit at the *same score* (0.68) for diastolic blood pressure — an unresolved cross-trait pleiotropy signal that the researcher correctly notes could not be corroborated or refuted by any independent literature (rs1431229 has zero external citations); (2) zero Open Targets evidence for FEV1 or FEV1/FVC, i.e., no spirometric concordance at all; (3) AGBL1's only prior "disease-causing gene" claim (Fuchs corneal dystrophy, Riazuddin 2013) has been serially and independently debunked by at least three follow-up groups (Tsedilina 2023, Tsedilina 2025, Frausto 2015) on both genetic and expression grounds. That track record — a gene repeatedly nominated as causal by initial linkage/statistical work and then walked back once follow-up functional/genetic data arrive — is directly relevant context for evaluating a new, also-statistical (fine-mapping/L2G) causal claim at a different disease. It doesn't prove AGBL1 is wrong for COPD, but it should lower the prior placed on positional/statistical nomination alone.

**KLHL25 — WEAKENED (bordering NONSPECIFIC).** The strongest single number in the whole dossier is KLHL25's FEV1/FVC L2G score of 0.88, exceeding AGBL1's COPD L2G of 0.68, and prior 2 says spirometric concordance should strengthen prioritization. But this is undercut by an unresolved, load-bearing gap: the KLHL25 gene body sits 500-650kb outside this locus's actual credible-set window, and Open Targets/the researcher could not confirm the FEV1/FVC signal shares the same causal variant(s) as rs1431229's credible set. This could be an entirely separate, non-overlapping GWAS signal that merely lands near KLHL25 in the genome. The immune mechanism (CUL3-KLHL25-ACLY axis controlling iTreg vs. Th17 fate) is real, well-supported biochemistry and thematically consonant with prior 3's Th17/Treg-relevant COPD biology — but it was established entirely in generic mouse/human T-cell systems and lung-adenocarcinoma cell lines, never in COPD lung tissue or patients, and the only airway-correlative data are in allergic/pediatric-atopic **asthma**, a distinct (largely Th2-skewed) inflammatory program from typical neutrophilic/Tc1/Tc17 COPD. Combined with KLHL25's demonstrated non-immune, non-lineage-restricted role (4E-BP1 degradation) and its incidental appearance across many unrelated cancer gene panels, the immune story reads as plausible-but-generic rather than COPD- or even lung-specific.

### (b) Smoking / spirometry / immune-program specificity

- **Smoking-mediation:** Neither gene shows evidence of smoking-mediation as classically defined (via nicotinic receptor/dependence pathways). The locus is genomically distant (~7.5Mb) from CHRNA5-A3-B4, and no smoking-initiation/cessation/heaviness GWAS literature was found linking this locus to smoking behavior. However, this is a weaker negative than it looks: the researcher checked PubMed/GWAS-Catalog literature, not whether rs1431229 or the credible-set variants show sub-threshold associations in raw GSCAN/smoking-GWAS summary statistics. Absence of a *published* smoking-behavior hit is not equivalent to absence of smoking-mediated signal — this should be checked directly against smoking-GWAS summary stats before ruling smoking out, especially since COPD GWAS here is unadjusted for smoking per the stated prior.
- **Spirometry concordance:** This is the most important discriminator, and it cuts against AGBL1: zero FEV1/FEV1-FVC evidence for the gene that actually overlaps the credible set. Per prior 2, a COPD association without spirometric support "warrants consideration of alternative pathways" — and here we have a specific alternative already sitting in the data (DBP/vascular pleiotropy). KLHL25 has the spirometry concordance prior 2 would want to see, but it's attached to a gene that may not even share the causal variant with this locus's COPD signal. The gene with credible-set overlap lacks spirometric support; the gene with spirometric support lacks credible-set overlap. That mismatch is itself the central finding of this locus.
- **Immune-program specificity:** Only KLHL25 has an immune mechanism at all, and it is not COPD- or lung-specific: iTreg/Th17 switch biology in generic T cells, plus asthma (not COPD) correlative transcriptomic/methylation data. AGBL1 has no immune-program evidence whatsoever — the researcher explicitly found nothing linking it to any specific inflammatory cell type or program, and correctly flagged the "cilia/tubulin deglutamylation" mechanism as unsupported speculation rather than a finding.

### (c) Overclaiming / underweighting

- No clear instance of the researcher's prose overclaiming beyond what the cited abstracts support — hedging language ("correlative," "exploratory," "incidental," "unresolved," "bioinformatic") is used consistently and appropriately throughout, and the researcher proactively flagged its own AGBL1-cilia hypothesis as unsupported rather than passing it off as a finding. This is good practice.
- **Underweighted by the dossier:** the AGBL1/DBP pleiotropy deserves more emphasis than a passing mention. A variant equally likely (by L2G score) to be "the" causal variant for blood pressure as for airway obstruction, in a GWAS that is not adjusted for smoking and where COPD case ascertainment often correlates with cardiovascular multimorbidity (age, obesity, inactivity), raises a live alternative: this could be a comorbidity/confounding or collider-bias artifact rather than a lung-intrinsic mechanism, or it could reflect genuine pulmonary-vascular (rather than airway-epithelial) biology — COPD does have a vascular remodeling/pulmonary-hypertension component that would be a different mechanism than the airway/parenchymal one implicitly assumed.
- **Also underweighted:** for KLHL25, the framing "MAGMA/PoPS-prioritized gene" is stated as if it were independent, COPD-specific computational support, but the only concrete OT scores given are for FEV1/FVC and FEV1, not for COPD itself (COPD disease-term evidence for KLHL25 is zero). It's worth asking synthesis-lead to confirm whether KLHL25's MAGMA/PoPS prioritization was actually driven by COPD GWAS signal at all, or whether it was inherited/derived from the same-region spirometry GWAS — if the latter, the "two independent lines of evidence" framing (COPD prioritization + FEV1/FVC L2G) would be partly circular.
- The physical non-overlap of KLHL25 (500-650kb outside the credible set) should be weighted as seriously as the classic "nearest-gene bias" concern in the framework, just inverted: this is a distal, similarity/expression-based (PoPS) nomination reaching outside the fine-mapped interval, which is exactly the scenario the framework instructs reviewers to scrutinize.

### (d) Alternative hypotheses for synthesis-lead

1. **Vascular/pleiotropic confound, not lung-intrinsic biology.** The AGBL1 credible-set signal may be primarily a blood-pressure/vascular regulatory variant that shows up in COPD GWAS via comorbidity, collider bias in case definition, or genuine but distinct pulmonary-vascular remodeling biology (rather than airway/parenchymal mechanism). This deserves explicit weighing given no independent literature could adjudicate the cross-trait signal.
2. **Long-range regulatory target, neither AGBL1 nor KLHL25.** AGBL1 is a very large gene; large introns in such genes are known in other loci (e.g., FTO/IRX3) to harbor enhancers acting on distant genes via chromatin looping rather than the overlapping gene itself. A positional/L2G pipeline anchored on nearest overlap would miss this. No Hi-C/chromatin-interaction or eQTL colocalization evidence was reported by the researcher for this locus — that is a concrete follow-up the researcher could do (eQTL/colocalization search for the credible-set region in lung tissue, e.g., GTEx lung, before assuming AGBL1's introns act on AGBL1 itself).
3. **Overall locus strength is modest.** The Open Targets COPD association score itself (0.25 overall / 0.41 genetic-association) is not high in absolute terms. Combined with the credible-set/spirometry mismatch described above, this locus may simply be a weaker, more equivocal signal than others in the GWAS, and synthesis-lead should calibrate confidence accordingly rather than force a single-gene story.
4. **Smoking-mediation via non-classical pathways is not fully excluded** — only the classical CHRNA5-A3-B4-type mechanism was checked and ruled out by genomic distance/absence of literature; a direct check of this locus's variants against smoking-initiation/cessation/heaviness GWAS *summary statistics* (not just published papers) has not been reported and would be worth doing before fully setting prior 1 aside for this locus.
