## Genes marked CONTESTED / WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (full reasoning, verbatim)

### PITX2 — CONTESTED

The evidence assembled is honestly labeled throughout — the researcher does not overclaim
(Acquaah-Mensah correctly flagged as bioinformatic/hypothesis-generating, Banerjee correctly
flagged as developmental not adult-disease, cancer papers correctly kept separate). No
translation-creep from "associated" to "causal" is evident in the summarized claims.

But the cumulative case is weaker than the citation count suggests, and there's a live
competing explanation the evidence itself supports:

- **Genetics-heavy, biology-empty.** 5 Open Targets COPD credible-set rows (0.58-0.78) but
  PITX2 fails to crack the top 50 of 6,323 all-datasource COPD targets — meaning almost no
  non-genetic corroboration exists for PITX2-COPD specifically, despite PITX2 being a
  well-studied, well-phenotyped gene (unlike FAM241A, this isn't "understudied," it's
  "extensively studied and still nothing surfaces for COPD"). Every mechanistic lung finding
  in hand is either embryonic/developmental patterning (Banerjee) or lung-cancer-specific
  (Zhang, Wang, Soukup) — none touch adult COPD-relevant biology (protease-antiprotease
  balance, emphysema, airway remodeling, neutrophilic/CD8+/Th17 inflammation), and the
  researcher confirms this gap persisted across multiple search phrasings.
- **No spirometry concordance.** Per the pulmonary-function prior, this is exactly the
  situation that should trigger scrutiny: a COPD case-control genetic signal with no
  corroborating FEV1/FEV1-FVC hit. An independent PubMed check by the skeptic (see below)
  corroborates zero literature at the intersection of 4q25/PITX2 and lung function.
- **Smoking-mediation is directly supported, not just theoretical.** All three credible-set
  variants (including the PIP=0.556 lead SNP) hit GWAS Catalog for smoking
  initiation/cessation/status and correlated behavioral traits, with none hitting
  FEV1/FEV1-FVC/COPD directly. Per the smoking-mediation prior, this is close to the
  textbook signature of a smoking-behavior locus riding into a COPD GWAS via unadjusted
  smoking confounding.
- **A specific, already-verified paper argues against the alternative "shared
  cardiopulmonary mechanism" framing.** Naesilla/Quint/Zuber 2026 (PMID 41241414) directly
  examined COPD-AF comorbidity with bidirectional MR and found limited direct genetic
  causal evidence between COPD/CRD and AF/CVD, with the comorbidity better explained by
  shared upstream smoking and IL6R/IL1RN inflammatory pathways. This is meaningful
  contradicting evidence against treating "PITX2 is the AF gene at chr4q25, therefore it
  plausibly also drives COPD via a shared cardiopulmonary axis" as a strong inference — the
  paper cited for exactly that purpose concludes the opposite of what a naive pleiotropy
  story would predict.
- **PITX2 is the textbook example of nearest/regulatory-distance bias done right for AF,
  not necessarily for COPD.** This exact gene desert is the well-established long-range
  PITX2 cardiac enhancer architecture. That strengthens the case that if something at this
  desert acts on disease, PITX2 is the mechanistically privileged candidate over
  ENPEP/FAM241A — but the enhancers characterized in that AF literature are cardiac-tissue
  enhancers; tissue-specific enhancer activity in lung is not established here, so "PITX2 is
  the right gene for AF" does not transitively establish "PITX2 is the right gene for COPD"
  even setting the LD question aside.

Taken together, the more parsimonious explanation is that this signal reflects
smoking-behavior pleiotropy and/or AF-COPD comorbidity/ascertainment overlap in an older,
heavily-smoking population, rather than a direct PITX2 lung mechanism. PITX2 should be
presented as the strongest of the three by genetic score but with the smoking/AF-confound
explicitly flagged rather than treated as a clean lung-biology hit.

### ENPEP — WEAKENED

- Genetic support is markedly weaker than PITX2 (OT score 0.093, doesn't rank top 50) and
  the gene is essentially LoF-tolerant (obs/exp 0.857), which argues against a
  dosage-sensitive causal mechanism (in contrast to PITX2's constraint).
- The literature offered is honestly framed as non-COPD: the 1993 paper's immune angle
  (BP-1/6C3 B-lymphocyte differentiation marker) is about B-lymphopoiesis, not the
  neutrophilic/CD8+/Th17/macrophage programs the immune-complexity prior flags as
  COPD-relevant — a mismatch the researcher doesn't paper over. The 2024 Arppo atlas paper
  is COVID/ACE2-cofactor focused, noting para-alveolar fibroblast expression as an
  incidental finding, not a COPD result.
- No overclaiming detected — both citations are used only for baseline
  expression/discovery facts, not COPD claims.
- An alternative-pleiotropy pattern parallel to PITX2 was flagged but not pursued: 9
  additional PMIDs link ENPEP to hypertension/blood-pressure genetics, unexamined by the
  researcher (listed above). ENPEP (aminopeptidase A) has a known mechanistic role in the
  renin-angiotensin system (Ang II -> Ang III). Given COPD and hypertension share risk
  factors (age, smoking, systemic inflammation) and cardiometabolic comorbidity is common in
  COPD cohorts, this is a live alternative explanation — ENPEP's genetically best-supported
  disease association elsewhere may be cardiovascular/BP-related, echoing the PITX2/AF
  situation — that the researcher's own findings surface but leave unresolved.

The evidence assembled is real but thin (weak score, broad/nonspecific tissue expression, an
off-topic immune marker discovery, a COVID-focused atlas paper) and does not support
treating ENPEP as a COPD-relevant lung gene; the inference that ENPEP belongs in this
candidate set rests almost entirely on physical proximity in a gene desert rather than on
any COPD-relevant biology, and an unresolved hypertension-pleiotropy alternative should be
checked before this gene is weighted at all in synthesis.

### FAM241A / C4orf32 — CONTESTED

- Zero COPD genetic evidence of any kind (0 Open Targets credible-set rows for COPD, versus
  PITX2's 5 and ENPEP's 1) — the weakest genetic case of the three despite being labeled
  "nearest gene."
- All literature is lung-cancer bioinformatic/correlational (MR+coloc gene-prioritization
  screen for lung cancer risk; a computationally-predicted ceRNA "tumor suppressive axis"
  in Cr(VI)-transformed bronchial cells) — neither paper makes any COPD claim, and both are
  appropriately labeled hypothesis-generating rather than mechanistic by the researcher. No
  overclaiming detected.
- The gene is functionally uncharacterized beyond these two cancer-correlation papers — no
  domain, no pathway, no developmental role, no COPD-relevant immune or structural biology
  of any kind.
- The "nearest gene" framing is itself questionable in this instance: the locus is a
  documented gene desert with the well-characterized chr4q25/PITX2 long-range regulatory
  architecture as backdrop. Given this is the classic distal-PITX2-enhancer desert, the
  linear-distance argument for FAM241A being the functional target is undercut by a
  well-known competing mechanistic story (PITX2 regulation) at the same desert — this is
  exactly the nearest-gene-bias scenario the pipeline's priors warn about, and here it isn't
  hypothetical: there's a specific, well-established alternative target gene for this exact
  desert architecture.

Not on the strength of a paper specifically refuting FAM241A, but because (a) it has zero
COPD genetic support of any kind — the weakest of the three by a wide margin, (b) all its
literature is cancer-specific and correlational, and (c) the desert's well-established
regulatory relationship is with PITX2, a different gene, which the pipeline's own
structural context supplies as a directly competing explanation for this same locus. This
satisfies the "implicates a different gene at the same locus" contradiction criterion using
material already in the record (no new citation needed).

- ENPEP: fetch ("ENPEP COPD") was also blocked by the same cookie-consent interstitial —
  inconclusive, no independent check obtained.
- FAM241A/C4orf32: zero independent search or fetch attempts made (budget exhausted before

So: PITX2 got a shallow-but-partially-successful independent pass, ENPEP got a
blocked/inconclusive attempt, FAM241A got no independent pass at all. This critique's
contradiction-search coverage is materially incomplete, particularly for ENPEP and
FAM241A, and a follow-up pass should be considered if resources allow.

## Locus-level synthesis note (from red-team-skeptic, preserved verbatim)

Across all three genes, the single most load-bearing fact is the GWAS Catalog
smoking-behavior check: all three credible-set variants hit smoking-initiation/
cessation/behavioral-trait GWAS records and none hit FEV1/FEV1-FVC/COPD directly. Per the
smoking-mediation and pulmonary-function priors, this is close to the canonical signature
of a smoking-mediated signal rather than a direct-lung-biology signal, and it should anchor
overall confidence at this locus regardless of which of the three genes is favored. One
important caveat (skeptic's own reasoning, not a new citation): GSCAN/Saunders-type
multi-trait smoking-behavior GWAS are enormous (hundreds of thousands to millions of
participants) and report genome-wide-significant hits at thousands of loci genome-wide,
some with very small effect sizes — a GWAS Catalog "hit" alone doesn't establish that
smoking behavior is a large or dominant contributor at this specific locus, only that it's
plausible and unrefuted. The researcher's own caveat that PMID-to-SNP-row attribution
wasn't literally confirmed (inferred from phenotype/method-signature matching) compounds
this — treat the smoking-mediation case as directionally strong but not fully nailed down,
not as fully closed.
