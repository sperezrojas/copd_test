## Excluded citations

### CLAIM_MISMATCH

**Bravo-Gutiérrez OA, Falfán-Valencia R et al. 2021. "Hypomethylation of AHRR (cg05575921)
Is Related to Smoking Status in the Mexican Mestizo Population." Genes. PMID 34440450, DOI
10.3390/genes12081276.**

Researcher's claim as stated: "companion/earlier study by the same Mexican group also
assayed PRSS23 cg23771366 alongside the primary AHRR cg05575921 signal; AHRR was
significantly hypomethylated in smokers but the abstract does not report a significant
PRSS23 finding in this smaller sample."

Citation-verifier verdict: **CLAIM_MISMATCH.** "Abstract focuses on AHRR cg05575921
hypomethylation in smokers. The abstract specifically states 'We observed significant
hypomethylation of cg05575921 in smokers compared to NS' and discusses only this site in
detail. However, keywords list 'PRSS23, cg23771366' indicating these were studied. The
abstract does NOT report PRSS23 cg23771366 results explicitly — it only discusses AHRR
cg05575921. This is a partial mismatch: the paper did study PRSS23 cg23771366 but the
abstract doesn't report the findings for this site."

Disposition: excluded from the evidence table. The paper's existence and its AHRR finding
are real, but the specific PRSS23-related claim attributed to it in the researcher's
write-up is not supported by the verifiable abstract text, so it does not meet the bar for
a VERIFIED row.

No RETRACTED or NOT_FOUND citations were identified for this locus — all other 24 citations
returned by the researcher were verdict VERIFIED by the citation-verifier.

## CONTESTED genes (red-team-skeptic reasoning, verbatim)

### CCDC81 — WEAKENED, bordering on CONTESTED for the causal-gene claim

> The inference moves from "sits under the peak, is annotated by convention" to "is
> functionally plausible via ciliogenesis" without acknowledging that a purpose-built
> statistical model looking at this exact signal assigns it zero probability, and that its
> own direct functional literature is non-lung basic cell biology stitched together with a
> categorically different disease (PCD) for plausibility. The competing positive L2G scores
> for PRSS23 and ME3 at the same locus function as de facto contradicting evidence for
> CCDC81's primacy — recommend CONTESTED for "CCDC81 is the most likely effector gene,"
> HOLDS only for the narrower, purely descriptive claim that the credible set sits in
> CCDC81 introns.

Supporting detail from the same critique:

> Open Targets L2G = 0 for both COPD and FEV1 despite CCDC81 being the nearest gene and the
> annotation gene for the credible set. L2G models incorporate distance as a feature, so a
> zero score for the nearest gene is not "absence of evidence" — it is evidence that an
> integrative model examining chromatin interaction, eQTL, and other regulatory features
> actively deprioritized CCDC81. This is a meaningful data point against CCDC81 as the
> effector gene...

> Leigh 2009 is doing rhetorical bridging work — PCD is a distinct, rare Mendelian
> ciliopathy with a completely different genetic architecture and inheritance pattern than
> common-variant COPD risk; citing it to make "ciliogenesis gene → COPD-relevant" plausible
> is a leap the abstract itself does not support.

### PRSS23 — CONTESTED

> Not because a paper argues against PRSS23 specifically, but because (1) a citation
> attributed to it was already caught fabricating/overstating support, (2) its only
> "mechanistic" data are cross-tissue and non-lung, (3) its strongest remaining human data
> (EWAS) are confounded by plausible reverse causation that the researcher's writeup does
> not appear to rule out, and (4) its statistical support (FLAMES) is not independent of
> L2G. Recommend synthesis lead treat PRSS23 as the weakest-supported of the three
> literature-wise despite having the mid-range L2G score, and treat all its remaining
> citations as needing a second look.

Supporting detail:

> EWAS associations showing PRSS23 differential methylation in smokers/COPD patients cannot,
> on their own, distinguish: 1. A germline mQTL mechanism (credible-set variants → altered
> PRSS23 methylation/expression → disease risk)... 2. Reverse causation (smoke exposure
> itself alters methylation at this locus, independent of genotype)... 3. Disease-state-
> associated methylation (COPD pathology changes methylation as a downstream effect, not a
> cause). Nothing in the material indicates the researcher checked whether these
> differentially methylated CpGs are under mQTL control by the actual credible-set variants
> ... Absent that, citing smoking/COPD EWAS as support for PRSS23's causal role in this GWAS
> signal is a meaningful overclaim.

> Researcher correctly flagged that PRSS23 sits ~300-470kb from the credible-set window and
> that FLAMES prioritization rests on long-range regulatory linkage, not direct overlap...
> PRSS23's case rests almost entirely on the strength of one computational method (FLAMES)
> layered on top of the same-flavor L2G score (0.482 COPD) already given as context — this
> is largely the same type of statistical evidence counted twice, not independent
> corroboration.

### ME3 — WEAKENED (not CONTESTED)

> No credible contradicting evidence was surfaced against ME3... The gap here is
> under-evidencing, not contradiction: ME3 is the best-supported gene by computational
> prioritization but has essentially zero direct literature support beyond that, and the
> oxidative-stress story is generic enough that it should not be allowed to read as
> independent corroboration of the L2G score — it is the same "this gene could plausibly
> matter" inference restated in narrative form, not a second, independent line of evidence.

ME3 is included here for completeness (not excluded from the main evidence table) since its
WEAKENED verdict is materially different from CONTESTED and no specific claim was disputed.

## Other limitations flagged by the red-team-skeptic, carried forward

- **Search-budget exhaustion:** the red-team-skeptic agent exhausted its WebSearch budget
  (200/200) before it could independently pull the researcher/verifier checkpoint files or
  run confirmatory/contradicting-evidence searches of its own. Its critique was built
  entirely from the detailed context supplied in the dispatch prompt (which itself was
  drawn faithfully from the researcher and verifier checkpoint files) plus general domain
  knowledge, not independent primary-source re-verification. This is a genuine limitation
  of this locus's review and is disclosed here for transparency.
- **Contradicting/alternative-gene search not run:** no pipeline stage (researcher,
  citation-verifier, or red-team-skeptic) ran an explicit search for literature naming a
  different gene at 11:86.3-86.5 Mb, or literature arguing against CCDC81/PRSS23/ME3
  specifically. This remains an open task for a future iteration of this review before the
  ME3 prioritization above should be treated as final.
- **Smoking-trait GWAS check — resolved, not a gap:** the red-team-skeptic initially flagged
  this as incomplete, but the researcher had in fact checked it (see researcher.md,
  "Smoking-behavior GWAS overlap" section) and found no PubMed-indexed evidence that
  rs11234628 or this locus is a reported smoking-initiation/cessation/heaviness GWAS signal.
  This is a genuine negative finding, distinct from the separate PRSS23
  methylation-smoking-exposure literature discussed above.
- **HIKESHI thoroughness flag — resolved, not a gap:** the red-team-skeptic initially
  flagged uncertainty over whether HIKESHI (physically in-window, not a pipeline candidate)
  received any literature search. The researcher had in fact searched and found no
  lung/COPD-relevant literature for HIKESHI (see researcher.md and the evidence table's
  HIKESHI row, PMID 28000699, an unrelated Mendelian CNS disease association).
