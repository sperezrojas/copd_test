## Excluded citations (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH)

### 1. Liu M, Jiang Y, Wedow R, et al. "Association studies of up to 1.2 million individuals
yield new insights into the genetic etiology of tobacco and alcohol use." Nat Genet.
2019;51(2):237-244. PMID 30643251, DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0307-5.

**Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH.**
Researcher's claim: this is the largest tobacco/alcohol-use GWAS (406 loci) and the standard
reference for asking whether the TNRC6A/16q13 locus is smoking-behavior mediated; researcher
explicitly could not confirm from PubMed abstract/metadata whether this region reached
genome-wide significance for smoking initiation, cessation, or cigarettes/day.
Citation-verifier's finding: the paper is real and correctly described (566 variants, 406
loci, 150 pleiotropic loci), but neither the abstract nor accessible text mentions
TNRC6A/16q13 specifically — the claim of relevance to this locus could not be substantiated
(would require a supplementary-table lookup not completed). Excluded from the main table
because the citation does not actually support (or refute) a TNRC6A-smoking link; treating its
mere existence as "context" would overstate what was verified. The underlying question
(is this locus smoking-behavior mediated?) remains genuinely open — see the Prioritized gene
section of the main table.

### GENE: TNRC6A

**LD/nearest-gene bias — not a major concern here.** This is an unusually clean locus: TNRC6A
spans essentially the entire credible set, is the sole candidate across FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS/
ABC/nearest-gene, and the two flanking genes (SLC5A11, ARHGAP17) have no credible-set overlap
and no prioritization support. I have no basis to doubt gene assignment on LD grounds alone.

**But the pleiotropy point cuts the other way and the researcher underweights it.** TNRC6A is
the top L2G gene at this position for ~20 unrelated traits (HDL subfractions, adiposity/BMI,
eGFR, **educational attainment/cognitive function**). The researcher reports this as
"structured context, not a literature claim" and moves on, but this is actually load-bearing
for prior #1: educational attainment is a well-established genetic/phenotypic correlate of
smoking initiation and heaviness (SES-mediated pathways), and eGFR/adiposity/lipid pleiotropy
is the signature of a regulatory hub locus with broad, non-lung-specific transcriptional
effects rather than a lung-restricted mechanism. This raises — but does not resolve — an
alternative hypothesis (locus acts on a shared regulatory/behavioral axis, with COPD risk
arising via smoking-correlated behavior/SES rather than direct pulmonary biology) that the
researcher's write-up doesn't explicitly weigh against the ciliogenesis mechanism. This should
be surfaced to synthesis-lead as an open question, not resolved as either confirmation or
refutation.

**Smoking-mediation (prior 1) — genuinely unresolved, not "ruled out."** The one large
smoking-GWAS check (Liu 2019, 406 loci) came back CLAIM_MISMATCH — the abstract doesn't mention
the locus, but the verifier also couldn't confirm absence (would need supplementary tables).
The researcher correctly declines to claim this as either supporting or refuting a smoking
link, but then doesn't flag the corollary: **absence of a positive smoking-GWAS hit is not
evidence against smoking-mediation either**, especially given the educational-attainment
pleiotropy noted above. Treat this dimension as an open gap, not a cleared prior.

**Spirometry (prior 2) — this is the most important weakness and I think the researcher's
framing under-states it.** There is **no FEV1/FEV1-FVC hit anywhere in the literature
searched**, and — more pointedly — the locus is **absent from the 82 loci in Sakornsakolpat
2019** (the major prior COPD case-control GWAS) and absent from the DLCO GWAS. The only
"evidence" of a TNRC6A-COPD genetic association at all is the Open Targets L2G score, which is
a model output over presumably the same (or a related) GWAS underlying this locus file — i.e.,
**not independent replication, potentially close to circular.** Per prior 2, "a COPD
association without supporting pulmonary-function evidence warrants consideration of
alternative pathways" — this locus is a textbook case of that warning, and I'd flag it more
strongly than the researcher did. This absence could simply reflect discovery in a
larger/more recent meta-analysis (novel loci not in a 2019 GWAS is unsurprising), but the
researcher has not made that affirmative case, and neither could I (search budget). This is a
genuine, currently unaddressed gap.

**Immune/inflammatory (prior 3) — essentially null for COPD-relevant programs.** The only
immune-adjacent citation (Bhanji 2007) is generic systemic autoimmune/neurologic autoantigen
biology (Sjögren's, SLE, RA, PBC) — off-target relative to neutrophilic/CD8+/Th1/Th17/
macrophage programs relevant to COPD. No COPD-relevant immune mechanism was found. The
researcher states this plainly and doesn't overclaim it — correct handling.

**Structural/developmental lung biology — the genuine strength, appropriately caveated.** Liu
2023 (PMID 37127654) is real, TNRC6A-specific (not paralog), lung-relevant, mechanistic
evidence: TNRC6A/AGO2 apical granules required for multiciliogenesis in airway epithelium, with
LOF disrupting ciliogenesis gene expression. This is plausibly relevant to COPD via mucociliary
clearance impairment, a recognized component of COPD pathophysiology, and is the one piece of
evidence in this whole record that argues for a direct, non-smoking, non-generic pulmonary
mechanism. The researcher correctly separates this from the Guo 2017 paralog (TNRC6C) data and
correctly notes that full TNRC6A knockouts are embryonic lethal, so no in vivo TNRC6A-specific
lung phenotype has actually been demonstrated — this is honest hedging, not overclaiming. Dai
2018 (lung cancer/circRNA) is tangential and correctly kept as weak circumstantial support
only.

**Overclaiming check:** I did not find instances where the one-line domain claims materially
exceed the underlying quoted text — the researcher has been unusually disciplined here (e.g.,
explicitly separating TNRC6A from TNRC6C findings, explicitly labeling the Open Targets L2G
score as non-literature, explicitly flagging the Moser rsID mismatch as unresolved). This is a
well-hedged write-up; if anything it slightly underclaims by not drawing out the
educational-attainment/pleiotropy implication above.

**Rejected-citation pattern is informative and under-discussed.** Two of the most directly
relevant papers one would want as positive evidence — the largest tobacco/alcohol GWAS (Liu
2019) and the largest prior COPD GWAS (Sakornsakolpat 2019) — were both flagged CLAIM_MISMATCH
because neither actually supports a hit at this locus. That is a real pattern: the two most
authoritative "gold-standard" GWAS in the smoking and COPD domains, respectively, do not
corroborate this locus. Combined with the absence of independent spirometry support, this is
meaningfully more concerning than a simple "gap," even though it stops short of an affirmative
refutation.

**Verdict: WEAKENED.** The inference oversells the case in one specific way: the write-up
presents TNRC6A's airway multiciliogenesis biology (Liu 2023) as the anchor for a pulmonary
mechanism while treating the absence of spirometry/COPD-GWAS replication and the ambiguous
smoking-mediation status as "gaps" rather than as substantive countervailing evidence. The
clean gene-prioritization (no LD ambiguity) and the genuine lung-relevant mechanism are real
strengths, but the locus's status as an outright pleiotropic hub (educational attainment,
adiposity, lipids, renal function) alongside non-replication in the two most relevant prior
large-scale GWAS (smoking and COPD) means the case is more ambiguous than a straightforward
"strongest-domain: structural lung biology" framing implies. I would not go as far as
CONTESTED, because I have no specific paper naming a different causal gene at this locus or
explicitly refuting TNRC6A — that search remains undone (budget exhausted) rather than
negative.

### GENE: SLC5A11 and GENE: ARHGAP17

No credible-set overlap, no prioritization support in any method, and no lung/COPD literature
identified for either. I have no independent search evidence to add or contest here
(zero-search-pass gene, as disclosed above). Their inclusion as non-candidates for regional
completeness is reasonable and I see no basis to elevate either over TNRC6A. **HOLDS** (i.e.,
the researcher's decision to exclude them holds) — but flagged as a zero-independent-search
gene per the disclosure above, so this is a weak "holds," not a verified one.

### Net comparison to researcher's overall assessment

The researcher's own write-up is careful and already surfaces most of the individual caveats
(missing spirometry hit, paralog confound, unresolved smoking-locus overlap, L2G circularity
risk). Where I differ is in aggregation: taken together, (1) no independent
spirometry/COPD-GWAS replication, (2) unresolved-but-plausible smoking-behavior confounding
reinforced by the educational-attainment pleiotropy, and (3) a rejected-citation pattern hitting
exactly the two most relevant "gold standard" papers, add up to a **more ambiguous case than
"TNRC6A, strongest domain = structural lung biology" suggests** on its own. This is not a case
for demotion or CONTESTED, but synthesis-lead should not treat the ciliogenesis mechanism as
free-standing confirmation of a direct pulmonary pathway without weighing the non-replication
and pleiotropy evidence explicitly, and should flag the never-completed variant-level
(rs8049667/rs6497759/rs3803716) and locus-replication searches as outstanding work rather than
settled negatives.
