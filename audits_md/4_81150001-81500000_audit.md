## Red-team-skeptic critique (verbatim)

**Disclosed search-coverage limitation:** the skeptic's WebSearch budget was exhausted
(200/200) before any query returned results; it performed no independent literature search
of its own for this locus and based its critique entirely on the pasted researcher
findings and verified citations. This locus therefore has zero independent
contradiction-search coverage from the review stage. If search budget becomes available
in a later pass, this locus should be reprioritized for a fresh check.

### Locus-level framing issue
The lead variant rs6831114 has PIP=0.186 in a 48-variant, all-intergenic/MODIFIER credible
set with no missense/splice variant anywhere, and the GWAS Catalog lookup for the lead
variant itself returned 404 (uncorroborated). A PIP of 0.186 is low in absolute terms — the
credible set is diffuse and even the fine-mapping's best guess carries substantial
uncertainty about which variant, let alone which gene, is causal. Any gene-level story
built on top of this fine-mapping is inherently a low-confidence exercise regardless of how
compelling the downstream biology narrative sounds. The three internal prioritization
methods disagree (FLAMES->PRKG2, PoPS->CFAP299, MAGMA/ABC->NA), which is itself evidence of
a genuinely ambiguous locus rather than convergent support for one gene.

### GENE: PRKG2 — WEAKENED
The two "lung-relevant" citations are real but narrower than the researcher's framing
suggests:
- PMID 35313961 (AT2 organoid Prkg2-KO): baseline alveolar epithelial developmental/
  regenerative biology in a constitutive KO with no cigarette-smoke, elastase, or other
  emphysema/COPD-relevant injury model tested. The leap to "therefore lung-relevant to
  COPD" is not supported by the paper itself.
- PMID 19359370 (ENaC/fluid clearance): airway epithelial ion-transport physiology relevant
  to pulmonary edema/fluid clearance, not to airflow obstruction, emphysema, or chronic
  inflammation — the pathophysiologic domain of COPD. Being stretched from "PKG-II acts in
  an airway-derived cell line" to implicit "lung disease relevance" without a mechanistic
  bridge to obstruction or inflammation.
- The bulk of PRKG2's functional literature (acromesomelic dysplasia, CFTR-mediated
  secretion in jejunum/colon/pancreas, HTLV-1 neuroinflammation co-expression module) is
  either explicitly non-pulmonary or in unrelated disease contexts — appropriately labeled
  as such by the researcher.
- Zero PubMed hits for PRKG2 x COPD, PRKG2 x FEV1/spirometry, PRKG2 x smoking traits — a
  meaningful absence given the searches were broad.
- The credible-set PRKG2 variant itself (rs3114135, intron_variant) has PIP=0.0014 —
  negligible. FLAMES flagging PRKG2 as "nearest gene" is a positional default, not evidence
  the causal signal acts through PRKG2 specifically.

Verdict: WEAKENED. The organ biology is real and directionally plausible but is generic
organ physiology, not COPD-specific or even airflow-obstruction-adjacent evidence, and it
is being asked to carry the weight of an entire gene nomination in the total absence of any
genetic (GWAS/coloc/MR/TWAS) or spirometric evidence.

On the immune-eQTL question: the researcher's framing as an "unexplained gap" is correct
and appropriately cautious. These are QTL-only credible sets with explicitly no
disease-GWAS colocalization per Open Targets; broad eQTL detection across many immune cell
subsets is a common feature of many loci with strong local regulatory variants and is not
on its own informative about disease-relevant tissue of action — if anything, the
conspicuous absence of a lung-tissue eQTL despite 41 total QTL credible sets is a mild
negative signal for a lung-autonomous mechanism. This should remain an open question, not
evidence.

### GENE: RASGEF1B — WEAKENED (borderline not-a-peer-candidate)
No internal prioritization method (nearest-gene/FLAMES, MAGMA, PoPS, ABC) flagged
RASGEF1B. It was added purely because it physically overlaps the far end of a 350kb-wide
locus window — a boundary-overlap gene in a wide, low-resolution window, with no specific
credible-set variant tying to it at all.

The TLR3/TLR4/NF-kB/macrophage RasGEF1b literature is genuinely reproducible and
mechanistically detailed, but it is a generic innate-immune-activation pathway
(chemotaxis, IL-6, CXCL1, ICAM-1) demonstrated in general macrophage/LPS/TLR contexts,
COVID-19 PBMCs, and one goat bronchial-epithelial-infection model — none in COPD, none in
human lung tissue under smoke exposure, and no GWAS/coloc/MR tie to COPD, spirometry, or
smoking whatsoever. A generic macrophage TLR-signaling gene is a plausible biological
category fit under the immune-complexity prior, but plausibility of category is not the
same as locus-specific evidence.

Verdict: WEAKENED, bordering on should-not-be-treated-as-a-candidate-at-all. The case for
RASGEF1B rests entirely on (1) weak positional overlap that no statistical
fine-mapping/colocalization method endorses, and (2) a real but disease-nonspecific
mechanism. Including RASGEF1B alongside PRKG2 and CFAP299 as a peer "candidate gene" risks
implying a level of statistical support it does not have.

### GENE: C4orf22 / CFAP299 — CONTESTED
Park et al. 2014 (PMID 24739539) is negative evidence, not supporting evidence: a
functional follow-up at an unrelated blood-pressure GWAS locus (4q21, lead SNP rs16998073)
containing a gene cluster (ANTXR2/PRDM8/FGF5/C4orf22). The authors tested candidates and
concluded ANTXR2 was causal for that signal, explicitly ruling out Fgf5 and, by
construction of their conclusion, not supporting C4orf22 either. This should be read as:
when this gene has previously come up as a candidate in a GWAS gene cluster, it was
evaluated and not selected as causal. This is a genuine data point against treating C4orf22
as a likely causal gene by default whenever it appears near a GWAS signal.

The second citation (PMID 30820741) undermines the intuitive "CFAP-family -> cilia/
PCD-like mucociliary dysfunction -> COPD" hypothesis that the gene name invites: the actual
functional data show a testis-specific germ-cell apoptosis/cell-cycle role via
bcl-2/caspase signaling, not an axonemal/ciliary-motility phenotype. No PCD/situs/airway-
cilia phenotype has been reported for this gene despite its family name.

Combined with zero lung or immune-cell eQTLs in Open Targets (testis/brain/fibroblast
only) and zero GWAS/coloc/MR evidence beyond the PoPS score itself, CFAP299's case is thin,
and the one piece of "prior candidate gene" history at a different locus is actively
unfavorable.

Verdict: CONTESTED. Not because a paper argues "CFAP299 is not the COPD gene at
4:81.15-81.5Mb" specifically (no such paper exists), but because (a) the gene has a
documented history of being considered and rejected as a causal candidate in a GWAS
gene-cluster context at a different locus, and (b) the one plausible lung-relevant
mechanistic hypothesis suggested by its gene family name is directly contradicted by the
only specific functional data available. This should be flagged as evidence undermining
rather than supporting the PoPS prioritization, not merely "no supporting literature
found."

PRKG2 by virtue of being the nearest-gene/FLAMES pick.
