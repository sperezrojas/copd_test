# Audit trail — Locus 15:89,300,001-89,500,000 (GRCh38)

This appendix records excluded citations and contested/weakened gene verdicts, with the
red-team-skeptic's reasoning preserved verbatim, for full traceability. It is kept separate
from `evidence_tables/15_89300001-89500000.md` so that evidence tables can be shared without
also sharing this audit trail.

## Excluded citations

No citation returned by the researcher was marked RETRACTED, NOT_FOUND, or CLAIM_MISMATCH by
citation-verifier. All 9 citations produced by the researcher for this locus were VERIFIED at
the level of existence, non-retraction, and accurate claim correspondence. There is therefore
no "hard exclusion" appendix of failed citations for this locus.

One citation carries an important **scope caveat** rather than a failure, and is recorded
here for transparency even though it remains in the evidence table:

- **Sakornsakolpat P, Prokopenko D, Lamontagne M, et al. Nature Genetics, 2019.**
  PMID 30804561 / DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0342-2. VERIFIED as an existing paper accurately
  reporting 82 COPD GWAS loci (UK Biobank + ICGC). However, citation-verifier could NOT
  confirm from the abstract/available text whether this specific 15q26.1 (~89.3-89.5 Mb)
  locus is among the 82 reported loci — this would require access to supplementary tables
  not available to the verifier. This citation should be used in synthesis only as general
  background on the existence of this major prior GWAS, not as evidence that this specific
  locus was independently replicated by it. The researcher itself flagged this as
  unconfirmed rather than asserting either way.

## Contested / weakened gene verdicts (red-team-skeptic reasoning, verbatim)

### ACAN — CONTESTED

"This is the most serious problem in the whole set. ACAN's gene body (88.80-88.88 Mb) lies
430-500 kb outside the stated locus window (89.30-89.50 Mb) — it is not physically in this
LD block by any conventional definition. Open Targets shows zero `gwas_credible_sets`/L2G
evidence for ACAN-COPD (only weak literature-mining scores of 0.02-0.14), which directly
contradicts the MAGMA/PoPS prioritization for this locus. This is not merely 'weak
supporting evidence' — it is an independent gene-prioritization pipeline (Open Targets'
L2G, purpose-built for exactly this causal-gene-assignment task) actively failing to
reproduce the MAGMA/PoPS signal. Combined with zero PubMed literature connecting ACAN to
lung/airway biology (multiple targeted negative searches) and a biology mismatch
(cartilage/skeletal dysplasia vs. lung obstruction), this cannot be waved through as merely
'weak.' The physical-distance anomaly itself suggests either (a) a locus-definition/window
artifact in the input pipeline (ACAN may be reflecting a separate, adjacent independent
association signal that got merged into this window's MAGMA gene-set test), or (b) a
long-range regulatory hypothesis (enhancer for ACAN sitting in this window) that has no
chromatin/Hi-C/eQTL evidence offered here.

CONTESTED — Open Targets' L2G zero-score for COPD is credible, independently-sourced
contradicting evidence against the MAGMA/PoPS-driven ACAN prioritization (this Open Targets
record was provided as database context, not literature, so it doesn't need
citation-verifier treatment, but should be flagged to synthesis-lead as the basis for
downgrading ACAN sharply — likely below POLG despite the flashier prioritization scores).
Recommend synthesis-lead explicitly interrogate why PoPS/MAGMA assigned ACAN to this window
given the physical distance, rather than accepting the prioritization at face value."

### POLG — WEAKENED

"The 'nearest gene' label is doing more work than the data supports: all three credible-set
variants sit in an intergenic gap ~65 kb+ from POLG's coding sequence, and the lead
variant's annotation (splice_region/non-coding-transcript-exon) is most consistent with
hitting an unannotated lncRNA, not POLG itself. No COPD/TWAS/colocalization/spirometry
literature was found for POLG at all, and its dominant monogenic phenotype (Alpers, MNGIE,
PEO — Hayhurst 2018) is neuro-hepatic, not pulmonary — biologically discordant with a
lung-obstruction mechanism. The researcher correctly bounded the Li 2025 MR finding to IPF
and did not extend it to COPD — this is the right call and should not be reinterpreted by
synthesis-lead as supportive of COPD causality; IPF is a restrictive fibrotic disease with
a distinct genetic architecture (e.g., MUC5B, TERT) from obstructive COPD, and 'Grade 1 MR
for IPF' is not transferable evidence. The Open Targets COPD score (0.406) is itself
derived from `gwas_credible_sets` evidence — i.e., it likely just reflects this same
locus's credible set being nearest to POLG by genomic proximity, not an independent line of
evidence, so it shouldn't be read as corroboration. The Loughran 2020 POLGARF finding adds
a genuine complication: the POLG transcript locus encodes two distinct proteins via an
overlapping ORF, so even if a regulatory variant here affects 'POLG' expression, the
functionally relevant hit could be POLGARF (nucleolar/secreted, uncharacterized in lung)
rather than the mitochondrial polymerase. Researcher's own 'weak/uncertain'
characterization is accurate and not overclaimed.

WEAKENED — the case rests on physical proximity alone; the credible set doesn't touch
POLG's coding sequence, there is zero direct pulmonary genetic evidence, and the one
lung-disease MR hit is for a different disease that should not be conflated with COPD."

### FANCI — HOLDS (recorded for completeness, not contested)

"Researcher-added, not flagged by any prioritization method. The one supporting citation
(Jin 2023) uses FANCI purely as a downstream DNA-damage-response marker in a PM2.5 mouse
model — not a causal test, and correctly characterized as such by the researcher ('NOT
tested as a causal driver'). Core biology (Fanconi anemia, bone-marrow failure/cancer
predisposition) is a genome-instability syndrome unrelated to airway obstruction. No
overclaiming detected — the researcher's 'weak' label matches the thin evidence exactly.
HOLDS as an honestly-labeled weak candidate; no basis to either strengthen or contest it
further, but also no reason to elevate it above POLG or RHCG."

### RHCG — HOLDS as an accurately-labeled data gap (recorded for completeness)

"Physically the only protein-coding gene fully inside the stated window, and by raw
distance the credible-set variants are roughly as close to RHCG as to POLG's coding
sequence — this is a legitimate positional argument the researcher raised appropriately (as
a caveat, not an assertion). However, RHCG's proposed airway-epithelial ammonia-transporter
role was explicitly flagged by the researcher as background knowledge, not a located
citation — a real evidence gap, honestly disclosed rather than papered over with an uncited
claim. Given zero literature either way, this cannot be marked CONTESTED or fully supported
on genetic grounds — the researcher's 'weak/insufficient literature, plausible positional
candidate' framing does not overreach.

HOLDS as an accurately-labeled data gap, but flag for synthesis-lead: this gene's
lung-epithelial biology claim needs a dedicated literature check (PubMed/Google Scholar for
'RHCG lung epithelium,' 'RHCG bronchial,' 'Rhcg knockout airway') before the locus writeup
either credits or dismisses it — this was not adequately searched (zero hits across
'multiple queries' per researcher, but the specific literature likely exists in renal/dermal
physiology journals that may need broader terms)." The red-team-skeptic's own independent
search budget was also exhausted before this could be checked — this remains an open
evidence gap for any future re-review of this locus.

### POLGARF — HOLDS (correctly unassessable, recorded for completeness)

"Only the discovery paper exists; no disease association of any kind. Researcher's
'insufficient evidence to assess' is accurate and appropriately non-committal. HOLDS —
correctly labeled as unassessable, not weakened by overclaim."

## Process notes / evidence gaps disclosed by the pipeline agents

- The researcher's own WebSearch budget was exhausted before it could complete a dedicated
  GWAS-catalog/PhenoScanner-style check for smoking-behavior GWAS overlap at this locus. It
  confirmed only that this locus (15q26.1, ~89.3-89.5 Mb) is genomically distinct from the
  CHRNA5/CHRNA3/CHRNB4 cluster (15q25.1, ~78.85-78.95 Mb), ~10 Mb away, but could not rule
  out an independent smoking-behavior signal at this exact locus.
- The red-team-skeptic's own WebSearch budget was exhausted before it could run any of its
  five planned independent contradiction-search queries (Sakornsakolpat locus list, RHCG
  airway biology, ACAN-COPD, FANCI-lung disease, smoking-GWAS overlap). Its critique is
  therefore based entirely on re-analysis of the researcher's reported findings and the
  Open Targets structured data, not new literature. This is disclosed here rather than
  silently treated as a completed independent check.
- No candidate gene at this locus has any FEV1/FEV1-FVC spirometric GWAS evidence reported
  by either the researcher or Open Targets — a notable absence under this pipeline's prior
  that spirometric concordance strengthens prioritization.
- FANCI, RHCG, and POLGARF were added by the researcher as physically-overlapping
  protein-coding genes not flagged by any internal prioritization method (FLAMES/MAGMA/
  PoPS/ABC); they were consequently not queried against Open Targets in the orchestrator's
  step-1 structured lookup (which only queried POLG and ACAN, per the locus input file's
  listed candidates). This is a coverage gap in the Open Targets summary, disclosed above
  and in the main evidence table, rather than silently omitted.
