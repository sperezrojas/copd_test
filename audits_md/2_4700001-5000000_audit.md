# Audit appendix — locus 2:4,700,001-5,000,000 (GRCh38)

This appendix lists all excluded citations (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH) and all
genes/claims the red-team-skeptic flagged, with reasoning preserved verbatim from the
checkpoint files. Companion evidence table: `evidence_tables/2_4700001-5000000.md`.

## Excluded citations

1. **Grimm et al. 2019** ("SOX transcription factors in cancer" — SOX family review), PMID
   30914279, DOI 10.1016/j.semcancer.2019.03.004.
   **Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH.**
   Claim as originally presented by researcher: "Notes SOX11 is highly expressed in
   small-cell lung cancer (SCLC), alongside SOX2/SOX4."
   Citation-verifier's reasoning (verbatim): "Abstract does not specifically state that
   SOX11 is highly expressed in SCLC. Abstract mentions SOX11 as favourable in 'other
   cancer types' and reviews lung tumours generally, but does not explicitly connect SOX11
   expression to SCLC."
   Disposition: excluded from the evidence table. Note that the same underlying factual
   claim (SOX11 highly expressed in SCLC) IS independently supported by a separate,
   VERIFIED citation (Castillo et al. 2011, PMID 22084397), which remains in the evidence
   table — so the claim is not left wholly unsupported, only this specific citation is
   excluded.

No citations for this locus received a RETRACTED or NOT_FOUND verdict. 12 of 13 citations
produced by the researcher were VERIFIED; only this one showed CLAIM_MISMATCH.

## Genes/claims flagged by red-team-skeptic

**Disclosure carried over from the critique itself:** the red-team-skeptic's web-search
budget was exhausted before it could run independent contradiction-searches (its queries
for "rs7585899 chronotype GWAS," "SOX11 COPD lung function GWAS," and "2p25.3 COPD GWAS
SOX11 LINC01249" all failed to return results). Its critique below is based on re-reading
and re-analysis of the researcher's already-verified record, not an independent literature
search. This limitation should be weighed when reading the verdicts below — none of the
three candidate genes at this locus received an independent contradiction-search pass.

### SOX11 — WEAKENED (not CONTESTED, but flagged as at risk of over-weighting)

Verbatim reasoning: "SOX11 is ~855kb from the lead variant, outside the defined locus
window, and every direct statgen search (COPD, rs7585899, 2p25.3, spirometry) returned zero
hits... The developmental biology is real but is being asked to do more work than it can
support if read uncritically. Sock et al. (PMID 15254231) is a constitutive knockout with
perinatal lethality and multi-organ malformation... this is embryonic organogenesis, and
pleiotropic embryonic organogenesis at that, not a lung-selective phenotype. Using it as
COPD-relevant evidence requires an unsupported leap from 'Sox11 is required for lung
development in utero' to 'SOX11 variants modulate adult airway obstruction risk decades
later,' with no dose-response, no adult conditional-knockout data, and no eQTL/expression
data in adult lung tissue presented anywhere in this record... The VILI paper (Fang et al.,
PMID 30879763) is an acute ventilator-injury model, mechanistically and temporally distant
from a chronic, largely smoking/inflammation-driven adult disease... The immune evidence is
honestly characterized as weak/tangential by the researcher's own conclusion, and I agree:
SOX11 in B-cell lymphoma TME (Balsas 2021) and SCLC (Castillo 2011, and Grimm 2019 which the
citation-verifier already flagged as CLAIM_MISMATCH) is a cell-intrinsic oncogenic
transcriptional program in a completely different cell lineage... and disease context...
There is no neutrophil, macrophage, CD8+/Th1/Th17 airway-inflammation literature on SOX11 at
all... I'd flag that even presenting SOX11 as 'the' candidate gene for this locus... risks
distance/LD bias — SOX11 is being discussed at length not because fine-mapping/coloc data
implicate it, but because it is the nearest thing in Open Targets with a name and a PubMed
record. That imbalance (most literature = most attention) should be explicit to
synthesis-lead as a bias in what got written up, not evidence of biological relevance."

Verdict as stated: **WEAKENED** — "evidence is accurately characterized by the researcher,
but its very existence (relative to LOC105373397 and LINC01249 having ~0 literature)
creates a 'streetlight effect' risk: SOX11 could be over-weighted downstream simply because
it has papers, when distance (outside window, ~855kb) and total absence of direct genetic
evidence argue it should be weighted comparably low to the other two candidates."

### LOC105373397 — HOLDS (null finding, correctly excluded)

Verbatim reasoning: "The researcher's own locus-resolution work is the strongest part of
this record: it shows the pipeline's nearest-gene call is a computationally-predicted,
functionally uncharacterized transcript whose true coordinates (chr2:4,096,388-4,114,720)
sit ~604kb outside the defined 4.7-5.0Mb window and ~723-741kb from the lead variant and
top-PIP credible-set variants. This is not 'nearest gene near the credible set' — it's an
artifact of gene-desert nearest-gene logic reaching outside the window entirely. Zero
PubMed hits, no ortholog, no curated function. There is no case here to weaken; there was
never a case."

### LINC01249 — HOLDS (null finding, honestly reported as off-topic)

Verbatim reasoning: "Single PubMed hit (Huang et al. 2021, PMID 34533693) is a small,
likely underpowered (124 discovery + 313 replication) Chinese Han GWAS of thyroid hormone
levels (T3/T4) — not COPD, not lung function, not even the same LOC identifier
(LOC105373394 vs. this locus's LOC105373397... 'same gene-poor neighborhood,'... but
explicitly not the same gene). This is appropriately caveated as unrelated and should not
be read as supporting evidence for anything COPD-relevant. There is no structural or immune
literature at all for LINC01249."

### Chronotype competing hypothesis — flagged as adequately caveated in sourcing but at risk of overstatement downstream

Verbatim reasoning: "This is the most important structural issue in the whole record. The
Open Targets database context... places the lead variant rs7585899 and four other top-PIP
credible-set variants as fine-mapped hits for 'chronotype measurement'... with zero L2G
gene prediction. That is a striking, on-point structural finding: it directly addresses the
actual credible-set variants at this actual locus, unlike the SOX11/LINC01249 literature,
which addresses genes near but outside the window. Yet the researcher is explicit and
correctly cautious that no literature was found naming this exact locus/rs7585899/SOX11/
LINC01249 in a chronotype context — the chronotype link rests purely on the structured
database... there is a real risk that a downstream synthesis step reads 'GWAS on this exact
SNP found chronotype in UK Biobank' plus 'smoking initiation/heaviness causally decreases
odds of being a morning person (Gibson et al., MR)' and concludes 'this locus operates via
nicotine/circadian pathways' — which is a coherent narrative but is stitched together from
(a) an unverified database record for the specific SNP and (b) verified literature that is
about chronotype-smoking correlation in general, not about this SNP or gene. The individual
pieces are honestly sourced, but the composite causal chain (this locus → chronotype →
smoking behavior → COPD) has not actually been demonstrated by any single verified source.
This should be flagged to synthesis-lead as a hypothesis, clearly labeled
speculative/composite, not as literature-supported mediation."

Also flagged: "Given the strength of prior #1 (smoking-mediation should be actively weighed
as an alternative to direct pulmonary biology, especially absent FEV1/FEV1-FVC support) and
the total absence of any spirometric or COPD-direct evidence for SOX11/LINC01249/
LOC105373397, the chronotype/smoking-behavior hypothesis is at least as plausible as —
arguably more plausible than — the SOX11 developmental-biology narrative, precisely because
it's anchored to the actual lead variant rather than to a gene 855kb away."

### Explicitly NOT marked CONTESTED

Verbatim reasoning: "Strictly, 'contested' implies a literature disagreement — a paper
actively arguing against a gene or implicating a different gene at the same locus in a way
that conflicts with a claim being made. I did not find (and could not independently search
for, given budget exhaustion) such a paper. Nothing here rises to CONTESTED in that narrow
sense: SOX11 evidence isn't being outright disputed by a rival paper, it's just weak,
indirect, and possibly mismatched in life-stage/tissue/disease context — that's WEAKENED,
not CONTESTED. If anything is 'contested' it's the whole locus's candidate-gene assignment
— Open Targets' own L2G pipeline computed nothing for the chronotype credible set, and the
internal FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS/ABC pipeline computed nothing for COPD either. That is two
independent computational pipelines agreeing there is no confident gene assignment here —
which is itself a meaningful negative signal, not silence."

### Alternative explanation: statistical noise / winner's-curse (raised by skeptic, not previously emphasized by researcher)

Verbatim reasoning: "A third, simpler possibility: this could be a genuine COPD-GWAS hit
that is either (a) a false positive/winner's-curse artifact in a gene desert region with no
coding variants, no eQTL/coloc signal reported, and no computational prioritization tool
converging on any gene, or (b) a real but currently unresolvable regulatory signal (e.g.,
acting on a distant gene via long-range chromatin interaction, plausible in gene deserts)
that current tools simply cannot resolve at this locus — not necessarily chronotype-
mediated at all. Given zero FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS/ABC signal and zero L2G signal even for the
(arguably better-powered, larger n) chronotype GWAS, I'd weight 'unresolved regulatory
signal or noise' and 'chronotype/smoking-behavior mediation' as roughly comparably
plausible, rather than treating chronotype as the default leading alternative to SOX11
developmental biology, as the researcher's closing sentence does."
