## Excluded citations (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH)

### CLAIM_MISMATCH

**PMID 40423607 — Gao S et al. Heart Rhythm. 2025;DOI 10.1016/j.hrthm.2025.04.016**

Researcher's claim: presented as a "companion mechanistic paper" to PMID 42639838 (Gao S et
al., Europace 2026), asserted to describe the same DENND3 p.R534S cardiac dyadic
microdomain / Rab11b-BIN1 mechanism.

Citation-verifier verdict: **CLAIM_MISMATCH**. Verbatim reasoning from citation-verifier:
"Paper exists (PMID 40423607, DOI 10.1016/j.hrthm.2025.04.016), published May 27, 2025. Not
retracted. However, the abstract does NOT describe the same mechanism as #3. Instead of
emphasizing the BIN1/dyadic microdomain/Rab11b pathway, the abstract states: 'Rab5 showed
ectopic distribution... while other Rab proteins did not display such changes. The GTPase
activity of Rab5 increased, while Rab12 activity decreased...' and focuses on
'Rab-mediated trafficking pathways critical for ion channel distribution' — notably absent
are mentions of BIN1, dyadic microdomains, and Rab11b. The mechanism described is distinct
from #3's dyadic/BIN1 axis."

Red-team-skeptic's independent comment on this same item: "the researcher's characterization
of the Gao et al. Heart Rhythm 2025 paper (PMID 40423607) as a 'companion mechanistic paper'
describing 'the same BIN1/Rab11b/dyadic mechanism' as the Europace 2026 paper is not
supported by the verifier's read... This is a clean example of the claim being
strengthened/homogenized in translation beyond what the cited abstract supports."

Disposition: excluded from the main evidence table entirely. The related, correctly
characterized citation (PMID 42639838) is retained in the main table on its own merits.

## Genes marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic (full reasoning, verbatim)

### TRAPPC9 — CONTESTED

Verbatim from red-team-skeptic:

> This is the gene I'd push back on hardest. It does not physically reside in the locus
> (ends ~590kb upstream), has no credible-set variant annotated to it, and never tops L2G
> at any of the ~129 regional credible sets for any trait in Open Targets — its only
> genetic-association support for COPD-relevant biology is the Boueiz 2017
> emphysema-distribution GWAS, and even there, **both the researcher and the verifier
> explicitly could not confirm whether that TRAPPC9-region signal is the same LD block as
> this locus's lead variant rs4961255, or a distinct, non-overlapping association**
> elsewhere near TRAPPC9. This is not a minor caveat — it's the load-bearing uncertainty
> for the entire TRAPPC9 candidacy at this specific locus. If it's a distinct signal (which
> the physical 590kb gap and TRAPPC9's absence from every other regional credible set makes
> plausible), then TRAPPC9's presence here is purely a FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS statistical
> artifact — these gene-based tests can draw on genome-wide expression/functional priors
> that pull in a well-annotated neurodevelopmental/NF-kB gene independent of this locus's
> actual causal signal, exactly as the researcher itself cautioned. Open Targets' COPD
> association score for TRAPPC9 (0.00394) being driven *only* by literature/text-mining and
> having zero genetic-association datatype support is consistent with this being a
> text-mining/functional-annotation artifact rather than a genotype-phenotype link. The
> rest of the TRAPPC9 literature (NIBP syndrome, NF-kB scaffold, CpG-BMI methylation) is
> non-lung, non-COPD, and doesn't rescue the case.
>
> **Verdict: CONTESTED.** The physical non-overlap, the total absence of L2G support
> anywhere in the region, and — most importantly — the unresolved ambiguity over whether
> the one COPD-subphenotype signal invoked (Boueiz 2017) is even the same association as
> this locus, together constitute credible reason to doubt that TRAPPC9 is implicated by
> this locus at all, as opposed to being implicated by a separate, physically distinct
> signal near TRAPPC9's own gene body. I'd flag this to the synthesis-lead as needing
> full-text confirmation of the Boueiz 2017 lead SNP position before TRAPPC9 is treated as
> more than a genome-wide-statistical-method artifact for this specific locus (this is a
> request for someone with remaining budget to pull full text/supplementary tables — not a
> new citation I'm asserting, so nothing here needs UNVERIFIED-marking by the
> citation-verifier).

## Genes marked WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (not CONTESTED, but flagged — included for
completeness beyond the pipeline's minimum requirement)

### DENND3 — WEAKENED

Verbatim from red-team-skeptic:

> The statistical case (nearest gene, lead PIP 0.501, hosts most of the credible-set
> variants by count if not by weight) is real but stands largely alone — no independent
> COPD/lung-function GWAS, TWAS, MR, or colocalization study names DENND3, and this was
> checked directly against the two major COPD papers in the field. The structural
> literature (autophagy in HEK293 cells, hereditary hemochromatosis in liver, cardiac dyad
> arrhythmia) is mechanistically interesting but has **no lung tissue, no lung phenotype,
> and no COPD relevance** in any of it — three different organ systems, none of them lung.
> The immune literature is explicitly characterized by the researcher itself as "diffuse,
> nonspecific, cross-disease bioinformatic co-occurrence" (Alzheimer's perivascular
> macrophages, LUAD cancer stemness, gastric cancer, moyamoya, PSC) — this is exactly the
> "hub gene across unrelated diseases" pattern the priors say to treat skeptically, and the
> researcher appropriately hedged rather than overclaimed here.
>
> [Overclaiming instance re: PMID 40423607 — see CLAIM_MISMATCH section above.]
>
> **Alternative explanation / LD concern:** the Open Targets background context is a
> serious complication for the "DENND3 is the locus's gene by proximity/PIP" inference.
> Across ~129 other-trait credible sets in this region, L2G favors **SLC45A4 over DENND3 at
> nearly all of them** (0.45–0.75 vs 0.07–0.4), including at the smoking-initiation
> credible set (SLC45A4 0.58 vs DENND3 0.18) — the one exception being credible sets keyed
> to the lead variant itself for AST. That means DENND3's primacy is largely an artifact of
> it being nearest gene to the single lead variant with the highest PIP, not a broader
> pattern of regulatory/functional linkage across the locus. Combined with (i) zero COPD or
> FEV1/FEV1-FVC credible sets anywhere in Open Targets for this region, and (ii) the
> locus's apparent co-localization with a smoking-initiation signal in near-perfect LD with
> rs4961255, the smoking-behavior-mediated explanation is at least as well supported as any
> lung-structural mechanism for DENND3 — and there is no spirometric concordance evidence
> to counterbalance it (prior #2's warning sign applies directly).
>
> **Verdict: WEAKENED.** The statistical prioritization is real, but the inference that
> DENND3 acts through pulmonary/airway-immune biology overreaches what the cited literature
> shows (zero lung-disease-specific mechanism, one clear instance of two-paper conflation
> flagged above) and is further undercut by L2G data pointing away from DENND3 across the
> region and by an unresolved, plausible smoking-mediation alternative.

### SLC45A4 — WEAKENED

Verbatim from red-team-skeptic:

> This gene has essentially no functional or disease literature at all — the researcher
> found zero PubMed results for "SLC45A4 COPD," no characterization of the transporter's
> substrate or tissue expression, and the only non-locus hit is a bovine dairy GWAS paper
> listing it among ~20 candidate genes (correctly flagged as uninformative). To the
> researcher's credit, no speculative content was manufactured to fill this gap — this is
> the opposite of overclaiming, if anything an appropriately conservative null report.
>
> The interesting wrinkle is that SLC45A4 physically hosts the sole missense (rs3739238)
> and sole splice-region (rs3739235) credible-set variants, and Open Targets L2G
> structurally favors it over DENND3 at most regional credible sets — yet Open Targets' own
> COPD association score for SLC45A4 is exactly zero. That's a real tension: the strongest
> coding/splice-relevant variant annotation and the L2G-preferred gene identity in this LD
> block have *no* accompanying COPD or lung phenotype anywhere, genetic or literature. This
> is not evidence against SLC45A4 so much as evidence that nobody has looked — a gap, not a
> contradiction.
>
> **Verdict: WEAKENED.** Not because any specific claim overreaches (there's barely a claim
> to overreach — the researcher was appropriately restrained), but because "candidate gene"
> status here rests on missense/splice positional plausibility with zero corroborating
> functional or disease literature; the case is more a statistical placeholder than an
> evidenced biological mechanism.

## Red-team-skeptic's disclosed search-budget limitation (transparency note)

The red-team-skeptic subagent disclosed, before delivering its critique, that its web-search
budget was already exhausted (200/200) before any of its own independent contradiction
searches could execute for this locus. Its critique for all three genes is therefore based
entirely on critical re-analysis of the researcher's findings, the citation-verifier's
verdicts, and the Open Targets context already assembled — not on new literature the
skeptic found itself. The skeptic explicitly flagged two items that would benefit from a
follow-up search pass with remaining budget: (1) full-text confirmation of the Boueiz et
al. 2017 (PMID 27669027) lead-variant position relative to rs4961255, and (2) independent
corroboration of the Open Targets smoking-initiation co-localization claim (rs4961255 is
not indexed in PubMed). Neither follow-up was performed in this pipeline run; both are
noted here as open items for any subsequent review of this locus.
