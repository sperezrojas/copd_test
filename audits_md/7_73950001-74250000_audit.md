# Audit / excluded-evidence appendix — locus 7:73,950,001-74,250,000 (nearest gene ELN)

This file documents every citation excluded from the main evidence table
(`evidence_tables/7_73950001-74250000.md`) because citation-verifier returned
RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH, plus every candidate gene the red-team-skeptic
marked CONTESTED, with reasoning preserved verbatim from the pipeline's checkpoint
files. A reviewer can use this file to see exactly what was checked and rejected, and
why, without it being mixed into the clean evidence table.

**Update (2026-09-11): targeted re-opening.** A genome-build error in the internal
MAGMA/PoPS gene-location merge file was found and fixed, surfacing a new candidate gene,
RFC2, never evaluated in the original review. The RFC2 evaluation (search, citation
verification, and critique) is appended at the end of this file. It does not change the
original ELN/LIMK1/GTF2I/GTF2IRD1 findings below, which are preserved unmodified.

## Excluded citations (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH)

### PMID 40155373 — CLAIM_MISMATCH
You D, Wu Y, Lu M, et al. "A genome-wide cross-trait analysis characterizes the shared
genetic architecture between lung and gastrointestinal diseases." Nature Communications,
2025. DOI: 10.1038/s41467-025-58248-w.
Cited as: Open Targets' curated literature source for both ELN's and LIMK1's
gwas_credible_sets L2G-style evidence at this locus.
Why excluded: Verifier's read of the abstract confirms "66 candidate pleiotropic genes"
enriched in immune/inflammatory pathways in a lung-GI comorbidity analysis, but the
abstract does NOT specifically name ELN, LIMK1, or this chr7:74.0-74.1 Mb region. The
claim that this paper substantiates ELN/LIMK1's involvement at this locus is therefore
unconfirmed from the source itself.

### PMID 30875648 — CLAIM_MISMATCH

"Association between polygenic risk for tobacco or alcohol consumption and liability to

## Genes marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic

### GTF2I — CONTESTED
Red-team-skeptic's reasoning (verbatim): "This is the gene where the build-mismatch
issue is most consequential. GTF2I's GRCh38 coordinates are ~400-500kb outside the
stated locus window, and the only reason it's a candidate at all is that MAGMA (a
coordinate-dependent gene-scoring method) evidently used GRCh37 gene models against a
GRCh38-defined credible set. That is a plausible, and frankly fairly damning,
methodological artifact explanation that the researcher flagged appropriately — I'd
elevate it from 'caveat' to 'likely disqualifying' language in the write-up... On top of
that: the Open Targets L2G curation source for GTF2I (30940143) is a *different* paper
from the one supporting ELN/LIMK1, itself doesn't confirm GTF2I by name in its abstract,
zero PubMed COPD/lung-function-specific literature exists, and the one immunology
citation offered (thymoma/myasthenia gravis, 32700803) is CLAIM_MISMATCH on the specific
detail claimed and in any case describes a somatic cancer-driver mutation context,
entirely unrelated to germline COPD risk or airway immunology. There is no cell-type-
specific or lung-immune literature for GTF2I at all. **CONTESTED** — not on the basis of
a paper directly refuting GTF2I's role in COPD, but on the basis of a structural/
methodological problem (likely GRCh37/38 build mismatch placing the gene ~400-500kb
outside the actual credible-set window) that undermines the only reason GTF2I is a
candidate at all, combined with zero confirmed COPD/lung literature. I'd flag this to
synthesis-lead as: verify whether the internal MAGMA pipeline used GRCh38 SNP positions
against a GRCh37 gene model file — if confirmed, GTF2I should likely be dropped from
this locus entirely rather than carried forward as a weak candidate."

### GTF2IRD1 — CONTESTED
Red-team-skeptic's reasoning (verbatim): "Same build-mismatch problem as GTF2I
(~400-600kb outside window, GRCh37 partial overlap only at the 3' end — even weaker
positional case than GTF2I). Open Targets shows *complete absence* of COPD association
evidence (not just low — absent), and the researcher found zero PubMed COPD/lung/airway
literature. Of the ten general GTF2IRD1 papers searched, several were CLAIM_MISMATCH
(cancer hub-gene papers mischaracterized as developmental biology), and every VERIFIED
paper concerns Williams-Beuren neurodevelopmental/craniofacial/auditory/behavioral
biology — none touch lung, airway, or inflammatory biology in any way. There is no
COPD-relevant claim being made here that survives scrutiny at all; this gene appears to
have been carried forward purely by a PoPS score that, given the build-mismatch issue,
may not even correspond to genes correctly localized to this window. **CONTESTED** — for
the same structural/build-mismatch reason as GTF2I, compounded by a complete absence of
any COPD/lung association evidence in Open Targets and zero relevant literature. This is
the weakest of the four candidates and I'd recommend synthesis-lead treat it as very
likely a pipeline artifact rather than a genuine candidate pending confirmation of the
build-mismatch hypothesis."

### ELN — WEAKENED (not CONTESTED, but flagged for overclaiming risk)
Red-team-skeptic's reasoning (verbatim, condensed from full critique in
`evidence_tables/_status/7_73950001-74250000/critique.md`): "The positional case for ELN
is the strongest of the four genes at this locus, and it is plausible on prior
biological grounds... But the literature record as actually verified does not establish
COPD-relevant biology for this specific common variant — it establishes rare-variant/
haploinsufficiency biology in different tissues (artery) and different diseases

confirmed. **WEAKENED** — real positional/structural plausibility, but the literature
invoked to support a COPD-specific common-variant mechanism is either CLAIM_MISMATCH

Mendelian/haploinsufficiency, vascular). No confirmed FEV1/FEV1-FVC concordance, no

### LIMK1 — HOLDS (weak bystander candidate, not contested because no strong claim was made)

## Methodological caveats carried forward from researcher + skeptic

- **GRCh37/GRCh38 build mismatch (structural, not literature):** GTF2I and GTF2IRD1's
  GRCh38 gene coordinates fall several hundred kb outside this locus window, while their
  GRCh37 coordinates fall inside it. This strongly suggests the internal pipeline's
  MAGMA/PoPS gene-to-locus assignment used mismatched genome builds. **Update
  2026-09-11: confirmed** — the study team found and fixed this bug in the MAGMA/PoPS
  gene-location merge file, and the corrected re-merge surfaced RFC2 as a new candidate
  at this locus (see below). This retroactively validates the original review's
  hypothesis about GTF2I/GTF2IRD1's likely erroneous inclusion.
- **Red-team-skeptic search-budget note:** the skeptic's WebSearch calls returned
  "budget exhausted" errors and it was unable to perform any independent
  contradiction-search of its own for any of the four genes; its critique is based
  entirely on scrutinizing the researcher's findings and the citation-verifier's
  verdicts, not on independent literature discovery. This is disclosed here rather than
  silently treating the critique as if an independent search had been performed. (The
  same search-budget limitation recurred in the RFC2 critique pass below.)
- **Smoking-mediation prior:** no evidence (positive or negative) was found connecting
  this locus to smoking-initiation/cessation/heaviness GWAS signals; this should be
  described as untested, not as ruling out a smoking-mediated mechanism.
- **Spirometric-concordance prior:** the two candidate sources that could have
  established FEV1/FEV1-FVC concordance for ELN (PMID 30804561, PMID 30804560) were both
  CLAIM_MISMATCH/unconfirmed from abstracts alone; full-text locus-table verification
  (checking whether this chr7:74.0-74.1 Mb region appears in either paper's reported loci
  tables) was not performed in this pass and is recommended as the highest-value follow-up
  to strengthen or weaken the ELN case.

## TARGETED ADDITION (2026-09-11): RFC2 evaluation

**Background:** the genome-build bug hypothesized above (GRCh37 gene models used against
a GRCh38-defined locus/credible-set window) was confirmed and fixed by the study team in
the MAGMA/PoPS gene-location merge file. The corrected re-merge surfaced RFC2 as a
candidate gene at this locus that was never evaluated in the original review. Unlike
GTF2I/GTF2IRD1, RFC2's candidacy is NOT itself a build-mismatch artifact — orchestration
independently confirmed via Ensembl REST that RFC2 (chr7:74,223,231-74,254,458, GRCh38)
genuinely overlaps the locus window (73,950,001-74,250,000) in correctly matched GRCh38
coordinates (~26.8 kb of the ~31.2 kb gene body falls inside the window). RFC2 was
therefore researched in full rather than ruled out on procedural grounds alone.

### RFC2 citations — all VERIFIED as real papers, but none support any COPD/lung/immune claim

- **PMID 29247873** — VERIFIED as existing (Aghdam et al., "The Ability of Different
  Imputation Methods to Preserve the Significant Genes and Pathways in Cancer," Genomics
  Proteomics Bioinformatics 2017), but confirmed to be a false-positive hit: this is a
  statistical-imputation-methods paper for cancer microarray data that surfaced in search
  only via the generic MeSH term "Replication Protein C," and makes no substantive claim
  about RFC2's biology or lung relevance. Excluded from the evidence table as
  non-informative (not a CLAIM_MISMATCH in the technical sense — the researcher never
  claimed it supported a COPD link — but excluded for the same reason as
  verified-but-irrelevant citations elsewhere in this file).
- **PMID 42244520, 38669181, 34751190, 16980295** — all VERIFIED as real, accurately
  described papers on RFC2's biochemical role in the RFC/CTF18-RFC clamp-loader complex
  (PCNA loading during DNA replication and repair). None of the four have any lung,
  airway, immune, or COPD relevance whatsoever — they are foundational/general molecular
  biology and cancer-therapeutics-mechanism papers only. Excluded from the evidence table
  for the same reason as GTF2IRD1's verified-but-irrelevant general-biology papers above:
  real and accurate, but not evidence for or against this locus's COPD association.

### RFC2 — WEAKENED, bordering on non-candidate (red-team-skeptic verdict)
Red-team-skeptic's reasoning (verbatim, full critique in
`evidence_tables/_status/7_73950001-74250000/critique.md`): "Orchestration confirmed
RFC2's physical overlap with the window is real (not a build-mismatch artifact), so it
cannot be dismissed on the same procedural grounds as GTF2I/GTF2IRD1. But the substantive
case for RFC2 as a candidate gene is essentially empty, on different grounds: Zero
aggregated COPD association score across all Open Targets datasources (stronger absence
than a merely low score). No overlap with the fine-mapped credible-set variant cluster —
the ~26.8kb of gene body inside the window is at the far telomeric edge, not near any
high-PIP variant. gnomAD constraint (pLoF oe 0.82, missense oe 0.95) shows RFC2 is
dosage-tolerant, which actively undercuts any plausible haploinsufficiency/dosage
mechanism — this is a substantive argument against candidacy, not just an absence of
evidence. No LD data (r² between rs34208922 and any RFC2-region variant) was presented
anywhere in the dossier... this gap should be flagged to synthesis-lead/orchestration as
unaddressed — it's the one piece of evidence that could actually elevate or fully exclude
RFC2, and it's missing... **Verdict on RFC2: WEAKENED, bordering on non-candidate** — The
one piece of evidence that is real — genuine GRCh38 physical overlap with the outer edge
of the window — cannot support the inference that RFC2 is a viable candidate gene at this
locus. There is no statistical-genetic, spirometric, immune, or mechanistic case, and the
dosage-tolerance data actively argue against a haploinsufficiency mechanism. I stop short
of CONTESTED because I have no new contradicting citation of my own to offer (my searches
were blocked by budget exhaustion, not because I found and am withholding a rival paper) —
CONTESTED should be reserved for cases with an actual competing citation in hand.
**Effect on the locus-level prioritized-gene call: No change.** ELN remains the
prioritized gene at MODERATE confidence... This is not a tie — the evidentiary gap
between ELN (COPD score 0.29, lead variant in gene body, weakened-but-real
disease-biology literature) and RFC2 (COPD score absent entirely, no overlap with the
credible-set cluster, no literature of any relevance) is large and one-directional."

Red-team-skeptic also confirmed the classical "elastin causes emphysema" mechanistic
prior was correctly kept out of the RFC2 assessment in both directions: "Nothing in the
RFC2 evidence table invokes elastic-fiber or structural-protein biology; the researcher
evaluated RFC2 purely on its own DNA-replication/clamp-loader biology and explicitly
declined to force a mechanistic narrative it doesn't have."

### Open gap flagged for future follow-up
Red-team-skeptic identified that no LD computation (r² between rs34208922 and any
variant in the RFC2 gene region) was performed in this pass. Physical window-overlap is
not equivalent to LD with the credible-set signal, and this pipeline did not compute it.
This gap does not change the current conclusion (the evidentiary case against RFC2 stands
on Open Targets absence + zero literature + dosage-tolerance data alone), but closing it
would make RFC2's exclusion airtight rather than merely well-supported, and is
recommended as a follow-up if the study team wants full closure on this locus.
