# Audit appendix — locus 16:57,900,001-58,150,000 (nearest gene MMP15)

This file records everything excluded from the evidence table (`evidence_tables/16_57900001-58150000.md`), plus every CONTESTED/WEAKENED verdict and its full reasoning, preserved verbatim from citation-verifier and red-team-skeptic where possible, for audit purposes.

## Excluded citations (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH)

**USB1 — poikiloderma with neutropenia / lung-bronchiectasis complications claim**
- Verdict: NOT_FOUND
- Reason: The researcher's write-up stated that USB1 biallelic loss-of-function causes poikiloderma with neutropenia, and that "pulmonary fibrosis/bronchiectasis has been described in some case reports of this syndrome in the broader literature," but provided no PMID/DOI for this specific claim. Citation-verifier could not verify a claim with no citation attached and marked it NOT_FOUND. Excluded from the evidence table per pipeline rule that only VERIFIED citations are used.
- Red-team-skeptic follow-up: independently confirmed via search that the underlying biology is real and citable — candidate sources found were PMID 29072891 (GeneReviews entry on poikiloderma with neutropenia), PMID 27247962 (Suter et al. 2016), and PMID 39607724 (Tekcan et al. 2024) — none of which were ever submitted by the researcher or run through citation-verification, so none carry a VERIFIED verdict and none appear in the evidence table. The skeptic's assessment: this looks like a citation-sourcing gap by the researcher rather than a fabricated claim, but even if properly cited, this monogenic biallelic-LOF disease phenotype would only support a distinct, rare recessive-disease mechanism — not evidence that the common noncoding variants annotated to USB1 in this locus's credible set (all PIP < 0.0015) drive the population-level COPD GWAS signal. Recommendation for any future re-run of this locus: have the researcher redo this specific search and properly cite it with an explicit LOF-vs-common-variant generalizability caveat, or drop USB1 as a candidate on statistical grounds (PIP < 0.0015) regardless of the citation gap.

No citations were marked RETRACTED or CLAIM_MISMATCH for this locus. All 20 originally-submitted numbered citations plus the one citation added during red-team critique (PMID 36574455) were independently VERIFIED — see `evidence_tables/_status/16_57900001-58150000/verified_citations.md` for full per-citation justification.

## Genes/claims marked CONTESTED or WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning preserved)

### MMP15 — WEAKENED (overall "strongest candidate" framing)

Verbatim skeptic reasoning:

> Pulled the actual abstracts:
> - **PMID 21946350** (2011, Repapi/Hancock lung-function GWAS, n=48,201+46,411): abstract text is "new regions showing association... in or near MFAP2, TGFB2,... MMP15, CFDP1..." — it names **no SNP**, and gives no LD/positional confirmation that whatever hit "near MMP15" in 2011 is the same signal/LD block as this project's rs11076213 (3'UTR, PIP=0.53). This is a textbook case of the "same gene name, unconfirmed same-locus" triangulation error flagged in the task brief. It should be reported as "an independent GWAS 15 years ago also named MMP15 among 16 lung-function loci — LD/positional overlap with the current credible set is not established from this citation alone," not as confirmatory triangulation.
> - **PMID 41253406** (2025, GRADS cohort, n=39 PI*MZ + 27 PI*ZZ): confirmed small/exploratory (n=39). Importantly, the abstract states "alveolar MMP3 and MMP15 were uniquely associated with worse lung function... among PI*MZ individuals" — MMP15 is not singled out; it's one of two MMPs (MMP3 is an unrelated gene on chr11) in a small protein-biomarker study in a rare monogenic AAT-deficiency subgroup. This is protein-level correlational evidence in a genetically distinct population, not genetic evidence tying this GWAS locus to lung mechanism in the general COPD population this meta-analysis represents.
> - **PMID 38660492** (2024 MR/coloc): confirmed MMP15 evidence used **whole-blood eQTL (eQTLGen)**, not lung tissue, against spirometry-defined COPD. A blood eQTL is a materially weaker tissue-context match for a "lung protease" mechanistic story than a lung eQTL would be.
>
> On the Open Targets L2G contradiction: the researcher's line that this "does not appear to undermine the case for MMP15" is not adequately justified as written... That's a real, not fully resolved, tension, and the researcher's dismissal reads as motivated reasoning toward the biologically satisfying protease-antiprotease narrative (exactly the bias flagged in the task brief) rather than an argued rebuttal.
>
> Smoking: the conclusion "no smoking-behavior mediation evidence found" is under-hedged given only PubMed/bioRxiv were searched.

New supporting citation surfaced by the skeptic and independently VERIFIED post-critique: PMID 36574455 (Chun et al. 2022, PLoS Genetics) — a genuinely lung-tissue MMP15 eQTL, "suggestive" pleiotropy with OSA/FEV1-FVC, added to the evidence table.

### ZNF319 — WEAKENED (dismissal as "weak" judged under-argued)

Verbatim skeptic reasoning:

> Pulled independent literature: ZNF319 is associated with a pathogenic missense variant causing autosomal-recessive leukodystrophy... and with a de novo variant in non-syndromic cleft lip/palate. No lung or immune phenotype turned up in this pass. This is somewhat more informative than "absence of a lung-specific PubMed hit" — it's evidence that ZNF319's only two described human disease phenotypes point to unrelated organ systems, which is a real (if not conclusive) argument for deprioritizing it. That said, the researcher should not simply wave away the fact that ZNF319 has the **highest independent L2G (0.518)** and is the **most LoF-constrained gene at the locus (oe=0.391)** — constraint and L2G are statistical/genomic signals independent of whether a PubMed search surfaces a matching phenotype, and understudied zinc-finger TFs routinely lack disease literature simply because no one has looked in the relevant tissue... the current framing risks under-weighting real statistical signal in favor of the better-fitting MMP15 story, which is exactly the pattern-matching bias the task brief warns about.

### SPMIP8 — CONTESTED

Verbatim skeptic reasoning:

> Confirmed via independent search: PMID 41550493 (2025, mouse knockout study) shows Spmip8's only established molecular role is flagellar/testicular... there is no described immune function anywhere in the literature for this gene. The breadth of eQTL activity across Treg/Th1/Th2/Th17/monocyte/NK cell types is real per the researcher's report, but eQTLs are ubiquitous and low-specificity; citing "broad immune eQTL" as evidence of an "immune candidate" for a gene whose only known biology is spermatid flagella, with a missense credible-set variant at PIP=0.0012 (essentially fine-mapped out), risks the same "good story" bias flagged for MMP15 — except here it's fitting the immune-complexity prior rather than the protease prior. This should be flagged as CONTESTED, with the "second-highest L2G" fact weighed against the essentially-zero fine-mapping PIP for the SPMIP8 missense variant rather than treated as comparably strong to the eQTL breadth.

(Orchestrator note: PMID 41550493 was already present in the researcher's original citation list as citation 13 and independently VERIFIED — no new citation-verification action was needed for this specific PMID.)

### CNGB1 — WEAKENED

Verbatim skeptic reasoning:

> No credible-set variant is annotated to CNGB1 at all — it is a pure PoPS (polygenic functional-similarity) call. Independent search confirms CNGB1's canonical and essentially only described function is the beta subunit of the retinal photoreceptor cyclic-nucleotide-gated channel, causative for autosomal-recessive retinitis pigmentosa; no lung, airway, or smoking-behavior literature surfaced. PoPS is known to occasionally prioritize genes on the basis of gene-set/pathway similarity to known trait genes without any direct variant support, and a retina-specific ion channel with zero fine-mapped variants at a COPD locus is a plausible false positive of that mechanism.

### CFAP20 — WEAKENED

Verbatim skeptic reasoning:

> Found zero literature connecting CFAP20 to lung, COPD, or immune biology in this pass (query returned no results). It has no annotated credible-set variant at all — it is a purely positional candidate discovered by an Ensembl region query, with no statistical fine-mapping or MAGMA/PoPS/L2G support. Absent a specific sourced mechanism from the researcher, this candidate should be carried in the evidence table as speculative/exploratory only, clearly distinguished from the credible-set-supported genes.

### TIPINP2 — HOLDS (excluded as non-plausible candidate, no critique needed)

The researcher independently confirmed via Ensembl REST query that TIPINP2 (ENSG00000261151) is annotated as a **processed pseudogene**, not a protein-coding gene, despite being named via one very-low-PIP (0.0014) credible-set variant. No literature exists for it, consistent with pseudogene status. Red-team-skeptic reviewed this and found the researcher's diligence here sound; no further action needed. TIPINP2 is not carried forward in the evidence table or prioritized-gene synthesis as a plausible causal candidate.

## Methodological caveat carried into this audit

The red-team-skeptic subagent reported it could not directly open the local researcher.md / verified_citations.md files in its working environment (WebFetch does not support file:// paths, and its WebSearch budget was reportedly exhausted before its first call), and substituted direct Europe PMC REST API lookups by PMID/keyword instead. This let it independently pull real abstract text for the MMP15, ZNF319, CNGB1, SPMIP8, and USB1 citations (as reflected above), but it explicitly flagged that it got **no independent literature pass at all on CFAP20** beyond one negative query, and could not directly quote-check the researcher's exact wording for caveats (e.g., whether the LD-uncertainty issue for PMID 21946350 was already noted in researcher.md). The orchestrator has cross-checked the skeptic's specific claims against the researcher's actual checkpoint file (`evidence_tables/_status/16_57900001-58150000/researcher.md`) where possible and incorporated them into the evidence table and prioritized-gene synthesis above; this caveat is preserved here for transparency about the critique stage's own limitations.
