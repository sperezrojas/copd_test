## Genes flagged WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (neither gene was marked CONTESTED)

Per pipeline instructions, this appendix must preserve verbatim any CONTESTED gene reasoning.
Red-team-skeptic did not mark either candidate gene CONTESTED at this locus — both were marked
**WEAKENED**. That reasoning is preserved verbatim below for full traceability, since it
materially shaped the confidence level in the Prioritized Gene section of the evidence table.

### LILRB2 — WEAKENED (verbatim from red-team-skeptic)

> Real statistical/variant-level case within the LILR cluster, but COPD-specific mechanistic
> evidence is absent, spirometry concordance is explicitly negative in the largest available
> study, the L2G signal is multi-trait pleiotropic rather than COPD-specific, paralogs are not
> cleanly excluded, and the missense-variant literature contains corrected/reversed/unconfirmed
> claims that should be downweighted or excluded per the list above.

Supporting detail (verbatim): "The strongest gene-specific (not just locus-specific) evidence is
that two credible-set variants are missense changes within the LILRB2 coding sequence (rs383369
R20H, PIP=0.378; rs386056, PIP=0.047) and one is an intronic LILRB2 variant carrying the top PIP
(0.433). That's better than pure "nearest gene" bias — it's variant-level gene assignment, not
proximity inference. However, the Open Targets record itself undercuts confidence: LILRA1 and
LILRB5 show molecular-QTL colocalization at the same variants in monocyte/macrophage panels but
simply lack a GWAS study to generate an L2G score — that's an artifact of statistical
power/study availability, not evidence those paralogs are uninvolved. In a family of
near-identical duplicated genes with shared regulatory architecture, this is a real, unresolved
ambiguity, and the case rests substantially on the L2G algorithm's output plus generic
LILR-family immunobiology (inhibitory receptor on myeloid cells) that mechanistically could
apply about equally to LILRB2, LILRB3, or LILRA5."

On pleiotropy (verbatim): "A variant with equally high L2G to LILRB2 for hematocrit and HDL-C,
in addition to COPD, is more consistent with LILRB2 sitting at a generic myeloid/blood-cell
regulatory hub than with COPD-specific pulmonary biology. This should temper — not eliminate —
confidence in a COPD-specific mechanism."

On spirometry discordance (verbatim): "Per this pipeline's own prior #2, spirometry concordance
is supposed to strengthen prioritization, and its absence 'warrants scrutiny.' Here we have an
actual negative: the largest multi-ancestry lung-function GWAS (~400K, 279 signals) found
nothing at 19q13.42/LILRB2. That is a direct strike against a baseline-airflow-limitation
mechanism."

Thoroughness flag (verbatim): "The researcher's own stated gap (no COPD sputum/BAL/CD8+
T-cell/macrophage-polarization literature for LILRB2) should be taken seriously. LILRB2 has
multiple aliases (ILT4, CD85d, LIR-2, MIR-10); I can't confirm from the record whether
alias-based searching was exhaustive. Recommend synthesis-lead flag this for a possible
supplementary search pass before finalizing, rather than treating current absence as definitive
absence."

### NLRP12 — WEAKENED (verbatim from red-team-skeptic)

> The PoPS signal is a genuinely independent line of evidence (function-similarity-based, not
> positional), and dismissing it outright would be overreach given prior #3's instruction to
> consider heterogeneous inflammatory programs rather than defaulting to the positionally-obvious
> gene. But it currently has zero statistical fine-mapping or colocalization support at this
> locus, sits physically outside the credible-set window, and its only human-disease phenotype
> is mechanistically distant. This is WEAKENED — a plausible, mechanistically interesting
> alternative that should remain flagged in the synthesis as low-confidence rather than being
> either promoted to a real competing candidate or fully dropped. It should not be called
> CONTESTED, because nothing in the record actively argues against an NLRP12 role in COPD
> specifically — the evidence is simply absent/indirect, not contradictory.

On the negative allergic-airway mouse study (verbatim): "correctly, the researcher/framing notes
this tested a Th2/OVA/HDM asthma-type model specifically and found no phenotype; it does not
test a neutrophilic, cigarette-smoke, or innate-immune/inflammasome-driven model, which is the
more COPD-relevant inflammatory program per this pipeline's prior #3. NLRP12 is a documented
negative regulator of NF-κB/inflammasome signaling in myeloid cells (Allen 2012, Lukens 2015),
so a hypothesis where NLRP12 loss-of-function or reduced expression permits excess NF-κB-driven
neutrophilic/innate inflammation in the lung is mechanistically coherent and untested directly,
not refuted."

## Full red-team-skeptic critique (verbatim)

### Preliminary disclosure — search budget exhausted before independent checks could run

I attempted four targeted contradiction/verification searches (paralog-specificity in COPD
fine-mapping, smoking/nicotine-GWAS lookup for rs383925/rs383369, NLRP12-lung-function check,
LILRB2 lung eQTL check) and all four failed — the session's web search budget was already
exhausted (200/200 used) before my queries ran. I was not able to complete even a shallow
independent pass on either gene. This critique is therefore based entirely on internal analysis
of the researcher/verifier record provided, not on any new literature I retrieved.
Synthesis-lead should treat both LILRB2 and NLRP12 as having received zero independent
contradiction-search coverage from me, and should weight that gap accordingly (e.g., a
follow-up pass on LILR-family GWAS/smoking-GWAS lookups is genuinely still needed, not just a
formality).

### Smoking-mediation

The diesel-exhaust MR finding (Li A 2022, PMID 35602164) is worth flagging explicitly as a
distinct confound axis from the pipeline's core smoking-behavior concern (prior #1). Prior #1 is
about behavioral smoking genetics (initiation/dependence/heaviness) confounding unadjusted COPD
case-control GWAS. The diesel-exhaust MR result is about a toxicant/particulate-exposure-response
mechanism — plausibly relevant to cigarette smoke as an inhaled particulate too, but that would
actually be a genuine lung-relevant inflammatory pathway (response to inhaled particulates), not
a behavioral confound. These should not be conflated: this citation raises an
"inhalation-response biology" hypothesis, not evidence that the locus is really about smoking
initiation/nicotine dependence rather than lung pathology. Given searches for direct
smoking-initiation/nicotine-dependence GWAS associations at rs383925/rs383369 failed to run
(budget exhaustion), this specific prior-#1 check is unresolved and should be flagged as an open
item for a follow-up GWAS-catalog lookup, not assumed either way.

No new citations were surfaced by this critique (independent search budget was exhausted before
any query executed), so no additional items were sent back to citation-verifier.

## Additional context notes (not citations, not evidence — locus/gene-mapping caveats from the researcher)

- NLRP12 (the PoPS-prioritized gene) is **not physically within** the 19:54,200,001-54,350,000
  credible-set window: its gene body is at chr19:53,792,139-53,824,403 (GRCh38), ~375-560 kb
  centromeric of the window. PoPS is a similarity-based method not restricted by physical
  distance, so this does not itself invalidate the PoPS score, but it does weaken the positional
  plausibility of a direct regulatory relationship between the credible-set variants and NLRP12.
- LILRA1 (ENSG00000104974) is annotated as an unprocessed pseudogene in the current Ensembl
  GRCh38 gene set for this region — it shows molecular-QTL colocalization at the same
  credible-set variants (per Open Targets) but was not pursued as an independent protein-coding
  candidate given this pseudogene annotation.
- RPS9 (a housekeeping ribosomal protein gene) also physically spans much of this window but has
  no documented COPD/immune/structural lung biology and was not pursued as a candidate.
- Background-only oncology literature on LILRB2/ILT4 (7 PMIDs: 38705566, 37622462, 33537094,
  38313233, 37444609, 32549766, 38450191) was spot-checked for existence only (all VERIFIED as
  real, non-retracted publications) but deliberately not itemized as individual COPD-supporting
  evidence rows in the main table, per both the researcher's and red-team-skeptic's judgment that
  these are background biological-plausibility context (LILRB2 is genuinely expressed/functional
  in lung tissue myeloid/tumor microenvironment) rather than COPD-specific findings.
