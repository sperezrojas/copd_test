# Audit trail — Locus 2:144,500,001-145,700,000 (nearest gene ZEB2)

This file records everything excluded from the main evidence table
(`evidence_tables/2_144500001-145700000.md`) and why, plus the red-team-skeptic's full
gene-level contestation reasoning, preserved verbatim where required. Intended for internal
review only — do not distribute alongside the evidence table without also sharing this file
if traceability of exclusions matters.

## Excluded citations (citation-verifier verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH)

All six of the following were returned by the researcher but downgraded by the
citation-verifier and therefore do NOT appear in the main evidence table. None were
RETRACTED or NOT_FOUND — no citation received either of those verdicts at this locus.

1. **PMID 36477530** — Saunders GRB et al., "Genetic diversity fuels gene discovery for
   tobacco and alcohol use," Nature, 2022. Claimed: rs7421123 associated with "smoking
   initiation" p=1×10⁻¹³⁷ and rs2138448 with p=3×10⁻¹⁵, both in this study.
   **Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH.** The paper is real and on-topic (3.4M-person tobacco/alcohol
   GWAS, 2,143 loci), but the specific SNP/p-value claims could not be confirmed from the
   abstract — likely reported only in supplementary tables, which the verifier did not have
   access to. Not a contradiction of the claim, but not confirmable either.

2. **PMID 34855049** — Pasman JA et al., "Genetic Risk for Smoking: Disentangling Interplay
   Between Genes and Socioeconomic Status," Behavior Genetics, 2021. Claimed: rs1516141
   associated with "smoking status measurement" p=8×10⁻²⁵.
   **Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH.** Real paper, but it is a GWAS-by-subtraction study of smoking
   liability independent of educational attainment, not a primary discovery GWAS; the
   specific SNP/p-value is not stated in the abstract.

3. **PMID 30643258** — Karlsson Linnér R et al., "Genome-wide association analyses of risk
   tolerance and risky behaviors...," Nature Genetics, 2019. Claimed: rs2890772 associated
   with "risk-taking behaviour" p=4×10⁻⁹.
   **Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH.** Real, on-topic paper (99 loci for risk tolerance/risky
   behaviors), but SNP/p-value not confirmable from abstract.

4. **PMID 31689377** — Wootton RE et al., "Evidence for causal effects of lifetime smoking
   on risk for depression and schizophrenia: a Mendelian randomisation study," Psychological
   Medicine, 2019. Claimed: rs2890772 associated with "smoking behavior trait" p=2×10⁻²².
   **Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH — genuine misattribution, the strongest exclusion at this
   locus.** This paper is explicitly a Mendelian-randomization causal-inference study that
   USES external smoking-initiation GWASs (GSCAN, or the authors' own lifetime-smoking GWAS)
   as instrumental variables to test smoking's causal effect on depression/schizophrenia. It
   is not itself a smoking-behavior discovery GWAS, so a SNP-trait association cannot
   correctly be attributed to this paper — the true source is very likely the GSCAN/Liu et
   al. instrument GWAS it references, not this MR study.

5. **PMID 37069358** — Cosentino J et al., "Inference of chronic obstructive pulmonary
   disease with deep learning on raw spirograms identifies new genetic loci and improves
   risk models," Nature Genetics, 2023. Claimed: rs13428413 (intronic to TEX41, within this
   locus's credible set) associated with "Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease" p=2×10⁻⁸.
   **Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH.** This is the single most consequential exclusion at this
   locus: it was the ONE directly-COPD-attributed variant found anywhere in this credible
   set outside of ZEB2's locus-level naming in Sakornsakolpat 2019. The paper is real (a
   deep-learning spirogram GWAS identifying 67 new COPD loci), but the specific SNP/p-value
   could not be confirmed from the abstract — it may be reported in the paper's supplementary
   locus list, which neither the researcher nor red-team-skeptic could access with the tools
   available. This directly undermines the "TEX41 carries an independent, non-smoking-
   mediated COPD signal" hypothesis floated by the researcher — see CONTESTED reasoning
   below.

6. **PMID 27903959** — Lv H et al., "Genome-wide haplotype association study identify the
   FGFR2 gene as a risk gene for acute myeloid leukemia," Oncotarget, 2017. Claimed:
   rs17409500 (within this locus's credible set, intronic to TEX41) associated with acute
   myeloid leukemia p=8×10⁻⁶ and p=2×10⁻⁷.
   **Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH — genuine misattribution.** The verifier confirmed the actual
   abstract names entirely different SNPs (rs7090018, rs2912759), not rs17409500. Likely a
   GWAS-Catalog attribution error. The verifier additionally noted Oncotarget's own history
   of journal-quality/indexing concerns as further reason for caution, independent of the
   SNP mismatch itself.

## CONTESTED gene: TEX41 (red-team-skeptic verdict, reasoning preserved verbatim)

> I'm marking this CONTESTED, but on a different basis than "found a competing paper" (my
> searches failed) — on the basis of the *pattern of citation failures already in the
> record*, which the task instructions explicitly say is itself informative.
>
> - The **only** direct COPD attribution for a TEX41-intronic variant (rs13428413,
>   Cosentino 2023) is CLAIM_MISMATCH — unconfirmable from the abstract.
> - A second credible-set-variant attribution (rs17409500 → AML, Lv et al. 2017) is a
>   **genuine misattribution**: the verifier confirmed the source abstract names entirely
>   different SNPs (rs7090018, rs2912759). This is not "unconfirmable," it's actively wrong,
>   on top of the underlying journal (Oncotarget) carrying its own quality concerns.
> - The one VERIFIED eQTL evidence for TEX41 (Galatà 2021, mastocytosis GWAS) uses a
>   variant (rs4662380) that is **not itself in this locus's credible set**, and the
>   phenotype (mastocytosis) has no connection to COPD.
> - The rest of TEX41's literature is exclusively oncology across ~7 disparate non-pulmonary
>   (and one pulmonary-but-oncologic, i.e., lung adenocarcinoma bone metastasis biology, not
>   airway/COPD biology) cancer contexts — no lung-development, airway-remodeling, or
>   COPD-mechanistic study exists for TEX41 at all.
>
> Two out of three attempts to give TEX41 a disease-specific credible-set-variant claim
> failed verification, one via outright misattribution. Combined with zero COPD-relevant
> mechanistic literature and an eQTL result anchored outside the credible set, this reads as
> a lncRNA nominated purely because credible-set variants happen to sit in its introns
> (classic LD/positional bystander), with citation support that collapsed on verification
> rather than held up. This should be reported to synthesis-lead explicitly as
> CONTESTED-by-citation-pattern rather than CONTESTED-by-competing-paper, so it isn't
> misread as "a paper argues TEX41 is wrong" — no such paper was found (or searched for,
> given my budget exhaustion).

## Genes assessed as weak but NOT contested (no affirmative contradicting evidence found — distinct status from CONTESTED)

- **GTDC1 / QTMAN**: "No affirmative contradicting evidence exists (nor could I search for
  any), so this doesn't meet the bar for CONTESTED. But the MAGMA nomination itself is weak
  on its face: the gene sits 31-390 kb *outside* the locus boundary, is not overlapped by
  any credible-set variant, is LoF-tolerant (oe~1.0, opposite of ZEB2's constraint profile),
  carries a weak Open Targets COPD score (0.021, no genetic_association contribution), and
  its only literature (Pichler 2025) is pure background biochemistry (tRNA mannosyltransferase
  function) with zero disease framing. The inference 'MAGMA flagged it, therefore viable
  candidate' is weakened to the point of non-credibility given the total absence of
  corroborating genetic or mechanistic evidence and its physical exclusion from the credible
  set — this looks like a MAGMA gene-set/window artifact rather than a genuine candidate."
  (red-team-skeptic, verbatim)
- **LINC01412**: "No dedicated literature exists under this gene name, and the one
  credible-set variant sitting in its introns (rs10496964, the epilepsy SNP) is explicitly
  attributed by its source paper to neighboring ZEB2, not to LINC01412. There is essentially
  no independent claim being made about this gene to contest — it functions only as a
  positional label for a cluster of credible-set variants." (red-team-skeptic, verbatim)
- **LINC01966**: "Zero PubMed literature, zero Open Targets disease associations, no
  internal prioritization flag, fully nested within a TEX41 intron. This is the
  weakest-supported entry in the set and was never seriously advanced as a candidate by the
  researcher. Correctly weak, not contested (no evidence exists on either side)."
  (red-team-skeptic, verbatim)

## Process caveats disclosed by the red-team-skeptic (preserved for transparency)

> My WebSearch budget was already exhausted before I could run any searches for this locus
> (all four queries — ZEB2+COPD, rs13428413, TEX41+lung function, GTDC1/QTMAN+COPD —
> returned "budget exhausted, 200/200 used"), and the two WebFetch attempts against PubMed
> abstract pages returned only cookie-consent boilerplate, not article content. I was unable
> to perform any independent contradiction-search for any of the five candidate genes at
> this locus. This critique is therefore based entirely on re-analysis of the researcher's
> findings and the citation-verifier's verdicts as supplied, plus domain reasoning — it does
> not add a single new verified or unverified citation.

The red-team-skeptic separately flagged a cross-cutting process observation: "the repeated
pattern at this locus of CLAIM_MISMATCH verdicts specifically clustering on SNP/p-value-level
claims (as opposed to gene-general claims) suggests the researcher agent may be
systematically over-specifying claims beyond what abstracts support when a real, on-topic
paper is available — worth flagging as a process observation across genes, not just this
locus." This is passed along verbatim for whoever runs subsequent loci to watch for the same
pattern.
