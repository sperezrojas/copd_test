## Genes marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic

### NKX2-2 — CONTESTED

Full reasoning (verbatim from critique, see `evidence_tables/_status/20_21050001-21650000/critique.md`):

"The case against NKX2-2 as the causal COPD gene rests on genuinely strong non-lung
colocalization (brain DTI/refractive-error, L2G ~0.75) plus zero direct COPD genetic
association — this part is solid. But the supporting cancer-marker citations (Wang,
Yang) are being used past their evidentiary scope (diagnostic IHC in a specific neoplastic
context, not COPD-relevant airway/PNEC mechanism), and they sit in unreconciled tension
with the researcher's own Lawson citation showing NKX2-2 is expressed and functionally
relevant in a different lung NE tumor class (SCLC). Net: the 'weak case' conclusion is
probably right, but arrived at partly via the wrong evidence, and the internal
contradiction in the verified record should be flagged to synthesis-lead rather than
smoothed over as merely 'inconsistent.'"

Specific unreconciled contradiction: Lawson et al. 2011 (PMID 21642373, VERIFIED) shows
NKX2.2 overexpression increases SCLC chemoresistance and its absence predicts better
chemo outcomes — i.e. NKX2-2 is expressed/functional in SCLC — while Wang et al. 2010
(PMID 20599218, VERIFIED) and Yang et al. 2018 (PMID 29713473, VERIFIED) show NKX2-2 is
absent in bronchopulmonary typical carcinoids and lung well-differentiated NETs. Skeptic's
reconciliation: "SCLC and typical bronchopulmonary carcinoid are biologically distinct
neuroendocrine lung tumor classes (SCLC: high-grade, typically TP53/RB1-null, arising via
dedifferentiation/reprogramming; carcinoid: low/intermediate-grade, distinct
differentiation program) — so 'NKX2-2 present/functional in SCLC' and 'absent in
carcinoid' are not strictly contradictory findings about the same cell-of-origin state,
but the researcher's write-up presents them as an unresolved tension without noting this
distinction... Either way, neither finding speaks directly to non-neoplastic airway/COPD
biology, which is the more fundamental limitation."

Also flagged: the Ewing sarcoma pleiotropy argument (Machiela et al. 2018, PMID 30093639)
is "appropriately used as a caveat in substance, but the phrasing overreaches slightly...
'unrelated to airway biology' claims more than the data support — a locus having one
well-documented non-pulmonary association does not logically exclude an independent lung
mechanism; it only means the best-characterized mechanism at this locus is non-pulmonary."

### XRN2 — WEAKENED (not fully CONTESTED, but downgraded from the researcher's framing)

"Statistical prioritization (FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS convergence, some Open Targets
genetic-association signal) is the most credible plank at this locus and should be
reported as such. But the mechanistic citations (NSCLC EMT/miR-10a, PARP1 synthetic
lethality, gut XRN2-HIV) are general cell-biology/oncology papers with no COPD, no
airway-immune, and in one case no lung specificity at all; calling this 'structural/
immune' domain evidence for COPD overstates relevance even though the researcher
appropriately hedges with 'speculative as applied to COPD.'... the Shrine/Sakornsakolpat
citations should not be treated as confirmed locus-specific background until gene/locus
presence in their tables is verified — currently they add an unearned impression of
GWAS-table support."

### NKX2-4 — WEAKENED toward a more assertive negative

"The 'insufficient evidence either way' framing implies equipoise that the data don't
support. PIP 0.0098 (essentially negligible fine-mapping support) plus zero dedicated
literature is not a 50/50 call — it's a case with no positive evidence and weak-to-no
credible-set support. I'd reframe as WEAKENED toward negative ('no credible support;
paralogy to NKX2-1/TTF-1 is suggestive homology-based speculation only, not evidence')
rather than genuinely undetermined."
