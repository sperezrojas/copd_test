# Audit / excluded-evidence appendix — locus 3:114,350,001-114,550,000 (ZBTB20)

This file is the traceability record of everything excluded from the main evidence table (evidence_tables/3_114350001-114550000.md), plus the full reasoning behind the CONTESTED gene-level verdict. It exists so a reviewer can be handed the evidence table alone without the audit trail, or both together.

## Excluded citations (CLAIM_MISMATCH)

Two citations returned by the researcher were verdicted CLAIM_MISMATCH by the citation-verifier and are excluded from the evidence table:

1. **Cai M, Dai S, Chen W, et al. "Environmental factors, seven GWAS-identified susceptibility loci, and risk of gastric cancer and its precursors in a Chinese population." Cancer Med. 2017. PMID: 28220687, DOI: 10.1002/cam4.1038.**
   - Researcher's claim: ZBTB20 rs9841504 (distinct SNP, gastric-cancer locus) was associated with severe intestinal metaplasia/dysplasia and evaluated for interaction with smoking/alcohol, with no significant SNP-by-smoking interaction reported for this variant.
   - Verifier finding: the paper does confirm the ZBTB20 rs9841504 association with severe IM/dysplasia, but the abstract does NOT state "no significant SNP-by-smoking interaction" for ZBTB20 specifically — the interaction analyses reported in the abstract cover other SNPs (PSCA, PRKAA1, SLC52A3), not ZBTB20's rs9841504.
   - Red-team-skeptic addendum: this citation should be treated as contributing nothing to the smoking-interaction evidence base (neither positive nor negative) — the paper simply didn't test that specific interaction, rather than testing it and finding a null result.

2. **PMID: 22374165 — Zhang Y, et al. "The zinc finger protein ZBTB20 regulates transcription of fructose-1,6-bisphosphatase 1 and β cell function in mice." Gastroenterology. 2012.**
   - Researcher's claim: links ZBTB20 to "hepatocyte glucose metabolism" (liver-specific claim).
   - Verifier finding: the paper's actual title/abstract focus is on pancreatic **β-cell function** and insulin secretion, not hepatocytes — an organ/cell-type misattribution.
   - Red-team-skeptic addendum: flagged as a caution on abstract-reading care; combined with the Cai et al. mismatch above, this is a mild pattern (2 of ~21 citations for this gene) that should make synthesis cautious about assuming perfect fidelity in the remaining VERIFIED citations' characterizations, even though the verifier did not flag further issues.

No citations were verdicted RETRACTED or NOT_FOUND for this locus (19 of 21 citations were VERIFIED; see evidence_tables/_status/3_114350001-114550000/verified_citations.md for the full verdict list).

## CONTESTED gene: ZBTB20 (at the COPD-relevance level, not the gene-identity level)

The red-team-skeptic marked **ZBTB20 CONTESTED**, with the following reasoning preserved verbatim from the critique (evidence_tables/_status/3_114350001-114550000/critique.md):

> "The critic marks the ZBTB20 -> COPD inference CONTESTED — not because a paper argues for a different causal gene, but because the trait-level evidence directly contradicts the premise that this locus acts on COPD through pulmonary biology at all. The Open Targets structured lookup is unambiguous: an explicit curated-association query for COPD/FEV1/lung-function/emphysema returned zero hits, and the credible-set search at the exact lead variant found no COPD or pulmonary-function GWAS credible set whatsoever — only smoking-cessation, smoking-behavior, mean corpuscular volume, height, blood pressure, and aortic cross-sectional area. This locus's presence in this review rests on it being in the credible set of whatever COPD GWAS generated the locus list, not on convergent independent COPD/FEV1 signals — a materially weaker starting evidentiary position than most loci in a COPD prioritization pipeline. The critic is not contesting 'ZBTB20 is the right gene at this LD block' (that part holds); it is contesting 'this locus has anything to do with COPD as a lung disease' as opposed to being a smoking-behavior/general pleiotropic locus that entered the COPD GWAS via smoking-related case ascertainment."

Supporting reasoning on alternative explanations (verbatim, condensed from the critique):

> "Given (a) L2G 0.61+ specifically for smoking-cessation and smoking-behavior GWAS at this exact variant, (b) an independent MTAG explicitly naming ZBTB20 as a smoking-behavior locus, (c) zero COPD/FEV1 curated associations, and (d) zero FEV1/spirometry GWAS literature hits, the most likely single explanation is that this locus entered the COPD credible-set list because smoking behavior is a strong causal/confounding driver of COPD case status in an unadjusted GWAS — exactly per prior #1... A third alternative not explicitly discussed by the researcher: this locus is broadly pleiotropic (height, diastolic BP, aortic cross-sectional area, MCV, alcohol/risk-taking, plus Primrose-syndrome neurodevelopmental phenotypes) in a way that suggests a general growth/developmental transcription-factor hub... rather than a tissue-specific COPD mechanism. High pleiotropy across unrelated organ systems with zero lung-specific curated disease association is itself an argument that any COPD signal here is more likely a byproduct of the smoking-behavior/general pleiotropy axis than a lung-autonomous mechanism."

## TIGIT: flagged as genuinely unresearched, not evaluated

TIGIT (an inhibitory immune-checkpoint receptor on T/NK cells) sits near this locus and appears as a weak Open Targets L2G competitor (0.05-0.16, vs. ZBTB20's 0.61-0.65) at the lead variant. It was not one of the locus's designated candidate genes (nearest/FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS/ABC all point to ZBTB20 or ZBTB20-AS5), so it was not independently researched by the researcher, and the red-team-skeptic's own attempt to search for TIGIT-COPD contradicting evidence failed due to session WebSearch-budget exhaustion. Per the critique: "flag to synthesis-lead as a genuinely unresearched candidate at this locus, though L2G scores argue against it being the driver gene." This is noted here for traceability, not as a contested verdict (no positive TIGIT evidence was ever produced to contest).

## Disclosed search-budget gaps (material limitation, both researcher and red-team-skeptic stages)

- **Researcher stage**: WebSearch budget was exhausted before two planned queries could run — "ZBTB20-AS5 lncRNA gene function" and "ZBTB20 rs1606494 COPD GWAS." bioRxiv/medRxiv MCP tools do not support keyword search (date/category-filtered only), so no targeted preprint search for COPD/lung-function evidence on this gene was possible at all in this pipeline.
- **Red-team-skeptic stage**: independently attempted two contradiction-search queries ("ZBTB20 COPD lung function GWAS" and "TIGIT COPD lung emphysema GWAS gene") to fill exactly this gap; both failed for the same reason (session WebSearch budget already exhausted at 200/200).
- **Net effect**: the single most decision-relevant missing query in this entire locus review — a direct, unconstrained search for "ZBTB20 COPD" / "ZBTB20 rs1606494 lung function" / any recent COPD-spirometry GWAS or preprint naming this locus — was never successfully executed by any stage of this pipeline. Given Open Targets already shows zero COPD/FEV1 curated associations at this locus, it is plausible this query would return nothing new, but that is an assumption, not a confirmed finding. This is the primary reason the Prioritized-gene confidence in the main evidence table is capped at LOW rather than MODERATE, and is flagged here explicitly as a recommended follow-up search for the study team (ideally in a fresh session with unexhausted search budget) before this locus's conclusion is treated as final.
