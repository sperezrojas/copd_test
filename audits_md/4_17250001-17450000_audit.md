# Audit trail: locus 4:17,250,001-17,450,000 (lead variant rs921371; nearest/FLAMES gene QDPR)

This file preserves everything excluded from the main evidence table
(`evidence_tables/4_17250001-17450000.md`) plus the full reasoning behind every CONTESTED /
WEAKENED verdict, verbatim, so a reviewer can audit how the final synthesis was reached
without needing the evidence table itself to carry this material.

## Excluded citations (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH)

### CLAIM_MISMATCH: Open Targets' internal citation for QDPR FEV1/FEV1-FVC evidence

Open Targets' own database cites **PMID 30595370** as the source for its QDPR/FEV1/FEV1-FVC
`gwas_credible_sets` genetic-association evidence records (score ~0.074-0.075). The
researcher interpreted this as Open Targets likely intending to reference Shrine et al.
2019 (the standard large lung-function GWAS). Citation-verifier looked up PMID 30595370
directly and found it is actually **Kichaev et al., American Journal of Human Genetics
2019, "Leveraging Polygenic Functional Enrichment to Improve GWAS Power"** — a GWAS
statistical-methods/power paper with no relationship to lung function or COPD. The correct
PMID for Shrine et al. 2019 ("New genetic signals for lung function highlight pathways and
chronic obstructive pulmonary disease associations across multiple ancestries," Nat Genet
51(3):481-493) is **PMID 30804560** (DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0321-7, PMC6397078), which was
independently confirmed by the researcher via full-text PMC retrieval and by
citation-verifier via PubMed lookup.

**Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH.** Excluded from the evidence table as a positive citation.
Implication: Open Targets' own structured-database metadata contains an internal citation
error for this specific evidence record, which somewhat undermines confidence in that
record's provenance (though not necessarily its underlying L2G score computation, which is
a separate statistical pipeline from the citation metadata field).

## Citations verified but excluded from prominent evidence-table use, or flagged for restricted interpretation

None of the remaining 10 verified citations were excluded outright, but several carry
explicit interpretive restrictions preserved here verbatim from the researcher and
red-team-skeptic:

- **Wang et al. 2026 (PMID 42678580, DNMT1/QDPR in lung adenocarcinoma):** VERIFIED to
  exist and match its claim, but both the researcher and skeptic flagged this as lung-CANCER
  epigenetic/chemosensitivity biology, mechanistically unrelated to airway obstruction,
  emphysema, or COPD-relevant inflammation. Skeptic quote: "High risk if not firewalled...
  if it appears at all, it must be labeled explicitly as lung-cancer-only and
  non-COPD-relevant, or dropped from the evidence table entirely to avoid the citation
  being skimmed later as generic 'QDPR + lung' support." Retained in the evidence table
  only with this explicit caveat attached.
- **Background BH4/macrophage/iNOS pathway papers (PMID 15601772, 15565652, 10878285,
  10435048):** VERIFIED to exist, but the researcher explicitly stated these are not
  QDPR-specific — they concern GCH1 (BH4 synthesis), not QDPR (BH4 recycling) — and were
  not independently critiqued gene-by-gene by the skeptic. Included in the evidence table
  only as labeled background-pathway context, not QDPR-specific mechanistic evidence.
- **Liu et al. 2019 / GSCAN (PMID 30643251):** VERIFIED as a real, correctly-identified
  paper, but neither the researcher nor the skeptic could confirm or rule out whether this
  exact locus overlaps a genome-wide-significant smoking-behavior signal in this dataset.
  This remains an explicitly open, unresolved question — not evidence for or against
  smoking-mediation at this locus.

## Genes marked CONTESTED or WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (reasoning preserved verbatim)

### QDPR — CONTESTED

"The case for QDPR is not 'real evidence with an overreached inference' (WEAKENED) — it is
closer to 'no substantive statistical-genetic evidence survives scrutiny.' Every
load-bearing piece either evaporates on inspection (wrong OT citation) or is structurally
weak (nearest-gene heuristic, sub-threshold L2G, absence from the field's largest
lung-function meta-analysis). At the same locus, Open Targets' own L2G algorithm ranks a
neighboring gene (CLRN2, 0.476) nearly 4x higher than QDPR (0.123) — this is 'a different
gene implicated at the same locus' per the contradicting-evidence criterion, even though
the trait context (body weight) differs. Combined, this is credible contradicting evidence
against QDPR's primacy, not merely absence of confirming evidence. Caveat for the audit
trail: this is not a single adversarial paper saying 'QDPR is wrong, it's actually gene X
for COPD' — it is a convergence of (i) database-internal contradiction (wrong citation),
(ii) silence in the largest relevant GWAS, and (iii) algorithmic outranking by a neighbor
for an unrelated trait. This should be weighed as strong but indirect contestation, not a
definitive refutation."

On the neurobiology/smoking hypothesis: "Mostly [appropriately hedged] — the researcher
explicitly labels it 'biologically plausible but currently unconfirmed at this locus' and
flags the GSCAN locus-overlap question as open rather than resolved... the one piece of
human genetic evidence cited in support of the BH4/dopamine mechanism (Clot et al. 2009)
actually screened QDPR in a dystonia cohort and found zero QDPR mutations — all pathogenic
variants were in GCH1/TH/SPR/PARK2. That is not a rebuttal of the common-variant COPD
hypothesis (different variant class, different disease), but it is a data point that
QDPR-specific human phenotypic evidence for this pathway is thin even in a disease where
the mechanistic story would predict it."

Thoroughness gaps flagged: "The researcher explicitly notes two unresolved gaps: (1)
whether a more recent (post-2019) large-scale lung-function GWAS names QDPR, and (2)
whether GSCAN/smoking-behavior GWAS shows a genome-wide-significant signal at this exact
locus. Given QDPR is the only official candidate at this locus, both of these are
decision-relevant and should be closed out before final determination."

### CLRN2 (off-list) — CONTESTED

"Physically farther from the lead variant than QDPR, yet outranks it in Open Targets' own
L2G (0.476 vs 0.123) — but that credible set is anchored to a FinnGen body-weight GWAS,
not lung function or COPD. No PubMed evidence connects CLRN2 to any pulmonary phenotype;
the one verified CLRN2 paper (Wentling et al. 2026) is inner-ear/Usher-syndrome auditory
biology with no plausible lung mechanism.

...this is the most interesting gap in the researcher's write-up. The researcher's
dismissal ('higher L2G is likely regulatory/eQTL-driven in the FinnGen body-weight dataset
rather than proximity-driven; no biological mechanism plausibly links CLRN2 to COPD')
addresses whether CLRN2 itself is a COPD gene, but doesn't engage with the locus-level
question: if the strongest algorithmic gene-assignment at this exact lead variant is for
body weight, and BMI/adiposity is a well-established COPD comorbidity and effect-modifier
(bidirectional relationships with FEV1/FVC via chest-wall mechanics, diaphragm excursion,
cachexia in advanced disease, and shared smoking-BMI genetic correlations), then a live
alternative hypothesis is: this locus's primary phenotype is metabolic/adiposity-related,
and the FEV1/FEV1-FVC/COPD associations are secondary — either genuine pleiotropy through
a shared metabolic-pulmonary pathway, or confounding via smoking (which independently
affects both body weight and lung function), or a spirometry artifact of body habitus
rather than intrinsic airway/parenchymal biology. This hypothesis is not resolved by
anything in the current evidence base... and I did not get to search it independently
(budget exhausted). This should be flagged as a specific, testable follow-up (e.g., check
whether rs921371/proxies appear in GIANT BMI/WHR GWAS or UKB body-composition GWAS, and
whether an MR of BMI-on-FEV1 implicates this region) before concluding the locus is 'QDPR,
smoking-mediated' versus 'adiposity-mediated pleiotropy with secondary spirometric
signal.'

Verdict: CONTESTED — not as an established COPD gene (it has no direct lung evidence and
shouldn't be promoted to candidate status on current evidence), but as a genuine challenge
to QDPR's claimed primacy at this locus, via an under-explored metabolic-mediation pathway
that neither the researcher nor I have been able to rule in or out."

### LAP3 (off-list) — WEAKENED

"Most physically distal gene at the locus. The only 'COPD' signal is 8 EuropePMC
text-mining co-occurrence records (low scores 0.02-0.12) that the researcher explicitly
declined to pull and verify, and that are not genetic-association evidence at all —
text-mining co-occurrence can mean nothing more than both terms appearing in the same
review article. No PubMed evidence connects LAP3 to COPD, airway/alveolar structural
biology, or lung immune/inflammatory disease. If these EuropePMC hits were ever promoted
into a synthesis narrative as 'LAP3 has COPD literature support,' that would be a clear
overclaim — the underlying records are unverified co-occurrence counts, not vetted
citations, and should be marked UNVERIFIED for the citation-verifier if anyone attempts to
use them downstream.

Verdict: WEAKENED (in the conditional sense that the only available 'evidence' —
unverified text-mining hits — would represent inference overreach if used to support
candidacy; on current information there is no real case for or against LAP3, it is
essentially a non-starter that received a shallow/incomplete pass, not a full one)."

## Unresolved gaps carried forward from both researcher and red-team-skeptic

1. Red-team-skeptic's own web-search-based contradiction pass across all three genes
   (QDPR, CLRN2, LAP3) plus a targeted rs921371/smoking-locus check returned "search
   budget exhausted" before any results were retrieved — **zero independent adversarial
   web searches were completed for this locus.** The skeptic's critique is reasoning over
   the existing evidence base supplied by the researcher and Open Targets, not
   independently confirmed absence of contradicting literature. Flagged explicitly by the
   skeptic as a limitation of this review, not of the researcher's work.
2. Whether a post-2019 large-scale multi-ancestry lung-function GWAS (more recent than
   Shrine et al. 2019) names QDPR with higher confidence — not resolved in this review.
3. Whether GSCAN or another smoking-initiation/cessation/heaviness GWAS shows a
   genome-wide-significant signal overlapping rs921371 or its LD proxies — not resolved in
   this review.
4. Whether this locus overlaps a BMI/adiposity GWAS signal (beyond the single FinnGen
   body-weight credible set already identified via Open Targets) and whether a
   Mendelian-randomization analysis of BMI-on-FEV1/COPD implicates this region — not
   investigated in this review; flagged as the key follow-up needed to adjudicate the
   CLRN2/metabolic-mediation alternative hypothesis.
5. The 8 LAP3 europepmc COPD-co-occurrence records (PMIDs/preprints 39635041, PPR231094,
   PPR206620, 36675165, 34145303, 35436182, 37511021, 29097502) were never independently
   pulled, read, or verified by the researcher, citation-verifier, or red-team-skeptic in
   this review — they remain unvetted Open Targets text-mining signals only, explicitly
   not usable as evidence unless independently investigated in a future pass.
