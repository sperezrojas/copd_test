## CONTESTED gene verdicts (preserved verbatim from red-team-skeptic)

### PTPRE — CONTESTED

Full critique, verbatim from red-team-skeptic:

---

**LD/nearest-gene bias.** Independent check: the credible-set window (127.95-128.10 Mb) falls
almost entirely within the PTPRE gene body (127.91-128.09 Mb); the nearest other annotated
gene, FANK1, sits just outside the window (127.58-127.69 Mb). So this is not a classic
"several plausible genes in one LD block" situation — PTPRE genuinely is the only gene
physically overlapping the window, which is a legitimate mitigating point against
nearest-gene bias. However, that only establishes positional exclusivity, not functional
causality: the researcher's findings contain no GTEx (or other) lung-tissue eQTL
colocalization for rs10829316->PTPRE at all. An intronic PIP=0.947 variant with no reported
lung eQTL/regulatory link is a real gap — the case rests on position alone, not on a
demonstrated lung regulatory mechanism. This should be flagged as a thoroughness gap: request
the researcher explicitly check GTEx (or equivalent) lung eQTL/sQTL colocalization for PTPRE
before synthesis.

**Smoking-mediation.** The researcher is appropriately transparent that PTPRE could not be
confirmed as a named/implicated gene in any of the three major smoking GWAS papers searched
(Liu 2019, Saunders 2022, Toikumo 2024) — the Open Targets smoking-initiation link (score
~0.10, itself weak) is unconfirmed by literature, only speculated. My own independent search
for "PTPRE smoking initiation GWAS fine-mapped/credible set" also returned nothing naming
PTPRE. So the smoking-mediation hypothesis is currently unsupported by any verified citation —
it is not a demonstrated alternative pathway, just a plausible one based on a weak
structured-database score. This should not be treated as established; it's speculative on
both sides (neither a direct-lung nor a smoking-behavior mechanism is actually demonstrated).

**Spirometry/pulmonary-function concordance.** None found — explicitly absent, and my
independent search of major spirometry/COPD GWAS literature (Shrine 2019, TOPMed WGS papers,
Hall 2019, etc.) also surfaced no PTPRE mention and no result for rs10829316 specifically. Per
the pipeline's own stated prior, a COPD association without FEV1/FEV1-FVC support warrants
real scrutiny of alternative pathways — that prior applies squarely here, and the case is
further weakened by there being no direct COPD association evidence either (only Open
Targets' single weak, score-0.02 text-mining co-mention, explicitly non-genetic).

**Structural/developmental lung biology.** Completely absent — the researcher searched and
found nothing, and correctly excluded PTPRA (a distinct paralog) papers that were
miscategorized. This is a genuine, well-documented gap, not a search failure.

**Immune/inflammatory biology.** The immune evidence is real but consistently off-tissue:
osteoclast/bone biology (Finkelshtein, Granot-Attas, Sheng & Lau, Aicher), JAK-STAT
regulation in murine myeloid leukemia cell lines (Tanuma), and HPA neutrophil/monocyte-
enriched expression (database, unverified, not independently peer-reviewed). None of this has
been tested in lung, airway, or COPD models. Given the pipeline's prior on inflammatory
heterogeneity in COPD (neutrophilic, macrophage, innate programs are all relevant), the
neutrophil/myeloid expression pattern is consistent with a plausible COPD mechanism in
principle, but this is speculative extrapolation from bone/leukemia-cell-line biology — not
demonstrated lung-relevant biology. This should be labeled clearly as suggestive-but-untested,
not supporting evidence.

**Asthma/atopy findings.** Both cited associations (Tremblay 2008, Murk 2011) are contested —
one failed replication in an independent cohort, the other lost significance after covariate
adjustment. These should not be counted as positive evidence for PTPRE in any COPD-adjacent
airway phenotype; the researcher correctly flags this, but a synthesis lead skimming quickly
could mistake "two asthma papers" for supporting evidence rather than two non-replicated
findings.

**Contradicting/alternative-hypothesis evidence.** The most reproducible, independent genetic
signal for PTPRE across the entire evidence base is not pulmonary or even smoking-behavioral —
it's neurodevelopmental: TWAS association with autism spectrum disorder at brain tissue
(Rodriguez-Fontenla 2021) and brain-restricted eQTL colocalization with the ASD GWAS signal at
r=0.97 (Dominguez-Alonso 2023), corroborated by PTPRE differential expression across
schizophrenia stages in prefrontal cortex (Narayan 2008). Three independent CNS-focused
findings converging on brain-specific PTPRE regulation is a more consistent, more reproducible
signal than anything COPD- or lung-adjacent in this evidence base. This constitutes a genuine
alternative hypothesis: this locus's brain-expression/regulatory architecture may make it more
plausible as a pleiotropic neurobehavioral locus (potentially feeding into smoking behavior
via CNS pathways, consistent with prior #1, though unconfirmed) than as a direct lung-biology
locus. I am not asserting these ASD papers directly refute a PTPRE-COPD causal link (they
don't test COPD), but they do substantively undercut the specificity and strength of the case
for a pulmonary mechanism, and should be weighted as contesting evidence for a lung-causal
interpretation of this locus.

**Overclaiming check.** The researcher's own summary judgment is notably measured and does not
overclaim — it explicitly states the CNS signal is the most consistent one and flags
smoking-initiation as "plausible-but-unconfirmed." The overclaiming risk lies downstream, at
synthesis: there is a real risk that a synthesis step could compress "immune biology is
genuine" + "neutrophil-enriched expression" into an implied COPD-relevant neutrophilic-
inflammation story, without carrying forward the caveat that none of it was tested in
lung/airway tissue and that the single most robust genetic signal points to brain, not lung,
biology.

**Thoroughness flag for synthesis lead.** Two gaps worth flagging before finalizing: (1) no
lung-tissue eQTL/sQTL colocalization evidence was reported for rs10829316-PTPRE — this should
exist or be explicitly confirmed absent (GTEx lung, etc.) rather than simply not mentioned;
(2) confirm whether major spirometry/COPD GWAS papers (Shrine et al. 2019 Nat Genet,
Sakornsakolpat et al., recent TOPMed/multi-ancestry lung-function WGS papers) were
specifically checked for PTPRE or rs10829316 and returned null, versus not checked at all —
the write-up states "no FEV1/FEV1-FVC GWAS literature naming PTPRE was found" but doesn't
confirm which specific major papers were searched.

**Verdict: CONTESTED.** The inference that PTPRE is the causal COPD gene at this locus rests
almost entirely on positional proximity (sole gene in a gene-dense-free window) plus a single
high-PIP intronic variant, with no direct COPD or FEV1/FEV1-FVC association, no lung eQTL
colocalization reported, non-replicated/adjustment-nullified asthma associations, immune
biology confined to bone/leukemia-cell-line contexts never tested in lung, and — most
importantly — the single most reproducible independent genetic signal (three converging
papers: TWAS + brain eQTL coloc r=0.97 + differential CNS expression across schizophrenia
stages) pointing to a neurodevelopmental/CNS trait rather than pulmonary or even confirmed
smoking-behavioral biology. This is credible contesting evidence against a confident
lung-causal claim for PTPRE, even though it does not directly test or refute COPD. Recommend
synthesis lead treat PTPRE as a low-confidence/uncertain candidate at this locus, explicitly
noting the CNS-trait alternative hypothesis and the missing lung eQTL evidence, rather than a
well-supported pulmonary or immune COPD gene.

## Other notes preserved for traceability

- **Region check:** the researcher independently confirmed via Ensembl REST that PTPRE
  (ENSG00000132334, 127,906,800-128,085,856 GRCh38) is the only protein-coding gene whose body
  substantially overlaps the 10:127,950,001-128,100,000 locus window. Three PTPRE-antisense
  lncRNAs also overlap (non-coding, not pursued as candidates). MKI67
  (128,096,659-128,126,458) clips the region's far 3' boundary (~90 kb from the lead/credible-
  set variant) but was treated as a boundary artifact, not a serious candidate — searched for
  MKI67 + COPD/lung-function/airway-epithelium literature and found nothing.
- **Paralog caution:** PubMed MeSH groups PTPRE with its distinct paralog PTPRA (chromosome
  20, "Receptor-Like Protein Tyrosine Phosphatases, Class 4"). Lung-fibrosis papers that
  surface under naive "PTPRE" searches — Aschner et al. 2022 (PMID 35670474, DOI
  10.1152/ajplung.00436.2021) and Aschner et al. 2014 (PMID 24650563, DOI
  10.1016/j.ajpath.2014.01.016) — are actually about PTPRA/PTPα in fibroproliferative
  ARDS/bleomycin-induced pulmonary fibrosis, and were correctly excluded from the PTPRE
  evidence base by the researcher. Recorded here so this exclusion is not silently lost.
- **Open Targets tooling limitation:** general GraphQL schema-introspection calls
  (`get_open_targets_graphql_schema`, `get_type_dependencies`) repeatedly exceeded the tool's
  output size limit (>70,000-107,000 characters) for this session regardless of how narrowly
  scoped the category/type list was. Query shape was instead worked out empirically via
  trial-and-error against the live API. This was sufficient to retrieve target-level
  constraint/tractability and gene-disease evidence records, but a locus-specific
  colocalization (`CredibleSet` type) query could not be constructed in the time available.
  This is a session tooling limitation, not a confirmed absence of colocalization data in
  Open Targets for this locus — flagged so a future re-run knows to retry this specifically.
