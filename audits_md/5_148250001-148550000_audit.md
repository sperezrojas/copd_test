### Caveat on an otherwise-VERIFIED citation

- **Meyer-Hoffert U et al., PLoS ONE 2009, PMID 19190773 (SPINK9).** VERIFIED WITH LOCATION
  DISCREPANCY. The biological claim used (SPINK9/LEKTI-2 is a skin stratum-corneum-localized
  kallikrein-5-specific protease inhibitor, expressed in human skin/keratinocytes) is
  accurately represented. However, the source paper itself states the gene is at
  "chromosome 5p33.1" -- this does not match current genome builds (SPINK9 is at 5q32, and
  "5p33.1" is not even a valid cytogenetic band, since chromosome 5's short arm does not
  extend to band 33). This is a discrepancy in the source paper's own stated location, not
  a citation-accuracy problem for the claim as used here. Retained in the evidence table
  with this caveat rather than excluded.

## Genes marked CONTESTED or otherwise flagged by red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning)

### SH3TC2 -- CONTESTED

"No literature connects this gene to lung/COPD/FEV1 at all -- only MAGMA/PoPS
prioritization (proximity/expression-based, not tied to any specific credible-set variant),
and its only validated biology (CMT4C, Schwann-cell myelination, LOEUF ~0.80 consistent with
recessive-disease tolerance) is unrelated to lung. Ordinarily that would justify calling it
unsupported. However, Open Targets structured data contradicts a clean dismissal: SH3TC2
carries the single highest FEV1 L2G-style scores at the entire locus (0.79-0.83), despite
zero COPD-trait rows. This is a genuine, unresolved discordant data point that argues
against fully writing SH3TC2 off. This specific numeric claim should be flagged as
UNVERIFIED/needs tracing (variant IDs, study accession) before being used to argue either
for or against SH3TC2 in synthesis.

VERDICT: CONTESTED. Not because of dueling literature (there is none), but because there is
a real, high-magnitude, unexplained quantitative signal (OT FEV1 L2G ~0.8) in tension with
the 'no lung connection' conclusion the literature absence would otherwise support. Should
not be resolved by default toward HTR4 without someone tracing the underlying OT rows."

### HTR4 -- WEAKENED (not contested, but confidence capped)

"The statistical-genetics case (three independent FEV1/COPD-concordant GWAS) is genuinely
strong and satisfies prior #2 well. But the mechanistic case overreaches if presented as
'HTR4 causes COPD-like airflow obstruction' -- the mouse/human tissue data collectively
point to a reversible, vagally-mediated AHR phenotype with no structural or inflammatory
COPD-model phenotype, which is a materially different (and asthma-adjacent) mechanism than
classic COPD pathology. The Shrine 2023 citation is used in a way that implies more
contemporary confirmation than it delivers. Smoking-mediation is plausibly (not
conclusively) ruled out."

Specific mechanistic-mismatch reasoning (verbatim): "What the mouse data actually show is a
reversible, cholinergic/vagally-mediated bronchoconstrictive phenotype with explicitly no
structural, inflammatory, or fibrotic phenotype in any COPD-relevant injury model (LPS =
neutrophilic/innate, bleomycin = fibrotic/injury, HDM = allergic). That is close to being
negative evidence for HTR4 acting through classic COPD mechanisms, and positive evidence for
an AHR/neural-reflex mechanism that overlaps more naturally with asthma or asthma-COPD
overlap (ACO) than with emphysema-type COPD... the neural/vagal mechanism (House 2017) is a
plausible alternative but is a distinct, more speculative causal chain than 'HTR4 acts in
the lung.'"

### FBXO38 -- HOLDS as not-a-strong-candidate, but PD-1 contradiction flagged as a distraction risk

"Not prioritized by any statistical method, found only via manual proximity scan, zero
lung/COPD literature. The Meng 2018 vs. Dibus 2024 PD-1-ubiquitination contradiction is real
(both VERIFIED, genuinely head-to-head) and the cGAS-STING/CD8+ T-cell finding (Wu 2024) is
a distinct, more recent immune mechanism -- but neither touches lung, airway, or COPD in any
way, and FBXO38's only validated human disease role (dHMN type IID, motor-neuron axon
biology) is also lung-irrelevant. Including a paragraph on an unrelated immune contradiction
for a gene with no connection to this locus's phenotype is not wrong to document (it shows
due diligence that the neighbor gene was checked and its own literature is internally
contested on an unrelated topic), but it risks reading as locus-relevant immune biology if
synthesis isn't careful -- it is not. Recommend this be compressed to one sentence in
synthesis."

### SPINK7 / SPINK9 / SPINK13 -- HOLDS as not credible candidates

"All three are real genes with real (verified) biology, but SPINK7's epithelial-barrier
evidence is entirely esophageal (eosinophilic esophagitis, esophageal cancer), SPINK9 is
skin-restricted (also note: the cited source paper itself mislabels the cytogenetic location
as '5p33.1,' a red flag about that paper's own care with genomic coordinates, though not
about the biological claim), and SPINK13 has no literature at all. All three sit ~150+ kb
from the nearest credible-set variant, well outside anything a PIP-weighted credible set
implicates, and none is flagged by any prioritization method. This is appropriately treated
as very-low-priority background, not a real competing hypothesis."
