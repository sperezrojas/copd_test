## CONTESTED genes (red-team-skeptic reasoning, preserved verbatim)

### KCNB2 — CONTESTED (physical coordinate mismatch)

> This is the clearest problem gene at the locus. The researcher's own Ensembl REST check establishes that KCNB2 physically sits at chr8:72,537,220-72,938,349 — **760kb to 1.2Mb outside the stated locus window and with zero credible-set variants anywhere near it**. That is not a marginal edge-case like LY96's 9kb overlap; it is a flat non-overlap. Its only claim to being "at this locus" is that an upstream PoPS annotation named it, and PoPS is a method that intentionally does *not* restrict to physical proximity (it prioritizes on functional similarity across the genome, which is a known and often-cited limitation of the method) — but even PoPS implementations are typically still restricted to a defined cis-window per locus, and a ~1Mb miss from the stated window suggests either (a) the input pipeline used a different/wider locus definition when generating the PoPS call than the credible-set window handed to this reviewer, or (b) a genuine pipeline error in gene-locus assignment. Either way, this needs to be resolved upstream before KCNB2 is treated as a serious candidate for *this* credible set.
>
> The biological plausibility argument the researcher offers (neuronal K+ channel → smoking-behavior mediation hypothesis) is reasonable in the abstract and consistent with prior #1 (smoking-mediated pathway), but it is doing work to rescue a gene that isn't even genomically linked to the signal — that's backward-reasoning risk (biological plausibility being used to paper over a coordinate mismatch) and should be named as such. The "smoking initiation" Open Targets flag (score 0.405) is a database-level score with **no corroborating literature found by the researcher**, so it can't independently rescue the candidacy either.
>
> **Verdict: CONTESTED.** The physical non-overlap with the credible set is itself the contradicting evidence — it directly contradicts the premise that KCNB2 is implicated by this locus at all. I'd recommend synthesis-lead either drop KCNB2 from consideration for this locus pending resolution of the coordinate discrepancy with whoever generated the PoPS input file, or explicitly carry it forward labeled as "PoPS-named but genomically non-overlapping — likely erroneous inclusion."

### LY96 (MD-2) — CONTESTED (as a claim about this specific locus)

> This is the gene where the write-up risks the most seductive overclaim, and it's worth being explicit about why. The mechanistic literature is genuinely the strongest COPD-specific biology at the entire locus: MD2/TLR4/MyD88 signaling directly implicated in cigarette-smoke-driven airway inflammation and ferroptosis in both mouse models and human airway tissue, across two independent groups (Xi 2025, Pace 2015), plus a gene-environment endotoxin-exposure interaction study (Smit 2011). That's real, and it's COPD- and smoking-specific in a way nothing else at this locus is.
>
> But **is the "compelling" framing justified as evidence for this locus?** No — and this needs to be stated plainly to synthesis-lead. LY96 was not surfaced by any of the four prioritization methods (nearest-gene, FLAMES, PoPS, or credible-set annotation); it was added via mandatory whole-region gene enumeration. Zero credible-set variants map to it. The physical overlap is the first ~9kb of a 41kb gene at the extreme 3' edge of the stated window, and the nearest actual credible-set variants sit 120-240kb away. There is no colocalization, no eQTL link, no eQTL-mediated regulatory hypothesis offered beyond speculative "possible long-range enhancer contact." This is close to the textbook case the task description warns about: strong biological priors pulling toward a gene that the fine-mapping evidence does not actually support. The compelling biology is a reason to study LY96 as a COPD gene *in general* (e.g., in its own right, independent of this GWAS locus), not a reason to believe this particular locus's signal acts through it. Synthesis-lead should not let the strength of the mechanistic story override the near-total absence of statistical/positional linkage.
>
> **The internal Xi 2025 vs. Pace 2015 contradiction** compounds this problem at a second level, independent of the locus-linkage issue: the two papers disagree on the *direction* of MD2 dysregulation in COPD/smoking (Xi: MD2 up in COPD lungs and CSE-exposed mice, driving inflammation; Pace: MD2 down in smoker/COPD human airway epithelium, with CSE decreasing MD2 in vitro). The researcher flagged this but didn't resolve it, and it shouldn't be waved away as a minor caveat — a pathway where the direction of effect flips between a 2015 human ex vivo study and a 2025 mouse/cell-line study undermines confidence in "MD2/LY96 is a clean, direction-consistent COPD driver" as a general claim, not just as it pertains to this locus. Plausible explanations exist (species, tissue compartment — large airway epithelium vs. whole lung/cell line, disease stage, protein vs. transcript), but none are tested or resolved in the cited material, so this should be carried forward as an open contradiction, not smoothed over.
>
> **Verdict: CONTESTED** — specifically contested *as a claim about this locus*. The fine-mapping/positional evidence does not support LY96 being the gene through which this credible set acts (this is close to a textbook non-supported "nearest enumerated gene" inclusion, the inverse problem of LD/nearest-gene bias — here it's "compelling biology, wrong gene-locus link"), and even the general LY96-COPD mechanistic literature is internally directionally inconsistent between its two key papers. Recommend synthesis-lead present LY96 as "biologically interesting, statistically unsupported at this locus" rather than as a leading candidate, and flag the Xi/Pace directional conflict explicitly rather than let the researcher's summary line ("strongest COPD-specific mechanistic literature of any gene here") stand unqualified — as written, that line risks readers weighting LY96 more heavily than the (absent) fine-mapping evidence warrants.

### UBE2W and TCEB1/ELOC — not formally CONTESTED but WEAKENED (included for completeness)

> **UBE2W verdict: WEAKENED.** The PIP mass genuinely implicates UBE2W's genomic footprint at this locus, but the inference that this represents a *COPD-relevant* mechanism is not supported — the literature is generic cell biology with no lung/COPD specificity, and the repeated non-lung L2G pattern across independent GWAS at the same variant should be read as mild active evidence pointing this locus's function elsewhere (metabolic/behavioral), not toward pulmonary biology.
>
> **TCEB1/ELOC verdict: WEAKENED, bordering null.** The case collapses almost entirely once the one lung-adjacent citation (Gupta 2024, excluded above as CLAIM_MISMATCH) is stripped out as invalid; what remains is generic VHL/HIF biology with no COPD link, positional presence in the window without fine-mapping support, and constraint metrics (oe=0, fully LoF-intolerant) that don't favor a common-variant regulatory mechanism.
