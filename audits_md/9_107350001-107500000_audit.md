CLAIM_MISMATCH but worth flagging here:

  cookie-wall/access limitations. Treat any claim that these two papers "support" this
  specific locus as UNVERIFIED at the locus-attribution level, even though the papers

- For Liu M et al. 2019 (GSCAN, PMID 30643251), the researcher's original DOI
  (10.1038/s41588-018-0193-3) was INCORRECT; the citation-verifier supplied the correct
  DOI (10.1038/s41598-018-0307-5 — corrected in the evidence table to
  10.1038/s41588-018-0307-5). Whether this exact region reaches genome-wide significance
  for smoking initiation in this paper is likewise UNCONFIRMED.

## Genes marked CONTESTED / WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning)

### RAD23B — CONTESTED (dispatch 1) / WEAKENED (dispatch 2)

Two independent red-team-skeptic dispatches were run on this locus (the first could not
complete any independent web search due to an exhausted search budget; the second
partially succeeded via WebFetch and surfaced a new citation). Both flagged RAD23B on
essentially the same grounds. Verbatim reasoning from each:

**Dispatch 1 (CONTESTED verdict), verbatim:**

> LD / gene-distinguishing problem (central issue): The credible-set variants are
> intergenic, sitting between RAD23B and KLF4, and Open Targets' own L2G rankings flip
> order across the two FEV1/FVC studies queried (RAD23B top in one, KLF4 top/comparable
> in the other, 0.317-0.535 range). This is exactly the "evidence doesn't clearly
> distinguish this gene from its neighbor" scenario the framework asks reviewers to
> flag. The researcher acknowledges this directly but still writes a moderately
> favorable summary judgment for RAD23B. Given the rank-flip, I don't think the current
> evidence licenses treating RAD23B as more likely causal than KLF4 (which is out of
> scope for this gene-level review but is the obvious rival explanation).
>
> Smoking-mediation confound: RAD23B's own top cross-trait associations in Open Targets
> are "smoking initiation" and "alcohol drinking" (general, not locus-specific), and the
> entire cancer-risk literature for this gene is smoking-interaction-dependent (GxE with
> smoking/heavy drinking in bladder, laryngeal, lung, breast, colorectal studies). This
> is a live alternative hypothesis (Prior #1) that the researcher names but doesn't
> resolve... Per Prior #2, FEV1/FVC concordance is meaningful supporting evidence for
> pulmonary biology if it's independent of smoking-mediated pathways — that independence
> was not established here, so the "meaningful lung-function colocalization" framing is
> doing more work than the evidence supports.
>
> Verdict: CONTESTED. The Open Targets L2G rank-flip means the evidence genuinely does
> not distinguish RAD23B from KLF4 at this locus — this is the framework's explicit
> trigger for a CONTESTED flag on gene-assignment grounds, independent of any new
> literature I could find. Compounding this, the only two citations that could have
> anchored RAD23B specifically in primary GWAS literature (#1, #3) have unconfirmed
> locus-attribution, and the remaining cancer-genetics literature is
> smoking-interaction-dependent, leaving smoking-behavior mediation as an unresolved
> competing explanation rather than a ruled-out one.

**Dispatch 2 (WEAKENED verdict, with new KLF4 finding), verbatim:**

> The Open Targets L2G/colocalization evidence for FEV1/FVC is real but (a) not
> independently confirmed against the primary Shrine/Sakornsakolpat papers' own locus
> tables (both remain UNVERIFIED at the attribution level — this is the single largest
> hole in the case), and (b) explicitly rank-flips against KLF4 across the two credible
> sets, which the researcher honestly discloses but which undercuts the headline framing
> of "meaningful lung-function colocalization support" as if it were RAD23B-specific...
> The new finding that KLF4 (the competing L2G gene, "out of scope" per the candidate
> list) has established direct biology in airway epithelial basal/secretory-cell
> differentiation makes the unresolved RAD23B/KLF4 tie more consequential: the stronger
> mechanistic story at this locus may belong to the gene this pipeline excluded from
> consideration. Smoking-mediation cannot be ruled in or out.
>
> Verdict: WEAKENED. The lung-function colocalization signal at this region is plausible
> but (1) not confirmed as attributable to RAD23B specifically rather than KLF4 in the
> primary GWAS papers' own tables, (2) unsupported by any COPD-specific or
> airway-structural/immune literature, and (3) the strongest disease-relevant
> mechanistic biology at this locus arguably belongs to the neighboring, excluded gene
> (KLF4). The researcher's own hedging is appropriately calibrated in the detailed text;
> the risk is a reader taking the "Meaningful... colocalization support" framing as
> gene-resolved when it isn't.

Both dispatches disclosed that the session's web-search budget was exhausted before they
could run their own independent contradiction searches; dispatch 2 partially compensated
via WebFetch (dbSNP, GWAS Catalog, PubMed static search pages) but could not access full
text/supplementary tables of the key primary GWAS papers. **This locus's RAD23B-vs-KLF4
gene assignment should be treated as unresolved, not as a settled call**, notwithstanding
RAD23B being named the (LOW-confidence) prioritized gene in the main evidence table by
elimination (OR13C5 was independently deprioritized by both dispatches, see below), not
because RAD23B's own case is strong.

### OR13C5 — WEAKENED (both dispatches, "bordering on effectively non-viable")

**Dispatch 1, verbatim:**

> Verdict: WEAKENED. "Weakened" here is generous — there is essentially no
> COPD/lung-function-relevant case to weaken. PoPS prioritization is a similarity-based
> signal that is not corroborated by L2G, colocalization, or any lung-relevant
> literature; the single cited paper is off-target on variant, age group, and disease.
> This gene should be treated as a low-confidence PoPS artifact rather than a serious
> candidate, and the researcher's own downgrade of it appears sound.

**Dispatch 2, verbatim:**

> Verdict: WEAKENED, bordering on effectively non-viable as a candidate — the evidence
> base is thin enough (one indirect pediatric-infection paper, no statistical-genetics or
> structural link) that "WEAKENED" likely undersells how little there is; I would not
> upgrade it to CONTESTED only because no paper is actively arguing against OR13C5, there
> simply is nothing substantive for it. Flagging for synthesis-lead that this gene
> received no independent verification pass this round.

## Open items flagged by red-team-skeptic for any future re-review of this locus

- Whether the 9:107.4Mb region is tabulated as a genome-wide-significant hit in Shrine
  et al. 2019 (PMID 30804560), Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019 (PMID 30804561), or the GSCAN
  smoking-initiation GWAS (Liu et al. 2019, PMID 30643251) was not resolved after two
  independent red-team-skeptic search attempts (both hit web-search budget exhaustion;
  the second obtained partial access via WebFetch but could not reach the relevant
  full-text/supplementary-table pages). This is the single largest unresolved
  evidentiary gap for this locus and is load-bearing for RAD23B's tier.
- rs12004443 (the lead variant) is not present in the GWAS Catalog and has 0 citations
  in dbSNP as of the September 2026 check — consistent with it being a fine-mapped
  credible-set variant rather than a catalog-indexed sentinel SNP, but it means the lead
  variant itself carries no independent, publicly checkable confirmation of a
  genome-wide-significant signal outside of this pipeline's own fine-mapping output and
  the Open Targets structured lookup.
