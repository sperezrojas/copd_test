# Audit trail — locus 1_117250001-117650000 (MAN1A2)

This file documents excluded citations and CONTESTED/WEAKENED gene-level judgments, with
reasoning preserved verbatim from citation-verifier and red-team-skeptic. Not intended for

## Excluded citations

### CLAIM_MISMATCH

1. **Soremekun et al. 2021**, "Genome-Wide Association and Mendelian Randomization Analysis
   Reveal the Causal Relationship Between White Blood Cell Subtypes and Asthma in
   Africans," Frontiers in Genetics, PMID 34925446, PMC8674726, DOI
   10.3389/fgene.2021.749415.
   - Originally proposed by the researcher (with explicit uncertainty flagged) as possible
     evidence linking LINC01525 to a white-blood-cell-subtype/asthma association in an
     African-ancestry GWAS/MR study.
   - **Citation-verifier verdict (verbatim):** "CLAIM_MISMATCH. Full-text review confirms
     the five novel genes reported are FLG-AS1, HMGN3P1, and MGST3 (plus others with
     corrupted formatting) — LINC01525 is NOT mentioned anywhere in this paper. The
     researcher's tentative claim linking LINC01525 to this study is incorrect and must be
     EXCLUDED from the evidence table."
   - **Red-team-skeptic comment (verbatim):** "The rejected Soremekun citation ... is a
     meaningful red flag pattern, not just a discarded citation. It suggests that when
     evidence for a gene is this thin, there is elevated risk of citation
     hallucination/mismatch being introduced to manufacture apparent support. This should
     be explicitly escalated to synthesis-lead as a caution about the evidence-thinness of
     this candidate ... Flag explicitly that this gene's 'evidence base' was one
     hallucinated citation away from having zero substantive content."
   - **Disposition:** Excluded entirely from the evidence table. LINC01525's only remaining
     substantive citation is Gong et al. 2022 (PMID 36045381), which is verified but
     entirely unrelated to lung/COPD biology (prostate-cancer gene-fusion partner).

## Gene-level CONTESTED/WEAKENED judgments (red-team-skeptic, verbatim)

No gene at this locus was marked CONTESTED (i.e., no directly contradicting literature was
identified) — but the red-team-skeptic explicitly notes this reflects incomplete
independent search coverage (its own WebSearch budget was exhausted before any
contradiction-search queries could be run), not a confirmed absence of contradicting
evidence. Several genes were marked WEAKENED:

### MAN1A2 — WEAKENED

"Real proximity/LD anchoring and real pleiotropy, but the 'developmental ciliation
biology' and 'smoking-mediated' narratives both overreach what's confirmed (knockout-vs-
hypomorph mismatch; database-only smoking claim with no primary-literature confirmation),
and the absence of FEV1/FEV1-FVC and COPD-specific genetic-association evidence is not
adequately foregrounded as a concern."

Specific sub-points raised:
- "Smoking-mediation hypothesis is asserted from a structured database, not literature ...
  at present the 'MAN1A2 acts via smoking-behavior neurobiology' framing is an inference
  drawn from an aggregator score with no traceable primary-literature backing. It should be
  reported as a plausible, testable hypothesis ... not as an established mediation
  pathway."
- "Ningappa mouse-ciliation study is a homozygous-null developmental/neonatal phenotype,
  not adult COPD biology ... MAN1A2 has oe=0.324 (moderately LoF-constrained), and the
  credible-set variants here are all intronic/UTR MODIFIER-impact — i.e., regulatory/
  hypomorphic at most, not null alleles. Extrapolating from a complete knockout's lethal
  respiratory-failure phenotype to the effect of common noncoding regulatory variants is a
  substantial leap in effect size and developmental timing that should not be allowed to
  read as mechanistic support for adult airflow obstruction."
- "No FEV1/FEV1-FVC evidence at all — a genuine warning sign under Prior 2 ... compounded
  by zero COPD genetic_association/L2G rows in Open Targets despite this gene sitting
  directly under a genome-wide-significant COPD lead variant."
- "Alternative hypothesis not addressed: MAN1A2 as a generic pleiotropic eQTL/pQTL hub
  rather than a COPD-causal gene ... consistent with it being a 'promiscuous' local-eQTL
  gene picked up wherever a strong cis-eQTL exists, independent of tissue-specific disease
  mechanism."

### PTGFRN — WEAKENED

"Bordering on 'should not be retained as a candidate gene for this locus at all,' since it
fails the most basic test (physical/credible-set overlap) before any biological evidence is
even considered."

Specific sub-points raised:
- "Physically outside the locus and credible set (360-473kb away), supported only by PoPS
  ... PoPS is a genome-wide network/pathway-similarity method explicitly designed to
  nominate genes without regard to physical proximity to the credible set — it is the
  converse failure mode of 'nearest-gene bias': here a statistical-similarity method is
  pulling in a gene with zero physical or fine-mapping linkage to this locus's actual
  credible-set variants."
- "The Colin 2011 citation using a Calu-6 lung-cancer xenograft is correctly caveated by
  the researcher as a tumor-angiogenesis model, not COPD-relevant ... but worth flagging
  that 'Calu-6' (a lung-derived cell line) could easily be misread downstream as 'lung
  evidence' if the caveat is dropped in later synthesis."

### LINC01525 — WEAKENED

"The only near-claim of relevance (Soremekun) was fabricated/mismatched and correctly
stripped; what remains is essentially no evidence. Flag explicitly that this gene's
'evidence base' was one hallucinated citation away from having zero substantive content."

### VPS25P1 — HOLDS (as non-candidate)

"No claim is being overreached here because essentially no claim is being made — the
researcher's implicit conclusion (this is not a serious candidate) is well supported by the
total absence of evidence rather than contradicted by it."

### TENT5C — HOLDS (as non-candidate)

"This is the cleanest example in the set of appropriately reporting a true negative without
overreach."

## Cross-cutting caveats raised by red-team-skeptic (verbatim, condensed)

1. "The single highest-value unresolved fact at this locus is whether MAN1A2 actually
   appears in the primary GSCAN/Saunders smoking-initiation GWAS. This was not confirmed
   and should be resolved before any smoking-mediation narrative is written into the final
   synthesis."
2. "No gene at this locus has FEV1/FEV1-FVC support. Under Prior 2, that is a locus-wide
   warning sign, not just a MAN1A2-specific one."
3. "PTGFRN's candidacy is a methodological artifact of PoPS reaching outside the physical
   locus window and should probably be down-weighted more aggressively than 'PoPS-
   prioritized' alone conveys."
4. "No gene reaches CONTESTED status on the evidence in hand, but this reflects incomplete
   search coverage (mine included) rather than a confirmed absence of contradicting
   literature — flag this as an open item rather than a resolved one." (Red-team-skeptic's
   own WebSearch budget was exhausted before any independent contradiction searches could
   be run for any gene at this locus.)
5. "LINC01525's fabricated citation is worth a second look by whoever curates the evidence
   pool for this locus, as a signal that thin-evidence genes in this pipeline are
   vulnerable to spurious link construction."
