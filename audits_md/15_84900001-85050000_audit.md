# Audit trail (excluded evidence / contested calls) — Locus 15:84,900,001-85,050,000

This appendix records every citation excluded from the evidence table
(`evidence_tables/15_84900001-85050000.md`) for verdict reasons (RETRACTED /
NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH), every gene examined but not carried into the evidence
table, and the red-team-skeptic's reasoning verbatim, for full traceability.

## Excluded citations (CLAIM_MISMATCH)

Both excluded citations concern **SEC11A**, a gene lying entirely outside the
84,900,001-85,050,000 locus window (84,669,529-84,716,466, GRCh38) and not overlapped
by any credible-set variant. The researcher flagged SEC11A's lung relevance as
"speculative extrapolation" from an oncology mechanism (TGF-α/EGFR secretion via
SPC18), not evidence-based.

1. **Oue N, Naito Y, Hayashi T, et al. "Signal peptidase complex 18, encoded by
   SEC11A, contributes to progression via TGF-α secretion in gastric cancer."**
   Oncogene. 2013;33(30):3918-26. PMID 23995782, DOI 10.1038/onc.2013.364.
   - Verdict: **CLAIM_MISMATCH**
   - Reason (citation-verifier): The paper's abstract confirms SPC18/SEC11A promotes
     TGF-α secretion in gastric cancer cells, but the researcher's claim additionally
     asserted this occurs via "EGFR-pathway-driven cell proliferation" — the
     abstract/results reviewed do not explicitly demonstrate that the proliferative
     effect is mediated through the EGFR pathway specifically, only that TGF-α
     secretion and cell growth are affected.

2. **Shigematsu Y, Oue N, et al. "SEC11A Expression Is Associated with Basal-Like
   Bladder Cancer and Predicts Patient Survival."** Pathobiology. 2019;86(4):208-216.
   PMID 31163419, DOI 10.1159/000497206.
   - Verdict: **CLAIM_MISMATCH**
   - Reason (citation-verifier): The paper confirms SPC18/SEC11A upregulation in
     basal-like bladder cancer and its role as an independent prognostic classifier,
     but the researcher's claim that this operates "via [the] same TGF-α/EGFR
     mechanism" was not demonstrated in this paper's results section — the
     TGF-α/EGFR link is mentioned only as background from other studies, not shown
     here.

Note: the other two SEC11A citations (Yamamoto et al. 2020, PMID 32564026; Kim SW
2022, PMID 35953114) were VERIFIED, but SEC11A as a whole was still not carried into
the evidence table because it lies outside the locus window, is not overlapped by any
credible-set variant, and has zero lung/COPD-specific literature — see "Genes examined
and excluded" below. Its citation set showing a 50% CLAIM_MISMATCH rate is itself
flagged by the red-team-skeptic as a pattern worth noting: "It suggests a
mechanism-first, evidence-second framing was applied here... which the researcher
appropriately caught and labeled 'speculative extrapolation.'"

## Genes examined and excluded from the evidence table

None of the genes below were marked CONTESTED by the red-team-skeptic (see "CONTESTED
determination" below for why), but none were carried into the main evidence table
either, for the reasons given.

### ALPK3 (84,817,176-84,875,708, GRCh38 — outside the locus window, no credible-set overlap)
Established cardiac sarcomere-assembly kinase; biallelic/monoallelic variants cause
pediatric and adult hypertrophic/dilated cardiomyopathy. No lung, COPD, or immune
literature found or biologically expected. Red-team-skeptic: "Correctly deprioritized
— cardiac sarcomere and cerebellar-ataxia genes respectively, no lung/immune biology,
no positional support. HOLDS."

### ZNF592 (84,748,348-84,806,445, GRCh38 — outside the locus window, no credible-set overlap)
Zinc-finger transcription factor; biallelic loss-of-function/missense variants cause
a recessive neurodevelopmental disorder with cerebellar atrophy (CAMOS). Citation
PMID 20531441 (Nicolas E et al., Eur J Hum Genet. 2010;18(10):1107-13, DOI
10.1038/ejhg.2010.82) — VERIFIED as describing exactly this. No lung/COPD/immune
relevance found. Red-team-skeptic: "HOLDS" on exclusion.

### SEC11A (84,669,529-84,716,466, GRCh38 — outside the locus window, no credible-set overlap)
Encodes SPC18, an ER signal-peptidase-complex subunit promoting TGF-α/EGFR-ligand
secretion; all four citations found were in oncology contexts (gastric, bladder,
esophageal cancer; parathyroid adenoma) with zero lung/COPD-specific literature. Two
of four citations failed verification as CLAIM_MISMATCH (see above). Red-team-skeptic:
"HOLDS on the deprioritization, but note the citation-quality pattern as a caution
about this researcher pass's tendency to reach for mechanism."

This limitation was not remediated before finalizing this locus (per pipeline
instructions, the orchestrator does not perform additional literature search itself);
it is preserved here as an explicit caveat for the study team.

## Red-team-skeptic's bottom-line assessment (verbatim)

> This locus does not have a well-supported candidate gene. Every gene examined lacks
> COPD, spirometric, TWAS, colocalization, or MR evidence in Open Targets or PubMed.
> SLC28A1 wins only by default (nearest gene, credible set now confirmed intronic
> rather than intergenic) with zero functional/disease evidence. PDE8A has an
> attractive mechanistic story but weaker physical proximity and zero genetic
> evidence. ALPK3/ZNF592/SEC11A are appropriately excluded. I'd recommend the
> synthesis explicitly state "no compelling causal gene identified at this locus"
> rather than naming SLC28A1 or PDE8A as a preferred candidate — manufacturing a
> front-runner here would overstate what the evidence supports. This also means the
> locus is a candidate for the "thoroughness" flag: before finalizing, someone should
> check whether this exact locus has appeared (under any gene label) in prior
> published COPD/spirometry/smoking GWAS papers' supplementary tables, since that
> check was not done by the researcher and could materially change the picture.
