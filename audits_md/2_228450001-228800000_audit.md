## Genes marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic

### CCL20 — CONTESTED (skeptic's reasoning, verbatim)

"This is the weakest of the four genes and I disagree with treating it as a serious
independent candidate at this locus.

- Never nominated by L2G across 18 GWAS studies (COPD or otherwise), and its aggregate OT
  COPD association score is driven almost entirely by literature/text-mining, not
  genetics. This is close to a textbook case of PoPS picking up a gene based on
  pathway/expression similarity to "inflammation genes" generally, untethered from the
  actual statistical fine-mapping at this specific locus (~820kb from the lead variant,
  well outside the credible set).
- The supporting literature itself undercuts a specific causal role: a UC/gut paper
  (Labbé 2011), a review mostly about IBD/psoriasis/RA (Xu 2026), a mouse IL-17A model
  where CCL20 rises alongside IL-6 (Ding 2025), a rat CO-induced (not genetic) emphysema
  model with CCL20 as one of several co-expressed cytokines (Chen 2025), and — most
  tellingly — Tasena et al. 2026, where the verifier explicitly notes IL-6, not CCL20, was
  the mechanistically proven driver in that same paper. That last point is a direct
  internal contradiction: the citation being used to support CCL20 is a paper whose own
  data point away from CCL20 specifically.
- Li et al. 2024 (review) states CCL20-CCR6 shows 'heightened expression' in COPD and
  proposes blockade as a therapeutic target — this is correlative/aspirational language
  from a review, not a causal or genetic claim, and should not be strengthened into 'CCL20
  drives COPD inflammation' in any synthesis. Reviews citing other reviews on a common
  'inflammation gene' narrative risk circularity — worth flagging explicitly.

Verdict: CONTESTED — I did not find new contradicting citations myself (search budget
exhausted), but the evidence already assembled is internally contradictory (Tasena 2026
downgrades CCL20 relative to IL-6) and structurally nonspecific (never genetically
nominated at this locus, evidence is entirely animal-model or other-organ-system
literature, score driven by text-mining not genetics). The researcher's own
characterization — 'real but nonspecific/secondary to its primary disease associations
elsewhere' — supports downgrading rather than treating CCL20 as a live candidate gene at
this locus."

## Genes marked WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (not excluded, but flagged as thin evidence)

### PID1 — WEAKENED
"Real evidence exists — Kim et al. 2014 (PMID 24737086) is a direct human COPD
case-control genetic association for PID1, and notably it survived adjustment for smoking
intensity with no SNP×smoking interaction... However: it's a single candidate-gene study
in one population (Korean, 1000/1000), unreplicated... Sakornsakolpat 2019 and Shrine 2019
are cited but the researcher/verifier could not confirm this specific gene/locus pairing
appears in either paper's tables — these are landscape citations, not confirmatory
evidence... Fischer 2018 (liver/metabolic), Erdreich-Epstein 2013 and Xu 2017 (brain tumor
apoptosis, NFκB-responsive) are non-lung, non-immune mechanistic papers... No lung
eQTL/TWAS evidence at all, and PID1's L2G edge over SPHKAP in COPD-specific GWAS is thin
(0.47 vs 0.41)."

### SPHKAP — WEAKENED
"Symmetric problem to PID1: no dedicated lung/COPD literature at all — every mechanistic
citation (Lacaná 2002 fibroblasts, Kovanich 2010 heart/spleen, Means 2011 mitochondria) is
non-lung. The only lung-relevant evidence is the L2G edge in FEV1/FVC- and PEF-specific
GWAS... this rests on Open Targets L2G scores alone, with zero independent literature
substantiating a lung mechanism for SPHKAP itself. One gap worth flagging explicitly for
synthesis-lead: SPHKAP's known function is negative regulation of SPHK1 (sphingosine
kinase). Sphingolipid/ceramide signaling has an independently established literature in
emphysema/COPD pathogenesis... that the researcher does not appear to have searched for in
connection with SPHKAP specifically."

### LINC01807 — HOLDS (for the modest claim actually made)
"The researcher is appropriately cautious here: it explicitly labels 'likely non-causal
host locus' as inference/judgment rather than a literature-sourced claim, and flags that
Yan et al. 2026 (melatonin GWAS, Han Chinese) may not even be the same LD block, with GTEx
enrichment (neural/vascular/adrenal) pointing away from lung relevance anyway... This
should not be read as evidence FOR PID1 or SPHKAP, though — hosting a credible set is a
distance/LD fact, not a functional endorsement of either flanking gene."

## Process caveats

1. **Red-team-skeptic search budget exhaustion.** The skeptic's web-search tool reported
   its session search budget (200/200) was exhausted before any query returned results.
   Verbatim from the skeptic: "None of the four genes received any independent search from
   me this session — my critique below is based entirely on the researcher's provided
   findings, the Open Targets structured data, and my own reasoning about what that
   evidence does and doesn't support... synthesis-lead should treat my 'CONTESTED' calls
   below as reasoned from internal inconsistencies in the evidence already assembled, not
   as freshly verified by a new search." This means the critique is a reasoning-based
   audit of the researcher's assembled evidence, not an independently re-searched
   contradiction check. The skeptic explicitly recommended a follow-up search pass on: PID1
   replication in non-Korean cohorts, SPHKAP-sphingolipid-pathway COPD literature, and any
   paper naming an alternative causal gene at the 2q37/CCL20 region.

2. **Locus-window vs. candidate-gene distances.** Per the researcher's Ensembl REST lookup
   (structured lookup, not a literature claim), none of the three protein-coding candidate
   genes (PID1, SPHKAP, CCL20) physically falls inside the stated locus window
   (2:228,450,001-228,800,000); all are flanking genes at increasing distance from the lead
   variant (PID1 ~213kb, SPHKAP ~455kb, CCL20 ~820kb). The entire credible set sits inside
   the neighboring lncRNA LINC01807, which itself is prioritized by none of
   L2G/MAGMA/PoPS/ABC. This is presented in the main evidence table as context for why
   PID1's "nearest gene" status is a weaker signal than usual, and why no gene-specific
   coding/splice mechanism is expected at this locus.

3. **Background-only citations (PMID 30643251, 33082346, 38816585).** These three GSCAN/
   smoking-behavior GWAS/TWAS papers were verified as real, non-retracted publications, but
   were cited by the researcher only as negative/background search targets (i.e., PID1 and
   SPHKAP were searched for and NOT found named in any of them). They are included in the
   main evidence table for completeness and traceability but were not subjected to the
   red-team-skeptic's gene-level critique, since they make no claim about any candidate
   gene at this locus.
