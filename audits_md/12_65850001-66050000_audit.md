# Audit trail — Locus 12:65,850,001-66,050,000 (nearest gene HMGA2)

This file records everything excluded from the main evidence table
(`evidence_tables/12_65850001-66050000.md`), plus the full reasoning behind the
red-team-skeptic's CONTESTED verdict, preserved verbatim for traceability. This is the
audit/reasoning trail; it is intentionally kept separate from the evidence table so the
evidence table can be distributed on its own.

## Excluded citations

### 1. Sakornsakolpat P, Prokopenko D, Lamontagne M, et al. "Genetic landscape of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease identifies heterogeneous cell-type and phenotype associations." Nature Genetics. 2019. PMID: 30804561; DOI: 10.1038/s41588-018-0342-2.

**Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH — excluded from the main evidence table.**

Citation-verifier's full reasoning: "According to PubMed, the study correctly identifies
35,735 cases/222,076 controls and 82 genome-wide-significant COPD loci (DOI:
10.1038/s41588-018-0342-2). The full text confirms that 'None of the novel loci appeared
to be explained by cigarette smoking, and variant effect sizes in ever- and never-smokers
and including and excluding self-reported asthmatics were similar,' supporting the smoking
status claim. However, the full text does not mention HMGA2 or the 12q14.3 locus among the
identified loci, and supplementary tables (where the 82 loci are listed) are not
accessible in the full text retrieved. A targeted search for 'Sakornsakolpat COPD GWAS
HMGA2' returns zero results in PubMed. The researcher correctly flagged inability to
confirm HMGA2 from extracted text. The claim that HMGA2/12q14.3 is one of the 82 identified
loci cannot be verified from the paper's available content."

A dedicated follow-up research pass (after red-team critique) re-examined this paper
specifically and additionally confirmed: the primary COPD case-control logistic regression
model was adjusted for age, sex, genotyping array, smoking pack-years, ever-smoking status,
and principal components — NOT height (height entered only implicitly via GOLD/FEV1%-
predicted case ascertainment, and explicitly only in the paper's separate SpiroMeta
height-adjusted replication step). Whether 12q14.3/HMGA2 is among the paper's 82
genome-wide-significant loci remains formally unconfirmed either way — this is an open
item that would require direct inspection of the paper's supplementary locus tables (not
accessible via the full-text extraction tools used in this review).

**Why excluded rather than included with a caveat:** the specific, gene-identifying claim
("HMGA2 is one of this GWAS's 82 hits") is the one at issue, and it could not be verified.
The parts of the paper that WERE verified (smoking-stratification methodology, overall
study design) are not gene-specific evidence for HMGA2 and would not belong in a
gene-level evidence table regardless of verification status. This citation is therefore
omitted from the table entirely rather than partially included.

## CONTESTED gene: HMGA2

Preserved verbatim from the red-team-skeptic's full critique (also on file at
`evidence_tables/_status/12_65850001-66050000/critique.md`):

> **Verdict: CONTESTED.**
>
> Not because a competing *gene* was found at this locus (it's a gene desert; HMGA2 is the
> only plausible candidate) but because a credible, evidence-backed **competing causal
> hypothesis** was found and not ruled out: that the "HMGA2-COPD" signal substantially
> reflects height/anthropometric pleiotropy rather than direct pulmonary or COPD-relevant
> biology. Supporting this: the flagship COPD-GWAS citation for this locus could not be
> verified as actually reporting HMGA2 as a hit (CLAIM_MISMATCH); every credible-set
> variant's strongest and most consistent GWAS Catalog signal is anthropometric; the
> credible set contains a variant (rs1042725) that is independently one of the
> best-replicated height variants in the genome; no direct height-mediation test exists;
> and all non-statistical (immune, structural/developmental) evidence found is off-tissue
> or off-disease relative to COPD/emphysema. This is not a new external citation I'm
> asserting (my attempt to independently search failed due to web-search budget
> exhaustion), so nothing here needs to be sent back to the citation-verifier as
> UNVERIFIED — the contest is an inference-level one built entirely from the researcher's
> own already-verified evidence, not a new source. I recommend synthesis-lead (a) treat
> HMGA2 at this locus as height-confounding-contested rather than a clean COPD/pulmonary-
> biology hit, and (b) request a targeted follow-up search of the primary quantitative
> lung-function GWAS literature (Shrine et al. lineage, height-adjusted vs. unadjusted
> models) before finalizing, since that check was not completed and is the most direct
> available resolution of the open question.

**Orchestrator follow-up action taken in response to this critique:** a targeted follow-up
researcher pass was dispatched specifically to check the Shrine et al. lineage
quantitative lung-function GWAS literature, per the skeptic's recommendation (b) above.
That follow-up (full text in `evidence_tables/_status/12_65850001-66050000/researcher.md`,
section "6. Follow-up: height-adjusted lung-function GWAS check") found, and
citation-verifier confirmed with high confidence, that HMGA2/12q14.3 does NOT appear among
the 279 genome-wide-significant signals reported by Shrine et al. 2019 (PMID 30804560) — a
large (n=400,102), height-adjusted lung-function GWAS. This finding is now included
affirmatively in the main evidence table as verified negative evidence, and it further
supports rather than resolves the skeptic's CONTESTED verdict: it does not rescue HMGA2 as
a directly-replicated pulmonary-function locus, and if anything strengthens the case that
this locus's association profile is predominantly anthropometric/cardiometabolic rather
than pulmonary.

**Additional caveat preserved from the critique, for balance:** the skeptic also noted a
genuine, unproven alternative in the other direction: "HMGA2's biology is fundamentally
about growth-plate/allometric scaling (chromatin architecture, TSS remodeling per Dobersch
et al. 2021). It is conceivable this acts on lung developmental growth (e.g.,
airway-vs-parenchyma dysanapsis) rather than on stature as a pure statistical confounder —
a genuine lung-developmental mechanism sharing the same growth pathway as height, not
merely a height-driven statistical artifact. This is speculative and untested." This
possibility is not evidenced by any specific citation in this review and should not be
read as resolving the CONTESTED status — it is recorded here only so the height-pleiotropy
interpretation is not treated as proven either.

**Presentation critique also noted (not a citation or gene-verdict issue):** the skeptic
flagged that the researcher's original write-up listed the Sakornsakolpat 2019 citation as
the first piece of statistical-genetics evidence, framed as "the major prior COPD
case-control GWAS... cited as background," with its CLAIM_MISMATCH caveat only appearing
afterward — creating a risk that a reader skimming top-to-bottom could momentarily read it
as establishing HMGA2 as a confirmed COPD hit before reaching the correction. The
underlying citation handling was accurate; this was a presentation/ordering concern, now
addressed in the final evidence table by omitting this citation from the table entirely
(see exclusion above) rather than including it with a trailing caveat.
