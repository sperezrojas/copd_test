## Genes marked CONTESTED / WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning)

### PRMT6 — verdict: WEAKENED (bordering CONTESTED)

> The genetics side is essentially null, and this is being papered over by
> mechanistic narrative. There is no COPD, FEV1, or FEV1/FVC GWAS/TWAS/coloc
> evidence for PRMT6 anywhere (PubMed, preprints, or Open Targets: PRMT6-FEV1 and
> PRMT6-FEV1/FVC = 0). Open Targets has zero credible sets for this entire genomic
> region, meaning L2G/colocalization — the standard tools for adjudicating which
> gene a non-coding credible set acts through — simply cannot be applied here. The
> entire 13-variant credible set is non-coding and does not touch any gene body;
> PRMT6 is implicated purely by proximity (~6kb) plus researcher-supplied
> mechanistic literature. This is the textbook "nearest-gene bias" scenario the
> review framework warns about, except there isn't even a second nearby candidate
> with comparable proximity to triangulate against (NTNG1 is 90kb away, clearly
> farther). The abundant PRMT6 eQTL/tuQTL signal in GTEx is uninterpretable as
> locus-specific support without a disease-side credible set to colocalize against —
> citing "abundant eQTL signal" as if it strengthens the case is a subtle overclaim;
> it only shows PRMT6 expression is regulated by nearby variants somewhere, not that
> this specific COPD credible set acts through it. Also worth flagging: the lead
> variant's own PIP is only 0.27 in a 13-variant set — this is a poorly-resolved
> fine-mapping signal even before asking which gene it points to, and Open Targets
> showing literally zero credible sets for this region at all is unusual enough to
> warrant an independent sanity check that this locus reflects a genuine, replicated
> genome-wide-significant signal rather than a single-study/single-biobank artifact.

> Smoking-mediation (Prior #1). The one piece of statistical-genetics evidence
> connecting PRMT6 to smoking (Rosoff 2024 TWAS/MR, PMID 38888899) actually cuts
> against a direct-pulmonary mechanism and toward smoking-neurobiology, per Prior
> #1. But there's a further problem: a TWAS association from a lifetime-smoking-index
> instrument reflects predicted expression correlated with smoking behavior — it does
> not establish that the same causal variants/LD block are involved as the COPD
> credible set at chr1:106.97-107.05Mb. No smoking-initiation/cigarettes-per-day GWAS
> lists this locus as genome-wide significant. So the "PRMT6 is smoking-related"
> claim and the "PRMT6 is the COPD-locus gene" claim may be two independent,
> non-corroborating pieces of evidence that happen to share a gene symbol, not a
> coherent smoking-mediated-COPD story. This should not be read as confirming a
> smoking-mediated mechanism for this locus.

> Structural/mechanistic literature — real overreach risk. Five papers from one lab
> (Xiangya Hospital/Central South University, overlapping authors across a decade)
> is a single non-independent line of evidence, correctly flagged as such by the
> researcher — treat it as n=1 research program, not 5 replications. More
> importantly, this is directly contradicted on direction of effect by an
> independent, more recent group (Zhang et al. 2026, PMID 42592683): CSE increases
> PRMT6 and drives PI3K/Akt/mTOR-mediated EMT/invasion in an NSCLC model, the
> opposite of the Xiangya group's "CSE decreases PRMT6, PRMT6 is protective"
> narrative. The NSCLC/COPD context difference tempers but does not eliminate this
> concern — it demonstrates PRMT6's response to cigarette smoke is cell-type/
> context-dependent, which undermines confidence in a single fixed directional
> mechanism being extrapolated to human COPD genetics. Given one lab's serial output
> is the "strongest evidence for this gene" per the researcher's own framing, and
> that evidence's core direction-of-effect claim has a documented counter-example,
> this does not constitute strong biological plausibility support so much as a
> plausible-but-unresolved hypothesis.

> Immunology (Prior #3 — cell-type/context specificity). This is the weakest and
> most scattered part of the case. The cited immune biology spans heterotopic
> ossification, neuropathic pain microglia, alcohol-associated liver fibrosis,
> gastric cancer, chronic HIV CD4+ T cells, and COVID interferon signaling —
> essentially no COPD-relevant immune cell type (airway neutrophils, lung
> macrophages, Th1/Th17/Tc17) is examined in a COPD context. The gastric-cancer
> finding that PRMT6 suppresses CD8+ T-cell activity is directionally opposite to
> what COPD's CD8+-T-cell-driven pathology would predict if the same mechanism
> generalized — correctly flagged by the researcher as "mixed/contested." Treating
> this grab-bag of pleiotropic, tissue-mismatched immune findings as generically
> supportive of a COPD immune mechanism would be an overclaim; synthesis should not
> upgrade this to "PRMT6 has immune-relevant biology in COPD" — it has
> immune-relevant biology in numerous unrelated tissues, with no COPD-specific
> immune-cell data at all.

> Bottom line on overclaiming: the single biggest risk is translating "PRMT6 is
> protective against emphysema in mice (one lab's model)" into "PRMT6 is the causal
> COPD GWAS gene at this locus." The GWAS side of this locus provides literally zero
> direct support (no COPD, FEV1, or FEV1/FVC signal for PRMT6 in Open Targets; the
> credible set is non-coding and non-genic; PIP is low; fine-mapping resolution is
> poor). The mechanistic case is real but single-group, directionally contested by an
> independent paper, and the immune evidence is non-specific pleiotropy from
> unrelated tissues. This gene should be presented as a biologically plausible,
> statistically unsupported candidate — not as a confirmed COPD gene with
> corroborating mechanism. WEAKENED, with the overreach specifically being: treating
> single-lab mechanistic plausibility as if it substitutes for the complete absence
> of statistical genetic support tying the locus to this gene.

### NTNG1 — verdict: HOLDS (as an appropriately deprioritized weak candidate; not contested)

> The researcher's characterization here is honest and not overclaimed — "weak
> secondary candidate" is exactly what this evidence supports. Positional distance
> (90kb vs. PRMT6's 6kb) already argues against it. The RLS GWAS hit (Schormair
> 2017, PMID 29029846) is a neurological trait with no lung/COPD/smoking relevance —
> a different locus function entirely (synapse formation), and the researcher
> didn't try to stretch it into pulmonary biology. The SCLC MR finding (Wu et al.
> 2023, PMID 37745050) is lung-cancer risk, not COPD, and a cancer-risk MR
> association (plasma protein -> cancer risk) is mechanistically orthogonal to
> airway obstruction/emphysema/chronic inflammation — correctly labeled
> "mechanistically orthogonal" by the researcher rather than stretched into a COPD
> story. No structural-lung or immune-inflammation literature exists for NTNG1 at
> all. No overreach to flag here — this gene is correctly treated as essentially
> unsupported and should not receive further weight in synthesis beyond
> "positionally present, no direct evidence either way, deprioritized versus PRMT6
> on proximity grounds alone."

## Process transparency: red-team-skeptic search-budget exhaustion

The red-team-skeptic agent dispatched for this locus reported that its web-search
tool budget (200/200) was already exhausted before it could make any WebSearch calls.
**It performed zero independent contradiction searches for this locus** — its
critique above is based entirely on statistical/study-design reasoning applied to the
researcher's own already-reported findings (which happen to contain several internal
contradictions: the opposite-direction NSCLC paper, the opposite-direction
gastric-cancer CD8+ T-cell paper, the HIV CD4+ T-cell downregulation paper), not on
new literature it independently found. It reported finding no new citations to route
to citation-verifier (consistent with having done no searches).

The skeptic explicitly flagged that if search budget is renewed, priority follow-up
searches for this locus would be:
1. Independent replication or refutation of the Xiangya-group PRMT6-CSE-emphysema
   story from any other laboratory.
2. Constitutive or lung-specific Prmt6-knockout mouse phenotyping.
3. Whether rs72697623 or nearby SNPs appear in any independent COPD/spirometry GWAS
   (UK Biobank, SpiroMeta, FinnGen) outside whatever meta-analysis produced this
   credible set, given Open Targets shows zero credible sets for this region
   entirely.

This is flagged here as a genuine coverage gap in the pipeline's contradiction-search
step for this locus, not a clean pass — a reviewer or a future re-run with restored
search budget should treat the WEAKENED verdict on PRMT6 as resting on sound
statistical reasoning from already-known facts, but not on an independent literature
search for additional contradicting evidence.

## Locus-level caveat carried into the evidence table

Both the researcher and red-team-skeptic independently converged on flagging this as
a statistically "orphan" locus: Open Targets has zero GWAS credible sets for this
genomic region under any trait, the lead variant rs72697623 (PIP 0.27) has no
independent GWAS Catalog, ClinVar, or PubMed record, and no COPD, FEV1, or FEV1/FVC
GWAS/TWAS/colocalization evidence exists anywhere for PRMT6 or NTNG1. All
disease-relevant reasoning at this locus is necessarily derived from PRMT6's
mechanistic/functional literature (itself dominated by a single non-independent
research group) rather than from convergent statistical genetics, and this should be
weighed heavily by the study team in deciding how much further investment this locus
merits relative to loci with direct genetic (L2G/colocalization/fine-mapping)
support.
