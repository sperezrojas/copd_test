# Audit trail — locus 2:61850001-62250000 (nearest gene COMMD1)

This is the audit/exclusion appendix. It is intentionally separate from
`evidence_tables/2_61850001-62250000.md` so the evidence table can be shared without
also sharing this internal reasoning trail.

## Citation verification outcomes

**All 13 citations returned by the researcher were VERIFIED** by the citation-verifier
(exist, not retracted, claims accurately reflect the source). **No citations were
excluded** on RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH grounds — there is no exclusion
list for this locus on verification grounds.

One item was specifically flagged by the researcher as needing confirmation and was
resolved by the citation-verifier:
- Brown et al 2025 (Research Square preprint, PMID 40709250) — the researcher could not
  confirm from the abstract alone whether the chr2p15 gene named in this AS GWAS
  meta-analysis was B3GNT2 specifically (as opposed to COMMD1 or another 2p15 gene). The
  citation-verifier retrieved the full text (PMC12288525) and confirmed: "using 2SMR, we
  found evidence to support B3GNT2 ... as an AS-causative gene at chromosome 2p15" —
  B3GNT2-specific, not COMMD1. This citation remains an unpublished, not-yet-peer-reviewed
  preprint (dated 2025-07-14); weighted accordingly (preprint-strength, not
  journal-peer-reviewed strength) in the evidence table's skeptic verdict.

## Genes considered and scoped out before the evidence table

  decision was not separately contested by the red-team-skeptic (skeptic's critique
  scope was limited to COMMD1/CCT4/B3GNT2 per the orchestrator's dispatch, which judged
  FAM161A's exclusion reasoning sound enough not to require re-litigation).

## Red-team-skeptic verdicts (verbatim reasoning preserved)

**Disclosed limitation (stated by the skeptic itself):** "My web-search budget was
already exhausted (200/200 used by prior turns in this session) before I could run any
independent verification searches for this locus. Every judgment below is therefore
based on critical analysis of the materials provided (researcher findings, citation-
verifier output, Open Targets summary) plus domain reasoning, not fresh literature
search." This applies to all three gene verdicts below — none of them reflect an
independent contradiction-search pass; they are critiques of the researcher's own
assembled evidence.

### COMMD1 — WEAKENED

No gene here has any direct FEV1/FEV1-FVC/COPD genetic signal anywhere in the literature
or in Open Targets. The skeptic's full reasoning:

> The strongest piece of evidence — rs55677935 missense, PIP 0.58 — is a
> positional/statistical argument, not a demonstrated functional one. None of the 7
> citations characterize this specific variant: no CADD/PolyPhen/AlphaMissense score, no
> stated protein domain, no experimental allele-specific assay. "Missense + high PIP" is
> being used as a proxy for functional relevance without direct support... CF/oncology
> transfer to COPD: a stretch, and a bigger stretch for oncology than for CF... The
> researcher's own catch — COMMD1 is down in emphysema (item 1) but up and
> prognostically bad in NSCLC (item 7) — is a real directional inconsistency that
> undercuts any unified "COMMD1 dose matters the same way everywhere" narrative and
> should be preserved, not softened, in synthesis... CRP colocalization: it is doing two
> different jobs and only one is legitimate... the "which gene" question and the "is
> this lung biology" question are being conflated. The CRP data answers the former; it
> does not answer, and arguably argues against, the latter... The one direct-tissue
> paper (item 1, Besiktepe 2017) is protein-correlational, cross-sectional,
> within-patient... and doesn't test the genetic variant at all — it can't distinguish
> COMMD1 loss as a cause of emphysema from COMMD1 loss as a downstream consequence of
> tissue hypoxia/destruction.

### CCT4 — HOLDS

> No COPD/lung literature, no credible-set variant (PoPS-only, network-similarity-based
> prioritization, which is known to be susceptible to false positives at large loci),
> constraint pattern consistent with general essentiality (core chaperonin subunit)
> rather than disease-specific dosage sensitivity, and the one functional citation is
> lung cancer tumor-immunology... a context with no clear bridge to COPD's airway
> inflammatory programs, correctly flagged as such by the researcher rather than
> stretched... The researcher's own conclusion — CCT4 is the weakest candidate with
> essentially no COPD-relevant support — is an accurate, non-overreaching read of thin
> evidence. Nothing here is being oversold.

### B3GNT2 — WEAKENED (not CONTESTED; reason differs from researcher's own framing)

> This is the most interesting case for the "same locus, different disease" question.
> The AS genetics is genuinely strong and multiply replicated... real biology, not
> noise. But three structural facts argue against treating it as a true competing causal
> hypothesis for THIS COPD credible set specifically: (1) B3GNT2 sits ~200kb from the
> lead variant, (2) it has zero assigned credible-set PIP, and (3) its case rests
> entirely on ABC (enhancer-promoter contact) prioritization, a method with a known
> non-trivial false-positive rate especially at this range. 200kb is generally outside a
> typical single LD block, so absent an explicit check that the AS-associated B3GNT2
> variants ... are in LD with rs55677935 or the other COMMD1 credible-set variants —
> which none of the provided materials establish — the more parsimonious read is that
> this is a distinct, independent GWAS signal in a gene-dense immune-relevant region of
> chr2p13-15, not the same causal variant re-emerging under a different disease
> label... AS and COPD are phenotypically and mechanistically quite different
> (HLA-B27-restricted, NK-cell/Th17 seronegative spondyloarthropathy vs. smoking-related
> airway/parenchymal disease with neutrophilic/CD8+/macrophage programs), and there's no
> established epidemiological or shared-inflammatory-subtype literature connecting
> them... This is not a CONTESTED call against COMMD1 (I don't have evidence the AS and
> COPD signals are the same variant, so it isn't head-to-head contradicting evidence) —
> it's a case where B3GNT2's own COPD-relevance claim is weaker than its write-up
> implies.

**No gene at this locus was marked CONTESTED by the red-team-skeptic.** All three
(COMMD1, CCT4, B3GNT2) received WEAKENED/HOLDS verdicts as documented above and in the
main evidence table; none were judged to carry directly contradicting head-to-head
evidence against another candidate at this specific locus.
