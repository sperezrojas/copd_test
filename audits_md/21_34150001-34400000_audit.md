## 2. Gene-level CONTESTED / WEAKENED reasoning (verbatim from red-team-skeptic)

### Search-coverage disclosure (read first)

My web-search budget was exhausted before I could run any queries (all four of my planned
contradiction-search queries — KCNE2 COPD, rs62213732 smoking, LINC00310 lung/COPD,
C21orf62 lung/COPD — returned "budget used" errors, zero results). I performed zero
independent searches for any of the three candidate genes. This is not "searched and found
nothing" — it is "not searched." Synthesis-lead should treat the smoking-mediation question
and the contradicting-evidence question for all three genes as still fully open, and should
request either a raised search budget or a fresh researcher/critique pass focused
specifically on (1) smoking-initiation/heaviness GWAS at this locus, and (2) any papers
arguing against KCNE2/LINC00310/C21orf62 involvement in COPD or pulmonary phenotypes. My
critique below is therefore based entirely on close re-reading of the verified findings
already supplied, not on new evidence.

### KCNE2 — WEAKENED

Soler Artigas et al. 2011 is genuine, credible spirometric evidence that this genomic
region affects FEV1/FEV1-FVC — that holds regardless of which gene is responsible. But the
leap from "a locus near KCNE2" to "KCNE2 is the mechanism" is not supported by
colocalization, TWAS, or any variant-level data — Open Targets itself gives this
gene-locus pairing a COPD association score of 0.013, and no credible-set variant with
meaningful PIP sits in KCNE2 (max 0.0076, all intronic, no missense/splice).

The Zhou et al. 2019 mouse knockout is the most substantive direct pulmonary-mechanism
evidence for KCNE2, but it is under-scrutinized for a major confound: this is a germline,
whole-body Kcne2-knockout, and Kcne2 loss is well established to cause cardiac phenotypes.
A systemic KO with impaired gas exchange, pulmonary vascular leakage, and elevated
inflammatory cytokines is entirely consistent with the lung phenotype being secondary to
cardiac/circulatory dysfunction (e.g. cardiogenic pulmonary edema/hypoxia) rather than
cell-intrinsic lung KCNE2 biology. The researcher already correctly downweighted the
inflammatory findings (TNF-a, IL-6, BAL leukocytes) as likely downstream-of-injury rather
than a distinct immune mechanism — the same logic should extend to "impaired gas exchange"
and "pulmonary apoptosis/vascular leak," which could equally be downstream of a cardiac
primary defect. This is not addressed in the findings as presented.

Compounding this: (a) the Adler et al. 2020 "Disputed" reappraisal of KCNE2 for LQTS — the
field's most rigorous curators don't consider the cardiac phenotype well-established, which
should raise the bar for pulmonary causal claims about the same gene too; and (b) the
Sabater-Lleal 2014 CAD/carotid-IMT association at the same locus independently supports a
vascular/cardiovascular pleiotropy explanation for this genomic region rather than
lung-intrinsic biology.

Overclaiming check: the individual sentence-level claims from Zhou et al. are accurate
restatements, not overstated. The overreach is at the inference layer: real spirometry +
real mouse pulmonary phenotype are being combined into an implied "KCNE2 -> lung function"
story without addressing the systemic-KO/secondary-cardiac confound, the cardiovascular
pleiotropy alternative, or the absence of any locus-specific colocalization anchoring the
mouse biology to the actual human GWAS signal at this position.

Verdict on weighting: No — the mouse knockout paper is not being appropriately weighted
against the disputed cardiac status and the near-zero Open Targets COPD score; both should
pull confidence down further, and the systemic-KO-confound is a new point raised here that
was absent from the original findings.

### LINC00310 — WEAKENED

Hosting the lead variant and >90% of credible-set PIP mass is the single strongest
statistical evidence at this locus for any gene, but this must not be conflated with
mechanistic support. Statistical fine-mapping tells you where the causal variant(s)
probably sit, not what gene they act through — an intronic/lncRNA-locus variant can readily
be a regulatory element for a neighboring gene (KCNE2 sits right there). The researcher
does flag this ambiguity explicitly, which should be preserved in synthesis, not smoothed
over.

On mechanism: what exists is genuinely thin and non-specific to COPD — breast cancer
proliferation (Li 2018) and cardiomyopathy ceRNA bioinformatics (Chen 2022) are different
tissues/diseases entirely. Ma et al. 2020 is in airway epithelial cells but the endpoint is
occupational-carcinogen-induced malignant transformation, not inflammation, remodeling, or
any COPD-relevant phenotype — this would be oversold if framed as "airway-relevant" without
that caveat. Willis-Owen 2018 is the only direct LINC00310+COPD co-occurrence, but it is
peripheral quadriceps muscle, not lung, one of 18 differentially-expressed transcripts in a
broad list, and purely observational with no mechanism — a COPD-comorbidity/systemic
muscle-wasting signal at best, not lung-disease evidence.

New point: Turley 2020 and Saw 2020 (SCAD GWAS) plus Sabater-Lleal 2014 (CAD/cIMT, filed
under KCNE2) together constitute three independent vascular/arteriopathy GWAS hits at the
same 21q22.11 cytogenetic band. This is a real pattern, not noise, and should be weighted
as a serious alternative hypothesis: this locus may be fundamentally a vascular-disease
locus whose COPD "signal" reflects LD-tagging of a distinct vascular mechanism, pleiotropy
through pulmonary vascular pathology (mechanistically distinct from airway obstruction/
inflammation), or GWAS COPD-subtype misclassification. This convergent vascular signal is
at least as compelling an alternative explanation as smoking-mediation and was not
explicitly weighed in the original findings.

Verdict on conflation: "Hosts the lead variant" and "has mechanistic support" should be
clearly separated in synthesis: statistical/positional primacy is strong; functional/
mechanistic support for a COPD-relevant role is essentially absent.

### C21orf62 / EPCIP — CONTESTED

Elevated beyond WEAKENED to CONTESTED. The Open Targets QTL footprint for this gene
clusters ~1-1.5 Mb outside the actual credible-set window (32.5-33.5 Mb vs. this locus's
34.15-34.40 Mb) — a direct, quantitative argument against C21orf62 being the causal gene
for this fine-mapped signal. PoPS is a genome-wide similarity-based prioritization method
that does not require or demonstrate positional/regulatory overlap with the credible set,
so its nomination here is decoupled from the actual statistical fine-mapping evidence at
this locus — functionally equivalent to a contrary finding for this specific locus, even
though no paper explicitly "argues against" C21orf62.

Compounding this: zero functional validation exists anywhere in the literature for
C21orf62 itself — in both recent papers where it appears (chicken infection transcriptome,
glioma prognostic signature), the authors validated other genes in their signatures,
leaving C21orf62 as an unvalidated passenger correlate in both. The one paper with real
functional characterization (Lea 2023) is renal ciliary/ADPKD biology, unrelated to lung.
gnomAD LoF-tolerance (oe ~1.06) further argues against a haploinsufficiency mechanism,
though this doesn't fully rule out a regulatory/expression-modulating mechanism.

Verdict: C21orf62/EPCIP should be effectively excluded as a candidate at this specific
locus. The combination of (i) QTL footprint outside the credible-set window, (ii) zero
direct functional validation in any paper, (iii) only unrelated-tissue biology (renal
cilia) where validation does exist, and (iv) LoF-tolerant constraint metrics is close to a
disqualifying evidence pattern. Recommend demoting to background/exclude rather than
listing alongside KCNE2/LINC00310 as a live candidate.

### Cross-cutting points for synthesis

- Smoking-mediation: genuinely unresolved — flagged as a gap by the researcher (WebSearch
  failure) and independently re-flagged by the skeptic (own searches also failed, for an
  unrelated budget reason). Two independent search attempts have now failed to produce an
  answer either way; this is an explicit open question, not a checked negative.
- Vascular pleiotropy as an alternative to both smoking-mediation and lung-intrinsic
  biology: three independent GWAS signals (CAD/cIMT, SCAD x2) at the same cytogenetic
  band, plus the KCNE2 mouse phenotype's plausible cardiac-secondary confound, make "this
  locus is fundamentally cardiovascular/vascular, with COPD association reflecting
  pleiotropy or a pulmonary-vascular subphenotype rather than airway biology" a hypothesis
  at least as strong as smoking-mediation.
- Thoroughness flag: Open Targets shows no COPD-specific L2G/colocalization for any of the
  three genes at this locus; the one COPD GWAS Open Targets has doesn't contribute a
  credible set overlapping this window; and the skeptic's own contradiction-search pass
  could not be executed at all. Recommend treating this locus's overall COPD evidence as
  weak/thin across all three candidates, and recommend a follow-up research pass
  specifically targeting smoking-behavior GWAS overlap and contradicting evidence if this
  locus is advanced for further work.
- No new citations were introduced by the red-team-skeptic (no searches were executable),
  so no additional citation-verifier pass was required before final synthesis.
