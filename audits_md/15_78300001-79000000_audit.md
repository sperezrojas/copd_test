One citation carries a gene-identity caveat and is excluded from use as evidence for the
gene it might naively be assumed to support:

- **Plantier L et al. "Dysregulation of elastin expression by fibroblasts in pulmonary
  emphysema: role of cellular retinoic acid binding protein 2." Thorax. 2008. PMID
  18621984.** VERIFIED to exist and VERIFIED to accurately report its actual finding: this
  paper is about **CRABP2** (a paralog gene on a different chromosome), not **CRABP1** (the
  gene that lies within this chr15 locus, at 15:78,340,260-78,348,250). The researcher
  explicitly flagged this risk of confusion when reporting it; citation-verifier
  independently confirmed the paper's abstract discusses CRABP2 specifically and states
  CRABP1 mRNA was expressed similarly in control and emphysema fibroblasts (i.e., CRABP1
  itself showed no differential finding in this paper). This citation is therefore excluded
  from the CRABP1 evidence base and is not usable as support for CRABP1 at this locus.

## Genes marked CONTESTED or WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning preserved)

**CHRNA5 — WEAKENED** (skeptic verdict, not CONTESTED, but with substantial caveats):
"The statistical case is real but the researcher's synthesis undersells how much it depends
on smoking-related traits versus lung-specific biology, and overstates gene-level
specificity. Three problems: (i) the locus is a near-perfect LD block spanning
CHRNA5/CHRNA3/CHRNB4/PSMA4/HYKK — the 231 credible sets hitting this one variant across
essentially every smoking-adjacent phenotype (plus 'Dentures') is itself evidence the
variant's effect is generic/behavioral, not CHRNA5-specific; (ii) modern L2G disagrees on

and the most contemporary multi-ancestry WGS study (Kim 2026) does NOT colocalize CHRNA5 at

researcher records but doesn't foreground in the summary judgment; (iii) the
developmental-biology evidence (Porter/Reynolds) shows expression and regulation, not a
causal or smoking-independent functional role in emphysema/remodeling."

**ACSBG1 — WEAKENED:** "Near-null genetic association, sits outside the strict locus

**CHRNA3 — WEAKENED:** "Statistically indistinguishable from CHRNA5 (same LD block,

**CHRNB4 — CONTESTED:** "The researcher's own findings show direction-of-effect

### Critique of "smoking-independent" framing (CHRNA5 and IREB2)

Both framings are overclaimed relative to the evidence actually presented, in a specific
and correctable way: the researcher lumps interaction-only and subgroup findings in with
genuine "surviving adjustment" findings as if they all point the same direction.

- For CHRNA5: Li Q 2015 found no main effect, only a smoking-dose interaction — this is
  evidence FOR smoking-mediation, not independence, and citing it under a
  "smoking-independent" umbrella inverts its actual implication. Ganbold 2021 is explicitly
  a gene-environment synergy (interaction) model — again, evidence that the effect requires
  smoking exposure to manifest, not evidence of a smoking-independent lung mechanism. Only
  Saccone 2010 (lung cancer, not COPD directly, and only nominal COPD p=0.01) and Hansen
  2010 (lung cancer, not COPD) show effects surviving pack-year adjustment, and neither is a
  COPD/spirometry endpoint. So the actual "survives adjustment" evidence for COPD/lung-
  function specifically is thin.
- For IREB2: the "independent of smoking" claims (Zhou 2012 non-smoker subgroup,
  Nedeljkovic 2018 methylation) are each individually weak on close inspection (small
  subgroup p-value; residual-confounding-prone methylation covariate adjustment) rather
  than robust demonstrations of a smoking-independent pathway.

**Recommendation:** treat interaction/subgroup findings as evidence bearing on whether
smoking mediation is occurring (they support it) rather than as support for
smoking-independent lung biology, and discount methylation-based "independent of smoking"
claims given known EWAS confounding limitations.

### Additional confounds/alternative explanations not raised by the researcher

- Gene-level attribution instability at this exact locus is itself the strongest available
  evidence, and it argues against confident single-gene prioritization: top-L2G gene
  changes across GWAS samples (HYKK vs. CHRNA5 vs. CHRNA3) and across eQTL studies (PSMA4 in
  Kim 2026 vs. IREB2/CHRNA3/PSMA4 convergence in Nedeljkovic vs. CHRNA5 in "most" credible
  sets per Open Targets) is a coherent pattern, not noise.
- The "Dentures" phenotype hit (a purely behavioral/socioeconomic trait sharing this exact
  variant) is a useful sanity check: it strongly suggests the locus's broad phenome-wide
  footprint is substantially explained by smoking (and correlated behaviors) as a shared
  upstream cause, a parsimony argument favoring smoking-mediation as the default null
  hypothesis at this locus specifically.
- Residual confounding by imperfectly measured smoking covariates: "survives pack-year
  adjustment" claims should not be read as ruling out smoking mediation, since self-reported
  pack-years is a coarse, error-prone proxy for the full causal pathway that this exact
  nicotinic-receptor cluster plausibly influences.
- Population/exposure heterogeneity: findings scattered across Chinese Han, Mongolian,
  African-American, and Hainan (areca-nut-exposed) populations — different ancestries, LD
  patterns, and in the Hainan case a distinct behavioral confound. Aggregating these as
  generically supportive of a single gene's mechanism risks masking real population-specific
  heterogeneity.

## Orchestrator's note on scope decision

The locus input file only lists CHRNA5 (nearest gene) and ACSBG1 (MAGMA/PoPS) as candidate
genes. The orchestrator extended the researcher's brief to also cover CHRNA3, CHRNB4,
IREB2, PSMA4, and HYKK given this locus's well-documented multi-gene LD-block structure and
the fact that Open Targets' own L2G data (a structured lookup, not a literature claim)
showed the "top gene" at the lead variant flipping between CHRNA5, CHRNA3, HYKK, and PSMA4
depending on which GWAS sample was queried. This was judged necessary to give a fair,
non-arbitrary answer to the smoking-mediation-vs-direct-biology question this locus was
flagged for — evaluating CHRNA5 in isolation, without its LD neighbors, would have made it
impossible to tell whether any COPD signal was gene-specific or merely locus-wide.
