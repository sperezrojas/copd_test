# Audit trail: locus 16:87,300,001-87,500,000 (nearest gene ZCCHC14)

This file contains everything excluded from the main evidence table
(`evidence_tables/16_87300001-87500000.md`), plus the full skeptic reasoning, so a
reviewer can see what was found and discarded and why.

## Excluded citations

### RETRACTED

- **Zhu L et al. "Regulation of Cigarette Smoke (CS)-Induced Autophagy by Nrf2." PLoS
  One. 2013;8(4):e55695. PMID 23585825. DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0055695.**
  Claimed: cigarette-smoke extract increases LC3B-I/II and autophagosome formation in
  airway epithelial cells via an Nrf2-p62-dependent pathway (MAP1LC3B, structural
  domain). Citation-verifier confirmed this article is marked "Retracted Publication"
  in PubMed's article_types field. Excluded entirely from the evidence table; the
  red-team-skeptic separately confirmed this exclusion was correctly honored and not
  smuggled back into the researcher's summary judgment for MAP1LC3B.

### CLAIM_MISMATCH

- **Jorgenson E et al. "Genetic contributors to variation in alcohol consumption vary
  by race/ethnicity in a large multi-ethnic genome-wide association study." Mol
  Psychiatry. 2017;22(9):1359-1367. PMID 28485404. DOI 10.1038/mp.2017.101.**
  Claimed: reports ZCCHC14 as an alcohol-consumption GWAS locus. Citation-verifier
  confirmed the paper is a genuine multi-ethnic GERA alcohol-consumption GWAS
  reporting novel loci AUTS2, SGOL1, SERPINC1, but found no mention of ZCCHC14 or
  rs1050847/rs1050846 in the abstract. Verifier checked abstract-level text only, not
  full supplementary gene/locus tables — this residual uncertainty was not resolved in
  either direction and should be treated as excluded pending a full-text/supplement
  check, not as a confirmed negative.

- **Kranzler HR et al. "Genome-wide association study of alcohol consumption and use
  disorder in 274,424 individuals from multiple populations." Nat Commun.
  2019;10:1499. PMID 30940813. DOI 10.1038/s41467-019-09480-8.**
  Claimed: reports ZCCHC14 as an AUDIT-C/AUD GWAS locus. Citation-verifier confirmed the
  paper is a genuine 274,424-person MVP GWAS identifying 18 genome-wide-significant
  loci, but found no mention of ZCCHC14 in the abstract. Same abstract-only-check
  caveat as above applies.

- **Zhou H et al. "Genome-wide meta-analysis of problematic alcohol use in 435,563
  individuals yields insights into biology and relationships with other traits." Nat
  Neurosci. 2020;23(7):809-818. PMID 32451486. DOI 10.1038/s41593-020-0643-5.**
  Claimed: reports ZCCHC14 as one of 66 genome-wide-significant genes via MAGMA.
  Citation-verifier confirmed the paper reports 29 independent PAU risk loci (19 novel)
  and 66 GWS genes via MAGMA, but found no mention of ZCCHC14 in the abstract. Same
  abstract-only-check caveat as above applies.

  **Downstream consequence:** these three papers were the researcher's attempt to
  independently confirm, against primary large-scale smoking/alcohol-behavior GWAS
  literature, the Open Targets structured lookup's finding that ZCCHC14 has a strong
  smoking-initiation L2G score (0.766). All three failed verification. As a result,
  the "this locus is smoking-behavior-mediated" hypothesis for ZCCHC14 currently rests
  only on the unverified Open Targets L2G score and on a nicotine-dependence paper
  (Wang KS 2012, PMID 22377092 — VERIFIED, included in the main table) that uses
  different SNPs than this locus's lead variant with no established LD/proxy
  relationship. The red-team-skeptic flagged that the researcher's own summary language
  ("consistent with a smoking/addiction-behavior-mediated mechanism hypothesis") is
  more confident than this surviving evidence actually supports.

## Genes/findings the red-team-skeptic marked WEAKENED (verbatim reasoning)

No gene at this locus was marked CONTESTED in the sense of "credible literature
actively arguing against it" — but all three genes were marked WEAKENED, and the
skeptic was explicit that this is a limited audit (see disclosed process gap below),
not a clean bill of health. Full verbatim critique is preserved here from
`evidence_tables/_status/16_87300001-87500000/critique.md`:

### ZCCHC14 — WEAKENED

"Real genetic association exists at this general locus for cerebrovascular/addiction
traits, but the specific inferential chain to COPD-relevant pulmonary or even confirmed
smoking-initiation biology is not established — three of the load-bearing
smoking-initiation citations were CLAIM_MISMATCH, the surviving nicotine-dependence hit
uses different SNPs with no demonstrated LD relationship, and molecular function points
toward hepatotropic viral RNA biology with no lung link. The 'smoking-mediated' framing
is asserted more confidently in the summary than the surviving evidence supports."

The skeptic additionally raised an alternative hypothesis not previously foregrounded
by the researcher: given ZCCHC14's independent, verified associations with lacunar
stroke (PMID 33773637) and intracerebral hemorrhage (PMID 31430377, PMID 31646585),
"this locus may be pleiotropic for vascular/cerebrovascular biology, not smoking
behavior, and the COPD GWAS signal could reflect shared vascular-endothelial mechanisms
... rather than either lung-structural or smoking-behavior biology."

### FBXO31 — WEAKENED (bordering on insufficient evidence to prioritize)

"None argues against it — there's just nothing substantive for it." The skeptic
concurred with the researcher's own conservative framing that the Cyclin
D1/DNA-damage-response/senescence connection to COPD epithelial senescence is
"exactly the kind of 'biology exists somewhere in the literature, therefore it's
relevant to this disease' reasoning the priors warn against," and that the researcher
correctly declined to assert it as established.

### MAP1LC3B — WEAKENED (flagged as the item most in need of caution)

"The mechanistic case is the deepest of the three genes, but the researcher's own
admission that it's genetically unlinked to the credible set (ABC annotation +
proximity only, competing directly against ZCCHC14's coding-adjacent top-PIP variants)
means this should not be presented as gene-prioritization evidence in the GWAS sense —
it's disease-biology-in-general-vicinity evidence." The skeptic identified the central
tension for this locus: "the gene with the best biology has the weakest direct genetic
linkage to the credible set, and the gene with the top PIP variants has the weakest
biology." The skeptic also noted that Scarpa 2012 (PMID 23018286)'s own authors state
their ceramide/LC3B/caspase-3 finding is not COPD-specific (also seen in IPF), and that
MAP1LC3B's immune evidence is restricted to macrophage/innate biology (LC3-associated
phagocytosis, TLR4 crosstalk) with an explicit gap in T-cell/Th1/Th17/Tc17/neutrophil
evidence — this should be reported as "innate/macrophage-restricted evidence only," not
generalized to broad immune involvement. Finally, the skeptic noted that because nearly
every MAP1LC3B structural paper uses a cigarette-smoke-exposure model, this literature
demonstrates MAP1LC3B is smoke-*responsive*, which is "fully consistent with — not an
alternative to — the smoking-mediated-signal hypothesis," a subtly different claim than
MAP1LC3B causing COPD via lung structural biology independent of smoking exposure.

## Locus-wide gaps and process limitations (verbatim from skeptic + researcher)

1. **No FEV1/FEV1-FVC GWAS, colocalization, TWAS, or MR literature was found naming
   any of the three genes.** Per biological prior #2, this absence of spirometric
   concordance is a meaningful gap that should weigh against high-confidence
   prioritization of any single gene at this locus.
2. **PIP-vs-biology mismatch:** the two highest-PIP credible-set variants (67% of
   credible-set mass) are ZCCHC14 3' UTR/synonymous variants, while the strongest
   COPD-relevant mechanistic literature belongs to MAP1LC3B, which is linked to the
   locus only via ABC regulatory annotation and physical proximity, not direct genetic
   evidence (eQTL/colocalization). This is the central unresolved tension at this locus.
3. **Red-team-skeptic's contradiction-search pass was not completed for any of the
   three genes** due to web-search budget exhaustion — the skeptic explicitly disclosed:
   "zero independent contradiction-searches were completed for any of the three genes
   at this locus... Synthesis-lead should not read 'no CONTESTED verdict' here as
   equivalent to 'actively confirmed absence of contradicting literature' — it means
   the search wasn't done." This is preserved here verbatim as a known limitation of
   this locus's review; a follow-up red-team pass (or a fresh dispatch with a larger
   search budget) is recommended before treating this locus's prioritization as final.
4. **rs1050847's full GWAS Catalog trait/PMID list could not be parsed** by the
   researcher's tooling despite confirming 9+ genome-wide-significant association
   records exist for this variant — recommended as a direct follow-up query (by variant
   or by ZCCHC14 gene page) for anyone extending this review.
5. Per the researcher's note, gene-region resolution (confirming ZCCHC14, FBXO31, and
   MAP1LC3B are the only three protein-coding genes in this ~150kb interval, and that
   no additional overlooked candidate exists) relied on the Open Targets structured
   lookup provided by the orchestrator and was not independently re-verified against
   Ensembl by the researcher.

## Open Targets caveats (structured database lookup, not literature — for completeness)

- `search_entities("FBXO31")` returned two Ensembl target IDs; one (ENSG00000112029)
  resolved to FBXO5 on chromosome 6, a different gene entirely, and was discarded by
  the orchestrator after confirming genomic coordinates. The correct FBXO31 ID used
  throughout is ENSG00000103264 (chr16:87,326,987-87,392,142).
- None of the three genes has any Open Targets `genetic_association`-datatype evidence
  for COPD (MONDO_0005002) or FEV1 (EFO_0004314) — all evidence counts were exactly
  zero for these terms. This was treated as an informative absence (per pipeline
  instructions) rather than omitted, but it also means the Open Targets database
  currently offers no locus-to-gene or colocalization evidence to arbitrate between
  the three candidate genes for COPD specifically — all of the "smoking initiation"
  L2G scores cited in this review are for a different trait, not COPD or FEV1 itself.
