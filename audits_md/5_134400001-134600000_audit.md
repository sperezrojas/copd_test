   - Claim as offered by researcher: this GWAS of smoking initiation/cessation/heaviness and alcohol use (406 loci) is very likely the source underlying Open Targets' smoking-initiation/cessation association scores for JADE2/this locus.

Excluded from the evidence table because the specific gene-locus attribution (not just the study's existence) could not be verified — the researcher's broader smoking-mediation argument for JADE2 does not depend on this citation and rests instead on the Yang et al. 2022 (PMID 35773316, VERIFIED) smoking-interaction finding and the Open Targets pleiotropy pattern, both of which remain in the evidence table.

## Genes/claims flagged CONTESTED or WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning)

### JADE2 — WEAKENED (mechanistic inference, not gene identification)

> "The statistical case for JADE2 as the regulatorily-implicated gene is genuinely strong and not overclaimed: it's the sole L2G target across 233 rows at rs329120, contains the whole credible set, and is under strong LoF constraint. The researcher does not overreach in describing this. However, the inferential leap from 'JADE2 is the best-supported gene at this locus' to 'JADE2 drives COPD' is exactly the leap prior #1 and #2 warn against, and the researcher correctly does NOT make it — the write-up is carefully hedged throughout ('plausibly,' 'in whole or part,' 'not a single definitive study'). What synthesis-lead should weigh: (a) zero FEV1/FEV1-FVC/COPD colocalization anywhere in the 233-row OT catalog at this variant — prior #2's absence-of-spirometric-concordance flag applies directly and is a real strike against a lung-intrinsic mechanism; (b) the OT trait profile is dominated by smoking initiation/cessation/nicotine dependence, and this is independently corroborated by Yang et al. 2022 (PMID 35773316, VERIFIED) showing a smoking-status-dependent effect on lung cancer at the same credible-set variant (rs329118) — two independent lines converging on smoking-behavior mediation, per prior #1. One additional caveat worth surfacing that the researcher didn't fully unpack: JADE2's L2G/ABC dominance at this variant reflects that it's the nearest gene with strong regulatory activity across *many* traits (metabolic, endocrine, cardiac, hematologic) — i.e., this looks like a highly pleiotropic regulatory locus, not necessarily a COPD-specific one, and L2G scores are not COPD-trained, so a high L2G doesn't itself add COPD-specific evidence. My independent PubMed check confirms zero JADE2-COPD/spirometry literature exists. **WEAKENED** — the gene-identification (JADE2 as physically/regulatorily implicated) holds up, but the inference that this represents lung-intrinsic COPD biology overreaches what the evidence supports; the pattern is more consistent with smoking-behavior-mediated or generically pleiotropic biology than airway-specific mechanism, and the researcher's own hedged conclusion should be preserved as-is (not upgraded) in synthesis."

This WEAKENED verdict is reflected in the evidence table (JADE2 rows carry a "WEAKENED" skeptic-verdict annotation) and in the Prioritized Gene section's LOW confidence rating — it is not treated as a reason to exclude JADE2 as the top candidate (no competing gene is better supported), but as a reason to not overstate mechanistic certainty.

### C5orf66 — HOLDS (as an "exclude as likely artifact" call)

> "This is a clean case of likely nearest-gene/annotation artifact, corroborated by two independent methods (Ensembl region-overlap, Open Targets alias resolution) both placing C5orf66/PITX1-AS1/EPIST/LINC02900 ~450-800kb outside the locus near PITX1. My independent PubMed check for PITX1-lung-function/COPD/spirometry returned zero hits, which is at least consistent with (doesn't strengthen or weaken) the exclusion call — it just confirms there's no rescue narrative via PITX1 either. The literature attached to 'C5orf66' itself (oncology, endometriosis) is irrelevant regardless of physical location. No contradicting evidence found. One minor note: a >450kb overrun is unusually large for a typical MAGMA gene-window artifact, so this might reflect a stale symbol/build mismatch rather than a window-overrun per se — worth a caveat but doesn't change the bottom line. **HOLDS** (as a 'likely artifact, exclude' call — the inference matches the evidence)."

Not contested; retained in evidence table as a negative/exclusionary finding, not as a candidate gene.

## Gene-identification note (not a citation exclusion, but relevant audit context)

Independent of any single citation, both the orchestrator's Open Targets lookup and the researcher's separate Ensembl REST API region-overlap query concluded that **C5orf66** (the MAGMA-prioritized gene per the locus input file) does not physically exist within the 5:134,400,001-134,600,000 interval — it resolves only to a lncRNA locus (aliases PITX1-AS1 / EPIST / LINC02900) roughly 450-800kb away, near the distinct PITX1 locus. This is very likely a stale gene-symbol/coordinate mismatch or MAGMA gene-window artifact rather than true biological evidence for C5orf66 at this locus. Conversely, **CDKN2AIPNL**, a protein-coding gene at the locus boundary, was not in any of the internal pipeline's prioritized-gene lists (nearest/FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS/ABC) but was added as a candidate by the researcher after independently identifying it via the same Ensembl region-overlap query. Both findings were corroborated independently by the orchestrator's own step-1 Open Targets lookup (for C5orf66) and flagged positively by the red-team-skeptic as valuable, non-obvious contributions. Open Targets step-1 data was not available for CDKN2AIPNL, since it was identified by the researcher only after the Open Targets lookup stage had already completed — this is a genuine data gap in the Open Targets summary for this locus, disclosed rather than silently omitted.
