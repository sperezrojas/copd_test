## Full red-team-skeptic critique (verbatim)

Independent searches performed (reported below with results) to test the researcher's
absence-of-evidence claims: SMOC1 + lung function/FEV1/COPD/spirometry/emphysema/IPF/asthma;
SMOC1 + smoking-GWAS (GSCAN/pack-years/cigarettes-per-day); rs185651603 directly;
SLC10A1/NTCP + lung/smoking; alternative-gene-at-this-locus searches against recent
multi-ancestry TOPMed WGS COPD/lung-function papers. None of these surfaced any SMOC1-lung,
SMOC1-smoking, or rs185651603-specific hit, nor any paper naming a different gene at this
exact 14:69.85-70.0 Mb interval. This corroborates the researcher's negative findings but
does **not** strengthen them into a positive case — it's a second confirmed absence, not new
evidence, and (like the researcher's) it's literature-search-based rather than a direct pull
of GSCAN/MVP summary statistics at this locus, so "no smoking overlap" should be reported as
unconfirmed/inconclusive, not ruled out.

**SMOC1** — This is the crux of the locus. The prioritization stack (FLAMES, top L2G 0.763,
credible-set variant sits in its intron, spans the whole region) is entirely
positional/computational; there is zero colocalization support (0 colocalizing pairs
locus-wide), zero literature-based statistical genetics (no PubMed FEV1/COPD/spirometry
hit), and zero GWAS Catalog spirometric association for SMOC1 (its other GWAS Catalog hits
are height/bone/hematologic — a pattern more consistent with a pleiotropic
developmental/growth gene than a lung-specific one). The 6 verified Mendelian-disease papers
are genuinely strong biology (BMP antagonist, causal for Ophthalmo-acromelic/Waardenburg
Anophthalmia syndrome, replicated in Smoc1-null mice) — but the researcher itself flags that
none of the six mention lung, airway, or alveolar phenotypes. That is an important,
correctly-reported gap, not an oversight. Per prior #2, a COPD-only association
(PIP=0.9957 in the COPD/airway-obstruction credible set) with no corroborating FEV1/FEV1-FVC
signal is exactly the pattern that "warrants consideration of alternative pathways" — here,
no alternative pathway (smoking or otherwise) was identified either, leaving the locus
mechanistically unresolved rather than explained. The L2G score of 0.763 should also not be
over-read: only 2 genes were scored at all in this window (SMOC1, SLC10A1), so "top"
reflects an uncontested field of two, not strong discrimination against a broad candidate
set, and it is uncorroborated by colocalization. Net: this looks like nearest-gene/
credible-set-containment bias plus real-but-off-target (ocular/limb) Mendelian biology,
presented in a way that could be mistaken for COPD-relevant mechanistic support if a reader
skims past "none mention lung." The moderate LoF constraint (oe=0.42) is a weak, nonspecific
supporting data point only.
**WEAKENED** — evidence for SMOC1 as *a gene in the region with a strong statistical/
positional claim* is real, but the inference that it is a biologically supported COPD gene
overreaches; the six Mendelian papers should not be read as mechanistic support for a
pulmonary role, and synthesis should explicitly flag the absence of any spirometric or
smoking concordance as leaving mechanism (or even locus assignment) unresolved.

**SLC39A9** — PoPS-only, zero L2G, zero Open Targets disease evidence for COPD, and per the
coordinates given (69,398,015-69,463,644) it lies entirely outside the analyzed
14:69,850,001-70,000,000 region (~390kb upstream of the region start, not merely "near" it)
— this is a materially weaker positional case than even SLC10A1/SRSF5. The 21 unreviewed
"SLC39A9 ZIP9 androgen receptor" PubMed hits are a minor thoroughness gap (a title-level scan
would take minutes and would let synthesis lead rule out an obvious lung hit with more
confidence), but given the gene doesn't even sit in the locus and has zero genetic/
statistical anchoring, deprioritizing without a deep dive is a defensible triage call, not a
suppressed finding.
**WEAKENED** (verging on justified deprioritization) — flag for synthesis lead: the 21 hits
were never screened even superficially; if synthesis wants to fully close this out, a quick
title/abstract scan is cheap insurance, but the prior is strongly against this gene
regardless of what's in those 21 hits.

**SLC10A1** — Correctly deprioritized: located within the region, second-ranked by L2G but
at 0.067 vs SMOC1's 0.763, weak Open Targets COPD score (0.025), and its established biology
(NTCP, hepatic bile-acid transporter, HBV/HDV receptor) has no documented pulmonary
mechanism. My own search found only that smoking affects hepatic NTCP *expression* (a liver
pharmacokinetic covariate, not a lung-disease mechanism) — this doesn't rescue the gene for
COPD relevance.
**HOLDS** — the researcher's negative conclusion is sound and appropriately not overclaimed.

**SRSF5** — Two verified citations tie SRSF5 knockdown to NSCLC proliferation/glycolysis and
an alternative-splicing prognostic signature in lung adenocarcinoma. The researcher
explicitly and correctly distinguished this as lung-*cancer* cell biology rather than
COPD-relevant structural/immune biology — no overclaiming here, and no L2G/prioritization
support in any case.
**HOLDS** — correctly characterized and deprioritized.

**SLC8A3** — Entirely outside the locus (44kb downstream of the region end, ~121kb from the
credible-set variant), no lung/COPD literature, established biology is neuronal/skeletal-
muscle calcium exchange (NCX3) with no plausible pulmonary tie-in.
**HOLDS** — correctly deprioritized.

### Overall notes for synthesis lead (from red-team-skeptic)

1. This locus's strongest claim (SMOC1) rests almost entirely on positional/computational
   prioritization (FLAMES, L2G among a field of 2, credible-set-variant containment) rather
   than on independent genetic (spirometry GWAS, colocalization, lung eQTL) or mechanistic
   (lung-specific) evidence. Treat it as an unresolved/positional candidate, not a
   biologically validated COPD gene, when writing the final synthesis language — avoid
   phrasing that lets six unrelated (ocular/limb) Mendelian citations imply pulmonary
   mechanism.
2. Per prior #2, the absence of any FEV1/FEV1-FVC concordance for this COPD-only signal is
   itself noteworthy and should be stated explicitly, not just implied by omission.
3. Smoking-mediation status is genuinely unknown here — neither the researcher's nor my
   independent literature search found evidence either confirming or excluding overlap with
   smoking-behavior GWAS loci, and neither of us queried GSCAN/MVP summary statistics
   directly. This should be reported as an evidentiary gap, not as a negative finding
   ("no smoking involvement"), if synthesis lead wants to make any smoking-related claim
   about this locus.
4. No contradicting paper naming a different gene at this exact 14:69.85-70.0 Mb interval
   was found by either the researcher or my independent search — I found no CONTESTED-grade
   evidence for any gene here.
5. Minor thoroughness gap: SLC39A9's 21 unreviewed PubMed hits should be at least
   title-scanned before final sign-off, though this is unlikely to change the prioritization
   given the gene's position outside the locus and complete absence of L2G/Open Targets
   support.
