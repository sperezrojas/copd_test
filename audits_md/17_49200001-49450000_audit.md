## Gene-level critique verdicts (verbatim reasoning, full detail)

### ZNF652 — WEAKENED
The fine-mapping case that ZNF652 is the gene at this locus is solid (carries essentially
all PIP mass; the only rival with meaningful weight, ZNF652-AS1, is an antisense transcript
whose span overlaps ZNF652 itself and is more parsimoniously the same regulatory signal).
However, the case that this locus is COPD/pulmonary-mechanistic is thin: (1) the only
VERIFIED disease-association literature for ZNF652 is blood pressure/hypertension — a
non-pulmonary, non-smoking trait, possibly reflecting horizontal pleiotropy; (2) the FEV1
evidence failed independent literature verification (see excluded citations above) and
rests only on the Open Targets structured score; (3) COPD case-control score in Open Targets
is near-null (0.0022) vs. a much stronger FEV1 score (0.357/0.588) — an asymmetry that
should be reported plainly; (4) zero structural/developmental or immune/inflammatory
mechanistic literature exists for ZNF652 — a genuine mechanistic blank. Conclusion: the gene
assignment is probably right, but claiming ZNF652 as a COPD-mechanistic candidate overreaches
the verified evidence.

### UTP18 — HOLDS (artifact dismissal justified)
The build-mismatch diagnosis is concrete and checkable: GRCh38 coordinates for UTP18 sit
~1.9 Mb outside the locus, while GRCh37 coordinates coincide almost exactly with ZNF652's
position — a specific mechanical signature of a genome-build bug in the MAGMA/PoPS gene-
boundary file, not an ambiguous inference. Combined with a total absence of lung/immune
literature and a purely cancer/ribosome-biogenesis functional profile, dismissal is
appropriate; the researcher's hedged language is correctly calibrated. Should be surfaced to
pipeline maintainers as a likely genome-build bug affecting MAGMA/PoPS gene annotation.

### FLJ40194 / ZNF652-AS1 — HOLDS (both)
Correctly deprioritized. ZNF652-AS1's near-total positional overlap with ZNF652 makes "this
is ZNF652's regulatory signal, not an independent lncRNA" the more parsimonious explanation;
no independent-causality claim was made. FLJ40194 has no literature and only low-PIP-tail
positional overlap. Trivial, low-priority calls, no overreach.

### GNGT2 — WEAKENED (CONTESTED-adjacent; not flagged by internal pipeline)
Real concerns: (a) zero statistical support — not selected by any of the four internal
prioritization methods; ~150-160kb from the lead variant with no credible-set variant in the
gene body; (b) all three verified papers are asthma-model-based, not COPD, and two of three
(PMID 42228185, PMID 39417697) appear to be sequential outputs of the same research
group/mouse model — not independent replication; the third (PMID 34785105) is also an
OVA-allergen-asthma model; (c) mechanistic mismatch risk — OVA/allergen-driven neutrophilic
asthma and cigarette-smoke-driven COPD share downstream inflammatory vocabulary (neutrophils,
Th17, IL-17) but differ in upstream trigger (allergen sensitization vs. chronic oxidant/PAMP
exposure); the biological priors explicitly flag this distinction. No cigarette-smoke or
human-COPD-tissue data exists for GNGT2 in the verified set. The researcher's own caveats
were honest, but "biologically plausible" framing is generous for a gene with no locus-level
statistical support and single-lab, wrong-disease-model evidence. Should be presented as
speculative/hypothesis-generating, not comparable footing to ZNF652 or PHB1.

### PHB1 — HOLDS (with caution against over-promotion; not flagged by internal pipeline)
Weak positional evidence (no direct credible-set variant in the gene body; only the
lowest-PIP tail of ZNF652-AS1 lies "near/within" PHB1 — proximity, not fine-mapping support)
paired with genuinely COPD/smoking-relevant mechanistic literature (PMID 34214709: human
COPD/smoker lung tissue mitophagy data; PMID 27327778: CSE-exposed A549 data explicitly
proposed relevant to COPD/IPF). Two of the four PHB1 papers (PMID 40482976 sepsis-ALI, PMID
29620269 LPS-ALI) are not COPD-specific and should be weighted lower. The enhancer-sharing
citation (PMID 35325318) is weaker supporting evidence than it looks: different disease
entirely (gastric cancer eQTL), different variant (~2Mb offset from the lead SNP) — shows
these genes CAN share a regulatory element somewhere, not that the COPD-associated haplotype
does so in lung. The researcher's framing is properly hedged. Caution for synthesis-lead:
PHB1's richer mechanistic story should not cause it to be promoted above ZNF652 purely
because it is more mechanistically legible than ZNF652's blank mechanistic slate —
mechanism-rich-but-positionally-weak genes are exactly the kind of case where mechanism bias
can distort prioritization.

### ABI3 — HOLDS (not flagged by internal pipeline)
Single verified citation, exclusively CNS-microglial/Alzheimer's context (PMID 30326945), no
lung relevance, no fine-mapping support. Correctly and briefly deprioritized.

### PHOSPHO1 — insufficiently documented to render a verdict; flagged for re-check
No verified citations shown at all for PHOSPHO1, positive or negative. Unclear whether it
received an actual literature search that came up empty, or was not seriously searched.
Researcher's note: PubMed search "PHOSPHO1 bone mineralization phosphatase" returned 43 hits,
all bone/skeletal-biology focused, no COPD/lung/immune relevance apparent from its
established function; not pursued further. Confirm before treating this omission as fully
informative.

## Overall CONTESTED determination

No gene was formally elevated to CONTESTED status based on the record as reviewed, but per
the search-coverage disclosure above this determination rests on a zero-independent-search
critique session and should NOT be treated as a clean bill of health. The closest candidate
for CONTESTED status is ZNF652's own FEV1 claim, whose three supporting citations were
rejected as CLAIM_MISMATCH — a literature-traceability failure internal to the researcher's
own citation chain, not an external paper arguing against ZNF652. This was called WEAKENED
rather than CONTESTED, but is flagged as the single highest-priority item for follow-up
verification in any future re-run of this locus with a fresh search budget.
