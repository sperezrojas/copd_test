## CONTESTED / WEAKENED gene verdicts (verbatim from red-team-skeptic)

### CHRNB2 — WEAKENED

> The smoking-mediation case is real but is an inference stack, not a demonstrated
> mechanism at this locus, and the researcher's "most plausibly mediated through smoking"
> framing should be read with that caveat made explicit in the audit trail:
>
> - The one piece of genuinely locus-specific evidence is that this credible set's 5
>   variants resolve to the same GWAS Catalog studyLoci as smoking-initiation/
>   nicotine-dependence hits, where Open Targets L2G favors CHRNB2 (0.627/0.783) over ADAR
>   (0.324/0.114). That is a real, variant-matched signal — not merely a
>   different-chromosome analogy — and I don't want to understate it.
> - However: (1) Open Targets shows no genetic_association datatype score for
>   CHRNB2-COPD at all; the reported COPD disease score (0.381) is built entirely from
>   animal_model and clinical datatypes, meaning Open Targets' own curated genetics
>   pipeline does not itself link CHRNB2 to COPD by GWAS evidence. This is a striking,
>   easily-overlooked fact that undercuts confidence more than the researcher's prose
>   conveys. (2) No formal statistical colocalization (e.g., coloc PP4) between the COPD
>   summary stats and the smoking-trait summary stats was reported — the "same studyLoci"
>   observation is suggestive shared architecture, not a proven shared causal variant test
>   against COPD itself. (3) None of the 8 literature citations use this locus's actual
>   credible-set SNPs; they use different CHRNB2 variants (rs2072659, rs2072658) from
>   unrelated family/candidate-gene smoking studies — this is analogical support for
>   "CHRNB2 as a gene family plausibly affects smoking behavior," not confirmatory
>   evidence for this specific locus. (4) The "CHRNA5/CHRNA3/CHRNB4-15q25.1-like
>   mechanism" language risks being read as if this is the same statistical signal — it is
>   not; 15q25.1 is a different chromosome and a different, independently-discovered
>   locus. This should be relabeled explicitly as a paralogy argument in the writeup, not
>   implied architectural equivalence. (5) Within CHRNB2's own literature, when CHRNB2 is
>   genotyped alongside its cluster-mates, the significant hit repeatedly lands on CHRNA5
>   (Pintarelli 2017) or CHRNB4/CHRNA3 (Liu 2018), not CHRNB2 individually — CHRNB2 is the
>   recurring "also-ran" in its own gene family, which is mild but real internal
>   contradicting evidence.
> - Total absence of CHRNB2 lung-function/structural/immune literature (independently
>   confirmed as a genuine zero, not a search-quality artifact) is real negative evidence
>   against a direct pulmonary mechanism, consistent with Prior 2's expectation that a
>   COPD-only signal without spirometric concordance warrants scrutiny for smoking
>   mediation. That part of the researcher's inference is sound.
> - The alternative "non-neuronal cholinergic signaling in airway epithelium, by analogy
>   to CHRNA7" is pure speculation with zero supporting literature (independently
>   confirmed no CHRNB2-specific airway-epithelial anti-inflammatory literature exists;
>   the cholinergic anti-inflammatory pathway is a CHRNA7 story). This should be labeled
>   hypothesis-generating only in any final report, not weighed as evidence.
>
> **Verdict: WEAKENED.** Smoking-mediation is the best-supported of the three hypotheses
> at this locus, but "most plausibly mediated through smoking" overstates what is actually
> a same-variant/different-trait L2G result plus cross-locus gene-family analogy, with
> Open Targets' own genetics-specific COPD score for CHRNB2 being empty. Recommend
> synthesis-lead state the smoking-mediation hypothesis as the leading but unconfirmed
> explanation, and explicitly note the absent COPD-genetic_association datatype.

### ADAR — HOLDS (as hedged by researcher)

> The "absence of evidence ≠ evidence of absence" point raised in the task is valid: the
> downstream_gene_variant/intron_variant credible-set annotations could still be real
> cis-eQTLs for ADAR that simply haven't been tested against COPD/lung GTEx data yet, and
> ADAR's strong LoF-intolerance means a regulatory (not necessarily coding) mechanism is
> plausible. But this cuts both ways — downstream/intronic annotations are among the
> weaker regulatory-variant categories generically, LoF-intolerance is a nonspecific
> gene-essentiality signal that says nothing about lung relevance, and ADAR's own L2G at
> the shared smoking-trait studyLoci is real but secondary to CHRNB2 (0.324 vs 0.627),
> meaning even if this locus's signal is smoking-mediated, CHRNB2 is favored over ADAR as
> the mediating gene. The one disease-relevant ADAR mechanism found (Magnaye 2021,
> miR-200b-3p editing, SOCS1/IL-4/IFN-γ, eosinophils) is a real and specific airway finding
> but is asthma, not COPD, with no established directionality for a COPD analog — the
> researcher was appropriately careful not to over-claim this. I independently confirmed
> zero ADAR-COPD/emphysema PubMed literature exists. Net: the researcher's "biologically
> plausible but weaker direct genetic support" framing is accurate and not overclaimed.
> I'd add one recommendation: before finalizing, check GTEx/lung-eQTL databases directly
> for an ADAR cis-eQTL at these exact variants — that's the concrete test that would move
> ADAR from "plausible" to "supported" or rule it out, and it appears not to have been
> done.

### KCNN3 — WEAKENED, recommend near-exclusion (not CONTESTED)

> This is not a case of credible contradicting evidence defeating a prior claim — it's a
> case where a real evidentiary basis for a locus-specific KCNN3 role never existed. KCNN3
> is absent from the credible set entirely (PoPS-only), has zero Open Targets L2G
> colocalization at every studyLocus checked for this region, and its Open Targets COPD
> score (0.261) is driven by genetic_association evidence that plausibly reflects KCNN3's
> unrelated atrial-fibrillation GWAS hits elsewhere in the genome, not this locus. I
> independently confirmed zero PubMed hits for KCNN3 + lung/airway/pulmonary, and the one
> immune-cell finding uncovered (Liu 2012, SK3 in CNS microglia) is CNS tissue, not lung.
> PoPS is a similarity-based prioritization method known in the field to sometimes flag
> genes based on shared expression/pathway features rather than physical/regulatory
> proximity to the causal variant — it should not be treated as comparable evidentiary
> weight to fine-mapping PIP or L2G colocalization. Recommend synthesis-lead explicitly
> de-prioritize KCNN3 below CHRNB2 and ADAR rather than listing it as a co-tier "weak
> candidate" — the appropriate framing is "no locus-specific evidence identified; retained
> only because a polygenic-similarity method flagged it," which is a materially weaker
> epistemic status than either CHRNB2 or ADAR.

## Additional residual concern flagged by red-team-skeptic (LD/haplotype-block artifact)

> The credible-set PIPs split roughly 0.5/0.5 across CHRNB2-annotated (0.29+0.21) and
> ADAR-annotated (0.24+0.23+0.026) variants — consistent with one (or a small number of)
> causal variant(s) whose fine-mapping signal cannot be cleanly assigned to one gene over
> the other from statistical fine-mapping alone. The only tie-breaker offered is Open
> Targets L2G computed on a smoking-behavior trait (not COPD), which favors CHRNB2 but is
> itself an extrapolation across phenotypes. Recommend synthesis-lead present CHRNB2 as
> the favored candidate on total weight of indirect evidence while explicitly disclosing
> that the credible set itself cannot statistically separate CHRNB2 from ADAR, and that no
> COPD-specific (as opposed to smoking-specific) colocalization exists for either gene.
