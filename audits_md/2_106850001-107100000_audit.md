## Genes/loci marked CONTESTED or WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic (verbatim reasoning preserved)

### RGPD3 — CONTESTED

> "This candidacy has a foundational problem independent of the literature: the
> researcher's own Ensembl-confirmed physical coordinates show RGPD3
> (2:106,391,290-106,468,413) does not overlap the stated locus window
> (2:106,850,001-107,100,000) at all — it sits 380-460kb outside it. Its inclusion rests
> entirely on a PoPS score, a genome-wide feature-similarity method that does not require
> or demonstrate physical or regulatory proximity to the credible set, and Open Targets
> shows no colocalization data for RGPD3 in this region to compensate. Its own QTL
> credible sets likewise sit outside this locus's span. This is worse than the standard
> 'LD/nearest-gene bias' concern — it isn't that RGPD3 is a plausible alternative gene
> inside a shared LD block; it's that RGPD3 arguably isn't 'at' this locus in any physical
> sense at all.
>
> The three verified citations (craniofacial morphology WES association, thyroid-cancer
> positive-selection signature, acral-melanoma somatic driver mutation) are unrelated to
> lung, immune, or COPD biology, and two are cancer-somatic-mutation contexts in a gene
> family (RGPD2/3/4/5/6 segmental duplication) that is intrinsically prone to
> mapping/genotyping artifacts and pleiotropic noise — a known confound for candidate
> genes in segmentally duplicated regions, which should further lower prior confidence
> rather than being treated as neutral.
>
> Verdict: CONTESTED — not because a paper directly argues against RGPD3 in COPD, but
> because the strongest available evidence (the researcher's own independently-confirmed
> physical coordinates) directly contradicts the implicit premise that RGPD3 is a locus
> gene at all. This should be flagged as a positional/statistical artifact concern rather
> than a literature-supported candidate; RGPD3 should not be carried forward as a
> co-equal candidate with ST6GAL2 without independent physical/regulatory evidence (e.g.,
> an actual coloc or chromatin-interaction result) tying the credible set to RGPD3, which
> does not currently exist."

### ST6GAL2 — WEAKENED

> "Positional case: it is the only protein-coding gene physically inside the stated
> window, which is modest but real support — there's no competing neighbor to be confused
> with inside the window itself... But 'only gene physically present' is not the same as
> 'functionally implicated': Open Targets shows no COPD/FEV1/FEV1-FVC L2G score, no
> colocalization, and no lung eQTL among 37 tissue-QTL credible sets... That is a real
> absence, not a gap in the researcher's search — it's a structural absence of the exact
> evidence type (prior #2) that would most directly support a pulmonary mechanism.
>
> The Groux-Degroote 2008 finding is genuine ex vivo human bronchial-tissue evidence...
> but it demonstrates inflammation-responsive expression, in a cystic fibrosis/Pseudomonas-
> adhesion frame, not a genotype-to-expression (eQTL) link, and not COPD. Using it to
> argue the GWAS signal 'acts through' airway mucin glycosylation would be an unsupported
> leap... I'd flag it as a landmine for the synthesis draft: if the final report
> compresses this into 'ST6GAL2 has airway-inflammation support,' that would overclaim
> relative to what the abstract actually shows.
>
> Critically, the profile that IS consistent across the strongest, most specific data —
> top Open Targets disease associations for cognitive traits..., the lead SNP's only
> GWAS Catalog hit being 'Intelligence,' predominant brain expression..., and OT QTL
> colocalization with educational attainment — points toward a neurobehavioral/cognitive
> pathway, not a pulmonary one... a locus whose most specific, best-powered associations
> are cognitive/behavioral is exactly the pattern prior #1 warns about. There is also no
> spirometry (FEV1/FEV1-FVC) support at all...
>
> Verdict: WEAKENED. Physical presence in the locus and the Groux-Degroote finding are
> real, but the totality of evidence (no lung eQTL/coloc, no spirometry concordance,
> dominant brain/cognitive signal, plausible smoking/behavior-mediation alternative) does
> not support treating ST6GAL2 as a pulmonary-mechanism gene; it should be presented as a
> positionally-plausible but mechanistically unsupported candidate, with the
> cognitive/behavioral-mediation hypothesis flagged as at least equally likely."

### Locus-level alternative hypothesis (verbatim)

> "Given (a) the lead variant's only GWAS Catalog association is 'Intelligence,' (b)
> ST6GAL2's top Open Targets disease associations are cognitive (intelligence,
> mathematical ability), (c) ST6GAL2 QTLs colocalize with educational attainment and
> metabolic traits (body-fat%, fasting glucose) rather than any respiratory trait, (d)
> there is no spirometry (FEV1/FEV1-FVC) signal reported anywhere in the dossier, and (e)
> ST6GAL2 is predominantly brain-expressed per multiple independent papers — the totality
> of evidence fits a smoking/behavior-mediation or cognitive-pathway hypothesis at least
> as well as, and arguably better than, a direct pulmonary mechanism. This is exactly the
> scenario prior #1 anticipates... I'd flag this as the leading alternative explanation
> for synthesis-lead, not as a forced conclusion — it's also possible this is simply an
> underpowered/noisy locus with no clear mechanism yet, given how thin and
> cross-tissue-scattered all the evidence is either way."

> GWAS'). Both queries failed — the WebSearch tool reported the session's search budget

> based entirely on the verified-citation dossier and Open Targets/Ensembl background
> already supplied, not on any new literature I found myself. Synthesis-lead should treat

> accordingly — this is a gap, not a clean bill of health."

This caveat should be weighed by the study team: the CONTESTED/WEAKENED verdicts above
are grounded in the existing verified dossier and Open Targets/Ensembl data (which are
solid), but no fresh literature search for direct rebuttal evidence was completed by the
skeptic due to search-budget exhaustion. A follow-up pass with restored search budget,
targeting GTEx lung-eQTL for ST6GAL2 and smoking/cognitive-trait genetic correlation at
this locus specifically, is recommended before treating this locus's conclusions as final.
