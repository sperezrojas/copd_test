# Audit trail — locus 9:131,900,001-132,150,000 (MED27 / PPP2R4)

This file preserves the excluded/contested material and full reasoning trail underlying
`evidence_tables/9_131900001-132150000.md`, for reviewers who want the full traceability but
are not part of the evidence hand-off itself.

## Citations excluded from the evidence table

**None.** All 14 citations returned by the researcher were verified (VERIFIED) by
citation-verifier — no citation was RETRACTED, NOT_FOUND, or CLAIM_MISMATCH. All 14 therefore
appear in the evidence table (including two off-topic ones for MED27, labeled as such rather
than omitted, for completeness/traceability).

Two citations carry verifier-noted caveats that limit their evidentiary weight even though
they are formally VERIFIED:
- **Saunders GRB et al. 2022, Nature, PMID 36477530** — paper itself verified (GSCAN
  multi-ancestry follow-up GWAS), but citation-verifier could not confirm from the abstract
  that its supplementary data specifically names MED27 or a signal at chr9:132,053,270
  (rs9411339). Used only as generic background/context in the evidence table, not as
  gene-specific evidence.
- **Yan Z et al. 2024, BMC Genomics, PMID 38937676** — paper and its MED27-pleiotropy finding
  verified, but the three SNPs it implicates (rs4382592, rs200968, rs1510719) were not
  confirmed to be rs9411339 or in LD with it. Flagged WEAKENED in the evidence table on this
  basis.
- **Shutta KH et al. 2025, medRxiv preprint, PMID 41445629** — DOI as originally reported by
  the researcher (10.64898/2025.12.18.25342312) was malformed; citation-verifier resolved and
  corrected it to **10.1101/2025.12.18.25342312**. Content verified: MED27 is genuinely named
  as one of 24 "multi-omic switch" genes distinguishing COPD from IPF, resolving the
  researcher's original uncertainty about whether this was an incidental search match. Still
  an unreviewed preprint; treated as hypothesis-generating only.

## Genes marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic

### PPP2R4 (approved symbol PTPA; ENSG00000119383) — CONTESTED

Reasoning (verbatim from red-team-skeptic, critique stage): "The genome-build mismatch is now
independently corroborated by a second source (Ensembl REST API), not just mygene.info, and
the coincidence that hg19 coordinates fall almost exactly inside the GRCh38 window is too
precise to be an accident. Combined with PPP2R4 never ranking top-L2G at rs9411339 across 43
studies and its COPD 'evidence' being 100% text-mining artifact with zero genetic-association
contribution, there is a specific, structural reason to believe PPP2R4 was nominated at this
locus in error, not on weak-but-real biology... I'd treat this as a confirmed pipeline
artifact, recommend PPP2R4 be dropped as a genuine candidate at this locus on
structural/positional grounds alone, independent of the weak literature evidence — and
recommend the synthesis lead flag the upstream PoPS/physical-overlap pipeline for a
build-consistency audit, since this kind of error could affect other loci too."

Supporting chain of evidence for this verdict:
1. Researcher independently found via mygene.info that PPP2R4/PTPA's GRCh38 coordinates
   (chr9:129,110,550-129,148,946) sit ~2.76 Mb outside the stated locus
   (9:131,900,001-132,150,000), while its hg19 coordinates
   (chr9:131,873,229-131,911,225) fall almost exactly inside the stated GRCh38 window.
2. Red-team-skeptic independently confirmed this via a direct Ensembl REST API query
   (rest.ensembl.org/lookup/id/ENSG00000119383), obtaining identical GRCh38 coordinates —
   i.e., two independent authoritative sources agree.
3. This pattern (hg19 coordinates numerically matching a GRCh38-labeled window) is a
   recognized signature of a genome-build mismatch bug in an upstream annotation/overlap step,
   not coincidence.
4. Corroborating structural genetics evidence: PPP2R4 never appears among Open Targets' own
   top L2G genes at rs9411339 across 43 independent GWAS/molecular-QTL studies with a credible
   set there (only MED27, NTNG2, TTF1, CFAP77 ever rank) — consistent with PPP2R4 not actually
   sitting at this locus.
5. PPP2R4's aggregated Open Targets COPD association score (0.081) is driven entirely by the
   "literature" (text-mining) datatype with zero genetic-association contribution — i.e., even
   setting the coordinate issue aside, there is no direct genetics support linking PPP2R4 to
   COPD.

**Orchestrator's recommendation:** Treat PPP2R4/PTPA as very likely mis-assigned to this
GRCh38 locus by an upstream build-mismatch error in the internal PoPS/physical-overlap
pipeline step. Its real (verified) lung-epithelial cancer biology (Meeusen 2021, Tohmé 2019,
Nigliato 2026) and its cystic-fibrosis lung-function modifier finding (Gisler 2013) remain
genuine, verified findings about PPP2R4/PTPA as a gene, but should not be weighted as evidence
for this specific locus's association with COPD given the coordinate mismatch. This is
recorded as a pipeline-level flag: the study team should audit whether other loci in this
review used the same mismatched-build gene-annotation source for PoPS overlap calls.

### MED27 (ENSG00000160563) — WEAKENED (not formally CONTESTED, but flagged as overreaching if
stated too strongly)

Reasoning (verbatim from red-team-skeptic): "No paper argues against MED27 or names a clearly
superior competing gene within the credible-set window. But the case as framed overreaches:
physical containment of a PIP=0.99 variant is genuine fine-mapping evidence that MED27 is the
regulatorily relevant gene at this locus, but it is evidence about which gene, not about which
mechanism. Zero COPD or FEV1/FVC association (Open Targets count=0 both), zero PubMed
COPD/emphysema/airway literature, an LoF-tolerant gnomAD profile, and a described-disease
phenotype (recessive neurodevelopmental/cerebellar disorder) with no lung involvement — this
is a thin foundation for a 'COPD gene' claim beyond statistical fine-mapping."

On the smoking-mediation narrative specifically (verbatim): "this is legitimately suggestive
that the locus's downstream phenotype channel is behavioral/smoking-related rather than
lung-intrinsic (consistent with Prior 1), but it should be reported as 'one exact-SNP
credible-set tag + one gene-level paper with unconfirmed LD' — not asserted as 'MED27 is a
smoking locus.' If the synthesis lead's draft states the smoking link more strongly than that,
it is overclaiming relative to the underlying (verified) citations."

The evidence table and prioritized-gene section above have been written to reflect this
caveat explicitly rather than overstating the smoking-mediation case.

## Gaps and limitations explicitly flagged during the pipeline (not citation problems per se,
but material to interpretation)

1. **Search-budget exhaustion during red-team critique.** The red-team-skeptic subagent's
   WebSearch budget (200/200) was already exhausted before its four planned
   contradiction-search queries executed. It substituted a WebFetch call to the Ensembl REST
   API (successful, used to confirm the PPP2R4 coordinate issue) but was unable to run any
   independent literature contradiction-search for either MED27 or PPP2R4. Its critique is
   therefore based on internal-consistency review of the researcher's own findings plus one
   structural (coordinate) verification, not on new adversarial literature search. This is a
   genuine limitation of this locus's review depth; a follow-up critique pass with restored
   search budget is recommended if resources allow.

2. **TTF1 and CFAP77 not fully researched.** Open Targets' L2G fine-mapping at rs9411339
   occasionally ranks TTF1 (ENSG00000125482 — confirmed to be "transcription termination
   factor 1," an RNA Pol I termination factor, NOT NKX2-1/thyroid transcription factor despite
   the confusable symbol) and CFAP77 (ENSG00000188523, a cilia/flagella-associated protein
   gene with a priori mucociliary-clearance plausibility for airway disease) as top genes for
   some of the 43 non-COPD studies at this variant. Neither gene was prioritized by any of the
   internal pipeline's four gene-prioritization methods (nearest/FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS/ABC) for
   this locus, and neither is physically inside the stated locus window per the researcher's
   Ensembl region check. The orchestrator performed a follow-up structured Open Targets lookup
   (not a literature search) after the critique flagged this gap: both genes show negligible-
   to-no COPD association (TTF1: score 0.0098, literature-only; CFAP77: count=0) and no
   FEV1/FEV1-FVC association (count=0 for both). Given this, and the lack of internal-pipeline
   prioritization, these genes were not escalated to a full researcher/citation-verifier pass,
   but this decision — and the negative Open Targets check supporting it — is recorded here for
   transparency.

3. **LD between rs9411339 and the smoking/GERD pleiotropic SNPs in Yan et al. 2024 was never
   established** by any stage of this pipeline (researcher did not have LD-calculation tools
   readily available; citation-verifier and red-team-skeptic have no LD/genotype tools). This
   is the single most important open question for confirming or refuting the smoking-mediation
   hypothesis at this locus, and is explicitly flagged as a "what would resolve this" item in
   the main evidence table's Prioritized Gene section.

4. **No COPD-specific or spirometry-specific GWAS/colocalization/TWAS study was found for
   either candidate gene** by the researcher, citation-verifier, or the orchestrator's own
   Open Targets queries, despite this locus reaching genome-wide significance in the source
   COPD GWAS meta-analysis (per the locus input file). This absence is itself an important,
   explicitly-reported finding, not an oversight: it is consistent with (though does not
   prove) a smoking-behavior-mediated rather than pulmonary-biology-mediated mechanism at this
   locus, per this pipeline's prior #1.
