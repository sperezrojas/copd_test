## Genes flagged CONTESTED or WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic

The red-team-skeptic did not mark any gene at this locus as CONTESTED in the strict sense
("a specific paper actively arguing against it or for a different gene"). It did, however, mark
two genes' key evidence as WEAKENED (interpretation-level concerns about overreach), which
materially affected the final prioritization call in the evidence table. Verbatim reasoning
preserved below (also in `evidence_tables/_status/15_49000001-49800000/critique.md`):

### FAM227B — WEAKENED
"The researcher's own framing ('high PIP is genomic-proximity-driven rather than
mechanism-driven') slightly conflates two different things: PIP=0.461 reflects confidence that
rs12232354 is the causal variant for the COPD association signal (a within-locus statistical
fine-mapping result, not a proximity heuristic) — it is actually the single strongest
gene-agnostic statistical fact at this locus. What's genuinely unresolved is which gene that
intronic variant regulates; intronic variants frequently regulate non-host genes, so 'sits in
FAM227B's intron' is itself a nearest-gene-style assumption, just one step more refined than
'nearest gene to the lead SNP.' The hyperthyroidism paper's lung PP4>0.8 colocalization is
legitimate circumstantial support that this region has cis-regulatory activity in lung tissue,
but it's for a different trait and doesn't establish direction of effect on COPD/FEV1. ... I'd
caution synthesis-lead against too readily demoting FAM227B below DTWD1 on the strength of 'no
direct literature' — that's absence of evidence, and it holds the internal fine-mapping signal
that a cross-cohort nearest-gene mention (DTWD1) does not clearly beat."

### DTWD1 — WEAKENED (the critique's strongest pushback)
"This is the one I'd push back on hardest. The researcher's own caveats (couldn't identify the
original discovery paper; can't confirm fine-mapped vs. nearest-gene-label attribution in PMID
38886662) are exactly right, but the final ranking (DTWD1 > FAM227B) doesn't follow through on
those caveats. Consider: DTWD1's 'replication' evidence is a single abstract sentence naming it
in a list of nearest-gene labels for a replicated regional association — this is evidentially the
same category of proxy (nearest-gene naming convention) as calling the credible-set gene
'FAM227B' because the lead SNP sits in its intron, just derived from a different cohort's
reporting convention instead of this study's own fine-mapping. It is NOT independent confirmation
that DTWD1-the-gene (as opposed to FAM227B, GALK2, or the region generally) is mechanistically
responsible. Given that DTWD1 and FAM227B are close together in this same ~800kb LD block, PMID
38886662's replicated 'DTWD1' locus may well be tagging the identical underlying signal as this
study's FAM227B-labeled credible set — in which case citing it as evidence for DTWD1 over
FAM227B is close to circular. The Open Targets COPD score (0.502, highest) doesn't resolve this
either, since L2G/aggregate scores can themselves be influenced by how many independent GWAS
papers used a 'DTWD1' nearest-gene label for hits in this region, recycling the same ambiguity
rather than adding new causal-gene evidence. ... What the replication does genuinely establish...
is that this genomic region (not necessarily this gene) carries a true, cross-ancestry-
replicating COPD case-control signal — useful for ruling out single-cohort artifact concerns,
but silent on gene-level attribution. ... I'd recommend synthesis-lead treat DTWD1 and FAM227B as
roughly tied on gene-attribution confidence rather than DTWD1 ranked first."

### FGF7 — HOLDS, with one characterization correction
The skeptic held the researcher's low-to-moderate confidence call but corrected the
characterization of PMID 15579628 (Plantier et al.) from "negative" to "mixed": "Plantier et al.
is characterized as purely 'negative,' but it also reported blunted IL-1β-induced KGF induction
specifically in emphysema fibroblasts — a positive (if weak, single-study, small-n, and possibly
consequence-rather-than-cause) mechanistic finding about a stimulus-dependent regulatory defect,
not just an absence of baseline difference." This correction is reflected in the evidence table
(Skeptic verdict = MIXED for that row).

### SECISBP2L, GALK2, COPS2 — HOLDS
No overreach identified for these three genes; the researcher's low/very-low confidence
assessments were judged appropriately hedged. See `critique.md` for full per-gene reasoning.

## Search-coverage caveat disclosed by red-team-skeptic

The red-team-skeptic's web search budget was exhausted (200/200) before it could run any
independent contradiction-search query for this locus. Its critique is therefore a desk review
of the researcher's own findings and the orchestrator-supplied Open Targets context, not a
literature search in its own right. It explicitly recommends a follow-up search — specifically
to identify the original discovery GWAS behind PMID 38886662's "DTWD1" mention, and to check
whether DTWD1 and FAM227B are in high LD in prior COPD GWAS — before treating the
DTWD1-vs-FAM227B prioritization call as settled. This caveat is carried through to the
"Prioritized gene" section of the evidence table, which is marked confidence LOW accordingly.

## Additional candidate gene identified outside the input file's prioritization methods

COPS2 (COP9 signalosome subunit 2) lies physically within the locus boundaries
(15:49,106,068-49,155,661) per an Ensembl REST region lookup performed by the researcher, but is
not flagged by nearest-gene, MAGMA, PoPS, ABC, or any credible-set variant in the locus input
file. It was added to the researched candidate set for completeness only. Both of its citations
(PMID 30503783, PMID 28423737) are tangential (an unrelated psychiatric GWAS naming it only as
one of several positional candidates near a haplotype, and an unrelated hepatocellular-carcinoma
pathway study). The researcher's own disposition: "not a plausible candidate based on current
evidence; noted for completeness only." No further action was taken on COPS2 in this pipeline
run; it was not queried against Open Targets by the orchestrator (that lookup was completed
before COPS2 was identified by the researcher).

disclosed as an open/inconclusive item rather than treated as a confirmed absence of
