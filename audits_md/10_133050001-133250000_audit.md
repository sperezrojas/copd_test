## Positional caveats (not citation issues, but material to weighing the evidence)

- **TCERG1L** (PoPS-prioritized) sits ~1.74 Mb outside/telomeric of the analyzed region
  (10:133,050,001-133,250,000) entirely, per direct Open Targets genomic-coordinate lookup
  (131,092,391-131,311,736 vs. the region's 133,050,001 start). No credible-set variant is anywhere
  near it. Per red-team-skeptic: "This looks like a case where PoPS ... picked up a
  plausible-sounding gene name with no actual connection to the locus's biology or position — a
  useful illustration of PoPS's known tendency to occasionally prioritize genes based on
  pathway/expression similarity rather than local signal."
- **VENTX** and **UTF1** (both identified by the researcher's own region lookup, not flagged by any
  prioritization method) lie within the analyzed 200 kb window but have no credible-set variant
  assigned to them (~77 kb and ~70 kb respectively from the highest-coordinate credible-set variant).
  VENTX in particular carries the single most disease-mechanistically-relevant biology at this locus
  (macrophage/proinflammatory function) but red-team-skeptic explicitly cautioned against letting
  that biological plausibility substitute for the complete absence of statistical/fine-mapping
  support — see the Prioritized-gene section of the evidence table for how this was handled.

## Full red-team-skeptic critique (verbatim)

### Cross-cutting methodological note (applies to all genes)
I ran independent searches for (a) rs2748432 / this exact 10:133Mb region in any published GWAS,
(b) ADGRA1/GPR123 and KNDC1 in smoking-initiation/nicotine-dependence GWAS papers (GSCAN and
others), (c) a competing gene named for this locus, and (d) COPD/lung literature the researcher
might have missed for each gene. I found **no published paper — GSCAN, other smoking GWAS, or
COPD/lung-function GWAS — that names this locus, ADGRA1, KNDC1, or GPR123 at all.** This
corroborates the researcher's own negative finding rather than contradicting it. The "10q23.31/
10q25 shared with COPD/lung cancer/smoking" hits that surfaced in my search are a different, more
centromeric/telomeric region from older candidate-gene literature, not this locus — I did not find
literature-level confirmation, only database (Open Targets) colocalization, that this specific
credible set overlaps smoking-initiation signal.

### GENE: ADGRA1
The dominant fine-mapping signal (PIP ~0.58+) is real and not obviously an LD-sharing artifact
with KNDC1 — the two genes' credible-set variant clusters are largely non-overlapping (ADGRA1
intronic vs. small KNDC1-upstream cluster), so this isn't simple nearest-gene bias between two
co-plausible neighbors at the same variants. However, the researcher's inferential chain has two
separate weaknesses:

1. **Smoking-mediation claim overreaches on the current evidence.** The researcher (correctly)
flags that no primary GSCAN/smoking-GWAS paper was actually confirmed to report this locus, and
the one smoking-GWAS citation submitted resolves to the wrong paper (Eucalyptus terpene-oil GWAS,
NOT_FOUND) while the correct GSCAN PMID (30643251) was never cited or checked against. My own
independent search likewise found zero literature placing ADGRA1/GPR123/this locus in a
smoking-behavior GWAS. So "consistent with, though not proof of, smoking-behavior mediation" is
appropriately hedged language — but the underlying evidentiary basis for even raising it is
Open-Targets-coloc-only, unconfirmed against primary summary statistics. This should be stated
even more plainly to synthesis-lead: the smoking-mediation hypothesis here rests entirely on an
unverified database coloc field, not on any literature, verified or otherwise.

2. **"Unresolved mechanism" vs. "evidence against lung-intrinsic mechanism."** The researcher
frames the CNS/neuroendocrine-only literature plus absent pulmonary/immune literature as leaving
the mechanism "unresolved." I'd push further: given (a) dominant fine-mapping mass, (b) exhaustive
functional literature in only two organ systems (hypothalamus, hippocampus) with a clear,
mechanistically dense, unrelated biology (thermogenesis, inhibitory synaptic tone), (c) zero
COPD/lung-function GWAS or coloc signal indexed anywhere in Open Targets for this locus, and (d)
OT's own top indexed traits for these credible-set variants being systolic BP, insomnia, GERD, and
back pain — this pattern is more consistent with a locus that is pleiotropic for
neurobehavioral/autonomic traits and reaches COPD via smoking-behavior or another indirect route
than with an unresolved-but-plausible lung mechanism. This is not proof, but the researcher's
"unresolved" framing understates how thin the positive case for lung-intrinsic biology actually
is.

3. Overclaiming check: the one-sentence synthesis matches the underlying VERIFIED abstracts
reasonably well (Zhang 2021, Tosun 2026/bioRxiv). No claim inflation detected for ADGRA1
specifically. The Hamann/misattribution CLAIM_MISMATCH is trivial (authorship error, not a content
error) and doesn't affect the gene-level inference.

4. No contradicting-gene literature found naming a different gene as causal at this exact locus
(also consistent with the general absence of any locus-specific COPD/lung literature).

**WEAKENED** — fine-mapping support is real and gene assignment is reasonably specific (not an
LD/nearest-gene artifact), but the researcher's "unresolved mechanism, possible smoking-mediation"
framing is too neutral. The evidence pattern (dominant CNS-only biology, zero lung/immune
literature, zero COPD/lung-function GWAS signal, coloc-only with smoking/behavioral/autonomic
traits) should be read as active evidence against a lung-intrinsic mechanism and in favor of an
indirect (behavioral/autonomic-pleiotropy) explanation, even though that explanation itself lacks
primary-literature confirmation. Synthesis-lead should treat ADGRA1 as low-confidence for direct
pulmonary biology despite its statistical dominance.

### GENE: KNDC1
Same structure as ADGRA1 but with weaker statistical support (PIP ~0.07, small upstream cluster) —
here LD/nearest-gene concerns are more legitimate, since this cluster sits between ADGRA1 and
KNDC1 and ABC-prioritization for enhancer-gene assignment is inherently softer than fine-mapping
PIP mass. The Alzheimer's linkage claim (Zhao 2020, LOD=2.4) was flagged by the verifier as
unconfirmed from the abstract — the researcher should not lean on the specific LOD value in
synthesis. The OncoTargets Ther claim (Yu 2020) was a CLAIM_MISMATCH and correctly was not pursued
further; good discipline here. No pulmonary/immune literature found, independently confirmed by my
searches. Same caveat as ADGRA1 applies to the smoking-mediation hypothesis (database-only,
unconfirmed in primary GWAS literature) — again the researcher hedges appropriately but the case
is thin on both sides (thin for lung mechanism, thin-but-only-database-supported for smoking
mechanism).

**WEAKENED** — modest statistical support, no pulmonary/immune biology, brain-restricted
expression, and the Alzheimer's-linkage citation's key quantitative claim is unverified. The gene
is appropriately deprioritized by the researcher already, but the parallel "smoking-mediation"
hedge for KNDC1 should be flagged even more explicitly as speculative-on-speculative (unconfirmed
coloc + unconfirmed neurobiology-implies-smoking logic) rather than presented as a real
alternative hypothesis on par with ADGRA1's.

### GENE: VENTX
This is the most mechanistically on-prior gene at the locus (macrophage/proinflammatory, directly
relevant to Prior #3), and my independent search for "VENTX COPD/emphysema" confirms the
researcher's characterization: no COPD-specific literature exists, only the general Wu 2011 JCI
macrophage-differentiation paper. The critical weakness the researcher already flags — zero
credible-set variant within ~77kb, i.e., no statistical fine-mapping support whatsoever — is
disqualifying for treating this as a genuine locus-level candidate rather than a speculative
aside. Under the "genes are the unit of evidence, not every variant" scope note, a gene that is
both positionally distant AND carries no assigned PIP mass is not something the credible set
implicates; including it is useful for completeness/hypothesis-generation but should not be
escalated to a prioritized candidate without independent statistical support (e.g., an unindexed
COPD coloc or eQTL link, neither of which was found).

**WEAKENED** — the biology is real and on-prior, but the inference that this is a "biologically
interesting alternative" for this locus overreaches given zero fine-mapping support at any
credible-set variant. It should be reported to synthesis-lead as a flagged non-candidate
(interesting gene, wrong locus-evidence), not as a live alternative to ADGRA1/KNDC1.

### GENE: UTF1
My independent search confirms the researcher's assessment: no COPD/airway-specific UTF1
literature exists beyond the non-specific IHC survey (Mouallif 2014) showing epithelial expression
across multiple organs including lung — not COPD-relevant, not remodeling-specific, not even
lung-selective. (Note: a search on "lung epithelium COPD" surfaced a POU5F1/OCT4 paper on Hedgehog
signaling in COPD epithelial remodeling — POU5F1 is a related-but-distinct pluripotency TF from
UTF1; worth noting to synthesis-lead as a "near-miss" in case it signals interest in this gene
family, but it is not evidence for UTF1 itself and should not be conflated.) Also no credible-set
variant assigned (~70kb away).

**WEAKENED/effectively HOLDS-as-weak** — researcher's own characterization ("weak candidate,
included for completeness") is accurate and not overreached. No further escalation warranted;
flagging to synthesis-lead only as a low-priority background note.

### GENE: TCERG1L
Positional case is the weakest of all: ~1.74Mb outside the region, PoPS-only prioritization,
negligible OT COPD score (0.0022), zero lung/immune literature (confirmed independently — all
three VERIFIED citations are epigenetic/cancer-biomarker papers in unrelated tissues: colorectal
cancer methylation, iPSC/ESC methylation, sperm methylation). The researcher's "weight very low"
conclusion is well-supported and not overreached anywhere. This looks like a case where PoPS
(which uses genome-wide similarity to known genes, not physical proximity) picked up a
plausible-sounding gene name with no actual connection to the locus's biology or position — a
useful illustration of PoPS's known tendency to occasionally prioritize genes based on
pathway/expression similarity rather than local signal.

**HOLDS** (as a correctly-deprioritized negative finding — the inference "should be weighted very
low" matches the evidence).

### GENE: RPL5P28 / ADGRA1-AS1 / MIR202 / MIR202HG
Minimal entries, no manufactured claims, reasoning (RPL5P28 downstream-gene-variant annotation
reflecting ADGRA1 3'-proximity rather than independent unit) is a reasonable and appropriately
hedged interpretation. No functional literature exists for these to search independently, and I
found none. **HOLDS** as an honest null/thin entry.

### Thoroughness check
The researcher's search strategy was adequate — I was unable to find, with independent searches,
any locus-specific literature (COPD, lung-function, or smoking-GWAS) for ADGRA1, KNDC1, or this
exact genomic interval that the researcher missed. I found no evidence of a contradicting/competing
gene being reported at this specific locus in the COPD/lung-function literature (the
10q23.31/10q25 hits in general searches refer to different, non-overlapping regions from older
candidate-gene-era papers, not the fine-mapped interval here). I do not believe further gene-level
searches are needed before synthesis; the locus genuinely appears to be a literature desert outside
CNS/neuroendocrine biology for the two positionally-supported genes.

### Overall notes for synthesis-lead
1. No candidate gene at this locus has direct, disease-relevant pulmonary or immune literature
   support. This is a real, not a search-failure, finding — independently confirmed.
2. The smoking-mediation hypothesis for ADGRA1/KNDC1 is plausible on priors (Prior #1) but
   currently rests entirely on an Open Targets database coloc field never confirmed against a
   primary smoking-GWAS paper (the one citation submitted for this purpose was
   NOT_FOUND/miscited). Treat this as a hypothesis requiring further verification, not as
   established supporting evidence, and note that the researcher's own citation error here (wrong
   PMID for GSCAN) is a minor but real thoroughness gap — a fixable one, since the correct PMID
   (30643251) is known and could be checked in a follow-up pass if this locus is prioritized.
3. Given dominant fine-mapping mass at ADGRA1 with no lung/immune biology and no COPD/lung-function
   GWAS signal anywhere, and given Prior #2 (spirometry/PF concordance strengthens
   prioritization), the absence of any FEV1/FEV1-FVC/spirometry association for this locus
   (confirmed by both OT context and my independent search) is itself meaningful negative evidence
   against a lung-intrinsic mechanism, and should be weighted accordingly rather than left as a
   neutral gap.
4. VENTX is worth keeping in the writeup as a hypothesis-generating aside (macrophage biology,
   Prior #3-relevant) but should not be escalated as a competing prioritized candidate given zero
   fine-mapping support — flag this distinction clearly for synthesis-lead so VENTX isn't
   inadvertently promoted above ADGRA1/KNDC1 on biological-plausibility grounds alone.
5. Overall this locus should likely be synthesized as low-confidence / plausibly
   smoking-behavior-mediated rather than lung-intrinsic, pending better characterization of the
   smoking-GWAS overlap.
