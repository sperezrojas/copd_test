# Audit trail: locus 1:72,350,001-73,700,000 (GRCh38) — LRRIQ3 / NEGR1

This appendix records every citation excluded from the evidence table, every gene marked
CONTESTED/WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic, and process limitations disclosed during this
locus's pipeline run. Reasoning is preserved verbatim from the source subagent where
possible.

## Excluded citations

### 1. Sakornsakolpat P, McCormack M, Bakke P, Gulsvik A, Make BJ, Crapo JD, Cho MH, Silverman EK. "Genome-Wide Association Analysis of Single-Breath DLCO." American Journal of Respiratory Cell and Molecular Biology, 2019. PMID: 30694715, PMC6503619, DOI: 10.1165/rcmb.2018-0384OC.

**Original claim (researcher):** In a GWAS of single-breath diffusing capacity (DLCO)
conducted in four COPD-enriched cohorts (COPDGene, NETT, GenKOLS, TESRA), NEGR1 was one
of 12 loci showing suggestive (not genome-wide-significant) association with DLCO in the
European-ancestry white analysis (P<1e-5 combined, P<0.05 in both COPDGene and GenKOLS).
The researcher self-flagged that the PubMed abstract had gene-symbol formatting corruption
and reported having cross-checked against PMC full text directly, but still marked the
citation UNVERIFIED pending citation-verifier confirmation.

**Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH (excluded).** Citation-verifier reasoning, verbatim: "Abstract
retrieved from PubMed has severe formatting corruption with gene names replaced by
commas/punctuation throughout the results section ('including variants near
,,,,,,,,,,,, and'). Cannot verify NEGR1 appears in the 12 suggestive loci list as specific
gene names are corrupted. Full text retrieval also returned empty."

**Why this matters (consequential, not routine):** This was the ONLY citation identified
in this entire locus review that would have provided directly-pulmonary (as opposed to
neurobehavioral/psychiatric) evidence for either candidate gene. Both the researcher and
the citation-verifier independently hit the same abstract-corruption obstacle and neither
could obtain usable full text to confirm NEGR1's presence in the results table. Per
red-team-skeptic: "the researcher should say explicitly ... that zero verified evidence in
this dossier supports a direct pulmonary mechanism for either gene; the case for prior #1
over prior #2 here rests entirely on an absence of pulmonary evidence plus an unverified
database colocalization, not on any positive verified pulmonary finding." This citation
is a candidate for a manual/follow-up re-check outside this automated pipeline (e.g.
directly retrieving the PMC6503619 PDF or supplementary table) if the study team wants to
close this gap, since the underlying paper may well contain the claimed finding — it is
excluded here only because it could not be confirmed through the tools available in this
pipeline, not because it was shown to be false or fabricated.

## Genes marked CONTESTED / WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic

### LRRIQ3 — WEAKENED
Red-team-skeptic verbatim: "The neurobehavioral/addiction literature is real (verified)
but none of it establishes LRRIQ3 as the causal gene over its immediate neighbors
(FPGT-TNNI3K, LINC01360) at this pleiotropic, gene-dense region — Johnston et al.'s own
framing undercuts gene-specificity. The Open Targets L2G 'preference' for LRRIQ3 is very
likely a distance artifact (flat 0.08 across 43 unrelated traits) and should not be
treated as corroborating evidence. The directional conclusion (smoking/neurobehavioral
over pulmonary) is reasonably supported by the totality of the pattern, but 'LRRIQ3
specifically' is not resolved by anything in this dossier."

Additional skeptic reasoning on gene-specificity (section b of critique, verbatim):
"Johnston et al. explicitly describe this as a 'gene-dense region' containing
LINC01360/LRRIQ3/FPGT/FPGT-TNNI3K, with SNPs linked to brain/CNS expression of 'nearby
genes' (plural, unspecified). Sanchez-Roige's rs640561 is 'near LRRIQ3' by nearest-gene
annotation, which is exactly the LD/nearest-gene bias this review is supposed to watch
for. None of citations 1-4 fine-map to LRRIQ3's coding sequence or regulatory region
specifically — they are all consistent with FPGT-TNNI3K or LINC01360 being the operative
gene instead."

### NEGR1 — WEAKENED, verging on CONTESTED for candidate-gene status itself
Red-team-skeptic verbatim: "NEGR1's literature (BMI/obesity, MDD, schizophrenia,
smoking-MDD shared architecture) is real but entirely CNS/neurodevelopmental in tissue
context, and the researcher's own 'cytokine receptor cross-talk' mention is correctly
flagged as tissue-mismatched to COPD-relevant lung immunology. With the DLCO citation
excluded as unconfirmed, NEGR1 has zero remaining verified pulmonary evidence, and it is
never the Open Targets L2G-preferred target at this locus. Its case for inclusion rests
almost entirely on physical proximity (PoPS prioritization) rather than any converging
functional or genetic evidence pointing toward lung/COPD biology specifically. Flagged as
the weaker of the two candidates on current evidence, notwithstanding PoPS's nomination."

## Process limitations disclosed during this run

1. **Red-team-skeptic search budget exhaustion.** The red-team-skeptic agent disclosed
   verbatim: "WebSearch was unavailable for this task (session budget exhausted before I
   could run any queries — all four of my search attempts returned 'budget used' errors).
   I substituted direct WebFetch calls against PubMed search URLs as a partial workaround.
   This gave a shallow contradiction-pass on both candidate genes plus the unlisted third
   candidate (FPGT-TNNI3K): 'LRRIQ3 lung,' 'NEGR1 lung function FEV1,' 'NEGR1 COPD,' and
   'FPGT-TNNI3K lung/COPD/smoking' — all returned zero relevant hits (one NEGR1-COPD fetch
   failed on a cookie wall rather than genuinely returning zero, so treat that one as
   unsearched, not confirmed-null). This is weaker than a full independent search and
   should not be read as an exhaustive contradiction search." This means the critique's
   own contradiction-search coverage is shallower than the pipeline's usual standard, even
   though its analytical critique of the researcher's existing citations (below) is
   substantive and was not limited by the search-budget issue.

2. **Lead variant literature gap.** A targeted PubMed search for the lead variant rsID
   "rs12142152" returned zero results. All gene-level GWAS literature for LRRIQ3 in this
   dossier is anchored to a different (LD-related, not identical) variant, rs640561, from
   the same broader chromosomal region. This substitution is disclosed in the evidence
   table and should be weighed accordingly.

3. **FPGT-TNNI3K not evaluated as an independent candidate.** The researcher noted that
   published GWAS repeatedly group LRRIQ3/FPGT/FPGT-TNNI3K/LINC01360 together as one
   pleiotropic LD block, and FPGT-TNNI3K sits immediately adjacent to (3' of) LRRIQ3, but
   FPGT-TNNI3K was not treated as an independent candidate gene given distance from the
   lead variant and pipeline scope (it was not in the locus input file's candidate-gene
   list). Red-team-skeptic's shallow contradiction pass on "FPGT-TNNI3K lung/COPD/smoking"
   returned zero hits, but flagged this coverage as shallow (see limitation #1). The study
   team may wish to independently evaluate FPGT-TNNI3K as a candidate if this locus is
   prioritized for follow-up, given that the strongest cited GWAS/statistical-genetics
   source for this locus (Johnston et al. 2019) names it alongside LRRIQ3.

## Open Targets structured-lookup caveats (not a citation issue, but relevant to this audit)
The Open Targets L2G score for LRRIQ3 (0.0804) is identical across all 43 credible sets
registered at the lead variant, spanning phenotypically unrelated traits (schizophrenia,
food-liking, liver enzymes, COPD, etc.). Both the researcher and red-team-skeptic
independently concluded this is most consistent with a static, distance-dominated feature
in the L2G model rather than trait-specific molecular evidence, and should not be treated
as gene-prioritization evidence distinguishing LRRIQ3 from NEGR1 or FPGT-TNNI3K at this
locus. Full supporting detail is in
`evidence_tables/_status/1_72350001-73700000/opentargets.md`.
