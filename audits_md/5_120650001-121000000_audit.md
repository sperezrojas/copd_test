# Audit trail — Locus 5:120,650,001-121,000,000 (PRR16)

This file records everything excluded from the main evidence table (evidence_tables/5_120650001-121000000.md),
plus the full CONTESTED reasoning from red-team review, preserved for traceability. It is
intentionally kept separate from the evidence table so the evidence table can be distributed
without also distributing this audit trail.

## Excluded citation content

### Yamamoto et al. 2014 (PMID 24656129) — mTOR/Hippo-independence sub-claim

**Excluded sub-claim:** "Largen acts independently of mTOR and Hippo signaling."

**Reason for exclusion — CLAIM_MISMATCH.** citation-verifier confirmed the paper, PMID, and DOI
are all correct and unretracted, and confirmed every other claim made about this paper (PRR16 =
Largen identity, discovery via genetic screen, stimulation of mitochondrial-function mRNA
translation, increased mitochondrial mass/ATP, increased cell size in vitro and in conditional
transgenic mice, no lung/airway/immune phenotype). However, the paper's abstract makes no mention
of mTOR or Hippo signaling at all, and therefore does not support or refute an "independence"
claim about those pathways. This specific sub-clause was dropped from the citation used in the
evidence table; the rest of the citation was retained as VERIFIED after a follow-up re-check with
the sub-clause removed.

**Process note:** this citation required three total verification passes. Pass 1 returned an
internally self-contradictory NOT_FOUND (the verifier reported checking a mistyped PMID,
24156129 — a transposition of the correct 24656129 — got an unrelated article, but then stated in
its own text "the correct PMID for the Largen paper is 24656129"). The orchestrator flagged this
inconsistency and requested a clean digit-by-digit re-check (pass 2), which confirmed the paper
exists/is unretracted and returned CLAIM_MISMATCH specifically on the mTOR/Hippo sub-clause. Pass
3 re-verified a trimmed claim with that sub-clause removed and returned a clean VERIFIED, which is
what appears in the main evidence table.

## CONTESTED gene/claim flags from red-team-skeptic

### CONTESTED: "PRR16 drives the anxiety association" at this locus (re: Li et al. 2023, PMID 37945807)

Red-team-skeptic's full reasoning (verbatim): "This is the single piece of evidence carrying
almost the entire 'psychiatric pathway' narrative, and it is weaker than it's being presented.
PRR16 is named only as one of several genes near a newly discovered risk locus; the paper's own
integrative fine-mapping and functional follow-up explicitly prioritized other genes at that
locus (CTNND1, RAB27B, BTN3A2, PCLO), and the mouse validation work was done on Ctnnd1, not
Prr16. The Open Targets L2G scores for PRR16 (~0.61-0.63) are modest, not the near-1.0 scores that
would indicate confident single-gene resolution, and L2G is a statistical/regulatory-distance-
based algorithm, not functional evidence. This is a textbook case of a multi-gene LD block where
the 'supporting' evidence is genuinely ambiguous between several plausible genes. I am marking the
claim 'PRR16 drives the anxiety association at this locus' as CONTESTED — the paper the
researcher cites as key evidence for PRR16 is itself evidence that the authors closest to the
data thought a different gene was more likely causal."

**Orchestrator's added note on this point (not part of red-team's own text, added for
traceability):** re-reading the researcher's original wording, the researcher described the
deprioritized genes (CTNND1, RAB27B, BTN3A2, PCLO) as being "at OTHER LOCI" — i.e., different
genomic loci among the meta-analysis's 14 total risk loci, not necessarily PRR16's own locus. Two
of those four genes (RAB27B, PCLO) are themselves also among the paper's 10 new-locus nearest-gene
names (alongside PRR16), meaning at those two loci the "prioritized/functionally-supported" gene
and the "nearest-gene locus label" happen to coincide. CTNND1 and BTN3A2 do not appear in the
10-new-loci name list and were most likely prioritized genes at previously-known (not new) loci.
Taken at face value, the verified citation content does not clearly establish that PRR16 was
specifically considered and rejected in favor of a different gene at its own locus — it may
instead be that PRR16 (like CNTNAP5, MAP2, RAB9BP1, BTN1A1, PTPRD, FARP1, CDH2) was reported only
via nearest-gene naming convention for its own locus, without the paper's deeper fine-mapping/
functional layer explicitly confirming or excluding it either way (silence, not necessarily
contradiction). Both readings — red-team's stronger "contested/deprioritized" framing and this
softer "not specifically assessed either way" framing — are preserved here since this could not
be adjudicated further within this review's scope. Either reading leads to the same downstream
conclusion used in the evidence table: the anxiety-locus evidence for PRR16 should be treated as
nearest-gene/L2G-level association only, not as confirmed fine-mapped causality, and it does not
raise PRR16's confidence for COPD causality regardless of which reading is correct.

### WEAKENED: "smoking-mediated pathway" narrative for this locus

Red-team-skeptic's reasoning (verbatim): "Two independent inferential leaps are being stacked
here, and neither has direct evidence: Leap 1: PRR16 -> anxiety/depression (already contested
above). Leap 2: psychiatric/behavioral loci -> smoking initiation/heaviness (zero smoking-GWAS
hits found for this locus, explicitly noted as absent by the researcher's own searches)... I'd
recommend synthesis-lead avoid language implying a specific mechanistic story and instead flag
this as a locus with weak/non-specific gene assignment and no COPD-relevant biology of any kind
(pulmonary, immune, or smoking) — full stop — rather than reaching for a psychiatric/smoking
narrative as a default explanation whenever pulmonary evidence is absent. That 'default to
psychiatric/smoking story' move is precisely the unfalsifiable pattern the task brief warns
against."

### WEAKENED: researcher's summary line "does not support a pulmonary-structural or
immune-inflammatory mechanism"

Red-team-skeptic's reasoning (verbatim): "This phrasing conflates absence of evidence with
evidence of absence. The searches returned zero hits for COPD/spirometry/smoking papers — that is
a true and useful negative finding, but it does not 'not support' a pulmonary mechanism in the
sense of positively arguing against one; it simply means no one has published on it... GTEx shows
lung expression (10.9 TPM) higher than most brain regions, and HPA IHC shows medium-level protein
specifically in alveolar cells and resident lung macrophages — a lung-tissue-resident expression
pattern that is at least as suggestive of a possible (untested) local pulmonary role as the
psychiatric GWAS hit is suggestive of a psychiatric one. The researcher's own data undercuts its
own summary sentence; synthesis-lead should soften 'does not support' to 'no direct evidence
found for or against.'" (This softened framing was adopted in the main evidence table's
"Prioritized gene" section.)

### Flagged gap (not a contested claim, but an unresolved open question): overlapping lncRNA
ENSG00000248927

Red-team-skeptic flagged that an unnamed lncRNA (ENSG00000248927) overlapping the lead variant
rs6878072 was never investigated by either the researcher or the red-team itself (the red-team's
own planned follow-up searches on this were not completed due to WebSearch budget exhaustion
during its dispatch — see its "Search-budget note," reproduced below in full for transparency).
This means the credible-set/L2G evidence assembled in this review does NOT positively distinguish
PRR16 from this lncRNA as the operative gene at this locus; PRR16 is best understood as "the only
candidate anyone has looked at," not "the confirmed candidate."

**Red-team's search-budget note (verbatim):** "My own independent verification searches (PRR16
5q23 COPD/spirometry, rs6878072 lung-function lookup, identity of the overlapping lncRNA
ENSG00000248927, and neighboring-gene search at this locus) could not be run — the session's
WebSearch budget was exhausted before I could execute them. The critique below is therefore based
entirely on re-analysis of the verified findings as provided, not on new independent search
confirmation. This is itself a gap the synthesis-lead should know about: none of the four
'contradicting evidence' searches I would have run for this locus were completed, so my
contradiction-search pass for PRR16 is effectively at 'none' rather than 'shallow' or 'full.' If
search budget can be restored, the two highest-value follow-ups are (1) confirming the
identity/expression pattern of ENSG00000248927, and (2) checking whether any published
lung-function or COPD GWAS lists an alternate gene at 5q23.2 near rs6878072."
