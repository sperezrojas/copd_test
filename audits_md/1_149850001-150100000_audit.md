## 2. Genes flagged CONTESTED or with substantive skeptic caveats

Red-team-skeptic's full verbatim critique is preserved in
`evidence_tables/_status/1_149850001-150100000/critique.md`. Per-gene verdicts and key reasoning are
excerpted below (verbatim from that critique).

### VPS45 — INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE, leaning CONTESTED as a common-variant COPD candidate

> "Boundary-ambiguous (gene body extends outside the stated locus window), not flagged by any of the
> five formal prioritization methods, no locus-specific COPD/FEV1 L2G evidence reported. Its one
> disease mechanism relevant to immune biology (Vilboux et al., congenital neutropenia) is a rare
> recessive LoF syndrome with a plausibly *opposite*-direction neutrophil phenotype from what COPD's
> neutrophilic-inflammation biology would predict, and its best-powered common-variant GWAS signal is
> for schizophrenia, not lung disease. Recommend synthesis-lead treat as a low-confidence, likely
> scope-creep addition unless independent locus-specific statistical evidence surfaces."

Full reasoning from the critique (verbatim):

> "I agree with flagging this hard. Two independent problems, both under-weighted in the write-up as
> presented:
>
> - **Positional/boundary issue**: the gene body (150,067,279–150,145,329) extends well beyond the
>   locus's stated upper boundary (150,100,000), and only its 5' end falls inside the defined window.
>   It was not flagged by *any* of the five formal prioritization methods (nearest gene, FLAMES,
>   MAGMA, PoPS, ABC) — it was added purely by the researcher's own regional enumeration. That's a
>   legitimate scope-creep concern: without any statistical method placing VPS45's credible-set/L2G
>   evidence at this locus (none was reported, in contrast to MTMR11/SF3B4/OTUD7B which all have Open
>   Targets L2G numbers cited), there's no genetic-fine-mapping reason to think this gene is even in
>   play for the COPD signal as opposed to an adjacent, independent LD block.
> - **Genetic-architecture mismatch, and possibly wrong-direction biology**: Vilboux et al. establishes
>   that *biallelic, recessive, severe LoF* mutations in VPS45 cause a congenital
>   neutropenia/neutrophil-dysfunction syndrome (impaired motility, increased apoptosis, reduced β1
>   integrin) — i.e., a rare monogenic disorder that produces neutrophil **deficiency/dysfunction**. A
>   common noncoding GWAS variant operates through a completely different mechanism (subtle expression
>   modulation, heterozygous, population-frequent) and, if anything, the Mendelian phenotype's
>   direction (loss of functional neutrophils) is the **opposite** of the excess/hyperactive
>   neutrophilic inflammation classically implicated in COPD. This should be stated explicitly as a
>   reason the neutropenia literature does *not* transfer to a common-variant COPD hypothesis, not
>   merely 'different architecture' as a soft caveat... the competing, better-powered VPS45
>   schizophrenia GWAS signal (common variant, same gene) is a more architecturally comparable finding
>   and points toward neuropsychiatric rather than lung/immune biology, further diluting the case."

### OTUD7B — WEAKENED

> "Richest general mechanistic literature (NF-κB, TCR signaling) of the four original candidates, but
> that literature is in endothelium/upper-airway/T-cells-generally/VSMC, not lower-airway lung tissue,
> and is demonstrably cell-type-context-dependent (An et al. 2022 counter-example). Genetically, it is
> absent from L2G at this locus's COPD/FEV1/FVC credible sets and has a negligible direct Open Targets
> COPD score despite being the VEP-annotated/PIP=0.894 gene at the lead variant — a real disconnect
> between fine-mapping proximity and colocalization-based gene assignment that argues against
> over-elevating it. The single 2026 transcriptomic correlation study is too weak (unreplicated,
> correlational, ML-derived) to bridge that gap and should stay explicitly hedged in synthesis."

Additional critique detail (verbatim), on why OTUD7B's rich literature should not be read as
"therefore most plausible":

> "there is an **asymmetry in how 'guilt by association' is being judged** across genes. The MTMR11
> hub-gene/ML co-expression panels... are correctly dismissed as 'nonspecific bioinformatics evidence,
> not functional/genetic evidence.' But OTUD7B's mechanistic literature... is real molecular biology,
> but **none of it is lower-airway/lung-parenchyma tissue, and none of it is COPD-specific**... Rich
> general-biology literature about a nearby gene, decoupled from locus-specific statistical evidence,
> is a form of narrative substitution the priors document warns against (a 'genes are the unit of
> evidence' story should not be won by whichever candidate has the best textbook chapter)."

### SF3B4 — WEAKENED

> "Real, functionally important, constrained gene, but PoPS prioritization is unsupported by
> locus-specific L2G (0.068, far below MTMR11) and the locus's best-replicated common-variant
> phenotype is anthropometric, not pulmonary. 'False-positive' is stated with more certainty than the
> evidence supports [in the researcher's original write-up]; 'non-specific/likely-uncoupled PoPS
> signal' is the more defensible framing. Lung-cancer-cell-line functional data give nonzero but

### MTMR11 — INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE

> "Top statistical (L2G) signal at both COPD and FEV1/FVC credible sets, but essentially zero
> dedicated mechanistic/developmental/lung literature, LoF-tolerant, and — most importantly — the same
> gene tops L2G at >150 unrelated credible sets for this variant, a pattern more consistent with a
> positional/technical L2G artifact at this densely-packed locus than trait-specific causal biology.
> Should not be promoted to 'top candidate' on statistics alone without acknowledging this artifact

### SV2A — INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE

> "No lung, spirometry, or direct smoking-literature link found. Smoking-mediation is honestly flagged

## 3. Methodological caveat on the original critique

The red-team-skeptic subagent's WebSearch budget was reported exhausted before it could run any
independent searches for this locus's original review; its critique above is based entirely on
internal-consistency analysis of the researcher's findings and the structured Open Targets context,
not on independent literature retrieval. It explicitly flagged three follow-up searches it was
unable to run: (1) "OTUD7B airway epithelium COPD emphysema bronchial" (to check for
lower-airway-specific mechanistic work beyond the single 2026 transcriptomic study); (2) whether
MTMR11's broad L2G-winning pattern has been discussed elsewhere as a known artifact of this LD block;
(3) "VPS45 COPD GWAS credible set L2G" (to confirm VPS45 truly has zero locus-specific statistical
support, as assumed from its absence in the Open Targets context provided). None of these were
executed, so the corresponding critique points should be read as well-reasoned hypotheses rather than
independently confirmed findings. No new citations were asserted as fact by the critique, so no
additional citation-verifier dispatch was required for this locus at that time.

## 4. ADDENDUM (2026-09-11): H3C15 targeted re-opening — full audit trail

### 4a. Why H3C15 was investigated

A genome-build error in the original MAGMA/PoPS gene-location merge file caused H3C15 (a
replication-dependent, canonical H3.1/H3.2-type histone gene, formerly named HIST2H3A; Ensembl
ENSG00000203852) to be omitted from this locus's original candidate-gene list. Per the re-opening
protocol, the orchestrator independently confirmed H3C15's genomic coordinates
(chr1:149,852,608-149,853,125, GRCh38) via Open Targets/Ensembl before proceeding — this confirmed
H3C15 **genuinely, physically overlaps** the locus window (1:149,850,001-150,100,000), ~68-170 kb
from the three credible-set variants. This is not an artifact of the build-error bug: the original
review's own regional Ensembl enumeration had already independently identified H3C15 at the same
coordinates, but only noted it collectively (with four other histone-cluster genes) as lacking any
documented lung/COPD-specific biology, without a dedicated per-gene write-up. Because H3C15
genuinely overlaps the locus, it proceeded to full researcher dispatch rather than being ruled out
at the coordinate-check stage.

### 4b. Open Targets structured lookup for H3C15 (not a citation — see full detail in
`evidence_tables/_status/1_149850001-150100000/opentargets.md`)

Independently queried by the orchestrator prior to researcher dispatch: H3C15 has only 11 total
Open Targets disease associations (vs. 283 for MTMR11, 107 for OTUD7B), none COPD/respiratory/
smoking-related, all `affected_pathway`- or `literature`-datatype-derived (zero
`genetic_association`-datatype contribution). H3C15 has **zero credible sets assigned to it
anywhere in Open Targets, for any trait, genome-wide** — a stronger and qualitatively different null
than any other candidate gene at this locus. No gnomAD constraint metrics are available (a general
limitation of short, intronless replication-dependent histone genes).

### 4c. H3C15 citations — all VERIFIED (no exclusions)

All 11 citations returned by the researcher for H3C15 were independently verified by
citation-verifier with verdict VERIFIED (full output in
`evidence_tables/_status/1_149850001-150100000/verified_citations.md`, Batch D). No RETRACTED,
NOT_FOUND, or CLAIM_MISMATCH verdicts. Two citations with unusually high PMIDs and future (2026)
publication dates were flagged for extra scrutiny and confirmed genuine: Wu et al. 2026 (Chem Biol
Drug Des, PMID 41637659) and Mohiuddin et al. 2026 (Front Immunol, PMID 41993174).

**Important caveat preserved from citation-verifier's own summary:** "Critically, as the
researcher's own write-up already made explicit, none of these 9 NETosis/DAMP citations (#2-#11,
excluding #1 which is gene-identity-confirmation only) establish H3C15-specific evidence — all
detect/manipulate the pooled histone H3 protein epitope (citrullinated H3, or generic 'histones' as
DAMPs) without any ability to attribute signal to the H3C15 gene locus specifically, as opposed to
any of its ~10-15 near-identical canonical H3.1/H3.2 paralogs." Verification of accurate
claim-correspondence to each source paper (i.e., VERIFIED status) is therefore not the same as
verification of H3C15-gene-specific relevance — this distinction is deliberate and load-bearing for
how the evidence table above weights these rows.

### 4d. H3C15 — red-team-skeptic verdict: INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE (does not disturb the MTMR11/OTUD7B
tie)

Full verbatim critique preserved in
`evidence_tables/_status/1_149850001-150100000/critique.md` (addendum section). Key excerpts:

On the family-level-vs-gene-specific distinction:

> "Yes, and I'd endorse it as the single most important move in this dossier. Anti-citrullinated-H3
> (citH3) antibodies used in essentially all cited NETosis papers... target conserved arginine
> residues (R2/R8/R17/R26) present identically across ~10-15 canonical H3.1/H3.2 paralogs... There is
> no protein-level or transcript-level way these assays could have detected H3C15 specifically rather
> than pooled H3... I find no overreach in how this distinction is drawn."

On whether H3C15's total genome-wide statistical null is comparable to SV2A's "wrong disease domain"
pattern:

> "Qualitatively different, and worse for H3C15. SV2A has a real, substantial, well-replicated GWAS
> signal — it's just for a different (CNS/neurological) trait domain, which is a 'wrong-neighborhood'
> problem, not a 'no genetic association exists' problem. H3C15 has zero
> genetic-association-datatype contribution to any of its 11 Open Targets disease associations... and
> zero credible-set assignments genome-wide. This is a true statistical-genetics null, not a
> redirection to another phenotype. That makes H3C15 evidentially weaker than SV2A, not comparable to
> it."

On whether H3C15 changes the tie:

> "No. H3C15 does not become a credible third contender, and it should not disturb the existing tie...
> H3C15 slots in below OTUD7B and well below MTMR11, materially on par with or slightly below SV2A...
> i.e., it belongs in the same INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE tier as BOLA1/VPS45/SV2A, not as a third pole in
> the tie. The genome-build error that surfaced H3C15 is worth noting for the record (it confirms
> H3C15's coordinates genuinely fall inside the window, so this isn't a spurious inclusion), but
> 'genuinely physically present' is a necessary, not sufficient, condition for candidacy — plenty of
> genes in a 250kb window will be true bystanders. Nothing here should soften the LOW-confidence
> MTMR11 call or elevate OTUD7B."

Final verdict (verbatim):

> "**H3C15: INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE.** Genuine physical presence in the locus, a real and
> well-documented family-level connection between citrullinated-H3 NETosis biology and COPD
> pathophysiology, but zero H3C15-gene-specific genetic association (no L2G, no credible-set
> assignment genome-wide, no eQTL/TWAS/MR/colocalization literature) and zero H3C15-specific (as
> opposed to pan-H3-paralog) mechanistic literature. This is a weaker profile than OTUD7B and much
> weaker than MTMR11's L2G-based (if artifact-suspected) signal. Does not disturb the
> MTMR11-OTUD7B tie."

**Overclaiming-risk warning (verbatim, important for anyone revising this synthesis further):**

> "High risk, and worth flagging explicitly. The NETosis/citH3-COPD literature... is genuinely
> strong, mechanistically clear, and even therapeutically actionable (roflumilast is an approved
> COPD drug shown to reduce NETosis) — which makes it narratively very tempting to fold into an
> H3C15 writeup as if it were gene-specific supporting evidence. A synthesis pass that lists
> 'NETosis/citrullinated histone H3 implicated in COPD pathology (Obermayer 2014; Totani 2021)' under
> an 'H3C15 supporting evidence' heading, without the paralog-indistinguishability caveat attached at
> every mention, would constitute exactly the citation-verifier-flagged pattern this pipeline is
> designed to prevent... I recommend the synthesis-lead either (a) omit the NETosis literature from
> the H3C15 entry entirely and note only that it exists as family-level context, or (b) if included,
> prefix every sentence with an explicit 'pooled/pan-paralog H3, not H3C15-specific' qualifier...
> The two 2026-dated papers (Wu et al., PMID 41637659; and the Mohiuddin et al. general-DAMP review,
> PMID 41993174) are especially likely candidates for this kind of quiet upgrading in a future pass...
> flag them by name for extra scrutiny at synthesis time."

This synthesis followed option (b): every H3C15 NETosis/DAMP row in the evidence table carries an
explicit "gene-family-level... NOT H3C15-gene-specific" qualifier, and the Prioritized-gene section
states plainly that H3C15 was evaluated and ruled out rather than silently omitted.
