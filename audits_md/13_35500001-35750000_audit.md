# Audit trail — locus 13:35,500,001-35,750,000 (NBEA)

This is the excluded/contested appendix for this locus. It is kept separate from
`evidence_tables/13_35500001-35750000.md` so that evidence tables can be shared without also
sharing the full audit trail.

## Excluded citations (RETRACTED / NOT_FOUND / CLAIM_MISMATCH)

All five citations below were checked directly by the citation-verifier against full text
(where available via PMC) or targeted searches. All five papers **exist and are not
retracted** — the issue is that none of them support the NBEA-specific claim the researcher
tentatively attached to them (the researcher had already flagged this as unconfirmed prior
to verification). Verdict category used: NOT_FOUND, applied to the specific claim
("NBEA/this locus is a reported signal in this paper"), not to the paper's existence.

1. **Sakornsakolpat P, et al. "Genetic landscape of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease
   identifies heterogeneous cell-type and phenotype associations." Nature Genetics
   2019;51(3):494-505.** PMID 30804561, DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0342-2.
   Verdict: **NOT_FOUND** (NBEA among reported loci). The paper exists, is not retracted,
   and does identify 82 COPD risk loci (verified via PMC6546635 full text), but **NBEA is
   not mentioned anywhere in the paper, and chromosome 13q13.3 (35.5-35.75 Mb) is not among
   the reported 82 loci.** The closest chromosome 13 locus reported is SERP2 at 13q14.11,
   outside the NBEA region. Excluded from the evidence table as non-supporting.

2. **Shrine N, et al. "New genetic signals for lung function highlight pathways and chronic
   obstructive pulmonary disease associations across multiple ancestries." Nature Genetics
   2019;51(3):481-493.** PMID 30804560, DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0321-7.
   Verdict: **NOT_FOUND** (NBEA among reported signals). Paper exists (has 2019 and 2024
   errata, not retracted) and defines 279 lung-function signals (verified via PMC6397078
   full text), but **NBEA is not mentioned and 13q13.3 is not among the reported signals.**
   Only DOCK9 (13:99,665,512) is reported on chromosome 13, well outside the NBEA region.
   Excluded from the evidence table as non-supporting.

3. **Shrine N, et al. "Multi-ancestry genome-wide association analyses improve resolution
   of genes and pathways influencing lung function and chronic obstructive pulmonary
   disease risk." Nature Genetics 2023;55:410-422.** PMID 36914875, DOI
   10.1038/s41588-023-01314-0.
   Verdict: **NOT_FOUND** (NBEA among the 559 mapped genes). Paper exists (October 2023
   erratum, not retracted) and reports 1,020 signals/559 causal genes (verified via
   PMC10011137 full text), but **NBEA does not appear anywhere in the text or among the 559
   mapped genes.** Genes highlighted include TGFB2, NPNT, LTBP4, FGFR1, GATA5, ABCA3,
   CLDN18 — not NBEA. This is the single most decisive available check for whether NBEA has
   independent lung-function GWAS support (per biological prior #2), and it is negative.
   Excluded from the evidence table as non-supporting.

4. **Liu M, Jiang Y, Wedow R, et al. "Association studies of up to 1.2 million individuals
   yield new insights into the genetic etiology of tobacco and alcohol use." Nature
   Genetics 2019;51(2):237-244.** PMID 30643251, DOI 10.1038/s41588-018-0307-5.
   Verdict: **NOT_FOUND** (NBEA/rs403904 for smoking). Paper exists, not retracted, reports
   566 variants/406 loci (verified via PMC6358542 full text), but **NBEA, the
   13:35.5-35.75 Mb region, and rs403904 are not mentioned and do not appear among the
   reported loci.** Excluded from the evidence table as non-supporting.

5. **Saunders GRB, Wang X, Chen F, et al. "Genetic diversity fuels gene discovery for
   tobacco and alcohol use." Nature 2022;612(7941):720-724.** PMID 36477530, DOI
   10.1038/s41586-022-05477-4.
   Verdict: **NOT_FOUND** (NBEA for smoking). Paper exists, not retracted, reports 3,823
   variants/2,143 loci (verified via PMC9771818 full text), but **NBEA, 13q13.3, and
   rs403904 are not mentioned and do not appear among the reported smoking/tobacco loci.**
   This was the single most important open item flagged for this locus (the researcher
   hypothesized this paper as the likely source of the Open-Targets-reported NBEA-smoking-
   initiation association); it is negative. Excluded from the evidence table as
   non-supporting.

**Material consequence of this finding:** NBEA does not appear, by name or by region, in any
of the three major prior COPD/lung-function GWAS (Sakornsakolpat 2019, Shrine 2019, Shrine
2023) or either of the two major smoking/tobacco-use GWAS (GSCAN 2019, Saunders 2022) that
were checked. This is independent, verified negative evidence bearing directly on this
locus's novelty and on the smoking-mediation hypothesis (prior #1) — see the CONTESTED
determination below and the Prioritized Gene section of the main evidence table.

## CONTESTED gene determination (red-team-skeptic, verbatim reasoning preserved)

**Gene: NBEA — marked CONTESTED** (with respect to a COPD-relevant pulmonary or
smoking-behavior mechanism specifically; NOT contested as the correct candidate gene at this
locus, since it is the only protein-coding gene present and all internal methods converge on
it).

Full verbatim critique from red-team-skeptic:

> ## 1. "Smoking-behavior/neurobiological mechanism" hypothesis — speculative overreach
>
> This is not adequately supported and should not be presented as a mechanism, only as a
> hypothesis. The chain of evidence is: rs403904 is a verified brain-tissue (CommonMind
> DLPFC, ROSMAP) exon-usage/sQTL for NBEA -> NBEA is a verified, well-established
> neurodevelopmental/epilepsy gene via LoF/haploinsufficiency -> Open Targets lists "smoking
> initiation" in NBEA's own top-25 disease/trait association list (aggregate score 0.323).
> But the citation-verifier independently checked all five candidate GWAS papers the
> researcher cited as likely sources for that smoking link (Sakornsakolpat 2019, Shrine
> 2019, Shrine 2023, Liu/GSCAN 2019, Saunders 2022) and NBEA is not a reported locus/gene in
> any of them. That means the only thing supporting "NBEA -> smoking behavior" is an
> unsourced, unverified Open Targets aggregate score — not a peer-reviewed GWAS, MR, or TWAS
> finding. Presenting this as a "smoking-mediated" mechanism would be overclaiming; at most
> it is a hypothesis-generating database flag that failed independent literature
> confirmation. The brain-tissue sQTL evidence is real molecular biology, but it establishes
> tissue plausibility (brain-expressed regulatory effect), not a validated
> smoking-phenotype association.
>
> ## 2. First-time discovery weight / winner's-curse alternative
>
> This is a live and, in my view, the leading alternative explanation. Three considerations
> converge:
> - The credible-set PIP tops out at 0.52 on a single variant, with signal split across two
>   3'UTR variants — this is a modest, non-dominant single-variant PIP, consistent with
>   either a real but weak/diffuse regulatory signal or a locus where fine-mapping hasn't
>   converged on one causal variant, which itself is compatible with residual LD/statistical
>   noise.
> - NBEA does not appear in any of the three large, well-powered prior GWAS most likely to
>   have caught it if it were a robust COPD or lung-function locus: Sakornsakolpat 2019 (82
>   COPD loci), Shrine 2019 (279 lung-function signals), and Shrine 2023 (559 lung-function
>   genes). These are not underpowered or narrowly scoped studies — they are the field's
>   benchmark meta-analyses for exactly these phenotypes. Non-appearance in all three,
>   verified independently by the citation-verifier, is meaningful negative evidence, not
>   merely "absence of evidence."
> - No lung/airway expression or eQTL/sQTL literature for NBEA exists at all (per the
>   researcher's search), and Open Targets shows no lung-function trait in NBEA's own
>   top-25 trait list and no COPD-specific L2G evidence.
>
> Taken together, this pattern (modest/split PIP + non-replication across three major
> independent prior GWAS + zero lung biology of any kind) is exactly what a false-positive
> or winner's-curse locus in a new internal GWAS looks like. I would weight this as at least
> as plausible as a genuine novel discovery, and flag it explicitly as an alternative
> hypothesis for synthesis rather than treating "first report" as inherently exciting.
>
> ## 3. CONTESTED vs WEAKENED
>
> I recommend CONTESTED, not merely WEAKENED, on the specific point of a COPD/pulmonary
> mechanism — because the non-replication in three major, directly relevant, well-powered
> prior GWAS constitutes active contradicting evidence for a robust lung-function/COPD
> signal at this locus, not just a gap in supporting evidence. This is different from an
> ordinary "no literature yet" situation: these are the exact studies (COPD case-control,
> two lung-function meta-analyses) that should have flagged this locus if the true effect
> size/frequency were consistent with what the current GWAS suggests.
>
> That said, the NBEA-as-a-real-gene biology (LoF constraint, neurodevelopmental/epilepsy
> role, brain sQTL at the lead variant) is solid and correctly verified — the problem is not
> that these citations are wrong, it's that they are being asked to carry weight for a
> phenotype (COPD/smoking) and variant class (common 3'UTR regulatory variant) they don't
> actually speak to. The three neurodevelopmental papers (Mulhern 2018, Boulin 2021, Huang
> 2025) describe rare, large-effect LoF variants causing haploinsufficiency-driven
> neurodevelopmental disease — this is a different variant class and effect-size regime from
> a common noncoding 3'UTR credible-set variant with PIP 0.52 in a population GWAS. Citing
> rare-LoF developmental biology to lend mechanistic plausibility to a common-variant
> COPD/smoking association is a class mismatch that should be flagged explicitly if it
> appears in the write-up, even though the individual citations are accurately characterized
> in isolation (which is exactly the kind of overreach-in-translation the review process is
> designed to catch).
>
> ## 4. Alternative explanations for the credible-set signal (to name explicitly for
> synthesis)
>
> - Statistical/regional false positive (winner's curse): discussed above; the leading
>   alternative given non-replication in three major prior GWAS and a non-dominant PIP.
> - LD with an unmeasured functional variant elsewhere: partially mitigated by Ensembl
>   confirming NBEA is the sole protein-coding gene in the 250kb window and by
>   FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS/nearest-gene convergence, but not eliminated — regulatory elements can
>   act at a distance, and non-protein-coding elements (lncRNAs, enhancers) in the interval
>   were not explicitly ruled out.
> - QTL effect on an unannotated NBEA transcript/isoform in an unstudied tissue: plausible
>   and, notably, not fully closed off by the evidence presented. [Orchestrator follow-up:
>   this was subsequently checked directly — see below.]
> - Genuine unappreciated NBEA biology in lung/immune tissue: cannot be ruled out. NBEA is a
>   BEACH-domain-containing trafficking protein; other BEACH-domain family members (e.g.,
>   LRBA) have well-documented roles in immune regulation (CTLA-4 trafficking, immune
>   dysregulation phenotypes), which is at least a plausible biological rationale for an
>   unstudied immune role of NBEA. This is flagged as an unsourced hypothesis worth a
>   targeted follow-up search, not as evidence — not a citation, and not used in synthesis.
>
> ## Overall determination
>
> CONTESTED — the gene-level biology (LoF constraint, neurodevelopmental role, brain sQTL)
> is real and correctly verified, but the specific inference that this locus reflects a
> COPD-relevant pulmonary or smoking-behavior mechanism is actively contradicted by
> non-replication across three major, well-powered prior COPD/lung-function GWAS, and the
> only smoking-trait link (Open Targets aggregate score) failed independent confirmation
> against the two largest smoking-GWAS papers checked. Winner's-curse/statistical artifact
> is a live, arguably leading, alternative explanation that should be presented to
> synthesis-lead on equal footing with "novel discovery."

### Orchestrator follow-up to the red-team's thoroughness flag

The red-team-skeptic asked whether a direct lung/immune-tissue eQTL/sQTL check for NBEA
(parallel to the Open Targets brain-QTL finding) had actually been performed. The
orchestrator ran this check directly against Open Targets' full QTL credible-set catalogue
for NBEA (104 credible sets across all tissues/cell types). Result: NBEA does have a bulk
lung eQTL (GTEx, lead variant rs562861, 13:35,106,321) and multiple immune-cell eQTLs
(BLUEPRINT monocytes; Schmiedel 2018 Th1/Th2/Th17/Tfh/Treg; Nathan 2022 and OneK1K
single-cell CD4/NK) in Open Targets — so lung/immune QTL resources for NBEA do exist and
were queried. However, none of these lung/immune eQTL signals are fine-mapped to, or overlap,
this locus's credible-set variants (rs403904/rs374452); their lead variants are all tens to
hundreds of kb away (closest ~74 kb, the NK-cell eQTL). Only the brain exon-usage/splicing
QTL (CommonMind DLPFC, ROSMAP) is fine-mapped specifically to rs403904/rs374452. This
confirms the brain-restricted finding is a genuine feature of which tissue's QTL signal
coincides with this locus's specific variants, not an artifact of unconsulted resources —
while still not proving the complete absence of a pulmonary/immune effect at this exact
variant, since QTL study power varies substantially by tissue and sample size. Full detail:
see the ADDENDUM section of `evidence_tables/_status/13_35500001-35750000/opentargets.md`.
