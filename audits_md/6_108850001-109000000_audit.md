# Audit trail — locus 6:108,850,001-109,000,000 (ARMC2)

This is the excluded-citation and contested-gene appendix for
`evidence_tables/6_108850001-109000000.md`. It exists so the evidence table can be
distributed without also handing out this audit trail.

## Excluded citations (CLAIM_MISMATCH / NOT_FOUND)

1. **Sakornsakolpat P, Prokopenko D, Lamontagne M, et al. "Genetic landscape of chronic
   obstructive pulmonary disease identifies heterogeneous cell-type and phenotype
   associations." Nature Genetics, 2019.** PMID 30804561 / PMC6546635 / DOI
   10.1038/s41588-018-0342-2.
   **Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH.** Abstract confirms 82 COPD GWAS loci were identified
   (35,735 cases/222,076 controls, UK Biobank + ICGC) but does NOT explicitly name ARMC2 or
   rs2798641. The researcher had flagged this citation as only a plausible, unconfirmed
   source of the ARMC2-tagged lung-function associations; citation-verifier could not
   confirm the gene/variant is explicitly named at this locus from the abstract text
   (supplementary tables were not checked). Excluded from the evidence table because the
   claim as originally framed (this paper supports ARMC2 at this locus) is not confirmed.

2. **Shrine N, Guyatt AL, Erzurumluoglu AM, et al. "New genetic signals for lung function
   highlight pathways and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease associations across
   multiple ancestries." Nature Genetics, 2019.** PMID 30804560 / PMC6397078 / DOI
   10.1038/s41588-018-0321-7.
   **Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH.** Abstract confirms 279 lung-function signals (400,102
   individuals, European ancestry) but does NOT explicitly name ARMC2 or rs2798641.
   Note: article has 2 published errata but is not retracted. Same exclusion rationale as
   above — this is very likely (per the researcher's own hedged framing) one of the source
   studies behind the GWAS Catalog's ARMC2 gene-mapping for rs2798641, but that specific
   attribution is unconfirmed and should not be presented as verified literature support.

3. **Markelov VA, Korytina GF, Aznabaeva YG, et al. "[Expression of Long Noncoding RNAs and
   Protein-Coding Genes Involved in Oxidative Stress and Cell Senescence in Patients with
   COPD]." Molekuliarnaia Biologiia (Mosk), 2024.** PMID 39970122 / DOI
   10.31857/S0026898424050119.
   **Verdict: CLAIM_MISMATCH.** The researcher's claim was that "FOXO3 mRNA levels
   positively correlated with lung-function parameters" specifically; citation-verifier
   found the abstract lists FOXO3 among a multi-gene panel found to correlate with
   lung-function parameters but does not individually specify FOXO3's own direction/
   magnitude of correlation. Excluded pending full-text confirmation of the FOXO3-specific
   finding.

4. **Giordani E, Houdeline S, Court M, et al. "ARMC2 loss impairs cilia structure and leads
   to primary ciliary dyskinesia symptoms in mouse organs." Frontiers in Cell and
   Developmental Biology, 2026.** PMID 42272638 / PMC13246696 / DOI
   10.3389/fcell.2026.1695239.
   **Verdict: NOT_FOUND.** Citation-verifier could not resolve this citation in PubMed;
   assessed as likely fabricated/hallucinated (the 2026 publication date on a paper the
   researcher itself flagged as needing confirmation is a red flag). This was the single
   most consequential exclusion in this locus's review: it was the researcher's only cited
   evidence for a *direct* in vivo airway/tracheal ciliary phenotype in ARMC2-deficient
   mice (impaired ciliary beating, tracheal mucus accumulation, PCD-like features). Its
   removal downgrades the ARMC2 mechanistic case from "confirmed direct airway mechanism"
   to "plausible mechanism by analogy from sperm-flagellum and algal-flagellum biology,
   unconfirmed in mammalian airway" — see red-team-skeptic critique below and the
   Prioritized Gene section of the evidence table for the full discussion. **This citation
   must not be used or cited anywhere downstream of this pipeline.**

## Additional PMIDs noted by researcher but never submitted for verification

The researcher flagged six additional PMIDs (40158138, 39417902, 38492154, 35543806,
34493464, 31621862) as redundant MMAF/ARMC2-infertility genetics papers, not individually
pulled for full metadata since they reiterate the same core Coutton et al. 2019 finding
(biallelic ARMC2 loss-of-function causing MMAF/asthenozoospermia). These were not sent to
citation-verifier and are not used anywhere in the evidence table; listed here only for
completeness/spot-check if a future reviewer wants to check them.

## Contested / weakened gene verdicts (red-team-skeptic)

Red-team-skeptic did not find any citation-level or literature-level evidence actively
contradicting either serious candidate gene at this locus, and explicitly declined to mark
either gene CONTESTED in the strict sense ("credible contradicting evidence found that
argues against this gene or for a different gene"). Both serious candidates were instead
marked **WEAKENED**, for distinct reasons — reasoning preserved verbatim below (full critique
also in `evidence_tables/_status/6_108850001-109000000/critique.md`):

### ARMC2 — WEAKENED

> "The statistical genetics (L2G, GWAS-GWAS colocalization, intronic position of all three
> credible-set variants) remains genuinely strong and this is very likely the correct gene
> at this locus by position/statistics. But the "mechanistic" case for a lung/airway-ciliary
> mechanism specifically has collapsed to inference-by-analogy (sperm flagellum, algal
> flagellum) now that the one direct-evidence citation (Giordani, PMID 42272638) is
> confirmed fabricated/NOT_FOUND, and the "reduced lung function" phrase in the surviving
> Lechtreck citation is likely circular back-citation to the same GWAS papers already
> separately flagged as CLAIM_MISMATCH rather than independent corroboration. Synthesis-lead
> should present ARMC2 as "strong positional/statistical candidate, mechanism in lung tissue
> not established," not as a gene with confirmed structural biology in the airway."

Additional points raised: (1) no eQTL/pQTL/sQTL colocalization exists for ARMC2 at this
locus (explicitly checked, absent) — so there is no expression-based functional-genomics
confirmation that ARMC2 expression specifically mediates the signal; (2) ARMC2's known
disease biology (MMAF) is a *recessive* loss-of-function phenotype restricted to the sperm
flagellum, a different zygosity and tissue context from what a common noncoding GWAS variant
of modest effect on lung function would need to be doing (most plausibly a partial
regulatory/expression effect in airway epithelium specifically — an alternative hypothesis
not directly tested by any evidence gathered here); (3) an independent PubMed check by the
skeptic for "ARMC2 respiratory/airway phenotype in MMAF patients" returned zero hits,
consistent with no published clinical report of respiratory/PCD-like symptoms in ARMC2-mutant
MMAF patients despite this being a well-studied recessive disease since 2019 — noted as mild
contradicting context, not proof of no effect.

### FOXO3 — WEAKENED

> "All the FOXO3 mechanistic literature (senescence/autophagy/SIRT-AMPK-FOXO3 axis, immune
> hub-gene analyses) is real, verified, and biologically coherent for a general
> "aging/senescence/oxidative-stress" contribution to COPD pathophysiology broadly, but none
> of it is tied to this specific locus/credible set, is smoke-exposure-response biology
> (arguably downstream consequence of disease/exposure rather than a germline causal
> mechanism), and is contradicted by the absence of any L2G/coloc/fine-mapping signal for
> FOXO3 at this locus. This is not evidence that FOXO3 is wrong as a general COPD-pathway
> gene — it's evidence that FOXO3 is not well supported as the causal gene *at this GWAS
> locus specifically*."

The skeptic also pushed back mildly on dismissing PoPS too quickly: "PoPS... is not nothing,
and dismissing it because 'L2G doesn't support it' is somewhat circular, since PoPS and L2G
are different, partially non-redundant methods... it should be reported as 'one method
flagged this gene; the other major statistical method and all mechanistic/variant-specific
literature checks failed to corroborate it,' not silently dropped." The skeptic separately
flagged that the Becher 2018 (Treg/FOXO3-FOXP3) and Parvin 2025 (mustard-gas PBMC hub genes)
immune citations, while verified as existing and accurately described, are both
non-COPD-specific (autoimmune/tumor immunology, and a chemical-injury lung-disease proxy
respectively) and should never be presented without that caveat attached.

### SESN1 — not contested

No independent search was performed by the skeptic (concurring with the researcher's own
treatment of SESN1 as a non-serious candidate given its very low Open Targets L2G score
~0.06 and the complete absence of COPD/lung-specific literature).

## Coverage-gap disclosure (from red-team-skeptic)

The skeptic disclosed that its own WebSearch quota was exhausted session-wide before it
could run a full contradiction-search pass on both ARMC2 and FOXO3; it substituted a small
number of direct PubMed WebFetch queries as a fallback (all returned zero hits, which is
itself informative but is "a shallow, single-pass check on each gene, not an exhaustive
contradiction search"). This is flagged here as a known coverage gap for any future,
more thorough re-review of this locus.
