Prioritized gene

FAM13A — Confidence: HIGH.

FAM13A is the clear best-supported causal gene at this locus. The evidentiary basis is unusually deep and multi-modal, and none of it was displaced by verification or critique: (1) every one of the 33 credible-set variants, including the lead variant rs7671167, falls within FAM13A's introns; (2) rs7671167 is the exact lead SNP from the original 2010 discovery GWAS (Cho et al., PMID 20173748), independently replicated across ~20 COPD GWAS/biobank studies and ~14 FEV1/FVC studies per Open Targets L2G (scores 0.71-0.86 and 0.84-0.85 respectively) — a level of cross-study, cross-trait genetic concordance far beyond typical single-study support, satisfying the spirometry-concordance prior directly; (3) a specific credible-set variant (rs2013701) has direct functional/CRISPR validation of allele-specific effects on FAM13A expression (Guo et al., PMID 30079747); (4) an interventional Fam13a-null mouse model shows resistance to both smoke- and elastase-induced emphysema, with a defined PP2A/GSK3β/β-catenin mechanism (Jiang et al., PMID 26862784); (5) a direct empirical test (Pillai et al., PMID 20656943) found FAM13A's effect on lung function is not associated with smoking intensity, unlike CHRNA3/5 — directly addressing the smoking-mediation prior, and consistent with the weak Open Targets smoking-initiation L2G signal for FAM13A; (6) both PKD2 and SPP1, the MAGMA/PoPS-nominated alternatives, physically lie 670kb-1.3Mb outside the locus window and away from the credible set, have zero L2G evidence for COPD/FEV1-FVC/smoking at any GWAS study in Open Targets, and their supporting literature describes a different gene (PKD1, not PKD2, in ADPKD) or non-specific downstream inflammatory-biomarker biology (SPP1), respectively — both marked CONTESTED by red-team critique (see audit).

This HIGH confidence call is tempered, not undermined, by three points the skeptic correctly raised: (a) FAM13A is also an IPF/pulmonary-fibrosis and interstitial-lung-abnormality susceptibility locus with an opposite risk-allele direction relative to COPD (Hobbs et al. 2017, PMID 28166215; Guzmán-Vargas et al. 2021, PMID 34490311) — the mechanism is better described as "FAM13A modulates lung epithelial repair/regeneration in a context-dependent way that can manifest as either emphysematous destruction or fibrotic remodeling" rather than a simple unidirectional damage narrative; (b) two genuine non-replications exist (Hardin et al. 2012 in a severe-COPD-enriched Polish cohort, PMID 22461431; Kim et al. 2014 in a Korean family-based replication, PMID 24387323); and (c) whether FAM13A reaches genome-wide significance for smoking initiation in the standard GSCAN reference GWAS (Liu et al. 2019, PMID 30643251) remains genuinely unresolved — the citation-verifier could not confirm this either way from the abstract, so the smoking-mediation conclusion, while well-supported by multiple other lines of evidence, is not airtight on this one specific point. Additionally, the immune/inflammatory evidence base for FAM13A itself (as opposed to the locus-class eQTL enhancer-enrichment finding in Castaldi et al. 2015) is thin and largely indirect — the dominant, well-supported mechanism is structural/epithelial/developmental (Wnt/β-catenin, mucociliary function, cellular senescence, TGFβ2 trafficking), not immune-cell-intrinsic, and any synthesis narrative should reflect that rather than lean on an inflammatory story this evidence base does not support.

None of these caveats create a genuine tie with PKD2 or SPP1 — the gap between FAM13A's evidence and either alternative is not close. The confidence level is HIGH for gene identity; the caveats above concern mechanism and generalizability, not which gene is causal.
