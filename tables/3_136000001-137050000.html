Prioritized gene

STAG1 — confidence: MODERATE (genetics/fine-mapping-only; no mechanistic or lung-tissue confirmation)

STAG1 is the best-supported candidate at this locus, but the support is entirely statistical/ positional, not mechanistic. It is simultaneously the nearest gene to the lead variant, and independently prioritized by FLAMES, MAGMA, and PoPS — a four-way convergence that is unusually strong for this pipeline. The lead variant (rs145172807, PIP=0.189) and the majority of the 212-variant credible set's PIP mass sit in STAG1/STAG1-DT intronic variants. Per the Open Targets summary above, STAG1 carries the 2nd-highest L2G-like score at the locus (0.370) and is strongly constrained against both loss-of-function (o/e 0.085) and missense variation (o/e 0.644), consistent with an essential, dosage-sensitive gene — a plausible substrate for a common regulatory variant to act on. Its only rival by raw score, PCCB (L2G-like 0.374, the locus's single highest), is explicitly rejected by both the researcher and skeptic as very likely a database-aggregation artifact: all PCCB credible-set variants carry negligible PIP (≤0.0019), PCCB is a recessive metabolic-disease gene with no LoF-constraint signal and no COPD-relevant literature, and the one lung-adjacent citation found (He et al. 2021, PMID 34635437) is incidental organ-panel biochemistry in an unrelated rare-disease mouse model, not evidence of common-variant lung causality (see audit for full reasoning).

Against STAG1, however, is a genuine and important gap: no COPD-GWAS, lung-function-GWAS (FEV1/FEV1-FVC), TWAS, eQTL, or colocalization literature was found for STAG1 despite dedicated searching, and the one behavioral citation retrieved (Arnau-Soler et al. 2019, PMID 30718454) is a whole-genome PRS cross-trait correlation that the skeptic explicitly says should carry near-zero weight as COPD-specific evidence — it is not being used to support this call. STAG1's established biology (cohesin complex, sister-chromatid cohesion, chromatin looping; de novo LoF/missense variants cause a neurodevelopmental cohesinopathy) provides no pulmonary phenotype in any characterized patient, and no molecular-QTL colocalization data could be retrieved via Open Targets for this gene (a tooling limitation, not confirmed absence). No gene at this locus — including STAG1 — has any smoking-behavior-trait or spirometric-trait literature to help distinguish a lung-biology-mediated versus smoking-behavior-mediated mechanism, which per the pipeline's biological priors should keep the whole locus's gene-assignment confidence capped at MODERATE at best regardless of which single gene is named.

Runner-up: none of the remaining candidates offer a comparably strong case. PPP2R3A has the locus's only lung-structural mechanistic literature (fibroblast proliferation in silicosis; PASMC proliferation in pulmonary hypertension) but very weak statistical support (low PIP, no LoF/missense constraint) and both mechanisms are non-COPD lung pathologies (fibrosis, vascular remodeling) rather than airway obstruction/emphysema. NCK1 carries the most credible-set variants by raw count and a specific, well-replicated T-cell-signaling literature (TCR proximal signaling, Treg development), but the skeptic flags this as generic T-cell immunology being stretched to fit the pipeline's inflammatory prior with zero COPD- or lung-specific data, and NCK1 is explicitly not LoF-constrained — a weaker case than STAG1's. MSL2 (ABC-prioritized) and SLC35G2 (carries the only missense/synonymous variants) are both weak on both statistical and literature grounds. IL20RB, added post hoc by the researcher via region enumeration, has no directly-annotated credible-set variant and was downgraded by the skeptic from "candidate" to "positional note only."

What would resolve this close call and raise confidence: (1) a lung-tissue or airway-epithelium eQTL/sQTL colocalization study for STAG1 at this locus (not currently retrievable via Open Targets or literature); (2) a direct lookup of this locus/lead variant against published FEV1/FEV1-FVC or COPD case-control GWAS summary statistics for concordance; (3) resolution of whether rs145172807 or other high-PIP STAG1/STAG1-DT variants show allele-specific chromatin activity (e.g. ATAC-seq/Hi-C) in lung tissue or airway cell types; (4) a smoking-initiation/ cessation GWAS lookup to rule in or out a behavioral-mediation pathway, given the sole (weak) behavioral hit found. The study team should weigh this recommendation accordingly — this is a statistically well-supported but mechanistically unconfirmed pick, not a settled one.
