Prioritized-gene synthesis

Prioritized gene: SLC28A1. Confidence: INSUFFICIENT.

No gene at this locus has genetic-association, spirometric-GWAS, TWAS, colocalization, or Mendelian-randomization evidence linking it to COPD or lung function — Open Targets records zero COPD association for SLC28A1, and the researcher's PubMed searches found none for SLC28A1, PDE8A, ALPK3, ZNF592, or SEC11A. Open Targets' L2G/colocalization data could not even be retrieved for this locus (the credible-set variants are absent from Open Targets' variant index), so the single most decisive structured-data source for gene assignment is simply unavailable here.

I still name SLC28A1 as the nominal top pick because it is the only candidate with any positional claim on the credible set itself: the researcher's independent Ensembl VEP re-check found the lead variant and the entire 11-SNP credible set fall inside the SLC28A1 gene body (intron_variant), contradicting the locus input file's "intergenic" annotation (see evidence-table header note). But per the red-team critique, this is a coordinate correction, not a mechanism — an intronic location does not establish that the credible set regulates SLC28A1 rather than a neighboring gene, and no functional, expression, or disease-association evidence corroborates it. The two literature citations connected to SLC28A1 (a CARDIA metabolite-QTL hit for a different, non-credible-set variant, and a paralog-only mucociliary-clearance modeling paper) add essentially nothing toward COPD-relevant biology, as both the researcher and skeptic note explicitly.

Runner-up: PDE8A. Has a genuinely relevant, verified mechanistic paper (Johnstone et al. 2018) proposing a role for PDE8A-regulated cAMP signaling in airway smooth muscle remodeling in asthma/COPD — but zero genetic-association evidence ties this locus to PDE8A, and PDE8A's 5' end only barely enters the locus window, ~10-20 kb from the credible set (worse physical proximity than SLC28A1). The skeptic explicitly warns against letting mechanistic plausibility substitute for absent genetics here.

What would resolve this: (1) an eQTL or colocalization analysis of the credible set against SLC28A1 and PDE8A expression in lung/airway tissue (not currently resolvable via Open Targets for this locus); (2) a direct check of whether this 15:84.9-85.05 Mb interval appears in the supplementary tables of major published COPD/spirometry GWAS (e.g., Sakornsakolpat 2019, Shrine 2019) or the GSCAN smoking-behavior GWAS under any gene label — the red-team-skeptic flagged that this specific check was not completed for this locus due to exhausted search budget on its end and should be done before treating this locus as settled; (3) fine-mapping or chromatin-conformation data indicating which gene's promoter/enhancer the credible set actually contacts.

Given the near-total absence of gene-specific COPD/lung-function evidence for every candidate examined, this call is best read as "no compelling causal gene identified at this locus" with SLC28A1 recorded only as the default positional candidate — not as a confident prioritization. The study team should weigh this locus as a low- priority/inconclusive case pending the follow-up checks above.
