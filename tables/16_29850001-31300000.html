Prioritized-gene synthesis

Prioritized gene: PRSS8 — Confidence: LOW.

This call is close and should be read as such. PRSS8 carries the largest credible-set PIP mass at this locus (lead variant rs9972727, PIP=0.43, plus rs12597511 and rs2855475), is the ABC-prioritized gene, and has by far the deepest mechanistic literature of any candidate here: an extensively replicated, decades-old role activating airway epithelial ENaC and regulating airway-surface-liquid volume and mucociliary clearance (rows above, PMIDs 11756432, 18310226, 27014936, 25260629), a disease process directly analogous to the mucociliary dysfunction seen in COPD, plus a mechanistic tie to neutrophil-elastase-driven airway pathophysiology (PMID 27014936) — a link to the neutrophilic inflammatory program named in this pipeline's biological priors.

However, this recommendation is explicitly weak, and the red-team-skeptic marked PRSS8 CONTESTED for good reason: (1) none of the mechanistic evidence comes from COPD or cigarette-smoke models — it is almost entirely cystic fibrosis (airway) and kidney (knockout mouse) literature; (2) Open Targets shows zero L2G/genetic-association evidence for COPD, FEV1, or FEV1/FVC at this locus for any gene, including PRSS8; (3) the one COPD-adjacent proteomic finding (Casselbrant et al. 2022, PMID 35263363) found PRSS8 associated with a combined CAD+airflow-obstruction phenotype but explicitly not with airflow obstruction alone — active counter-evidence against a pure lung mechanism, and consistent with this locus's dominant cardiometabolic/lipid pleiotropy; and (4) this locus's own GWAS Catalog record for the lead variant shows only triglyceride and cardiovascular-disorder associations, not lung-function or smoking-behavior ones.

Runner-up: KAT8, which carries the second-largest credible-set PIP mass, is the most missense-constrained gene in this candidate set, and has newly emerging (2025-2026) mechanistic evidence linking it to neutrophil recruitment and M2 macrophage polarization in airway inflammation (PMIDs 42244110, 41952982, 40250723) — but that evidence is exclusively from allergic-asthma (papain, house-dust-mite) and LPS acute-lung-injury mouse models, not COPD/smoke-exposure models, and its own locus-wide Open Targets L2G dominance (the single strongest of any candidate gene here) is for metabolic, anthropometric, and neurological traits, not respiratory ones.

What would resolve this tie: (a) a COPD- or cigarette-smoke-specific mouse/cell model showing PRSS8- or KAT8-dependent airway inflammatory or mucociliary phenotypes; (b) a COPD- or FEV1/FEV1-FVC-specific colocalization or fine-mapping study at this locus (none exists yet in Open Targets or GWAS Catalog for the lead variant); (c) tissue- and cell-type-resolved eQTL/sQTL colocalization in COPD-relevant airway tissue for PRSS8 vs. KAT8 vs. the other credible-set genes, to determine which gene's expression is actually regulated by the credible-set variants in lung.

The remaining candidates (BCKDK, STX1B — both undermined by a Parkinson's-disease GWAS signal that failed to replicate outside Caucasian populations, and by contradictory functional mast-cell data for STX1B; STX4 — internally contradicted mast-cell biology, STX3 vs. STX4; VKORC1 — a genetically and mechanistically confirmed warfarin pharmacogenomic "passenger" gene at this locus; ZNF668 and PRSS36 — essentially no lung/immune literature footprint; MYLPF/MYL11 — zero credible-set PIP and a gene body located ~750 kb outside the entire credible-set span) are not competitive with PRSS8 or KAT8 and are not recommended.

Given the locus-wide absence of COPD/FEV1/FEV1-FVC genetic evidence in Open Targets, the dominant lipid/cardiometabolic/pharmacogenomic pleiotropy at this locus, and the red-team-skeptic's explicit alternative hypothesis that the COPD association here may be a comorbidity/pleiotropy artifact rather than a genuine lung-mechanism signal, this locus's causal gene call should be treated as unresolved pending COPD- or smoke-specific functional or fine-mapping evidence. PRSS8 is named as the best-supported single pick on mechanistic-plausibility and PIP-mass grounds, but confidence is LOW, not MODERATE, precisely because that plausibility is not yet corroborated by any COPD-specific genetic or model evidence, and because credible counter-evidence and a parsimonious alternative (locus-wide metabolic/comorbidity pleiotropy) both exist.
