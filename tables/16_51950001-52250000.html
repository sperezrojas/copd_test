Evidence Table - Locus 16:51950001-52250000 (nearest gene TOX3)

Lead variant: rs17201148 (16:52,095,190, GRCh38), intronic in LINC02180, PIP=0.139 (the single highest-PIP variant in a diffuse ~50-variant credible set; no variant exceeds PIP~0.14). Candidate genes: TOX3 (nearest gene), C16orf97 (MAGMA- and PoPS-prioritized; resolves to LINC02911), LINC02180, LINC00919 (both credible-set genes).

Key structural caveat (applies to the whole table below): C16orf97 resolves to LINC02911 (NCBI Gene ID 388276), whose coordinates (chr16:52,005,486-52,078,516) sit essentially entirely inside the Ensembl gene body annotated as "LINC00919" (ENSG00000260268, chr16:52,005,425-52,085,177) -- confirmed by direct Ensembl REST coordinate lookup (structured database check, not literature). This means the MAGMA/PoPS "C16orf97" signal and the credible-set PIP mass annotated to "LINC00919" are very likely the same underlying genomic feature under two different catalog names (NCBI/RefSeq vs. Ensembl/GENCODE), not two independent converging lines of evidence. Separately, TOX3's own gene body (chr16:52,436,415-52,547,801) does not physically overlap this locus at all -- it starts ~186 kb beyond the locus's 3' boundary; TOX3 is included here only as the nearest gene by distance, and any TOX3-mediated mechanism at this locus would require long-range regulation, which is unconfirmed by any colocalization/eQTL evidence found in this review.

Prioritized gene

Prioritized gene: LINC02180. Confidence: LOW.

This call is close and should be read as a tentative positional pick, not a confident mechanistic conclusion. The evidence at this locus does not cleanly distinguish between the candidate genes, and every line of evidence carries a substantial caveat:

  • LINC02180 is the only candidate gene with a genuinely locus-specific structural claim to causality: it physically contains the lead credible-set variant (rs17201148, PIP=0.139) and several of the next-highest-PIP variants. That is real, if weak (PIP 0.139 is low in absolute terms), fine-mapping evidence pointing at this specific gene body. Against that, it has zero independent functional or disease literature (PubMed: zero hits), and even GWAS Catalog's own automated gene-mapping pipeline declined to attribute the one catalogued trait association at this locus to LINC02180, instead crediting the neighboring C16orf97 symbol -- a point the red-team-skeptic explicitly flagged as mild evidence against LINC02180 being the functionally relevant unit despite housing the lead SNP.
  • Runner-up 1: C16orf97 (=LINC02911). This is the gene both MAGMA and PoPS -- two independent statistical fine-mapping/prioritization methods -- converge on, and it is the gene GWAS Catalog's own pipeline credits with the one confirmed structured-database trait hit at this locus (rs17201148 / "risk-taking behaviour," independently re-confirmed via direct GWAS Catalog REST API query). However, this gene is marked CONTESTED above: its coordinates sit almost entirely inside the Ensembl "LINC00919" gene body, meaning the apparent convergence between "MAGMA/PoPS pick C16orf97" and "credible set implicates LINC00919" may be double-counting the same regulatory feature under two catalog names rather than two independent lines of support. Until that naming collision is resolved, C16orf97's apparent strength (two independent prioritization methods) is not fully trustworthy at face value.
  • Runner-up 2: TOX3. The nearest gene by distance, but its gene body does not physically overlap this locus (starts ~186 kb beyond the boundary), so any causal role would require unconfirmed long-range regulation. TOX3 does carry the only verified COPD-specific literature finding in this entire evidence base (PMID 32099002, naming TOX3 as a network "switch gene" linked to apoptosis regulation in a COPD transcriptomic analysis) plus a nontrivial Open Targets FEV1 L2G score (~0.57) and a smoking-initiation association (0.258) -- but none of these can be confirmed as specific to this particular credible set/locus, and the single literature-based statistical-genetics claim that would have tied TOX3/C16orf97 most directly to this locus's lead variant (the "risk-taking behaviour" GWAS hit) came back CLAIM_MISMATCH from citation-verifier (though a structured-database re-check corroborates the underlying number). TOX3's overall evidence base at this locus is otherwise dominated by off-topic breast-cancer and RLS findings that establish its general biology but say nothing about COPD.
  • LINC00919 is not separately rankable from C16orf97/LINC02911 given the coordinate overlap noted above, and independently carries zero disease associations and zero literature of its own.

What would resolve this tie: (1) a locus-specific colocalization or eQTL analysis distinguishing whether the credible-set signal regulates TOX3 (long-range), LINC02180, or the LINC00919/LINC02911/C16orf97 region specifically, in lung or relevant immune/behavioral tissue; (2) formal reconciliation of the LINC00919 (Ensembl/GENCODE) vs. LINC02911/C16orf97 (NCBI/ RefSeq) gene models to determine whether they represent one transcript or two; (3) supplementary table confirmation of the rs17201148/"risk-taking behaviour" association directly from the Karlsson Linnér et al. 2019 primary data (main text/abstract could not confirm it); (4) a direct COPD/FEV1 GWAS or fine-mapping paper naming any of these four genes at this specific position, which no search in this review was able to find. Given the diffuse credible set (max PIP 0.139 across ~50 variants) and the near-total absence of independent locus-specific mechanistic literature for every candidate, the possibility that this signal reflects fine-mapping noise without a single identifiable causal gene remains live and should be weighed by the study team alongside this recommendation.
