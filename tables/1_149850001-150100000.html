ADDENDUM NOTICE (2026-09-11, targeted re-opening)

This locus's original review (candidates MTMR11, SF3B4, OTUD7B, SV2A, plus VPS45/BOLA1 found via regional enumeration) is preserved below unchanged. A genome-build error in the original MAGMA/PoPS gene-location merge file was subsequently discovered, and a corrected re-merge surfaced H3C15 (a replication-dependent, canonical H3.1/H3.2-type histone gene, formerly named HIST2H3A) as a candidate gene at this locus that had not previously been individually evaluated (it was only noted collectively, as one of five histone-cluster genes, in the original review's regional-enumeration step). H3C15's coordinates (chr1:149,852,608-149,853,125, GRCh38) were independently re-confirmed to genuinely, physically fall inside this locus's window — this is not an artifact of the build-error bug — so it was researched, verified, and critiqued to the same standard as the other candidates. See the H3C15 rows below and the "Prioritized gene" section for the outcome: H3C15 does not disturb the original MTMR11/OTUD7B tie and has been added to the table at the same INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE tier as SV2A/BOLA1/VPS45.

Locus-level note (applies across genes)

The lead credible-set variant (rs11205303) and its correlated SNPs (rs11205277, rs67807996) have an independently published, genome-wide-significant common-variant association with mammographic breast density (Fernandez-Navarro et al. 2014) and, at the correlated SNP rs11205277, with growth-hormone treatment response in idiopathic short stature (Quigley et al. 2019, which explicitly nominates SF3B4 as most relevant of the four nearby genes) and with adult-height/infant-length GWAS loci generally (van der Valk et al. 2014). The locus's best-replicated common-variant phenotype in the literature is anthropometric/developmental (height, growth, breast density), not pulmonary or smoking-related — a locus-level alternative hypothesis (e.g., allometric lung-size effects on spirometry) that applies across all four originally-listed candidate genes and is not resolved by the current evidence.

Prioritized gene

MTMR11 — confidence LOW. (Reaffirmed following H3C15 addendum review; the tie with OTUD7B remains unresolved and H3C15 does not change this call.)

This call is close and should be read as a tiebreaker recommendation, not a confident causal assignment. The case for MTMR11 rests entirely on statistical gene-mapping: it is the only candidate gene at this locus with L2G evidence directly tying it to both the COPD case-control credible set itself (L2G=0.636) and the FEV1/FVC spirometry credible sets at the same locus (L2G=0.156–0.750), satisfying this pipeline's Prior 2 (COPD/spirometry concordance strengthens prioritization) better than any other gene here — SF3B4, OTUD7B, SV2A, and now H3C15 all have L2G scores at or near zero (H3C15: exactly zero, genome-wide) for the COPD/FEV1/FVC credible sets specifically. However, as red-team-skeptic states directly in the audit appendix, MTMR11 also "wins" L2G at the overwhelming majority (>150) of ~175 other, biologically unrelated credible sets tagged by this same variant (height, weight, glaucoma, coronary disease, breast cancer, etc.) — a pattern more consistent with a positional/technical L2G artifact at this densely-packed 1q21.2 locus (MTMR11/SF3B4/OTUD7B/SV2A are all within ~250 kb) than trait-specific causal biology. Critically, MTMR11 has essentially no dedicated mechanistic, developmental, or disease-specific literature in any tissue, lung or otherwise (see evidence table above) — this is a genuine, acknowledged gap, not a null result being smoothed over.

Runner-up: OTUD7B (also LOW confidence, biology-first case). OTUD7B is VEP-annotated to the lead credible-set variant itself (PIP=0.894) and has by far the richest mechanistic immune literature of any gene at this locus — well-replicated roles as an NF-κB-restraining deubiquitinase in endothelium/epithelium and as a positive regulator of TCR/Zap70 signaling in T cells, resonant with this pipeline's prior on CD8+ T-cell and innate inflammatory programs in COPD — plus one (unreplicated, correlational, ML-derived) 2026 COPD transcriptomic study linking its downregulation to Treg/eosinophil shifts. But none of this mechanistic work is in lower-airway/lung tissue specifically, its function is demonstrably cell-type-context-dependent (An et al. 2022 found no NF-κB/inflammatory effect of OTUD7B in vascular smooth muscle), and — most importantly — Open Targets L2G does not place OTUD7B as the regulatorily-implicated gene at this locus's COPD/FEV1/FVC credible sets at all, despite its physical proximity to the lead SNP. SF3B4 (the PoPS-prioritized gene) is a distant third: its PoPS signal looks more consistent with a non-specific pathway/expression-feature call than lung-relevant biology, given its dominant, independently-replicated common-variant phenotype at this locus is anthropometric (height/growth), not pulmonary.

H3C15 (2026-09-11 addendum): evaluated and ruled out as a competitive third contender, confidence INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE. H3C15 genuinely, physically overlaps this locus (~68-170 kb from the credible-set variants) — the genome-build error that surfaced it as a candidate did not erroneously place it here — so it received the same full-depth research, citation verification, and red-team critique as the other candidates. It did not survive that scrutiny as a competitive pick. Its one substantive domain of relevant biology — citrullinated histone H3 (citH3) in neutrophil extracellular traps (NETosis), which has genuine, well-replicated, even therapeutically-actionable COPD relevance (Obermayer et al. 2014; Wu et al. 2026; Totani et al. 2021; roflumilast, an approved COPD drug, reduces NETosis) — is, as verified by both the researcher and independently confirmed by red-team-skeptic, gene-family-level evidence, not H3C15-gene-specific evidence: citrullination occurs on arginine residues conserved identically across ~10-15 near-identical canonical H3.1/H3.2 histone paralogs, and no literature was found distinguishing H3C15 (or canonical H3 generally) from other paralogs (or from H3.3) in NET composition or immune-cell-proliferation specificity. Genetically, H3C15 is markedly weaker than every other candidate at this locus, including the other INSUFFICIENT-EVIDENCE genes: it has zero credible-set/L2G assignments in Open Targets for any trait, genome-wide (not merely absent at this locus's COPD/FEV1/FVC rows, as with OTUD7B) and zero genetic_association-datatype contribution to any of its disease scores. Red-team-skeptic explicitly judged this a "qualitatively different, and worse" pattern than SV2A's (SV2A at least has a real, if wrong-domain, neurological GWAS signal) and concluded H3C15 "does not become a credible third contender" and "should not disturb the existing tie." This synthesis concurs: H3C15 is filed alongside SV2A/BOLA1/VPS45 at the INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE tier, materially below both MTMR11 and OTUD7B.

What would resolve the tie: a lung-tissue-specific eQTL/colocalization analysis (e.g., GTEx lung or an airway-epithelium/alveolar-macrophage-specific eQTL dataset) that can discriminate among MTMR11/SF3B4/OTUD7B/SV2A at this single, tightly-linked LD block — the current L2G evidence draws on broad-tissue eQTL/chromatin features that do not appear to discriminate well among these physically clustered genes (see red-team-skeptic critique). Functional validation (e.g., CRISPRi in airway epithelial or immune cells) directly testing MTMR11 versus OTUD7B for a COPD-relevant phenotype would also resolve this. For H3C15 specifically, the missing piece is any H3C15-paralog-specific (as opposed to pooled-H3) eQTL, expression, or functional evidence in lung/immune tissue — absent that, its candidacy rests on physical proximity and generic pathway plausibility alone, which this pipeline's gene-as-unit-of-evidence framework does not treat as sufficient. Until such data exist, this locus should be read as "probably real (given COPD/FEV1/FVC concordance), gene assignment unresolved among MTMR11 and OTUD7B" rather than either "artifact" or "solved," with H3C15 considered and set aside rather than left unexamined.
