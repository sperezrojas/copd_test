Prioritized gene

ZEB2 — Confidence: MODERATE

ZEB2 is the best-supported candidate gene at this locus, but the case is genuinely mixed and should not be overstated. In favor: ZEB2 is the sole protein-coding gene anywhere in or near this >1.2 Mb window (all other candidates are lncRNAs identified only by incidental credible-set variant overlap, or — for GTDC1/QTMAN — a gene sitting entirely outside the locus boundary); it is prioritized by three of four internal methods (nearest gene, FLAMES, PoPS); it is explicitly named "ZEB2 at 2q22.3" as a candidate gene in a prior, verified 82-locus COPD GWAS (PMID 30804561); it has verified, disease-matched mechanistic evidence directly in a COPD/emphysema model (the miR-200b/ZEB2/senescence-inflammation axis, PMID 32070140) plus verified EMT/airway-remodeling biology in bronchial epithelial cells (PMID 25890309, albeit an asthma-context study); and it is strongly constrained against loss-of-function (gnomAD oe=0.058), consistent with a dosage-sensitive disease mechanism.

Against overconfidence: the credible set's single highest-PIP cluster (including the lead variant rs10204857) sits ~830 kb-1 Mb from ZEB2's gene body, and no eQTL or colocalization evidence was found anywhere in this review tying that cluster — or any other part of the credible set — to ZEB2 expression specifically; this remains substantially a nearest-gene/ sole-protein-coding-gene inference rather than a fine-mapping-resolved one. Four of the five citations originally built around a "smoking-initiation" narrative for the top-PIP cluster were downgraded to CLAIM_MISMATCH by the citation-verifier (see audit file), including one genuine misattribution — so that narrative is now substantially unsupported by verified literature, not just weakened. Separately, a verified epilepsy GWAS hit (PMID 22949513, rs10496964, within this credible set, also naming ZEB2) is flagged by the red-team-skeptic as under-weighted evidence for an alternative, neurodevelopmental/neurobehavioral mechanism (consistent with ZEB2's known role in Mowat-Wilson syndrome) rather than a pulmonary- structural one — this tension between disease-matched pulmonary mechanistic evidence and a verified CNS-phenotype hit at the same locus is genuinely unresolved. No FEV1/FEV1-FVC- specific association was found for this locus at all, which is a real evidentiary gap per this pipeline's prior favoring spirometric concordance. The red-team-skeptic was also unable to run any independent search for this locus (WebSearch budget exhausted before any query completed), so this critique reflects re-analysis of existing findings only, not an independently corroborated absence of contradicting literature.

Runner-up consideration: TEX41 was evaluated because a large sub-cluster of the credible set (~20 variants) sits in its introns, including the locus's one directly-COPD-attributed variant (rs13428413) — but that attribution failed citation verification (CLAIM_MISMATCH), a second TEX41 credible-set-variant disease attribution was a genuine misattribution (CLAIM_MISMATCH), and TEX41's only verified literature is entirely non-pulmonary oncology plus one off-credible-set eQTL in a disease-irrelevant context (mastocytosis). The red-team-skeptic marked TEX41 CONTESTED. It is not a credible runner-up on current evidence. GTDC1/QTMAN, LINC01412, and LINC01966 all have negligible-to-zero disease-relevant evidence and are not viable alternatives (weakened, not contested — no affirmative evidence exists on either side for these three).

What would resolve the remaining uncertainty: (1) an eQTL or colocalization study directly linking the credible set's top-PIP cluster (~145.35-145.42 Mb) to ZEB2 expression in lung or relevant tissue; (2) a direct FEV1/FEV1-FVC spirometry GWAS hit at this locus, to establish pulmonary-function concordance; (3) resolution of whether the Cosentino et al. 2023 and Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019 supplementary tables actually list this locus's specific credible-set variants (neither this pipeline's researcher nor its red-team-skeptic had tool access to check supplementary materials); (4) smoking-adjusted COPD GWAS results at this locus, to formally test whether the association attenuates after conditioning on smoking behavior — directly bearing on this pipeline's smoking-mediated-signal prior. Given these open items, MODERATE (not HIGH) confidence is assigned: ZEB2 is the clear best pick among the candidates evaluated, but the mechanism (pulmonary-structural vs. smoking/neurobehavioral- mediated) and the physical link between the top signal and the gene remain unresolved.
