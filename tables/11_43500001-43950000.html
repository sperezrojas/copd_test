Prioritized gene

HSD17B12 — Confidence: MODERATE.

HSD17B12 is the best-supported candidate gene at this locus, but the evidence stops short of HIGH confidence for two specific, skeptic-flagged reasons, so this call is made with those caveats stated explicitly rather than glossed over.

Supporting the call:

  • HSD17B12 is the nearest gene and is independently prioritized by FLAMES, MAGMA, PoPS, and ABC — full concordance across the pipeline's own orthogonal methods.
  • Open Targets' own L2G model, run independently of this pipeline, strongly and consistently resolves the lead variant rs7117036 to HSD17B12 (L2G 0.43-0.82) over all neighboring genes (ALKBH3/TTC17/ACCS, L2G 0.05-0.11) across dozens of shared-variant GWAS traits (see Open Targets summary table above).
  • Independent, pre-COPD-era genetic evidence directly supports pulmonary biology at this exact region: Loth et al. 2014 (PMID 24929828) found this locus genome-wide significant for FVC, with lung-tissue transcript detection — this satisfies the pipeline's prior #2 (pulmonary-function concordance strengthens prioritization) with real, gene-adjacent spirometric evidence, not just COPD case-control association.
  • HSD17B12 has a genuine, lung-expressed enzymatic function (Ashmore et al. 2018, PMID 29788210): it is the dominant lung microsomal enzyme metabolizing the tobacco carcinogen NNK. This is real pulmonary tissue biology, though — per the skeptic's critique — it argues for a smoking-exposure-conditional ("heaviness-mediated") mechanism rather than ruling out smoking-mediation altogether.
  • The credible set contains no missense or splice-altering variant in either HSD17B12 or MIR670HG, consistent with a regulatory/expression-modulating mechanism (supported indirectly by Bellemare et al. 2010's finding that complete HSD17B12 loss-of-function is embryonic lethal — full LoF is not what a common regulatory GWAS variant would produce).
  • MIR670HG, the only other locus-native candidate, is confirmed via Open Targets' genomicLocation field to be a genomically separate, non-overlapping gene (not a host gene nested within HSD17B12), and its only substantive supporting literature (Kupffer-cell/liver-macrophage phagocytosis; CNS-microglial neuroinflammation) is 0-for-2 on lung/airway tissue context and carries essentially no Open Targets signal (COPD-specific evidence count = 0, no meaningful L2G at this variant). MIR670HG is the runner-up only in the sense of being the other locus-native gene — the evidentiary gap between it and HSD17B12 is large.

Explicitly weighed against HIGH confidence (per skeptic critique, preserved here rather than smoothed over):

  • Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019 (PMID 30804561), the landmark COPD case-control GWAS, is real and its general findings are verified, but citation-verifier could not confirm that this specific locus/HSD17B12 is actually among its 82 genome-wide-significant loci, nor that the paper's own lung-eQTL S-PrediXcan analysis implicated HSD17B12. Direct, locus-specific COPD case-control replication in prior literature (independent of the present GWAS) therefore remains unconfirmed, not established.
  • The smoking-mediation question (pipeline prior #1) is not fully resolved. Open Targets' L2G model independently assigns substantial confidence to HSD17B12 for smoking-heaviness traits (cigarettes/day L2G 0.64-0.70; pack-years L2G 0.48) at this same lead variant — a real, nontrivial competing/co-occurring signal. The literature found (Ashmore et al.) supports a lung-intrinsic, exposure-conditional mechanism rather than a neurobiological smoking-initiation mechanism, and Loth et al. supports smoking-independent pulmonary-function biology — but no single piece of evidence cleanly separates "this locus affects COPD risk via smoking-heaviness-dependent carcinogen handling" from "this locus affects COPD risk via a smoking-independent pulmonary-function pathway." Both mechanisms are plausible and evidentially supported; they are not mutually exclusive, and the true balance between them is not resolved by current evidence.
  • No literature search was found (by researcher or skeptic) specifically testing for contradicting evidence (a null COPD association, or a competing gene assignment) at this locus — an absence of contradicting evidence that reflects a search gap rather than a confirmed clean negative.

What would resolve this to HIGH confidence: (1) direct confirmation (e.g., from the Sakornsakolpat 2019 supplementary locus table, or a subsequent COPD GWAS) that this exact region is a genome-wide-significant COPD locus independent of the present study, ideally with the S-PrediXcan/eQTL analysis specifically implicating HSD17B12; (2) a COPD- or FEV1-specific colocalization (h4) analysis at rs7117036, since Open Targets holds no COPD-specific credible set for this variant in its own database; (3) lung-tissue eQTL data (e.g., GTEx lung, or a dedicated airway-epithelium eQTL study) directly tying rs7117036 or this credible set to HSD17B12 expression in lung, to adjudicate between the smoking-heaviness-mediated and pulmonary-independent mechanisms.

Runner-up: MIR670HG — evidentially much weaker (no COPD/lung-tissue evidence of any kind, genomically confirmed as a separate gene from HSD17B12), retained as runner-up only because it is the other gene natively overlapped by a substantial fraction of the credible set's PIP mass, not because current evidence meaningfully supports it as competitive with HSD17B12.
