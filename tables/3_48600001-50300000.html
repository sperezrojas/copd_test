Prioritized gene

MST1 — confidence LOW.

This is a genuinely close call, and the call is being made under material uncertainty that the evidence available does not resolve. Reasoning:

  • By the structured genetics, MST1 is the strongest candidate: it carries the credible set's missense variant (rs3197999, R689C, PIP=0.054) with a replicated, functionally validated gain-of-function mechanism (Häuser 2012, PMID 22237417) and a cis-pQTL effect on circulating MST1 protein (Di Narzo 2017, PMID 28129359), and it has by far the highest Open Targets L2G-style score at this locus (0.608 vs. next-highest DAG1 at 0.270; see Open Targets summary above). Unlike BSN's own credible-set variants, MST1's missense variant shows no behavioral/smoking-trait association in the GWAS Catalog lookup, which somewhat reduces concern that its contribution to the COPD signal is confounded by smoking-behavior genetics (see GWAS Catalog subsection above).
  • However, the red-team-skeptic's critique (audits/3_48600001-50300000_audit.md) correctly identifies that every piece of MST1's mechanistic evidence is from IBD/PSC/gut tissue or a CNS-autoimmune (EAE) model — none of it has ever been tested in lung or COPD tissue, and independent PubMed checks for "MST1/RON + COPD" returned zero results. The absence of a smoking-trait GWAS Catalog hit for rs3197999 is being used as supporting evidence, but this is absence-of-evidence reasoning, not a positive finding.
  • The skeptic also raised a serious, unresolved structural concern: Latiano et al. 2010 (PMID 20024904) is the source citation for BOTH the BSN (rs9858542) and MST1 (rs3197999) IBD associations, and this pipeline's literature search did not establish whether these two variants are LD-independent within that paper. If they are not, the "three independent immune-disease genes at this locus" framing (BSN, MST1, RNF123 all IBD-associated) may really be one shared IBD-risk haplotype being counted under multiple gene names — a long-standing ambiguity in the 3p21.31/IBD-genetics literature that this review did not resolve for the COPD credible set either.
  • Runner-up: BSN (nearest gene, MAGMA-prioritized, carries the great majority of the credible set's PIP mass including the lead variant). BSN's own established Mendelian disease biology is exclusively neuronal (epilepsy/Landau-Kleffner; Chen 2025, Ye 2022, Conroy 2014, Ghiglieri 2010), it has no lung structural/immune literature, and its own credible-set variants (lead SNP and a missense variant) show associations with educational attainment and, notably, smoking cessation (p=2e-18) — a real, if not fully verified, red flag for a neurobehavioral/smoking-confounded mechanism rather than direct pulmonary biology, per prior #1. The skeptic judged the "smoking-mediated" framing partly overclaimed (educational-attainment pleiotropy is weak, non-specific evidence), but did not dismiss it outright, and BSN's genomic size alone (spanning most of the credible set) is a plausible alternative, non-causal explanation for its prominence.
  • Second runner-up: RNF123, which has its own independent, functionally-validated immune-disease mechanism (Crohn's-associated NLRP3-inflammasome restraint, PMID 41719337) — again gut-tissue-only evidence, but mechanistically distinct from both BSN and MST1, and a third candidate for the same "shared immune-regulatory hub" hypothesis the skeptic raised.
  • GPX1 and RHOA have the most COPD/lung-mechanistically-direct literature of any genes at this locus (GPX1: in vivo smoke-induced emphysema in knockout/transgenic mice; RHOA: macrophage efferocytosis, airway smooth muscle contractility with human patient data, diaphragm atrophy) but both have materially weaker direct statistical fine-mapping support (GPX1 has no credible-set variant at all — ABC-regulatory-link only; RHOA's variants all have PIP<0.008 and the second-lowest L2G score at the locus). The skeptic explicitly judged RHOA's inclusion among "the most compelling" genes as not supportable given how generic and pathway-level (rather than locus-specific) its cited evidence is, bordering on CONTESTED. DAG1 (PoPS-prioritized, second-highest L2G) has no COPD/lung-function literature and its strongest disease link (muscular dystrophy) operates through a different mechanism (respiratory muscle weakness) than classic COPD airflow obstruction; two newly-verified airway-tissue dystroglycan papers (Shareef 2021, White 2001) modestly soften but do not resolve this mismatch, since they describe Th2/asthma and epithelial-repair contexts, not COPD/smoke-response biology.

What would resolve this tie: COPD-specific (not IBD-borrowed) lung eQTL/pQTL colocalization data for MST1, BSN, and RNF123 individually; a conditional/joint fine-mapping analysis testing whether the BSN, MST1, and RNF123 credible-set signals are statistically independent of each other and of the shared IBD-risk haplotype identified in Latiano et al. 2010; direct functional validation of rs34762726's reported smoking-cessation association and its independence from the IBD-associated haplotype; and single-cell/bulk lung-tissue expression or perturbation data for MST1/MST1R (RON) and RNF123 in COPD-relevant cell types (macrophage, airway epithelium), which do not currently exist in the literature reviewed here. Given all of the above, this pick should be treated as the best-supported nomination for further investigation, not a settled causal-gene call — confidence is explicitly LOW, and the study team should weigh the BSN and RNF123 alternatives seriously.
