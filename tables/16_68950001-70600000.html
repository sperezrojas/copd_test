Prioritized gene

WWP2 — confidence LOW.

This call is close, and the case for WWP2 has a real structural weakness that must be stated plainly: both of the statistical-genetics citations that would have literature- confirmed WWP2 as the causal gene at this locus — Shrine et al. 2023 (the largest multi-ancestry lung-function GWAS to date) and Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019 (the principal COPD GWAS) — came back CLAIM_MISMATCH from the citation-verifier: neither paper could be confirmed to name WWP2 at a 16q22.1 locus in accessible text. WWP2's only verified, gene-specific mechanistic support (PMID 31399586) is a cardiac-fibrosis mouse model, not lung tissue. The red-team-skeptic rated WWP2 WEAKENED, borderline CONTESTED, on exactly these grounds.

Despite that, WWP2 is still the best-supported single pick among the three candidates, for three reasons visible in the tables above. First, it is the only gene at this locus independently prioritized by three different gene-prioritization algorithms (FLAMES, MAGMA, PoPS), which — while not itself a verified literature citation — is a form of convergent evidence distinct from, and not circular with, the L2G database score. Second, its Open Targets FEV1 evidence (0.723, n=18 contributing credible sets/studies) is by far the largest, most consistent quantitative signal at this locus for any gene/trait pair, concordant with prior #2 (spirometric evidence supporting lung-biology mediation), whereas NFAT5's FEV1 signal is weaker (0.506, n=2) and NQO1's is negligible (0.151, n=12). Third, its verified mechanistic biology (TGF-β/SMAD2-driven pathological fibrosis via HECT-domain ubiquitination) is at least directionally consistent with airway-remodeling/ECM pathways that COPD GWAS pathway-enrichment analyses have flagged as relevant to COPD risk loci in general, even though this specific paper's SMAD-binding/ECM enrichment claim could not be tied to this locus by name and the mechanism itself was shown in heart, not lung.

Runner-up: NFAT5, which is not clearly weaker — it has the only direct (if modest) COPD L2G evidence of the three genes (0.383, n=2), a verified and mechanistically coherent (if entirely non-pulmonary, autoimmune/renal-model) pro-inflammatory Th1/Th17/macrophage literature, and a constrained/LoF-intolerant gene (LOEUF 0.235) consistent with an essential regulatory role — but it also carries the locus's strongest smoking-initiation signal (0.647, n=6), raising a live alternative hypothesis (per prior #1) that this locus's COPD association is partly or wholly mediated through smoking-behavior biology rather than lung-intrinsic disease, and the red-team-skeptic noted this smoking-mediation evidence is exactly as database-score-dependent and literature-unconfirmed as WWP2's FEV1 claim. NQO1 is not a serious contender: its sole causal mechanistic paper is retracted, its fine-mapping PIP is the lowest in the credible set (0.011), it has zero COPD or FEV1/FVC L2G evidence, and it is not under selective constraint (LOEUF 1.23).

What would resolve this tie: (1) direct confirmation (via full-text/supplementary-table access) of whether Shrine et al. 2023 and/or Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019 name WWP2 (or NFAT5, or neither) as the prioritized gene at this specific 16q22.1 signal; (2) lung-tissue (airway epithelial or fibroblast) eQTL/colocalization data distinguishing WWP2 from NFAT5 and NQO1 at this locus, since none of the three genes currently has literature-confirmed, locus-specific colocalization; (3) a locus-level look at whether rs889398's dominant BMI association is causally upstream of, or independent from, its lung-function/COPD signal, since BMI is a known confounder/mediator of spirometric measurement and systemic inflammation. Given the CLAIM_MISMATCH status of both key WWP2 statistical-genetics citations and the unresolved BMI-pleiotropy concern affecting the whole locus, this recommendation should be treated as provisional, not a settled call — the study team should weigh WWP2 and NFAT5 as roughly comparable candidates pending the evidence above.
