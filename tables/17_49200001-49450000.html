Prioritized gene

ZNF652 — confidence: MODERATE.

ZNF652 carries essentially all of the fine-mapped signal at this locus: the lead variant (rs16948048, PIP=0.513) sits upstream of ZNF652, and the great majority of remaining credible-set PIP mass falls within the ZNF652 gene body itself (see full credible set in loci/17_49200001-49450000.md). The only credible-set-weighted rival, ZNF652-AS1, is an antisense transcript whose genomic span overlaps ZNF652 almost entirely — the red-team critique and researcher both judged this the same underlying regulatory signal rather than an independent gene, not a genuine statistical tie. UTP18 (the MAGMA/PoPS-prioritized gene) is very likely a genome-build coordinate artifact: its true GRCh38 position lies ~1.9 Mb outside this locus, and it carries zero Open Targets COPD/FEV1 evidence and no lung/immune literature — the red-team-skeptic explicitly endorsed dismissing it (verdict HOLDS on that dismissal). On pure fine-mapping/positional grounds, ZNF652 is the clear pick.

However, confidence is capped at MODERATE rather than HIGH because the mechanistic case for ZNF652 in COPD/pulmonary biology is weak by the verified evidence in the table above: its only VERIFIED disease-association literature concerns blood pressure/hypertension (rows 1-4), a non-pulmonary, non-smoking trait, and the three papers the researcher proposed as the likely source of ZNF652's Open Targets FEV1 signal (Shrine 2019 PMID 30804560, Wain 2017 PMID 28166213, Shrine 2023 PMID 36914875) all came back CLAIM_MISMATCH — the citation-verifier could not confirm ZNF652 is actually named in these papers. The FEV1 evidence therefore rests only on the structured Open Targets score (genetic_association datatype 0.588, gwas_credible_ sets resourceScore ~0.77), not on a verified primary-literature statement, and no structural or immune mechanistic literature for ZNF652 was found at all (a genuine gap, not a negative finding). Per the smoking-mediation prior, no smoking-behavior GWAS or literature link was found for ZNF652 either, so the blood-pressure association most plausibly reflects horizontal pleiotropy in a different tissue rather than smoking-mediated confounding of the COPD signal.

The runner-up is PHB1, flagged by both the researcher and red-team-skeptic as a biologically plausible complementary candidate: it has substantial, genuinely COPD- and smoking-relevant mechanistic literature (cigarette-smoke-induced mitochondrial dysfunction in human COPD/smoker lung tissue, PMID 34214709; CSE-exposed A549 mitochondrial pathway proposed relevant to COPD/IPF, PMID 27327778) plus two lung-injury-model papers of lower specificity (sepsis-ALI, LPS-ALI). However, PHB1's positional/fine-mapping support at this locus is thin and indirect — no credible-set variant falls directly within PHB1's gene body; the only link is the lowest-PIP tail of ZNF652-AS1 variants lying near/within PHB1, plus an enhancer-sharing study (PMID 35325318) conducted in a different disease (gastric cancer) at a different variant (~2Mb offset from this locus's lead SNP). The red-team-skeptic explicitly cautioned against letting PHB1's richer, more legible mechanistic story promote it above ZNF652 purely on mechanism-richness grounds when its statistical/positional support is much weaker.

What would resolve this call: (1) confirmation from the supplementary per-locus gene tables of the Shrine/Wain lung-function GWAS papers (inaccessible to this pipeline's verifier) of whether ZNF652 — or PHB1, or another 17q21 gene — is the gene formally assigned to the FEV1 signal Open Targets is reporting; (2) a lung-tissue-specific eQTL/colocalization analysis directly testing whether this locus's credible-set variants regulate ZNF652 vs. PHB1 expression in relevant lung cell types; (3) any direct functional study of ZNF652 in airway/alveolar epithelium or lung immune cells, which does not currently exist in the literature identified here. Also note: the red-team-skeptic's critique for this locus was conducted with zero independent literature searches (WebSearch budget was exhausted before it began), so the absence of a CONTESTED verdict here should not be read as an exhaustively adversarial-checked result — see the audit file for the full disclosure.
