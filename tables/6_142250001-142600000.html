Prioritized gene

ADGRG6 — Confidence: MODERATE.

ADGRG6 is the only protein-coding gene physically overlapping the credible-set interval (confirmed independently by the researcher via direct Ensembl genomic-overlap query), is the nearest gene to the lead variant, and carries by far the strongest and most consistent evidence at every level: (1) statistical genetics — five independent, large, cross-ancestry GWAS replications for FEV1/FVC spanning 2010-2024 (Hancock, Soler Artigas, Shrine, Yang, Wang — all SUPPORTED above), plus a consistently dominant Open Targets L2G score (0.62-0.83) across COPD case-control studies in multiple smoking strata and spirometric traits, far ahead of the next candidate (HIVEP2, 0.06-0.11); (2) a specific, re-verified candidate functional variant (missense Ser123Gly) and an intronic eQTL variant at the locus (Hall et al.); (3) two independent, verified pieces of direct cell-type-specific functional/mechanistic evidence in disease-relevant human lung cells — iPSC-derived alveolar type II cells under cigarette-smoke injury (Werder et al.) and primary airway smooth muscle/bronchial epithelial cells with an explicit airway-remodeling mechanism (Hall et al.); (4) no literature found directly linking ADGRG6 to smoking-initiation/nicotine-dependence genetics (GSCAN), and the strongest argument against pure smoking-mediation is the gene's own independently replicated FEV1/FVC association, since spirometric traits are not simple proxies for smoking behavior.

Confidence is capped at MODERATE rather than HIGH for two disclosed reasons: (a) the only verified immune/inflammatory mechanistic evidence for ADGRG6 is a single downstream gene (IL33) induced in a non-immune cell type (airway smooth muscle) — this is real but thin relative to the heterogeneous immune programs (CD8+ T-cell, Th17/Tc17, macrophage, etc.) implicated in COPD generally, and no lung-tissue (whole-organ) eQTL colocalization was found in the Open Targets structured lookup to complement the airway-cell-type functional data; (b) two citations initially used to argue ADGRG6 is a directly "well-replicated primary COPD-GWAS locus" (Sakornsakolpat 2019, Jackson 2016) were found by the citation-verifier to overstate their own papers' actual findings (ADGRG6 appears only as a fine-mapped variant, not a primary locus, in Sakornsakolpat; Jackson's blanket "confirmed" framing is undercut by its own failed MMP12 replication) — so the COPD-specific (as opposed to lung-function-specific) statistical case rests more heavily on Open Targets L2G/credible-set evidence than on independently verified COPD case-control replication literature. Two independent red-team-skeptic search passes found no genuine contradicting evidence against ADGRG6.

NMBR (PoPS-prioritized) and HIVEP2 (a distant secondary Open Targets L2G hit) are both weaker candidates: NMBR's gene body lies ~103kb outside the physical locus interval, has no COPD/lung- function GWAS or L2G signal, and its only mechanistic literature (NMBR-dependent mast-cell recruitment) is drawn from a neonatal bronchopulmonary-dysplasia model, a disease context distinct from adult COPD. HIVEP2's gene body lies ~151kb outside the locus on the opposite side, has a weak/ distant L2G score, no colocalization support, and its only literature (a Treg-immunosuppression mechanism and a SARS-CoV-2 lung-epithelium finding) is genuine but not COPD-specific — most likely reflecting LD/regulatory bleed-through from the ADGRG6 signal rather than an independent causal gene. Neither is a close competitor to ADGRG6 on the evidence gathered; this is not a tied-LD-block call between genes of comparable support.
