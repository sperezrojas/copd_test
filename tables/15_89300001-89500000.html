Prioritized-gene synthesis

Prioritized gene: POLG. Confidence: LOW.

This is a genuinely difficult, low-confidence locus, and the call is close. No candidate gene at this locus has both a strong statistical/positional case and supporting mechanistic literature — the red-team-skeptic's locus-level assessment ("no candidate gene has both a strong physical/statistical case and supporting mechanistic literature... present this as an unresolved candidate-gene locus with multiple weak positional candidates") is accurate and is not contradicted by anything below.

POLG is named as the best single pick, by elimination rather than by strong positive evidence, for the following reasons:

  • It is the only candidate with any Open Targets genetic-association evidence for COPD at all (aggregate score 0.406 from gwas_credible_sets), even though this likely just reflects the credible set's genomic proximity to POLG rather than an independent line of support (red-team-skeptic's caveat, preserved above).
  • It is the conventional "nearest gene" call, though the skeptic correctly notes this is weak on its own: none of the three credible-set variants sits within POLG's coding sequence — they fall in an intergenic gap ~65+ kb away, more consistent with hitting an unannotated lncRNA transcript than any protein-coding gene directly.
  • ACAN — despite carrying the more prominent MAGMA/PoPS prioritization scores — is CONTESTED (see audit appendix): its gene body lies ~430-500 kb outside the stated locus window, Open Targets shows zero L2G/credible-set evidence for ACAN-COPD (actively failing to reproduce the MAGMA/PoPS signal), and no literature of any kind connects ACAN to lung or airway biology. The skeptic's assessment that this looks like "a locus-definition/ pipeline artifact" rather than genuine gene-level evidence is well-reasoned given the physical-distance mismatch, and ACAN is therefore ranked below POLG here.
  • RHCG is a legitimate positional runner-up (it is the only protein-coding gene fully inside the stated window, and the credible-set variants are roughly as close to RHCG as to POLG's coding sequence), but it has zero supporting or refuting literature — this is a real evidence gap that neither the researcher nor the red-team-skeptic could close (WebSearch budgets were exhausted before a dedicated RHCG-airway-biology check could be completed by either agent). This is flagged explicitly as unresolved, not decided against RHCG.
  • FANCI is a weaker runner-up: one indirect, non-causal marker association in a PM2.5 airway-inflammation model (FANCI as a DNA-damage-response readout, not itself tested).

What would resolve this tie: (1) a dedicated, unexhausted literature/database search for RHCG in airway epithelium (e.g., RHCG knockout mouse airway phenotypes, RHCG lung eQTL/ sQTL data, single-cell airway expression atlases) — this was not completed in this run and is the single most likely evidence gap to change the ranking; (2) direct confirmation of whether Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019's 82 COPD loci include this specific 15q26.1 region (would upgrade or further weaken the "nearest gene" framing for POLG); (3) a targeted check of whether this locus overlaps any smoking-initiation/cessation/heaviness GWAS signal (per this pipeline's smoking-mediation prior) — neither the researcher nor the skeptic could complete this check; (4) tissue-specific eQTL/sQTL/chromatin-interaction data (e.g., GTEx lung, ABC model) for the intergenic credible-set region, to determine which flanking gene (POLG, RHCG, or the unannotated lncRNA itself) is regulatorily linked to the lead variant; (5) an explanation from the internal pipeline for why MAGMA/PoPS assigned ACAN to this window despite its physical distance, which would either rehabilitate or further discredit ACAN as a candidate.

Given the physical location of the credible set outside POLG's coding sequence, the absence of any COPD- or spirometry-specific literature for any candidate gene, and the unresolved RHCG and smoking-locus questions, this locus's causal gene assignment should be treated as provisional. POLG is the best-supported single pick available from current evidence, but only marginally ahead of RHCG, and confidence is LOW — not MODERATE — because the supporting evidence is proximity-based rather than mechanism-based.
