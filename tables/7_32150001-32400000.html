Prioritized gene

PDE1C — Confidence: LOW

PDE1C is the only candidate gene nominated at this locus by every internal method (nearest gene, FLAMES, MAGMA, PoPS), all high-PIP credible-set variants are intronic within PDE1C, and it is consistently the top-L2G gene at the lead variant across the large majority of the 86 GWAS traits that overlap this variant in Open Targets — so by elimination and by regional fine-mapping, PDE1C is the best-supported gene at this locus. There is no credible rival gene in the record: AVL9 only out-competes PDE1C on L2G for adiposity/ body-composition traits (not smoking or lung traits), and LSM5 never wins.

However, confidence in PDE1C specifically for COPD (as opposed to merely being the correctly fine-mapped gene at this position) must be rated LOW, for reasons the red-team critique makes explicit and that this synthesis will not paper over:

  1. There is no independent COPD or FEV1/FEV1-FVC genetic-association evidence for PDE1C anywhere in this review — not in Open Targets (COPD score driven only by literature datatype, no genetic_association sub-score at all; FEV1/FEV1-FVC absent entirely) and not in the verified literature (zero PubMed hits for PDE1C+COPD or PDE1C+lung function). Prior #2 (concordance with spirometric traits strengthens prioritization) is not met at all at this locus — this is a real gap, not a forced-fit absence.
  2. The literature case for smoking-mediation (prior #1), while directionally the best- supported hypothesis, is thinner than the researcher's original framing suggested. Of three literature pillars offered, one (Cochrane/Schuit) is retracted and excluded entirely; one (Wu et al., melanoma) uses a different variant (rs215605, not the lead rs12701207) and shows a SNP×smoking-status interaction on melanoma risk — not a PDE1C-smoking-phenotype association — and should not be counted as an independent smoking-behavior pillar; and one (GSCAN) is only inferred to involve this locus via Open Targets credible-set overlap, with PDE1C's actual membership in that paper's own loci table unconfirmed. The genuinely strong evidence for smoking-mediation is the Open Targets L2G/credible-set data itself (86 credible sets, dominated by smoking/ behavioral traits, PDE1C consistently top-L2G) — which is structured database evidence, not verified literature, and is reported as such above.
  3. The pulmonary-vascular-remodeling/PAH literature (Rybalkin/Schermuly/Cai/Murray/ Knight) is real, well-replicated, and the most direct human-lung-tissue evidence found for PDE1C — but per the red-team critique, this is mechanistically distinct from airway-obstruction/emphysema biology, and PAH-type remodeling in COPD is generally understood as a downstream consequence of advanced disease/chronic hypoxia rather than an established upstream cause of airflow obstruction. This is hypothesis-generating (e.g., for a COPD-with-pulmonary-hypertension subgroup) rather than direct evidence for the core COPD phenotype.
  4. No immune/inflammatory mechanism was found connecting PDE1C to any COPD-relevant inflammatory program (T-cell, neutrophil, macrophage) — this domain does not appear to apply here based on current literature, beyond one unexplained Open Targets lymphocyte- count L2G association with no mechanistic support.
  5. A real, unresolved alternative-tissue gap: given that smoking-behavior/cognitive pleiotropy is the dominant signal at this locus, the more mechanistically apt biology to search would have been PDE1C's CNS/neuronal expression and any role in reward/ dopaminergic or nicotine-response pathways (by analogy to PDE1B's established striatal/ addiction biology) — this was not searched in this review round and is flagged as a follow-up rather than resolved.
  6. Population-stratification/pure pleiotropy was not tested as a null alternative. The locus's broad multi-domain trait promiscuity (smoking, cognition, lipids/adiposity, lymphocyte count) with no single unifying mechanism leaves this as a live, unexamined possibility.
  7. The red-team-skeptic disclosed that its own independent contradiction-search pass could not be executed this round (search budget exhausted) — so the absence of contradicting evidence found here should be read as untested, not as a clean bill of health.

Runner-up: none credible — AVL9 and LSM5 are the only other genes competing at L2G at this variant, and neither has any support for the smoking- or lung-relevant trait categories that dominate this locus's association profile.

What would resolve this: (a) direct confirmation (full-text/supplementary-table check) of whether PDE1C/this exact locus appears among the 279 signals in Shrine et al. 2019 or any other independent lung-function GWAS, which would materially raise or lower confidence; (b) a genuine smoking-behavior GWAS hit at the lead variant itself (rather than a different PDE1C variant in a melanoma gene-environment study), ideally with Mendelian- randomization or multivariable adjustment for smoking showing whether the COPD association at this locus attenuates once smoking behavior is accounted for; (c) any airway epithelial, alveolar, or COPD-relevant immune-cell PDE1C expression/functional data, which is entirely absent from the current record; (d) a search of PDE1C's CNS/neuronal/reward-pathway biology, given the locus's dominant behavioral-trait profile.
