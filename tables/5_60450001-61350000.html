Prioritized gene

Prioritized gene: LINC02057. Confidence: LOW.

This is a genuinely close call across a locus where every candidate gene's evidence is weak, and the call should be read as "least-bad positional candidate," not as a confident functional pick. Reasoning:

  • Fine-mapping/positional evidence favors LINC02057. The lead variant (rs194369, PIP=0.109) and the large majority of the credible set's higher-PIP variants sit physically within/near LINC02057 (see evidence table and researcher notes). In a credible set with no coding or splice variant anywhere (115 variants, all intron/UTR/intergenic/regulatory), positional concentration under a single gene is the strongest form of evidence still available, even though it is not corroborated by any mechanistic literature for LINC02057 (a real, acknowledged evidence gap, not a negative finding — see LINC02057 row above).
  • The pipeline's computational top pick, ZSWIM6, is weaker than it first appears. Despite being the FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS-prioritized gene, ZSWIM6 sits 150-500kb from the credible set's actual variant mass with no eQTL/Hi-C/ABC evidence located connecting the credible set to ZSWIM6 expression specifically. Per the red-team-skeptic critique, MAGMA/PoPS gene-level scores are known to be biased toward large, well-annotated, constrained genes and can reflect a neighboring gene's regulatory landscape without being the causal gene. ZSWIM6's genetic-association profile (Open Targets: 743 total associated traits) is dominated by its own dominant gain-of-function Mendelian disease (acromelic frontonasal dysostosis) and a bundle of neuropsychiatric/behavioral traits (schizophrenia 0.465, bipolar 0.341, alcohol drinking 0.329, autism 0.326, ADHD/OCD/ Tourette/MDD/anorexia ~0.214 each), with "smoking initiation" (0.347) only one thread among several and not even the dominant one. The critique explicitly WEAKENED the "ZSWIM6 = smoking-mediated COPD signal" narrative: it is a plausible inference, not a confirmed one (no primary smoking-GWAS paper was independently verified for this locus), and a generic neurobehavioral-pleiotropy explanation is at least as well supported by the same data.
  • NDUFAF2, ERCC8, and ELOVL7 all lack any COPD/lung-function-relevant common-variant or mechanistic evidence. NDUFAF2's one pulmonary case-report link (neonatal persistent pulmonary hypertension, PMID 40462436) was explicitly CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic on logic grounds — it reflects a mechanistically distinct pulmonary vascular phenotype from a severe recessive disease, and should not be read as support for a common-variant COPD mechanism. Its apparent Open Targets "COPD" score is a clinical/drug-precedence artifact, not genetic evidence. ERCC8 (Cockayne syndrome) and ELOVL7 (prostate-cancer lipogenesis) have no pulmonary or COPD-relevant literature at all beyond weak, unverified Open Targets smoking-initiation aggregate scores similar in magnitude to ZSWIM6's.
  • SMIM15 (nearest gene/ABC pick) and SMIM15-AS1 are essentially literature blanks — SMIM15's one candidate supporting citation (Abusheikha et al. 2025) was found by citation-verifier to be a CLAIM_MISMATCH (the paper does not actually mention SMIM15) and is excluded from the evidence table entirely; SMIM15-AS1 has zero PubMed-indexed literature. Both rest on proximity/ABC-score alone, with no supporting function.

Runner-up: ZSWIM6 (the pipeline's own computational top pick, but weakened per above). What would resolve this tie: direct eQTL or Hi-C/promoter-capture evidence linking the credible-set region to either LINC02057 or ZSWIM6 expression in lung/airway tissue; independent confirmation (not just an Open Targets aggregate score) of a genome-wide- significant smoking-initiation or cigarettes-per-day GWAS hit at this exact locus; and a direct check of the Shrine et al. 2019 and Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019 supplementary gene-level tables for this specific sub-locus, which neither the researcher nor the red-team-skeptic was able to independently confirm in this research pass (search-budget constraints on both sides — see critique). Given the critique's most important observation — the total absence of FEV1/FEV1-FVC concordance for any gene at this locus, despite Shrine and Sakornsakolpat being companion COPD/lung-function GWAS papers from the same consortium generation — the study team should also weigh the alternative possibility, raised explicitly by red-team-skeptic, that this locus's COPD signal is a statistical/pleiotropic artifact (e.g., driven by smoking behavior, psychiatric comorbidity, or non-pulmonary case-ascertainment correlates) rather than a genuine pulmonary-biology locus with a single identifiable causal gene at all. Confidence is marked LOW (not INSUFFICIENT) because the positional/fine-mapping case for LINC02057 is real and specific, even though it lacks any corroborating functional literature.
