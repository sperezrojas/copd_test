Prioritized gene: THSD4 — confidence MODERATE

THSD4 is the best-supported candidate at this locus, but the evidence does not support HIGH confidence and the skeptic's critique materially changes how it should be read.

Why THSD4 over CT62/LRRC49: THSD4 is the only protein-coding gene physically overlapping the locus and credible set; it is the original and repeatedly-replicated discovery gene for the quantitative FEV1/FVC signal at 15q23 (Repapi 2010, Yao 2013 in an independent Hutterite population); and it is the sole Open Targets L2G-predicted gene across all 88 GWAS credible sets examined at this locus, including for COPD directly. LRRC49 does not physically overlap the locus or THSD4's gene body at all (its MAGMA hit is most parsimoniously a window artifact per both the researcher and skeptic), was never an L2G hit, has a ~13x weaker COPD association score than THSD4, and its only substantive gene-specific literature is unrelated breast-cancer biology — it is NOT SUPPORTED as a candidate and can be confidently deprioritized. CT62 (an lncRNA nested in THSD4's first intron) has essentially zero dedicated literature and no Open Targets genetic-association signal at all; per the skeptic, this should be read as INSUFFICIENT evidence rather than confident exclusion, since no lung-tissue expression data was checked to directly test whether CT62 itself could plausibly act in lung — but there is no positive evidence for it either, so it does not compete with THSD4 for the "most likely causal" call.

Why not HIGH confidence for THSD4: The skeptic's critique identifies real, specific gaps that cap this at MODERATE rather than HIGH: (1) THSD4's mechanistic case (fibrillin-1 microfibril assembly / elastic-fiber biology) rests entirely on non-lung tissue — a thoracic aortic aneurysm mouse model (Elbitar 2020) and a bone-aging mouse model (Yin 2024) — with zero direct evidence of a lung/alveolar/emphysema phenotype for THSD4 loss-of-function in any species; this is a cross-tissue analogy, not a demonstrated lung mechanism. (2) The only molecular-QTL (eQTL) evidence tying the GWAS signal to THSD4 expression is a single, very recent (2026), as-yet-uncorroborated study (Kim et al.) that names THSD4 alongside three other candidate genes, not uniquely. (3) THSD4's COPD case-control replication history (as distinct from its robust quantitative FEV1/FVC signal) is genuinely mixed: Hardin 2012 and Yang 2013 are independent negative replications, and Soler Artigas 2011 found only a "suggestive" signal even in a >31,000-person cohort. (4) The same credible set colocalizes just as strongly (H4>0.95) with blood pressure, pulse pressure, and aortic-diameter/distensibility GWAS as with lung-function GWAS, raising a genuine open question — not yet resolved by any cited evidence — of whether the causal mechanism at this locus is lung-intrinsic or a tissue-general vascular/ connective-tissue mechanism that happens to also register in spirometry. (5) The argument that this locus is not smoking-mediated (based partly on Open Targets colocalization with COPD in never-smokers) is only partly literature-grounded — the "zero PubMed hits for THSD4+smoking" finding is a verified absence-of-literature result, but the never-smoker colocalization itself is structured database output, not an independently verified literature claim, and should be weighted accordingly.

What would resolve this: (a) a THSD4 lung/alveolar-specific knockout or conditional knockout phenotype (emphysema susceptibility, alveolar elastic fiber structure) in animal models; (b) independent replication of the Kim et al. 2026 lung eQTL colocalization, ideally showing THSD4 as uniquely top-ranked versus ADAM19/C4B/PSMA4; (c) GTEx or equivalent lung- tissue expression data for CT62 to fully rule it out; (d) statistical fine-mapping or tissue-specific eQTL work distinguishing whether the vascular/connective-tissue colocalization and the lung-function colocalization reflect the same or distinct causal mechanisms.
