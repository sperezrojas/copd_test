Prioritized gene

ADGRA1 — Confidence: LOW.

ADGRA1 is the best-supported single candidate at this locus by statistical/fine-mapping criteria: it contains the dominant share of credible-set PIP mass (the lead variant rs2748432, PIP=0.462, plus the second-highest variant rs761777, PIP=0.117, and several lower-PIP variants are all ADGRA1 intronic/downstream), and it is the top-scoring Open Targets L2G gene for 3 of the 4 GWAS traits indexed at this locus's credible sets (systolic blood pressure 0.479, GERD 0.745, back pain 0.465), narrowly losing only the insomnia trait to KNDC1 (0.261 vs 0.284). Per red-team-skeptic, this statistical dominance is genuine and not simply an LD/nearest-gene-bias artifact shared with KNDC1, since the two genes' credible-set variant clusters are largely non-overlapping.

However, the confidence is LOW, not higher, for reasons the evidence table above and the audit appendix document explicitly: no COPD, lung-function, spirometry, or any other respiratory-disease GWAS signal is indexed for this locus anywhere in Open Targets, and no PubMed/bioRxiv literature search (by either the researcher or the red-team-skeptic, independently) found any pulmonary or immune-relevant biology for ADGRA1 at all. Every VERIFIED functional citation for ADGRA1 places it exclusively in CNS/neuroendocrine biology — hypothalamic sympathetic/thermogenic regulation (PMID 33824276) and hippocampal inhibitory-interneuron synaptic function (PMID 41961591/40766348). Per Prior #2 (spirometry concordance), this is exactly the pattern that "warrants consideration of alternative pathways," and the red-team-skeptic explicitly argued this pattern should be read as active evidence against a lung-intrinsic mechanism, not merely a neutral evidentiary gap: "given ... dominant fine-mapping mass ... exhaustive functional literature in only two organ systems (hypothalamus, hippocampus) with a clear, mechanistically dense, unrelated biology ... zero COPD/lung-function GWAS or coloc signal indexed anywhere ... this pattern is more consistent with a locus that is pleiotropic for neurobehavioral/autonomic traits and reaches COPD via smoking-behavior or another indirect route than with an unresolved-but-plausible lung mechanism."

The most plausible alternative explanation raised by both the researcher and red-team-skeptic — Prior #1, smoking-behavior mediation — is itself only a hypothesis, not established evidence: it rests entirely on an Open Targets database colocalization field (this locus's indexed GWAS credible sets colocalize strongly, h4=0.84-0.99, with smoking-initiation GWAS signals), which neither agent was able to confirm against the primary smoking-GWAS literature. The one citation submitted to ground this hypothesis in primary literature (intended to be Liu et al. 2019 GSCAN) was NOT_FOUND by citation-verifier — the researcher cited the wrong PMID, which resolves to an unrelated Eucalyptus GWAS paper — so as things stand, the smoking-mediation hypothesis for this specific locus is unconfirmed in the literature, not verified.

Runner-up: KNDC1. KNDC1 carries materially less fine-mapping mass (~0.07 combined PIP vs ADGRA1's ~0.58+) and is the ABC-prioritized (enhancer-based, not fine-mapping-based) candidate. It shares ADGRA1's pattern exactly: purely neuronal functional literature (cerebellar dendritic-growth regulation, PMID 28264072), no pulmonary/immune biology, no COPD/lung-function GWAS signal, and the same unconfirmed smoking-mediation hypothesis. Per red-team-skeptic, KNDC1's case is "thin on both sides" and its statistical support is weaker and more susceptible to ordinary LD/nearest-gene uncertainty than ADGRA1's.

Not escalated: VENTX. VENTX has the single most COPD-mechanistically-relevant biology at this locus (macrophage terminal differentiation and proinflammatory activation, PMID 21670496, directly on Prior #3) but zero credible-set variant lies within ~77 kb of it. Per red-team-skeptic, this should be treated as a flagged hypothesis-generating aside, not a competing prioritized candidate — escalating it would mean letting biological plausibility override the complete absence of locus-specific statistical support, which this synthesis declines to do.

Not competitive: TCERG1L, UTF1. TCERG1L (PoPS-prioritized) sits ~1.74 Mb outside the analyzed region entirely — a materially weaker positional case than even a distant-but-in-region gene — and all three of its VERIFIED citations are unrelated-tissue epigenetic/cancer-biomarker findings with no lung or immune relevance. UTF1 has only a non-specific multi-organ epithelial expression survey mentioning lung tissue among many others, no credible-set variant assigned, and no COPD-specific finding.

What would resolve this call: (1) confirmation, using the correct GSCAN GWAS Catalog accession/ PMID (per red-team-skeptic, the correct paper is Liu et al. 2019, PMID 30643251 — not independently re-verified in this run, flagged for a follow-up citation-verifier pass if this locus is prioritized further), of whether this exact 10:133.05-133.25 Mb interval is genome-wide significant for smoking initiation, cessation, or heaviness; (2) any lung-tissue eQTL/pQTL colocalization for ADGRA1 or KNDC1 with the COPD signal at this locus (none found in Open Targets as queried); (3) a direct FEV1/FEV1-FVC spirometry GWAS association at this interval, which is currently entirely absent from both the literature and the Open Targets database as reviewed. Absent that, this locus should be treated by the study team as a statistically dominant but mechanistically unresolved (and plausibly indirect/smoking-behavior-mediated rather than lung-intrinsic) candidate-gene call, not a biologically confirmed pulmonary COPD gene.
