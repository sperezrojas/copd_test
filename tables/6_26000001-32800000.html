COPD locus evidence table: 6:26,000,001-32,800,000 (nearest gene HLA-DQA1)

Region: 6:26,000,001-32,800,000 (GRCh38), the extended MHC/HLA class II region on chromosome 6. Lead variant: rs9272466 (6:32,637,919, intron variant in HLA-DQA1, PIP=0.403). Credible set: 17 variants, all clustered within a ~24 kb span (6:32,619,017-32,642,624), including one missense variant (rs9272785, PIP=0.057).

IMPORTANT SCOPE AND CAUTION NOTE: This 6.8 Mb window spans one of the most gene-dense, structurally complex, extended-linkage-disequilibrium regions in the entire human genome (HLA-A/B/C, HLA-DRB1/DQA1/DQB1/DPA1/DPB1, C4A/C4B, TNF, and dozens of other genes). Because all 17 credible-set variants cluster tightly at the HLA-DQA1 locus itself, candidate-gene research for this locus was deliberately scoped to the genes at/immediately flanking that cluster (HLA-DQA1, HLA-DQB1, HLA-DRB1) plus the two genes nominated by the internal statistical pipeline via genome-wide gene-based tests (CSNK2B via MAGMA, HIST1H4A/H4C3 via PoPS) — not an exhaustive enumeration of every gene across the full 6.8 Mb span. MHC fine-mapping is notoriously unreliable for pinpointing a single causal gene because of extreme, extended LD across the classical HLA genes (HLA-DRB1, HLA-DQA1, HLA-DQB1, and neighboring genes are largely co-inherited as haplotypes). This locus's causal gene, if resolvable at all, should be treated with extra caution regardless of any single gene's apparent statistical support. Even the field's own reference COPD GWAS (Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019) excludes the MHC region from its standard-threshold primary locus list, analyzing it separately — an acknowledgment from within the field that this region does not behave like a typical, cleanly resolved locus.

This absence should not be read as evidence against a causal role for any of these genes; the literature evidence assembled above is doing essentially all of the evidentiary work at this locus.

Prioritized gene

Prioritized call: HLA-DQA1/HLA-DQB1/HLA-DRB1 haplotype block (classical HLA class II region) — no single confidently causal gene named. Confidence: LOW.

This is one of the loci in this review where naming a single, confidently causal protein-coding gene is not supported by the evidence, and doing so would manufacture certainty the data do not have. The reasoning:

  • The statistical signal is real and substantial (lead PIP=0.403, a tight 24 kb credible-set cluster including one missense variant), and it concordantly appears across multiple independent large-scale spirometry and COPD/asthma-COPD-overlap GWAS in this haplotype block (Hancock 2012, Wain 2015, John 2022), with some evidence explicitly reported as smoking-independent (Wain 2015: stronger in never-smokers) — satisfying biological prior #2 (spirometric concordance) and offering some evidence against pure smoking-mediation (prior #1).
  • However, per the red-team critique, none of the population-scale GWAS hits actually localize inside HLA-DQA1 itself — their lead SNPs are intergenic or nearer HLA-DQA2/HLA-DQB1 — so citing them as HLA-DQA1-specific evidence reflects nearest-gene/LD-block bias rather than gene-specific resolution. HLA-DQA1's own most specific literature (its missense credible-set variant, rs9272785) is tuberculosis-susceptibility evidence, not COPD evidence.
  • HLA-DRB1 has the most direct COPD case-control/spirometry allele-level literature (Díaz-Peña 2020), but this is CONTESTED: a small, unreplicated Chilean cohort finding directly conflicts in direction of effect with a separate small occupational-cohort study (Karzakova 2017) for a related DRB1*01-family allele.
  • The mechanism invoked throughout — CD4+ T-cell antigen presentation and autoimmune/infectious-disease susceptibility (tuberculosis, celiac disease, type 1 diabetes, rheumatoid arthritis) — is imported wholesale from other diseases and has not been directly tested in COPD lung tissue or COPD immune cells; per prior #3, this is a plausible but unconfirmed inflammatory mechanism, and it points toward a type-2/eosinophilic or asthma-COPD-overlap pathway (John 2022) rather than classical neutrophilic, smoking-driven COPD biology.
  • Even the field's own primary reference COPD GWAS (Sakornsakolpat 2019) excludes the MHC region from its standard locus list, underscoring that this region is not expected to yield a cleanly resolved single-gene signal by ordinary GWAS/fine-mapping standards.
  • CSNK2B and HIST1H4A/H4C3 (the MAGMA and PoPS picks) are effectively refuted as candidates: CSNK2B has zero COPD-relevant evidence and an extreme loss-of-function-intolerance profile inconsistent with a common-variant disease gene, most parsimoniously explained as a MAGMA artifact of dense regional LD; HIST1H4A/H4C3 has zero COPD-relevant evidence, sits 6.6 Mb from the credible-set cluster, and is confounded by dozens of near-identical histone paralogs.

What would resolve this: HLA-specific fine-mapping using classical allele/haplotype imputation (e.g., HIBAG, SNP2HLA) with COPD/lung-function as the outcome in a large, ancestrally diverse cohort, ideally paired with cell-type-resolved (CD4+ T-cell, alveolar macrophage, airway epithelial) expression/eQTL colocalization specific to HLA-DQA1 vs. HLA-DQB1 vs. HLA-DRB1 vs. HLA-DQA2, and a direct test of whether the association is mediated by antigen-presentation/autoimmune pathways versus a distinct, non-immune mechanism. Until such data exist, this locus should be reported to the study team as "HLA class II region (DRB1-DQA1-DQB1 haplotype), gene-level causality unresolved" rather than attributed to HLA-DQA1 specifically, notwithstanding that HLA-DQA1 is the nearest gene and carries the lead/missense variant.
