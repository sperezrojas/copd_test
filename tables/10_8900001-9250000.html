Notes on evidence weight and confound handling

  • Smoking-mediation check: No PubMed evidence was found linking GATA3 or this locus to smoking-behavior GWAS traits (initiation, cessation, heaviness, nicotine dependence). The Shrine 2023 lung-function GWAS excluded 8 signals it determined were primarily smoking-driven; whether this locus was among them could not be confirmed from accessible text. On the evidence assembled, this locus does not appear to be a smoking-behavior GWAS locus.
  • ACOS/allergic-comorbidity confound: This is the dominant alternative explanation surfaced by both the researcher and red-team-skeptic. The locus is overwhelmingly an asthma/allergic-disease/eosinophil-trait locus by GWAS credible-set membership (per Open Targets, see summary below) and by the independently verified literature above. The one GWAS specifically designed to separate asthma-predominant from COPD-predominant genetic signal in asthma-COPD overlap (John et al. 2022, PMID 35104449) did NOT find GATA3 among its significant loci. Whether the "COPD" cases in the underlying COPD GWAS cohorts were spirometry-confirmed (GOLD-defined) versus self-report/ICD-code-defined — which would bear directly on how much of this signal could reflect misclassified/comorbid asthma — was not resolved by either the researcher or the skeptic and is flagged as an open question for the study team.

Prioritized gene

Prioritized gene: GATA3 — Confidence: LOW

GATA3 is named here because it is the only plausible protein-coding candidate physically/regulatorily associated with this locus — no competing gene exists in the interval, and a long-range enhancer 930kb from GATA3's gene body is the most parsimonious explanation for the credible set's location. But this is a recommendation by elimination, not by direct COPD-specific support, and confidence must be scored accordingly.

The evidence assembled is, on balance, a well-replicated asthma/allergic-Th2 locus whose COPD GWAS association most likely reflects shared genetic liability with asthma and/or comorbid-asthma (ACOS/misclassification) contamination of COPD case-control cohorts, rather than COPD-intrinsic biology:

  • Open Targets shows negligible aggregate COPD association for GATA3 versus a much stronger asthma signal, and the only locus-specific L2G score at this exact variant names GATA3 for allergic asthma (0.615), not COPD or lung function — despite a COPD credible set existing at this same variant.
  • The COPD credible set colocalizes at h4>0.99 with FEV1 and several asthma/allergic credible sets, which is evidence for one shared signal, not independent COPD-specific support layered on the asthma signal.
  • The single functional (CRISPR) study of the exact fine-mapped lead variant (Kumagai et al. 2024, PMID 38923989) found rs12413578 itself has NO effect on GATA3 expression in Th2 cells — the statistical fine-mapping (PIP=0.997) and functional causality diverge for this specific variant, which is a genuine weakness in the causal chain from credible set to GATA3.
  • The one GWAS purpose-built to separate asthma-predominant from COPD-predominant signal in asthma-COPD overlap (John et al. 2022, PMID 35104449) did not find GATA3/10p14 among its 8 significant loci.
  • The clinical trial most likely to show a COPD-relevant GATA3 effect (Greulich et al. 2018 SB010 DNAzyme trial, PMID 29615049, in COPD patients pre-selected for sputum eosinophilia) engaged the target (eosinophils fell) but did not improve lung function or symptoms.
  • The one study that directly clustered the asthma-COPD-overlap phenotype by transcription-factor expression (Hirai et al. 2017, PMID 28658564) found that phenotype was paradoxically Th2-LOW by TBX21/GATA3 ratio, undercutting a simple "GATA3 drives ACOS" narrative.
  • Countervailing this: a genuine, if minority, GATA3+/ILC2-driven eosinophilic-COPD endotype is measurable in human COPD lung tissue (Jogdand et al. 2020, PMID 32060064) independent of asthma comorbidity in that cohort, so a "purely an asthma-misclassification artifact, zero COPD-relevant biology" reading would also overreach in the other direction.

There is no credible alternative candidate gene to name as a runner-up at this specific locus — the Ensembl-interval search found no other protein-coding gene — so this is not a two-gene tie in the usual sense. Rather, the uncertainty is about whether any single gene at this locus should be described as "the COPD gene" at all, versus the locus being better characterized as a pleiotropic asthma/Th2-allergic locus with a secondary, likely non-causal, statistical footprint in COPD GWAS. Confidence is marked LOW (not INSUFFICIENT, since GATA3 is not merely unstudied — it has been extensively studied and the evidence specifically weighs against a COPD-intrinsic mechanism rather than being silent on it). What would resolve this: (1) confirmation of whether the underlying COPD GWAS cohorts used spirometry-confirmed (GOLD) versus self-report/ICD-based case definitions, to quantify likely asthma-misclassification contamination; (2) a COPD-specific (not lung-function-general) eQTL/pQTL colocalization analysis at this exact locus, which was not available in Open Targets; (3) independent replication of the ILC2/GATA3-in-COPD mechanistic literature outside the one research group that produced most of it.
