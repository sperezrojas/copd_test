Prioritized gene

ILRUN — confidence LOW.

This call is close and should be read as such. Among the three original prioritized candidates, ILRUN and BLTP3A both have real claims and neither is decisively supported:

  • ILRUN is the nearest gene, hosts the lead credible-set variant (rs6907508, PIP=0.21) and additional credible-set variants, has the strongest FEV1/lung-function L2G evidence of the three genes in Open Targets (top score 0.596, 20 rows) with zero smoking-initiation L2G evidence (clean of the smoking-mediation confound that prior #1 asks us to screen for), and is highly LOF-constrained (pLI-like=1.0), consistent with a dosage-sensitive/essential gene. Its weakness is that Open Targets shows zero COPD-specific credible-set evidence for ILRUN despite the locus itself being a validated COPD GWAS hit — most plausibly a data-coverage gap in Open Targets' COPD-credible-set mapping rather than a true absence of effect (the same locus clearly IS COPD-associated per this pipeline's own input; the discordance is between two OT-internal disease/trait mappings, not between OT and the underlying GWAS). No lung- or COPD-specific literature was found for ILRUN; its best-characterized mechanism (IRF3/type-I interferon/TNF-α suppression) is real but was demonstrated only in non-lung cell systems, per the skeptic's critique (row-level verdicts above).
  • BLTP3A (runner-up) has the single strongest direct COPD L2G score (0.417) and FEV1 L2G score (0.695) of the three genes, and hosts the second/third-highest-PIP credible-set variants after the lead — genetically it looks at least as strong as ILRUN by raw Open Targets metrics. But per prior #1 it also carries a nontrivial smoking-initiation L2G score (0.480) that ILRUN entirely lacks, meaning BLTP3A's COPD/FEV1 signal cannot be cleanly separated from a smoking-behavior-mediated pathway using available data. The skeptic additionally notes that BLTP3A's only tissue-specific literature — a link to neuronal mitochondrial dynamics in Parkinson's disease (Jansen et al. 2017) — is, if anything, mildly consistent with a neurobiological/smoking-behavior mechanism rather than intrinsic pulmonary biology. BLTP3A is also LOF-tolerant (not dosage-sensitive), unlike ILRUN.
  • SNRPC is weak on every metric (OT and literature) and is most consistent with a MAGMA/ABC proximity artifact, per both the researcher and the skeptic.
  • SPDEF has by far the strongest COPD-relevant mechanistic literature at this locus (a well-established causal driver of goblet-cell hyperplasia/mucus metaplasia, including in a cigarette-smoke COPD mouse model) but was not flagged by any of the four prioritization methods and has no credible-set variant annotated to it — the skeptic rates its attachment to this specific GWAS signal as an unsupported "candidate gene" leap (WEAKENED/bordering CONTESTED), noting that ABC — a method purpose-built to detect exactly this kind of long-range regulatory link — pointed to SNRPC, not SPDEF. SPDEF is therefore not selected as the top pick despite its mechanistic appeal.
  • ANKS1A, PACSIN1, TAF11 have no plausible COPD-relevant evidence (PACSIN1 and TAF11 were marked CONTESTED and excluded from the table above; ANKS1A's citations are all in unrelated tissues/diseases).

Why ILRUN over BLTP3A: the deciding factor is prior #1 — distinguishing smoking-mediated from pulmonary-biology-mediated signals. ILRUN's genetic profile (strong FEV1, zero smoking-initiation signal) is the cleaner pulmonary-biology case, even though its literature support is generic/non-lung and its OT COPD score is null. BLTP3A's stronger raw OT COPD/FEV1 scores are undercut by a real, data-supported smoking-confound risk that ILRUN does not share.

What would resolve the tie: (1) a lung-tissue eQTL/colocalization study testing whether the credible-set variants regulate ILRUN, BLTP3A, or SNRPC expression specifically in airway/lung tissue; (2) a smoking-adjusted COPD or FEV1 GWAS at this locus to test whether the association attenuates after conditioning on smoking behavior (would argue against BLTP3A and, if it also attenuates ILRUN's FEV1 signal, would undercut ILRUN too); (3) single-cell/airway-epithelium expression and functional data for ILRUN and BLTP3A specifically (neither has ever been studied in lung tissue); (4) a dedicated regulatory (e.g. Hi-C/ABC re-analysis or reporter-assay) study testing whether the credible-set region contacts the SPDEF promoter, to adjudicate the skeptic's rejection of SPDEF as a locus-specific candidate. This remains the study team's call to make; ILRUN is offered here as the best-supported single pick, not a settled answer.
