Prioritized gene

FAM227B — confidence LOW. This is a genuinely close call between FAM227B and DTWD1, and the red-team-skeptic explicitly recommended treating them as "roughly tied... rather than DTWD1 ranked first." I am naming FAM227B as the marginal best pick, not because the literature establishes a COPD mechanism for it (it does not — see the FAM227B rows above, all of which are either tangential or evidence for a different trait/tissue), but because it is anchored in this specific study's own internal statistical fine-mapping: rs12232354 sits in a FAM227B intron and carries this locus's highest posterior inclusion probability (PIP=0.461) by a wide margin over any other single variant, and this is the one piece of evidence in this locus that is directly about this GWAS rather than an externally aggregated or cross-cohort proxy.

DTWD1 is the explicit runner-up and should be weighed nearly equally. Its case rests on (a) the highest Open Targets GWAS-credible-set-derived COPD score of the six candidates (0.502) and (b) a cross-ancestry Taiwanese COPD GWAS (PMID 38886662) explicitly naming DTWD1 among loci "previously reported in other populations... replicated and confirmed." Both the researcher and red-team-skeptic flagged the same fundamental weakness in this evidence, however: PMID 38886662's "DTWD1" mention is an abstract-level, nearest-gene-style label from a different cohort's own GWAS hit — it is not a fine-mapped or functionally validated attribution, the original discovery study it is replicating could not be identified, and because DTWD1 and FAM227B are physically close in the same LD block, this "replication" may simply be re-tagging the identical underlying statistical signal under a different nearest-gene convention rather than providing independent gene-specific evidence favoring DTWD1 over FAM227B. DTWD1's only clear molecular characterization (a ubiquitous tRNA-modification housekeeping enzyme, PMID 31804502) offers no lung- or immune-specific mechanism to break the tie in its favor either.

What would resolve this tie: (1) identifying the original discovery GWAS behind PMID 38886662's "DTWD1" mention and checking whether its own fine-mapping/credible set favors DTWD1 or FAM227B specifically; (2) a direct lung-tissue eQTL or colocalization analysis for COPD or FEV1/FEV1-FVC (not a different trait like hyperthyroidism) distinguishing which gene's expression the credible-set variants actually regulate; (3) LD/statistical-fine-mapping data confirming whether DTWD1's and FAM227B's reported COPD signals in different cohorts are in fact the same haplotype or two distinct, independent signals. Absent that, this call should be treated as provisional. FGF7 (plausible general biology, weak/mixed direct evidence), SECISBP2L (PoPS- prioritized but genetically and mechanistically pointing toward lung cancer, not COPD), GALK2, and COPS2 are all lower-priority candidates at this locus based on the evidence assembled here.
