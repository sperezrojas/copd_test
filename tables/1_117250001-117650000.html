Prioritized gene

MAN1A2 — Confidence: LOW.

MAN1A2 is the best-supported single candidate at this locus, but the support is weak and the confidence level should not be overstated. It is the nearest gene to the lead variant rs1289960, is ABC-prioritized, and physically harbors essentially the entire credible set (all intronic/UTR, MODIFIER-impact variants). No other gene in this locus comes close to this degree of physical/fine-mapping anchoring — PTGFRN (the only other prioritized candidate) is confirmed to lie 360-473 kb outside the locus and credible set entirely, and its candidacy rests solely on PoPS's genome-wide network-similarity statistic with zero gene-specific genetic or functional evidence (see audit: red-team-skeptic judged this "bordering on should not be retained as a candidate gene for this locus at all"). LINC01525 and VPS25P1 have essentially no independent supporting evidence beyond their positional credible-set assignment, and TENT5C was not implicated by any prioritization method.

However, the case for MAN1A2 acting through pulmonary biology specifically is not well supported by the verified evidence. Open Targets shows zero COPD genetic_association or L2G evidence for MAN1A2 despite it sitting directly under a genome-wide-significant COPD locus, and no association with FEV1 or pulmonary function measurement was found in Open Targets or the literature search — a genuine warning sign under this pipeline's spirometry-concordance prior. The one directly relevant mechanistic paper (Ningappa et al. 2021, PMID 34366878) shows Man1a2 homozygous-null mice have impaired lung ciliation and neonatal respiratory failure via ER-stress/UPR pathways, which is a biologically plausible, smoking-independent link to airway mucociliary biology — but it is a complete-knockout developmental/neonatal phenotype, and the credible-set variants here are regulatory/ hypomorphic at most, so extrapolating knockout severity to the effect of common noncoding variants is a substantial leap that the red-team-skeptic flagged as not adequately foregrounded. Meanwhile, MAN1A2 does carry a genuine Open Targets genetic_association signal with smoking initiation (0.534) that exceeds its COPD signal — consistent with this pipeline's Prior 1 (the parent COPD GWAS is not smoking-adjusted) — but no primary smoking-initiation GWAS paper (e.g., GSCAN/Saunders) naming MAN1A2 could be confirmed via PubMed search, so this remains an unconfirmed database-only inference, not an established mediation pathway. A third, equally plausible alternative raised by the skeptic is that MAN1A2 is simply a broadly pleiotropic, promiscuous local-eQTL/pQTL "hot gene" that recurs across many unrelated GWAS/omics screens (osteoarthritis CD4+ T-cell eQTL, venous thromboembolism PWAS, serum urate, COVID-19 susceptibility, cardiac autophagy, gout, hepatocarcinogenesis-via-paralog) — a pattern at least as consistent with nonspecific regulatory-hub status as with tissue-specific causal biology at this locus.

Runner-up: PTGFRN, but only on the strength of the PoPS statistical call — it is physically outside the locus, has no credible-set overlap, no Open Targets genetic evidence, and no COPD/lung-relevant literature of any kind.

What would resolve this: (1) direct confirmation (or refutation) of MAN1A2 in the primary GSCAN/Saunders smoking-initiation GWAS supplementary tables, to settle whether the smoking-mediation hypothesis has primary-literature grounding or is an Open Targets aggregation artifact; (2) a targeted look at FEV1/FEV1-FVC GWAS/colocalization data for this exact locus (not found by either the Open Targets lookup or the literature search here) — its continued absence would further weaken the pulmonary-biology case for MAN1A2; (3) direct lung-tissue or airway-epithelium eQTL/colocalization analysis for MAN1A2 in COPD-relevant cohorts (e.g., GTEx lung, or COPD case-control expression datasets), since no such analysis was identified in this search. Given the absence of COPD-specific genetic association, spirometric concordance, or confirmed smoking-GWAS support, this pick is correctly labeled LOW confidence rather than MODERATE or HIGH — the study team should weigh MAN1A2's strong positional/fine-mapping anchoring against the near-total absence of disease-mechanism-specific human genetic evidence before prioritizing it for follow-up.
