Open Targets summary (structured database lookup — not a verified literature citation; not passed through citation-verifier or red-team-skeptic)

SYT14 (ENSG00000143469), chr1:209,900,923-210,171,389 (GRCh38), within the locus window.

  • Genetic constraint (gnomAD): LoF oe = 0.411 (90% CI 0.309-0.553), most-constrained bin of 6 — a fairly LoF-constrained gene despite sparse disease literature. Missense oe = 0.760; synonymous oe = 0.827.
  • Disease association (aggregate score across all Open Targets datasources, 267 total diseases with any recorded association): ZERO recorded association with COPD (MONDO_0005002), FEV1 (EFO_0004314), or FEV1/FVC (EFO_0004713) — none of these three terms appear anywhere in SYT14's 267 disease associations. Top association: autosomal recessive spinocerebellar ataxia 11 (score 0.51). Weak associations present for smoking initiation (score 0.149, genetic_association 0.245) and smoking behavior (score 0.057, genetic_association 0.094).
  • L2G / colocalization at the exact lead variant (1:210,156,815 C>T / rs7527845): 5 GWAS credible sets in Open Targets' catalog contain this variant, none for COPD or lung function. In every one, SYT14 is the top L2G-predicted gene (score ~0.39-0.45), consistently beating neighboring gene SERTAD4 (L2G ~0.10-0.11): "Age at first birth" (GCST90000050, GCST90000048), "Refractive error" (GCST010002), "Age of smoking initiation (MTAG)" (GCST007462), "LDL cholesterol" (GCST90475420). These signals colocalize with each other very strongly (H4 = 0.989-0.990 via COLOC_PIP_ECAVIAR), consistent with one shared pleiotropic regulatory signal across multiple unrelated traits. No COPD or lung-function GWAS signal is registered at this exact variant in Open Targets at all — this is an absence-of-data caveat (the COPD GWAS behind this internal pipeline may not be integrated into Open Targets), not confirmation of a null pulmonary effect.
  • Search artifact noted: an Open Targets entity search on rsID rs7527845 also returned ATP13A2 (ENSG00000159363) as a hit; confirmed via genomic-location query that ATP13A2 sits at chr1:16,985,958-17,011,928, ~193 Mb from this locus. This is not a real candidate gene here and was excluded from all downstream research and synthesis.
  • SERTAD4 (L2G runner-up gene at the lead variant, score ~0.10-0.11 vs. SYT14's ~0.39-0.45 in the same credible sets) was not independently queried for constraint/disease association in Open Targets, since it is not part of the locus input file's candidate gene list; flagged here (and by red-team-skeptic) as an incomplete check.

Prioritized gene

SYT14 — Confidence: LOW

SYT14 is the only real candidate gene at this locus: nearest gene, FLAMES, MAGMA, and PoPS all independently converge on it, the entire 13-variant credible set is intronic/3'UTR to SYT14 with no ambiguous neighbor annotation, and Open Targets' own L2G analysis at the exact lead variant picks SYT14 over the only plausible neighbor (SERTAD4) by a wide margin (~0.39-0.45 vs ~0.10-0.11) across five independent GWAS traits. Gene identity at this locus is therefore well-supported and not seriously contested.

What is NOT well-supported is any specific mechanism connecting SYT14 to COPD. Verified literature and the Open Targets structured lookup agree that SYT14 has no documented pulmonary/airway biology at all (0 PubMed hits combining SYT14 with lung/COPD/airway terms; 0 of 267 Open Targets disease associations touch COPD, FEV1, or FEV1/FVC). Its best-supported biology is neuronal/synaptic: it causes autosomal-recessive spinocerebellar ataxia 11 (PMID 21835308), and a mouse-knockout study mechanistically ties it to bipolar disorder/mania (PMID 39966626), with additional rare-variant schizophrenia/bipolar evidence (PMID 34947986). The researcher initially proposed this locus is best explained as smoking-behavior-mediated (Prior 1) on the strength of Open Targets' colocalization of the lead variant with an "age of smoking initiation" GWAS signal. Red-team review WEAKENED this specific directional claim: the smoking-initiation link is aggregator-only (the primary GSCAN paper, PMID 30643251, could not be confirmed by citation-verifier to name SYT14 in its own text), the same lead variant colocalizes just as strongly with four other, non-smoking traits (age at first birth x2, refractive error, LDL cholesterol) that have no more mechanistic connection to COPD than smoking does, and the one citation that would have let anyone check the Prior-2 spirometry evidence directly (a claimed Shrine et al. lung-function GWAS, PMID 31649364) turned out to be a fabricated/wrong PMID (see audit). A genuine, unresolved mouse-vs-human tissue-expression contradiction for SYT14 itself (heart/testis-restricted and brain-absent in mouse per PMID 12801916, vs. brain/cerebellum-enriched in human per PMID 21835308) further undercuts confidence in any confident claim about this gene's tissue-expression pattern, including an implicit assumption that it is not expressed in lung — direct GTEx/Human Protein Atlas lung-expression data for SYT14 was not obtained in this review (a flagged gap, not a checked-and-negative result).

Net assessment: gene identity = SYT14, essentially uncontested. Mechanism = unknown. The evidence does not distinguish between "COPD signal here is smoking-behavior-mediated," "COPD signal here reflects some other neurobehavioral pleiotropy with no lung relevance," or "this locus has genuine but entirely unstudied direct lung biology" (given how sparse the SYT14 literature is overall, absence of lung papers is weak evidence of absence of lung biology). No runner-up gene is proposed, since SERTAD4's L2G scores are consistently and substantially lower than SYT14's at every credible set examined — this is a single-gene, uncertain-mechanism locus rather than a tied-gene locus. Confidence is marked LOW (not INSUFFICIENT) because gene identity itself is well-supported by multiple converging, verified lines of evidence; what is missing is mechanistic specificity, not gene assignment. Resolving the mechanism would require: (1) direct GTEx/Human Protein Atlas lung-expression data for SYT14, (2) primary-text confirmation (not aggregator-only) of whether SYT14/this variant is a genuine locus in the GSCAN smoking-initiation GWAS or any lung-function GWAS (Shrine et al. or successors) via a correctly-identified PMID, and (3) an independent SERTAD4 literature check to fully rule out the L2G runner-up.
