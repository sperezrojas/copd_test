Prioritized gene

MAML3 — confidence: LOW.

MAML3 is the only gene that physically overlaps this locus: all 20 credible-set variants, including the lead SNP rs6841661 (PIP=0.228), sit within MAML3 introns, and no other protein-coding gene's body overlaps the stated interval. That positional case is solid and is the primary reason MAML3 is named here over CCRN4L/NOCT, whose body sits ~800-900 kb outside the locus/credible-set region with six intervening protein-coding genes and no eQTL, colocalization, or chromatin-interaction evidence connecting it to these variants (researcher's Ensembl gene-content check; red-team-skeptic verdict: CONTESTED for NOCT, concluding the MAGMA/PoPS statistical flag should not be weighted as an evidentiary equal to MAML3's positional/mechanistic case absent a demonstrated regulatory bridge).

However, the case for MAML3 reflecting genuine, COPD-relevant pulmonary biology — as opposed to being a smoking-behavior/neurobehavioral pleiotropy locus that surfaces in this COPD GWAS only because the summary statistics are not adjusted for smoking (biological prior #1) — is NOT well resolved by the assembled evidence, and the red-team-skeptic explicitly downgraded the gene-level verdict to WEAKENED on this basis:

  • The two lung-tissue eQTL/phenotype findings supporting a pulmonary role (bronchial hyperresponsiveness severity in asthma, PMID 27709636; chronic mucus hypersecretion in heavy smokers without COPD, PMID 25234806) are both sub-genome-wide-significant, and neither is a COPD case-control or spirometric (FEV1/FEV1-FVC) result — per biological prior #2, the complete absence of any verified spirometry-trait association for MAML3 is itself a meaningful strike against confident prioritization.
  • MAML3/4q31.1 is confirmed ABSENT from the two largest, most directly relevant dedicated GWAS for this exact phenotype class — Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019 (82-locus COPD case-control GWAS, PMID 30804561) and Shrine et al. 2023 (1,020-signal multi-ancestry lung-function/COPD GWAS, PMID 36914875) — as well as from the largest dedicated smoking- behavior GWAS, GSCAN (Liu et al. 2019, PMID 30643251). These are real, informative null results from adequately powered studies (see audit appendix), not mere gaps.
  • Simultaneously, the Open Targets structured lookup shows MAML3's smoking- initiation/cessation/behavior and other neurobehavioral/addiction-trait association scores are comparable to or higher than its COPD score, and independent literature confirms MAML3 pleiotropy with smoking-related GERD (PMID 38937676), migraine (PMID 41420111), and aging/healthspan (PMID 37118362) — none of which are pulmonary.
  • No MAML3-specific (as opposed to MAML1/MAML2) immune-cell-type evidence exists relevant to COPD inflammatory biology (prior #3); mechanistic Notch-developmental evidence for MAML3 is generic (cardiac in zebrafish, pan-Notch in mouse), with no lung-specific developmental study identified.

Runner-up: CCRN4L/NOCT — not a viable competing pick at this credible set. Despite being the MAGMA- and PoPS-prioritized gene, NOCT/CCRN4L has no COPD or spirometry-trait association in Open Targets or literature, no immune/inflammatory connection, an extensively characterized systemic metabolic/circadian biology with no lung mechanism, and sits far outside the physical locus/credible set with no demonstrated regulatory link. The red-team-skeptic's CONTESTED verdict reflects that its statistical prioritization is directly contradicted by the positional/mechanistic evidence, not merely under-supported.

What would resolve this call: (1) a genome-wide-significant, smoking-adjusted COPD case-control or FEV1/FEV1-FVC association specifically at rs6841661/this credible set in a large, direct lung-function GWAS (the two largest available did not detect it); (2) a direct comparison of whether the Open Targets smoking-initiation signal for MAML3 comes from a study that also captures this exact credible set (unresolved in this pipeline — GSCAN 2019 does not show it, but Open Targets' smoking-initiation score likely draws from a larger/more recent smoking GWAS not checked here); (3) any eQTL/chromatin-interaction evidence linking the credible-set variants to CCRN4L/NOCT expression, which would revive it as a genuine competing candidate; (4) a lung/airway-specific (as opposed to cardiac or generic) developmental or immune mechanistic study for MAML3 specifically.

Given the unresolved smoking-mediation-vs-lung-biology question (which the researcher could not adjudicate and the red-team-skeptic explicitly flagged as at least as well-supported toward smoking-mediation as toward lung-intrinsic biology), the absence of any spirometry-trait evidence, and the confirmed null results in the two largest dedicated COPD/lung-function GWAS, this pick is offered as the best-supported one available — not a confident causal-gene call — for the study team's own judgment.
