Gene-level skeptic notes (see audits/5_170800001-171200000_audit.md for full verbatim critique)

  • RANBP17 — WEAKENED. The red-team-skeptic found the researcher's negative findings (no lung/ COPD/immune literature for RANBP17) well-supported, but weakened the gene's overall candidacy: the "smoking-behavior-mediated" hypothesis rests entirely on Open Targets' model-derived L2G score pattern (RANBP17 dominant at the lead variant across smoking/adiposity trait credible sets), not on any literature source that names RANBP17 as the causal gene in a smoking-behavior GWAS. Combined with RANBP17's LoF-tolerant constraint and dense LD neighborhood (NPM1, KCNIP1, TLX3 also physically present), the skeptic judged RANBP17's case for any COPD mechanism (direct or smoking-mediated) as weak and indirect.
  • FGF18 — CONTESTED. The skeptic judged the researcher's framing ("FGF18 has much stronger mechanistic plausibility than RANBP17") as mildly overclaiming when applied to this specific locus. FGF18's mechanistic literature is genuine and repeatedly replicated, but (a) its gene body sits ~220 kb outside the strict credible-set window (a MAGMA/PoPS flanking-window artifact, not physical/fine-mapping support), (b) it is only a minor, never-top L2G gene at the actual lead variant rs6880972, (c) its best direct COPD evidence (Open Targets L2G ≈0.224) has unresolved study/variant provenance and cannot be confirmed to derive from this locus's credible set, and (d) every mechanistic paper is in a fetal/neonatal/developmental or restrictive-fibrotic (IPF) disease context, never adult COPD/emphysema, and the one paper that might have bridged this gap (Duong et al. 2022) was rejected by citation-verifier as CLAIM_MISMATCH.
  • Alternative-gene / pleiotropic-hub consideration. The skeptic noted this locus shows the signature of a broad, low-confidence, pleiotropic regulatory hotspot (278 noncoding variants, max PIP 0.065, at least 5 plausible cis genes plus FGF18 from outside the window, and strong multi-trait pleiotropy at the lead variant — smoking, adiposity, CRP, HDL, educational attainment, ADHD, restless legs) rather than a single well-resolved causal gene. NPM1 (COPD L2G 0.056 per Open Targets) was flagged as deserving at least brief consideration rather than exclusion by fiat, though it was not a locus-file candidate gene and was not independently researched in depth.
  • Search-budget caveat: The red-team-skeptic's web-search tool was exhausted before it could run any independent contradiction search of its own; its critique above is based entirely on the researcher's findings and citation-verifier annotations, not on new independent literature checks.

Prioritized gene

Gene: FGF18 Confidence: LOW

This is a close call between two genes with independently different, non-overlapping weaknesses, and neither the researcher's evidence nor the red-team-skeptic's critique resolves it cleanly — the skeptic explicitly flagged this locus as more consistent with a "broad, pleiotropic regulatory hotspot" than a single well-resolved causal gene.

I lean toward FGF18 over RANBP17 for the following reasons, while explicitly acknowledging the skeptic's CONTESTED flag on it: FGF18 has repeatedly replicated, multi-species, multi-laboratory mechanistic evidence directly implicating it in alveolar septation and elastogenesis (tropoelastin, lysyl oxidase, fibulins) — biology that is mechanistically on-target for the structural/elastin-balance pathophysiology central to COPD/emphysema (see the FGF18 rows in the evidence table above), and its gnomAD LoF constraint (oe=0.227, dosage-sensitive) is directionally consistent with a gene where reduced dosage plausibly matters for disease risk. By contrast, RANBP17's entire verified literature base (nucleocytoplasmic transport, testis-preferential expression, neurodevelopment/dystonia, stem-cell lineage commitment) has no plausible connection to any COPD-relevant pathway, and it is LoF-tolerant (oe=1.14) — arguing against dosage-sensitivity mattering even if it were the correct gene.

However, this preference is weakly held and confidence is capped at LOW because: (1) FGF18's own COPD-specific Open Targets L2G evidence (0.224) has unresolved study/variant provenance and cannot be confirmed to arise from this locus's actual credible set; (2) FGF18's gene body sits ~220 kb outside the strict locus boundary and is only a minor, never-top L2G gene at the actual lead variant rs6880972, meaning the fine-mapping data itself does not point to FGF18 — it is a MAGMA/PoPS flanking-window candidate, not a fine-mapped one; (3) every mechanistic FGF18 paper is in a fetal/neonatal/ developmental or restrictive-fibrotic (IPF) disease context, never adult COPD/emphysema, so extrapolation to adult obstructive lung disease is unconfirmed; and (4) RANBP17 is not cleanly ruled out either — it remains the dominant statistical-genetics (L2G/eQTL) signal at the actual lead variant, just for smoking/adiposity traits rather than COPD/spirometry directly, and a smoking-mediated mechanism (prior #1) acting via RANBP17-linked neurobiology cannot be excluded, only rendered speculative by the absence of any literature source naming RANBP17 in a smoking-GWAS paper.

Runner-up: RANBP17 (nearest gene, ABC-prioritized, dominant lead-variant L2G gene for smoking/ adiposity traits, but no COPD/spirometry evidence and no plausible COPD-relevant biology in its literature).

What would resolve the tie: (a) resolving the study/variant provenance of FGF18's 0.224 COPD L2G score in Open Targets to confirm whether it derives from rs6880972's credible set or a distinct signal near FGF18's own gene body; (b) a GWAS-eQTL colocalization (H4) statistic between the COPD GWAS signal and RANBP17 or FGF18 expression QTLs, which could not be retrieved here; (c) any adult COPD/emphysema or spirometry (FEV1/FEV1FVC) GWAS or functional paper directly naming either gene, which the researcher was unable to find for either; (d) full-text confirmation of whether the GSCAN/MVP smoking GWAS papers specifically nominate RANBP17 as the credible-set gene at this locus, which could not be confirmed from abstracts alone. Given the low, closely-matched confidence, the study team should weigh both candidates and treat neither as settled.
