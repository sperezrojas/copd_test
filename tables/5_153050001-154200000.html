Prioritized gene

GRIA1 — confidence: LOW.

GRIA1 is the best-supported candidate at this locus, but only by elimination and by a "least-bad" margin, not because the evidence is strong. It is independently flagged by two orthogonal computational methods (FLAMES, PoPS) plus nearest-gene proximity, and carries the only non-trivial Open Targets genetic-association signal at this locus (score 0.388, two L2G-like evidence rows ~0.56/0.32) among the six genes evaluated — SAP30L and LINC01470 both show zero Open Targets genetic-association evidence for COPD (see Open Targets summary above). GRIA1 also shows detectable gnomAD pLoF constraint (O/E 0.326), consistent with it being a gene where damaging variation is at least moderately selected against.

However, red-team-skeptic's critique (audit file) is decisive on how far this can be pushed: every COPD/lung-function statgen citation for GRIA1 (Sakornsakolpat PMID 30804561, Shrine PMID 36914875, Saunders/GSCAN PMID 36477530) is a "could not confirm locus-specific overlap from the abstract" citation, not a direct positive hit. The one citation that directly and explicitly names GRIA1 as a prioritized causal gene (Nievergelt et al. 2024, PMID 38637617) does so for PTSD, not COPD or lung function. No structural or immune lung-biology literature exists for GRIA1 itself; the only glutamate-receptor/airway literature identified (Anaparti 2015/2016, Strapkova & Antosova 2012) concerns NMDA-type (GRIN) receptors, an explicitly different receptor subtype from AMPA-type GRIA1, and should not be read as GRIA1-specific evidence. The credible set (~89 variants, including the lead variant rs57976819) is 100% annotated to LINC01470, not GRIA1 — GRIA1 itself carries no credible-set variant.

Per biological prior #1 (smoking-mediated effects), the pattern of evidence — real computational/statistical prioritization, real family-level (GRIA4) and PTSD-gene-list support for a neurobehavioral role, zero pulmonary-structural or lung-immune literature — is more consistent with a smoking-behavior/neurobehavior-mediated mechanism than with direct pulmonary biology. But per red-team-skeptic's critique, this remains an inference by analogy (paralog + different-phenotype GWAS gene list), not a confirmed finding: no citation here shows GRIA1 (or this locus) as a genome-wide-significant smoking-initiation, cigarettes- per-day, or cessation locus in GSCAN or an equivalent smoking-behavior GWAS. That specific check (direct GSCAN/PGC substance-use locus-table lookup at 5:153,205,072, and gene-level overlap between GRIA1 and the 559-gene Shrine et al. lung-function list) could not be performed in this run (researcher lacked access to the full supplementary tables; red-team- skeptic's search budget was exhausted before it could attempt this independently) and is the single most useful follow-up to resolve this locus with higher confidence.

Runner-up candidates and why they were not chosen: SAP30L (MAGMA-prioritized) is undermined by sitting ~246kb physically outside the stated locus boundary and having zero COPD/lung/immune-specific literature (only cardiac/hematopoietic zebrafish and pure Sin3A/ HDAC biochemistry) — red-team-skeptic marked it CONTESTED. LINC01470 carries the lead variant and nearly the whole credible set but has zero independent literature of any kind and zero Open Targets evidence; the hypothesis that it regulates GRIA1 in cis is explicitly speculative (no eQTL, Hi-C, or expression data cited) and was marked CONTESTED by red-team- skeptic as a specific inference, though the gene's mere existence/positional role is not in dispute. FAM114A2 (not internally prioritized) has a genuine, mechanistically grounded immune signal (macrophage TNFalpha/autophagy, PMID 34380996) but in IBD via a different variant with no LD/colocalization check against this locus's credible set — red-team-skeptic marked this WEAKENED rather than usable as locus-specific evidence. MFAP3 and GALNT10 were both marked CONTESTED (paralog-substitution and wrong-organ-oncology evidence, respectively) and are not viable alternatives on current evidence.

What would resolve this call: (1) direct lookup of this locus/GRIA1 in the GSCAN smoking-initiation/heaviness/cessation locus tables and in the Shrine et al. 559-gene lung-function list; (2) an eQTL/colocalization analysis of the credible-set variants against GRIA1, SAP30L, and FAM114A2 expression in lung and relevant immune-cell tissues; (3) an LD check between the FAM114A2 IBD eQTL variant rs34767465 and this locus's credible set. Absent these, GRIA1 is offered as the best-supported single recommendation, but confidence is LOW and the smoking-mediated-versus-direct-pulmonary-biology question at this locus should be treated as open.
