Prioritized gene

Gene: KLF12 — Confidence: LOW

This call is close, and the evidence genuinely does not cleanly distinguish KLF12 from LINC00402 as the causal gene at this locus — the red-team-skeptic explicitly marked KLF12 CONTESTED and recommended LINC00402 be treated as a live alternative, not a footnote. I am naming KLF12 as the best single pick because it is the only gene here with any positive genetic-association signal connecting it to a pulmonary-function trait (the Open Targets FEV1/FVC score, entirely gwas_credible_sets-driven) and it is the PoPS-prioritized nearest gene, which — absent a resolving eQTL/chromatin-interaction study — remains the default prioritization heuristic this pipeline otherwise relies on. However, that same FEV1/FVC signal is not independently corroborated by a named peer-reviewed lung-function GWAS (Wyss et al. 2018, PMID 30061609, does not list KLF12), and the skeptic correctly notes it is close to circular (Open Targets' own L2G-style gwas_credible_sets score is likely just re-encoding the same proximity/regulatory assumption being evaluated). Working against KLF12: it sits 82-282 kb from the actual credible set (which lies entirely within LINC00402), it has essentially no literature-documented lung-specific biology outside of lung-cancer cell lines (anoikis, PD-L1/CD8+ T-cell suppression, IL-8/M2-macrophage polarization — all tumor-context, and in the PD-L1 case arguably direction-inverted relative to COPD's pathogenic CD8+ T-cell biology), and there is a directly on-point regional precedent (Hoskins et al. 2016, PMID 28172817) in this exact chr13q22.1 KLF-family gene desert where the nearest genes (KLF5/KLF12) were demonstrably NOT the causal target of a different GWAS signal — the true target (DIS3) was reached via a 570 kb chromatin loop. A COPD-specific gene-prioritization study (Zainab/Anzawa/Kinoshita 2024, PMID 39509417) also does not name KLF12.

Runner-up: LINC00402 — physically contains all 39 credible-set variants and has the single most substantive, mechanistically coherent piece of immune biology found at this locus (Peltier et al. 2021, PMID 33731431): a T-cell-enriched, TCR-responsive lncRNA that augments IL-2 signaling and allo-specific T-cell proliferation — directionally compatible with (not inverted relative to) COPD's CD8+/T-cell inflammatory programs, unlike the KLF12 tumor-immune-evasion evidence. Its case is weakened by the fact that this mechanism was shown in a transplant-alloimmunity/GVHD context, not airway inflammation, and Open Targets shows zero COPD/FEV1-FVC association score for LINC00402 — but the skeptic notes that absence of COPD literature is close to the base rate for any lncRNA (a systematically understudied gene class) and should not be read as strongly as the equivalent absence would be for a well-studied protein-coding gene.

What would resolve the tie: a lung-tissue (or sorted lung-immune-cell, e.g., CD8+ T cells or alveolar macrophages) eQTL or chromatin-conformation study (Hi-C/HiChIP/ promoter-capture Hi-C) specifically naming either KLF12 or LINC00402 as the regulatory target of this credible-set region — the direct analog of the DIS3 chromatin-loop evidence that resolved the equivalent ambiguity in the Hoskins 2016 locus. Neither the researcher nor the red-team-skeptic was able to complete this specific check in this pass (GTEx/lung-eQTL lookup and GWAS Catalog smoking-trait lookup for rs1331781 were both attempted and left incomplete due to search-tool budget exhaustion — see audit appendix). Given this, confidence is marked LOW rather than MODERATE or HIGH, and the study team should treat this locus as substantively unresolved pending a dedicated eQTL/colocalization follow-up, not as a settled KLF12 finding.
