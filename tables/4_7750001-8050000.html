Prioritized gene

AFAP1 — Confidence: LOW

AFAP1 is the only candidate gene at this locus: all four internal prioritization methods (nearest gene, FLAMES, MAGMA, PoPS) agree on it, and all 39 credible-set variants are intronic within the AFAP1 gene body itself (not merely nearby in a multi-gene LD block), which structurally weakens the usual "nearest-gene-bias" concern relative to most loci in this review. AFAP1 is also independently, explicitly named as a replicated COPD-associated locus in an East Asian case-control GWAS (Lin WD et al. 2024, PMID 38886662, VERIFIED), and Open Targets shows strong, concordant genetic-association evidence across COPD, FEV1, and FEV1/FVC ratio (scores ~0.8-0.9), consistent with biological prior #2. There is no evidence of a smoking-behavior-mediated mechanism (zero smoking-trait genetic association in Open Targets, and the researcher's targeted literature search found no smoking-GWAS overlap either), consistent with prior #1 favoring a pulmonary-biology interpretation over a neurobehavioral one.

However, confidence cannot be rated higher than LOW for two compounding reasons, both raised independently by the researcher and the red-team-skeptic:

  1. The gene-level statistical case is thinner than the citation count suggests. Of the 10 statistical-genetics citations the researcher surfaced, 5 were CLAIM_MISMATCH on verification specifically because AFAP1 is not named in their abstracts (Shrine 2019, Shrine 2023, Wyss 2018, Zhou/GBMI 2022, You D 2025 — see excluded appendix); only one paper (Lin WD 2024) explicitly and verifiably names AFAP1 as COPD-associated. No colocalization, TWAS, or eQTL study naming AFAP1 as the causal/functional (as opposed to merely nearest/statistically-mapped) gene was found by the researcher.

  2. AFAP1's only well-validated human functional/mechanistic biology is ocular, not pulmonary, and there is a specific, unresolved variant-level pleiotropy concern. AFAP1's best-characterized human disease association, with direct tissue-expression and functional-validation support (retina, optic nerve, trabecular meshwork, retinal ganglion cells), is primary open-angle glaucoma/intraocular pressure — repeatedly replicated across independent cohorts (Gharahkhani 2014, Chen 2026, both VERIFIED). Critically, the actual lead COPD credible-set variant in this analysis, rs73073939 (PIP=0.519), is the same variant Open Targets indexes into a glaucoma credible set (L2G=0.78 for AFAP1) — a shared-variant overlap, not just a shared gene. No literature search performed in this pipeline run (by either the researcher or, notably, the red-team-skeptic, whose search budget was already exhausted before it could run its own contradiction-search pass for this locus — see audit file) could establish whether the COPD/lung-function and POAG/IOP signals at this locus reflect one shared causal mechanism or two independent, coincidentally co-located signals. Zero literature connects AFAP1 to airway epithelium, alveolar development, protease-antiprotease balance, mucociliary function, smoking behavior, or any immune/inflammatory program relevant to COPD; the one AFAP1 knockout mouse's only reported phenotype (mammary lactation defect) has no lung component.

What would resolve this: a formal colocalization/LD analysis between this locus's COPD/ FEV1 credible set and the AFAP1 POAG/IOP credible set (GCST011441) to test whether rs73073939 and the POAG-associated variants represent the same causal signal; a lung-tissue eQTL or TWAS study directly testing whether AFAP1 transcript levels mediate the pulmonary-function association; and completion of the region gene-content enumeration (not finished this session) to formally rule out an unrecognized neighboring gene. There is no runner-up candidate gene to name at this locus (no other gene was proposed by any prioritization method or found in the credible set), so this is not a tie between two genes — it is a single candidate whose statistical case is solid but whose causal/mechanistic pulmonary story is unconfirmed and complicated by unresolved ocular pleiotropy at the lead variant itself.
