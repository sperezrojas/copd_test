Prioritized gene

HIVEP1 — confidence LOW.

HIVEP1 has the stronger case on every structural/statistical-genetics axis available: it is the nearest gene to the lead variant, independently prioritized by both PoPS and ABC (versus ADTRP's single FLAMES-only prioritization), carries several credible-set intronic variants within its gene body (e.g. rs9470768, PIP=0.024), has a substantially higher Open Targets gwas_credible_sets evidence score (0.657 vs. 0.083), and is strongly LoF-intolerant in gnomAD (oe=0.167) — consistent with an essential, tightly-regulated transcriptional regulator, unlike the LoF-tolerant ADTRP (oe=0.732). ADTRP's gene body also sits ~43kb outside both the defined locus boundary and the entire 127-variant credible-set span, meaning no credible-set variant is physically near it — a substantial strike against it as the effector gene here, independent of any literature.

However, per the red-team-skeptic's critique (preserved in full in the audit appendix), this confidence is capped at LOW rather than MODERATE/HIGH because: (1) no PubMed-indexed literature directly links HIVEP1 to COPD, emphysema, or spirometric traits (FEV1/FEV1-FVC) — prior #2's expected concordance is entirely absent for both candidates; (2) HIVEP1's well-replicated disease association (venous thromboembolism/pulmonary embolism, via rs169713) involves a different variant than this locus's credible set, with LD to the COPD signal not established in any paper found — this should be read as gene-level pleiotropy at best, not locus-level concordance; (3) HIVEP1's substantial immune/NF-κB mechanistic literature (sepsis monocytes, NASH-liver Th17, macrophage polarization/M.tb response, leukocyte/exercise studies) is real but entirely non-lung-tissue, so its relevance to COPD-specific inflammatory programs (per prior #3) is inferential, not demonstrated; and (4) whether the COPD signal at this locus is smoking-mediated (prior #1) is genuinely unresolved — the researcher's and skeptic's searches for a direct smoking-initiation/cessation GWAS hit for HIVEP1 were both left incomplete due to tool-budget exhaustion, not completed negative findings.

Runner-up: ADTRP is weaker on essentially every axis (single-method prioritization, lowest L2G score, LoF-tolerant, physically outside the credible-set span, and its own best-established disease association — CAD via rs6903956, a different variant — is likewise cardiometabolic rather than pulmonary). It is not a close call between the two candidates on current statistical/positional grounds; the tie that remains open is not "HIVEP1 vs. ADTRP" but "HIVEP1-via-fine-mapping vs. no literature-confirmed mechanism at all." What would resolve this: (a) an FEV1/FEV1-FVC or direct COPD PheWAS hit for either gene, (b) an LD check between rs169713/rs6903956 and this locus's credible set to determine whether the VT/CAD and COPD signals are the same or independent, and (c) a completed smoking-initiation/cessation GWAS lookup for HIVEP1 to rule in or out prior #1's smoking-mediation pathway. The study team should weigh HIVEP1 as the best-supported nomination for further functional/colocalization follow-up, not as a settled call.
