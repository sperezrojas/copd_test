Prioritized gene

TBC1D2B — confidence LOW.

This is a close call between two weakly-supported candidates, and neither gene has COPD-specific or spirometry-specific literature support, nor a GWAS/L2G signal built on this locus's own credible set (as opposed to a nearby but distinct variant/study). Both genes were independently marked CONTESTED by two red-team-skeptic passes (see audit file). Given that, TBC1D2B is named as the marginally better-supported pick for these locus-specific reasons:

  1. TBC1D2B is the only one of the two genes with any direct COPD GWAS/L2G evidence in Open Targets at all (score 0.426), even though that evidence is confounded by an MTAG study mixing COPD with colon polyp, built on a variant outside this locus's window, and outranked at its own credible set by a non-candidate neighboring gene (SH2D7). LINGO1 has zero COPD GWAS/L2G evidence of any kind — its Open Targets genetic signal in this region is exclusively for smoking initiation.
  2. TBC1D2B has GTEx lung eQTL/sQTL/txQTL evidence; LINGO1 has none (its eQTL footprint is brain/testis/skin/heart/artery-dominated).
  3. LINGO1's best-established, genome-wide-significant genetic association (essential tremor) is a CNS movement-disorder phenotype that does not even reliably replicate in a related CNS phenotype (null in the largest Parkinson's disease meta-analysis, PMID 22710005) — a red flag for the general robustness of LINGO1 GWAS signals independent of COPD — whereas TBC1D2B's Mendelian and cell-biology literature, while also non-pulmonary (a craniofacial/neurodevelopmental syndrome with no reported pulmonary phenotype), does not contain a comparable within-domain replication failure.

However, this call should be treated as genuinely close and low-confidence, not a resolved answer, for several reasons documented above and in the audit: TBC1D2B's own COPD evidence is itself weak/confounded and positionally displaced from this locus's actual lead variant/credible set; seven independent Mendelian loss-of-function case series show no pulmonary phenotype for TBC1D2B, which is genuine (if imperfect) evidence against a major TBC1D2B lung-structural role; and the entire 84-variant credible set is intergenic, 70-93kb from either gene's body, which is also consistent with a distal regulatory element acting on a gene outside this window entirely (no chromatin-conformation/Hi-C data for this interval was located by either red-team pass — a genuine, unresolved gap). LINGO1 remains the runner-up and is the internal pipeline's own top statistical pick (MAGMA+PoPS), but its case is undercut by a total absence of COPD/spirometry evidence and a well-supported competing explanation (smoking-initiation-mediated confounding in an un-adjusted-for-smoking COPD GWAS, per pipeline prior #1).

What would resolve this tie: (a) colocalization of this locus's own credible set (built around rs12899237, not the off-window MTAG-derived credible set) against a COPD-only (non-MTAG) GWAS and against lung-tissue eQTL for both genes; (b) promoter-capture Hi-C or other chromatin-conformation data for this intergenic interval in lung/airway epithelial or immune cell types, to test whether the credible set instead targets a gene outside this window; (c) confirmation of whether the TBC1D2B pan-lung-cancer-subtype signal (PMID 29486777) physically overlaps this locus or reflects LD tagging into the distinct, ~490kb-away CHRNA5/IDH3A/PSMA4 15q25 smoking locus. Confidence is marked LOW (not INSUFFICIENT) because there is at least some locus-adjacent, gene-specific structured evidence (Open Targets L2G/eQTL) distinguishing the two genes, even though it does not rise to a settled call.
