Prioritized gene

JADE2 — Confidence: LOW.

JADE2 is, without question, the best statistically and regulatorily supported gene at this locus: it physically contains the entire credible set (rs329120, rs329118, rs329122, rs329124), is both the nearest gene and the ABC-prioritized gene, and is the sole Locus-to-Gene (L2G) target across all 233 GWAS-trait/QTL rows overlapping the lead variant in Open Targets (L2G ~0.85-0.89), including direct JADE2 eQTL/tuQTL. It is also strongly LoF-constrained, consistent with an essential regulatory gene (core HBO1 histone-acetyltransferase complex subunit). No competing gene at this locus comes close on genetic-mapping grounds: C5orf66 (the MAGMA-prioritized alternative) is very likely a mapping artifact — independently confirmed via Ensembl region-overlap to sit ~450-800kb outside the interval near the distinct PITX1 locus, with a literature base (oncology, endometriosis) entirely unrelated to lung or COPD biology; LINC01843, while physically present, is never the L2G target and has only nonspecific multi-lncRNA cancer-signature literature; and CDKN2AIPNL (an unprioritized gene at the locus boundary, identified only via independent Ensembl lookup) has no credible-set variant and only weak, non-genetic, asthma-context expression evidence.

The confidence rating is LOW rather than HIGH or MODERATE specifically because — per the red-team-skeptic's critique — the statistical/regulatory case for JADE2 as the gene at this locus is strong, but the case that this locus's COPD association reflects a lung-intrinsic mechanism specific to JADE2 is weak. Across Open Targets' full 208-trait disease-association catalog and all 233 trait rows at the lead variant, there is zero COPD or FEV1/FEV1-FVC signal — a locus this densely phenotyped in Open Targets would be expected to show at least some spirometric/COPD colocalization if the mechanism were lung-intrinsic (pipeline prior #2). Instead, the trait profile is dominated by anthropometric/metabolic/endocrine/hematologic pleiotropy and, notably, smoking-behavior traits (initiation, cessation, nicotine dependence, lifetime smoking index — all with L2G/association scores comparable to or exceeding any lung-adjacent trait). This is independently corroborated by a separate, COPD-unrelated lung-cancer GWAS (Yang et al. 2022, PMID 35773316, VERIFIED) showing the credible-set variant rs329118 has a smoking-status-dependent effect on lung cancer risk (stronger in ever-smokers). No published literature links JADE2 to airway remodeling, emphysema, alveolar development, protease-antiprotease biology, mucociliary function, or any COPD-relevant inflammatory/immune cell program (this is reported as a genuine evidence gap, not a null finding, per the researcher). Taken together, this locus's association with COPD is plausibly mediated in whole or in part through smoking-behavior biology (consistent with pipeline prior #1 — the parent COPD GWAS is not adjusted for smoking) rather than through lung-intrinsic biology specific to JADE2, and this inference should not be overstated: it rests on a convergence of indirect/absence-based evidence rather than a single definitive mechanistic study.

What would resolve this: (1) a direct COPD or FEV1/FEV1-FVC colocalization/GWAS signal at this locus (currently entirely absent from Open Targets' indexed studies, which may simply reflect that the parent COPD GWAS used in this pipeline is not yet indexed there, rather than a true absence of lung-function association — this ambiguity should be checked against the internal COPD GWAS's own summary statistics for FEV1/FEV1-FVC concordance if available); (2) mediation/conditional analysis of the COPD association at rs329120 adjusting for smoking initiation/heaviness, to directly test whether the COPD signal survives smoking adjustment; (3) any airway-epithelial, alveolar, or COPD-relevant-immune-cell functional/expression study of JADE2 specifically (none currently exists). Runner-up candidates (LINC01843, CDKN2AIPNL) are not close competitors on the gene-identification question — the ambiguity here is not "which gene" but "which mechanism for the one well-supported gene" — so this LOW confidence reflects mechanistic uncertainty about JADE2 itself, not a tie between candidates.
