Prioritized gene

SENP7 — LOW confidence.

SENP7 has the strongest statistical-genetic support at this locus: it is the nearest gene, the ABC-prioritized gene, and physically hosts the top three credible-set variants by PIP (rs57867809 PIP=0.127, rs4456838 PIP=0.124, rs4524254 PIP=0.119), all clustered in a single ~1kb intronic window. The Open Targets structured lookup independently corroborates SENP7 as the consistent top locus-to-gene (L2G) target across ~101 non-COPD GWAS credible sets at this same locus (rows above), which at minimum establishes SENP7 as the gene most frequently and confidently implicated by regulatory/expression architecture in this region across many traits.

However, this recommendation is offered with LOW confidence, not moderate or high, for reasons the red-team-skeptic's critique makes explicit and that are preserved in the audit appendix: (1) there is zero PubMed literature of any kind — statistical-genetic, structural/ developmental, or immunological — directly connecting SENP7 to COPD, FEV1, FEV1/FVC, or any pulmonary trait; (2) the cross-trait L2G pleiotropy pattern that nominates SENP7 is built almost entirely from blood-cell-count, biochemical, anthropometric, and dermatologic traits, with no FEV1/FEV1-FVC or smoking-behavior credible set among them — this argues at least as strongly for a hematologic-trait-mediated mechanism (e.g., eosinophil/neutrophil counts, which are themselves COPD-subtype-relevant biomarkers) as for a lung-intrinsic SENP7 mechanism, and the biological-priors checklist's spirometry-concordance criterion is unmet; (3) SENP7's established molecular biology (deSUMOylation of KAP1/HP1α, heterochromatin/DNA-repair regulation, oocyte/embryo meiotic epigenetic reprogramming) offers no obvious a priori COPD mechanism, and the one bioinformatic "immune" correlate found (kidney-transplant Th17 hub-gene analysis) is incidental, not mechanistic, while a second paper reports a negative finding for SENP7-immune-infiltration correlation.

The runner-up, IMPG2, covers a substantial secondary fraction of the credible set (~60 variants, max PIP=0.046) but has an established, retina-restricted structural biology (interphotoreceptor matrix proteoglycan; retinitis pigmentosa gene) with no lung phenotype in any of the ~12 PubMed hits reviewed, and only ever appears as a lower-scoring secondary L2G contributor relative to SENP7 at the same credible sets — the pattern most consistent with IMPG2 being an LD-driven passenger rather than the causal gene, though this is an absence-of-evidence inference, not a definitive exclusion.

NFKBIZ was also seriously considered given its strong a priori biological fit to this pipeline's inflammatory/Th17 priors (IκBζ is a validated IL-17/NF-κB pathway node driving neutrophil-chemokine induction in psoriasis), but it was not selected as the top pick because its locus-specific statistical support is essentially absent: it has zero credible-set variant overlap (unlike PCNP or TRMT10C, which at least have low-PIP variants), it is nominated by PoPS alone, and Open Targets records no genetic-association evidence linking it to COPD. The one COPD-airway-epithelium citation used in its favor (Jamieson et al. 2018) demonstrates IL-17C/NF-κB/neutrophil biology directly in COPD bronchial epithelial cells but does not itself name NFKBIZ as the mediator, so it functions as pathway-plausibility support, not a NFKBIZ-specific finding.

What would resolve this call: (1) an FEV1/FEV1-FVC-specific or COPD-specific fine-mapping/ colocalization analysis at this locus (not currently in Open Targets) to test whether SENP7's L2G dominance extends to spirometric traits, not just blood-cell/biochemical traits; (2) an eQTL/sQTL colocalization analysis in lung tissue or airway epithelium/immune cells specifically for SENP7 vs. IMPG2 vs. PCNP at this locus; (3) any direct SENP7-lung or SENP7-neutrophil mechanistic study, which does not currently exist in PubMed; (4) for NFKBIZ, any locus-specific eQTL or regulatory evidence connecting the fine-mapped credible-set variants (not merely PoPS pathway similarity) to NFKBIZ expression or function.
