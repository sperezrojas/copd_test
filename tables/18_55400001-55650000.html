Prioritized gene

Prioritized gene: TCF4. Confidence: LOW.

TCF4 is the only gene with a direct positional/mechanistic tie to this locus: it is the nearest gene, the FLAMES-prioritized gene, and six of the seven credible-set variants (all but the unannotated lead variant itself) sit in its introns. Open Targets' own L2G model independently assigns TCF4 as the causal gene at the lead variant rs596668 for a forced-vital-capacity (FVC) GWAS credible set (score ~0.79) and for FEV1/pulmonary-function-measurement traits generally (datasource scores 0.68-0.74, TCF4 the sole gene with any such evidence) — evidence consistent with biological prior #2 (spirometric concordance strengthens prioritization). By contrast, ATP8B1's only mechanistic lung literature (two mouse-model papers, PMID 30636723 and 35204783) describes a pulmonary fibrosis phenotype — structurally the opposite direction from COPD's airflow-obstructive/emphysematous pathology — and ATP8B1 physically sits ~2 Mb outside this locus's credible-set window, supported only by a genome-wide, distance-agnostic method (PoPS), with zero pulmonary-function-trait evidence in Open Targets despite its COPD case-control score. The red-team-skeptic marked ATP8B1 CONTESTED on exactly these grounds (see audit appendix), and that verdict is adopted here: ATP8B1 is not treated as a serious competing candidate for this specific locus.

However, confidence in TCF4 itself is capped at LOW, not HIGH or MODERATE, for reasons the skeptic verdict (WEAKENED) makes explicit and which are not contradicted by anything above: (1) TCF4 has NO direct GWAS colocalization/L2G evidence linking it to COPD case-control status in Open Targets — its COPD association score is driven entirely by literature text-mining: the COPD-specific genetic evidence this project cares about most directly is absent, not merely weak; (2) the three statistical-genetics citations most relevant to this locus (Sakornsakolpat COPD GWAS, Shrine spirometry GWAS, GSCAN smoking GWAS) were all confirmed to exist and be methodologically as described, but in no case could the researcher or citation-verifier confirm from available text whether this specific chr18:~55.5Mb locus is actually among each paper's reported genome-wide-significant loci — this remains an open, unresolved question rather than a demonstrated overlap for any of the three; (3) the variant/locus is extraordinarily pleiotropic (TCF4 is the L2G-favored gene for ~147 unrelated traits at this exact variant per Open Targets), which substantially discounts the evidentiary weight of any single-trait signal, including the pulmonary-function one, as proof of lung-specific causal biology at this particular gene rather than a generic nearest-gene/regulatory-hub artifact; and (4) the biological prior about smoking-mediated effects is genuinely live and unresolved here, not merely a formality: TCF4 is a well-replicated schizophrenia/psychiatric-behavioral GWAS gene with a documented TCF4 x smoking-behavior gene-environment interaction (PMID 24413739) on a schizophrenia endophenotype, so a smoking-initiation/behavioral pathway is at least as circumstantially plausible as a direct pulmonary mechanism, and nothing in the verified evidence distinguishes between the two hypotheses. The immune biology (pDC/type-I IFN regulation, PMID 18854153, and a single very recent computational regulon paper, PMID 42653451) is genuine and gene-specific but is established in non-lung contexts and is not COPD-specific.

What would resolve this: direct confirmation (e.g., from supplementary tables of Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019, Shrine et al. 2019, and Liu et al. 2019/GSCAN) of whether this exact locus reaches genome-wide significance in the COPD case-control GWAS, the spirometry GWAS, and/or the smoking-behavior GWAS respectively — this would directly discriminate between a direct-lung mechanism, a smoking-mediated mechanism, and a false/nonspecific pleiotropic signal for TCF4. A lung-specific (vs. blood/whole-body) eQTL or single-cell airway-epithelium/immune-cell expression and functional (e.g., CRISPR perturbation) dataset for TCF4 would also help move this beyond positional and database-level inference. Runner-up ATP8B1 is not considered a live alternative at this locus given the distance and CONTESTED fibrosis-phenotype mismatch, but its own COPD-GWAS evidence (independent of this locus) may warrant separate follow-up given it is real, just seemingly unconnected to this credible set.
