Prioritized-gene synthesis

Prioritized gene: PTPN22 — Confidence: LOW

None of the six genes assessed at this locus has direct, gene-specific, causal COPD or spirometric-trait evidence. Across 356 independent GWAS credible sets sharing the lead variant rs6679677, zero are for COPD or a spirometric trait (FEV1, FEV1/FVC) — a strikingly complete absence of lung-biology concordance (prior #2), and the one COPD-specific, gene-and-disease-matched test available (Cao et al. 2024, PMID 39346628) found no causal effect of PTPN22 on COPD by its own Mendelian randomization, only a confounded observational correlation with immune-cell infiltration. Red-team-skeptic marked PTPN22's evidence CONTESTED overall for exactly this reason, and marked PHTF1's autoimmune-genetics case WEAKENED (Ban et al. 2016, PMID 27486433, is a discriminating null for PHTF1's own tag SNP in a population where PTPN22 R620W cannot confound the result). RSBN1 — the locus file's actual nearest-gene candidate — has no lung, immune, or COPD literature of any kind and is essentially never the top Open Targets L2G gene at this variant; its established biology (spermatogenesis-specific H4K20 demethylase) offers no plausible mechanism.

Despite this, if forced to name a single most-likely-relevant gene for the study team to weigh, PTPN22 is the best-supported pick: it is overwhelmingly the top locus-to-gene (L2G) assigned gene at the lead variant across the vast majority of the 356 associated studies, has the deepest and most repeatedly replicated regional statistical-genetics evidence of any of the six genes (via its coding variant R620W, for T1D, RA, celiac disease), documented (if lung-tissue-unconfirmed) T-cell and macrophage immunobiology that is at least mechanistically plausible for COPD's known immune complexity (prior #3), and appears as one of 16 genes in a FinnGen pleiotropic comorbidity factor that includes COPD (Mishra & Mishra 2025, PMID 41299637). PHTF1 is the runner-up, on the strength of its distinct (if only partially replicated) autoimmune-genetics signal and the highest Open Targets smoking-initiation score among the genes assessed — but per the skeptic's critique, that smoking-initiation score is an unconfirmed aggregate metric, not a literature-verified smoking-GWAS finding, and is subject to the same LD/nearest-gene attribution ambiguity that clouds the whole locus.

What would resolve this call: a COPD- or FEV1/FEV1-FVC-specific GWAS colocalization or fine-mapping result at this exact locus that discriminates between PTPN22 and PHTF1 (analogous to how Ban et al. 2016 discriminated PHTF1 from PTPN22 for autoimmune thyroid disease using a population where R620W is monomorphic); a positive, verified smoking-initiation or smoking-heaviness GWAS hit naming PTPN22 or PHTF1 specifically at this variant; or COPD-relevant single-cell/tissue data (airway epithelium, alveolar macrophage, or CD8+ T-cell) for either gene, rather than the synovial/hepatic tissue evidence available today. Absent that, this locus should be reported to the study team as an autoimmune/blood-cell-trait locus (most likely driven by PTPN22 R620W for those traits) with, at best, a weak and unconfirmed pleiotropic or smoking-behavior link to COPD — not as a locus with an established COPD mechanism.
