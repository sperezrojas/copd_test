Prioritized-gene synthesis

Prioritized gene: RAD51B — Confidence: LOW

This is a close, genuinely unresolved call, and the red-team-skeptic marked every candidate gene at this locus WEAKENED or CONTESTED — no candidate here should be presented with confidence higher than LOW.

I name RAD51B as the best-supported single pick for the following reasons, all drawn from rows above: (1) it is the only gene at this locus with unanimous convergence across three independent internal prioritization methods (FLAMES, MAGMA, PoPS — see locus input file), a computational signal not shared by ACTN1, MAGOH3P, or ZFP36L1; (2) it carries the strongest Open Targets structured-lookup signal of any gene/trait pair examined at this locus (5 gwas_credible_sets FEV1 evidence rows, scores 0.63–0.72, plus a FEV1/FVC row at 0.63), which — despite the caveat below — is at least directionally consistent with pipeline prior #2 (spirometric-trait concordance); and (3), critically, RAD51B does not carry a well-established alternative Mendelian disease mechanism that would fully explain a genetic signal at this locus the way ACTN1's macrothrombocytopenia biology does for ACTN1.

However, this pick should not be overstated. Per red-team-skeptic: RAD51B's gene body sits ~70kb outside the defined locus/credible-set window, on the flank opposite the fine-mapped variant; no verified literature citation in the table above directly ties RAD51B to COPD or lung function specifically (PMID 26635082 is explicit negative evidence from an independent lung-function GWAS; PMID 31095684 is an asthma, not COPD, finding; PMID 30956756 reflects RAD51B's dominant DNA-repair/oncology biology, not pulmonary biology); and the Open Targets FEV1 evidence rows have null variant/study identifiers, so it cannot be confirmed they represent the same LD block as rs117951468 rather than an independent RAD51B-proximal signal. RAD51B's oncology-dominant literature footprint (breast/ovarian/prostate cancer susceptibility) remains an unresolved competing explanation for why this gene keeps surfacing in GWAS more broadly.

Runner-up: ACTN1. ACTN1 is physically the nearest protein-coding gene, overlaps the locus window, and is LoF-constrained, with one small (unreplicated, non-English-language) bioinformatics emphysema-tissue expression finding (PMID 31937048) as its only lung-specific literature. Its case is weakened specifically because its best-replicated, strongest human genetics phenotype by far is an unrelated Mendelian disease (autosomal-dominant congenital macrothrombocytopenia via platelet/megakaryocyte cytoskeletal dysfunction — PMID 23434115, PMID 34502272), a serious and literature-verified alternative explanation for why ACTN1 (a highly conserved, constrained cytoskeletal gene) would show up in a GWAS association or constraint metric without necessarily reflecting lung-intrinsic biology.

MAGOH3P (the VEP nearest-gene/pseudogene annotation of the lead variant itself) and ZFP36L1 (a researcher-added, proximity-based candidate with a plausible but nonspecific and statistically unsupported immune mechanism) are both judged too weakly supported to name as the prioritized gene; see the audit appendix for the CONTESTED reasoning on MAGOH3P.

What would resolve this tie: a published or internally-run gene-specific lung-tissue eQTL/colocalization analysis anchored on rs117951468 itself (rather than nearest-gene or genome-wide prioritization-tool proximity) directly assigning the regulatory effect to either ACTN1 or RAD51B; confirmation (from Open Targets or the source GWAS) of whether the gwas_credible_sets FEV1/COPD evidence rows for ACTN1 and RAD51B correspond to the same LD block as this locus's own credible set; and a dedicated smoking-initiation/cessation (e.g., GSCAN) lookup for rs117951468 specifically, since the current absence-of-evidence for smoking mediation rests on incomplete Open Targets datasource coverage per red-team-skeptic, not a confirmed negative result.
