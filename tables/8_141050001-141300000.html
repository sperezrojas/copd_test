Prioritized-gene synthesis

Prioritized gene: DENND3 — Confidence: LOW

This locus does not yield a confident causal-gene call, and the study team should weigh this recommendation accordingly. No candidate gene has any COPD- or spirometry-specific GWAS, colocalization, or fine-mapping evidence anywhere in the literature or in Open Targets' own database at this exact locus — an unusual and important gap given the strength of the fine-mapped signal (lead PIP 0.501). All three genes' evidence was rated WEAKENED or CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic; none reached an unqualified positive verdict.

I name DENND3 as the best-supported single pick, on the basis of (a) it is the nearest gene and hosts the single highest-PIP variant in the credible set (rs4961255, PIP=0.501, row 1 above), (b) it is the only one of the three candidates with any plausible, if untested, mechanistic link to a COPD-relevant general biological process — its role as a Rab12 GEF driving autophagy induction (row 2) is mechanistically the kind of pathway implicated elsewhere in COPD alveolar-epithelial and macrophage biology, even though this specific paper never tests lung tissue, and (c) at the two credible sets keyed to the lead variant itself, Open Targets' L2G model actually favors DENND3 over SLC45A4 (Open Targets summary above), unlike at nearly every other regional signal.

This call is close, and I want to be explicit about why. SLC45A4 is a credible runner-up: it physically hosts the majority of the credible set including the locus's only missense (rs3739238) and only splice-region (rs3739235) variants, and Open Targets' L2G model (a variant-to-gene model, distinct from simple physical proximity) favors it over DENND3 at nearly every other regional GWAS signal captured here, including the smoking-initiation credible set. Against this, SLC45A4 is completely functionally uncharacterized in the literature (row 10 above is the only non-locus SLC45A4 citation found, and it is cross-species and uninformative) and has zero COPD-association evidence anywhere, including in Open Targets. Neither gene has any spirometric (FEV1/FEV1-FVC) concordance to break the tie — the single strongest form of evidence under this pipeline's priors is entirely absent for both.

TRAPPC9, despite being the FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS-prioritized gene (i.e., the pipeline's own statistical gene-level methods' top pick), is my explicit lowest-confidence candidate of the three and was marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic. It does not physically overlap this locus (its gene body sits ~590kb outside the window), never tops Open Targets' L2G model for any trait here, and its one COPD-subphenotype genetic-association finding (Boueiz et al. 2017, emphysema distribution, row 12) has an unresolved and load-bearing ambiguity: neither the researcher nor citation-verifier could confirm from the abstract whether that reported TRAPPC9-region signal is the same LD block as this locus's lead variant, or a distinct, non-overlapping association elsewhere near TRAPPC9's own gene body. I flag this explicitly rather than defer to the statistical prioritization: FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS methods can implicate genes via genome-wide expression/functional priors independent of a locus's actual fine-mapped, physically-linked causal gene, and every piece of locus-specific evidence here (physical overlap, credible-set annotation, L2G) points away from TRAPPC9, not toward it.

There is also a genuine alternative, non-pulmonary hypothesis that applies regardless of which of the three genes is named: this locus co-localizes (per Open Targets, not independently corroborated in primary literature since rs4961255 is not PubMed-indexed) with a large smoking-initiation GWAS signal in near-perfect LD with the lead variant, and the region's GWAS credible sets are otherwise dominated by blood-biomarker, blood-cell-count, and cardiovascular traits rather than any lung-function signal. Per this pipeline's own prior on smoking-mediated effects, a smoking-behavior-mediated (neurobiological) or non-pulmonary hematologic/biomarker mechanism is at least as well supported by the available evidence as any lung-structural or airway-immune mechanism for DENND3, SLC45A4, or TRAPPC9.

What would resolve this tie / raise confidence: (1) a COPD- or FEV1/FEV1-FVC-specific fine-mapped GWAS or colocalization result at this exact locus (currently absent from both the literature and Open Targets); (2) full-text confirmation of the Boueiz et al. 2017 emphysema-distribution lead-variant position relative to rs4961255, to determine whether TRAPPC9's COPD-subphenotype evidence is genuinely locus-specific or an independent signal; (3) any functional characterization of SLC45A4 (currently a complete literature gap) or of its missense variant rs3739238; (4) lung-tissue-specific eQTL colocalization for DENND3 or SLC45A4 at this locus; (5) independent confirmation (beyond the structured Open Targets attribution) of the smoking-initiation co-localization claim.
