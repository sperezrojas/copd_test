Evidence Table - Locus 3:10150001-10350000 (nearest gene TATDN2)

Lead variant: rs2543459 (PIP=0.871, upstream_gene_variant, annotated to TATDN2) Candidate genes: TATDN2 (nearest gene, dominant credible-set PIP mass); IRAK2 (MAGMA- and PoPS-prioritized, overlapped only by low-PIP intronic credible-set variants); ATP2B2 (added via targeted re-opening after a genome-build correction to the study team's MAGMA/PoPS gene-location merge file surfaced it as a previously-unevaluated candidate — see "ATP2B2 addendum" note below).

All rows below use only citations verdict VERIFIED by citation-verifier. Two researcher-cited sources were excluded as CLAIM_MISMATCH — PMID 29848961 (original review) and PMID 42570138 (ATP2B2 addendum) — see audit appendix (audits/3_10150001-10350000_audit.md).

This locus's review was originally completed as a two-gene contest (TATDN2 vs. IRAK2, both CONTESTED, final call IRAK2/LOW confidence). It was subsequently re-opened after the study team discovered a genome-build error in the MAGMA/PoPS gene-location merge file; the corrected re-merge surfaced ATP2B2 (PMCA2) as a candidate never evaluated in the original review. The orchestrator independently confirmed via Open Targets that ATP2B2 spans chr3:10,324,023-10,708,007 (GRCh38, strand -1) and overlaps the locus analysis window (3:10,150,001-10,350,000) only marginally — about 26 kb at its extreme 3' end — and does not overlap the actual credible-set variant span (3:10,196,440-10,281,424) at all; the nearest credible-set variant (rs4684676, PIP=0.0011) is ~42.6 kb from ATP2B2's nearest edge. ATP2B2 was not flagged by MAGMA, PoPS, nearest-gene, or ABC prioritization. A full targeted literature search and red-team critique were nonetheless conducted (per the study team's request) and are included below for completeness and traceability, but the red-team-skeptic's verdict is RULED OUT for ATP2B2 (see Prioritized-gene synthesis and audit appendix for full reasoning) — its inclusion is assessed as most likely a window-boundary artifact of the merge-file correction rather than genuine evidence of causal involvement at this locus.

Prioritized gene

Prioritized gene: IRAK2 Confidence: LOW

This call is unchanged from the original review. The addendum investigation of ATP2B2 (PMCA2), prompted by a genome-build correction to the study team's MAGMA/PoPS gene-location merge file, does not create a three-way tie and does not shift the prioritization away from IRAK2. ATP2B2's positional claim to this locus is markedly weaker than either TATDN2's or IRAK2's: it overlaps the analysis window only at its extreme 3' end (~26 kb of a >380 kb gene), does not overlap the actual credible-set variant span at all (nearest credible-set variant is ~42.6 kb away), and was not independently flagged by MAGMA, PoPS, nearest-gene, or ABC prioritization — all four gene-prioritization methods already applied to this locus declined to nominate it even after the merge-file fix (per the researcher's review of the locus input file). Its literature is genuinely informative but entirely non-supportive: ATP2B2/PMCA2's best-established roles are cochlear hair-cell (PMID 23792079) and cerebellar Purkinje-cell (PMID 22789621) calcium handling, with a human hearing-loss genetic association (PMID 31886164) and zero Open Targets COPD/FEV1-FVC association score. Most informatively, the researcher specifically investigated whether PMCA-family airway-smooth-muscle or T-cell calcium-signaling biology might extend to PMCA2 by paralog compensation, and found isoform-resolved negative evidence at every turn: airway smooth muscle and T-cell literature consistently and specifically implicates PMCA1 (ATP2B1) and/or PMCA4 (ATP2B4), never PMCA2 (ATP2B2), with no redundancy/compensation literature bridging the gap (PMID 30280184, 24912144, 38763427, 37905552, 28534960, 31866874, 32966125, 27528628, 26729804 — all VERIFIED). Per red-team-skeptic critique, ATP2B2's extreme genetic constraint (LoF oe=0.123, far higher than TATDN2's 0.708 or IRAK2's 0.759) is a red herring for lung relevance — it tracks with ATP2B2's real top disease associations (hearing loss, neurodevelopmental disorders, epilepsy), not with any pulmonary phenotype. The red-team-skeptic's explicit verdict for ATP2B2 is RULED OUT, with the assessment that its surfacing by the corrected merge file most likely reflects a window-boundary artifact (the fix changed which genes' coordinate ranges are flagged as "overlapping the analysis window," not new evidence of a mechanistic role at this locus) — a finding the orchestrator flags to the study team as worth checking systematically across other loci reprocessed by the same corrected merge file, since a gene clipped only at its 3'/5' terminus by a window boundary is a materially different (and much weaker) kind of "candidate" than a gene overlapped by credible-set variants or fine-mapping signal.

With ATP2B2 ruled out, the original two-gene TATDN2-vs-IRAK2 tension stands exactly as before, and the reasoning for the IRAK2/LOW call is unchanged from the original review: the credible-set fine-mapping signal at this locus is overwhelmingly annotated to TATDN2 (lead variant rs2543459, PIP=0.871, plus nearly all remaining PIP mass across upstream/intronic/synonymous/missense TATDN2-annotated variants), yet TATDN2's established biology (TatD-family nuclease, R-loop resolution, germ-cell genome maintenance) offers no plausible mechanism for airflow obstruction, emphysema, or airway inflammation, and no COPD/lung-function literature exists for the gene at all. By contrast, IRAK2 — the nearest gene overlapped by only ~2% of cumulative PIP mass via low-PIP intronic variants — is independently prioritized by two gene-level statistical methods (MAGMA and PoPS), is a canonical Myddosome/TLR/IL-1R-family signaling node consistent with this pipeline's prior on inflammatory heterogeneity in COPD, and has two pieces of evidence that are directly COPD-specific rather than extrapolated from asthma or sepsis models: elevated sputum expression predicting exacerbation frequency in a COPD(+asthma) cohort (PMID 28223794), and hub-gene status in a COPD blood/lung shared differential-expression network (PMID 36194576).

However, the red-team-skeptic's critique substantially tempers this pick and is the reason confidence remains LOW rather than MODERATE: (1) MAGMA and PoPS are not independent confirmatory statistical-genetic evidence on the order of fine-mapping or colocalization — MAGMA's gene-based window may not be statistically distinguishable from the same TATDN2-dominant association signal given the genes' physical proximity, and PoPS is known to favor well-annotated, biologically "compelling" genes (which IRAK2, a well-studied innate-immune gene, certainly is) independent of whether it is the true local causal gene; (2) the two direct-COPD citations are correlative transcriptomic/expression evidence, not genetic association, and cannot establish that IRAK2 dysregulation is causally upstream of COPD risk rather than a downstream consequence of airway inflammation; (3) the bulk of the remaining IRAK2 literature is asthma/pediatric or generic sepsis/LPS mechanism, extrapolated to COPD; (4) no fine-mapping, colocalization, TWAS, or Mendelian-randomization study anywhere in the literature or in Open Targets ties this locus's specific credible-set variants to IRAK2 expression or function — Open Targets shows zero genetic-association/colocalization evidence for any of the three genes at this locus for COPD or FEV1/FVC. The runner-up, TATDN2, cannot be ruled out on current evidence: it carries the dominant PIP mass and one credible architectural hypothesis (GHRLOS antisense-transcript overlap, and a separate, non-credible-set eQTL note suggesting this general genomic region has cis-regulatory reach to IRAK2) that is compatible with either gene being the true regulatory target.

What would resolve this tie: a lung/airway-tissue eQTL or splice-QTL colocalization analysis directly using this locus's own credible-set variants (particularly the lead variant rs2543459 and the missense variant rs2241314) against TATDN2 and IRAK2 expression; an ABC/Hi-C or other enhancer-promoter linking dataset for this locus (currently NA in the internal pipeline); a COPD-specific or FEV1/FVC Mendelian-randomization or TWAS analysis; and completion of the smoking-behavior GWAS overlap check that neither the researcher nor the red-team-skeptic could complete in either review session (WebSearch budget exhausted both times) — since the COPD GWAS underlying this locus is not smoking-adjusted, ruling in or out a smoking-initiation/nicotine-dependence mechanism at this exact locus is a prerequisite for confidently assigning either gene to a pulmonary-biology pathway versus a neurobehavioral/smoking-mediated one. Separately, the study team should also consider auditing whether the corrected merge file is over-including genes based on wide analysis-window overlap (as with ATP2B2 here) rather than credible-set-span overlap at other reprocessed loci. The study team should weigh this recommendation accordingly and treat it as provisional pending that follow-up work.
