Open Targets summary (structured database lookup — not a verified literature citation; did not go through citation-verifier or red-team-skeptic)

Locus-to-gene (L2G) predictions for lead variant rs62201738, across 18 GWAS studies (COPD case-control, FEV1, FEV1/FVC, PEF, asthma, smoking-status) whose credible sets contain this variant: the ONLY two genes ever nominated are PID1 and SPHKAP.

  • PID1 scores higher in 5/6 direct COPD case-control GWAS and both combined smoking-status+COPD GWAS (e.g. PID1 0.470 vs SPHKAP 0.409 in GCST90399697).
  • SPHKAP scores higher in essentially all FEV1/FVC-ratio- and PEF-specific GWAS (e.g. SPHKAP 0.724 vs PID1 0.182 in GCST007431).
  • FEV1-specific studies split (one favors each gene).
  • CCL20 and LINC01807 are never nominated by L2G in any of the 18 studies.

Colocalization (GWAS-GWAS, COLOC/eCAVIAR): Cross-trait H4 > 0.95 consistently between COPD, FEV1/FVC, FEV1, PEF, asthma, and smoking-status-measurement GWAS — confirms a single shared locus signal spanning pulmonary function, COPD case-control status, and smoking behavior. No eQTL/pQTL/sQTL/tuQTL colocalization data resolvable at this exact variant (count = 0) — absence of data, not evidence against a molecular-QTL mechanism.

Genetic constraint: PID1 LoF oe = 0.518 (0.312–0.905), moderately constrained. SPHKAP LoF oe = 0.478 (0.392–0.586), constraint score ≈2.4×10⁻⁶ — more strongly LoF-constrained than PID1. CCL20 too small a gene (few expected LoF variants) for an informative constraint estimate. LINC01807 has no constraint data (non-coding gene).

Prioritized gene

PID1 — confidence LOW.

This is a close call between PID1 and SPHKAP, and the pipeline's own red-team-skeptic explicitly recommends against treating either as clearly preferred. I am naming PID1 as the marginally better-supported pick, but the case is genuinely thin and could reasonably be called a tie.

Why PID1, on balance: (1) PID1 is the only one of the two genes with any direct human COPD case-control genetic-association evidence from a source outside the discovery GWAS itself — the Kim et al. 2014 Korean candidate-gene study (PMID 24737086) found a PID1 SNP associated with COPD after adjusting for smoking intensity, with no SNP×smoking interaction detected, which is at least a modest data point against the "this locus is purely a smoking-behavior signal" alternative hypothesis. (2) PID1 outscores SPHKAP by L2G in 5 of 6 direct COPD case-control GWAS and in both combined smoking-status+COPD GWAS, which is the disease definition this pipeline is built around, even though the skeptic correctly notes this margin is thin (0.47 vs 0.41) and that spirometric-trait GWAS (which favor SPHKAP) should be weighted comparably, not discounted, per this pipeline's own priors on lung-function evidence.

Why this is not a HIGH- or even MODERATE-confidence call: SPHKAP wins L2G decisively in essentially every FEV1/FVC-ratio- and PEF-specific GWAS at this locus (e.g. 0.72 vs 0.18 in GCST007431) — spirometric traits that are directly core to COPD pathophysiology per this pipeline's own biological priors — and SPHKAP is more strongly LoF-constrained than PID1 (a marker of likely functional importance, though not of tissue specificity). Neither gene has a shred of independently established lung-specific structural, developmental, or immune/inflammatory literature: PID1's documented biology is insulin/lipid-metabolism signaling (liver) and growth/apoptosis regulation (brain tumors); SPHKAP's is cAMP/PKA scaffolding (heart, spleen, mitochondria) and sphingosine kinase regulation (fibroblasts) — none of it lung tissue. No eQTL/pQTL/sQTL colocalization data exists at this variant to nominate either gene via a molecular mechanism. The entire credible set sits inside the neighboring lncRNA LINC01807, which is itself never prioritized by any method (L2G/MAGMA/PoPS/ABC) and has no COPD or lung association at all — the causal mechanism, whichever gene it points to, most plausibly acts through distal regulatory elements within/near LINC01807, a hypothesis this literature review cannot further adjudicate.

Runner-up: SPHKAP, essentially tied. What would resolve the tie: (a) a lung-tissue eQTL or splice-QTL colocalization at rs62201738 (or its LD proxies) for either gene, which Open Targets currently lacks; (b) an independent PID1 replication study outside the single Korean cohort, or conversely a null-replication report; (c) any literature search connecting SPHKAP's sphingosine-kinase-inhibitory function to the established ceramide/sphingolipid-signaling literature in emphysema pathogenesis, which the skeptic flagged as an unexplored but plausible mechanistic thread that was not searched in this pass.

CCL20 is explicitly downgraded and not treated as competitive here: it is never nominated by L2G at this locus in any of 18 GWAS studies, its aggregate Open Targets COPD association is driven almost entirely by literature/text-mining rather than genetics, it sits ~820kb from the lead variant (well outside the credible set), and one of its own key supporting citations (Tasena et al. 2026, PMID 41955101) names a different gene (IL-6) as the mechanistically proven driver in the very same experiment. Its real CCL20-CCR6/Th17 airway-inflammation literature is set against a much larger and better-established CCL20 literature in gut (IBD) and skin (psoriasis) mucosal immunity, exactly as the pipeline's biological priors anticipated as a possible outcome.

LINC01807 is carried forward only as the physical, non-causal host of the credible set (no computational gene-prioritization method nominates it, and it has no COPD association in Open Targets), not as a competing causal candidate.

Caveat on search coverage: the red-team-skeptic's own web-search budget was exhausted before it could run any independent contradiction search this session — its critique is reasoned from the assembled evidence, not from a fresh literature check. A follow-up search pass (PID1 replication outside the Korean cohort; SPHKAP-sphingolipid-pathway COPD literature; any paper naming an alternative causal gene at this 2q37 locus) is recommended before this locus's prioritization is finalized at higher confidence.
