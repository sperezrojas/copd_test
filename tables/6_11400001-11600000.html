Prioritized gene

Gene: TMEM170B Confidence: LOW

TMEM170B is named by every prioritization method that made a call at this locus (nearest gene, FLAMES, PoPS), it is the only protein-coding gene physically in the 200kb window, and it carries the only non-zero Open Targets L2G score (0.808) for the COPD/chronic-airway-obstruction credible set at this variant (see Open Targets summary above). By elimination and by the L2G/positional argument, it is the best-supported single candidate to name — there is no competing protein-coding gene in the region, so this is not really a "close call between two genes" situation but rather "one candidate, weakly supported."

That said, confidence must be LOW, not higher, because the weight of the assembled evidence actually cuts against a confident TMEM170B-causal-via-lung-transcription story, exactly as red-team-skeptic flagged (critique verdict: CONTESTED — see audit). Three specific reasons:

  1. No verified literature — cancer-biology or agricultural-genomics only (the six VERIFIED rows above) — establishes any lung, airway, or COPD-relevant function for TMEM170B. Its only documented mechanism (Wnt/β-catenin pathway suppression in breast/cervical/pancreatic/ hepatocellular cancer) is a plausible-sounding but entirely untested bridge to airway epithelial biology; treating it as supportive would be overclaiming.
  2. The Open Targets colocalization pattern is discordant with a TMEM170B-expression mechanism: the same COPD credible set colocalizes strongly with platelet-trait and cognitive/behavioral-trait GWAS signals, but shows no eQTL colocalization for TMEM170B itself in any tissue queried. This raises two genuine alternative hypotheses that the assembled evidence does not rule out: (a) a platelet/megakaryocyte-biology mechanism unrelated to TMEM170B transcription, and (b) per this pipeline's smoking-mediation prior, a neurobehavioral/smoking-proxy pathway (via the educational attainment/cognitive-performance/ADHD-ASD colocalization) rather than direct pulmonary biology. Neither alternative could be confirmed or refuted with the literature searches completed so far.
  3. A physically overlapping lncRNA (ENSG00000233656) spans the entire credible set and was not excluded as an alternative functional unit; ABC (designed to catch enhancer-promoter links) made no call at all at this locus, which is itself mildly discordant with a straightforward TMEM170B cis-regulatory mechanism.

What would resolve this: (a) a completed GWAS Catalog cross-check of rs693829's full reported trait profile (not completed here due to a WebSearch budget limit hit by both the researcher and red-team-skeptic — this is a concrete, actionable follow-up, not a fundamental barrier); (b) a direct eQTL/expression study of TMEM170B in lung/airway epithelial tissue or sorted airway immune-cell populations; (c) any functional study testing TMEM170B knockdown/knockout in airway epithelial models for a Wnt-pathway-mediated phenotype; (d) fine-mapping or functional annotation of the overlapping lncRNA ENSG00000233656. Until then, TMEM170B should be treated as the pipeline's best positional candidate, not a validated COPD gene, and the study team should weigh the platelet- and behavioral-trait pleiotropy explicitly before treating this locus as evidence of TMEM170B-driven airway biology.
