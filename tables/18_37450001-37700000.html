Prioritized gene

CELF4 — Confidence: LOW.

CELF4 is the only candidate gene at this locus by every available prioritization method (nearest gene and ABC concordant; MAGMA/PoPS/FLAMES not informative; Ensembl confirms no other protein-coding gene in the interval), so there is no competing gene to weigh it against — the "which gene" question is essentially answered by elimination, not by gene-specific mechanistic evidence. Confidence is nonetheless LOW, not HIGH, because the mechanistic case for why CELF4 drives this COPD association is weak to actively contradicted: (1) Open Targets shows a real, fairly strong locus-level COPD genetic-association evidence score (~0.87, gwas_credible_sets datasource) but zero colocalization between any of CELF4's 24 tissue QTLs — including its own lung eQTL — and COPD or any lung-function trait; the lung eQTL colocalizes instead with BMI and waist-hip ratio; (2) a dedicated COPD gene-by-smoking-interaction GWAS (Kim et al. 2021, VERIFIED) does not find this locus among its genome-wide-significant hits; (3) no PubMed literature search (by researcher or the red-team-skeptic's attempted independent follow-up, which could not execute due to session web-search budget exhaustion) found any direct link between CELF4 and lung structural/developmental biology, immune/inflammatory biology, smoking-initiation/cessation GWAS, or COPD/FEV1 replication; (4) CELF4's well-replicated, verified literature role is neuronal (excitatory-neurotransmission/seizure-susceptibility gene per Wagnon 2011 and Yang 2007) and, independently, an obesity-locus role (Fjorder 2019, verified) — not pulmonary or immune. Red-team- skeptic marked CELF4 CONTESTED on this basis (see audit file) — a real statistical signal with a mechanism that has been actively searched-for and repeatedly redirected toward obesity/metabolic and neurobehavioral pleiotropy rather than direct pulmonary or smoking-mediated biology. This locus is a case where "gene identity" is not in question but "causal mechanism relevant to COPD" is genuinely unresolved; the study team should weigh whether to treat this as a real but indirect/pleiotropic (obesity- or behavior-confounded) signal rather than a mechanistically validated pulmonary-disease gene. What would resolve this: a direct CELF4-lung/airway functional study, a COPD or FEV1/FEV1-FVC GWAS colocalization specifically (not just BMI/WHR), or a smoking-behavior GWAS colocalization at this exact locus — none of which currently exist in the evidence gathered. The red-team-skeptic's independent confirmation search for this call could not be completed due to a session-wide web-search tool budget limit (see audit file); this should be revisited with a fresh search budget before treating the CONTESTED call as fully adversarially tested.
