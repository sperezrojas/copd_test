Prioritized gene

Prioritized gene: ELN Confidence: MODERATE (unchanged by the 2026-09-11 RFC2 addition)

ELN is the best-supported candidate at this locus, but the case is weaker than the classical "elastin causes emphysema" mechanistic prior alone would suggest, and this table does not overclaim beyond what verifier + skeptic actually support.

In favor: ELN is the only candidate gene at this locus with (a) a physically confirmed GRCh38 position inside the locus window, (b) the lead credible-set variant (rs34208922, PIP 0.70) located directly within its gene body (3'UTR), and (c) no competing coordinate/ build-mismatch problem (unlike GTF2I and GTF2IRD1, both of which the researcher and red-team-skeptic independently identified as likely included via a GRCh37/GRCh38 gene-model mismatch — see build caveat above — and which red-team-skeptic marked CONTESTED for that reason). LIMK1, ELN's only real within-window competitor, is annotated solely to low-PIP (≤4.2%), non-coding, intronic/downstream credible-set variants, has a much lower Open Targets aggregated COPD score (0.06 vs. ELN's 0.29), and has zero literature connecting it to lung/airway/immune biology — red-team-skeptic agreed this is best read as a bystander gene tagged by the same LD block as the dominant ELN signal, not an independent candidate.

Against (why confidence is MODERATE and not HIGH): red-team-skeptic identified that nearly every literature citation assembled specifically to support ELN's COPD relevance failed on verification or describes the wrong genetic architecture. The Open Targets L2G-curated source paper (PMID 40155373) and both candidate foundational COPD/lung-function GWAS papers (PMID 30804561, PMID 30804560) were all CLAIM_MISMATCH — their abstracts do not name ELN, LIMK1, or this chr7 region, so there is currently no literature-confirmed FEV1/FEV1-FVC spirometric concordance or COPD-locus-table listing for this signal (a genuine absence per this pipeline's spirometry prior, not just an unconfirmed footnote). The only VERIFIED literature that does connect ELN to disease is either (i) a rare, high-penetrance familial coding mutation (G773D, PMID 16081882) in early-onset COPD — a different variant and architecture than the common 3'UTR rs34208922 — or (ii) rare-variant Mendelian/haploinsufficiency biology in arterial/vascular tissue (Williams-Beuren arteriopathy, supravalvular aortic stenosis; 8 PMIDs), not lung parenchyma. ELN's own gnomAD pLoF constraint score is essentially zero (population-tolerant of LoF), which sits somewhat awkwardly next to a haploinsufficiency-based mechanistic story. No smoking-behavior GWAS overlap was found either way (untested, not refuted) — this locus's dependence on smoking-mediated vs. direct pulmonary biology remains unresolved.

Runner-up: LIMK1, only by virtue of sharing the same LD block and locus window — not on independent evidence of its own, which is weaker than ELN's on every axis checked (Open Targets score, PIP, literature volume).

RFC2 (evaluated 2026-09-11, does not change the call): RFC2 was surfaced as a new candidate after the study team fixed a genome-build error in the internal MAGMA/PoPS gene-location merge file. Orchestration independently confirmed RFC2's GRCh38 coordinates (chr7:74,223,231-74,254,458) genuinely overlap the locus window — unlike GTF2I/GTF2IRD1, this is not a build-mismatch artifact. However, on the full weight of evidence gathered, RFC2 does not compete with ELN for the prioritized-gene call: (1) it does not overlap the actual fine-mapped credible-set variant span (nearest edge ~96 kb from the credible-set cluster, versus the lead variant sitting directly inside ELN's 3'UTR); (2) Open Targets shows a complete absence of COPD or emphysema association evidence from any datasource (not merely low — absent, the same profile GTF2IRD1 had); (3) no PubMed-indexed statistical-genetics, structural/developmental-lung, or immune/inflammatory literature connects RFC2 to COPD, lung function, smoking-behavior traits, or airway/immune biology of any kind; (4) gnomAD constraint data show RFC2 is dosage-tolerant (pLoF oe 0.82, missense oe 0.95), which argues against — rather than for — a haploinsufficiency-style mechanism that might otherwise make a passenger gene near a GWAS signal more plausible. Red-team-skeptic reviewed this dossier and concluded RFC2 is "WEAKENED, bordering on non-candidate," explicitly declining to mark it CONTESTED only because no independent contradicting citation was in hand (its own search budget was exhausted), not because a genuine case for RFC2 exists. Per pipeline convention this is treated as an evaluated-and-rejected candidate, not a genuine competitor to ELN — this is not a tie: the evidentiary gap between ELN (Open Targets COPD score 0.29, lead variant in gene body, weakened-but-real disease-biology literature) and RFC2 (COPD score absent entirely, no credible-set overlap, no relevant literature at all) is large and one-directional. Note explicitly: the classical "elastin causes emphysema" mechanistic prior that supports ELN's plausibility does not apply to, and was not applied to, RFC2 (a DNA-replication clamp-loader subunit) — RFC2 was evaluated strictly on its own merits, and none were found.

What would resolve remaining uncertainty: (1) a genuine colocalization or fine-mapping analysis that explicitly names this chr7:74.0-74.1 Mb region and assigns it to ELN vs. LIMK1 in a COPD or FEV1/FEV1-FVC GWAS (full-text check of Sakornsakolpat 2019 and Shrine 2019's locus tables, which the researcher could not confirm from abstracts alone, would be the fastest way to close this gap); (2) an eQTL/functional study of the rs34208922 3'UTR variant itself (e.g., effect on ELN mRNA stability/expression in lung tissue); (3) resolution of whether GTF2I/GTF2IRD1's inclusion in this locus is genuinely a GRCh37/GRCh38 build artifact in the internal MAGMA/PoPS pipeline (a pipeline-methods check, not a literature question) — if confirmed, both should likely be dropped from consideration at this locus entirely; (4) an LD computation between rs34208922 and any variants in the RFC2 region — red-team-skeptic flagged this as the one still-missing piece of evidence that could make RFC2's exclusion fully airtight rather than merely well-supported (not required to reach the current conclusion, but would close the gap).

The study team should weigh this recommendation alongside the caveats above; ELN's positional/fine-mapping case is the strongest of the now-five candidates evaluated at this locus, but the locus-specific literature record is thinner, once verified, than the classical elastin-emphysema mechanistic prior might suggest at first glance.
