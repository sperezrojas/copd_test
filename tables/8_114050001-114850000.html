Prioritized gene

INSUFFICIENT — no gene prioritized with meaningful confidence.

Neither candidate gene is well supported, and the honest call at this locus is that the causal gene/mechanism has not been identified by any of nearest-gene, MAGMA, PoPS, or ABC methods. Reasoning:

  • This is a genuine ~800kb intergenic gene desert (Ensembl lookup, Open Targets summary above): no protein-coding gene physically overlaps the locus or the 137-variant credible set. TRPS1 sits ~1.0-1.4 Mb telomeric; CSMD3 ends ~1.0 Mb centromeric. ABC prioritization returned NA for both, and FLAMES returned NA — i.e. neither of the two regulatory/functional prioritization methods in this pipeline's own toolkit could anchor either gene to this window.
  • The lead variant (rs290632, PIP=0.176) carries no dedicated literature and no GWAS Catalog phenotype of its own; fine-mapping resolution is very poor (max PIP 0.176 across 137 variants spanning ~600kb), so smoking-mediation (prior #1) cannot even be assessed one way or the other — the relevant data are simply absent, not negative.
  • CSMD3 (evidence table rows 1-5): the single best locus-specific finding — a 3C chromatin interaction from a region ~113kb outside the credible set to the CSMD3 promoter, associated with a lung-function-decline trajectory in a small (n=581) childhood asthma cohort with an unreplicated (or at least unreported) COPD follow-up (PMID 27367781) — is real but explicitly marked WEAKENED by red-team-skeptic, not sufficient alone to establish causality. Countering it: Open Targets shows a near-zero aggregate COPD genetic-association score for CSMD3 with no genetic_association datatype score and no independent L2G/credible-set record at all; CSMD3's status as one of the largest human genes is a documented, self-acknowledged confound for MAGMA/PoPS gene-based prioritization (and for its frequent appearance as a "passenger" gene in cancer mutation catalogs) independent of true causal biology; and the "immune infiltration" cancer-genomics literature (PMID 42724489, PMID 42327582) is non-mechanistic and contributes no germline-immunology evidence per red-team-skeptic. Red-team-skeptic verdict: CONTESTED.
  • TRPS1 (evidence table rows 6-9): zero PubMed hits tie it to COPD, spirometry, or smoking behavior at all; its only lung-tissue-relevant data point (IHC marker studies, PMID 40170411) shows LOW/absent TRPS1 expression in lung tissue relative to breast — mild evidence against, not for, a prominent native lung role; its strongest "respiratory" literature (PMID 12446778) is a severe homozygous knockout mouse dying of a mechanical thoracic-skeletal defect, a poor analogy for a common regulatory variant of modest effect. Red-team-skeptic verdict: WEAKENED, bordering CONTESTED.
  • Neither gene has any Open Targets evidence for FEV1 or FEV1/FVC — the pipeline's prior #2 (spirometric concordance strengthens a locus) is simply unaddressed for both candidates, which itself argues that the causal mechanism at this locus has not been correctly localized to either gene by the methods applied so far.

Runner-up framing: if forced to rank, CSMD3 has marginally more specific (though single-study, unreplicated, and confound-laden) locus-relevant evidence than TRPS1, whose candidacy is essentially proximity-only. But this is a close call built on weak evidence on both sides, not a confident pick.

What would resolve this: (1) confirmation of whether the McGeachie et al. COPD-cohort replication of rs4445257/lung-function-decline actually reached significance; (2) independent Hi-C/eQTL/ABC data at higher resolution for this specific window in lung-relevant tissue/cell types (airway epithelium, alveolar macrophage, fibroblast) to determine which gene(s), if any, this desert region regulates; (3) fine-mapping refinement (this credible set's max PIP of 0.176 across 137 variants is too diffuse to confidently anchor to either gene); (4) a search (not completed here due to red-team-skeptic's search budget exhaustion) for GWAS Catalog / smoking-behavior associations at rs290632 and its LD proxies, and for any other genes further outside the two ~1Mb windows examined that might be better regulatory targets of this desert.

Confidence: INSUFFICIENT.
