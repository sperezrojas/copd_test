Prioritized gene

ASTN2 — confidence: LOW.

ASTN2 is the only protein-coding gene whose body physically contains the entire credible set (per the researcher's Ensembl lookup, all 23 credible-set variants including the lead rs7034049 fall within ASTN2 introns/exon), and it has one independently-verified, gene-specific replication in spirometric GWAS (Soler Artigas et al. 2015 — satisfying prior #2's concordance-with-lung-function criterion). On physical/positional grounds alone, ASTN2 is the best-supported single gene at this locus.

However, confidence must be kept LOW, not higher, for several concrete reasons preserved from the evidence table and critique above: (1) the only COPD-specific (as opposed to spirometry-specific) association found — Joo & Himes 2022 — is a gene-based test that cannot statistically separate ASTN2 from its physically-adjacent, pipeline-unscreened neighbor TRIM32, and is itself an unreplicated conference-proceedings finding, not a peer-reviewed journal result; (2) essentially all of ASTN2's mechanistic/functional literature (Wilson 2010, Chang 2017, Lara 2019, Lionel 2013, Levine 2026) describes a neuronal-glial migration/ receptor-trafficking gene family with no documented lung, airway, or immune-cell biology of any kind; (3) Open Targets independently flags ASTN2's single strongest disease/trait association as smoking initiation, not any pulmonary trait, raising a genuine, unresolved possibility that this locus's COPD GWAS signal (unadjusted for smoking) is mediated through a neurobehavioral/smoking-initiation pathway rather than pulmonary structural biology (prior #1) — though the one candidate-gene study testing this directly for ASTN2 (Freitag 2016, ADHD/ anxiety) found only nominal, uncorrected associations, so this alternative mechanism is itself unconfirmed rather than demonstrated; (4) no dedicated GTEx lung-expression lookup or direct rs7034049 GWAS Catalog/PhenoScanner confirmation was completed in this pipeline run (flagged gaps, not negative findings).

Runner-up / unresolved co-candidate: TRIM32. Not in the original pipeline candidate list (FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS/ABC all NA or silent on it) and not independently scored by Open Targets in this session, but raised by the researcher as the physically-adjacent gene actually implicated in the CNV-deletion literature (correcting an erroneous premise about CTNNA3, which is on a different chromosome), and co-named alongside ASTN2 in the one COPD-specific gene-based association study found. TRIM32 is an E3 ubiquitin ligase causing limb-girdle muscular dystrophy 2H and Bardet-Biedl syndrome 11 (Mendelian muscle/ciliopathy disease) with no COPD- or lung-specific functional literature identified in this pipeline run. What would resolve the tie: (a) fine-mapping/colocalization evidence (eQTL or Hi-C/ABC) distinguishing which gene's expression the credible-set variants regulate; (b) a dedicated FLAMES/PoPS/MAGMA/ Open Targets pass on TRIM32 itself; (c) confirmation of whether rs7034049 or the ASTN2/TRIM32 locus specifically appears in the Sakornsakolpat 2019 or Shrine 2019 COPD/lung-function locus tables (currently unconfirmed — see audit); (d) direct evidence (GWAS Catalog/PhenoScanner) on whether rs7034049 or its proxies are reported lead SNPs for smoking-initiation traits, to adjudicate prior #1 vs. prior #2 at this specific locus.

COL27A1 is not recommended as the prioritized gene at this locus: despite genuine developmental lung expression and one striking mouse perinatal-lethality phenotype, it has no COPD-specific literature, no independent spirometry signal, a purely skeletal/craniofacial Mendelian disease phenotype, and — most decisively — sits ~2.6 Mb from every credible-set variant, well outside plausible direct cis-regulatory range for a PoPS-only flag (a method that does not require or imply physical proximity). This is treated as effectively ruled out for this specific locus, pending the one independent coordinate re-check noted in the audit appendix.

This is the pipeline's best-supported recommendation for the study team's own judgment, not a substitute for it — particularly given how much of the case rests on unresolved ASTN2/TRIM32 gene-ambiguity and an untested smoking-mediation alternative.
