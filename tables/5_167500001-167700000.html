Prioritized gene

TENM2 — Confidence: LOW.

TENM2 is the only candidate gene at this locus: it is the nearest gene, the FLAMES-prioritized gene, and the PoPS-prioritized gene, and all 49 credible-set variants (including the lead variant rs11747772, PIP=0.148) are intronic to TENM2 with no missense/splice variants implicating any other transcript. In that narrow sense — which gene is physically/ positionally implicated at this locus — the call is not close: there is no competing candidate gene in the evidence gathered. However, confidence is capped at LOW because the verified evidence does not establish why TENM2 is associated with the COPD GWAS phenotype, and the two competing explanations are not resolved in TENM2's favor for a pulmonary mechanism:

  • The strongest, most specific, and best-verified evidence (Quach et al. 2020, PMID 33144568; Trofimova et al. 2026, PMID 41619226; Abdurahaman et al. 2025, PMID 41428022) supports prior #1: TENM2 is a genome-wide-significant nicotine-dependence/smoking-behavior locus acting through synaptic/CNS biology, and its single highest Open Targets disease association out of 600 diseases is smoking initiation. Because the COPD GWAS summary statistics are not adjusted for smoking behavior, this locus's COPD association is plausibly substantially confounded by or mediated through smoking-initiation/nicotine-dependence genetics rather than independent pulmonary pathology. and confirmed (per citation-verifier) NOT to contain TENM2 among its 279 reported signals — meaning there is currently no verified primary publication establishing a TENM2-specific pulmonary-function association at all, despite Open Targets carrying a nonzero score for those traits.
  • There is a total absence of COPD-specific, structural/developmental lung, or immune/inflammatory mechanistic literature for TENM2 (both the researcher and red-team- skeptic agree on this).
  • Red-team-skeptic explicitly flagged the researcher's argument that TENM2's lung eQTL "complicates" the smoking-confound interpretation as overreaching speculation not supported by the underlying paper, and characterized the overall picture as "WEAKENED bordering on effectively recharacterized" for a pulmonary-disease role, while stopping short of a formal CONTESTED label because no paper was found that positively contradicts TENM2 or implicates a different gene (see audit file for full reasoning).

What would resolve this: (1) a formal colocalization analysis between the internal COPD credible set at this locus and the TENM2 lung eQTL (or brain eQTLs) to test whether the same causal variant(s) drive both signals; (2) a genetically-informed analysis (e.g., MR or smoking-stratified GWAS) testing whether the TENM2-COPD association attenuates after conditioning on smoking-initiation/nicotine-dependence genetic liability; (3) direct confirmation (Ensembl region overlap query) that no other protein-coding gene lies within or overlaps this 200kb interval, which was flagged as an incomplete check by both the researcher and red-team-skeptic. Until then, TENM2 is the correct gene call by elimination, but its COPD-relevance should be reported as more consistent with a smoking-mediated pathway (prior #1) than a demonstrated pulmonary mechanism (prior #2), and should not be over-interpreted as established structural or inflammatory lung biology (prior #2/#3), for which there is no supporting evidence at all.
