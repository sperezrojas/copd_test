Prioritized gene

Gene: SLC9A2 Confidence: LOW

This call is close and should be read as the least-weak option among four genes with no strong evidence, rather than a confident pick. The single most COPD-relevant finding at this locus is the 2026 unified-airway-disease TWAS (PMID 42653451, VERIFIED), which names SLC9A2 as a top lung-expression signal for a shared COPD/asthma/bronchiectasis/CRSsNP genetic-liability factor. However, the red-team-skeptic's critique (preserved in full in the audit appendix) correctly notes this is a single, very recent, non-COPD-specific, non-colocalized finding, and that SLC9A2 sits ~390kb outside the stated locus window with no L2G/colocalization data tying it to this exact signal. Independent NHE-family literature (PMID 30300884) supports plausibility of an ion-transport role in airway mucociliary epithelium but is not SLC9A2-isoform-specific, and the original NHE-2 expression-profiling paper (PMID 7685026) found lung to be a comparatively low-expression tissue for this transporter. Open Targets shows no aggregate COPD association signal and no L2G/coloc data for SLC9A2 at all.

The runner-up is TMEM182 (the nearest gene), whose literature is exclusively about muscle/adipose developmental biology (myogenesis, cardiomyocyte differentiation, fat deposition) with zero direct lung evidence; the red-team-skeptic marked TMEM182 CONTESTED because its verified evidence affirmatively supports an alternative, non-pulmonary mechanism (body composition/muscle biology) rather than a COPD-relevant one, and any COPD link for TMEM182 depends on an untested comorbidity narrative (e.g., sarcopenia/respiratory-muscle-wasting or obesity-COPD interaction) rather than on the cited biology itself. Given the Open Targets pleiotropy pattern is dominated by body-composition/anthropometric traits sharing this exact lead SNP, TMEM182's adiposity/muscle biology is at least as plausible a mediating pathway for this locus's COPD signal as SLC9A2's epithelial-transporter hypothesis — this is precisely why the call is close.

CAPZBP1 and LINC01965 are not viable candidates: CAPZBP1 resolves to a functionless processed pseudogene inside the window with no literature of its own (the RCSD1 literature cited under this symbol is very likely a misattribution to a different gene on a different chromosome, per the red-team-skeptic's CONTESTED verdict), and LINC01965 has no literature at all.

What would resolve this tie: (1) a COPD-specific (not multi-disease latent-factor) colocalization or fine-mapping result directly linking rs17343925 (or a variant in strong LD) to SLC9A2 lung expression; (2) replication of the SLC9A2 lung-MTWAS finding by an independent group; (3) confirmation of whether this locus/lead variant appears in the Shrine et al. 2019 (PMID 30804560, VERIFIED) spirometry GWAS supplementary signal list — this pipeline could not access those tables, and a positive FEV1/FEV1-FVC hit would meaningfully strengthen a direct-pulmonary-biology case for whichever gene it implicates; (4) a Mendelian-randomization or comorbidity-adjusted analysis testing whether TMEM182-mediated body composition/lean mass causally affects COPD risk or severity, which would help adjudicate between the adiposity-mediated and epithelial-transporter hypotheses. Given the weakness of evidence on both sides, confidence is marked LOW rather than MODERATE, and this recommendation should be weighed accordingly by the study team.
