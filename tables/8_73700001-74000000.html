Prioritized gene

UBE2W — confidence: LOW.

This is a genuinely difficult call and the evidence base does not cleanly support any single gene as a COPD-relevant mechanism. UBE2W is named here as the best-supported candidate on positional/statistical grounds only: it carries roughly 85% of the credible set's cumulative PIP mass (intronic/3'UTR/downstream variants directly in the gene body), and Open Targets independently identifies it as the consistent top locus-to-gene (L2G) prediction at the lead variant across 14 GWAS credible sets — a pattern that, while built for non-COPD traits, at minimum confirms this variant's regulatory activity is real and centered on UBE2W rather than a neighboring gene. However, per the red-team critique, this same L2G pattern (BMI, insomnia, HDL cholesterol, type 2 diabetes, obesity — with zero COPD/FEV1 credible sets appearing at all in Open Targets) should be read as mild-to-moderate evidence against a lung-specific mechanism at this locus, not merely as neutral or missing data. No verified literature ties UBE2W to COPD, spirometry, or any COPD-relevant inflammatory program (neutrophilic, Th1/Th17, macrophage) — its verified literature is limited to general cell biology (N-terminal ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme activity; priming of the antiviral restriction factors TRIM21 and TRIM5α; CHIP/Hsp70 proteostasis), none of it lung- or COPD-specific (see rows above, skeptic verdict WEAKENED).

Why not the alternatives: TCEB1/ELOC has no credible-set variant annotated to it, is not among Open Targets L2G predictions at the lead variant, and its only literature is generic VHL/HIF-pathway biology dominated by renal cell carcinoma — verdict WEAKENED, bordering null. KCNB2's physical coordinates (confirmed via Ensembl REST) do not overlap this locus's stated window or any credible-set variant at all (~760kb-1.2Mb gap) — its only claim to inclusion is being named the PoPS-prioritized gene in the input file, which the red-team critique judged a likely pipeline/window-definition discrepancy; verdict CONTESTED, and it should be treated as effectively excluded from this locus pending upstream resolution rather than as a genuine competing candidate. LY96 (MD-2) has by far the most compelling COPD- and cigarette-smoke-specific mechanistic literature of any gene considered here (verified papers showing TLR4/MD2/MyD88 axis perturbation in cigarette-smoke-exposed mouse and human COPD airway tissue), and is a runner-up worth flagging explicitly — but it was not identified by any of the four internal prioritization methods, no credible-set variant maps to it, its physical overlap with the stated window is only the first ~9kb of a 41kb gene at the extreme 3' edge, and the nearest actual credible-set variants sit 120-240kb away with no colocalization or eQTL evidence offered to bridge that gap. Its own mechanistic literature is also internally contradictory on direction of effect (MD2 up in COPD per PMID 41177411 vs. MD2 down in smoker/COPD airway epithelium per PMID 26068048), further weakening confidence in it as a clean story even on its own terms. GYG1P1, STAU2, and TMEM70 have no COPD/lung-relevant literature and are appropriately deprioritized (verdict HOLDS as non-informative).

What would resolve this: (1) a COPD-specific or lung-function (FEV1/FEV1-FVC) GWAS colocalization or eQTL-in-lung-tissue analysis directly testing UBE2W vs. LY96 vs. neighboring genes at this credible set; (2) clarification from the upstream pipeline of why KCNB2 was named PoPS-prioritized for a window it does not physically overlap; (3) an independent literature search (not completed in this run due to the red-team-skeptic's search-budget exhaustion) explicitly probing for COPD/smoking-behavior literature on UBE2W and LY96 beyond what was found here; (4) resolution of the directional conflict between the two LY96/MD2 COPD-tissue papers. Absent that, this call should be treated as provisional and low-confidence, not as a settled assignment.
