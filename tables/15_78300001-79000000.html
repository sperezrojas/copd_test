Evidence table — Locus 15:78,300,001-79,000,000 (GRCh38)

Nearest gene: CHRNA5. Lead variant: rs17486278 (15:78,575,140, PIP=0.958, intron_variant of CHRNA5, MODIFIER impact) — sole credible-set variant. MAGMA/PoPS prioritized gene: ACSBG1. FLAMES/ABC: NA.

This is the canonical, extremely well-replicated 15q25.1 nicotinic acetylcholine receptor gene cluster (CHRNA5-CHRNA3-CHRNB4), one of the most pleiotropic loci in human genetics for smoking behavior, lung cancer, and COPD. Because the input file's candidate-gene list (CHRNA5, ACSBG1) does not capture the well-established multi-gene LD block at this locus, the orchestrator directed the researcher to also cover CHRNA3, CHRNB4, IREB2, PSMA4, and HYKK as secondary/comparison candidates, since a fair judgment about the causal gene here requires being able to distinguish CHRNA5 from its neighbors. Prioritized gene

Prioritized gene: CHRNA5 (confidence: LOW)

This call is close, and the evidence genuinely does not cleanly resolve a single causal gene at this locus — that ambiguity is itself the headline finding, not a caveat to bury. Four genes have real, independent claims to causality, each resting on a different evidentiary pillar:

  • CHRNA5 remains the single most consistent top-L2G gene across the largest number of independent GWAS at the exact lead variant, and has by far the strongest nicotine-dependence genetic signal (0.581) and plausible smoking-independent developmental lung-epithelial expression (Porter 2011, Reynolds 2010 — both VERIFIED). But per red-team-skeptic, the "smoking-independent COPD" framing for CHRNA5 is overclaimed: two of the researcher's own supporting citations (Li Q 2015, Ganbold 2021) are interaction-only findings that actually argue FOR smoking-mediation, not against it, and the single most contemporary multi-ancestry eQTL colocalization study (Kim et al. 2026) does not implicate CHRNA5 at all, favoring PSMA4 instead. CHRNA5 is also not top L2G in either the largest (HYKK) or smallest (CHRNA3) COPD-labeled GWAS sampled.
  • PSMA4 is the runner-up and, on a strict "modern colocalization/MR" reading, arguably the better-supported gene: it has the most consistent 2018-2026 TWAS/colocalization/MR evidence specifically for lung-function/COPD traits (Sakornsakolpat 2018, Wang 2024, Kim 2026 — all VERIFIED, SUPPORTED per skeptic), is the only non-CHRNA5 gene with any smoking-initiation signal, and is comparatively less entangled in the locus's broader smoking-behavior pleiotropy. Its weakness is the complete absence of an established structural or immune mechanistic story — its case rests on statistics alone.
  • IREB2 has the deepest historical mechanistic literature and, critically, the single strongest PIECE of causal evidence at this entire locus: Cloonan et al. 2016 (Nat Med, VERIFIED) shows Irp2(IREB2)-knockout mice are protected from cigarette-smoke-induced emphysema/bronchitis — a true loss-of-function causal experiment, a higher evidentiary tier than any association or colocalization data for any other gene here. However, this mechanism is explicitly smoke-exposure-dependent (a distinct claim from behavior/nicotine-dependence-independence per Prior 1), and IREB2 is never the top L2G gene in modern statistical fine-mapping, creating a genuine, unresolved tension between mechanistic depth and statistical fine-mapping support.
  • HYKK is CONTESTED and should not be treated as a serious independent candidate: its one "top L2G" result is directly undercut by Nedeljkovic 2018's own finding that the HYKK-tagging variant's regulatory effects act on IREB2/CHRNA3/PSMA4, not HYKK.
  • CHRNA3 is essentially indistinguishable from CHRNA5 by any evidence gathered (comparable magnitude, always co-reported, no distinguishing mechanism) — a real alternative/co-causal candidate but not separately actionable.
  • CHRNB4 is CONTESTED (directionally conflicting effect estimates across populations) and is the weakest of the nicotinic subunit genes.
  • ACSBG1, the pipeline's own MAGMA/PoPS pick, has essentially no direct genetic-association or colocalization support at this locus in any dataset queried, and its one specific immune mechanism (Kanno 2021, Treg mitochondrial fitness in airway inflammation, VERIFIED but WEAKENED per skeptic) is in a general allergic-airway model, not COPD, with no eQTL/regulatory bridge to this variant. It should not be prioritized over the LD-block genes on current evidence, though the Treg mechanism is real biology worth independent follow-up regardless of this locus's genetics.

CHRNA5 is named the top pick because it has the broadest base of convergent evidence (replicated statistical association at the exact lead variant, the strongest nicotine-dependence genetic signal, plausible non-neuronal lung developmental biology, and top L2G status in the plurality of GWAS sampled), but this is explicitly a plurality, not a majority or consensus, call. What would resolve the tie: a COPD or lung-function GWAS formally adjusted for a rich smoking-heaviness/nicotine-dependence covariate (not merely pack-years) that shows whether the CHRNA5/PSMA4/IREB2 signals persist, attenuate, or disappear; a lung-tissue-specific (not blood) colocalization/fine-mapping analysis with enough independent GWAS samples to stop the top-L2G gene from flipping across studies; and resolution of whether the Cloonan 2016 Irp2-knockout phenotype is replicated in a model that can distinguish nicotine-receptor-independent smoke toxicology from nicotine-receptor pharmacology. Given all of this, confidence is marked LOW rather than MODERATE or HIGH — the study team should treat CHRNA5, PSMA4, and IREB2 as three live hypotheses, not a settled call.
