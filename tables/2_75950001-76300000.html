Prioritized gene

GCFC2 — confidence: LOW.

GCFC2 is the best-supported single pick at this locus, but the case is close and should not be overstated. It is the internal pipeline's nearest-gene/FLAMES candidate, and it is the only gene Open Targets' own L2G model favors at a COPD-related credible set in this region (L2G 0.577 vs MRPL19's 0.056; TACR1 wasn't even in the L2G candidate set) — this is real, if imperfect, statistical-genetics support (the L2G score comes from a nearby but non-identical lead SNP, per the Open Targets summary above and the skeptic's critique, so it should be read as suggestive rather than definitive for this exact credible set).

However, the verified literature evidence table above shows that GCFC2's entire specific literature footprint is neurocognitive (developmental dyslexia, general cognitive ability, hippocampal volume, white-matter structure) — not pulmonary, not classically immune. The red-team-skeptic marked GCFC2 CONTESTED specifically on the claim of a direct pulmonary/immune mechanism: Open Targets shows null colocalization with FEV1/FEV1-FVC/spirometry despite strong colocalization with cognitive/psychiatric traits at the same credible set; the one candidate immune mechanism (GC-BP/IL-12 repression in efferocytosing macrophages, Kim et al. 2004) failed citation verification on whether GC-BP is even the same gene product as GCFC2; and GCFC2 shows gnomAD LOEUF ~1.618, i.e., no haploinsufficiency-type constraint signature. The asthma-vs-COPD association-score asymmetry (0.023 vs 0.213) further suggests the signal may be more smoking/cohort-composition-mediated than reflective of shared airway-obstruction biology (prior #1), though this remains a hypothesis, not an evidenced finding.

The runner-up, MRPL19, is weaker on every axis (10x lower L2G, no independent literature, a generic mitochondrial-translation housekeeping function with no COPD/lung-specific literature at all) but cannot be fully excluded, because GCFC2 and MRPL19 are co-regulated (a shared risk haplotype attenuates expression of both genes per Anthoni et al. 2007) — the existing evidence does not include gene-specific fine-mapping, chromatin-interaction, or allele-specific-expression data that would cleanly separate the two.

TACR1, added after red-team-skeptic flagged it as an under-searched but biologically compelling candidate, has by far the strongest COPD-mechanism-specific literature of the three genes at this locus — causal mouse-knockout data showing NK1-receptor-dependent, cigarette-smoke-driven macrophage/dendritic-cell recruitment and MMP-12-mediated emphysema, plus human airway-tissue expression data showing altered NK1R expression in COPD versus smoking controls. But this evidence carries zero direct genetic linkage to this specific credible set: no eQTL, no colocalization, no L2G inclusion, and no chromatin-interaction (Hi-C/ABC) evidence bridges the ~750-900kb distance, nearly double that of GCFC2/MRPL19. The skeptic's final verdict on TACR1 was "INSUFFICIENT-DISTANCE-EVIDENCE" — a biologically plausible but genetically unlinked candidate, not a co-equal alternative causal gene as currently evidenced. The skeptic also flagged unresolved internal tension in the TACR1 human/mouse evidence itself (Bai et al.'s reduced NK1R expression in established COPD sits awkwardly against De Swert et al.'s causal knockout-protects- against-damage model), further limiting how much weight TACR1's mechanistic story can carry without direct genetic confirmation.

What would resolve this three-way tie: (1) a direct colocalization or fine-mapping analysis using this pipeline's own credible set (not a nearby proxy) against GCFC2, MRPL19, and TACR1 eQTL/pQTL data in lung and relevant immune-cell types; (2) chromatin-interaction (Hi-C/ABC/ promoter-capture Hi-C) data for the credible-set interval to determine whether any regulatory loop reaches TACR1 despite the linear distance; (3) allele-specific expression or reporter-assay data at the credible-set lead variant(s) themselves (rs35328078 and the next few highest-PIP variants) to determine which gene's expression they actually perturb; (4) a smoking-initiation/ smoking-heaviness GWAS lookup specifically at this credible set to adjudicate whether the null Open Targets smoking-trait result is a true null or a data-coverage gap, which bears directly on whether prior #1 (smoking-mediated confounding) is operative here.

Given the absence of any of that discriminating evidence, GCFC2 is named as the single best- supported pick on statistical-genetics/positional grounds alone, with confidence marked LOW because the mechanistic case for GCFC2 specifically causing COPD pathology is actively contested, MRPL19 cannot be excluded as a co-regulated bystander, and TACR1 remains a live, biologically motivated alternative that current evidence can neither confirm nor rule out at this locus. This recommendation should be weighed by the study team alongside the runner-up candidates, not treated as a settled call.
