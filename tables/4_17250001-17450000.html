Prioritized gene

QDPR — confidence: LOW.

This call is close, and the evidence genuinely does not cleanly resolve which gene (if any) drives this locus's association with COPD/lung function. QDPR is named here rather than a "no call" for one reason only: it is the sole gene the internal pipeline's own candidate-gene procedures (nearest gene, FLAMES) actually nominated at this locus — MAGMA, PoPS, and ABC returned nothing, and neither CLRN2 nor LAP3 is an official candidate under this pipeline's own gene-nomination rules, despite CLRN2's higher Open Targets L2G score for an unrelated trait (body weight, not lung function).

Against QDPR, however, the record is genuinely weak, and the red-team-skeptic explicitly marked QDPR CONTESTED (see evidence table and audit appendix): it has zero COPD-labeled evidence in Open Targets, it is absent from the main text of the largest classic lung-function GWAS (Shrine et al. 2019, PMID 30804560, VERIFIED), its only pulmonary-function genetic-association records are sub-threshold L2G scores whose cited source (per Open Targets' own metadata) turned out to be the wrong paper entirely (CLAIM_MISMATCH), and it is outranked nearly 4-fold by a non-candidate neighboring gene (CLRN2) in Open Targets' own L2G algorithm at this exact locus (for a body-weight trait). No COPD-specific structural/developmental or immune/inflammatory mechanistic literature for QDPR was found. A biologically plausible route through smoking-related neurobiology exists (QDPR's role in BH4-dependent dopamine synthesis, supported by Kurosaki et al. 2019, PMID 30739038, VERIFIED) but this remains mechanistically speculative at this specific locus — no locus-level overlap with the standard smoking-behavior GWAS (GSCAN, PMID 30643251) was confirmed or ruled out, and the one human-genetics paper testing the BH4 pathway directly (Clot et al. 2009, PMID 19491146, VERIFIED) found zero QDPR mutations in its cohort.

Runner-up: CLRN2 (off-list; not promotable to official candidate status on current evidence, since it has no direct lung/COPD mechanism and its higher L2G score is anchored to an unrelated body-weight trait) — flagged by the skeptic as raising a genuinely under-explored alternative hypothesis: that this locus's primary phenotype may be metabolic/adiposity-related, with COPD/FEV1 associations arising as secondary pleiotropy, smoking-driven confounding, or a body-habitus spirometry artifact, rather than reflecting intrinsic airway or parenchymal lung biology at either QDPR or CLRN2.

What would resolve the tie: (1) a check of whether a post-2019 large-scale multi-ancestry lung-function GWAS names QDPR with higher confidence than Shrine et al. 2019 did; (2) a direct locus-level (rs921371 and LD proxies) lookup against GSCAN/other smoking-initiation-cessation-heaviness summary statistics to confirm or rule out smoking-mediation; (3) a direct locus-level lookup against BMI/adiposity GWAS (e.g. GIANT, UKB body-composition) and/or a Mendelian-randomization analysis of BMI-on-FEV1/COPD at this region, to adjudicate the metabolic-mediation alternative hypothesis raised for CLRN2; (4) gene-specific (not regional) colocalization or eQTL evidence in lung tissue for QDPR, CLRN2, and LAP3, none of which was available in this review. Given the number of open, decision-relevant questions identified independently by both the researcher and the skeptic, confidence is marked LOW rather than MODERATE or HIGH, and this recommendation should be weighed by the study team accordingly rather than treated as a settled call.
