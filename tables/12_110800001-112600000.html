COPD locus evidence table — 12:110,800,001-112,600,000 (nearest gene ALDH2)

Region: 12:110,800,001-112,600,000 (GRCh38). Lead variant: rs671 (12:111803962, missense_variant in ALDH2, PIP=0.178 — the well-characterized ALDH2*2/Glu504Lys East Asian loss-of-function allele). Credible set: 282 variants (ABF fine-mapping). This is the well-known, gene-dense 12q24.12 "pleiotropy hotspot" (ALDH2/SH2B3/ATXN2/BRAP region), associated with many traits beyond lung disease (alcohol consumption/use disorder, blood pressure, platelet/hematopoietic traits, autoimmune disease, lipids, coronary disease, etc.). Internal pipeline candidate genes: nearest = ALDH2, MAGMA = ATXN2, PoPS = CUX2, ABC = NAA25. The only credible-set variant besides rs671 with missense/splice consequence is rs3752630 (TMEM116, PIP=0.0039); all other 280 variants are intronic/UTR/intergenic/synonymous/ upstream/downstream and were rolled into gene-level evidence per pipeline scope rules.

Prioritized gene

ALDH2 — MODERATE confidence.

ALDH2 is the best-supported single gene at this locus, but "best-supported" here means best-supported relative to a genuinely crowded, unresolved field at a gene-dense pleiotropy hotspot — not a clean, high-confidence call. The case for ALDH2 rests on: (1) it is the nearest gene to, and contains, the lead credible-set variant itself (rs671, PIP=0.178, missense, the single highest-PIP variant in the entire 282-variant credible set); (2) the highest Open Targets L2G score of any gene at the locus, and uniquely, the top gene in TWO independent Open Targets COPD credible sets (0.897 and 0.746); (3) the only gene-specific human+mouse lung mechanistic literature at the entire locus (Kuroda et al., PMID 28431562); (4) direct human COPD-tissue evidence of dysregulated expression (elevated ALDH2 in COPD BALF, PMID 24188068); and (5) a smoking-conditional gene-environment interaction directly on airway obstruction (PMID 25978981), consistent with this pipeline's smoking-mediation prior.

However, per red-team-skeptic's critique (preserved in audits/12_110800001-112600000_audit.md), this evidence base is weaker than a simple "top gene" label conveys: the flagship Kuroda paper explicitly reports NO association with clinical COPD/asthma diagnosis (only a spirometric FEV1/FVC effect), its mouse data are directly contradictory depending on the injury model (smoke-resistant but infection-susceptible), and a large Chinese-biobank Mendelian-randomization analysis (PMID 42020159) found genetically-instrumented alcohol consumption — the most obvious causal pathway linking ALDH2 to disease — does NOT cause COPD. The correct reading is that ALDH2 has a real, smoking-conditional effect on spirometric lung function (satisfying this pipeline's prior #2, concordance with FEV1/FEV1-FVC), but the mechanism connecting that to a COPD case-control GWAS hit remains unresolved between smoking-behavior mediation, direct pulmonary aldehyde-detoxification biology (independent of alcohol), and residual confounding — not a clean, single-mechanism causal story.

The runner-up field is unusually crowded for a locus this size: SH2B3 (Open Targets L2G 0.877, second-highest at the locus, extensive but entirely non-pulmonary immune/hematopoietic literature) and CUX2 (PoPS-prioritized, second-highest single-variant PIP, and the most consistent top-L2G gene across five independent Open Targets credible sets, yet with zero corroborating literature of any kind beyond unrelated cortical-neuron biology) are both genuine alternative candidates that the current evidence cannot rule out. HECTD4, PTPN11, and RPL6 share one unresolved credible set; TRAFD1 and NAA25 share another; ACAD10, BRAP, and ALDH2 itself share a third. Given this degree of unresolved LD/shared-credible-set ambiguity across the locus, and given that ALDH2's own literature actively complicates rather than simplifies a direct-causation story, confidence is capped at MODERATE rather than HIGH. What would resolve this: COPD-specific (not proxy-trait) colocalization or fine-mapping that separates ALDH2's signal from SH2B3/CUX2/BRAP/ACAD10 at base-pair resolution; a smoking- stratified COPD GWAS or gene-by-smoking interaction analysis at this exact locus (to test whether the residual COPD association survives smoking adjustment, as it did not for alcohol in the Zeng MR study); and any dedicated CUX2 lung-biology functional study, given its statistical consistency remains otherwise unexplained.
