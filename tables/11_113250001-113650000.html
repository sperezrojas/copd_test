Prioritized gene

DRD2 — confidence: LOW.

DRD2 is the best-supported single candidate at this locus, but only narrowly, and the case is best read as "a smoking-behavior-mediated signal that may or may not specifically implicate DRD2 itself," not as direct pulmonary-biology evidence for DRD2. The reasoning:

  1. Statistical evidence does not distinguish DRD2 from TMPRSS5. L2G scores from Open Targets are essentially tied between the two genes at both COPD-labeled credible sets (DRD2 ≈0.49–0.49 vs. TMPRSS5 ≈0.50–0.43), and there is zero molecular-QTL colocalization evidence for either gene at this locus (a data gap, not informative either way). Positional proximity (credible set ~91 kb from DRD2's TSS vs. ~110–120 kb from TMPRSS5's) is a weak nearest-gene heuristic that red-team explicitly warns should not be allowed to break this statistical tie on its own.

  2. Neither gene has genome-wide spirometric (FEV1/FEV1-FVC) concordance — a real tested-negative per the companion Shrine et al. 2019 lung-function GWAS and Open Targets, which under biological prior #2 argues against a straightforward direct- pulmonary-biology mechanism for either gene at this specific locus.

  3. The locus's COPD signal colocalizes strongly and broadly with smoking-behavior and neuropsychiatric/addiction GWAS traits (cigarettes/day, smoking initiation, alcohol/ cannabis use disorder, addiction risk factors, neuroticism, anxiety) but not depression — directly consistent with biological prior #1 (smoking-mediated effects). DRD2 is the classical dopaminergic reward-pathway gene, giving this pattern a plausible (if unproven at this specific credible set) mechanistic story that TMPRSS5 entirely lacks.

  4. TMPRSS5 has a documented (if dated) expression pattern arguing against lung relevance — four independent studies (1 human, 2 mouse, 1 review) consistently describe spinesin/TMPRSS5 as CNS/spinal-cord-restricted, with the founding 2001 paper explicitly testing lung tissue by Northern blot and finding no signal. Combined with a LOEUF of 1.319 (LoF-tolerant) and its position ~37 kb outside the nominal locus boundary, TMPRSS5's case is weak-to-mildly-disconfirmed rather than merely under-studied.

  5. However, DRD2's own case is substantially weakened by two things the evidence table above documents directly: (a) the extensive DRD2/addiction literature is genuinely mixed, with about as many null/contradicting results (PMID 26416825, 21806388, 18690117, 15511716) as positive ones, and its strongest positive result (PMID 26624925, the Taq1A meta-analysis) concerns a variant >165 kb from this locus's actual credible set; and (b) two independent studies that directly compared DRD2 to its neighbor ANKK1 (PMID 18354387, 21663922) found ANKK1 — not DRD2 — carries the more robust, functionally plausible (missense) association signal in this gene cluster, explicitly concluding DRD2 itself is unlikely to be the causal gene in the classic addiction association. ANKK1 was not a candidate gene surfaced by this locus's own prioritization pipeline (nearest gene / MAGMA / PoPS / FLAMES / ABC), so it cannot be formally prioritized here, but this caveat means confidence in DRD2 specifically — as opposed to "something in the DRD2/ANKK1/ TTC12/NCAM1 cluster acting on smoking behavior" — should be low.

Runner-up: TMPRSS5, supported only by a tied L2G score and MAGMA prioritization, with no corroborating literature found and one line of (dated) evidence arguing against lung expression.

What would resolve this tie: (a) a lung- or airway-epithelium eQTL/sQTL panel colocalized against this locus's COPD credible set (currently absent from Open Targets for both genes); (b) a smoking-stratified or Mendelian-randomization analysis testing whether this locus's COPD association persists after conditioning on smoking behavior/heaviness; (c) confirmation of whether this 11q23 region is actually reported as genome-wide significant in the primary COPD GWAS (Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019) — flagged by red-team as unconfirmed and foundational; (d) modern single-cell/GTEx-based lung expression data for TMPRSS5, since the existing evidence is Northern-blot-based and two decades old.

This is the study team's evidence to weigh, not a final call — the above is the best- supported recommendation this pipeline can offer given the verified literature and structured database evidence assembled to date.
