Prioritized gene

WNT2 — confidence LOW.

WNT2 is the best-supported candidate at this locus, but the call is not a confident one, and the study team should weigh it accordingly. The case for WNT2 rests on two legs, both of which the skeptic critique weakened rather than eliminated:

  1. Developmental/mechanistic biology (strong but indirect). Fourteen VERIFIED citations from multiple independent labs across two decades converge on WNT2/Wnt2b as necessary and sufficient for lung endoderm specification, branching morphogenesis, airway smooth muscle differentiation, and maintenance of the adult alveolar fibroblast/AT2 progenitor niche (rows 5-18 above). This is genuine, reproducible mouse-genetics biology. However, as the red-team-skeptic noted, complete embryonic Wnt2/Wnt2b loss (lung agenesis) is a categorically different biological regime from what a common noncoding regulatory variant plausibly does in adult COPD risk, and the single most COPD-specific claim in the dossier — "deficient canonical Wnt responsiveness in COPD" — comes from one 2026 review (Ren et al., PMID 42719019), not primary human COPD tissue data, and sits in direct tension with a VERIFIED primary finding that WNT2 is upregulated in IPF (Kaya et al., PMID 41822191) and in scleroderma-associated pulmonary hypertension (Koçak et al., PMID 32996251) — i.e., the pathway's direction of effect in human chronic lung disease is not consistently "deficient," undercutting mechanistic coherence for COPD specifically.

  2. Locus-specific statistical genetics (modest, unconfirmed by primary literature). Open Targets' L2G analysis names WNT2 as the sole gene at this locus (L2G 0.39-0.41) across three COPD GWAS study loci — but this is a structured-database result, not a verified literature citation, the three "colocalizing" study loci are almost certainly the same underlying signal observed in overlapping cohorts rather than independent replications, and there is no supporting molecular-QTL (eQTL/pQTL) colocalization. Critically, none of the four verified primary GWAS papers cited (Shrine 2019, Sakornsakolpat 2019, Wain 2015, Wain 2017) could be confirmed — even by the citation-verifier — to name WNT2 or 7q31.3 among their reported signals from abstract/metadata alone, and the lead credible-set variant itself (PIP=0.987) is an upstream_gene_variant with no gene assignment. This is the textbook profile of a nearest-gene call that has not been mechanistically resolved.

Why this isn't a clean call: CTTNBP2, the PoPS-prioritized alternative, is fairly characterized as CONTESTED as a direct pulmonary-biology gene — it has zero L2G support at this locus, zero recorded COPD association in Open Targets' aggregate data, and its entire verified literature is neurodevelopmental/ASD biology with no plausible lung mechanism. CTTNBP2 is not a serious competing causal candidate for direct pulmonary pathophysiology. However, per the red-team-skeptic, CTTNBP2's own aggregate disease-association profile (dominated by smoking initiation, smoking cessation, nicotine dependence, and psychiatric traits) raises a distinct, unresolved possibility: that this locus's COPD GWAS signal (uncorrected for smoking, per this pipeline's biological priors) is substantially smoking-mediated at the locus level, independent of which physical gene is the operative effector. If so, WNT2's strong lung-developmental biology, while real, could be biologically true yet causally irrelevant to why this specific SNP appears in the COPD GWAS. CFTR, which sits physically within this region (though ~210 kb from the lead variant) and has well-established obstructive/bronchiectatic lung biology, was outside this review's formal candidate-gene scope (not nearest/FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS/ABC-prioritized) and was not independently evaluated — its distance from the PIP-0.987 lead variant argues against it, but this was asserted rather than demonstrated against the actual credible-set LD span.

What would resolve this: (a) direct confirmation from the Shrine 2019 / Sakornsakolpat 2019 / Wain 2017 supplementary tables of whether WNT2/7q31.3 is actually named as a lung-function/COPD signal; (b) eQTL or pQTL colocalization data for WNT2 (or CFTR, or ST7) specifically at this credible set, which Open Targets did not return; (c) a dedicated search of the smoking-initiation/cessation GWAS literature (e.g., GSCAN consortium) for a signal at this exact position, to test the smoking-mediation hypothesis directly rather than infer it from aggregate association scores; (d) the actual physical span of the credible set relative to CFTR and ST7, to rule those genes in or out on LD grounds rather than distance alone.

Given all of this, WNT2 is named as the best-supported single candidate, but confidence is LOW, not MODERATE — the mechanistic case is strong in isolation but not yet tied to this locus by any verified primary-literature GWAS text, and an unresolved smoking-mediation alternative for the locus as a whole has not been excluded.
