Prioritized gene

DBH — confidence: LOW.

This is a close call between DBH and FAM163B, and the pipeline's own statistical evidence and the independent mechanistic literature point in different directions — that tension, not a clean win, is the honest summary of this locus.

By locus-to-gene (L2G) score, the credible-set/positional evidence favors FAM163B: it is the nearest gene, hosts 7 of 9 credible-set variants, and is the clear L2G leader in Open Targets' own analysis of the adjacent COPD credible set (0.70/0.68 vs. DBH's 0.06-0.09). But the red-team-skeptic marked FAM163B CONTESTED, and I agree with that call: the only dedicated functional characterization of FAM163B (PMID 40999800, VERIFIED) describes a brain-restricted neuronal membrane protein with no lung, airway, or immune connection, and its other two citations (skeletal-muscle co-expression, PMID 31959871; a computational breast-cancer marker, PMID 37761960) are equally non-pulmonary. FAM163B's aggregated Open Targets COPD score is almost entirely genetic/positional (europepmc score 0.012) — there is essentially no independent literature bridging the statistical signal to a plausible lung mechanism.

DBH, by contrast, has a comparatively weak L2G/positional case (it is the minority gene at the same credible sets) but a genuinely compelling, independently-assembled mechanistic case that ties directly to this locus's own lead variant: rs3025343 is itself DBH's well-replicated smoking-cessation SNP (PMIDs 20418890, 28371857, 24667010, 39673390, all VERIFIED), and this same variant is nominally associated with smoking cessation specifically within a COPD patient population (PMID 21685187, VERIFIED) — directly instantiating this review's prior #1 (smoking-mediated signal) at this exact locus. There is also one independent, genome-wide-significant association between the DBH locus and post-bronchodilator FEV1 in smoker cohorts (PMID 26634245, VERIFIED), which is suggestive of prior #2 (a lung-function-specific component) though it does not rule out residual smoking-dose confounding and has not been replicated. The red-team-skeptic marked DBH WEAKENED, not CONTESTED — chiefly for overstating "best genetically supported gene in cluster" against the L2G picture — but characterized it as "the more interesting candidate" with a mechanism that is real and lead-variant-specific.

Runner-up: FAM163B (statistically/positionally favored, but its L2G lead is undermined by a credible mechanistic alternative at the same lead variant, and its own functional literature argues against a lung role). ABL1 is explicitly deprioritized: it sits ~2.7-3.0 Mb outside the locus interval, has zero GWAS/L2G genetic evidence for COPD in Open Targets, and its only mechanistic support comes from nonspecific multi-kinase-inhibitor pulmonary-fibrosis models (not COPD/emphysema biology) plus a directional tension (pharmacologically inhibiting ABL1 causes drug-induced interstitial lung disease). The skeptic rated ABL1 "WEAKENED, bordering on exclusion" and recommended treating it as a low-confidence, likely-noise candidate rather than a co-equal contender.

What would resolve this tie: (1) a formal statistical fine-mapping/colocalization analysis testing whether rs3025343 (or its correlated credible-set variants) acts as a cis-eQTL/enhancer for DBH expression/activity specifically (versus FAM163B) in a relevant tissue (adrenal medulla, sympathetic neurons, or — if a lung mechanism is to be tested — airway/lung tissue, where neither gene currently has QTL data in Open Targets); (2) a mediation analysis partitioning this locus's COPD association through a smoking-behavior GWAS (initiation/cessation/heaviness) versus a lung-function GWAS (FEV1, FEV1/FVC) adjusted for smoking, to determine whether the DBH mechanism is necessary and sufficient or whether an independent lung-structural effect remains; and (3) replication of the Lutz et al. 2015 FEV1 signal in an independent smoker cohort. Given the genuine, unresolved tension between L2G/positional evidence (FAM163B) and mechanistic/smoking-behavior evidence tied to the exact lead variant (DBH), confidence in DBH as the top pick is LOW, and the study team should weigh this call carefully rather than treat it as settled.
