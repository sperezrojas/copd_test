Prioritized gene

FOXP2 — confidence: LOW.

FOXP2 is named as the best-supported single candidate at this locus, but with low confidence and an important caveat: the strongest evidence favors FOXP2 as the correct gene assignment at this locus (it is the sole protein-coding gene overlapping all 29 credible-set variants, including the lead variant rs10249234 itself; Open Targets' own L2G model independently and consistently favors FOXP2 over MDFIC by roughly an order of magnitude across dozens of GWAS traits sharing this variant; and FOXP2 is highly LoF-constrained, consistent with dosage-sensitive biological importance). MDFIC, by contrast, was marked CONTESTED by the red-team-skeptic as the causal gene at this locus: it lies ~228 kb outside the credible-set interval with no ABC/regulatory link, has no COPD genetic-association evidence in Open Targets, and its own L2G score at the lead variant is ~15x lower than FOXP2's — an internal inconsistency that undercuts the FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS prioritization signal that elevated it in this pipeline's own upstream analysis.

However, confidence is capped at LOW because the evidence does not clearly establish why FOXP2 is associated with COPD. The strongest statistical-genetics support for FOXP2 at this exact locus (antisocial-behavior GWAS, rg=0.54 with smoking) and the genome-wide pattern of behavioral/neuropsychiatric GWAS traits sharing this L2G signal both point toward a smoking-initiation/neurobehavioral-mediation pathway (this pipeline's prior #1) rather than direct pulmonary biology. This is actively reinforced by a well-powered (~400K-person) lung-function GWAS explicitly finding zero FOXP2 variants associated with lung function (PMID 36221131), and by three separate COPD GWAS/WGS papers not reporting FOXP2/7q31 as a hit. The one genuine lung-epithelial mechanistic finding for FOXP2 (POT1/AT2 senescence, PMID 40685333) is from an idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis model, not COPD, and should be treated as a plausible-but-unproven extrapolation, not established COPD biology.

Runner-up: MDFIC remains the runner-up only because it is the pipeline's own statistically-prioritized candidate, not because the literature or Open Targets L2G support it — on both of those grounds it is weaker than FOXP2, not closer to it.

What would resolve this / raise confidence: (1) lung-tissue-specific eQTL/pQTL colocalization data directly at this locus for FOXP2 vs. MDFIC (not queried/available in this pass); (2) ABC or other chromatin-interaction evidence testing whether the credible-set interval regulates the distal MDFIC promoter (explicitly flagged by the red-team-skeptic as unresolved, not settled-negative); (3) a smoking-adjusted COPD GWAS at this locus, which would directly test whether the association attenuates after conditioning on smoking behavior — the single most direct test of the smoking-mediation hypothesis raised throughout this evidence base; (4) a COPD- or emphysema-specific (not IPF) functional follow-up of the FOXP2-POT1-AT2 senescence mechanism.
