Prioritized gene

Gene: none — INSUFFICIENT confidence for any single gene.

This locus lacks a credible candidate gene under the evidence gathered. None of the three genes investigated has support that would justify a HIGH, MODERATE, or even a confident LOW call:

  • LOC124903233 (the pipeline's own nearest-gene designation) has zero literature in any domain, an unresolved coding/non-coding annotation conflict, and — critically — its own genomic coordinates fall outside the locus window as defined in the input file. The red-team-skeptic marked this CONTESTED and flagged it as not a viable candidate at all, recommending an upstream QC check of the locus-boundary/nearest-gene assignment rather than treating this as a real biological candidate.
  • LINC02335 has the strongest positional claim (dozens of low-PIP credible-set variants sit in its introns) but zero independent literature of any kind — no expression data, no functional characterization, nothing linking it to lung, immune, or any other biology. The skeptic marked this CONTESTED as well: positional overlap by many low-confidence noncoding variants is not sufficient grounds for candidacy on its own.
  • PCDH17 is the only gene with a substantial literature base, but that literature is exclusively non-pulmonary (oncology across six cancer types, one of which — nasopharyngeal carcinoma, PMID 30863170 — is retracted and excluded; and neurodevelopmental/psychiatric biology via a genuine genome-wide-significant mood-disorder association, PMID 28070120/38747058). It sits >1.3 Mb from the nearest credible-set variant, and its inclusion as a candidate reflects only that it is the sole conventional protein-coding gene inside an arbitrarily wide 3.1 Mb window — not independent locus-to-gene evidence (no colocalization, no L2G score, no fine-mapped coding/regulatory variant). The one immune-adjacent finding (PCDH17 methylation as an RA biomarker, PMID 40410297) is a single peripheral-blood association study in a non-COPD autoimmune disease and was assessed by the skeptic as noise rather than meaningful immune evidence. The skeptic's verdict was WEAKENED, bordering CONTESTED.
  • No spirometry, FEV1/FEV1-FVC, or other pulmonary-function evidence was found for any of the three genes — the single strongest form of evidence this pipeline's priors call for is entirely absent here.
  • No verified smoking-behavior evidence exists either: the only candidate link (a UK Biobank dietary-trait GWAS association for the lead variant itself, PMID 32066663) failed citation verification (CLAIM_MISMATCH) and is excluded. The lead variant rs9316807 therefore has zero verified external phenotype association in this review.
  • Open Targets independently corroborates this picture: zero COPD disease-association evidence for all three genes.

If forced to name a single best-supported candidate for the study team's consideration despite this, PCDH17 would be the (weak) pick — it is the only gene with any literature at all and is under strong evolutionary constraint (LoF oe=0.295) — but this pick is not well supported: it requires accepting an >1.3 Mb positional gap and relying on non-pulmonary (neurodevelopmental/oncology) biology with no established or even plausible-and-evidenced bridge to airflow obstruction or COPD-relevant inflammation. LINC02335 is the runner-up by physical proximity to the credible set alone, with no literature to weigh against that proximity.

What would resolve this: lung-tissue eQTL or promoter-capture Hi-C/chromatin-interaction data connecting credible-set variants to LINC02335, PCDH17, or any other gene; a colocalization analysis against lung-function (FEV1/FEV1-FVC) GWAS; or resolution of the LOC124903233 boundary/annotation discrepancy upstream. Absent that, this locus should be reported to the study team as not resolved to a single credible candidate gene, rather than assigned to PCDH17 or any other gene by default.

Confidence: INSUFFICIENT.
