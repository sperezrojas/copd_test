Prioritized gene

IL18R1 — confidence LOW.

This call is close, and the pipeline's standing instruction against manufacturing false certainty applies directly here. Both IL1RL1 and IL18R1 have substantive, VERIFIED evidence, and neither has evidence that cleanly resolves the central open question at this locus: whether the COPD genetic signal is COPD-specific pulmonary biology or a reflection of shared asthma/Th2 pleiotropy (the one piece of direct statistical evidence bearing on this, Bu & Li 2025's colocalization finding, names IL1RL1, IL18R1, AND IL18RAP together in the same colocalized COPD-allergy hotspot — it does not distinguish among them, and if anything argues for shared/pleiotropic signal rather than COPD-specificity for any of them, per the red-team-skeptic's critique above).

Why IL18R1 over IL1RL1, on balance: (1) IL18R1 carries the single highest Open Targets GWAS-credible-set (L2G-type) score at the entire locus (0.833, vs IL1RL1's best row of 0.772); (2) IL18R1's strongest piece of literature evidence (Singh et al. 2011, ECLIPSE cohort, PMID 21441172) is a genuinely COPD-specific, disease-severity/emphysema-graded, cell-type-resolved human finding (alveolar-macrophage IL-18R protein 23.2% in COPD vs ~2-2.5% in controls) in an ex-smoker cohort with no asthma-comorbidity framing — the single cleanest piece of COPD-specific (not pathway-generic, not purely observational-biomarker, not mouse-model) evidence for any gene at this locus. By contrast, IL1RL1's larger literature base is predominantly pathway-level mechanistic/observational evidence (mouse models, human histology/biomarker correlation) that the skeptic correctly notes does not bear on locus-specific causal attribution, plus mixed/largely null astegolimab trial data that should not be read as validating a COPD-specific mechanism, plus the field's oldest and strongest asthma-genetics pedigree (a point against COPD-specificity, not for it).

Why confidence is LOW, not MODERATE or HIGH: (a) the "neutrophilic IL18R1 vs Th2 IL1RL1" distinction that would most cleanly justify preferring IL18R1 is itself WEAKENED by the skeptic — Sawada et al. 2013 (filed under IL18R1) shows IL-18-axis signaling drives Th2/CD4/ IL-13 airway hyperresponsiveness in an asthma model, and Cayrol & Girard 2022 note ST2 is also expressed on neutrophils, so the clean pathway-based gene-splitting narrative is more heuristic than evidenced; (b) no colocalization analysis restricted to IL18R1 alone (as opposed to the whole 2q12 block) was found, so IL18R1's L2G score and the Bu & Li hotspot cannot on their own distinguish it from IL1RL1 or IL18RAP as the specific causal gene; (c) the red-team-skeptic's own search-budget exhaustion means this locus did not receive an independent contradiction- search pass, so this pick should be treated as provisional pending that additional pass; (d) no study was found that directly tests smoking-status interaction or ACO-exclusion-specific effect sizes at this exact locus.

Runner-up: IL1RL1 (nearest/FLAMES-prioritized gene, essentially tied on the merits — second-highest L2G score, largest total literature base, hosts the lead variant and the overwhelming majority of credible-set PIP mass). What would resolve the tie: a colocalization or fine-mapping analysis performed separately for IL18R1 vs IL1RL1 (not the whole 2q12 block) against both COPD and asthma/allergic-disease GWAS; a COPD GWAS that explicitly excludes ACO/self-reported-asthma cases from its primary case definition and reports this locus's effect size and significance; and a direct locus-specific FEV1/FEV1-FVC effect size (from Shrine et al. 2019 or a successor study) for both genes' credible-set variants. IL18RAP and SLC9A4 are not competitive at this locus (low Open Targets scores, minimal literature, and for SLC9A4 no plausible mechanistic connection to lung/immune biology). MAP4K4 is explicitly de-prioritized: despite being the MAGMA/PoPS statistical pick, it has zero GWAS credible-set evidence, no credible-set variant annotated to it, and the red-team-skeptic flagged its inclusion as CONTESTED in the sense that it looks like a statistical-genetics prioritization artifact (MAGMA/PoPS aggregating locus-wide signal or pathway similarity) rather than a gene with independent evidentiary support — its functional literature, while real, is drawn from sepsis/LPS, adipose/metabolic, air-pollution-particulate, and acute-lung-injury contexts, none of which is a chronic cigarette-smoke/COPD model.

The study team should weigh this LOW-confidence call accordingly: this locus is best described currently as "IL18R1 and IL1RL1 both plausible, genuinely tied pending further locus-resolution work," not as a resolved single-gene call.
