Prioritized gene

Gene: NRXN3 — Confidence: MODERATE

NRXN3 is the only candidate gene at this locus by every internal method (nearest gene, FLAMES, MAGMA, PoPS) and structurally the only plausible gene — the credible set sits entirely within NRXN3's genomic footprint, and Open Targets independently confirms NRXN3 as the sole L2G-predicted target across all 30 GWAS credible sets that overlap this position, including the COPD study itself (L2G = 0.556). There is no LD/paralog ambiguity to resolve here: whichever biological process this locus operates through, NRXN3 is the gene it operates through. On gene attribution alone, confidence would be HIGH.

Confidence is capped at MODERATE, not HIGH, because the evidence base overwhelmingly supports a smoking-behavior and/or adiposity-mediated mechanism rather than a direct pulmonary or immune mechanism for the COPD GWAS signal at this locus — a materially different and weaker claim about COPD causation than "NRXN3 acts in the lung." Specifically: (1) all 18 VERIFIED literature citations describe NRXN3's role in smoking initiation/heaviness/cessation, alcohol/opioid/nicotine/cocaine dependence, impulsivity, and obesity/central-adiposity GWAS, or its canonical purely-neuronal presynaptic cell-adhesion biology (Südhof reviews); (2) the researcher found zero direct lung/airway/epithelial literature and zero immune/inflammatory literature for NRXN3, reported as a genuine gap rather than papered over; (3) Open Targets shows no colocalization with FEV1/FEV1-FVC across 30 examined credible sets, in direct tension with biological prior #2; (4) one MR study (PMID 40869120, VERIFIED) links an NRXN3 obesity-associated variant to COPD/asthma risk specifically via a body-composition-mediated pathway, not a lung mechanism; and (5) the red-team-skeptic's determination for this gene was WEAKENED (not CONTESTED — gene attribution is not in dispute) — specifically weakened with respect to any claim of direct pulmonary/immune causation, while assessing the smoking/behavioral-mediation evidence as strong and internally consistent. A prior large COPD GWAS (Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019, PMID 30804561) additionally does not report this 14q24/NRXN3 locus among its 82 genome-wide-significant COPD loci (CLAIM_MISMATCH on the researcher's use of that citation — see audit appendix), which is mild non-replication evidence and tempers confidence further. Whether NRXN3/14q24 appears among the 559 genes in the largest lung-function GWAS to date (Shrine et al. 2023, PMID 36914875) remains an open, unresolved question (abstract-level review could not settle it) and is the single highest-value follow-up that could shift this call in either direction.

Bottom line for the study team: NRXN3 is very likely the correct gene assignment at this locus, and this is very likely a genuine COPD GWAS signal reflecting NRXN3's well-established role in smoking-behavior and/or adiposity biology — exactly the kind of smoking-mediated signal anticipated by biological prior #1, given that the underlying COPD GWAS summary statistics are not adjusted for smoking. It should not be interpreted as evidence of a direct pulmonary structural or inflammatory role for NRXN3 unless and until direct lung-tissue or airway-specific evidence emerges.
