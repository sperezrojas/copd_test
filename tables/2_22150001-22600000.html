Evidence Table — Locus 2:22150001-22600000 (gene desert)

Region: GRCh38 chr2:22,150,001-22,600,000. Lead variant: rs7599976. Nearest gene (internal pipeline): intergenic. FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS/ABC prioritization: all NA — no computational signal at this locus. Credible set: 49 variants (ABF fine-mapping, max PIP ~0.044, none individually strong — a poorly-resolved, unconcentrated signal), all intronic/non-coding-transcript variants annotated to a single lncRNA, LINC01830. No missense or splice-site credible-set variants exist at this locus.

This is a genuine gene desert: an independent Ensembl gene-overlap query for this exact region found only non-coding content overlapping the locus (LINC01830, LINC03116, LINC01884, an unannotated lncRNA transcript, MT-CYB/MDM2 processed pseudogenes, an RNA5SP87 pseudogene) — no protein-coding gene overlaps the locus itself. The nearest protein-coding genes, APOB/TDRD15 (~1.0-1.2 Mb upstream) and KLHL29 (~1.0 Mb downstream), are both more than 1 Mb from every credible-set variant.

Prioritized gene

Prioritized gene: none (INSUFFICIENT confidence — this locus has no strong candidate).

No candidate gene at this locus has both a plausible mechanism and direct genetic evidence for COPD. The evidence table above and the Open Targets summary converge on the same conclusion from independent angles:

  • The only gene physically overlapping the credible set, LINC01830, has no literature characterization whatsoever (zero PubMed hits for the gene or the lead variant) and essentially no Open Targets disease-association data. The red-team-skeptic additionally flagged that describing LINC01830 as "the gene in the credible set" overstates the fine-mapping support: with 49 variants and a max PIP of ~0.044, this is a flat, statistically unresolved signal that happens to fall inside LINC01830 only because it is the sole annotated feature spanning this part of the gene desert — not because fine-mapping concentrated probability on this transcript. This is a WEAKENED, not a supported, candidate.
  • APOB, the nearest upstream protein-coding gene (~1.0-1.2 Mb away — a long reach for any cis-regulatory claim, with no coloc/eQTL evidence offered to bridge that distance), attracted the most literature in this search, but the red-team-skeptic correctly identified this as a "candidate-gene magnet" effect: APOB is a famous, heavily-studied lipid gene, so any locus search surfaces abundant APOB-lipid-lung literature regardless of relevance. The two studies that actually test APOB's own causal role in COPD via Mendelian randomization (Chen et al. 2026, PMID 42152286; Huang et al. 2025, PMID 39802042) are BOTH null for APOB specifically, with ApoA-I (a different gene/protein) carrying the protective signal instead. The remaining APOB citations are observational/non-causal or test lipid-pathway MR broadly, not APOB's own causal role, and none reference this locus, its credible-set variants, or LINC01830. Per the skeptic, this body of evidence on balance argues against, rather than for, APOB as the causal gene at this locus. CONTESTED.
  • TDRD15 and KLHL29, the nearest downstream/upstream flanking protein-coding genes, have no COPD/lung-function evidence in any domain and no plausible mechanism (TDRD15 is a germline/testis-restricted piRNA-pathway gene; KLHL29 has no established lung-relevant function and appears only in unrelated GWAS/TWAS studies of other traits). Both HOLD as implausible candidates.
  • The internal computational pipeline itself assigns no signal here (FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS/ABC all NA), and Open Targets' own L2G statistical model independently declines to assign a gene at this exact variant across every GWAS trait it is recorded for — two independent statistical approaches agreeing that this locus does not resolve to a single gene.
  • A specific alternative hypothesis worth naming explicitly (per the red-team-skeptic, who considered it under-weighted rather than over-weighted in the initial write-up): the lead variant is recorded by Open Targets as a credible-set member for a cluster of psychiatric/behavioral GWAS traits (depression, bipolar disorder, trauma exposure, risk-taking behavior) and for hypertension and body-composition traits, with zero recorded association to any lung-function or spirometric trait. Given prior #1 (COPD GWAS signals may reflect smoking-initiation/nicotine-dependence neurobiology rather than pulmonary biology) and prior #2 (absence of spirometric concordance is a warning sign against pulmonary mechanism), this pattern is consistent with — though not proven to be — a neurobehavioral/smoking-behavior-mediated mechanism rather than direct pulmonary biology. This hypothesis could NOT be corroborated in primary literature in this search (GWAS Catalog REST lookups failed; PubMed searches for these trait combinations at this exact locus were unproductive), so it is reported as an unresolved, database-sourced lead for the study team's own follow-up (e.g., checking the specific GCST accessions directly against GWAS Catalog), not as a demonstrated finding.

What would resolve this: a lung-tissue eQTL/colocalization lookup for LINC01830 and the surrounding desert region (flagged by the skeptic as a gap — not attempted here beyond the truncated Open Targets colocalisation query), independent confirmation of the psychiatric/behavioral-trait credible-set overlap against GWAS Catalog directly, or new COPD/lung-function-specific fine-mapping data with a more concentrated credible set. Until one of those is available, this locus should be reported to the study team as "no defensible single candidate gene," not forced into a pick.
