Prioritized-gene synthesis

Prioritized gene: MFHAS1. Confidence: LOW.

This locus does not have a single well-supported causal gene; the call is close among at least three candidates (MFHAS1, PINX1, PRAG1), and this pick should be weighed accordingly by the study team.

MFHAS1 has the second-highest Open Targets COPD L2G score at this locus (0.768, behind only PRAG1's 0.803), and — unlike PRAG1 — its spirometry concordance is smoking-independent: strong FEV1 (up to 0.759) and FEV1/FVC (0.642–0.666) L2G scores with no smoking-trait L2G evidence at all, the cleanest fit to biological prior #2 (pulmonary-function concordance without a smoking confound) of any gene with meaningful COPD L2G support at this locus. It also carries the second-largest credible-set variant share at the locus and is genetically LoF-constrained (oe=0.67), consistent with some dosage sensitivity. Its mechanistic literature (PMIDs 20616063, 26599367, 28471450, all VERIFIED) establishes a specific, non-generic role regulating TLR2/TLR-family innate immune signaling and M1/M2 macrophage polarization — directly relevant to the macrophage-centric inflammatory programs described in biological prior #3 — though, per the red-team critique, none of these three papers are lung-tissue experiments (they are sepsis/systemic monocyte-macrophage models), so this mechanism is inferred by extrapolation, not demonstrated in airway/alveolar tissue.

Why this is close, and the runner-up: PINX1 is a strong alternative pick. It has the single highest FEV1 L2G score of any gene at this locus (0.864) and — unlike MFHAS1 — actual lung-tissue experimental literature: a rat acute-lung-injury model (PMID 33819185) and airway-smooth-muscle NF-κB/chemokine biology (PMID 29305433), plus a COPD lung-transcriptomic hub-gene finding (PMID 39798034, correlational, not causal). PINX1's case is weakened by having no direct COPD L2G evidence (only the correlational transcriptomic citation) and by one supporting citation (PMID 28372542) being confounded by smoking status. PRAG1 has the single highest raw COPD L2G score (0.803) but was downgraded by red-team critique because its Open Targets trait profile is more consistent with smoking-behavior mediation than direct pulmonary biology (cigarettes-per-day L2G 0.886 exceeds its COPD score), and it has zero lung-specific mechanistic literature of any kind — its supporting citations describe generic, non-lung PEAK-family scaffold signaling.

What would resolve the tie: (1) a primary spirometry or COPD GWAS/fine-mapping paper with formal colocalization naming one of these three genes specifically (none was found by either the researcher or the red-team-skeptic); (2) lung- or airway-tissue-specific functional validation of MFHAS1's TLR2/macrophage mechanism, which currently rests entirely on sepsis/systemic-inflammation models; (3) a formal test of whether smoking initiation/heaviness statistically mediates the PRAG1 and XKR6 COPD associations, to either confirm or rule out prior #1's smoking-mediation alternative for those two genes; (4) clarification of whether this unusually wide (2.9 Mb) credible set genuinely represents one signal or (per both the researcher and skeptic) partially merges a distinct, adjacent MSRA-centered signal ~1.7-2.1 Mb from the lead variant — MSRA has the single most on-target COPD-pathway-specific mechanistic literature at the whole locus (the classic alpha-1-antitrypsin/protease-antiprotease pathway, PMIDs 34674978 and 6600900) but was excluded from consideration as this locus's causal gene on positional/statistical grounds, not mechanistic weakness.

RP1L1 (carrier of the locus's only missense credible-set variants) is explicitly not recommended despite its coding variants: red-team-skeptic marked it CONTESTED, citing gnomAD constraint metrics arguing against dosage sensitivity (LoF oe=1.63, missense oe=1.53, both indicating tolerance), a retina-restricted, mechanistically unrelated known disease biology (occult macular dystrophy), zero lung/COPD literature, and credible-set PIPs for both missense variants several orders of magnitude below the lead variant (0.413) — see audit appendix. XKR6, TNKS, CLDN23, SOX7, and MSRA are documented above but not prioritized: XKR6 leans smoking-mediated with no COPD L2G; TNKS has strong smoking-independent spirometry concordance but zero lung-specific literature and no COPD L2G; CLDN23 and SOX7 have weak-to-null evidence across the board; MSRA is flagged as a likely distinct, adjacent signal rather than this locus's causal gene.
