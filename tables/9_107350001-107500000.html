Prioritized gene

Candidate: RAD23B. Confidence: LOW.

RAD23B is nominally the best-supported candidate gene among this locus's two official internal-pipeline candidates (RAD23B, OR13C5), but the case for it is weak and explicitly contested, and this call is close.

Reasoning: OR13C5 can be confidently deprioritized — it has no L2G support, no colocalization evidence, is essentially LoF-unconstrained (consistent with typical olfactory-receptor-gene biology), and its only literature hit (Salas et al. 2017, PMID 29162850) concerns a different variant, a different age group (infants), and a different disease (acute RSV bronchiolitis) than COPD. Both red-team-skeptic passes independently concur OR13C5 is "WEAKENED... bordering on effectively non-viable."

That leaves RAD23B as the only remaining official candidate, but the evidence for RAD23B itself is genuinely thin and does not clearly distinguish it from its physical neighbor KLF4 (out of scope as an official candidate at this locus, since it was not flagged by nearest-gene/FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS/ABC in the internal pipeline, but relevant as the alternative causal-gene hypothesis raised by Open Targets' own L2G analysis and by the red-team-skeptic). Specifically:

  • Open Targets' L2G score for RAD23B (0.487) versus the competing gene KLF4 (0.317-0.535) flips rank order across the two independent FEV1/FVC GWAS credible sets queried — this is not a clean, gene-resolving signal.
  • Neither of the two primary GWAS papers most likely to underlie this locus's signal (Shrine et al. 2019 lung-function GWAS, PMID 30804560; Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019 COPD GWAS, PMID 30804561) could be confirmed — even after two independent red-team-skeptic search attempts — to actually name this specific 9:107.4Mb region/RAD23B in their own tables. This is the single largest unresolved evidentiary gap at this locus.
  • RAD23B has no COPD-specific, airway-remodeling, or lung-immune literature; its only disease-relevant literature (10 of 15 verified citations) concerns smoking- and alcohol-associated cancer risk (lung, laryngeal, bladder, colorectal, breast, non-Hodgkin lymphoma) via its role in nucleotide excision repair — a genotoxic- carcinogenesis axis, not an airflow-obstruction/emphysema axis.
  • RAD23B's own top Open Targets cross-trait associations are "smoking initiation" and "alcohol drinking," raising Prior 1 (smoking-behavior mediation) as a live, unresolved alternative to direct pulmonary biology — this was not ruled out by any citation found.
  • By contrast, the competing gene KLF4 has an established, disease-relevant mechanistic story in human lung epithelium (Dong et al. 2025, PMID 41075787: KLF4/KLF5 are direct HIF targets promoting basal and secretory cell fates — directly relevant to COPD-associated goblet-cell hyperplasia/airway remodeling), which RAD23B entirely lacks.

Both red-team-skeptic passes flagged RAD23B as CONTESTED/WEAKENED on essentially these same grounds (the RAD23B-vs-KLF4 L2G rank-flip and the unconfirmed locus-attribution in the two primary GWAS papers), and neither pass could rule out smoking-behavior mediation.

What would resolve the tie: (1) direct confirmation from the Shrine 2019 / Sakornsakolpat 2019 supplementary tables (not just abstracts) that this exact 9:107.4Mb region/RAD23B is a reported hit, and whether it survives smoking-adjusted analyses; (2) a COPD-specific (not just FEV1/FVC) fine-mapped credible set at this locus with gene-specific L2G/colocalization; (3) RAD23B- or KLF4-specific eQTL/pQTL colocalization in lung tissue; (4) any functional study directly testing RAD23B (versus KLF4) in airway epithelial differentiation, oxidative-stress response, or emphysema models. Runner-up: KLF4 (not an official candidate per this locus's internal pipeline, but the strongest rival hypothesis on current evidence).

Given the L2G rank-flip, the unresolved locus-attribution gap, the live smoking-mediation alternative, and KLF4's stronger disease-relevant mechanistic story, this recommendation is offered as the best-supported call among the two official candidates, not as a resolved finding — confidence is marked LOW, and the study team should weigh whether KLF4 (despite being outside this locus's original candidate list) warrants separate follow-up.
