Prioritized-gene synthesis

Prioritized gene: ARPP21 — Confidence: LOW

ARPP21 is the only candidate gene at this locus by every prioritization method used (nearest gene, FLAMES, PoPS) and is independently confirmed as the only protein-coding gene in the 250kb window, so it is named here by default of being the sole nominee, not because the evidence affirmatively establishes it as a COPD-causal gene through a defined mechanism. Confidence is LOW (bordering on INSUFFICIENT) for the following reasons, all drawn from the evidence and Open Targets summary above:

  1. The pipeline's own spirometry-concordance prior is contradicted, not just unmet. There is no FEV1/FEV1-FVC association for ARPP21 anywhere in Open Targets or PubMed (see negative findings above). Per the pipeline's stated prior #2, "a COPD association without supporting pulmonary-function evidence warrants consideration of alternative pathways" — this locus is close to a textbook example of that pattern.
  2. The one piece of quantitative evidence linking this locus to COPD (L2G 0.8899, aggregate association 0.329) traces entirely to a single MTAG study combining COPD with GERD — not a COPD-only GWAS — and the citation-verifier independently confirmed that the underlying paper (PMID 40155373) does not name ARPP21 or this locus in its main text. The skeptic's WEAKENED verdict on this point stands: COPD-relevance here is a database pattern, not a confirmed, literature-discussed finding.
  3. The strongest, most specific, locus-level genetic signal at this credible set is colocalization with adiposity/body-fat-distribution traits (waist-hip ratio, waist circumference, abdominal adiposity; h4 up to 0.99 across 9+ independent GWAS), not with any lung trait. No lung eQTL/pQTL/sQTL colocalizes here at all.
  4. ARPP21's own best-characterized biology (RNA-binding protein antagonizing its hosted microRNA, miR-128, during neuronal dendritogenesis; secondary roles in muscle differentiation and several cancers) has no plausible direct mechanistic link to airway/alveolar structure, protease-antiprotease balance, or COPD-relevant immune programs, and Human Protein Atlas data show negligible lung/airway expression.
  5. The red-team-skeptic raised a specific, unresolved alternative causal hypothesis that neither the researcher nor the skeptic could search further this session (WebSearch budget exhausted for both): the credible set could plausibly act on the hosted microRNA MIR128-2 (embedded in an ARPP21 intron) rather than on ARPP21 protein-coding function/dosage — a hypothesis consistent with the recurring "ARPP21-as-miR-128-host" theme across the verified structural-biology citations, and with the absence of any eQTL/pQTL colocalization for ARPP21 itself.

What would resolve this: (a) confirmation (or refutation) of whether PMID 40155373's supplementary data actually discusses ARPP21/this locus; (b) MIR128-2-specific QTL or functional data at this locus to adjudicate ARPP21-vs-miR-128-2 as the operative causal unit; (c) a COPD-only (non-MTAG) GWAS or fine-mapping study independently confirming this locus, ideally with spirometric trait concordance; (d) direct literature search (once available) on ARPP21 and waist-hip-ratio/adiposity GWAS, since the current strongest signal is a database pattern not yet corroborated by primary literature. Given all of the above, this is not a confident lung-biology call — it is the default nomination in a single-candidate-gene locus where the dominant, well-supported genetic signal points toward adiposity/body-composition biology (and possibly GERD-related comorbidity/misclassification) rather than direct pulmonary or smoking-neurobiology pathways. The study team should weigh this locus as a candidate confounded-by-obesity/GERD signal at least as strongly as a candidate direct-COPD-biology signal.
