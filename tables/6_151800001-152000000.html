Lead variant: rs3757323 (6:151,880,872, GRCh38, intron_variant, PIP=0.633). All 11 credible-set variants (cumulative PIP ≈0.98) are intronic within ESR1 itself; no missense/splice-donor/ splice-acceptor variant present. Prioritized gene

ESR1 — confidence: LOW.

ESR1 is the correct gene to prioritize at this locus in the narrow sense that it is the only protein-coding gene physically present in the window and every method that returned a result (nearest gene, FLAMES, PoPS) converges on it; there is no plausible competing gene to weigh against it here (unlike the broader, telomeric 6q25.1 breast-cancer peak, where CCDC170 has been proposed as an alternative effector — CCDC170 lies entirely outside this specific window, per direct Ensembl query, so that alternative-gene concern does not transfer cleanly to this locus). Genetic constraint data (LoF oe=0.296) is consistent with ESR1 being a plausible dosage-sensitive disease gene in general.

However, confidence in a lung-autonomous COPD mechanism for ESR1 at this locus must be rated LOW, not higher, for reasons documented in the evidence table and audit trail: (1) the three largest, best-powered, most directly relevant primary GWAS (Wain 2017, Shrine 2019 lung-function GWAS; Liu et al. 2019 GSCAN smoking-initiation GWAS) were checked by the citation-verifier and confirmed to NOT name this locus/ESR1 among their genome-wide-significant loci; (2) the one direct gene-specific human genetic-association finding (Obeidat et al. 2011) is a nominal, smoking-subgroup-restricted, likely underpowered signal, not a robust genome-wide-significant association; (3) the Open Targets L2G/colocalization pattern — while showing decisively that ESR1 (not a neighbor) is the effector transcript at this SNP across dozens of other GWAS traits — is, per the red-team-skeptic, more consistent with a smoking-behavior/neuropsychiatric-pleiotropy hub (near-1 colocalization with smoking initiation, depression, anxiety, PTSD, educational attainment, reproductive-timing traits) than with a lung-restricted signal, and this smoking-initiation colocalization could not be independently corroborated in the primary GSCAN paper; (4) most of the mechanistic immune literature relevant to inflammatory programs (Th17, M2 macrophage, neutrophilic) is drawn from asthma or particulate/ozone-exposure models, not COPD or cigarette-smoke models directly, and is explicitly extrapolated by hedged review language rather than demonstrated in COPD; and (5) one immune finding (Oh et al. 2021) shows the opposite direction from a simple "ERα drives T-cell inflammation" model in a lung-tumor context, which further complicates a clean mechanistic story.

The two citations that most directly support a genuine lung-autonomous, smoke-conditional mechanism — Tam et al. 2016 and Sathish et al. 2015, both cigarette-smoke mouse/cell models showing sex-dimorphic, ERα-dependent airway remodeling and airway-smooth-muscle dysfunction — are real and hold up under critique, and they are consistent with prior #1's framing that pulmonary biology can be conditional on (rather than independent of, or purely upstream of) smoking exposure. This is the most defensible version of a "lung biology" story for this locus: not that ESR1 acts on COPD risk independently of smoking, but that ERα signaling modulates the pulmonary response to cigarette smoke once smoking has occurred, while a separate (or overlapping) part of the same signal's pleiotropy plausibly acts through smoking-initiation/behavioral pathways upstream of lung exposure altogether.

There is no runner-up gene to name (ESR1 is the only candidate at this locus), so this is not a tie between genes needing resolution — the uncertainty here is entirely about mechanism (smoking-behavior-mediated vs. smoke-conditional-lung-autonomous vs. weak/winner's-curse signal), not about which gene. What would resolve this: (a) direct confirmation of whether this exact locus/SNP appears in the internal COPD GWAS's own smoking-adjusted or smoking-stratified analyses, if available; (b) a look-up of whether rs3757323 or its proxies appear in a more recent (post-2019) GSCAN/smoking-initiation GWAS release, to resolve the unverified Open-Targets-derived smoking-initiation colocalization; (c) direct COPD-model (not asthma/particulate) functional follow-up of ERα in airway epithelium/macrophages under chronic cigarette-smoke exposure. Given the weak/negative direct GWAS evidence and the unresolved smoking-mediation question, LOW confidence is the appropriate rating for the study team to weigh; this is a plausible, biologically motivated pick by elimination (sole gene in window) rather than a strongly evidenced causal-gene call.
