Prioritized gene

Prioritized gene: TMEM18 — Confidence: LOW

TMEM18 is named by every internal prioritization method at this locus (nearest gene, FLAMES, PoPS) and is independently confirmed as the sole protein-coding gene in the region, so there is no competing gene to weigh it against in the usual "tight LD, multiple candidates" sense. The call is nonetheless LOW confidence because the verified evidence does not support TMEM18 as a COPD/lung-biology gene in its own right — it supports TMEM18 as a robust, independent contributor to BMI/adiposity genetics (Open Targets: 127 L2G rows, score up to 0.55; mechanistically validated via hypothalamic PVN appetite circuitry, Larder et al. 2017, PMID 28811369 verified) and a plausible, moderate contributor to smoking-initiation genetics (Open Targets: 7 L2G rows, score 0.42-0.44, independent of the BMI evidence base), with the COPD association at this locus most parsimoniously explained as pleiotropy transmitted through one or both of those pathways rather than through any independent pulmonary mechanism.

Every verified citation directly linking TMEM18 to COPD (Mikkelsen et al. 2025, PMID 40953920; Çolak et al. 2016, PMID 27118560) does so only via a composite BMI allele score in which TMEM18 is one of 3-5 unweighted SNPs, never isolated or tested individually — per the red-team-skeptic's explicit reframing, this is BMI-pathway/instrument-level evidence, not TMEM18-gene-specific evidence, and should not be read as stronger than that. Open Targets shows zero L2G/genetic-association evidence connecting TMEM18 to COPD directly and zero evidence of any kind for FEV1/lung function; no dedicated colocalization, conditional, or genomic-SEM analysis was found that isolates a COPD- or lung-function-specific causal signal at this locus distinct from BMI or smoking. The lead variant rs4423631 itself has no GWAS Catalog trait associations of any kind (not even BMI), and the credible set is broad and flat (max PIP 0.037, 173 variants, entirely noncoding) — an architecture consistent with a large regulatory LD block rather than a well-resolved causal mechanism.

The red-team-skeptic's verdict for TMEM18 is WEAKENED, not CONTESTED: there is no literature actively disputing TMEM18's role or proposing a different causal gene at this locus (it is the sole protein-coding gene here, so there is no runner-up candidate gene to name), but the inference that TMEM18 itself — as opposed to the broader BMI/smoking pathway, which composite instruments cannot attribute to any single SNP — is a COPD-relevant gene overreaches what the gene-specific evidence supports. What would resolve this: a colocalization or fine-mapping analysis testing whether the COPD credible set at this locus shares its causal variant(s) with the BMI and/or smoking-initiation credible sets at the same locus (rather than three independent, uncoordinated GWAS signals that happen to overlap), and/or a single-SNP (not composite-score) MR analysis isolating TMEM18's rs6548238 (or another TMEM18-specific instrument) as the exposure for COPD risk, independent of FTO/MC4R. Absent that, this recommendation should be read as "TMEM18 is the only gene physically present to prioritize, but the mechanism most likely runs through BMI/adiposity and/or smoking-behavior pleiotropy rather than pulmonary biology intrinsic to TMEM18" — a locus-level call under real uncertainty, not a confident gene-level mechanistic finding.
