Prioritized gene

APOE — Confidence: MODERATE.

APOE is the clear better-supported candidate relative to ZNF180: the sole credible-set variant (rs429358, PIP=0.9987) is a missense variant unambiguously located inside APOE itself, which is a direct and strong form of variant-to-gene evidence; ZNF180's candidacy by contrast rests entirely on a PoPS similarity-based score with zero Open Targets association data, zero GWAS-credible-set evidence, and zero lung/COPD/immune literature of any kind (table row, PMID 39054954, is CRC-specific and irrelevant to lung biology). This is not a close call between the two named candidates.

However, confidence in APOE is capped at MODERATE rather than HIGH because red-team-skeptic identified a real, unresolved gap between the strength of the genetic signal (Open Targets L2G-analog resourceScore 0.95, genetic_association datatype score 0.72) and the strength of the mechanistic case for a lung-autonomous causal pathway specifically:

  • No headline COPD or spirometry (FEV1/FEV1-FVC) GWAS reporting rs429358/APOE as a genome-wide-significant hit was located, and no direct evidence of colocalization with an independent lung-function GWAS signal at this exact locus was found or ruled out (search budget exhausted on both the researcher's and skeptic's passes) — per this pipeline's biological prior #2, a COPD association without independently confirmed pulmonary-function concordance warrants weighing alternative pathways, and that check remains open.
  • A population cohort that directly tested this (Taiwan Biobank, PMID 38866261) found the cohort's airflow-limitation signal was carried by a different gene (LEPR), not APOE-rs429358, even though APOE-rs429358 showed a robust signal for metabolic syndrome/fatty liver in the same data.
  • Two live, non-mutually-exclusive alternative explanations remain unruled-out: (1) smoking-behavior mediation (Zhang 2020's rs429358 x smoking-status interaction; Coppens 2021's nicotine-abstinence EEG effect in epsilon4 carriers) and (2) mortality/survivorship-bias confounding in case-control ascertainment, given APOE's well-documented general association with all-cause/cardiovascular mortality and aging-pleiotropy, reinforced locally by serum ApoE protein predicting one-year AECOPD mortality (PMID 40433394) and organ-aging/COPD-onset associations (PMID 40634782).
  • The knockout-mouse emphysema literature that would most directly support a lung-autonomous causal mechanism is thinner than its apparent volume suggests: of ~13 Apoe-/- emphysema papers, 8-9 are from a single industry-funded (Philip Morris International) systems- toxicology program not designed to isolate ApoE's own mechanistic contribution (Apoe-/- used only as a convenient comorbidity host strain), and the two most directly relevant papers outside that program have an internal tension: Cabanski 2015 finds the smoke-susceptible emphysema phenotype is shared across 5 mouse strains via a generic NF-kB/TLR4/TNF-alpha pathway (not uniquely ApoE-dependent), and Moos 2024 finds genetic ApoE deletion had "limited impact on inflammation and mortality" in a fibrosis model. Only Goldklang 2012 and Arunachalam 2010 are both non-industry-funded and directly implicate ApoE loss-of-function in emphysema susceptibility.

Set against this, genuine non-industry, mechanistically direct, lung-autonomous ApoE biology independent of its Alzheimer's/lipid/cardiovascular fame is real and reasonably well supported: ApoE-dependent surfactant lipid secretion and signaling in alveolar type II cells (PMID 8483941, 9316499), a developmental role in fetal surfactant synthesis (PMID 12114192), and marked ApoE enrichment/oxidative-injury protection in alveolar type I cells (PMID 16497717) — these are non-trivial, non-industry, and not obviously reducible to APOE's neuro/lipid/CVD roles.

Net call: APOE is the single best-supported candidate gene at this locus and should be prioritized for follow-up, but this is a case where the genetic signal (strong) and the mechanistic/directional evidence for a lung-specific causal pathway (real but incomplete, and competing with un-excluded smoking-mediation and mortality-confounding explanations) are not yet fully concordant. What would resolve this to HIGH confidence: (1) an explicit colocalization/concordance check of this exact locus against a large independent FEV1/FEV1-FVC GWAS (e.g., SpiroMeta, UK Biobank spirometry) — the single highest-value missing piece; (2) confirmation of whether the COPD-risk allele direction at this locus is epsilon4-concordant (would support the pleiotropy/mortality-confounding explanation) or discordant (would argue against it); (3) a direct Mendelian-randomization test of APOE/rs429358 against smoking-initiation, cigarettes-per-day, or cessation phenotypes in a well-powered GWAS consortium (e.g., GSCAN), which was searched for but not confirmed or ruled out in this review. ZNF180 remains a distant runner-up with essentially no orthogonal support and should not be prioritized ahead of APOE absent new evidence.
