Prioritized Gene

PRMT6 — Confidence: LOW

PRMT6 is named as the best-supported single candidate at this locus, but with low confidence, for the following reasons drawn directly from the evidence table and Open Targets summary above:

  • It is the only candidate gene supplied by the internal pipeline (nearest gene; no FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS/ABC support was available to cross-check), and it sits ~6 kb from the credible-set variants versus NTNG1's ~90 kb — proximity favors PRMT6 if a single nearby gene is being sought.
  • However, the statistical-genetics case for PRMT6 at this specific locus is essentially null: Open Targets has zero GWAS credible sets for this region at all (no L2G, no colocalization, for or against any gene), the credible set itself is entirely non-coding/intergenic-intronic with a modest top PIP (0.27), and there is zero COPD, FEV1, or FEV1/FVC evidence for PRMT6 in Open Targets or the published literature. This is a "nearest-gene by default" call, not a genetically resolved one.
  • The best mechanistic evidence — a coherent, repeated finding that cigarette-smoke exposure downregulates PRMT6 in airway epithelium/lung tissue, that PRMT6 is reduced in human COPD lung parenchyma, and that restoring PRMT6 is protective against CSE-induced inflammation, apoptosis, and emphysema in mouse models — is real (5 verified papers) but comes from a single overlapping-authorship research group and is directly contradicted on direction of effect by an independent group's NSCLC study (PMID 42592683), where CSE instead increases PRMT6. This was assessed by red-team-skeptic as WEAKENED, not confirmed.
  • The one statistical-genetics link to smoking behavior (Rosoff et al. 2024 TWAS/MR) is consistent with prior #1 (a possible smoking-mediated pathway rather than direct pulmonary biology) but is an independent expression-smoking association that does not demonstrably share the same causal variants as this locus's credible set — it should not be read as confirming a smoking-mediated mechanism specifically for this GWAS signal.
  • PRMT6's immune biology is broad but non-lung-specific pleiotropy (macrophage NF-κB amplification in unrelated tissues, HIF-1α/glycolysis in microglia, anti-fibrotic role in liver macrophages), and where COPD-relevant CD8+ T-cell biology is directly examined (gastric cancer), the direction is opposite to what COPD's pathogenic CD8+ T-cell excess would predict — explicitly CONTESTED, per prior #3's call to distinguish specific from nonspecific/contested immune evidence.

Runner-up: NTNG1 (LOW priority) — weaker positionally (90 kb from the credible set) and with no structural-lung or immune-inflammation literature at all; its two verified associations (restless legs syndrome GWAS; small-cell lung cancer MR) are both plausibly unrelated to COPD pathophysiology and were not stretched into a COPD story by the researcher.

What would resolve this call: (1) independent replication (or refutation) of the Xiangya-group PRMT6-CSE-emphysema mechanistic story by an unrelated laboratory; (2) a genuinely replicated genome-wide-significant COPD or spirometry (FEV1/FEV1-FVC) signal at this locus in an external GWAS resource (UK Biobank, SpiroMeta, FinnGen) — the complete absence of any Open Targets credible set for this region is itself unusual and worth independently sanity-checking; (3) lung-specific or constitutive Prmt6-knockout mouse phenotyping to establish a causal (not just correlative/ overexpression-rescue) role in emphysema; (4) any COPD-specific immune-cell (airway neutrophil, lung macrophage, Th1/Th17/Tc17) study of PRMT6, since none currently exists. Given the near-total absence of statistical genetic support and the single-group, directionally-contested mechanistic literature, this locus should be treated as a plausible-but-unresolved candidate rather than a confirmed COPD gene, and the study team should weigh independently whether to prioritize follow-up here.
