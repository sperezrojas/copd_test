Overall assessment

PRMT6 is the best-supported candidate by proximity, but the locus lacks direct genetic resolution and the mechanistic literature is mixed. The evidence is plausible but not definitive, so this should be treated as a low-confidence candidate rather than a confirmed COPD gene.
