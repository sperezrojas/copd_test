Prioritized gene

SLC7A10 — confidence LOW.

This is a close call and the evidence does not cleanly point to a single gene for COPD specifically, so the confidence is set low despite SLC7A10 being an unambiguous statistical winner for a related-but-distinct phenotype cluster.

SLC7A10 is the nearest gene, the sole gene in the PIP=0.99 credible set, and — per the Open Targets structured lookup — is the consistent, strongly-favored locus-to-gene (L2G) target at the exact lead variant across 44 independent fine-mapped GWAS credible sets (L2G ~0.70–0.77 vs. CEBPA's ~0.05–0.06), with near-unity colocalization (h4≈0.998) tying this single signal across the allergic/atopic/eosinophilic/asthma phenotype space. However, as both the researcher and red-team-skeptic emphasize, none of that statistical evidence is COPD-specific: no isolated/pure-COPD GWAS credible set exists at this variant, no FEV1/FEV1-FVC/spirometric concordance was found (a prior this pipeline weighs heavily), and the only COPD-adjacent colocalization is with composite phenotypes that bundle COPD together with asthma/bronchitis/ emphysema (FinnGen "Asthma/COPD," ICD10 J40-J47). The most parsimonious reading, per the skeptic's WEAKENED verdict, is that this is a genuine and robust asthma/allergic/eosinophilic disease locus that appears in COPD GWAS through case-definition overlap with asthma-COPD overlap syndrome (ACO) or comorbid atopic disease in COPD cohorts, rather than through a demonstrated mechanism of airflow obstruction or structural lung damage. A genuine eosinophilic-COPD-endotype interpretation cannot be excluded but is not independently supported here.

Runner-up: CEBPA, on the strength of an unrelated line of evidence — direct, mechanistically specific, smoking-conditional causal biology (epithelial Cebpa loss → protease/antiprotease imbalance → emphysema-like pathology in mice, downregulated in human emphysema tissue; smoke-induced upregulation in lung fibroblasts linked to elastin loss) that SLC7A10 entirely lacks. But CEBPA has no locus-specific statistical support of its own at this locus (not the nearest gene, not flagged by PIP/PoPS/L2G; L2G score ~0.05–0.06 in the only fine-mapping data available, which is for allergic traits, not COPD/spirometry) — crediting it with this credible set's genetic signal risks illegitimately transferring statistical evidence to a neighboring gene on mechanism-only plausibility, per the skeptic's explicit flag.

NUDT19 is not a serious competing candidate: it is marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic and its own gene body sits outside the physical locus/credible-set window; LRP3 and WDR88 have no plausible COPD-relevant biology and are not prioritized (see audit for full reasoning).

What would resolve the SLC7A10-vs-CEBPA tie: a COPD- or spirometry-specific (not allergic-trait) fine-mapping or colocalization analysis at this exact variant; an eosinophilic-COPD-endotype-stratified GWAS; or molecular-QTL (eQTL/sQTL) colocalization tying the credible-set variant to SLC7A10 or CEBPA expression specifically in lung tissue (airway epithelium or fibroblasts) — none of which currently exists in the evidence reviewed.
