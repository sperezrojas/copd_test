Prioritized gene

Prioritized gene: BCL11B. Confidence: LOW.

This call is close, and the evidence base does not cleanly resolve it — red-team-skeptic marked BCL11B CONTESTED and C14orf177/LINC02914 WEAKENED (see audits/14_99100001-99350000_audit.md for full reasoning), so this recommendation should be read as the best available call under real uncertainty, not a confirmed conclusion.

Why BCL11B over C14orf177/LINC02914 despite the CONTESTED verdict: BCL11B is the nearest gene, the sole FLAMES-prioritized gene, and all 35 credible-set variants (including the lead variant rs807734) fall within its introns — a positional/architectural argument, not a confirmed mechanistic one, but a real one. It is a strongly LoF-constrained, dosage-sensitive protein-coding gene (gnomAD LOEUF ≈0.27) with an established Mendelian disease role (haploinsufficiency syndrome) and a well-verified, if COPD-untested, role as a master transcription factor for T-cell/ILC lineage programs (T-lineage commitment, MAIT17, ILC2) that are at least plausibly relevant to this pipeline's prior on heterogeneous inflammatory programs in COPD (rows for PMID 33870128, 32203192 above). By contrast, C14orf177/LINC02914 is a lncRNA with essentially no disease-association evidence in Open Targets (max score 0.0022) and mixed/negative bystander evidence at this same 14q32 locus in an unrelated disease context (AML amplicon, PMID 24441149) and in the Alzheimer's linkage study (PMID 26365416, which explicitly favored BCL11B).

Why confidence is only LOW, not MODERATE or HIGH: No literature citation with a VERIFIED verdict directly ties BCL11B to COPD or spirometry via a confirmed locus-table association, colocalization, TWAS, or Mendelian-randomization analysis — every such claim in the researcher's original synthesis was CLAIM_MISMATCH or unconfirmed on verification (see audit). The only literature-verified, gene-specific, lung-relevant GWAS finding newly surfaced during this review (Xu et al. 2025, pneumothorax) actually points to the runner-up gene, C14orf177, not BCL11B, at this same cytogenetic locus — though it is a different phenotype (pneumothorax, not COPD), a single population, and has no functional follow-up, and no LD/positional overlap with the rs807734 credible set has been confirmed. The "smoking-mediated" hypothesis for BCL11B is plausible (Open Targets smoking-initiation scores exceed its pulmonary-function scores) but likewise rests on an unconfirmed variant-level literature claim (rs807734 in GSCAN) per citation-verifier.

Runner-up: C14orf177/LINC02914, on the strength of the independent Xu et al. 2025 pneumothorax GWAS signal specifically attributed to it at this locus.

What would resolve the tie: (1) Confirmation of whether Xu et al. 2025's pneumothorax lead variant is in LD with, or overlaps, the rs807734 COPD credible set (a positional/LD check that neither the researcher nor red-team-skeptic could complete this session); (2) a dedicated BCL11B (or C14orf177) colocalization, TWAS, or Mendelian-randomization analysis against COPD/spirometry GWAS signals, ideally stratified by smoking status, to distinguish a direct-lung-biology mechanism from a smoking-behavior-mediated one; (3) confirmation from GSCAN's own supplementary tables of whether rs807734 (or a tight proxy) is genuinely among its genome-wide-significant smoking-behavior hits; (4) any expression/functional data on C14orf177/LINC02914 in airway or lung tissue, which is currently entirely absent from the literature under either gene name. The study team should weigh this LOW-confidence call accordingly and treat both genes as open candidates pending that follow-up.
