Open Targets summary (structured database lookup — NOT a verified literature citation; did not go through citation-verifier or red-team-skeptic)

PRR16 (ENSG00000184838, protein_coding):

  • Genetic constraint: pLoF oe = 0.296 (oeUpper/LOEUF-like = 0.622 — moderate, not extreme, intolerance to loss-of-function variation); missense oe = 1.095 (not missense-constrained); synonymous unremarkable.
  • Locus-to-gene (L2G) / colocalization at this exact locus: queried the two highest-PIP credible-set variants resolvable in Open Targets (rs6878072 PIP=0.094, rs6877942 PIP=0.055). Both have 9 credible sets each, ALL from FinnGen R12 GWAS of psychiatric/behavioral traits (anxiety disorders, recurrent/chronic depression, depression, depression-or-dysthymia, mood [affective] disorders, "any mental disorder," depression-medication use). For every one of these 18 credible-set instances, Open Targets' L2G algorithm assigns PRR16 as the causal gene, with scores ~0.61-0.63. Zero credible sets exist in Open Targets for COPD or spirometry/lung-function traits at this locus — an absence-of-data finding (Open Targets' COPD GWAS ingestion may not have called a credible set here), not evidence of no effect.
  • Aggregate target-disease association (all data types, not locus-specific L2G): PRR16 has association scores for 111 diseases/traits. Top-ranked: neurodegenerative disease (0.460, pathway-only), major depressive disorder (0.439, genetic_association 0.722), alcohol drinking (0.392, genetic_association 0.644), anxiety disorder (0.322, genetic_association 0.530). COPD ranks 26th of 111 (score 0.226; genetic_association sub-score 0.371, literature sub-score only 0.030 — this genetic_association sub-score likely derives in part from the same GWAS signal under evaluation here, so it should not be read as independent corroboration). No spirometry/FEV1/FEV1FVC trait appears anywhere in this list. Asthma scores far lower (0.0037, literature-only).
  • Overlapping feature at the lead variant: rs6878072 also resolves to an unnamed lncRNA, ENSG00000248927 (no HGNC symbol), overlapping this genomic position. This lncRNA is not part of the internal pipeline's candidate-gene list and was not investigated further by the researcher or red-team (search-budget exhausted for the red-team's planned follow-up); the red-team explicitly flags this as an unresolved gap — the credible-set/L2G evidence available does not distinguish PRR16 from this lncRNA (or from any other unexamined transcript in the window).
  • Expression (GTEx v8 / Human Protein Atlas, database lookups, not peer-reviewed citations): PRR16 is broadly expressed across tissues (highest in cultured fibroblasts, 57.7 TPM; artery-aorta 24.1 TPM); lung expression is moderate (~9-11 TPM), notably higher than most brain regions and much higher than liver or whole blood. HPA immunohistochemistry localizes PRR16 protein to alveolar cells and resident lung macrophages at medium level; single-cell clustering places it in a "Fibroblasts — ECM organization" cluster with low cell-type specificity (tau ~0.71). PRR16 is explicitly annotated "Not detected in immune cells" across curated blood immune-cell panels (T/B/NK/monocyte/neutrophil/dendritic cell), arguing against a circulating-immune-cell-specific role but not against a tissue-resident (lung macrophage/alveolar) role.

Prioritized gene

PRR16 — Confidence: LOW.

PRR16 is named here not because the evidence strongly implicates it in COPD biology, but because it is the only candidate gene the internal pipeline (nearest gene + FLAMES) or this review identified at this locus — there is no competing candidate gene to weigh it against, only an unexamined overlapping lncRNA (ENSG00000248927) that was never investigated (see audit). This is a "best available pick by default," not a confidently supported causal call, and the confidence label reflects that.

The evidence assembled is unusually clean in its negative findings: systematic literature searches for PRR16/this locus combined with smoking-behavior traits (initiation, heaviness, nicotine dependence, cessation) and with COPD/spirometric traits (FEV1, FEV1/FVC) each returned zero PubMed hits (see evidence table rows 5-6). The credible set is 100% non-coding across ~110 variants with no missense or splice variants, and MAGMA/PoPS/ABC gave no supporting signal. PRR16's founding functional paper (Yamamoto et al. 2014) describes a general, non-lung, non-immune cell-size/mitochondrial-translation mechanism; a second paper (Kang et al. 2022) shows PRR16 can drive EMT in a breast-cancer cell line only, never studied in lung tissue.

The one substantive genetic signal located anywhere for this locus is Open Targets' L2G assignment of PRR16 across 9 psychiatric/behavioral FinnGen credible sets (anxiety, depression, mood disorder) at the two highest-PIP variants, corroborated by a verified literature citation (Li et al. 2023, PMID 37945807) naming PRR16 as the nearest gene at a new anxiety-disorder risk locus. However, red-team review contests treating this as confirmed PRR16-causality even for the anxiety phenotype: that same paper's own deeper fine-mapping/functional-validation layer prioritized different genes (CTNND1, RAB27B, BTN3A2, PCLO) elsewhere in its results, and PRR16 itself received no functional follow-up (see audit for a fuller discussion of a genuine ambiguity in how to read "other loci" in that paper). Even taking the anxiety association at face value, it is a psychiatric-trait finding, not a pulmonary one, and the "smoking-mediated pathway" narrative connecting it to COPD is, per red-team, a two-step inference (PRR16-to-anxiety, then anxiety/depression-to-smoking) with no direct evidence for either step at this locus specifically — it should not be read as an established mechanism.

Runner-up: the overlapping lncRNA ENSG00000248927 (no HGNC symbol) is a formally undifferentiated alternative at this locus — the available fine-mapping/L2G evidence does not distinguish it from PRR16, and it was not investigated in this review (a follow-up flagged by red-team, not completed here due to search-budget limits on that agent's turn).

What would resolve this: (1) direct identity/expression/functional characterization of ENSG00000248927 to determine whether it is a more plausible regulatory candidate than PRR16 at this locus; (2) a dedicated smoking-initiation/heaviness GWAS (e.g., GSCAN or successor) cross-referenced against this exact locus, to test the smoking-mediation hypothesis directly rather than by inference from psychiatric-trait proxies; (3) direct COPD/spirometry fine-mapping or colocalization at this locus (currently absent from Open Targets), to establish whether any pulmonary signal exists at all, independent of the psychiatric one; (4) confirmation of whether Li et al. 2023's deeper functional/fine-mapping layer assessed PRR16 directly (and excluded it) or simply never assessed it (silence) — this materially affects how much weight the anxiety-GWAS evidence should carry even for the psychiatric-pathway hypothesis.

Given the near-total absence of pulmonary-specific evidence, the contested strength of even the non-pulmonary (anxiety) signal, and the unexamined lncRNA alternative, INSUFFICIENT evidence exists to confidently prioritize PRR16 (or any gene) as causal for COPD at this locus. LOW confidence is assigned rather than INSUFFICIENT only because PRR16 is at least the consistently-named candidate across every tool used (nearest-gene, FLAMES, and Open Targets L2G for the correlated psychiatric traits) — but the study team should treat this locus as needing substantially more direct pulmonary/smoking-behavior evidence before drawing any causal conclusion.
