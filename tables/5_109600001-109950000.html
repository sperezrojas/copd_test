Prioritized gene

FER — confidence LOW.

This is a close call between FER and MAN2A1, and the honest answer is that the verified evidence does not cleanly distinguish them — no lung-tissue data, no confirmed gene-specific naming in the largest available lung-function GWAS (Shrine et al. 2023), and no colocalization evidence resolvable from Open Targets for either gene. Within that constraint, FER is the better-supported pick for three reasons that are each in the evidence table/Open Targets summary above, not new claims: (1) FER has the higher Open Targets COPD genetic-association/L2G-proxy score of the two candidates with any COPD evidence at all (0.610 vs. MAN2A1's 0.469), and is independently the FLAMES-prioritized gene — a functional/transcriptomic prioritization method, unlike MAN2A1's status which rests solely on physical proximity; (2) FER shows higher gnomAD LoF constraint (oe=0.562, bin 1/6) than MAN2A1 (oe=0.581, bin 2/6), consistent with (though not proof of) greater dosage sensitivity; (3) even after red-team-skeptic stripped out the paralog-conflated (Fps/Fes, FES) and allergic/mast-cell-paradigm citations, FER retains one direct in vivo finding (PMID 16009680, Qi et al. 2005, Gut) showing FER itself — not a paralog — limits both neutrophil-driven inflammation and epithelial-barrier breakdown under endotoxin challenge, which is mechanistically concordant with COPD's neutrophilic/epithelial-barrier biology per this pipeline's priors, even though the tissue studied was intestine, not lung.

Runner-up: MAN2A1. Its case is weaker on both statistical-genetics and mechanistic-specificity grounds: the credible set's PIP mass (0.81 combined) sits in the intergenic lead variant and the LINC01848-proximal variant, not in MAN2A1 itself (only the low-PIP tail, each <0.03, falls in MAN2A1 introns/3'UTR) — a pattern the red-team-skeptic explicitly flagged as consistent with nearest-gene bias rather than gene-specific fine-mapping support. MAN2A1's own Open Targets profile is also more strongly associated with smoking initiation, alcohol drinking, schizophrenia, and COVID-19 than with COPD itself, raising a real (if unconfirmed) possibility of smoking-mediated or otherwise non-pulmonary pleiotropy per this pipeline's prior #1. Its immune literature, while real, is paradigm-mismatched to COPD (systemic lupus-like autoimmunity, tumor immunology) rather than neutrophilic/innate-airway biology.

TMEM232 is not a serious contender for the top pick: Open Targets records no COPD genetic-association evidence for it at all, it is gnomAD LoF-tolerant (oe=1.058, the least constrained of the three protein-coding candidates), and its only real literature is a repeatedly-replicated, cross-ancestry atopic dermatitis/allergic rhinitis association at the ambiguous TMEM232/SLC25A46 gene pair — red-team-skeptic marked this CONTESTED as a genuine competing (Th2/allergic, not COPD-classic) hypothesis for this region, not evidence for a TMEM232-COPD mechanism.

LINC01848 cannot be ruled in or out: it carries more fine-mapped posterior probability (PIP 0.32 + 0.04) than MAN2A1 does, but zero literature of any kind was located for it in the time available this session, so it is marked INSUFFICIENT rather than being promoted or dismissed.

What would resolve this tie: lung-specific eQTL/pQTL/sQTL colocalization for FER versus MAN2A1 at rs9326785 (or the credible set as a whole); confirmation from Shrine et al. 2023's (PMID 36914875) supplementary locus tables of which gene, if either, was specifically named as the prioritized gene at this signal; a dedicated GWAS Catalog/PheWAS/GTEx-lncRNA follow-up search for LINC01848 given its large fine-mapping weight; and a targeted smoking-initiation/cigarettes-per-day GWAS locus lookup at this exact position to test the smoking-mediation hypothesis flagged for MAN2A1. Given the number of open questions above, this recommendation should be treated as a starting point for the study team's own review, not a settled call.
