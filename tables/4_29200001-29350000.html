Prioritized-gene synthesis

Prioritized gene: LINC02472 — Confidence: INSUFFICIENT

This locus does not support a confident gene-level call, and that absence of resolution is itself the finding. The statistical fine-mapping is excellent (rs76849161, PIP=0.999, essentially the entire credible set on one variant), but every independent line of biological/functional evidence is null: FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS/ABC prioritization methods all returned NA for this locus; LINC02472, the only gene the credible-set variant physically falls within, has zero Open Targets disease associations of any kind (not just for COPD), zero L2G/genetic-association evidence, no genetic constraint or tractability data (expected for a non-coding RNA, but still uninformative), no on-topic PubMed literature (its only PubMed hit is an incidental, off-topic gastric-cancer metabolomics gene-list mention), no GWAS Catalog entry for the variant itself, and near-zero GTEx expression in lung or immune tissue (detectable only in testis). As the red-team-skeptic emphasized, physical containment of a noncoding intronic variant within a lncRNA transcript is weak causal evidence on its own — it establishes proximity, not function — and the credible-set variant could in principle be a distal regulatory element for a gene outside this ~150kb window (a recognized pattern at gene-desert loci), which cannot currently be tested here because no ABC/Hi-C/eQTL colocalization data exist for this region.

The only other gene considered, PCDH7 (~1.43 Mb away), is not a formally prioritized candidate by any method in the input pipeline. It does have a real, verified, if merely suggestive (not genome-wide-significant), DLCO/nominal-COPD association (Sakornsakolpat 2019) and notable behavioral/smoking-trait pleiotropy elsewhere in the genome-wide literature (childhood aggression, schizophrenia-smoking colocalization). However, as the skeptic stressed, no LD or colocalization analysis links PCDH7's associated variant (rs7664851) to rs76849161, and at 1.43 Mb apart these are far more likely to be two independent, coincidentally nearby signals than a single shared causal mechanism. PCDH7's smoking-related pleiotropy therefore cannot be invoked to argue this locus operates through a smoking-mediated pathway without that shared-signal check, which has not been done.

Runner-up: PCDH7 (very low confidence, distance-limited, unconfirmed shared signal). What would resolve this call: (1) LD/colocalization analysis between rs76849161 and rs7664851 (PCDH7's DLCO signal) to test whether they represent the same or independent signals; (2) chromatin conformation (Hi-C/ABC) or eQTL colocalization data for the rs76849161 credible-set region, to test whether this regulatory element targets LINC02472, PCDH7, or some other gene; (3) disease-state or airway-specific single-cell/bulk expression data for LINC02472 (smoke-exposed or COPD-affected lung tissue specifically, not healthy bulk GTEx) to test whether its testis-restricted expression in healthy donors understates a context-specific lung role. Until then, this locus should be carried forward as functionally uncharacterized rather than confidently assigned to LINC02472 — the study team should weigh whether to treat LINC02472 as a placeholder label for "the gene desert containing this fine-mapped signal" rather than a mechanistically supported candidate.
