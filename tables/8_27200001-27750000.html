Prioritized gene

Gene: CHRNA2 Confidence: LOW

This locus is a genuinely close call among three plausible candidates — CLU, CHRNA2, and EPHX2 — none of which is confirmed by locus-specific eQTL or colocalization evidence tying the credible-set variants to a specific gene's expression (a gap both red-team-skeptic passes identified as the single biggest missing evidence category, cutting equally against all candidates). Given that constraint, CHRNA2 is the best-supported single pick, for three convergent reasons documented in the table above: (1) among Open Targets' structured L2G scores (informational context, not literature), CHRNA2 has the single highest overall COPD disease-association score of any gene at this locus (0.376 vs. CLU's 0.109) and ties or beats CLU on L2G for both COPD-labeled GWAS studies and for "chronic airway obstruction"; (2) unlike CLU and EPHX2, CHRNA2's candidacy is corroborated by independently-verified primary literature (PMID 24253422, PMID 33454625, PMID 28604730, PMID 41579494, PMID 41645249) establishing it — distinct from the far more famous chr15 CHRNA5-CHRNA3-CHRNB4 cluster — as a genuine nicotine-dependence, smoking-behavior, and cross-trait-pleiotropy gene at this exact chr8p21 locus; and (3) nearly all of the ~40 GWAS credible sets sharing this locus's genetic signal are themselves labeled as smoking-behavior/nicotine-dependence/psychiatric traits rather than spirometric traits, consistent with prior #1 (smoking-mediated effects must be actively weighed against lung-intrinsic biology, not assumed away) and consistent with the complete absence of any FEV1/FEV1-FVC credible set at this locus (prior #2's expected concordance signal is simply missing here).

This call should be read as a smoking-behavior-mediated mechanism, not a lung-intrinsic one — CHRNA2's own established biology is a CNS/neuronal nicotinic-receptor subunit implicated in nicotine reward/dependence, not a lung-expressed or airway-structural gene; no evidence found here supports a direct pulmonary role for CHRNA2 itself. The COPD case-control association at this locus is best explained, on current evidence, as an indirect consequence of CHRNA2's effect on smoking initiation/heaviness/dependence rather than a direct effect on airway biology, which is why red-team-skeptic marked CHRNA2 CONTESTED specifically as a direct-lung- mechanism gene even while marking it STRENGTHENED as the smoking-mediation explanation.

Runner-up 1: CLU (nearest gene, PoPS-prioritized). Real, replicated correlational/ mechanistic evidence ties CLU protein to COPD severity, FEV1, and CT-emphysema, plus a distinct fibroblast-activation/lung-injury-repair mechanism (PMID 35191419, 23207572, 29133960) — but red-team-skeptic marked this WEAKENED because none of it is locus-specific (no eQTL/colocalization evidence), the studies are correlational/reverse-causation-susceptible, CLU's L2G scores are tied with or below CHRNA2's for COPD-relevant traits, and CLU's own single strongest statistical signal at this locus is for a non-pulmonary trait (monocyte count, L2G 0.69 — the highest score for any gene/trait pair examined), raising an unaddressed alternative hypothesis (comorbid cardiovascular/monocyte biology rather than airway-intrinsic mechanism).

Runner-up 2: EPHX2 (not flagged by any of the four internal pipeline methods). This gene has the mechanistically strongest evidence in the entire locus — two independent in vivo Ephx2-knockout mouse studies plus a pharmacological inhibitor study, all concordant, directly showing that EPHX2 loss/inhibition protects against cigarette-smoke-induced emphysema, inflammation, and lung-function decline (PMID 28028752, 41944769, 23434473). But red-team- skeptic marked this WEAKENED/UNRESOLVED because none of these studies test the GWAS locus, lead variant, or an eQTL link between credible-set variants and EPHX2 expression — they show EPHX2 protein function matters for smoke-induced lung damage in mice, not that common regulatory variation at this human locus acts through EPHX2. It was also not flagged by any of the four internal pipeline prioritization methods, an absence that itself, per red-team- skeptic, warrants caution before promoting EPHX2 as a synthesis override.

What would resolve this three-way tie: a locus-specific eQTL or GWAS-colocalization study (in lung tissue for CLU/EPHX2, or in brain/neuronal tissue for CHRNA2) directly linking the credible-set variants (rs73229093 and correlated variants) to expression of a specific gene; and/or a spirometry-trait (FEV1, FEV1/FVC) GWAS credible set at this locus, which was notably absent from all ~40 credible sets examined via Open Targets and would materially strengthen a lung-intrinsic (CLU or EPHX2) story if found, or its continued absence would further strengthen the CHRNA2/smoking-mediation story. The study team should weigh this LOW-confidence call accordingly — it is a best-supported recommendation given real evidentiary gaps, not a settled conclusion.
