Prioritized gene

MECOM — confidence: LOW.

MECOM is the best-supported candidate at this locus, but the case falls well short of a confident mechanistic call, and the red-team-skeptic's WEAKENED verdict is being carried forward rather than overridden. The supporting evidence is real but narrower than it first appears: (1) a long-standing, verified discovery association with FEV1/FEV1-FVC (Soler Artigas 2011) and a suggestive COPD rare-variant burden signal (Artigas/Wain 2017) — both in the evidence table above; (2) consistent, near-unanimous Open Targets L2G assignment to MECOM over LINC01997 at the lead variant across 25 independent GWAS studies, including COPD itself; and (3) strong GWAS-GWAS colocalization between the COPD signal and FEV1/PEF/FEV1-FVC that persists in never-smokers (H4=0.99), arguing against a purely smoking-behavior-mediated mechanism (satisfying prior #1) and for genuine pulmonary-function concordance (prior #2).

Against this, per the red-team-skeptic's critique (preserved verbatim in the audit file), several things pull confidence down to LOW rather than MODERATE or HIGH: (a) five of the seven verified citations are hematopoietic/leukemia background biology (Paul 2011 — at a different locus entirely, 7q22.3, not this credible set; Germeshausen 2018; Bard-Chapeau 2014; Nakamura 2018; Nguyen 2020) that establish MECOM is a real, dosage-sensitive transcription factor in blood-cell biology but provide no COPD- or lung-specific mechanism; (b) the same credible set colocalizes just as strongly with height, educational attainment, platelet volume, brain imaging, glaucoma, and creatinine as with spirometric traits, raising a live alternative hypothesis that this is a generic developmental/organ-growth locus rather than a COPD-specific one; (c) a CLAIM_MISMATCH-flagged finding (Higbee et al. 2024, PRISm GWAS) turned out, on verifier re-check, to actually name a MECOM-nearest novel signal (rs7652391) — but PRISm is a restrictive/mixed spirometric pattern, and its co-occurrence with the obstructive COPD signal is a complicating factor for a "COPD-specific" narrative, not simply more confirmatory evidence (excluded from the main table per the CLAIM_MISMATCH rule, but noted here as a caveat, not fabricated as a table row); and (d) no molecular-QTL (eQTL/pQTL/sQTL) evidence anywhere links the credible-set variants to MECOM expression, splicing, or protein level — the statistical gene-assignment case (L2G, nearest-gene exclusion of LINC01997) is stronger than the molecular-mechanistic case, which is currently absent. No COPD-relevant immune/inflammatory-program evidence (prior #3) was found for MECOM at all.

Runner-up: LINC01997, which the red-team-skeptic called CONTESTED and recommended de-prioritizing — it has zero disease-association literature and was never an L2G candidate across 25 independent studies at the lead variant, despite being the dominant nearest- transcript annotation for the credible set. This is fairly one-sided against LINC01997, but the skeptic flagged a residual, second-order caveat: L2G models may be systematically biased against lncRNAs (sparser functional-annotation/eQTL training features), so the consistent non-selection of LINC01997 is suggestive, not fully dispositive.

What would resolve the tie/raise confidence: (1) a lung-tissue eQTL or sQTL (e.g., GTEx lung, Lung eQTL Consortium) showing the credible-set variants alter MECOM expression or splicing in airway/lung parenchymal tissue specifically, which is currently entirely absent; (2) a dedicated fine-mapping/functional-genomics paper on this specific 3q26.2 locus (none was found in the primary literature search, and the researcher's own search budget was exhausted before this could be fully ruled out); (3) an independent replication of the COPD-specific rare-variant signal (Artigas/Wain 2017) in a larger, non-overlapping cohort; and (4) direct adjudication of whether the height/education/platelet-volume colocalization reflects true biological pleiotropy of MECOM or LD-driven co-localization with a distinct nearby causal variant — which the current data cannot distinguish. The study team should weigh this LOW- confidence call accordingly and treat it as a starting hypothesis for functional follow-up, not a settled conclusion.
