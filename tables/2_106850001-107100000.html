Prioritized gene

Prioritized gene: ST6GAL2 Confidence: LOW

ST6GAL2 is the recommended candidate at this locus, but the confidence is LOW and the call should be read as "least-bad available candidate," not a well-supported causal gene. The case for ST6GAL2 rests almost entirely on it being the sole protein-coding gene physically within the stated locus window (confirmed directly via Ensembl REST API) and on one verified ex vivo human bronchial-mucosa finding (Groux-Degroote et al. 2008, PMID 17944600) showing IL-6/IL-8 induce ST6GAL2 expression and airway mucin sialylation changes. Against that, the red-team-skeptic's critique — which I adopt — identifies a materially weaker picture than the positional case alone suggests: Open Targets shows zero COPD/FEV1/FEV1-FVC genetic-association evidence, zero L2G score, and zero lung-tissue eQTL/colocalization for ST6GAL2 across 37 molecular-QTL credible sets; the lead variant's only GWAS Catalog association is "Intelligence"; ST6GAL2's own top overall disease associations in Open Targets are cognitive traits; and its QTLs colocalize with educational attainment and metabolic traits, not respiratory ones. Two of the eleven ST6GAL2-specific literature citations (sheep muscle-area GWAS, schizophrenia pharmacogenomics) are failed replications and provide no positive support. The skeptic's alternative hypothesis — that this locus reflects smoking-behavior/cognitive biology rather than direct pulmonary mechanism, consistent with prior #1 (COPD summary statistics are not smoking-adjusted) — fits the totality of the verified evidence at least as well as a pulmonary-mechanism story, and there is no spirometry (FEV1/FEV1-FVC) concordance anywhere in the dossier to support prior #2's expectation for a genuine pulmonary-biology locus. The skeptic's verdict on ST6GAL2 is WEAKENED, not CONTESTED, and I have followed that: ST6GAL2 is still the single best-supported gene at this locus by a clear margin over the alternative, but "best-supported" here means "only gene physically present with any inflammation-relevant airway biology at all," not "credibly shown to drive this GWAS signal via a specific mechanism."

Runner-up: RGPD3 (PoPS-prioritized). This gene is explicitly NOT a viable co-equal candidate on current evidence. The red-team-skeptic marked it CONTESTED: the researcher's own Ensembl-confirmed coordinates show RGPD3's gene body sits 380-460kb outside the stated locus window entirely, its QTL credible sets sit outside this locus's actual credible-set span, Open Targets shows no colocalization data for it in this region, and its three verified literature citations (craniofacial morphology, thyroid-cancer selection scan, acral-melanoma somatic mutation) carry no lung, immune, or COPD relevance. RGPD3's candidacy appears to rest entirely on a PoPS score computed over a window wider than (or otherwise inconsistent with) the stated locus boundary, or on a gene-family mapping artifact given RGPD3 sits in a segmentally-duplicated paralog cluster (RGPD2/3/4/5/6) — this is flagged for the study team as a possible pipeline/annotation discrepancy worth checking independently of literature review.

What would resolve this: (1) a colocalization result directly tying the COPD credible set at this locus (not a wider window) to ST6GAL2 expression/splicing in lung or airway tissue specifically (none currently exists in Open Targets, which is GTEx-covered for many other tissues but not shown here for lung eQTL of ST6GAL2); (2) a genetic-correlation or MR analysis testing whether this locus's COPD association is mediated through smoking-initiation/cognitive-trait genetics, as the skeptic proposes, versus a direct effect on lung-function; (3) independent confirmation (or correction) of why PoPS prioritized RGPD3 despite its gene body sitting outside the stated locus window — if that turns out to be a window-definition mismatch rather than a genuine PoPS signal tied to this credible set, RGPD3 should likely be dropped from consideration at this locus altogether. Given the search-budget exhaustion the red-team-skeptic reported (no independent contradiction search completed for either gene), this locus would benefit from a follow-up pass with fresh search budget targeting GTEx lung-eQTL for ST6GAL2 and smoking/cognitive-trait genetic correlation specifically.
