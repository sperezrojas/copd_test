Prioritized gene

HHIP — Confidence: HIGH

HHIP is the best-supported candidate gene at this locus by a wide margin, and this is one of the more clear-cut calls in this pipeline. The case rests on four independently convergent, VERIFIED evidence streams, none of which the red-team-skeptic overturned:

  1. Multi-decade, repeatedly replicated statistical genetics, from the original 2009 discovery GWAS (Pillai PMID 19300482; Wilk PMID 19300500) through the 2019 mega-GWAS (Sakornsakolpat PMID 30804561; Shrine PMID 30804560) to a 2024 dysanapsis GWAS (Debban PMID 38935874), with concordance across COPD case-control, FEV1/FVC, and emphysema- imaging phenotypes — exactly the cross-trait concordance pattern that this pipeline's prior #2 treats as strong evidence for direct pulmonary biology.
  2. A locus-specific, gene-level test against the smoking-mediation alternative (prior #1) that HHIP passes: Pillai 2010 (PMID 20656943) explicitly dissociates the HHIP signal from smoking pack-years/intensity (in contrast to CHRNA3/5 in the same cohorts), and van der Plaat 2016 (PMID 27612410) shows genome-wide significance for FEV1/FVC in never-smokers with no smoking-interaction term. This is a rare case where the smoking confound was directly tested and ruled out for this specific gene, not merely assumed away.
  3. Deep, mechanistic developmental biology: classic Hedgehog-pathway lung-branching- morphogenesis knockout studies (Chuang/McMahon 2003, PMID 12569124) and Hhip+/- mice developing spontaneous age-related emphysema (Lao PMID 27444019) and impaired alveologenesis/BPD-emphysema linkage (Ye 2025, PMID 40333979) provide a causal, loss-of-function-to-phenotype bridge in vivo, though as the red-team-skeptic noted, the evidence assembled here does not establish the direction of the human GWAS effect on HHIP expression, so the mouse-to-human bridge is suggestive rather than fully closed.
  4. An emerging, single-study immune mechanism (Wang et al. 2023, Immunity, PMID 36822205) linking stromal Hhip loss to a hedgehog-IL-7-driven tissue-resident-lymphocyte expansion that suppresses alveolar stem cells via IFNgamma. Per the red-team-skeptic's critique, this should be weighted as a promising but unreplicated single-study lead, not a co-equal pillar alongside the statistical-genetics/spirometry evidence — the confidence rating here does not depend on this immune finding holding up.

This is corroborated by the Open Targets structured lookup (not itself evidence, but consistent context): HHIP is the dominant L2G gene at this locus by a wide margin across essentially every COPD/lung-function GWAS credible set, while FREM3 and SMARCA5 never appear in L2G predictions at all and show no COPD association in Open Targets' own disease-association data.

Runner-up genes and why they are not competitive: FREM3 (MAGMA-prioritized) and SMARCA5 (PoPS-prioritized) both have essentially no COPD/lung-relevant literature (zero PubMed hits in the researcher's targeted searches), and both were marked CONTESTED by the red-team-skeptic — not on the strength of any specific rebuttal paper, but because (a) an independent Ensembl coordinate check found both genes' bodies lie physically outside the stated locus window (FREM3 ~550kb-1.1Mb centromeric; SMARCA5 further still), consistent with these MAGMA/PoPS nominations reflecting LD-block/feature-similarity artifacts rather than direct mechanistic implication, and (b) the one citation offered in support of FREM3 (Band et al. 2015, PMID 26416757) was found by citation-verifier to actually attribute its malaria-resistance signal to the neighboring glycophorin gene cluster (GYPA/GYPB/GYPE), not specifically to FREM3 — a CLAIM_MISMATCH excluded from the evidence table above (see audit file). SMARCA5's only two offered citations are similarly off-target (a paralog-based neurodevelopmental-disorder paper) or unreliable (a paper misattributing Williams syndrome to SMARCA5, also excluded as CLAIM_MISMATCH). Neither gene shows any signal on the smoking/spirometry/immune priors this pipeline applies, because there is no lung-relevant evidence base to apply those priors to.

Because the case for HHIP is convergent across independent evidence types, directly tests and rules out the leading smoking-mediation alternative, and because the two competing candidate genes have both a coordinate-based structural reason and an evidentiary vacuum counting against them, this call is rated HIGH confidence rather than the more cautious MODERATE/LOW that this pipeline reserves for loci with only locus-level or paralog-analogy evidence. The main residual uncertainty — noted by the red-team-skeptic — is that the assembled evidence does not establish the direction of effect linking the GWAS risk allele to HHIP expression level, and the immune mechanism (Wang et al. 2023) is still a single study; a direction-of-effect eQTL/MR analysis and independent replication of the stromal immune mechanism would further strengthen (or could qualify) this call.
