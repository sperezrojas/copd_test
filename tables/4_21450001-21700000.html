Prioritized gene

KCNIP4 — confidence: LOW.

KCNIP4 is named by every gene-prioritization method that returned a result at this locus (nearest gene, FLAMES, PoPS) and is the sole protein-coding gene physically overlapping the credible set (all 14 credible-set variants, including the lead rs11733529, are intronic within KCNIP4 itself) — there is no competing gene to weigh against it, and gene identification at this locus is genuinely uncontested. Open Targets' independent GWAS credible-set analysis at this exact locus corroborates this with a reasonably strong, uncontested L2G score of 0.710 for a COPD phenotype (biobank PheCode 496).

However, per the red-team-skeptic's WEAKENED verdict (preserved verbatim in the audit appendix), the mechanism by which this locus acts on COPD risk is unresolved and is not well supported as direct pulmonary/structural lung biology: (1) no paper or Open Targets entry links KCNIP4 to FEV1, FEV1/FVC, or any spirometric trait despite a comprehensive check of 242 associated traits; (2) the single directly-pulmonary literature finding (Himes et al. 2013, VERIFIED) supports asthma/airway-hyperresponsiveness at nominal significance only, a related but mechanistically distinct trait from COPD's fixed airflow obstruction, with no functional follow-up; (3) the two most authoritative landmark GWAS that could have anchored either a direct COPD signal (Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019, 82 loci) or a smoking-initiation signal (Liu et al. 2019 GSCAN, 406 loci) were both checked specifically for a KCNIP4/chr4p15.2 entry by the citation-verifier and came back NOT_FOUND — meaning the actual source GWAS underlying this pipeline's own COPD credible set at this locus has not been identified in the literature reviewed, a real thoroughness gap; (4) KCNIP4's only molecular-biology characterization found (four VERIFIED papers) is exclusively neuronal/CNS (Kv4-channel auxiliary subunit, EF-hand Ca2+-binding protein), with zero lung, airway, or alveolar biology in any retrieved source; (5) the one COPD-specific credible set with colocalization data (Open Targets GCST90476027) colocalizes only with fasting glucose and systolic blood pressure — not lung function or any lung molecular QTL — actively pointing away from a lung-tissue mechanism.

Two alternative, non-mutually-exclusive hypotheses are at least as well supported by the current evidence as direct pulmonary biology, and neither can currently be ruled in or out: (a) smoking-behavior/neurobehavioral mediation, given KCNIP4's dominant neurobehavioral/psychiatric trait profile and independent smoking-interaction signals in unrelated cancer GWAS (lung cancer, Poirier 2015; pancreatic cancer, Tang 2014), though the specific claim that KCNIP4 is a genome-wide-significant GSCAN smoking-initiation locus was NOT_FOUND on full-text check; and (b) metabolic/cardiovascular comorbidity or phenotyping artifact in the biobank-derived "Chronic airway obstruction" phenotype, given the glucose/blood-pressure colocalization pattern.

What would resolve this: (i) identifying and citing the actual source GWAS for this pipeline's COPD credible set at this locus (it is not Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019); (ii) a KCNIP4 lung-tissue or airway-epithelium eQTL/sQTL colocalization with the COPD or lung-function signal specifically; (iii) a direct FEV1/FEV1-FVC GWAS association for this locus; (iv) functional/mechanistic follow-up of the Himes et al. 2013 asthma/AHR association in airway tissue; (v) explicit adjustment for smoking behavior in a COPD GWAS at this locus to test whether the signal attenuates (supporting smoking mediation) or persists (supporting direct pulmonary biology). Until any of these are available, KCNIP4 remains the best-supported gene by physical/statistical prioritization but the causal pathway is unresolved, and confidence is marked LOW rather than MODERATE or HIGH.
