Prioritized-gene synthesis

Prioritized gene: CIPC Confidence: LOW

This is an unusually literature-poor locus: no candidate gene has any direct COPD-, lung-function-, or spirometry-specific GWAS, TWAS, MR, colocalization, or functional literature, and this absence is independently corroborated by an Open Targets curated-evidence check (COPD disease-association score = 0 for every candidate, validated against a positive control) and an exhaustive PubMed/bioRxiv search for each gene. No missense, splice-donor, or splice-acceptor credible-set variant exists to anchor a gene-specific functional argument, and the lead variant (rs204983, PIP=0.39, combined with rs204984 PIP=0.29 to carry ~68% of the credible set's PIP mass) is a non-coding, downstream_gene_variant with zero variant-specific literature, sitting in an intergenic gap roughly equidistant between CIPC and the 3' ends of LINC02288/LINC02289. Per the red-team-skeptic, gene assignment at the lead-variant level is genuinely unresolved, not merely under-annotated — nearest-gene status for CIPC is a naming convention here, not a demonstrated mechanism, and this is exactly the "LD-block, multiple plausible genes" scenario described in this pipeline's prior #1 guidance.

CIPC is named the top pick because it is (a) the pipeline's nearest-gene call, (b) a CLOCK-BMAL1-interacting circadian repressor sitting within a genomic neighborhood (and, via the class-level review literature, a broader biological pathway) with substantial, independently verified evidence that circadian-clock disruption produces COPD-like emphysematous, inflammatory, and epithelial pathology in mouse models (9 VERIFIED citations above), and (c) the sole candidate with a (modest) top-ranking L2G score at this locus for any trait (diastolic blood pressure, 0.467) among the pipeline-listed candidates. However, per the skeptic's critique, this case is genuinely weak: every one of the supporting circadian-biology citations studies BMAL1/CRY1/CRY2/PER/REV-ERBα, never CIPC itself, and CIPC — a peripheral repressor of, not a core component within, that transcription-factor loop — is never functionally tested in any cited lung/COPD model. No CIPC-specific eQTL or expression link to the lead variant was identified. CIPC's own disease-genetics evidence (a brain-tissue ASD TWAS hit; L2G support clustered in blood-pressure/metabolic/smoking traits) points away from, not toward, a pulmonary mechanism, and CIPC is entirely absent from the L2G list for the one directly pulmonary GWAS credible set identified in this region (FEV/FVC ratio).

Runner-up: IRF2BPL (not listed in the original locus-prioritization file). IRF2BPL has the single strongest quantitative signal connecting this locus to a directly COPD-relevant trait — sole L2G target (score 0.533) for an FEV/FVC-ratio GWAS credible set overlapping this region — which would ordinarily be compelling under this pipeline's prior #2 (pulmonary-function concordance). However, this rests entirely on one unverified, non-literature structured-database data point, with zero corroborating PubMed literature, against a large (7 VERIFIED citations), independently well-documented alternative explanation: IRF2BPL is an established, LoF-constrained, de novo neurodevelopmental disease gene (NEDAMSS) with no lung, airway, or immune connection in any paper found. Per the skeptic, the more parsimonious explanation for IRF2BPL's broad L2G prominence across this locus's largely metabolic/anthropometric credible-set cluster is a constraint/pleiotropy-driven statistical artifact common to L2G-style methods, not tissue-specific pulmonary biology — this is not resolved evidence against IRF2BPL, but it is a real and unaddressed competing explanation.

Why this is a close, low-confidence call, and what would resolve it: The verified literature does not cleanly distinguish CIPC from IRF2BPL (or, at the lead-variant level, from the neighboring lncRNAs LINC02288/LINC02289) as the causal gene at this locus. CIPC's case rests on nearest-gene status plus an unproven pathway-level analogy; IRF2BPL's case rests on a single unverified L2G score with a strong competing non-pulmonary explanation. Neither has direct COPD/lung-function literature. What would resolve this: (1) a CIPC-specific or IRF2BPL-specific eQTL/pQTL/chromatin-interaction study directly linking the lead variant (rs204983/rs204984) to expression of either gene in lung or airway tissue; (2) full-text/supplementary-table review of the major lung-function/COPD GWAS meta-analyses (Shrine et al. 2019, PMID 30804560; Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019, PMID 30643253; John 2022; Patchen 2024) for a locus-table entry over chr14:76.9-77.2Mb, which this review could only check at the abstract level and which the red-team-skeptic flagged as a genuine, unresolved thoroughness gap that could materially change this locus's confidence rating; (3) a functional (not just database-level) COPD/lung-function study of IRF2BPL to test whether its FEV/FVC L2G signal reflects real pulmonary biology or pleiotropy/constraint artifact; and (4) the red-team-skeptic's own contradicting-evidence web search was left incomplete due to search-budget exhaustion and should be re-run before this locus is treated as fully resolved. Given all of the above, this pick is marked LOW confidence, not higher, and the study team should weigh CIPC and IRF2BPL as roughly comparably (weakly) supported pending the above follow-up.
