Prioritized-gene synthesis

Prioritized gene: KLHL29 — Confidence: LOW / INSUFFICIENT.

KLHL29 is the only candidate gene supplied for this locus by the internal pipeline (nearest gene and FLAMES-prioritized), and it does carry a real, repeated, gene-specific structured-database signal: high L2G scores for FEV1 and pulmonary function measurement (up to 0.816, across roughly a dozen-plus independent lung-function GWAS studies per Open Targets) and an even higher L2G-like score for smoking initiation (0.93). However, this is where the case stops being solid. The credible set itself (41 variants, all noncoding/MODIFIER) carries no gene-level annotation at all — the pipeline's own fine-mapping does not anchor to KLHL29 specifically, and no MAGMA, PoPS, or ABC-model support is listed. No colocalization (eQTL/pQTL) evidence was retrieved. Critically, neither of the two largest, most systematic human lung-function/COPD variant-to-gene mapping papers in the field (Shrine et al. 2019, 107 genes; Shrine et al. 2023, 559 genes) could be confirmed — by the researcher or by an independently re-checking citation-verifier — to name KLHL29 among their putative causal genes, despite Open Targets showing a strong FEV1 L2G signal at this locus. Similarly, the flagship GSCAN tobacco/alcohol GWAS names PDE4B and CUL3 (KLHL29's own binding partner) in its main text but not KLHL29 itself. The only mechanistic paper on KLHL29 (Yao et al. 2023) establishes a real CUL3-adaptor/ubiquitination mechanism but entirely in triple-negative breast cancer, with zero lung, airway, or pulmonary-development data — it cannot be used to argue lung relevance. KLHL29 also appears recurrently as a candidate/QTL gene across unrelated GWAS in pigs, cattle, flatfish, and catfish for body size, milking speed, and heat stress, a pattern the red-team-skeptic judges as evidence of positional/LD promiscuity near GWAS peaks rather than trait-specific biology — actively undermining rather than supporting gene-specific causality. The red-team-skeptic's explicit verdict is CONTESTED, and additionally disclosed that its own independent contradiction/ neighboring-gene search was blocked by exhausted search budget — a genuine, unresolved coverage gap, not a clean negative. Because (i) no rival gene at this locus was identified as a competing candidate (there is no runner-up to name — KLHL29 is the only candidate gene the internal pipeline supplied), but (ii) the evidence for KLHL29 itself is positional/statistical rather than literature-confirmed, and (iii) the locus's strongest quantitative signal splits between a smoking-behavior trait and a pulmonary-function trait without primary literature to adjudicate between them, this recommendation should be read as "best/only available candidate," not as a confirmed causal-gene call. What would resolve this: (a) direct access to the Shrine 2019/2023 and GSCAN supplementary variant-to-gene/gene-prioritization tables to check explicitly whether KLHL29 (vs. any unlisted neighboring gene) is named; (b) an eQTL/ colocalization analysis for KLHL29 in lung tissue and/or brain tissue (given the smoking-initiation signal); (c) an independent literature/positional search for other genes physically present in the 2:23,050,001-23,500,000 interval, since none were supplied by the internal pipeline and the red-team-skeptic could not complete this check. Given the researcher's own hedged bottom line, the citation-verifier's independent failure to corroborate gene-level attribution in the two largest lung- function GWAS papers, and the skeptic's explicit CONTESTED verdict, confidence is capped at LOW/INSUFFICIENT — this is the study team's call to make with additional data, not a settled conclusion from this review.
