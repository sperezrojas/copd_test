Open Targets summary (structured database lookup — NOT a verified literature citation)

This section reports results from Open Targets Platform GraphQL queries performed directly by the orchestrator (mcp__open-targets__* tools). It did not go through citation-verifier or red-team-skeptic and should be weighed as database-derived structured evidence, not peer-reviewed literature.

NBEA (ENSG00000172915):

  • Genetic constraint (gnomAD): LOF constraint score = 1.0 (maximally constrained), observed/expected = 0.095 (95% CI 0.066-0.137; 21 observed LoF variants vs. 220.8 expected) — extremely LoF-intolerant. Missense constraint score = 5.46 (oe = 0.721). Synonymous constraint near-neutral (score 0.974, oe = 0.943), as expected.
  • L2G score / colocalization for COPD (this GWAS): No data available. This GWAS is not indexed in Open Targets' study catalog. The only credible sets overlapping the lead variant rs403904 in Open Targets are two brain molecular-QTL studies (see below), not COPD/disease studies; their L2G prediction arrays are empty (L2G is not computed for QTL-type studies).
  • Molecular QTL evidence at the lead variant (rs403904, 13:35,672,096): Fine-mapped (SuSiE) exon-usage/splicing QTL for NBEA (exon 35,672,232-35,672,263) in two independent brain cohorts: CommonMind Consortium DLPFC (beta = -0.280, p = 1.13e-15) and ROSMAP brain (beta = -0.252, p = 7.10e-15). No lung or immune-cell eQTL/sQTL credible set in Open Targets' QTL catalogue for NBEA is fine-mapped to rs403904 or rs374452 specifically — see follow-up below.
  • Follow-up lung/immune QTL check (prompted by red-team-skeptic critique): Open Targets' full QTL credible-set catalogue for NBEA (104 credible sets across all tissues/cell types) does include a bulk lung eQTL (GTEx, "upper lobe of left lung"; lead variant rs562861, 13:35,106,321, ~566 kb from this locus's credible-set variants) and multiple immune-cell eQTLs (BLUEPRINT monocytes; Schmiedel 2018 Th1/Th2/Th17/Tfh/naive Treg memory CD4 subsets; Nathan 2022 and OneK1K single-cell CD4/NK eQTLs). None of the spot-checked lung/immune eQTL signals' fine-mapped lead variants (rs562861, rs73489543, rs77090515, rs1903610, rs2798337, rs7320655/rs7339118) are within the fine-mapped credible set for, or plausibly overlap, this locus's high-PIP variants (rs403904/rs374452; closest is ~74 kb away). This indicates NBEA does have lung and immune-cell expression/QTL data available and queried (i.e., "brain-only" is not simply an artifact of unconsulted resources) — but the specific credible-set variants at this locus coincide with a fine-mapped molecular QTL signal only in brain, not lung or the immune cell types surveyed. This does not prove the absence of a pulmonary/immune-tissue effect at this exact variant (QTL study power varies by tissue/cell type and sample size), but it does not provide positive support for one either.
  • Overall target-disease association (Open Targets aggregated cross-source evidence, all data types, not restricted to this GWAS): NBEA does not appear in the top 25 targets associated with "chronic obstructive pulmonary disease" (MONDO_0005002); top hits for COPD are NR3C1, SERPINA1, CHRM3, ADRB2, FAM13A, PDE4A/B/C/D, CHRM1/2/4/5, CHRNA4, NPNT, PDE3A/B, ADORA1, CHRNA3, TET2, ADGRG6, GC, ADORA2B. NBEA's own top-25 associated disease/trait list is dominated by neurodevelopmental disorders/epilepsy (top hit: "neurodevelopmental disorder with/without early-onset generalized epilepsy," score 0.649) plus intellectual disability, autism spectrum disorder, and other neurodevelopmental HPO terms. Notably, "smoking initiation" (EFO_0005670) appears at score 0.323 (genetic_association component 0.570) in NBEA's own list — but the citation-verifier could not confirm this association against the primary literature (see excluded citations above); it exists only as an unsourced Open Targets aggregate score. No lung-function trait (FEV1, FEV1/FVC) appears in NBEA's top-25 list.

Prioritized gene

Prioritized gene: NBEA Confidence: LOW

NBEA is not really in competition with another gene at this locus — all four internal prioritization methods (nearest gene, FLAMES, MAGMA, PoPS) converge on it, and Ensembl confirms it is the only protein-coding gene physically in this ~250 kb window. So as a matter of "which gene does this locus most likely act through," NBEA is the only credible candidate and there is no runner-up gene to weigh it against.

The low confidence rating reflects a different problem: whether this locus's association with COPD in this GWAS reflects genuine NBEA pulmonary or smoking-behavior biology at all, versus a statistical/winner's-curse artifact. The evidence assembled here is a genuine double-edged pattern that the study team should weigh carefully:

  • For a real, brain/smoking-mediated mechanism (prior #1): the lead credible-set variant rs403904 is a strong, fine-mapped (SuSiE), doubly-replicated (CommonMind + ROSMAP) brain exon-usage/splicing QTL for NBEA — real molecular evidence the variant regulates NBEA, in brain tissue specifically. NBEA is an extremely LoF-constrained (gnomAD score 1.0), well-established neurodevelopmental/epilepsy gene (VERIFIED: Mulhern 2018, Boulin 2021, Huang 2025) acting via synaptic vesicle trafficking. Open Targets' own aggregate evidence flags a nontrivial NBEA-"smoking initiation" association. Together this is a plausible profile for a locus acting on COPD risk through smoking-initiation/nicotine-dependence neurobiology rather than lung-intrinsic biology (per prior #1), consistent with no structural/developmental or immune/inflammatory pulmonary biology being found for NBEA in the literature.

  • Against treating that as established (per red-team-skeptic, CONTESTED): the citation- verifier independently checked the three most relevant, best-powered prior GWAS — the ICGC/UKB COPD case-control GWAS (82 loci, Sakornsakolpat 2019), the 2019 lung-function GWAS (279 signals, Shrine 2019), and the 2023 multi-ancestry lung-function GWAS (559 genes, Shrine 2023) — and NBEA appears in none of them. The two large smoking/tobacco- use GWAS (GSCAN 2019, 406 loci; Saunders 2022, 2,143 loci) also do not name NBEA, so the Open-Targets-flagged "smoking initiation" association could not be traced to, or confirmed in, primary literature. The credible-set PIP is modest and split across two variants (0.52 and 0.46), consistent with either a real but weak/diffuse signal or an unresolved/noisy fine-mapping result. The follow-up Open Targets QTL check further shows that NBEA's own lung and immune-cell eQTL signals (which do exist and were queried) are led by different variants tens-to-hundreds of kb away, not by this locus's credible-set variants — so there is no positive lung- or immune-tissue molecular evidence tying rs403904/rs374452 specifically to NBEA regulation in those tissues. The three verified neurodevelopmental- disease citations describe rare, large-effect LoF variants — a different variant class and effect-size regime from the common noncoding 3'UTR variants in this credible set — so they establish that NBEA can cause severe neurodevelopmental disease via haploinsufficiency, but do not directly establish that a common 3'UTR variant produces a COPD-relevant phenotype through the same or a different (e.g., smoking-behavior) pathway.

What would resolve this: (1) direct confirmation (or refutation) of an NBEA-region signal for smoking initiation/cessation/pack-years in GWAS Catalog or GSCAN/Saunders supplementary tables (not just abstract-level search, which was the limit of this review's literature access); (2) a colocalization analysis between this COPD GWAS credible set and the CommonMind/ROSMAP brain exon-usage QTL (or a lung/blood eQTL/sQTL specific to rs403904/rs374452, were adequately powered data to become available) to test whether they share a causal variant, rather than only observing that both fall in the same gene; (3) replication of this locus in an independent COPD or lung-function cohort, given its absence from three major prior GWAS of directly overlapping phenotypes. Until then, NBEA is the best (and only) candidate gene at this locus, but the study team should treat the COPD association itself, and any smoking-mediated interpretation of it, as unresolved rather than established.
