Open Targets summary (structured database lookup — not a verified literature citation; not reviewed by citation-verifier or red-team-skeptic)

MDGA2 (ENSG00000139915):

  • Genetic constraint (gnomAD): LoF observed/expected (oe) = 0.257 (95% CI 0.185-0.362; expected 93.4, observed 24) — strongly constrained against loss-of-function variation, a dosage-sensitivity signal consistent with the Morsy et al. 2026 human LoF-disease finding above. Missense oe = 0.828 (mild constraint); synonymous oe = 1.009 (as expected, no constraint).
  • Aggregate COPD association score: MDGA2 does not rank in the top 500 of 6,323 targets Open Targets associates with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (MONDO_0005002) — its score is below 0.19, versus established COPD genes such as SCNN1B (0.806), CFTR (0.760), SERPINA1 (0.703), FAM13A (0.631), HHIP (0.485), IREB2 (0.293). This is a weak aggregate genetic-association signal for MDGA2-COPD specifically.
  • MDGA2's own top-scored disease/trait associations (out of 2,927 total): (1) skeletal system abnormality (0.341), (2) alcohol drinking (0.337), (3) smoking initiation (0.323), (4) ADHD (0.315), (5) obesity disorder (0.314), (6) mathematical ability (0.311). COPD does not appear in this top-25 list (score <0.247).
  • Lead variant (rs12889080) credible-set/colocalization survey: this variant is a fine-mapped credible-set member in 122 separate GWAS study-loci in Open Targets (all 122 inspected). The overwhelming majority (>100/122) are anthropometric/body-composition traits (BMI in 15+ cohorts, body weight, fat mass, lean mass, body fat percentage, waist circumference, basal metabolic rate). A minority are type 2 diabetes, HbA1c, sleep apnea, PTSD, pain, blood pressure, and psychiatric/ADHD-obesity composite traits. None of the 122 credible sets at this exact variant are COPD, lung function (FEV1/FEV1-FVC), or a direct smoking-behavior trait in Open Targets' own GWAS ingestion.
  • Colocalization: no COPD- or lung-function-specific study locus was found colocalizing with this variant; this absence is itself informative and is noted rather than omitted.
  • Interpretation flag (from the orchestrator's step-1 lookup, echoed and investigated in the researcher/skeptic stages): the lead variant's dominant cross-trait signature in Open Targets is adiposity/body-composition, not smoking or lung function directly — an alternative pathway hypothesis not covered by this pipeline's three standard priors. The researcher searched PubMed specifically for MDGA2 + obesity/BMI/adiposity literature and found zero results, so this remains an open, literature-unaddressed structured-database observation, not a corroborated mechanism.

Prioritized gene

Gene: MDGA2 — Confidence: LOW

MDGA2 is named as the prioritized gene at this locus by default, not because the evidence is strong, but because it is the only candidate: it is both the nearest gene and the FLAMES-prioritized gene, no MAGMA/PoPS/ABC gene competes with it, MDGA2 physically spans essentially the entire ~300kb locus window, and every gene-annotated credible-set variant sits within MDGA2 itself. There is no gene-identity ambiguity to resolve (unlike loci with multiple genes in LD) — the open question is entirely about mechanism, not which gene.

The verified evidence, however, does not support a COPD-specific or lung-specific mechanism with any confidence. As the red-team-skeptic's critique concludes (see audits/14_46700001-47000000_audit.md for the full critique), all three of this pipeline's standard priors are, at best, weakly and indirectly touched:

  • Prior #1 (smoking-mediation): the only variant-level evidence (Lai et al. 2025, row 1 above) is a sub-genome-wide-significant (P=4.52x10⁻⁷) association with a pooled, non-tobacco-specific substance-use-disorder trait — weaker than "smoking-mediated" implies, and no smoking-initiation/cessation/heaviness GWAS hit exists for this variant.
  • Prior #2 (spirometry): a clean, genuine absence — no FEV1/FEV1-FVC or COPD literature exists for MDGA2 at all, and the one lung-disease-adjacent finding (Noth et al. 2013 IPF signal) failed replication and concerns a mechanistically distinct fibrotic disease.
  • Prior #3 (inflammation): a modest, single-population SLE/monocyte-cytokine-inducibility signal (Hellquist et al. 2009) with no COPD-relevant airway inflammatory-program specificity (no neutrophilic/CD8+/Th1/Th17/macrophage evidence).

Two additional, non-prior alternative hypotheses were raised and remain unresolved: (a) an Open Targets-flagged but literature-uncorroborated adiposity/body-composition mediation pathway (122/122 of the lead variant's other GWAS credible-set memberships are anthropometric traits), and (b) a gene-size/pleiotropy artifact — MDGA2 is one of the largest genes in the genome (~430kb) and its promiscuous appearance across unrelated GWAS (SLE, IPF, depression, fibromyalgia, neuroticism, carnitine metabolites, colorectal cancer, anthropometric traits, substance use) may partly reflect its size rather than true shared biology.

MDGA2's best-replicated, most robust biology by far is CNS-synaptic/neurodevelopmental (a dosage-sensitive gene whose human loss-of-function causes epileptic encephalopathy, and whose common variants are linked to autism, neuroticism, and fibromyalgia) — mechanistically disconnected from lung pathophysiology as currently understood. The red-team-skeptic explicitly declined to call this locus CONTESTED (there is no competing gene to contest against) but rated the overall evidence base WEAKENED relative to the researcher's original framing.

What would resolve this: a direct FEV1/FEV1-FVC or COPD GWAS/fine-mapping hit at this variant or gene; a tobacco/nicotine-specific (not pooled-substance) GWAS association at rs12889080; airway/lung single-cell expression or functional data for MDGA2; or a literature-confirmed adiposity/BMI association for MDGA2 that could be tested for mediation of the COPD signal. Absent any of these, this locus should be treated as low-confidence and mechanistically unresolved — MDGA2 is reported here as the best (and only) candidate gene, not as a well-supported causal mechanism.
