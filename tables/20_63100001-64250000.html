Prioritized gene

CHRNA4 — confidence: MODERATE (unchanged from the original review)

CHRNA4 remains overwhelmingly the best-supported gene at this locus for gene identity (which gene the credible-set signal acts through): it is the only gene ever assigned an L2G score across all 45 GWAS credible sets overlapping this variant (Open Targets summary above — now independently re-confirmed against TCEA2 by name as well, with an identical null result), it is independently implicated in nicotine-dependence/smoking-behavior genetics by multiple verified statistical- genetics papers (Saccone 2010, Chen 2020, Csala 2017, and especially Thorgeirsson 2016's direct CHRNA4-COPD variant association), and the CHRNA4-COPD statistical association itself replicates cross-ancestry (Lin et al. 2024, Taiwanese cohort) in addition to the original UK Biobank-based signal. No competing gene in the ~1.15 Mb region — including the newly-added TCEA2 — ever receives a MAGMA/PoPS/ABC/FLAMES or L2G signal at this locus's credible set.

However, two considerations (both unchanged from the original review) keep confidence at MODERATE rather than HIGH, per the red-team critique (verdict: WEAKENED on the mechanism question) — and consistent with "never claim more certainty than the weakest link allows":

  1. Mechanism, not just identity, matters for how this locus should be interpreted. The best direct test available (Bazemore et al. 2024's formal mediation analysis, PMID 38827081) found the CHRNA4-COPD association to be substantially smoking-mediated, for both ICD- and spirometry-defined COPD (with the mediated proportion for spirometry-defined COPD actually larger after confounder adjustment). Open Targets shows no FEV1/FVC association and no lung- tissue molecular-QTL colocalization for CHRNA4 (the only molecular colocalization found is a brain-cortex exon-QTL). The one candidate lung-autonomous mechanistic paper (Wu 2019, PMID 31821848) is single-author, unreplicated, in a lower-tier journal, and itself entirely cigarette-smoke-exposure-conditional — it does not meaningfully counterbalance the mediation evidence. Per Prior 1, this locus's COPD signal should be interpreted as substantially smoking-behavior-mediated (CNS nicotinic-receptor biology affecting nicotine consumption/ dependence, which secondarily elevates COPD risk via heavier/more damaging smoking), not as evidence of an independent pulmonary mechanism for CHRNA4.
  2. RTEL1 is not fully, rigorously excluded — this is still true after the TCEA2 re-check. RTEL1 lies within the same ~1.15 Mb locus window and has well-established, independent pulmonary disease biology (telomere-maintenance-driven pulmonary disease, per Hoffman et al. 2018) that is completely orthogonal to nicotinic/smoking biology. The case against RTEL1 rests on (a) the credible-set variant being intronic within CHRNA4 with PIP~1.0, and (b) RTEL1 never receiving an L2G score at this locus — but the red-team-skeptic has now, on two separate occasions (the original review and this 2026-09-11 re-opening), disclosed that its own independent search budget was exhausted before it could run a targeted RTEL1-specific check (GTEx lung eQTL near rs6011779; "RTEL1 COPD/emphysema" literature search; a named Open Targets L2G/coloc query for RTEL1 specifically). This remains an open gap, not a confirmed absence of RTEL1 evidence.

TCEA2 — assessed and NOT elevated to co-runner-up status; effectively ruled out at this locus, though not with the same rigor as CHRNA4's positive case. TCEA2 genuinely, physically overlaps this locus's window (independently confirmed via genomic coordinates), which is why it was carried through the full researcher/citation-verifier/red-team-skeptic pipeline rather than dismissed outright. However, every subsequent line of evidence argues against it: it is the most physically distal gene from the credible-set variant (~697 kb), it never receives an L2G score across any of 45 overlapping GWAS credible sets, it has zero colocalization evidence (molecular or GWAS-GWAS) at this locus, its aggregated Open Targets disease-association profile (55 diseases) contains no COPD, lung-disease, or smoking-behavior entry at all, and its independently-verified literature describes only (a) testis/germ-cell-restricted developmental expression, (b) a generic RNA Pol II-elongation/genome-stability mechanism with no tissue specificity relevant to lung, and (c) a genuine but renal (chronic kidney disease/eGFR), not pulmonary, MR-supported causal protein signal. The red-team-skeptic explicitly noted that this last point (a real, replicated MR/coloc signal existing for a different organ) makes TCEA2's total silence on COPD/lung more, not less, informative — this is evidence of absence, not merely absence of evidence. TCEA2's candidacy was generated purely by physical-window inclusion after a build-coordinate fix, and per the skeptic, this should serve as a cautionary example that window-membership alone is a weak prioritization criterion, rather than as grounds to treat TCEA2 as comparable to RTEL1's genuinely open, biologically-plausible alternative-candidacy.

Runner-up: RTEL1 (LOW standalone support at this locus — no prioritization signal, distance from the credible-set variant, and a disease mechanism — pulmonary fibrosis — that is a poorer phenotypic match to COPD than CHRNA4's — but flagged, for a second time now, as an incompletely excluded alternative given two independent unresolved search-budget gaps).

What would resolve the remaining uncertainty: (1) a dedicated Open Targets/GTEx lung-tissue eQTL or sQTL query for RTEL1 near rs6011779, to positively confirm or rule out any regulatory link — attempted and not completed in both the original review and this re-opening; (2) a targeted PubMed search for "RTEL1 COPD" / "RTEL1 emphysema" — likewise attempted and not completed twice; (3) additional mediation or stratified analyses (e.g., never-smoker COPD cohorts) to further quantify the smoking-independent residual effect, if any, of the CHRNA4 locus on COPD risk; (4) independent confirmation of TCEA2's actual MAGMA Z-score/p-value or PoPS score in the corrected re-merge file (not available to this pipeline run), to establish whether TCEA2 was a statistically-ranked candidate at this locus or merely a gene whose physical coordinates now correctly fall inside the window without a strong statistical signal of its own.
