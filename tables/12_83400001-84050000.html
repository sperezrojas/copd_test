Note on GWAS/spirometry literature coverage: No PubMed-indexed COPD case-control GWAS, fine-mapping, or colocalization publication naming TMTC2 was found by the researcher, and no PubMed-indexed spirometry (FEV1/FEV1-FVC) GWAS publication specifically naming TMTC2 was retrievable by keyword search either. The red-team-skeptic flagged this as a likely search-methodology gap rather than a true absence: large spirometry GWAS meta-analyses (e.g., Shrine et al. 2019-type efforts) typically report gene-desert loci by genomic position with their own nearest-gene call in supplementary tables, which a "TMTC2" keyword search would not surface. This is an open item for the study team — a position-based (chr12:83.4-84.4 Mb), not gene-name-based, check of major published spirometry/COPD GWAS supplementary loci tables was recommended by the skeptic but not completed in this pipeline run.

Open Targets summary (structured database lookup — not a verified literature citation)

Source: Open Targets Platform GraphQL API, queried directly by the orchestrator. This section did not go through citation-verifier or red-team-skeptic.

  • Target-disease association (TMTC2–COPD, MONDO_0005002): Zero rows returned from both target.associatedDiseases and disease.associatedTargets — no direct TMTC2-COPD association evidence exists in Open Targets' integrated evidence graph.
  • Locus-to-gene (L2G) at lead variant rs324768 (12_83575321_A_G): This variant sits in fine-mapped GWAS credible sets for 32 different studies/traits in Open Targets (none a COPD case-control GWAS). TMTC2 is the only gene receiving any L2G score in every one of the 32 studies — no competing gene exists in Open Targets' data for this locus. Scores are low-to-moderate (never reaching a conventional high-confidence ~0.7+ threshold): FEV1 ≈0.555 (two studies), FVC ≈0.08-0.555 (three studies), smoking initiation ≈0.570, smoking cessation ≈0.570, educational attainment ≈0.557-0.570, and 0.08-0.57 across a wide range of other UK Biobank traits.
  • Colocalisation (COLOC_PIP_ECAVIAR): Near-perfect (h4>0.9) colocalisation between FEV1 and FVC credible sets at this locus, and between FEV1/FVC and numerous non-respiratory traits (educational attainment, walking pace, age at first birth/sexual intercourse, frailty, occupational prestige, CRP, white blood cell count, triglycerides, HDL, cystatin C/eGFR, glaucoma) — a broad UK-Biobank phenome-wide pleiotropy pattern. Colocalisation between smoking-initiation/cessation credible sets and FEV1/FVC credible sets is only moderate (h4≈0.49-0.69), distinctly weaker than the FEV1-FVC intra-trait colocalisation.
  • Genetic constraint (gnomAD): LOEUF (oeUpper, lof) = 0.639 (oe=0.476; 32 observed vs 67.27 expected LoF variants) — moderate constraint, not in the most extreme gnomAD decile. Missense oe=0.875 (mild constraint). Synonymous oe=1.006 (neutral, as expected).
  • Genomic architecture (Ensembl REST API lookup, also a structured database lookup, not a citation): TMTC2 (chr12:82,686,880-83,134,870, GRCh38) does not physically overlap the 650-kb locus window (83,400,001-84,050,000) at all — its 3' end sits ~265 kb before the window starts, and the lead variant is ~440 kb from the gene body. The window itself contains zero protein-coding genes, only five uncharacterized lncRNA loci. TMTC2 is nonetheless the only protein-coding gene within ~1.5 Mb, which is why it is the only gene receiving Open Targets L2G weight (the L2G algorithm has no competing protein-coding gene to consider at this locus).

Prioritized gene

TMTC2 — Confidence: LOW.

TMTC2 is named as the candidate gene here essentially by elimination, not by direct mechanistic or gene-specific statistical support: it is the only protein-coding gene within ~1.5 Mb of a locus that is itself a gene desert (no protein-coding genes in the 650-kb window; only lncRNAs), and it does not physically overlap the locus at all (265-950 kb away). The strongest positive evidence is locus-level, not gene-specific: near-perfect FEV1/FVC colocalisation (h4>0.9, Open Targets summary above), consistent with this pipeline's prior #2 that spirometric concordance strengthens prioritization — this locus almost certainly harbors a genuine pulmonary-function-associated variant. However, as the red-team-skeptic emphasized, h4>0.9 colocalization establishes that the locus affects lung function; it does not establish that TMTC2 is the mediating gene. Against a TMTC2-specific mechanism: zero lung eQTLs/sQTLs in GTEx v8 despite robust thyroid/liver eQTLs (evidence table Open Targets/eQTL findings above); no PubMed-indexed COPD or spirometry GWAS names TMTC2 directly (though this may be a search-methodology gap rather than true absence, per the skeptic's note above); TMTC2's only established tissue-specific human disease phenotype is autosomal-dominant sensorineural hearing loss (PMID 27311106, 29671961, 38769084) via a distinct coding variant unrelated to this locus's noncoding credible set; and its known molecular function (ER O-mannosyltransferase glycosylating cadherin/protocadherin domains, PMID 28973932, 33436046) has no demonstrated lung-remodeling or airway-epithelial role — this remains an untested hypothesis, not a finding.

On this pipeline's prior #1 (smoking-mediation): the locus's lead variant also sits in smoking-initiation and smoking-cessation credible sets (L2G ≈0.57 each), but colocalisation between these smoking traits and FEV1/FVC is only moderate (h4≈0.49-0.69) — clearly weaker than the FEV1-FVC intra-trait colocalisation (h4>0.9). This evidence does not cleanly support either a pure smoking-behavior-mediated mechanism or a pure lung-intrinsic mechanism; it is ambiguous. Compounding this, the locus colocalizes broadly across numerous non-respiratory UK Biobank traits (educational attainment, occupational prestige, walking pace, frailty, age at first birth/sexual intercourse, CRP, WBC count, lipids, eGFR, glaucoma) and is recurrently identified in cross-species (cattle) GWAS/eQTL studies for body-size/conformation traits (PMID 40383388, 37165455) — a pattern the red-team-skeptic characterized as more consistent with a general socioeconomic/behavioral/constitutional pleiotropy hub (with well-known confounding risks — assortative mating, social stratification, smoking-correlated SES) than with COPD-specific pulmonary biology.

No credible alternative candidate gene emerged: SLC6A15 (a neuronal/renal amino-acid transporter ~825 kb-1.7 Mb from the credible set) was named only as the other gene flanking an unrelated, phenotypically distinct pediatric "atopic march" (eczema→asthma) GWAS signal (PMID 26542096) in the same broad intergenic interval; that paper does not identify a causal gene, and SLC6A15 receives no Open Targets L2G support at this locus. It is not a serious rival candidate on current evidence, only a noted absence-of-alternative.

The red-team-skeptic's formal verdict was WEAKENED (not CONTESTED, since no source argues against TMTC2 or substitutes a better-supported rival gene) — this pipeline's synthesis concurs: TMTC2 remains the best-supported single candidate by default (sole protein-coding gene in range, sole L2G recipient, and the only gene with a documented human organ-specific disease mechanism at all, even if that organ is the ear, not the lung), but confidence should be marked LOW, not moderate or high. What would resolve this: (1) a position-based (not gene-name) re-check of major published spirometry/COPD GWAS supplementary loci tables at chr12:83.4-84.4 Mb, since gene-desert loci are systematically missed by keyword search (skeptic's recommendation, not completed in this run); (2) lung/airway-epithelium-specific single-cell eQTL or chromatin-conformation (e.g., Hi-C/ABC) data connecting the credible-set region to TMTC2's promoter, since bulk-tissue GTEx lung shows no eQTL signal; (3) any functional study of TMTC2 in airway epithelial or immune cells, which does not currently exist in the literature reviewed.
