Prioritized-gene synthesis

Prioritized gene: C6orf118. Confidence: INSUFFICIENT.

This is a genuinely difficult, low-confidence call, and none of the three candidates clears a bar that would justify more than INSUFFICIENT/LOW confidence. The honest summary: no candidate gene at this locus has both credible locus-specific genetic evidence AND credible COPD-relevant biology.

  • C6orf118 is named the prioritized pick only because it is the locus file's designated nearest gene and holds Open Targets' highest (if locus-linkage-unconfirmed) gwas_credible_sets score of the three (0.583, row 1 of the Open Targets summary above). But its literature base is essentially empty — two off-target citations (an untested cilia hypothesis, and an incidental proximity hit in an unrelated tuberculosis GWAS on a different SNP; rows 1-2 of the evidence table), both flagged WEAKENED by the skeptic — and its gnomAD LoF o/e of ~0.99 indicates no evolutionary constraint, undercutting a haploinsufficiency-type mechanism. There is no FEV1/ FEV1-FVC concordance and no smoking-trait overlap for this gene or this locus at all.
  • Runner-up: PDE10A. Physically adjacent to C6orf118, with a comparable/lower Open Targets credible-set score (0.431 vs. 0.583) but more evolutionary constraint (LoF o/e ~0.26 vs. ~0.99) and two legitimate literature hits (Odimba 2023 sex-stratified COPD signal; Melén 2011 developmental expression) after the citation-verifier removed a third citation (Hernandez-Pacheco 2021) that was found to actually describe a different gene (ROBO2), not PDE10A — see the audit appendix. PDE10A's only well-characterized biology is CNS/striatal pharmacology, which is arguably more consistent with a smoking-behavior-mediated mechanism (per biological prior #1) than a direct pulmonary one, a tension the skeptic raised and that is not resolved by current evidence either way.
  • QKI was deliberately excluded from the top pick despite having by far the strongest and most mechanistically detailed COPD/emphysema literature of the three (ciliated-epithelial-cell scRNA-seq changes, a validated RNA-binding mechanism controlling alpha-1-antitrypsin, and a knockout-mouse emphysema phenotype — rows 5-7 of the evidence table). The skeptic explicitly marked the locus-to-QKI inference CONTESTED: QKI has zero Open Targets genetic evidence (gwas_credible_sets rows) for COPD, sits ~1.2Mb from the credible set, and its entire Open Targets COPD association score derives from literature-mining, not genetics. This is a case where strong general disease-gene biology should not be allowed to backfill an absent statistical-genetics link — the reasoning preserved verbatim in the audit appendix.

What would resolve this tie: colocalization or fine-mapping evidence (eQTL/pQTL/sQTL colocalization in relevant lung/airway tissue) directly linking the credible set at 6:164.87- 164.89Mb to expression of C6orf118, PDE10A, or QKI; independent replication of the Odimba 2023 PDE10A-COPD signal (ideally in a mixed-sex, smoking-adjusted analysis); confirmation of what the overlapping lncRNA (ENSG00000309021) does, if anything, in cis; and resolution of the unexplained GWAS-Catalog-zero-hits anomaly for rs6902631 itself, which currently leaves this locus's very existence outside the source meta-analysis unreplicated. Given all of this, the study team should treat "C6orf118" here as a nearest-gene placeholder pick rather than a biologically justified nomination, and should weigh PDE10A and QKI seriously as alternatives pending the colocalization work above.
