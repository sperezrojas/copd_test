Prioritized gene

Recommended candidate: PITX2 — Confidence: LOW.

Reasoning: Among the three candidates, PITX2 has the strongest and most repeated genetics-specific signal (5 Open Targets GWAS-credible-set evidence rows, scores 0.58-0.78, exceeding FAM13A's 0.631 and HHIP's 0.485 in Open Targets' own all-datasource COPD ranking), is under measurable negative selection consistent with dosage sensitivity (pLoF obs/exp 0.195), and has genuine, non-AF, non-cancer developmental lung biology (a documented, functionally validated requirement for PITX2 expression in distal-proximal patterning of human iPSC-derived lung progenitors — Banerjee et al. 2018, PMID 29143419). It is also the mechanistically privileged gene at this specific gene desert, since this is the well-established chr4q25 long-range PITX2 regulatory architecture — a much stronger prior than pure physical distance would suggest for either ENPEP or FAM241A.

However, this call is genuinely close and the confidence is capped at LOW, not MODERATE or HIGH, for reasons the red-team-skeptic laid out in detail and that are preserved verbatim in the audit file: (1) despite being an extensively studied gene, PITX2 does NOT rank in Open Targets' own top 50 all-datasource COPD-associated targets, meaning there is almost no non-genetic corroborating evidence for a PITX2-COPD link specifically; (2) none of the three credible-set variants show any GWAS Catalog association with FEV1, FEV1/FVC, or COPD case-control status directly — all three instead hit smoking-initiation/cessation/ behavioral-trait GWAS records, the textbook signature the pipeline's smoking-mediation prior warns about; (3) a verified bidirectional Mendelian-randomization paper (Naesilla/ Quint/Zuber 2026, PMID 41241414) that was cited in support of the AF-comorbidity angle in fact concludes the opposite of a naive shared-mechanism story — it finds limited direct genetic causality between COPD/CRD and AF/CVD, with smoking and shared inflammatory pathways (IL6R, IL1RN) as the better explanation for their epidemiological comorbidity; and (4) every PITX2-lung finding in hand is either embryonic/developmental (Banerjee) or lung-cancer-specific (Zhang, Wang, Soukup) — none address adult COPD-relevant pathophysiology (protease-antiprotease balance, emphysema, airway remodeling, or any COPD-relevant inflammatory program).

Runner-up: ENPEP, which is weaker on every axis assessed (single weak Open Targets evidence row, LoF-tolerant, no COPD-specific literature, and its own unresolved hypertension/blood-pressure pleiotropy alternative), and FAM241A/C4orf32, which despite being the "nearest gene" by linear distance has zero COPD genetic evidence of any kind and no established biological function beyond two unrelated lung-cancer bioinformatic correlation papers — its nearest-gene status is specifically undercut here by the well-characterized competing PITX2 long-range regulatory architecture at this same desert.

What would resolve this call: (a) direct confirmation (not available in this review) of whether the primary COPD/lung-function GWAS papers (e.g., Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019, Shrine et al. 2019/2023) report FEV1/FEV1-FVC associations at this exact credible set, independent of smoking-behavior GWAS overlap; (b) a COPD-specific (not AF-specific or cancer-specific) colocalization or eQTL/TWAS analysis in lung tissue directly implicating PITX2, ENPEP, or FAM241A; (c) resolution of whether the smoking-behavior GWAS Catalog hits at these three variants are genome-wide-significant, locus-defining associations or minor/incidental hits in a very large multi-trait study, which was not established in this review; (d) a lung-specific (not cardiac) enhancer/chromatin-conformation study confirming this desert regulates PITX2 (versus ENPEP or another gene) in lung tissue specifically, since the well-established long-range regulatory evidence in the literature is cardiac-tissue-specific, not lung-tissue-specific. The study team should weigh this recommendation accordingly and treat it as a starting hypothesis, not a settled call.
