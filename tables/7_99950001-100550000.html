Prioritized gene

PVRIG — confidence LOW.

This call is close, and the evidence genuinely does not cleanly distinguish PVRIG from PILRA or CNPY4; here is the reasoning and what would resolve it.

PVRIG has the only direct genetic-association/colocalization evidence for COPD and FEV1/FVC of any gene at this locus (Open Targets: L2G=0.269, h4=0.92 with FEV1/FVC, h4=0.91 with a COPD/emphysema/bronchitis GWAS — see Open Targets summary above), and — notably — this same credible set is h3-dominant (i.e., NOT colocalizing) against several Alzheimer's disease GWAS studies at the identical locus, which is a meaningful piece of evidence against the alternative "this is just AD-locus LD bleed-through" explanation. PVRIG also has a biologically coherent, if not lung-tissue-proven, mechanism: it is a TIGIT-family inhibitory checkpoint receptor on T/NK cells (PMID 34174928, PMID 41485718, both VERIFIED), directly relevant to prior #3's emphasis on CD8+ T-cell and heterogeneous inflammatory programs in COPD.

However, this is a LOW-confidence call for four explicit reasons, all preserved from the red-team-skeptic critique in audits/7_99950001-100550000_audit.md: (1) the colocalization evidence supporting PVRIG is an Open Targets database output that neither the researcher nor citation-verifier could locate as an independent peer-reviewed publication — it has not been verified as a citable literature claim, only reported as a structured database signal; (2) all verified PVRIG mechanistic literature is drawn from tumor immunology/immune-checkpoint-exhaustion research, not lung or airway-inflammation studies, so "PVRIG immune-checkpoint biology operates in the COPD-relevant lung immune compartment" is a plausible extrapolation, not something directly demonstrated; (3) 7q22.1 was tied for the most candidate genes (17) of any locus in the source COPD GWAS (PMID 30804561, VERIFIED) and is known to harbor at least one genuinely distinct, independently-signaled AD locus (ZCWPW1) plus an AD-relevant missense variant in PILRA — in such a gene-dense, multi-signal LD block, single-causal-variant colocalization methods (as used by Open Targets) can be unreliable; and (4) the red-team-skeptic's search budget was exhausted before it could independently confirm or refute the PVRIG colocalization claim in the literature — this is a genuine, disclosed gap, not a resolved question.

The runner-up is PILRA: it has the most literature of any gene at this locus and a well-characterized, gene-specific immune mechanism (neutrophil/monocyte inhibitory receptor, PMID 23142774 and PMID 26840635, both VERIFIED), plus a specific COPD-MR/colocalization claim (PMID 42089751, VERIFIED as existing) — but that specific claim directly contradicts the Open Targets pipeline (which shows coloc support only for PVRIG, not PILRA) and was not robustly replicated in a related independent MR study (PMID 41286868, VERIFIED). Per the red-team-skeptic, PILRA is CONTESTED rather than confirmed as the top candidate, largely because its strongest literature is anchored to the missense variant rs1859788 that is independently and extensively characterized as an Alzheimer's disease risk variant — a textbook shared-variant pleiotropy/LD confound that the evidence here cannot cleanly resolve. CNPY4 (the gene overlapping the second-highest-PIP credible-set variant, PIP=0.153) is a distant third: it has a real, verified innate-immune mechanism (TLR1/TLR4 surface-trafficking chaperone, PMID 16338228 and PMID 22447933, both VERIFIED) that is plausible for COPD's innate/neutrophilic axis, but no COPD-specific genetic or functional evidence, and its one apparently lung-relevant citation (sphingolipid homeostasis, PMID 41279395) is a preprint whose "alveolar/surfactant" relevance was the researcher's own speculative extrapolation, not a finding in the source itself.

What would resolve the tie: (a) an independent, peer-reviewed colocalization or fine-mapping analysis of PVRIG vs. PILRA vs. CNPY4 at this exact locus using multi-causal-variant methods (e.g., SuSiE-coloc) rather than single-causal-variant assumptions; (b) a direct test of whether PVRIG, PILRA, or CNPY4 expression/function is altered in COPD-relevant lung tissue (airway epithelium, alveolar macrophages, or lung-infiltrating T/NK cells) rather than tumor, blood, or non-lung tissue; (c) resolution of the direct contradiction between PMID 42089751 (PILRA COPD-asthma colocalization) and the Open Targets pipeline (no PILRA coloc, PVRIG coloc only); and (d) a locus-specific check of whether this signal (or component parts of the credible set) associates with smoking-initiation/cessation GWAS traits, which neither the researcher nor red-team-skeptic could confirm or rule out here. STAG3 (the nearest gene and lead-variant host), TFR2, FBXO24, GPC2, MBLAC1, ZCWPW1, ZNF3, TAF6, NYAP1, and CASTOR3P are all judged weak candidates on the current evidence (see table above and audit appendix) and are not competitive with PVRIG/PILRA/CNPY4 for this call.
