Prioritized gene

TTL — confidence: LOW (unchanged from the original review; POLR1B evaluated and found not to alter this call).

TTL is far and away the best-supported gene at this locus by every statistical-genetics measure available: it is the nearest gene, the ABC-prioritized gene, and — most importantly — the exclusive Locus-to-Gene (L2G) target at the fine-mapped lead variant rs3811038 across all 12 disease/trait GWAS credible sets present there, including the COPD credible set itself (L2G=0.861). Neither of the two alternative candidates evaluated — ANAPC1 (PoPS-prioritized) or POLR1B (surfaced by the corrected genome-build merge on reopening) — mounts a serious competing case:

  • ANAPC1 never receives an L2G score at this variant, does not rank COPD among its top associated diseases, and does not appear to physically overlap the stated locus window at all based on its Ensembl GRCh38 coordinates (independently confirmed by the orchestrator) — a ~465 kb gap. Red-team-skeptic marked ANAPC1 CONTESTED on both grounds (coordinate discrepancy and affirmatively negative genetic/literature evidence).
  • POLR1B, added on reopening after a corrected MAGMA/PoPS gene-location merge surfaced it as a candidate never before evaluated at this locus, also fails to mount a serious case, though via a distinct failure mode from ANAPC1's: its physical overlap with the locus window is real, but marginal and boundary-only (~8 kb of a ~38 kb gene, at the 5' end farthest from the credible-set variant); it receives a unanimous zero L2G score across all 33 credible sets tested at rs3811038; COPD does not appear anywhere in its full 223-disease Open Targets association profile (a stronger negative finding than ANAPC1's own low rank); its one well-established human disease role (Treacher Collins syndrome type 4) is a craniofacial/neural-crest developmental disorder whose documented respiratory phenotype (obstructive sleep apnea from micrognathia) is exclusively upper-airway/mechanical, anatomically and mechanistically distinct from COPD's lower-airway/parenchymal pathophysiology; and no immune/inflammatory or lung-ribosomopathy literature was found for it at all. Red-team-skeptic marked POLR1B CONTESTED and explicitly concluded that its "candidacy is a window/merge artifact rather than independent statistical-genetic evidence," driven by its tandem physical adjacency to TTL (~176 bp apart) rather than any evidence of its own. The skeptic explicitly assessed and rejected the possibility that POLR1B's addition creates a tie or lowers confidence to INSUFFICIENT: "the weaknesses are wildly asymmetric — TTL's problem is which pathway (smoking vs. lung) explains a real, strong signal, while POLR1B's problem is that it has no signal to explain."

This synthesis agrees with red-team-skeptic's assessment on both alternative candidates: this is not a close call between TTL and either ANAPC1 or POLR1B.

However, TTL's own case remains deliberately marked LOW confidence rather than HIGH, for a reason distinct from either alternative-candidate comparison: the COPD credible set at this variant colocalizes at essentially unity (h4~0.999) with 14 other credible sets, and every single one of them is a smoking-initiation, tobacco-use, nicotine-dependence, alcohol-consumption, externalizing-behavior, or educational-attainment trait — with zero colocalization with any spirometric trait, and no FEV1/FEV1-FVC credible set present at this variant in Open Targets at all. Per this pipeline's prior #1 (the COPD GWAS is unadjusted for smoking) and prior #2 (spirometric concordance strengthens a pulmonary case; its absence warrants considering alternative pathways), this is close to a textbook example of a locus whose COPD association is likely substantially or entirely a smoking-behavior signal rather than direct pulmonary pathobiology. Red-team-skeptic marked TTL WEAKENED (not CONTESTED) specifically because, despite this database pattern being internally consistent and compelling, the researcher could not locate any published paper that explicitly names TTL as the implicated gene in a smoking-behavior GWAS locus table (the two most likely candidate papers, Saunders et al. 2022 Nature and Brazel et al. 2018 Biological Psychiatry, were both VERIFIED as real, relevant GSCAN-family studies, but neither could be confirmed — from abstract/accessible full text — to specifically report this chr2:~112.5Mb locus or name TTL). TTL's own structural/developmental literature (TTL-null mouse phenotypes) is exclusively neuronal/CNS, with no direct evidence of a role in airway ciliary motility or lung structural biology despite TTL's enzymatic plausibility in the tubulin code (a paralog, TTLL1, does have a documented respiratory-ciliary phenotype, but the researcher and citation-verifier both explicitly confirmed this is a distinct gene and must not be conflated with TTL). No immune/inflammatory literature on TTL was found at all.

Bottom line: TTL remains the clear best pick among all three candidates evaluated at this locus (TTL, ANAPC1, POLR1B) — this is not a close call — but overall confidence that TTL represents a COPD-specific pulmonary mechanism (as opposed to a smoking-behavior/neurobiology signal that manifests in an unadjusted-for-smoking COPD GWAS) is LOW. The addition of POLR1B on reopening does not change this: red-team-skeptic explicitly evaluated whether POLR1B's addition should create a tie or lower confidence to INSUFFICIENT and concluded it should not, given the stark asymmetry between TTL's real-but-ambiguous-pathway signal and POLR1B's complete absence of any COPD-relevant signal at this locus. What would resolve the remaining TTL-specific uncertainty: (1) direct examination of the Saunders 2022 and/or primary GSCAN smoking-initiation GWAS supplementary loci tables to confirm whether TTL/this exact locus is explicitly reported there; (2) any functional or genetic study testing TTL directly (not the broader tyrosination pathway or TTLL-paralogs) in airway epithelium, cilia, or a smoking-independent lung phenotype; (3) a smoking-adjusted or smoking-stratified COPD GWAS re-analysis at this locus, which would directly test whether the COPD association survives conditioning on smoking behavior. A residual gap flagged by red-team-skeptic on both the original and reopened passes: its web search budget was exhausted before it could run independent contradiction-searches in either round, so both TTL's and POLR1B's contradicting-evidence coverage should be understood as unaudited by an independent search, not merely uncontested. Until then, this pipeline's best-supported recommendation remains TTL, held with LOW confidence and an explicit caveat that this locus may be primarily a smoking/nicotine-behavior locus rather than a lung-biology locus.
