Evidence Table - Locus 4:66100001-67250000 (nearest gene CENPC)

Candidate genes evaluated: CENPC (nearest gene; FLAMES-prioritized) and EPHA5 (PoPS-prioritized). MAGMA and ABC did not prioritize any gene at this locus. This is a large gene desert: no protein-coding gene overlaps the credible-set interval itself. CENPC's transcript (chr4:67,468,762-67,545,565) lies ~518 kb from the lead variant and is technically outside the locus's own stated upper boundary (67,250,000); EPHA5 (chr4:65,319,563-65,670,528) is ~1.28 Mb away, farther still. All ~45 credible-set variants are intergenic/MODIFIER impact (no missense/splice variants), so no dedicated variant-level literature search beyond the lead variant was required.

Only citations with a VERIFIED verdict are included below. See audits/4_66100001-67250000_audit.md for excluded/contested items and full reasoning.

  • L2G: CENPC is the only gene ever assigned a nonzero L2G score across all GWAS study-loci overlapping this locus (22 studies checked at the lead variant). COPD/chronic-airway-obstruction-specific L2G scores are low: 0.087 (GCST90476027, GCST90399695), 0.085 (GCST90480254), 0.074 (GCST90399694) — all well below Open Targets' own ~0.5 confidence threshold.
  • Colocalization (COPD study GCST90399695, credibleSet at rs13143549): strong colocalization (H4 0.87-0.98) with smoking initiation/heaviness ("ever smoker" H4=0.89, cigarettes/day H4=0.87-0.92), educational attainment, fluid intelligence, drinks per week, and lung cancer/family history of lung cancer, and with the other COPD/airway-obstruction GWAS themselves. No FEV1/FEV1-FVC/spirometric trait found among the 25 of 45 total colocalizing traits retrieved (remaining 20 not retrieved).
  • Overall target-disease association (all evidence types, genome-wide): top hit is "smoking initiation" (score 0.305); COPD does not appear in CENPC's top-25 associated-disease list.

Prioritized gene

CENPC — confidence LOW.

This is a close call between two weakly supported candidates, and the call should be read as low-confidence rather than a resolved answer. CENPC is favored over EPHA5 for three reasons visible in the table and Open Targets summary above: (1) it is the nearest protein-coding gene and the only FLAMES-prioritized candidate; (2) it is the only gene Open Targets' L2G algorithm ever nominates at any GWAS study-locus overlapping this region, including the COPD-specific studies (scores 0.074-0.087) — weak, but EPHA5 has zero L2G or colocalization support anywhere in Open Targets for this locus, a systematic null across all 16 of its independent credible sets; and (3) EPHA5 was explicitly marked CONTESTED by the red-team-skeptic, while CENPC was marked WEAKENED/borderline CONTESTED — a meaningfully less negative verdict.

That said, CENPC's own case is far from strong, and the skeptic's critique should weigh heavily on how this pick is used: CENPC's transcript sits outside the locus's own stated boundary in a genuine gene desert (a long-range, proximity-driven nearest-gene call); its COPD-specific L2G scores are well below Open Targets' own confidence threshold; there is no published literature placing CENPC in airway/alveolar structural biology or in any COPD-relevant immune program (the one candidate immunology citation, a 1998 CREST-antigen paper, was found on verification to be about CENP-A, not CENP-C, and is excluded — see audit); and, most importantly, this locus's COPD credible set colocalizes far more strongly with smoking initiation/heaviness and cognitive/behavioral traits than with any lung-function trait, and CENPC's own strongest genome-wide disease association is "smoking initiation," not COPD. The single most quantitatively substantial piece of evidence at this locus argues for a smoking/behavior-mediated mechanism (prior #1 in the pipeline's biological priors) rather than for CENPC acting through lung-intrinsic biology.

Runner-up: EPHA5 (PoPS-only, CONTESTED, no statistical or mechanistic support beyond tangential somatic-cancer literature).

What would resolve this: (a) direct confirmation of whether chr4:66.9-67.25 Mb appears among the loci reported in the Sakornsakolpat 2019 COPD GWAS (PMID 30804561) or the GSCAN 2019 smoking/tobacco GWAS (PMID 30643251) supplementary tables — neither could be confirmed from abstracts alone; (b) retrieval of the remaining 20 (of 45) colocalizing traits at this locus to rule out an undetected FEV1/FEV1-FVC signal; (c) a fresh independent literature/contradiction search for both genes and a re-check of the two EPHA5 somatic-mutation citations' broader context — the red-team-skeptic's search budget was exhausted before it could run its own contradiction-search pass on this locus, so this critique rests entirely on the researcher's original searches, the Open Targets pull, and one follow-up re-verification, not on an independent skeptic search; and (d) direct lung-tissue/airway-epithelium expression or functional data for CENPC, which is currently absent from the literature reviewed.
