Evidence table — locus 9:6,100,001-6,350,000 (nearest gene IL33)

Lead variant: rs2095044 (9:6,192,796, intergenic, VEP nearest gene GTF3AP1 — a processed pseudogene). Internal pipeline candidates: nearest gene = IL33; PoPS prioritized = IL33. Credible set is entirely non-coding (upstream/downstream/intronic/intergenic; no missense or splice variants), split between variants nearest the GTF3AP1 pseudogene and variants nearest/within IL33 itself. Only two protein-coding genes overlap the ~250kb locus window: IL33 and TPD52L3 (the latter at the extreme edge, unflagged by any prioritization method, no credible-set variant assigned, no lung-relevant literature — not carried into the table below as a genuine candidate).

IL33 (ENSG00000137033):

  • Genetic constraint (gnomAD): LoF obs/exp ≈ 1.14 (95% CI 0.777–1.684) — NOT constrained against loss-of-function. Missense oe ≈ 1.12, synonymous oe ≈ 1.19.
  • Target-disease association score for COPD (MONDO_0005002): 0.498 overall (genetic_association datatype 0.636, literature 0.962, animal_model 0.370, clinical 0.631) — ranks ~32nd of 6323 targets with any COPD evidence in Open Targets. For comparison, IL33's overall association score with asthma is 0.72 (genetic_association 0.92), and with allergic disease/allergic rhinitis/chronic rhinosinusitis with nasal polyps/wheezing it is 0.44–0.64 — i.e., IL33's asthma/allergic-disease signal is substantially stronger than its COPD signal by this metric.
  • Locus-to-gene (L2G) / colocalization pattern at lead variant rs2095044 (9_6192796_T_C): 111 credible sets indexed genome-wide at this position across all GWAS studies in Open Targets; IL33 is consistently the top L2G gene (scores 0.73–0.79) across nearly all of them. The overwhelming majority of these 111 studies are asthma, eosinophil count/ measurement, allergic rhinitis/hayfever, nasal polyps, chronic rhinosinusitis, wheeze, and asthma-medication-use traits. One study is a direct pulmonary-function GWAS: "Lung function (FEV1/FVC)" (GCST007080), IL33 L2G score 0.747. COPD-adjacent (not pure COPD case-control) phenotypes with IL33 as top L2G gene: FinnGen "Asthma/COPD" combined phenotype (score 0.781), "Chronic lower respiratory diseases" ICD J40-J47 (0.738–0.755), "Chronic bronchitis" (0.775), "Obstructive chronic bronchitis" (0.735–0.756). No pure "chronic obstructive pulmonary disease" case-control study appears among these 111 credible sets — the locus's COPD signal is only visible via composite/proxy phenotypes and via the separate disease-association score above, not via a dedicated COPD GWAS credible set with its own L2G run at this exact variant.

  • No data supporting GTF3AP1 as a functional disease gene; its nearest-gene VEP annotation for many credible-set variants most plausibly reflects proximity to IL33, not independent function.

Prioritized gene

IL33 — confidence: MODERATE.

IL33 is, with high confidence, the correct functionally-implicated gene at this locus relative to its physically-nearest alternative, GTF3AP1 (a processed pseudogene with zero disease associations, zero constraint data, and zero PubMed literature — see table row above and audit appendix). This part of the call is well-supported: PoPS prioritization, Open Targets L2G dominance across 111 indexed credible sets genome-wide at the lead variant (consistently ~0.73–0.79 for IL33), and an extensive, mechanistically coherent IL33 literature all converge, against a competing candidate with literally no evidence of its own. TPD52L3 is not a serious alternative (no prioritization signal, no credible-set variant, no literature).

Confidence is capped at MODERATE rather than HIGH, however, because the case for IL33 driving COPD-specific pathobiology (as opposed to a largely asthma/Th2/eosinophilic mechanism that spills into COPD case definitions and a genuine but narrower eosinophilic-COPD endotype) is genuinely contested, per the red-team-skeptic's critique:

  • The Open Targets L2G pattern at this exact variant is overwhelmingly asthma/allergy/ eosinophil-trait dominated; zero pure COPD case-control studies appear among the 111 indexed credible sets, and the best COPD-adjacent signal is a combined FinnGen "Asthma/COPD" phenotype — exactly the kind of comorbidity-conflated endpoint this pipeline's smoking/phenotyping prior warns about.
  • All three verified primary-literature spirometry studies (Ketelaar 2020, Li 2013, Tulah 2013) found NO IL33-FEV1/FEV1-FVC association, all within asthma cohorts; the only positive spirometric signal for IL33 is the Open Targets L2G score on a general- population lung-function GWAS, which is database evidence, not verified literature.
  • Candidate-gene COPD case-control literature is mixed-to-negative (one negative meta-analysis of 78 studies, one negative case-control study, one small unreplicated positive using a low-PIP credible-set SNP in a single ethnic group).
  • The single strongest COPD-specific genetic-plus-clinical evidence (Rabe et al. 2021, itepekimab) shows a real LOF/GOF dose-response with COPD risk in an independent exome dataset, but the phase 2a trial's all-comers primary endpoint failed, with benefit confined to former smokers only — a pattern that is at least as consistent with smoking-modulated IL-33 biology (cigarette smoke suppresses IL-33 release/expression, reversibly on cessation — Pace 2014, Faiz 2023) as with a smoking-independent COPD mechanism.
  • Mouse emphysema models directly disagree on IL-33's direction of effect (protective at homeostatic baseline per Morichika 2021; pathogenic during viral exacerbation per Gimenes 2019) — a genuine, unresolved contradiction rather than a settled mechanism.
  • The one originally-cited piece of direct ACO (asthma-COPD overlap) genetic evidence (Li et al. 2026) was found by citation-verifier to be a CLAIM_MISMATCH — the specific variant cited (rs2428305) is in KIAA1217, not IL33 — and has been removed from the evidence base entirely; it should not be read as supporting an IL33-ACO link.

Runner-up: none within this locus — GTF3AP1 and TPD52L3 are both essentially ruled out. The real "competing hypothesis" here is not a different gene but a different mechanism: IL33's association with COPD may be substantially attributable to (a) comorbid/overlapping asthma phenotyping in COPD GWAS cohorts (misclassification/ACO), and (b) a genuine but minority eosinophilic-COPD endotype that is itself Th2-skewed, rather than to a distinct, smoking-independent COPD structural/pulmonary mechanism. Evidence that would resolve this: a dedicated general-population (non-asthma-restricted, non-ACO) COPD GWAS with its own independent colocalization/L2G run at this variant, restricted to spirometrically-confirmed COPD without physician-diagnosed asthma; and a mechanistic study directly reconciling the opposite-direction emphysema mouse phenotypes (homeostatic-protective vs. exacerbation-pathogenic). Absent that, IL33 is the best-supported single gene call at this locus, but the specific claim "this locus drives COPD via IL33" should be qualified as "drives COPD-adjacent/eosinophilic-endotype/ACO-overlap phenotypes via IL33, with a smoking-history-dependent component," not treated as clean, COPD-specific causal biology.
