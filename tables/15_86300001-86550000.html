Prioritized gene

AGBL1 — LOW confidence.

This is a close call between two genes whose evidence sets point in different directions, and neither one clears a high bar. AGBL1 has the only positional/fine-mapping claim to this locus's actual credible set (all 89 variants are AGBL1 introns) and the only Open Targets COPD-disease-term evidence at this locus (L2G 0.68, association score 0.25). But that statistical case is weakened by several independent lines in the evidence table above: (1) the identical fine-mapped variant is equally the top L2G hit for diastolic blood pressure (Open Targets summary, unresolved cross-trait signal that no literature source could corroborate or refute — see researcher's rs1431229 lookup, zero PubMed/GWAS-Catalog hits); (2) AGBL1 has zero Open Targets evidence for FEV1 or FEV1/FVC, meaning no spirometric concordance despite Prior #2's expectation that a genuine pulmonary-biology signal should show this; (3) AGBL1's only prior track record as a nominated "disease gene" (Fuchs corneal dystrophy) was built on statistical/linkage evidence and has since been independently and repeatedly walked back on both genetic and expression grounds (PMIDs 37441688, 40244234, 26337789), which the red-team-skeptic explicitly flagged as relevant context for weighing a similarly statistical (fine-mapping/L2G) causal claim at a new disease; (4) no immune/inflammatory mechanism (Prior #3) and no airway/mucociliary mechanism has been established for AGBL1 despite biochemical plausibility (tubulin deglutamylation) — this remains an unsupported hypothesis, not a finding; (5) AGBL1-region variants (albeit different specific SNPs than this locus's credible set) recur across several vascular/hematologic trait associations (aPTT, carotid plaque), reinforcing a pattern of pleiotropy rather than lung-specific biology.

KLHL25 is the runner-up and cannot be ruled out: it has the strongest single quantitative signal in this locus's whole evidence base (FEV1/FVC L2G = 0.88, exceeding AGBL1's COPD L2G of 0.68) and a mechanistically well-supported immune function (CUL3-KLHL25-ACLY axis switching CD4+ T cells between iTreg and Th17 fates, PMID 34491895, PMID 40119138) that is thematically consonant with Prior #3's emphasis on T-cell program heterogeneity in COPD. But KLHL25's gene body sits 505-645 kb outside this locus's stated window and credible set, and it has zero Open Targets evidence for the COPD disease term itself — its prioritization rests entirely on MAGMA/PoPS (genome-wide statistical/similarity methods that can reach outside the fine-mapped interval) plus an FEV1/FVC signal whose underlying variant could not be confirmed (by either Open Targets or literature search) to be the same causal variant(s) as this locus's own credible set. Its immune mechanism, while real, was established in generic T-cell systems and lung-adenocarcinoma cell lines, never in COPD lung tissue or patients, and its only airway-correlative data are in allergic/atopic asthma (a distinct, largely Th2-skewed program) rather than COPD's more typical neutrophilic/Tc1/Tc17-dominated inflammation. KLHL25 is also demonstrably not immune-lineage-restricted (PMID 22578813, general translational-control role via 4E-BP1), further diluting the specificity of its immune story.

What would resolve this tie: (a) direct confirmation — e.g., colocalization analysis or shared credible-set membership — of whether KLHL25's FEV1/FVC association and this locus's COPD credible set share the same causal variant(s), rather than being two independent nearby signals; (b) an eQTL/chromatin-interaction (e.g., GTEx lung, Hi-C) check of whether this locus's credible-set variants regulate AGBL1, KLHL25, or a different gene entirely (per red-team-skeptic's alternative hypothesis that a large intronic region like AGBL1's could harbor a distal regulatory element acting on a non-adjacent gene, as seen at other GWAS loci); (c) a direct check of this locus's credible-set variants against smoking- initiation/cessation/heaviness GWAS summary statistics (not just published literature, which found nothing) to more rigorously exclude Prior #1; (d) COPD-specific (not FEV1/FVC-only) functional or expression evidence for KLHL25, or spirometry-specific evidence for AGBL1, to break the current spirometry/credible-set-overlap mismatch that is this locus's central finding. Given all of this, confidence is marked LOW rather than MODERATE or HIGH, and the study team should treat this as an open call between AGBL1 and KLHL25 (with a live possibility that neither is the true effector gene) rather than a settled recommendation.
