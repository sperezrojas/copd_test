Prioritized-gene synthesis

Prioritized gene: ADAM19. Confidence: MODERATE (downgraded from the researcher's initial "clear best-supported" framing after red-team critique).

ADAM19 has the best-supported genetic-association case of the five candidates at this locus: it is a genome-wide-significant original CHARGE spirometric GWAS locus (Hancock et al. 2010, PMID 20010835), carries by far the highest Open Targets composite association score (0.413) and L2G evidence (13 rows, up to 0.72) of any candidate gene here, and its credible set directly overlaps both FEV1/FEV1-FVC GWAS and COPD GWAS specifically partitioned into never-smoker and non-current-smoker strata (Open Targets structured lookup) — evidence against the association being purely a smoking-behavior/nicotine- pathway artifact (prior #1), consistent with prior #2 (spirometric concordance).

However, the red-team critique identified real overclaiming in the initial synthesis that this table's confidence rating accounts for: (1) ADAM19's own COPD case-control evidence is nominal/FDR-level, not genome-wide-significant (Wilk 2012, Castaldi 2011) — the genome-wide-significant signal is for a spirometric trait, not disease status; (2) the "convergent mechanistic evidence" cited for ADAM19 (cardiac developmental biology from Kurohara 2004; eQTL colocalization spanning esophagus/skin/testis/stomach/LCL/fat/muscle/ colon/breast but explicitly NOT lung tissue in the sampled data; immune evidence from muscle, kidney, and exploratory bioinformatic/rat-model sources) is analogical and non-pulmonary in the same way that was correctly used to downgrade TIMD4 — applying that standard evenhandedly, ADAM19's mechanistic case is weaker than the genetic-association tier alone suggests; (3) never-smoker stratification rules out a pure nicotine-behavior mechanism but does not establish a structural mechanism specifically, and sits awkwardly next to the two smoke-exposure-conditional expression findings (Vishweswaraiah 2018, Liu 2025) that were also cited in ADAM19's favor.

Most importantly, the red-team-skeptic flagged SGCD as a genuine rival, not a dismissible secondary candidate: SGCD is the only candidate gene at this locus with a confirmed LUNG-tissue eQTL (P=2.57e-9) and airway-smooth-muscle protein localization (Hansel et al. 2015, PMID 25514360) — tissue-specific evidence that ADAM19 itself lacks (its eQTL colocalization is entirely non-lung in the data reviewed here). SGCD's own statistical association is weaker in a different way (suggestive only, P 9.57e-8 to 4.6e-6, restricted to airway hyperresponsiveness within an all-smoker COPD cohort, so smoking- mediation is not excluded for SGCD either), and SGCD has zero Open Targets L2G evidence for COPD despite this. The 163-variant, 1.7 Mb-spanning credible set (with large ADAM19 and SGCD sub-clusters) is also consistent with broad, incompletely resolved LD across both genes rather than a single cleanly fine-mapped causal gene — a possibility the initial synthesis did not engage with.

Call: ADAM19 remains the single best-supported pick — it has a genome-wide-significant spirometric GWAS signal in its own right (SGCD does not), the largest and most convergent L2G/statistical evidence base, and its missense credible-set variant (rs1422795) explains most of the common-variant fine-mapping signal (London et al. 2014) — none of which SGCD can match. But this call is close, not clear-cut, specifically because SGCD offers tissue-specific (lung eQTL + airway-smooth-muscle) evidence ADAM19 lacks. Runner-up: SGCD. What would resolve the tie: a COPD/FEV1-specific lung-tissue eQTL or sQTL colocalization analysis for ADAM19 itself (not just the non-lung tissues sampled here), and/ or a formal statistical colocalization or conditional-analysis test distinguishing whether the ADAM19 and SGCD credible-set signals are independent or reflect a single shared LD block spanning both genes. TIMD4 (MAGMA-prioritized) is not competitive — it has zero L2G evidence and its entire evidentiary case is analogical efferocytosis biology from non-pulmonary tissues, consistent with a likely LD/gene-density artifact of the MAGMA gene-based test rather than a genuine independent signal. CYFIP2 and NIPAL4 are best regarded as bystander genes: both sit at the extreme low-PIP tail of the credible set (<0.001), neither has COPD-specific genetic evidence beyond one exploratory bioinformatic hit (CYFIP2), and both have well-characterized, respiratory-irrelevant Mendelian disease biology (severe neurodevelopmental disease for CYFIP2; congenital ichthyosis for NIPAL4).

This is the orchestrator's best-supported recommendation for the study team to weigh, not a final determination — the SGCD tissue-specificity finding in particular warrants follow-up before this locus's causal gene is considered settled.
