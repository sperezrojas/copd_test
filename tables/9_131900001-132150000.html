Prioritized gene

MED27 — confidence LOW.

MED27 is the only protein-coding gene whose body contains the PIP=0.99 credible-set variant rs9411339, and it is independently the top-ranked gene by Open Targets' own locus-to-gene (L2G) fine-mapping across all 43 GWAS/molecular-QTL studies with a credible set at this exact position — a strong, consistent statistical-genetics/positional case that MED27, specifically, is the gene this variant regulates. However, that is as far as the strong evidence goes. Neither Open Targets nor the researcher's targeted PubMed searches found any COPD or FEV1/FEV1-FVC association for MED27 (evidence table above, Open Targets summary), and no COPD/emphysema/airway-remodeling literature exists for this gene. The one piece of COPD-lung-tissue evidence (Shutta et al. 2025, PMID 41445629) is an unreviewed preprint in which MED27 is one of 24 exploratory differential-methylation genes, not a validated, gene-specific mechanism (see evidence table, "WEAKENED" verdict). MED27's only well-established human disease phenotype is an unrelated, recessive neurodevelopmental disorder with no pulmonary involvement (Yiliyaer 2025, Li 2025, Fazio 2025), and gnomAD shows MED27 is tolerant of loss-of-function (o/e 0.758) — arguing against a simple dosage-sensitivity mechanism at this common-variant GWAS locus.

The best-supported hypothesis for why this locus associates with COPD is that it acts through smoking-behavior biology rather than intrinsic pulmonary structure/immune biology (prior #1): the exact lead SNP is a credible-set member for "age of smoking initiation" in Open Targets, and an independent paper (Yan et al. 2024, PMID 38937676) names MED27 as a cross-trait pleiotropic gene linking GSCAN smoking-behavior traits to GERD. But per the red-team critique, this chain is circumstantial: the direct GWAS Catalog lookup for rs9411339 itself returns no smoking-trait association (only PUFA% and insomnia), and Yan et al.'s pleiotropic SNPs are not confirmed to be in LD with rs9411339. So the smoking-mediation explanation is plausible and consistent with the data pattern, but not proven.

The competing candidate, PPP2R4/PTPA (PoPS-prioritized), is CONTESTED and I do not recommend it: two independent coordinate lookups (mygene.info, Ensembl REST) agree its true GRCh38 location is ~2.76 Mb outside this locus, its hg19 coordinates suspiciously fall inside the stated GRCh38 window (a build-mismatch signature), it never ranks among Open Targets' top L2G genes here, and its only COPD "association" is a literature text-mining artifact with zero genetic support. Its real lung-epithelial biology (KRAS-mutant lung adenocarcinoma, TKI-resistant adenocarcinoma, mesothelioma, and a cystic-fibrosis FEV1-modifier signal) is either oncology-specific or from a different monogenic-disease context, and does not overcome the positional/L2G case against it belonging at this locus at all.

What would resolve this: (1) an LD check of Yan et al.'s pleiotropic SNPs (rs4382592, rs200968, rs1510719) against rs9411339 to confirm or refute the smoking-mediation chain; (2) a direct COPD or spirometry GWAS/colocalization analysis at this variant (none currently exists in Open Targets or the literature found); (3) confirmation/correction of the PPP2R4 genome-build issue in the upstream pipeline; (4) full-text confirmation of MED27's causal weight (versus incidental panel membership) in the Shutta et al. 2025 preprint once/if it is peer-reviewed. Given the strength of the positional/fine-mapping case for MED27 but the near-total absence of COPD- or lung-function-specific mechanistic support, and the circumstantial nature of the smoking-mediation explanation, confidence is LOW, not moderate or high.
