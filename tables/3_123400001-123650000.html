Prioritized gene

HACD2 — Confidence: LOW.

This is a close and genuinely unresolved call, and the two candidate genes' evidence bases point in different directions, as both the researcher and red-team-skeptic explicitly flagged.

HACD2 is favored on locus-resolution grounds: all 26 credible-set variants (lead PIP=0.433) map to HACD2, and Open Targets' own independent fine-mapping/L2G machinery — applied to different traits ingested at this exact physical position (UK Biobank body-fat-percentage GWAS, since OT has not ingested this project's COPD meta-analysis) — consistently and repeatedly ranks HACD2 far ahead of neighboring genes (L2G 0.61-0.76 vs. ~0.05-0.11 for ADCY5/PDIA5/CCDC14/ROPN1; see Open Targets summary above). MYLK, by contrast, has zero variant-level, eQTL, Hi-C, or L2G support at this locus in anything reviewed — its candidacy rests solely on FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS statistical gene-prioritization scores, and the red-team-skeptic's critique (see audit file) raises a specific, mechanistically grounded concern that such scores can be biased toward genes with rich pre-existing disease/pathway annotation (which MYLK has, via its asthma/ALI literature) independent of whether that gene is the true local regulatory target.

However, this recommendation is capped at LOW confidence, not higher, because:

  1. HACD2 has no COPD-specific, FEV1/FVC-specific, or verified smoking-related literature at all (evidence table above; the one smoking-initiation signal is an unverified, database-only claim the researcher could not confirm in PubMed — see audit). Its published biology (VLCFA/lipid metabolism, hematopoiesis, insulin signaling, cancer signaling) has no established connection to lung structure, function, or inflammation. The red-team-skeptic marked HACD2's disease-relevance case WEAKENED on exactly this basis, and further noted the L2G support being invoked is borrowed from an unrelated trait (adiposity) — raising a live alternative hypothesis that this locus could be an adiposity/metabolic locus whose apparent COPD association (if any) reflects a restrictive spirometric effect of obesity rather than obstructive lung biology.
  2. MYLK, while lacking locus-level support, has a real, verified, mechanistically coherent airway biology (myosin light chain kinase driving smooth-muscle contraction/airway hyperresponsiveness, plus verified asthma-genetics and acute-lung-injury-genetics literature) that HACD2 entirely lacks. The red-team-skeptic marked MYLK CONTESTED because this literature is asthma/ALI-specific (acute/allergic), not COPD-specific (chronic), and because of the L2G contradiction above — but it is not baseless.
  3. The candidate-gene list itself may be incomplete. Open Targets' own fine-mapping nominates ADCY5, PDIA5, CCDC14, and ROPN1 as alternative L2G candidates at this exact locus (for the non-COPD traits OT has ingested); none of these were evaluated by the researcher because they are outside the internal pipeline's candidate-gene list. The red-team-skeptic explicitly flagged this as a coverage gap, particularly for ADCY5 (a gene with an established, unverified-here, metabolic-trait GWAS literature that would be consistent with an adiposity-pleiotropy explanation for this locus).

Runner-up: MYLK. What would resolve the tie: (a) COPD- or FEV1/FVC-specific colocalization/eQTL analysis at this locus in lung tissue (airway epithelium, airway smooth muscle) rather than relying on adiposity-trait fine-mapping as a proxy; (b) a dedicated literature/functional pass on ADCY5, PDIA5, CCDC14, and ROPN1 before treating HACD2 vs. MYLK as the only two contenders; (c) independent confirmation (or refutation) of the smoking-initiation signal for HACD2 in primary GWAS literature (e.g., GSCAN/Liu et al. 2019), which the researcher could not locate; (d) any functional evidence of HACD2 activity in airway epithelium or surfactant-lipid metabolism, which is currently entirely absent from the literature. The study team should weigh this LOW-confidence call accordingly rather than treat it as settled.
