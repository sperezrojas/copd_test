Prioritized gene

Prioritized gene: MAP1LC3B Confidence: LOW

This call is close, and the evidence genuinely does not cleanly separate the three candidate genes — the honest answer is that this locus is unresolved between ZCCHC14 and MAP1LC3B, with FBXO31 a distant third.

Reasoning: MAP1LC3B has by far the deepest and most mechanistically coherent COPD-relevant literature of the three genes — direct mouse-knockout evidence that LC3B is required for cigarette-smoke-induced emphysema and ciliary/mucociliary dysfunction (Chen 2010, PMID 20956295; Lam 2013, PMID 24200693), a protease-antiprotease-linked autophagy mechanism (Hou 2014, PMID 24988221), the only finding in this entire locus's evidence base directly correlating with a spirometric trait, FEV1/FVC (Takasaka 2013, PMID 24367027), and a macrophage efferocytosis/LC3-associated-phagocytosis defect specifically documented in COPD patients (Asare 2021, PMID 34852704). This engages all three of the pipeline's biological-relevance categories (structural, protease- antiprotease, immune) more directly than either ZCCHC14 or FBXO31.

However — and this is the central reason confidence is LOW rather than MODERATE/HIGH — the red-team-skeptic identified a direct, unresolved tension that this synthesis will not paper over: the credible set's two highest-PIP variants (rs1050847, PIP=0.41, the lead variant; rs1050846, PIP=0.26 — together 67% of the credible-set mass) are ZCCHC14 3' UTR and synonymous variants, not MAP1LC3B variants. MAP1LC3B is linked to this locus only via ABC enhancer-promoter regulatory annotation and physical proximity, not by any verified eQTL or colocalization study tying credible-set variants to MAP1LC3B expression — this gap was explicitly searched for and not found. In other words: the gene with the top fine-mapped variants (ZCCHC14) has essentially no COPD-relevant biology, while the gene with the strongest COPD biology (MAP1LC3B) has the weakest direct genetic linkage to the credible set. Additionally, essentially all of MAP1LC3B's mechanistic literature was generated independently of, and predates, this GWAS signal — it establishes MAP1LC3B as smoke-responsive autophagy biology, which is also consistent with a smoking-behavior-mediated (rather than direct causal pulmonary) interpretation of the GWAS signal, since nearly every structural paper listed uses a cigarette-smoke-exposure model (per biological prior #1).

Runner-up: ZCCHC14, which carries the fine-mapping weight (the lead and 2nd-PIP variants) and a real, verified genetic association with a cerebrovascular trait cluster (lacunar stroke, PMID 33773637; intracerebral hemorrhage, PMID 31430377, 31646585) and a modest, imperfectly-matched nicotine-dependence GWAS hit (different SNPs than this locus's lead variant, PMID 22377092) — but zero pulmonary structural or COPD-immune literature, and a well-characterized molecular function (hepatitis A/B viral RNA-tailing cofactor) with no plausible bridge to airway biology. FBXO31 is not a serious contender: no COPD/lung-function genetic evidence, no COPD-specific structural or immune literature, and only a speculative-by-analogy connection (cell-cycle senescence) to COPD pathogenesis.

What would resolve this tie: (1) a colocalization or eQTL study directly linking the credible-set variants (or their LD proxies) to MAP1LC3B expression in lung/airway tissue — this is the single most decisive missing piece; (2) confirmation, via GWAS Catalog or the primary GSCAN/Saunders-type smoking-initiation GWAS supplementary tables, of whether ZCCHC14 and/or MAP1LC3B are genuinely named smoking-initiation loci (the Open Targets L2G scores for this trait are suggestive but currently rest on no verified primary citation — three attempts to confirm this against large modern alcohol/smoking GWAS papers returned CLAIM_MISMATCH); (3) any FEV1/FEV1-FVC GWAS, colocalization, TWAS, or MR study naming any of the three genes, which is currently completely absent from the evidence base — a significant gap per biological prior #2. The red-team-skeptic also disclosed that its own contradiction-search pass was not completed for any of the three genes (search-budget exhaustion) — see the audit file for this explicit limitation, which further supports treating this locus's prioritization as provisional pending additional targeted follow-up rather than final.
