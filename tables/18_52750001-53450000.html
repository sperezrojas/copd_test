Prioritized gene

Gene: DCC Confidence: LOW

This call is close, and confidence is capped at LOW because neither candidate has direct COPD or FEV1/FEV1-FVC evidence, and the largest dedicated COPD case-control GWAS (Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019, PMID 30804561) does not name either gene in its accessible full text — a live negative finding for both candidates, not merely an absence of positive support.

DCC is preferred over TCF4 primarily on genomic architecture, not mechanistic strength: DCC physically spans the entire 178-variant credible set and is the sole protein-coding gene overlapping the locus (Ensembl REST lookup, table row 1), whereas TCF4 sits ~1.77 Mb away and does not overlap the locus at all. The red-team-skeptic's critique (see audit appendix) raises a serious, unresolved possibility that TCF4's MAGMA/PoPS prioritization reflects a gene-assignment/LD-window artifact in this large 18q21 gene desert rather than a genuine long-range regulatory link — standard MAGMA/PoPS windows do not typically reach 1.77 Mb, and no independent second signal or enhancer-linkage evidence near TCF4 was found to support a real long-range mechanism. Absent that clarification, physical overlap is the more defensible default.

However, DCC's own mechanistic case is explicitly WEAKENED per the skeptic's critique (evidence table rows above): its lung-developmental and immune literature (Fearon 1990, Yokota 1991, Liu 2004, Bin 2015, Zhang 2018, Liu 2019, He 2014) almost entirely implicates the netrin-1 ligand or alternate receptors (UNC5B, neogenin, A2BAR), not the DCC receptor itself, in lung or immune biology. DCC's most plausible mechanistic story — smoking-related neurobehavioral genetics via its axon-guidance role — rests on a circular Open Targets association ranking (itself derived from other studies' own gene-assignment heuristics) plus two smoking-behavior GWAS (Liu 2019, Brazel 2018) that the researcher could not confirm actually name DCC/NTN1. This should be read as "smoking-behavior-plausible, lung-mechanism-absent," not as confirmed smoking mediation.

Runner-up: TCF4, marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic. TCF4 has a more specific and mechanistically well-characterized immune-biology story (essential, non-redundant regulator of plasmacytoid dendritic cell development and type-I interferon response, Cisse et al. 2008, PMID 18854153) and some direct lung/immune molecular-QTL evidence that DCC entirely lacks (2 GTEx lung transcript-usage QTLs, 3 stimulated-monocyte eQTLs, versus zero lung QTLs for DCC). Its single COPD-relevant statistical link (Tu et al. 2026, PMID 42653451) is a very recent, unreplicated, non-COPD-specific composite-liability regulon finding that neither confirms nor is contradicted by other evidence, and TCF4's dominant Mendelian/common disease burden (Pitt-Hopkins syndrome, schizophrenia, Fuchs corneal dystrophy) is mechanistically unrelated to COPD.

What would resolve the tie: (1) confirmation of the actual MAGMA/PoPS gene-assignment window used in this pipeline, to determine whether TCF4's 1.77 Mb prioritization is methodologically expected or an artifact; (2) a colocalization or fine-mapping analysis specifically testing whether this locus's COPD credible set colocalizes with TCF4's lung transcript-usage QTLs or monocyte eQTLs (versus any DCC signal); (3) independent replication of the Tu et al. 2026 TCF4-regulon/gAirwayDisease finding in a COPD-specific (not composite) analysis; (4) a targeted search of any post-2019 COPD/spirometry GWAS (the skeptic could not complete this due to search-budget exhaustion — see audit appendix) to check whether a more recent, larger study has reassigned this locus to a third gene. Until then, this locus should be treated as genuinely ambiguous between a physically-overlapping gene with a weak/circular mechanistic story (DCC) and a statistically-flagged, physically-distant gene with a more specific but so-far COPD-unconnected mechanistic story (TCF4).
