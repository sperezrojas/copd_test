Prioritized gene

JCAD -- MODERATE confidence.

The weight of independently-gathered, verified evidence favors JCAD over SVIL at this locus, on several converging lines: (1) JCAD physically sits adjacent to the credible-set span (several credible-set variants annotated downstream_gene_variant relative to JCAD), whereas SVIL's gene body does not physically overlap the stated locus window at all -- its nearest edge is >112 kb before the window starts and >210 kb from the credible-set variant span, a fact the researcher established independently via Ensembl REST and that the skeptic agreed raises a real possibility that SVIL's FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS prioritization is a statistical/window artifact rather than a genuine gene call, with no chromatin-interaction (Hi-C/ABC-model) evidence found to rescue a long-range-regulation explanation; (2) Open Targets' L2G algorithm consistently and substantially favors JCAD (L2G 0.56-0.64) over SVIL (L2G 0.05-0.09, or absent) across every overlapping credible set for quantitative pulmonary and blood-cell traits; (3) a same-locus, human, smoker-cohort GWAS of CT-quantified emphysema distribution (Boueiz et al. 2017, PMID 27669027) independently implicates KIAA1462/JCAD via a variant (rs35374984) effectively coincident with this locus's credible set; and (4) a direct functional COPD paper (Suraya et al. 2024, PMID 39273437) shows Jcad/Kiaa1462 knockout mice are protected from elastase-induced emphysema and lung inflammation, with altered TNF-alpha-driven inflammatory gene expression in pulmonary endothelial and epithelial cells, spanning both the structural (prior #2, pulmonary-function/imaging concordance) and immune (prior #3) domains laid out in this pipeline's biological priors.

Confidence is capped at MODERATE, not HIGH, for several reasons the red-team-skeptic raised and that are not resolved by the evidence gathered: (a) no COPD case-control genetic association is indexed for JCAD anywhere (Open Targets or the literature found) -- the genetic case rests entirely on quantitative pulmonary-function/imaging traits and colocalization, not a direct COPD diagnosis-based GWAS hit; (b) directionality is unconfirmed -- the Suraya et al. knockout/silencing study establishes that JCAD loss is protective in mice, but no eQTL/pQTL evidence ties the human COPD-risk allele at this locus to reduced (rather than increased) JCAD activity, so this citation is mechanistically supportive, not confirmatory of the GWAS direction; (c) the large majority of JCAD's mechanistic literature (7 of the 12 JCAD-specific citations above) is drawn from cardiovascular/coronary-artery-disease contexts, not pulmonary tissue, so the extrapolation to lung endothelial biology, while biologically plausible and partly corroborated by Suraya et al., is an extrapolation; and (d) strong locus-level colocalization with white blood cell count, monocyte count, and platelet count (h4 up to 0.98, same credible set as the FEV1/FVC-ratio signal) suggests this locus's biology may be a broadly pleiotropic vascular/inflammatory mechanism rather than a COPD- or lung-specific one -- consistent with, but not proof of, JCAD's known role in endothelial-monocyte adhesion.

Runner-up: SVIL, CONTESTED (see audit appendix) -- retained as the internal pipeline's statistical call, but its candidacy is substantially undercut by the physical-overlap discrepancy and consistently weak L2G/disease-association scores, and its literature support is uniformly off-tissue (cardiac muscle, skeletal muscle, colorectal cancer, one rare-disease cell line) or underpowered/off-target for this specific locus (nominal, uncorrected, different SNP; and a platelet-thrombosis SNP pair ~420 kb away from this credible set, excluded entirely -- see audit).

What would resolve the tie / raise confidence: (i) an eQTL or pQTL study establishing that the COPD-GWAS risk allele at rs4747692 (or its high-LD proxies) is associated with reduced JCAD expression/activity in lung tissue, matching the direction implied by the Suraya et al. knockout phenotype; (ii) a directly indexed COPD case-control GWAS/L2G call at this locus (currently absent from Open Targets); (iii) chromatin-interaction data (Hi-C, ABC model, or eQTL) specifically testing whether the credible-set region contacts the SVIL promoter despite the physical gap, to either rescue or further undercut SVIL's candidacy. Absent that, this is the orchestrator's best-supported recommendation for the study team to weigh, not a final determination.
