Prioritized gene

PENK — confidence LOW.

PENK is named here as the best-supported single pick, but this call is close and should be read as a plausibility ranking rather than a confirmed causal-gene call, per the red-team-skeptic's critique above. The case for PENK rests almost entirely on (1) physical proximity — the 150kb locus window itself contains no protein-coding genes, and PENK is the nearest one, immediately adjacent/upstream — and (2) biological plausibility, since PENK encodes proenkephalin, an opioid-neuropeptide precursor with well-established roles in CNS reward/addiction circuitry (row: PMID 21966993, rodent) and in T-cell/Treg-derived tissue-repair biology in other organs (rows: PMID 24055279, 32769209, 40359090), consistent with pipeline prior #1 (a smoking/substance-use-mediated mechanism) rather than prior #2 (direct pulmonary biology, for which there is zero supporting evidence anywhere in this review — see Open Targets summary above and the negative GWAS-literature checks, rows PMID 30643251 and 20418890).

Critically, per the skeptic's critique, the strongest piece of literature that would have made this a real human-genetics case for PENK — the Saunders et al. 2022 alcohol-consumption (DrnkWk) fine-mapped/TWAS locus, apparently describing BAM-12/BAM-22-generating biology — failed gene-identity verification and is excluded. What remains is an Open Targets database-level smoking-initiation score of uncertain provenance (not confirmed in any accessible primary paper naming PENK) plus non-lung immune/neurobiology plausibility. As the skeptic put it, this locus is better framed as "prior #2 has zero support and prior #1 has weak, mostly unconfirmed support" rather than as a well-evidenced smoking-mediated locus.

Runner-up: XKR4 (PoPS-prioritized) cannot be ruled out, but sits ~0.9-1.4 Mb from the locus/credible set, with eight intervening protein-coding genes (PLAG1, LYN, CHCHD7, TGS1, MOS, RPS20, SDR16C5, TMEM68) that were never individually screened against XKR4 in this review (an open thoroughness gap flagged by the skeptic). XKR4 has zero direct COPD/smoking/lung-function evidence of any kind (Open Targets or primary literature, despite a deliberate, thorough attempt to confirm a suspected smoking-GWAS link), and its best-supported biology — apoptotic-cell scramblase/efferocytosis — is only plausibly, not demonstrably, connected to the independently real efferocytosis-COPD literature (rows PMID 23637858, 32004356), since XKR4 itself was never tested in a COPD/lung model.

LINC00968 is the gene that physically contains both fine-mapped credible-set variants (PIP 0.43 and 0.29), but has no genetic-association evidence for any COPD-relevant trait and no reliable COPD-relevant functional literature of its own (its cancer-biology literature is oncology-biomarker work, partly retracted/flagged — see audit appendix). The researcher's assumption that LINC00968 is merely "a transcript body incidentally containing regulatory elements near PENK" is, per the skeptic, not itself demonstrated — no ABC/Hi-C/eQTL/colocalization evidence was presented in this review specifically linking the LINC00968-resident credible-set variants to PENK regulatory elements. This is exactly the kind of nearest-gene/LD-bias concern this pipeline is designed to surface, and it remains open.

What would resolve the tie: (1) confirmation (e.g., from GWAS Catalog or the Saunders et al. 2022 supplementary tables directly) of whether PENK is indeed the gene fine-mapped/TWAS'd to the DrnkWk phenotype, and whether XKR4 or PENK appear in the GSCAN/Saunders supplementary loci tables at all; (2) an explicit ABC/Hi-C/eQTL/colocalization analysis linking the LINC00968-resident credible-set variants (rs1866823, rs2246873) specifically to a PENK (or XKR4) regulatory element, rather than assuming this by elimination; (3) any direct FEV1/FEV1-FVC/COPD case-control genetic association for PENK, XKR4, or LINC00968, none of which currently exists in any source consulted. Until then, confidence in PENK as the causal gene at this locus should remain LOW, and the study team should weigh the intervening-gene and credible-set-linkage gaps noted above before treating this as resolved.
