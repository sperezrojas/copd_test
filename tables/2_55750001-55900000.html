Open Targets summary (structured database lookup — NOT a verified literature citation)

This section is a structured database lookup via the Open Targets Platform GraphQL API. It did not go through citation-verifier or red-team-skeptic and must not be treated as literature evidence.

EFEMP1 (ENSG00000115380):

  • L2G, COPD case-control: 0.36 at two independent COPD GWAS credible sets (GCST90399695, GCST90399693); 0.73 at a third credible set from an MTAG study combining COPD with diverticular disease (GCST90570622).
  • L2G, FEV1/FVC ratio: 0.69–0.78 across 5 independent studies.
  • L2G, FEV1 (absolute): 0.87–0.91 across >=10 independent studies/credible sets — the highest and most consistent L2G seen for this gene in any trait queried.
  • Colocalization: the COPD credible set colocalizes (h4~0.99) with FEV1 GWAS signals, plus diastolic blood pressure, pulse pressure, diverticulosis, inguinal/ventral/diaphragmatic hernia, female genital prolapse, and age-related eyesight deterioration. No colocalization with smoking-behavior GWAS (smoking initiation, cigarettes/day) among top ~25 colocalizing traits.
  • Broader disease-association profile: dominated by connective-tissue/ECM phenotypes (hernia, open-angle glaucoma, Doyne honeycomb retinal dystrophy, cutis laxa type 1D, familial drusen, myopia, diverticular disease, pelvic organ prolapse, atrial fibrillation, scoliosis).
  • Genetic constraint (gnomAD): LoF oe = 0.248 (90% CI 0.169–0.373, moderately-to-strongly constrained); missense oe = 0.710; synonymous oe = 1.015 (neutral, as expected).

PNPT1 (ENSG00000138035):

  • L2G, COPD: 0.086 at the same two credible sets (~4x lower than EFEMP1).
  • L2G, FEV1/FVC: 0.05–0.13 across 3 studies (substantially lower than EFEMP1).
  • Genetic constraint: LoF oe = 0.490 (CI 0.378–0.640) — less constrained than EFEMP1; missense oe = 0.971 (near-neutral); synonymous oe = 1.096 (neutral).
  • Lies ~117kb from the lead variant (2:55,634,061-55,693,863), entirely outside the stated locus window (2:55,750,001-55,900,000) per Ensembl coordinates.

Prioritized gene

EFEMP1 — Confidence: MODERATE.

EFEMP1 is the best-supported candidate at this locus, but confidence is capped at MODERATE (not HIGH) because the red-team-skeptic's critique identified real gaps between the verified-literature evidence base and the stronger unverified database signal, and because no independent contradiction-search pass was possible for this locus (search budget was exhausted before the skeptic could run one — see audit appendix).

Reasoning:

  • EFEMP1 is the sole gene overlapping the stated locus window; PNPT1, the only other gene with a non-trivial Open Targets L2G score at these credible sets, lies entirely outside the window (~117kb from the lead variant) and scores 4-9x lower on every L2G/constraint metric queried (Open Targets summary above), and has zero COPD- or lung-specific literature (evidence table PNPT1 rows) — its only mechanistically interesting biology (mitochondrial dsRNA/MDA5/interferon signaling) is explicitly speculative and studied only in rare monogenic disease and cancer cell lines, never in lung/COPD.
  • EFEMP1 encodes fibulin-3, an ECM/elastic-fiber structural protein with an extensive, verified body of structural/developmental biology (evidence table rows 5-13): knockout- mouse elastic-fiber loss and herniation, human Mendelian connective-tissue disease from EFEMP1 loss-of-function, direct MMP-12 substrate status (MMP-12 being the classic cigarette-smoke emphysema protease), and one direct human autopsy case with pulmonary emphysema alongside other connective-tissue pathology.
  • However, per the skeptic's critique, the verified-literature statistical-genetics case for a COPD/obstruction-specific (rather than FVC-volume, hernia, or composite-phenotype) mechanism is thinner than it first appears: none of the four verified GWAS citations is a direct COPD-case/control or FEV1/FEV1FVC hit, and the much stronger, more consistent FEV1/FEV1FVC L2G and COPD-FEV1 colocalization evidence lives only in the unverified Open Targets database layer, not in the verified literature. The n=1 human emphysema autopsy case is confounded by co-occurring systemic connective-tissue disease in that patient, and a genuine (if tissue-mismatched) directional contradiction exists in mouse retinal data (Stanton et al. 2017: Efemp1 loss was protective against smoke-induced pathology in that tissue).
  • No colocalization with smoking-behavior GWAS was observed (Open Targets), and no literature directly ties EFEMP1 or PNPT1 to smoking initiation/nicotine dependence — arguing against prior #1 (smoking-mediated confounding) at this locus specifically. The Nyberg et al. 2026 smoker-stratified emphysema signal instead suggests a gene-by-smoke- exposure interaction (structural fragility manifesting under smoke exposure), consistent with prior #2 (pulmonary-function evidence) rather than prior #1.
  • Immune/inflammatory evidence (prior #3) is genuinely weak/absent for EFEMP1 — this locus's most plausible mechanism is structural (elastic-fiber/connective-tissue integrity), not classic COPD inflammatory-program biology, which is itself a meaningful and specific finding, not a gap.

No runner-up gene is credibly competitive given PNPT1's physical location outside the locus window and its markedly weaker L2G/literature support. The tie here is not between two genes but between two levels of evidence for the same gene (verified literature vs. unverified database) — what would raise confidence to HIGH: (1) a COPD case-control or direct FEV1/FEV1FVC GWAS hit for EFEMP1 published as a primary, peer-reviewed literature finding (not just database L2G), (2) fine-mapping/eQTL work in lung tissue at rs75550771 that directly distinguishes EFEMP1 from neighboring genes, and (3) an independent contradiction-search pass (not completed this run due to search-budget exhaustion).
