Prioritized gene

Prioritized gene: LINC01865 — Confidence: LOW / INSUFFICIENT

This call is close, and it is being made for structural rather than functional reasons — the study team should weigh it accordingly.

Why LINC01865 over TMEM18: LINC01865 is the only candidate gene that physically overlaps this GWAS locus and its fine-mapped credible set. It contains the lead variant (rs62106258, PIP=0.351) and the second-highest-PIP variant (rs62107261, PIP=0.224) — i.e., credible-set mass totaling ~0.575 posterior probability sits inside this gene's transcript. TMEM18, by contrast, lies 110-240kb outside the queried locus interval entirely (Open Targets genomic location; confirmed independently by the researcher's Ensembl REST API query), has no locus-specific L2G or colocalization evidence in Open Targets, and — critically — every citation that could have established a genome-wide significant TMEM18/chr2p25.3 hit in the primary COPD case-control GWAS (Sakornsakolpat 2019), the primary lung-function GWAS (Shrine 2019), or the primary smoking-behavior GWAS (GSCAN/Liu 2019) came back CLAIM_MISMATCH from citation-verifier (see evidence table row citations and audit appendix) — the claimed locus-level link could not be substantiated from accessible text in any of the three papers most likely to carry it. TMEM18's only VERIFIED COPD-adjacent citation (Çolak et al. 2016) is a Mendelian-randomization-style analysis of exacerbation/pneumonia risk in patients who already have COPD, using TMEM18 as one of three SNPs in a composite BMI genetic instrument — this is evidence about a BMI-mediated modifier of COPD severity, not about COPD susceptibility or an intrinsic pulmonary mechanism, and red-team-skeptic flagged the broader BMI-mediated causal narrative built on this single paper as overclaimed relative to what it actually shows. Open Targets independently corroborates this gap: TMEM18 has no genetic_association datatype score for COPD and no association with any spirometric trait (FEV1, FEV1/FVC) at all in its aggregated evidence.

Why confidence is only LOW/INSUFFICIENT, not higher, despite naming LINC01865: LINC01865 has literally zero PubMed literature characterizing its expression, function, or disease association, and zero Open Targets disease-association or constraint data of any kind (both are true absences, not negative findings, per pipeline conventions — but they mean there is no positive functional evidence supporting LINC01865 either). The lead variant's PIP of 0.351 means the credible set itself is not tightly resolved — roughly 65% of posterior probability lies outside the single highest-PIP variant, and even considering all three LINC01865-overlapping variants together, meaningful fine-mapping uncertainty remains (the fourth credible-set variant, rs77165542, PIP=0.264, is intergenic and falls just outside LINC01865's boundary). Red-team-skeptic explicitly flagged TMEM18 as CONTESTED rather than ruled out, and raised a real alternative hypothesis that this locus's COPD association may currently be biologically unexplained altogether — a fine-mapped signal in a gene desert of uncharacterized lncRNAs, with TMEM18 reached for by proximity/prior fame (a streetlight-effect / availability-bias pattern) rather than by genuine locus-specific evidence.

Runner-up: TMEM18. If future data (see below) established genuine colocalization or a verified locus-level GWAS hit linking this exact signal to TMEM18 (via smoking-behavior or lung-function biology, or via long-range regulatory action of the LINC01865/lncRNA region on TMEM18 expression), TMEM18's much richer mechanistic literature (adipocyte differentiation, CNS/neuronal body-weight regulation, replicated obesity GWAS locus) would become directly relevant and could shift this call.

LOC124905962 is not treated as a competing candidate: it does not resolve to any Ensembl gene ID, has no literature, and most plausibly represents a RefSeq-only annotation of the same lncRNA-dense interval already covered by LINC01865 and its neighboring unnamed transcripts (per researcher's Ensembl region query) — an annotation artifact rather than an independently evidenced gene.

What would resolve this tie: (1) A lung-tissue or whole-blood eQTL colocalization (coloc/SMR) analysis testing whether this credible set's association signal colocalizes with LINC01865 or TMEM18 expression specifically; (2) direct extraction of summary statistics for rs62106258 (or LD proxies) from the Shrine 2019 lung-function GWAS, Sakornsakolpat 2019 COPD GWAS, and GSCAN 2019 smoking-initiation GWAS to determine, without relying on secondary citation claims, whether this locus is genome-wide significant in any of those three studies; (3) any functional/expression characterization of LINC01865 itself (e.g., tissue expression atlas data, CRISPRi screens, or enhancer-RNA evidence) that current PubMed-indexed literature does not yet provide.
