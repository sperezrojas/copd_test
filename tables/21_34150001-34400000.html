Prioritized gene

KCNE2 — confidence: LOW.

This is a genuinely close call between KCNE2 and LINC00310, and the evidence does not cleanly resolve it. LINC00310 hosts the lead variant and the overwhelming majority of fine-mapping PIP mass, which is the single strongest quantitative signal at this locus for any gene — but LINC00310 has no COPD-relevant functional characterization whatsoever: its only direct COPD co-occurrence is a peripheral (quadriceps muscle, not lung) transcriptomic correlation in a list of 18 genes (Willis-Owen et al. 2018), and its other literature is generic pan-cancer bioinformatics and cardiomyopathy ceRNA correlation, none of it lung-mechanistic.

KCNE2, by contrast, has two independent lines of genuinely pulmonary-relevant evidence not available for any other gene at this locus: an independent human spirometry GWAS hit (FEV1/FEV1-FVC, Soler Artigas et al. 2011 — direct pulmonary-function evidence per this pipeline's priors, not just COPD case-control) and a causal mouse-genetics experiment showing a specific lung phenotype on Kcne2 loss (Zhou et al. 2019), including a stated human lung-function association motivating that work. No other gene at this locus has comparable direct functional pulmonary evidence. This tips the balance toward KCNE2 over LINC00310 despite KCNE2 carrying almost no fine-mapping PIP mass itself.

However, confidence must be capped at LOW, not MODERATE, for reasons the red-team-skeptic raised and that are not resolved by any evidence in hand: (1) the Zhou et al. mouse model is a whole-body knockout, so the lung phenotype is at least as consistent with being secondary to KCNE2's well-documented cardiac/circulatory role as with a cell-intrinsic lung mechanism, and no colocalization or cell-type-specific data rules this out; (2) Open Targets' own COPD association score for KCNE2 is very low (0.013) against a strong cardiac-arrhythmia signature, and the field's own gene-disease curation now rates KCNE2 as "Disputed" even for its classic LQTS role, undermining confidence in KCNE2 causal claims generally; (3) three independent GWAS signals for vascular/arteriopathy phenotypes (CAD/carotid-IMT via Sabater-Lleal 2014; SCAD via Turley 2020 and Saw 2020, the latter two nominally attributed to LINC00310 but at the same cytogenetic band) raise a serious, unresolved alternative hypothesis that this locus is fundamentally cardiovascular/vascular rather than airway-intrinsic, with the COPD signal reflecting pleiotropy, pulmonary-vascular subphenotype effects, or LD-tagging; and (4) whether this locus's COPD association is smoking-mediated could not be determined in either the researcher's or the red-team-skeptic's searches (both hit search-tool budget limits), so this remains a fully open question, not a ruled-out one.

Runner-up: LINC00310 (on fine-mapping/positional grounds alone). C21orf62/EPCIP is excluded from serious consideration — its Open Targets QTL footprint sits outside this locus's actual credible-set window, it has zero direct functional validation in any paper identified, and its only characterized biology (renal ciliary/polycystin-1 interaction) is unrelated to lung. What would resolve the KCNE2-vs-LINC00310 tie: a COPD- or lung-function-specific colocalization or eQTL analysis at this locus (which Open Targets currently lacks), cell-type-resolved (airway epithelial vs. cardiac/vascular) expression/functional data for KCNE2, and a direct smoking-behavior-GWAS lookup for rs62213732 and its LD proxies.
