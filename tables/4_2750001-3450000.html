At the lead variant (rs76034781), across 33 non-COPD/non-lung GWAS credible sets, MSANTD1 is consistently a secondary L2G gene (~0.05-0.11) behind HTT (~0.12-0.90); no COPD or spirometry credible set was found anchored at this exact variant.

Not the top L2G gene in any credible set reviewed at its own region variant (rs16843629); GRK4 typically dominates there for lipid/blood-pressure traits, NOP14 usually second (0.15-0.45).
  • ADD1 (ENSG00000087274): LOF o/e = 0.273 (0.196-0.387, clearly LOF-constrained). COPD association score 0.039 (genetic_association 0.064 only). Prioritized gene

GRK4 — confidence LOW.

This call is close, and the underlying evidence does not cleanly separate GRK4 from ADD1 or HTT; readers should treat this as the current best-supported working hypothesis, not a resolved determination.

GRK4 is the only candidate gene at this locus with a directly on-point, gene-specific published statistical-genetics finding for COPD: a 2024 cross-tissue TWAS + FUSION + MAGMA + SMR + MR + colocalization study (PMID 39558015) that names GRK4 as a COPD susceptibility gene using a large FinnGen COPD GWAS and validates the finding in an independent COPD GWAS Catalog dataset. This converges with the Open Targets structured-data layer, where GRK4 is (a) the top L2G gene in the single FinnGen COPD credible set identified in this locus, narrowly ahead of ADD1 and SLC75A1, and (b) has the highest FEV1 genetic_association score of any of the 7 candidates — giving GRK4 the best concordance between a COPD case-control signal and a pulmonary-function trait among the genes reviewed here, consistent with prior #2 in the pipeline instructions.

However, this recommendation should be held with real caution, exactly as the red-team-skeptic flagged: (1) the TWAS finding is a single computational study without orthogonal functional validation; (2) the paper's proposed "inflammatory" mechanism is GeneMANIA network-inference speculation, not experimental immune-cell data, so it should not be read as evidence of a specific inflammatory program (neutrophilic, Th17, macrophage, etc.) — GRK4's COPD relevance is currently mechanism-agnostic; (3) part of GRK4's elevated Open Targets COPD score is literature-driven and may reflect the same TWAS paper being counted twice (once as a direct citation here, once via OT's literature-mining datatype), not two independent corroborating lines of evidence; and (4) no direct GRK4-smoking-initiation citation was found, despite GRK4 showing some OT smoking-initiation association, so a smoking-mediated alternative pathway (prior #1) cannot be ruled out either.

Runner-up: ADD1. ADD1 has the strongest positional/statistical support at this locus overall (PoPS-prioritized, by far the largest cumulative fine-mapping PIP mass across ~16 credible-set intronic variants, and the second-highest L2G score in the same FinnGen COPD credible set that favors GRK4), and the highest Open Targets smoking-initiation association score of any candidate — directly relevant to prior #1. But ADD1's only verified literature is for hypertension/cardiac-geometry phenotypes (including one gene x smoking interaction paper, but for hypertension, not COPD), and its smoking-initiation link is currently an Open Targets-only score without a citable GWAS paper — the red-team-skeptic explicitly warned against chaining these two unverified/off-target pieces of evidence into an "ADD1 is a smoking-mediated COPD gene" narrative. If a direct ADD1-smoking-initiation-GWAS or ADD1-COPD paper is later found, this call could flip.

HTT was explicitly not chosen despite dominating Open Targets' raw scores (highest COPD association score of all 7, and dominant L2G at both its own variant and the MSANTD1 lead variant) because the red-team-skeptic marked it CONTESTED: its apparent dominance is plausibly an artifact of HTT's enormous Huntington's-disease literature volume (3,145 OT-associated diseases, by far the most of any candidate), its only supporting spirometric evidence comes from a mechanistically distinct pathway (monogenic CAG-expansion neuromuscular/respiratory-effort decline, not airway-intrinsic biology), and it was not the top L2G gene in the actual COPD-labeled credible set (GRK4 was). See the audit file for the skeptic's full reasoning.

MSANTD1, despite being the nearest gene and the single highest-PIP fine-mapped variant in the credible set, has the weakest evidentiary support of all 7 candidates on every axis checked (lowest OT COPD score, lowest OT FEV1 score, no lung/immune-specific literature, never top L2G at its own variant) and is not recommended as the prioritized gene.

What would resolve this call: (a) independent replication of the GRK4-COPD TWAS finding in a second, non-overlapping cohort; (b) functional/experimental evidence (not network-inference) for a specific GRK4 inflammatory or airway mechanism; (c) a direct, citable ADD1-smoking-initiation GWAS publication (currently only an unverified Open Targets score); and (d) a locus-level fine-mapping or colocalization paper that explicitly adjudicates among MSANTD1/HTT/ADD1/GRK4 for the COPD phenotype specifically, since the internal credible set's top-PIP variant (MSANTD1) and Open Targets' independent L2G/association evidence (HTT, then GRK4/ADD1) currently point in different directions. The red-team-skeptic recommended a dedicated follow-up literature/contradiction-search pass on this locus before finalizing, since its own web-search budget was exhausted this session (see audit file) — the study team should weigh that limitation when deciding how much additional confirmatory search is warranted before acting on this locus's prioritization.
