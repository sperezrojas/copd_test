## Prioritized-gene synthesis

**Prioritized gene: PHACTR4. Confidence: LOW.**

This locus's causal gene assignment is genuinely unresolved, and this pick should be read as
the least-bad default rather than a confident call. Three genes have some claim to priority,
and none is well supported:

- **PHACTR4** is the nearest gene, contains by far the largest share of credible-set variants
  (the vast majority of the 159 credible-set variants, including the lead variant rs61783851
  and the sole missense variant rs61785974, fall within PHACTR4's introns/UTR), and is the
  default positional candidate. However, every VERIFIED literature finding for PHACTR4 is
  non-pulmonary and broadly pleiotropic (prostate cancer TWAS, type 1 diabetes immune-cell MR,
  neural tube/eye developmental biology, a multi-system congenital syndrome explicitly without
  a pulmonary phenotype, hepatocellular carcinoma, lupus nephritis biomarker) — the skeptic
  verdict for every PHACTR4 row above is WEAKENED, meaning the literature actively argues
  against, not merely fails to support, a lung-specific mechanism. Open Targets shows only a
  weak smoking-initiation signal and no COPD or lung-function evidence.
- **RCC1** (runner-up) is jointly prioritized by two independent statistical methods (MAGMA and
  PoPS) and is the most LOF-constrained gene at the locus, but has zero disease-relevant,
  lung-relevant, or immune-relevant literature of any kind (skeptic verdict: CONTESTED). Per
  the red-team critique, RCC1's essential nucleocytoplasmic-transport housekeeping role is
  exactly the profile MAGMA/PoPS can falsely prioritize at gene-dense loci (this locus packs
  eight genes into ~750kb), and the same prostate-cancer TWAS that flags PHACTR4 also flags
  RCC1 — supporting a "pleiotropic regulatory hub" alternative explanation over a COPD-specific
  mechanism.
- **OPRD1** (second runner-up, smoking-mediation hypothesis) is the only gene with a direct
  Open Targets COPD genetic-association score, consistent with prior #1 (smoking-mediated
  effects). However, the two PubMed-indexed genetic studies that directly test the relevant
  hypothesis (OPRD1 variants and nicotine dependence / persistent opioid use) are both null
  (skeptic verdict: CONTESTED), so this mediation hypothesis rests on an unverified database
  aggregate score rather than replicated genetics.

**What would resolve the tie:** (1) a lung-tissue or airway-epithelium eQTL colocalization
analysis specifically for PHACTR4, RCC1, and OPRD1 at this locus (Open Targets' locus-specific
L2G/coloc data could not be resolved for this write-up — see method note above); (2)
functional characterization of the credible-set missense variant rs61785974 itself (the
existing RPEL3-domain congenital-syndrome literature is suggestive but concerns different
variants and a non-pulmonary phenotype); (3) confirmation of whether this locus is among the
reported signals in Shrine et al. 2019's lung-function GWAS (PMID 30804560) — neither the
researcher nor the red-team-skeptic could resolve this, and per prior #2 this concordance/
discordance with spirometric traits is directly load-bearing for distinguishing a
pulmonary-biology mechanism from a smoking-behavior-mediated one; (4) a larger or independently
fine-mapped credible set — the current 159-variant set has a maximum PIP of 0.042, meaning
fine-mapping has not meaningfully narrowed the causal gene at all, which caps the confidence of
any single-gene call at this locus regardless of downstream literature.

Note: SESN2 and YTHDF2 have the most compelling COPD-specific mechanistic literature found
anywhere in this locus review (causal mouse emphysema data for SESN2; direct mediation of two
independent cigarette-smoke-induced COPD pathways for YTHDF2), but neither has any credible-set
variant, MAGMA/PoPS/ABC flag, or other statistical connection to this GWAS signal (SESN2 sits
~160kb outside the fine-mapped span). Per the red-team critique, these should NOT be read as
supported causal-gene candidates for this locus's GWAS signal specifically, despite their
strong general COPD biology — see audit file for full reasoning.
