## Prioritized gene

**FUBP1 — Confidence: LOW**

This call is close, and the internal pipeline's own nearest-gene/FLAMES pick (DNAJB4) and
PoPS pick (ZZZ3) are both named as runner-ups below for the study team's own weighing.

Reasoning: The single most specific, gene-resolving piece of evidence found anywhere at this
locus is that the fine-mapped credible-set variant itself (rs34517439, PIP=0.9998) — not a
proxy or LD-neighbor — is independently reported in two unrelated-disease GWAS contexts
(multiple myeloma risk, COVID-19 severity) as an eQTL lowering FUBP1 expression, with one of
those studies (Shi et al. 2023, PMID 37932299) demonstrating this specifically in lung
tissue and showing FUBP1 expression/antiviral function in airway epithelium (evidence table
rows for FUBP1, both marked WEAKENED). This is a stronger form of gene assignment than the
proximity-based nearest-gene/FLAMES heuristic that named DNAJB4, especially because DNAJB4's
own best-characterized biology (rows marked CONTESTED above) is a Mendelian myofibrillar
myopathy causing RESTRICTIVE respiratory failure via diaphragm/muscle weakness — a
mechanism categorically different from COPD's obstructive airway/parenchymal pathophysiology
— and DNAJB4 has no documented airway-epithelial, alveolar, or developmental lung biology at
all.

However, per the skeptic's critique, this pick should not be overstated: neither eQTL study
was conducted in a COPD context or colocalized against the actual COPD GWAS signal; the
bridge from FUBP1's epithelial interferon/antiviral biology to COPD pathophysiology is
plausible (COPD involves impaired antiviral responses and exacerbation susceptibility) but
unconfirmed by any direct COPD, spirometry, or airway-remodeling study of FUBP1. The
locus also shows pleiotropy across COVID-19 (twice), idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis, and
leukocyte telomere length at this same variant, which the skeptic argues is at least as
consistent with a broad host-defense/interferon-response or cellular-senescence locus as
with a COPD-specific obstructive-disease locus — none of those cross-trait studies
independently resolved the target gene as FUBP1 (only the myeloma and COVID-19 eQTL studies
did, via expression data).

**Runner-up 1 — DNAJB4 (nearest gene / FLAMES pick):** Deprioritized specifically because
the lead variant's own eQTL data point to FUBP1, not DNAJB4, as the regulatory target in
lung tissue, and DNAJB4's only well-established disease mechanism is phenotypically
discordant with COPD (restrictive vs. obstructive). Not eliminated as a candidate — no study
has performed COPD-specific functional or colocalization work on DNAJB4 either — but the
nearest-gene/FLAMES assignment looks more like a proximity artifact than a mechanistically
supported call given everything found.

**Runner-up 2 — ZZZ3 (PoPS pick):** Deprioritized because (a) its genomic coordinates
(independently confirmed via both Ensembl and Open Targets, both GRCh38) do not physically
overlap the stated locus interval at all (~216 kb away), casting doubt on whether it belongs
in this locus's candidate set in the first place, and (b) no COPD-, spirometry-, or
airway-specific mechanistic literature was found — its documented biology is a ubiquitous
ATAC-complex chromatin-regulatory role with incidental appearances in lung cancer, pancreatic
cancer, and vascular smooth muscle biology, none of it COPD-relevant. ZZZ3's higher Open
Targets L2G score for FEV1 versus DNAJB4 should be treated cautiously given this coordinate
discrepancy, since it may reflect a distinct GWAS signal rather than the same rs34517439
credible set.

**What would resolve the tie:** (1) Formal colocalization of the COPD GWAS credible set
against lung-tissue eQTL data for DNAJB4, FUBP1, and ZZZ3 specifically (coloc/SMR-HEIDI),
rather than relying on eQTL findings borrowed from non-COPD disease studies; (2) resolution
of whether the ZZZ3 PoPS window/coordinates reflect a genuine signal at this locus or a
scoping error (check GRCh37 vs. GRCh38 coordinates used in the internal PoPS run); (3) a
direct check of smoking-initiation/cessation/heaviness GWAS (e.g. GSCAN) at rs34517439,
which no agent in this pipeline was able to complete, given the very high fine-mapping PIP
and the locus's apparent involvement in interferon/senescence-related traits; (4) any direct
COPD case-control, spirometry, or airway-remodeling functional study of FUBP1, which
currently does not exist. Given all of the above, confidence in FUBP1 as the causal gene is
marked LOW rather than MODERATE or HIGH — this is the best-supported single pick from
currently verified evidence, not a settled call, and the study team should weigh the
runner-ups seriously pending the confirmatory work above.
