# Evidence table — Locus 1:43,300,001-43,850,000 (nearest gene PTPRF)

Lead variant: rs2842171 (1:43,574,179, GRCh38, PIP=0.162, PTPRF intron). 32-variant
credible set, all intron/upstream/intergenic consequence (no missense/splice-donor/
splice-acceptor variants); PTPRF is the annotated gene for the great majority of the
credible set. Candidate genes from internal pipeline: PTPRF (nearest gene), SLC2A1
(MAGMA-prioritized), ERMAP (PoPS-prioritized).

**RE-OPENING NOTE (2026-09-11):** SLC2A1 and ERMAP were subsequently discovered to
be artifacts of a genome-build error in the MAGMA/PoPS gene-location merge file
(`loci/copd-all-gene-prioritization.txt`) — both genes sit 500-730kb outside this
locus's stated window. A corrected re-merge surfaced **CDC20** as a new candidate
gene for this same locus, never evaluated in the original review. CDC20 has now
been independently confirmed to genuinely overlap this locus's window (unlike
SLC2A1/ERMAP) and has been fully researched, verified, and critiqued per the
pipeline's standard process; see the dedicated CDC20 section below and the
updated "Prioritized gene" synthesis at the end of this file.

All citations below carry a citation-verifier verdict of VERIFIED (checked
2026-09-11). "Skeptic verdict" reflects the red-team-skeptic's gene-level
assessment (see full reasoning in `audits/1_43300001-43850000_audit.md`), not a

**CDC20 physical-location note:** CDC20's genomic coordinates (chr1:43,358,928-
43,363,207, GRCh38, confirmed via Open Targets/Ensembl) genuinely fall inside
this locus's stated window (1:43,300,001-43,850,000) — unlike SLC2A1 and ERMAP,
which sit 340-500kb outside the window entirely. However, CDC20 sits ~145kb
upstream of the nearest credible-set variant and ~211-266kb upstream of the lead
variant/bulk of the PTPRF-annotated credible set — i.e., it overlaps the locus
window but not the credible-set span itself.

Colocalization (COLOC_PIP_ECAVIAR method): the COPD credible set colocalizes
strongly (H4 = 0.87-0.96) with five independent smoking-behavior credible sets
at this same locus/variant — smoking initiation, smoking cessation (x2), ever-
smoker, tobacco use disorder, age of smoking initiation — as well as with height,
cognitive/educational-attainment, C-reactive protein, and red-blood-cell-count
credible sets (broad pleiotropy). No colocalization with any spirometric
(FEV1/FEV1-FVC) trait was found among the credible sets examined. CDC20 does not
appear in any of these colocalizing credible sets' L2G predictions either.

## Prioritized gene

**PTPRF — confidence MODERATE (reaffirmed after CDC20 re-evaluation).**

PTPRF is the nearest gene, is annotated to essentially all 32 credible-set
variants (all intron/upstream, none missense/splice), and is the only candidate
gene with any COPD-specific genetic-fine-mapping signal at all: it is the top
Open Targets L2G gene (0.331) on the one fine-mapped study labeled for chronic
airway obstruction at this exact variant, and that same credible set colocalizes
strongly with independent COPD-related-trait fine-mapping. By contrast, SLC2A1
(MAGMA-prioritized) and ERMAP (PoPS-prioritized) both lie 500-750kb outside the
stated locus boundary, have zero L2G support across all 70 studies fine-mapped
at the lead variant, and were marked CONTESTED by the red-team-skeptic — their
gene-level prioritization scores are more plausibly an artifact of the broad
MAGMA/PoPS locus-window definition than evidence that they are the causal target
of rs2842171. Their positive literature findings (a single small MR study for
SLC2A1 with a confidence interval nearly touching the null; a genuinely
interesting but entirely non-lung B7-family immune mechanism for ERMAP) do not
overcome this physical/statistical gap.

However, the case for PTPRF is explicitly NOT a pulmonary-biology case: no
lung-specific structural, developmental, or immune literature for PTPRF was
found despite targeted searching (zero hits for "PTPRF airway epithelial cell
adhesion," "PTPRF lung function FEV1," and "PTPRF LAR phosphatase immune T
cell"), and Open Targets shows zero PTPRF association with FEV1, FEV1/FVC, or
general lung function. Instead, the same COPD credible set at this locus
colocalizes at H4 0.87-0.96 with multiple independent smoking-behavior credible
sets (smoking initiation, cessation, ever-smoker, tobacco use disorder, age of
initiation), and the one piece of independent literature that names PTPRF by
gene symbol (PMID 37173343) does so for impulsive personality / drug-
experimentation traits, not respiratory phenotypes. The red-team-skeptic
concluded this pattern is "a textbook signature of prior #1 (smoking-mediated),
not prior #2," and explicitly warned against treating "PTPRF is the dominant
credible-set gene" as equivalent to "PTPRF drives COPD through pulmonary
biology." I agree with that distinction and it is the reason confidence here is
MODERATE rather than HIGH: the gene-identification call at this locus (PTPRF
over SLC2A1/ERMAP) is reasonably well supported, but the mechanistic
interpretation is very likely smoking-behavior-mediated rather than
lung-parenchyma-mediated, and this should be weighed accordingly by the study
team — a spirometry-adjusted or smoking-adjusted reanalysis of this signal, or a
lung-tissue-specific eQTL check for PTPRF (vs. brain/CNS tissue) at rs2842171,
would help resolve which pathway is truly operating.

**CDC20 re-evaluation (2026-09-11): does not change this call.** A corrected
MAGMA/PoPS re-merge (fixing the same genome-build bug that produced the bogus
SLC2A1/ERMAP calls) surfaced CDC20 as a new candidate. Unlike SLC2A1/ERMAP,
CDC20 genuinely does physically overlap this locus's stated window
(chr1:43,358,928-43,363,207 vs. window 43,300,001-43,850,000), so it cannot be
dismissed as an outright build-bug artifact. However, after full researcher
work-up, citation verification (19/19 citations VERIFIED), and red-team-skeptic
critique, CDC20 is assessed as weaker than PTPRF on every measured axis: it
sits 145-266kb from the credible-set span itself (not overlapping it), has zero
L2G support across all 70 GWAS credible sets fine-mapped at the lead variant
(including the COPD-labeled study, where CDC20 does not appear at all), and has
an overall Open Targets COPD association score roughly 17-fold lower than
PTPRF's. Its literature case is real and verified but rests on senescence and
fibroblast-activation mechanisms demonstrated in idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis
and pulmonary arterial hypertension models — diseases mechanistically adjacent
to, but distinct from, COPD/emphysema — plus one direct but cancer-cell-line
demonstration that cigarette smoke impairs CDC20/APC-mediated protein
degradation. No COPD-specific, emphysema-specific, or airway-epithelial study of
CDC20 exists, and its "immune" literature is generic pan-cancer
tumor-immune-infiltration correlation with no airway or COPD specificity. The
red-team-skeptic characterized CDC20 as "inside the window, but outside the
signal" and explicitly recommended reaffirming PTPRF at MODERATE confidence
without promoting CDC20 to co-favorite or tie status. I concur: CDC20 is best
treated as a secondary, hypothesis-generating note (its lung-fibroblast
senescence mechanism, PMID 35988650, would be worth a dedicated lung-tissue
eQTL/GTEx check given senescence's recognized role in COPD pathophysiology) but
does not unseat PTPRF as the best-supported gene at this locus, nor does it
resolve the smoking-mediation-vs-pulmonary-biology ambiguity that keeps PTPRF's
confidence at MODERATE rather than HIGH.

Runner-up note: ST3GAL3 (not part of the original candidate list) has a
nonzero, real L2G signal on the COPD credible set itself and a concrete,
lung-relevant (though eosinophilic/asthma-context rather than COPD-context)
mechanism, and the red-team-skeptic recommended it receive a fuller supplementary
work-up before this locus's prioritization is treated as final. It is not named
as the top pick here because it was not part of the directed candidate list and
was not fully evaluated across all three evidence domains, not because current
evidence rules it out. CDC20 is now a second runner-up-tier note, on materially
weaker physical/statistical footing than ST3GAL3 (zero L2G vs. ST3GAL3's
nonzero 0.074) but with its own distinct (senescence/fibrosis) mechanistic
rationale worth flagging for any future eQTL follow-up at this locus.
