## Prioritized gene

**Prioritized gene: DPYD**
**Confidence: LOW**

DPYD is the best-supported single gene at this locus, but the support is
positional/statistical rather than mechanistic, and the red-team-skeptic critique
(preserved in full in the audit appendix) weakened rather than confirmed it. The case
for DPYD rests on: (1) unanimous nearest-gene/FLAMES/MAGMA/PoPS/ABC prioritization; (2)
an Open Targets gwas_credible_sets evidence score of 0.68 and genetic_association score
of 0.41 for COPD, versus zero comparable evidence for MIR137HG; and (3) a plausible,
though unproven, alternative-gene comparison — MIR137HG physically overlaps most of the
credible set's lower-PIP variants but was selected by none of the four prioritization
methods, and its COPD/FEV1 genetic evidence is entirely absent (row-by-row in the table
above). Against DPYD: the lead variant rs61787828 has no catalogued GWAS Catalog record
of its own; two rounds of red-team search attempts (both hampered by exhausted search
budgets, disclosed in the audit appendix) could not confirm this exact locus is among
the reported hits in the three large COPD/smoking GWAS papers cited; there is no
DPYD-FEV1 spirometric concordance in Open Targets; DPYD shows no genetic constraint
against loss-of-function; and DPYD's only established biology (pyrimidine catabolism,
fluoropyrimidine chemotherapy pharmacogenomics) and all of its lung-tissue-adjacent
literature (Shaul 2014, Hirota 2012, Tominaga 2016) are oncology-context, not
COPD/airway-remodeling-relevant. The one new piece of evidence surfaced during critique
— a DPYD-annotated association with age at first Pseudomonas aeruginosa infection in
cystic fibrosis (Lorè et al. 2020) — shows the region has some independent
chronic-lung-disease genetic relevance beyond this single COPD GWAS (arguing mildly
against a pure LD/positional artifact), but is phenotype-mismatched (airway infection
timing, not airflow obstruction) and does not resolve the mechanism question.

Taken together with the pipeline's smoking-mediation prior, the weight of evidence
(strong DPYD-region genetic associations with smoking cessation/initiation, absence of
FEV1 concordance, and this locus's near-coincidence with one of the most replicated
schizophrenia loci in the genome via MIR137HG/Ripke et al. 2011) favors a
smoking-behavior-mediated interpretation of this locus's COPD signal over an intrinsic
DPYD pulmonary mechanism. This is a plausible, evidence-consistent inference, not a
proven one (no formal colocalization or Mendelian-randomization analysis was available
to test it directly).

**Runner-up: MIR137HG** — marked CONTESTED by red-team-skeptic. Despite dense physical
overlap with the credible set, it was not selected by any prioritization method, has
zero direct COPD/FEV1 genetic evidence, and its only immune/inflammatory literature
(macrophage polarization) is non-lung, non-COPD, and directionally contradictory across
studies (pro-M2 in one acute-lung-injury model, pro-M1 in a glioma model). Its one
well-replicated genetic signal (schizophrenia) does not by itself establish a lung or
smoking-behavior mechanism, though it is biologically plausible given known
schizophrenia-smoking comorbidity.

**What would resolve this call:** (1) formal colocalization of the COPD credible set
with a lung-function (FEV1/FEV1-FVC) GWAS at this exact locus (e.g., Shrine et al.
UK Biobank spirometry GWAS) — searches for this were attempted but inconclusive due to
search-tool budget exhaustion, and this is the single highest-value follow-up; (2)
formal colocalization/Mendelian-randomization testing whether the COPD signal here is
fully explained by the smoking-initiation/cessation GWAS signal at 1p21.3; (3) direct
confirmation (via full supplementary tables, not abstracts) of whether this locus is
among the reported hits in Sakornsakolpat et al. 2019, Liu et al. 2019, or Saunders et
al. 2022; (4) lung/airway-immune-cell eQTL or ATAC-seq data distinguishing whether
credible-set variants regulate DPYD or MIR137HG expression independently. Until such
data are available, this call should be treated as the best-supported working
hypothesis, not a settled conclusion — the study team should weigh the LOW confidence
rating accordingly.
