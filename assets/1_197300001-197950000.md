## Prioritized gene

**Gene: DENND1B**
**Confidence: LOW**

DENND1B is the best-supported single candidate at this locus, but the case is materially hedged and should
not be read as an established COPD mechanism gene. In favor: (1) it is both MAGMA- and ABC-prioritized, and
essentially the entire 172-variant credible set falls within its intronic/regulatory footprint (see evidence
table rows and locus description above); (2) it has the only mechanistically specific, experimentally
validated biology among the four candidates — a Rab35 GEF controlling Th2-selective TCR down-modulation,
with a well-replicated (if imperfectly replicated — see the Ferreira 2011 non-replication and Duffy 2019
population-specific-effect rows above) genome-wide-significant asthma GWAS signal; (3) per the Open Targets
structured lookup, it has the highest genome-wide GWAS-L2G score of the three originally-flagged candidates
for both smoking initiation (0.840) and asthma (0.244), and the only lung-tissue QTL signal alongside
immune-cell (monocyte/neutrophil/macrophage) QTLs.

However, per the red-team-skeptic critique (preserved verbatim in the audit file), this case has a specific,
serious gap: **zero COPD-specific or spirometric (FEV1/FEV1-FVC) literature or colocalization evidence
exists for DENND1B anywhere** — the entire positive case is built from asthma/atopy/autoimmune-disease
genetics and mechanism, not from COPD or lung-function genetics directly. The pipeline's Prior #1 (smoking-
mediated effects) is directly relevant and unresolved here: DENND1B's single strongest documented
genome-wide trait association is smoking initiation (L2G=0.840, more than 3x its asthma L2G), and the
"asthma-COPD overlap syndrome" bridge invoked to connect DENND1B's Th2/asthma mechanism to this COPD signal
is asserted, not evidenced, by anything in the dossier — no COPD-cohort data links DENND1B variants to
eosinophilic/Th2-high COPD subtypes at this locus. The skeptic explicitly declined to elevate this to
CONTESTED (no paper argues against DENND1B), but marked it WEAKENED and recommended presenting it as
"best-supported gene at the locus generically, but via smoking-behavior and/or asthma/Th2 pathways whose
relevance to intrinsic COPD/emphysema pathophysiology is unestablished" rather than as a confirmed COPD gene.

**Runner-up: LHX9** (not in the input file's candidate list; surfaced by the researcher via direct gene
enumeration in the interval) has the only genuinely COPD-specific mechanistic literature at this locus —
increased expression in COPD alveolar type 2 cells correlating with airflow obstruction, IFN-gamma-inducible,
affecting AT2 cell survival (PMID 34548271) — but the red-team-skeptic flagged this inclusion as
methodologically questionable: LHX9 was declined by all four of the locus's statistical prioritization
methods (nearest-gene, MAGMA, PoPS, ABC), and there is no eQTL, colocalization, or fine-mapping evidence
connecting any of the 172 credible-set variants to LHX9 expression or function. It should be treated as an
interesting, hypothesis-generating, single-study lead rather than a genetically-linked candidate.

**Runner-up: C1orf53 and CRB1** are both best characterized, per the researcher and skeptic, as likely
proximity/LD artifacts (nearest-gene bias for C1orf53; PoPS/LD-tagging of DENND1B's real asthma signal for
CRB1) rather than independently causal genes — neither has any positive functional or disease-genetics case
in the literature reviewed.

**What would resolve this:** a COPD-specific or FEV1/FEV1-FVC-specific GWAS colocalization or TWAS result
directly implicating DENND1B (or any candidate) at this exact locus; a smoking-adjusted re-analysis of this
COPD signal to test whether it survives adjustment for smoking behavior (which would argue against pure
smoking-mediation and for genuine pulmonary/immune biology); or an eQTL/colocalization study connecting
credible-set variants to LHX9 expression specifically, which would elevate it from "physically nearby with
an unrelated COPD paper" to a genetically-supported candidate. Given all of this, confidence in DENND1B as
the causal gene is LOW, not MODERATE or HIGH — it is the best-supported candidate among weak options, not a
well-established one.
